You spent an hour editing a TikTok, saved it as a draft, and now you can't find it. To see your TikTok drafts, open the TikTok app, tap **Profile** and look for the **Drafts** folder at the start of your video grid, usually the first tile in your posts tab. Tap it to open every draft saved on that phone.

![How to see TikTok drafts: Profile, then the Drafts tile at the start of your video grid, stored on your phone only](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-drafts-hero-1200x675.webp)

If you don't see a Drafts folder, you have no drafts saved for that account on this device. That's because **TikTok drafts are stored on your phone rather than in your account**, and that one detail explains most "where did my drafts go?" moments.

This guide covers where drafts live, how to open and manage them, three ways to back them up, what to check when they disappear, and habits that stop you losing them.

## Why TikTok Drafts Live on Your Phone (and Why They Vanish)

![What keeps TikTok drafts safe and what deletes them, like reinstalling the app or switching phones](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-drafts-device-1200x675.webp)

TikTok saves drafts locally, on the phone where you created them, rather than uploading them to TikTok's servers. That keeps drafts quick to open, but it means your account doesn't know about them.

| Question | Short answer (at the time of writing) |
| --- | --- |
| Where is the Drafts folder? | Profile → first tile in your video grid, labeled Drafts |
| Where are drafts stored? | On the device where you created them, not in your TikTok account |
| Can I see drafts on another phone? | No. Drafts don't sync between devices |
| Can I see drafts on tiktok.com? | Drafts made in the mobile app don't appear on the web |
| What deletes drafts? | Deleting them, uninstalling the app, clearing the app's storage or data, or switching phones without a backup |
| How do I back up a draft? | Save it to your device, or post it with privacy set to Only me |

### What Most People Don't Know

- **Logging in on a new phone won't bring drafts with it.**
- **Uninstalling and reinstalling TikTok can delete drafts**, because the app's local storage is removed.
- **Clearing the app's data or storage** in your phone's settings (especially on Android) can remove them.
- **Switching accounts** can make drafts seem to vanish; they may simply be tied to the other account on that phone.

> **Key point:** Before you uninstall, reset or replace your phone, back up any draft you want to keep. Once the app's local data is gone, TikTok usually can't bring drafts back.

## How to See TikTok Drafts in the App (4 Steps)

![Four taps to see TikTok drafts: Profile, posts tab, Drafts tile, open a draft](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-drafts-steps-1200x675.webp)

TikTok updates its app often, so labels and positions can shift.

### Step 1: Tap Profile

It's in the bottom-right corner.

### Step 2: Switch to Your Posts Tab

Make sure you're on the tab that shows your posted videos (the grid icon). The Drafts tile doesn't show on the Liked or Favorites tabs, which is where people often look by mistake.

### Step 3: Tap the Drafts Tile

It's usually the first item in the grid, and it shows how many drafts you have.

### Step 4: Open a Draft

Tap any draft to open it in the editor, where you can keep editing, post it or save it.

> **Pro tip:** Inside the Drafts folder, tap **Select** (or a similar option) to choose several drafts at once and delete the ones you don't need. A shorter list makes the drafts you care about easier to find.

### Drafts vs Private Videos

| | Draft | Private video (Only me) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it's stored | On your phone | In your TikTok account |
| Visible to others | No | No |
| Survives a new phone | No | Yes |
| Survives reinstalling the app | Often not | Yes |
| Still editable with effects and sounds | Yes | No, it's a finished post |

Private videos appear in your profile like other posts, often under a lock icon or a separate private tab. That difference matters for backups.

## How to Back Up TikTok Drafts: 3 Ways

### Option 1: Save the Draft to Your Device

1. Open the draft from your Drafts folder.
2. Tap **Next** to reach the post screen.
3. Look for **More options** (or a settings menu) and turn on **Save to device**, or use a download option if your app version shows one.
4. Post the video with **Only me** privacy so the copy saves without anyone else seeing it, or follow the app's prompt if it offers a save-only option.

The saved copy is the finished video file. You won't be able to reopen it as an editable draft with separate effects, text and sounds. Our guide on [saving TikTok drafts to your camera roll](/blog/how-to-save-tiktok-drafts-to-camera-roll) has more detail.

### Option 2: Post It Privately With Only Me

Posting a draft with **Only me** privacy keeps it in your account, safe across devices. When you're ready to share it, change the privacy to public from the video's settings. This is the most reliable backup if you're about to switch phones.

### Option 3: Keep Your Raw Footage

If you film in your phone's camera app and import into TikTok, the original clips stay in your gallery even if the draft is lost. You can rebuild the edit later.

| Check | Fix | Why it works |

| 2. Old app version? | Update from your app store | Bugs can hide the Drafts folder |
| 3. Glitch? | Restart your phone | Clears a stuck app state |
| 4. Phone nearly full? | Free up space, reopen TikTok | Drafts may fail to save or load |
| 5. Recent reinstall or reset? | Look for saved copies instead | The drafts were likely removed with local storage |
| 6. Saved copies? | Check your camera roll and Only me posts | You may have a backup already |
| 7. Still missing? | **Settings and privacy → Report a problem** | Support can investigate bugs |

Because drafts live on your device, TikTok may not be able to restore them. Phone backups sometimes include app data, but whether they restore TikTok drafts depends on your device and backup settings, so don't rely on it.

## "TikTok Draft Recovery" Apps and Sites (Use With Caution)

Search for "recover deleted TikTok drafts" and you'll find apps and websites that claim to bring them back.

### The Real Risks

- **Many ask for your TikTok login**, which gives them full access to your account. Never share it.
- **They can't reach data that no longer exists.** If the drafts were removed with the app's storage, there's nothing on TikTok's side to recover.
- **Some are paid** and deliver nothing, or push you to install more software.

### What to Do Instead

- Check your camera roll and your Only me posts.
- Check whether your phone's own backup includes app data.
- Ask TikTok support through **Report a problem**.

> **Bottom line:** No outside app can recover drafts TikTok itself can't. Back up before you need to.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where are my drafts on TikTok?

Go to your Profile and tap the Drafts tile, which usually sits first in your video grid. If there's no Drafts tile, there are no drafts saved for that account on this phone.

### Why did my TikTok drafts disappear?

Drafts are stored on your device, so they can vanish if you uninstall the app, clear its data, switch phones or log into a different account. App bugs and low storage can also hide them.

### Can I see my TikTok drafts on another phone?

No. At the time of writing, drafts don't sync across devices. To move a video to a new phone, save it to your device and transfer the file, or post it with Only me privacy.

### Can I see TikTok drafts on a computer?

Drafts made in the mobile app don't appear on tiktok.com. Open them on the phone where you made them.

### How do I save TikTok drafts to my camera roll?

Open the draft, tap Next, and turn on the Save to device option before posting. Posting with Only me privacy saves a copy without anyone else seeing the video.

### Can TikTok support recover deleted drafts?

Support can help with app bugs, but because drafts are saved locally, TikTok usually can't restore drafts that were deleted along with the app or its data.

### Can you recover TikTok drafts after deleting the app?

Usually not. Once the app and its local data are removed, the drafts go too. Phone backups sometimes include app data, but don't rely on it.

### How many drafts can I have on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a limit. Your phone's free storage is the practical limit, and a nearly full phone can stop drafts saving.
