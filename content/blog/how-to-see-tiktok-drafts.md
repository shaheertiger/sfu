To see your TikTok drafts, open the TikTok app, tap **Profile** and look for the **Drafts** folder at the start of your video grid, usually the first tile in your posts tab. Tap it to open every draft saved on that phone. If you don't see a Drafts folder, you have no drafts saved on this device, because TikTok drafts are stored on your phone rather than in your account.

That last detail explains most "where did my drafts go?" moments. Here's how to find drafts, protect them and troubleshoot when they disappear.

## Where are my TikTok drafts? Quick answer

| Question | Short answer (at the time of writing) |
| --- | --- |
| Where is the Drafts folder? | Profile → first tile in your video grid, labeled Drafts |
| Where are drafts stored? | On the device where you created them, not in your TikTok account |

TikTok updates its app often, so labels and positions can shift. If something looks different, check TikTok's Help Center in the app under **Settings and privacy → Report a problem** or **Support**.

## How to see TikTok drafts in the app

1. Open TikTok and tap **Profile** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Make sure you're on the tab that shows your posted videos (the grid icon).
3. Tap the **Drafts** tile, usually the first item in the grid. It shows how many drafts you have.
4. Tap any draft to open it in the editor, where you can keep editing, post it or save it.

### How to delete or manage drafts

Inside the Drafts folder, tap **Select** (or a similar option) to choose several drafts at once, then delete the ones you don't need. Clearing out old drafts makes the ones you care about easier to find.

### Drafts vs private videos

A draft is an unfinished video stored on your phone. A private video, posted with the **Only me** privacy setting, is published to your account but visible only to you. Private videos appear in your profile like other posts (often under a lock icon or a separate private tab) and stay with your account when you change phones. That difference matters for backups.

## Why TikTok drafts are stored on your device

TikTok saves drafts locally, on the phone where you created them, rather than uploading them to TikTok's servers. That keeps drafts quick to open, but it means your account doesn't know about them. At the time of writing:

- **Logging in on a new phone won't bring drafts with it.**
- **Uninstalling and reinstalling TikTok can delete drafts**, because the app's local storage is removed.
- **Clearing the app's data or storage** in your phone's settings (especially on Android) can remove them.
- **Switching accounts** in the app can make drafts seem to vanish; they may simply be tied to the other account on that phone.

Before you uninstall, reset or replace your phone, back up any draft you want to keep.

## How to back up TikTok drafts

### Option 1: Save the draft to your device

This saves a copy of the video to your camera roll or gallery.

1. Open the draft from your Drafts folder.
2. Tap **Next** to reach the post screen.
3. Look for **More options** (or a settings menu) and turn on **Save to device**, or use a download option if your app version shows one.
4. Post the video with **Only me** privacy so the copy saves without anyone else seeing it, or follow the app's prompt if it offers a save-only option.

The exact steps vary by app version. Note that a saved copy is the finished video file: you won't be able to reopen it as an editable draft with separate effects, text and sounds.

### Option 2: Post it privately with Only me

Posting a draft with **Only me** privacy keeps it in your account, safe across devices. When you're ready to share it, you can change the privacy to public from the video's settings. This is the most reliable backup if you're about to switch phones.

### Option 3: Keep your raw footage

If you film in your phone's camera app and import into TikTok, the original clips stay in your gallery even if the draft is lost. You can rebuild the edit later.

## TikTok drafts disappeared: troubleshooting

If you're sure you saved drafts and they're gone, work through these checks.

1. **Check you're on the right account.** If you have more than one TikTok account on the phone, switch and look at each profile's Drafts tile.
2. **Update the app.** Bugs in older versions can stop the Drafts folder from showing. Update from your app store and reopen TikTok.
3. **Restart your phone** and check again.
4. **Check storage space.** If your phone is almost full, drafts may fail to save or load. Free up space, then reopen the app.
5. **Think about recent changes.** Did you reinstall TikTok, clear its data, restore your phone or move to a new one? If so, the drafts were likely removed with the local storage.
6. **Look for saved copies.** Check your camera roll for videos you saved from TikTok, and your profile for anything posted with Only me.
7. **Contact TikTok support.** In the app, go to **Settings and privacy → Report a problem**. Support can investigate bugs, but because drafts live on your device, TikTok may not be able to restore them.

### Can you recover TikTok drafts after deleting the app?

Usually not. Once the app and its local data are removed, the drafts go too. Phone backups sometimes include app data, but whether they restore TikTok drafts depends on your device and backup settings, so don't rely on it. Be cautious of apps or websites that claim to "recover TikTok drafts": many ask for your login, which you should never share.

## Habits that prevent lost drafts

- **Don't use drafts as long-term storage.** Post or save drafts within a few days.
- **Save to device before any phone change**, factory reset or reinstall.
- **Name your projects clearly in captions** so you can tell drafts apart.
- **Keep your original footage** in your gallery or cloud storage.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are my drafts on TikTok?

Go to your Profile and tap the Drafts tile, which usually sits first in your video grid. If there's no Drafts tile, there are no drafts saved for that account on this phone.

### Why did my TikTok drafts disappear?

Drafts are stored on your device, so they can vanish if you uninstall the app, clear its data, switch phones or log into a different account. App bugs and low storage can also hide them. Update the app, check each account, and look for saved copies in your camera roll.

### Can I see my TikTok drafts on another phone?

No. At the time of writing, drafts don't sync across devices. To move a video to a new phone, save it to your device and transfer the file, or post it with Only me privacy so it stays with your account.

### How do I save TikTok drafts to my camera roll?

Open the draft, tap Next, and turn on the Save to device option in the post settings before posting. Posting with Only me privacy saves a copy without anyone else seeing the video. The saved file is the finished video, not an editable draft.

### Can TikTok support recover deleted drafts?

Support can help with app bugs, but because drafts are saved locally on your phone, TikTok usually can't restore drafts that were deleted along with the app or its data. Back up important drafts before changing devices.
