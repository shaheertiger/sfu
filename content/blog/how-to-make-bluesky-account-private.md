You've been looking for the lock icon in Bluesky's settings and it isn't there. That's because you can't make a Bluesky account private. There's no "protected" or locked mode like X or Instagram, since every post, like, follow and block is stored as public data on the AT Protocol, the open network Bluesky runs on.

![Bluesky has no private accounts, but logged-out visibility, reply controls, blocks, mutes and DM settings limit who sees you](/images/blog/how-to-make-bluesky-account-private-hero-1200x675.webp)

What you can do is limit who sees you when they aren't logged in, control who can reply to and quote you, and block or mute the people you don't want around. None of it is a lock, and it helps to know exactly what each setting does and doesn't do.

This guide covers why private accounts don't exist on Bluesky, the six settings that help, and what to do if you really need a private space.

## Why Bluesky Can't Have Private Accounts (the Open Protocol Problem)

![What you can control on Bluesky, like replies, quotes and DMs, next to what you can't, like follower approval and hidden likes](/images/blog/how-to-make-bluesky-account-private-control-1200x675.webp)

On X or Instagram, one company stores your posts and decides who sees them. On Bluesky, your posts live as public records on the AT Protocol, and any app built on it can read them. A "private" switch in one app couldn't stop the others.

| You want to | Possible on Bluesky? | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approve followers | No | No private mode |
| Hide posts from logged-out visitors | Partly | Logged-out visibility setting |
| Stop strangers replying | Yes | Reply settings on each post |
| Stop quote posts | Yes | Quote settings, or detach a quote |
| Stop someone seeing your posts | Mostly | Block them |
| Keep DMs private | Yes | Message settings |
| Keep saved posts private | Yes | Bookmarks are private |
| Hide your likes | No | Likes are public data |

### What Most People Don't Know

Even blocks are public on Bluesky. Mutes are private, bookmarks are private, and DMs are private, but likes, follows and blocks are all part of the public record. Our guide to [who blocked me on Bluesky](/blog/who-blocked-me-on-bluesky) explains how that works.

> **Key point:** On Bluesky, assume anything you post, like or follow can be seen by anyone. The settings below reduce who bumps into you; they don't make anything secret.

## 6 Settings That Make Bluesky Feel More Private (Step by Step)

![Six Bluesky settings that limit exposure: logged-out visibility, reply controls, quote controls, hidden replies, blocks and mutes, DM settings](/images/blog/how-to-make-bluesky-account-private-settings-1200x675.webp)

Menu names change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Turn On Logged-Out Visibility

Go to **Settings > Privacy and security > Logged-out visibility** (in older versions it sits under **Moderation**) and switch on **Discourage apps from showing my account to logged-out users**.

This stops bsky.app and the official apps from showing your profile and posts to people who aren't signed in. It's a request, not a lock: other apps and anyone using the API can still read your posts, and Bluesky says so in the setting itself. It also turns off the RSS feed for your profile. Treat it as a speed bump, not privacy.

### Step 2: Control Who Can Reply

When you write a post, tap **Anyone can interact** below the text box (or **Post interaction settings** in the menu of a published post) and choose who can reply:

- Everybody
- Nobody
- Only people you mention
- Only people you follow
- Only people who follow you
- Members of a list you choose

### Step 3: Switch Off Quote Posts

In the same settings you can switch off quote posts. If someone already quoted you, open their quote and choose **Detach quote** to remove your post from it.

> **Pro tip:** Set defaults once in **Settings > Moderation > Interaction settings**, so every new post starts locked down. It's the step most people skip, then forget on the one post that draws a pile-on.

### Step 4: Hide Replies You Don't Want

On your own posts, open a reply's menu and pick **Hide reply for everyone**. It moves behind a "hidden replies" link at the bottom of the thread.

### Step 5: Block, Mute and Use Lists

- **Block** stops someone from seeing your posts while they're signed in, replying, quoting, following or messaging you. Blocks are public.
- **Mute** hides someone from your view without them knowing. Mutes are private.
- **Moderation lists** let you block or mute a whole list at once, including community-made lists of known spam or harassment accounts.
- **Muted words** hide posts that contain words, tags or phrases you choose.

### Step 6: Lock Down DMs

Under **Settings > Privacy and security > Chat**, choose who can message you: everyone, people you follow, or no one.

## What Each Setting Actually Protects (and What It Doesn't)

![Bluesky privacy settings compared: what each one stops and what still gets through](/images/blog/how-to-make-bluesky-account-private-limits-1200x675.webp)

| Setting | What it stops | What still gets through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logged-out visibility | Official apps showing you to signed-out visitors | Other apps and the API |
| Reply controls | Unwanted replies on that post | Quote posts, unless switched off |
| Quote controls | New quotes of your post | Screenshots |
| Block | That person seeing you while signed in | Logged-out viewing and other apps |
| Mute | You seeing them | Them seeing you |
| Chat settings | Unwanted DMs | Public replies, if allowed |

### Bookmarks: the One Private Thing You Save

Bookmarks (saved posts) are private and stored off-protocol, so no one else can see what you saved. That's the closest thing to private likes; see [are likes public on Bluesky](/blog/are-likes-public-on-bluesky).

## Need a Space Only Friends Can See? Your 3 Options

If you need a space only friends can see, Bluesky isn't the right tool. Your options:

1. **A second account** with a handle no one recognises. It's still public, just obscure.
2. **A group chat.** Bluesky added them in 2026, and messages aren't part of the public record the way posts are.
3. **A different app** built for private circles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you make a Bluesky account private?

No. Bluesky has no private or protected mode, because posts are public data on the AT Protocol. You can limit replies, quotes, DMs and logged-out visibility.

### Will Bluesky ever add private accounts?

Bluesky has said private data is hard on an open protocol, and there's no private-account mode at the time of writing. Bookmarks were built off-protocol to stay private, so more private features may follow.

### Does logged-out visibility hide my posts from search engines?

It asks Bluesky's own apps not to show you to logged-out visitors, which reduces exposure, but other apps and the API can still read your posts.

### Can people see my Bluesky posts without an account?

Yes, by default. Anyone can open your profile on bsky.app without signing in unless you switch on logged-out visibility.

### Can I stop someone from following me on Bluesky?

Only by blocking them. There's no follower approval or remove-follower button at the time of writing.

### Are my likes private on Bluesky?

No. Likes are public data. Bookmarks are private, so use them for posts you want to save quietly.

### Does the person know if I mute them?

No. Mutes are private. Blocks are public.

### Can I hide replies on my Bluesky posts?

Yes. Open the reply's menu and choose Hide reply for everyone.
