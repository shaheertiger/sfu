You tapped **Download your information**, submitted the request, and now you're refreshing your email. Instagram doesn't promise a fixed time. In our experience, a small request, such as only your followers and following in JSON, is often ready within minutes to a few hours, while a full archive with years of photos and videos can take a day or more. Instagram tells you when it's ready, by email and in the Download your information screen.

![An Instagram data export can take minutes or days, depending on how much you ask for, the format and how much media your account has](/images/blog/how-long-does-instagram-data-export-take-hero-1200x675.webp)

Those are estimates, not promises. Instagram's own wording on this has changed over the years, and the real wait depends mostly on how much you ask for.

This guide explains what decides the wait, how to request the fastest possible export, what to do if it seems stuck, and why you should download the file as soon as it arrives, because the link doesn't last.

## Why Instagram Doesn't Give a Fixed Export Time (and Why It Matters)

![What makes an Instagram export faster, like choosing only some information, next to what slows it down, like all your photos and videos in high quality](/images/blog/how-long-does-instagram-data-export-take-factors-1200x675.webp)

An export isn't a file Instagram keeps ready for you. When you submit the request, Instagram gathers everything you selected, packages it into one or more zip files and then tells you. That work sits in a queue with everyone else's requests, so the wait depends on two things you control and one you don't:

- **How much you ask for.** A list of usernames is tiny. Years of photos, Reels and messages are not.
- **How much is in your account.** Two people can tick the same boxes and wait very different times if one has posted 30 times and the other 3,000.
- **How busy Meta's systems are.** You can't see or change this, which is why nobody can give you an exact time.

It matters because people usually request an export for a reason with a deadline: checking [who doesn't follow them back](/blog/who-doesnt-follow-me-back-on-instagram), backing up before closing an account, or saving their work before a phone switch.

### What Most People Don't Know

The slow part is almost always media. The followers and following files are plain text, a few kilobytes even for a large account. Ticking "all your information" pulls every photo and video too, and that's what turns a quick request into a long one.

> **Key point:** Ask only for what you need. If you want your followers list, choose **Some of your information** and tick only **Followers and following**. It's the single biggest thing you can do to shorten the wait.

## How to Get What You Need Without an Export (Manual Method, No Wait)

![Three things you can check in the Instagram app without waiting for an export: your lists, one follower, and your own posts](/images/blog/how-long-does-instagram-data-export-take-manual-1200x675.webp)

Before you wait for a file, check whether you need one at all. A lot of what people export for is visible in the app right now. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

1. **Check one follower.** Open their profile and look for the **Follows you** label, or search their username in your own Followers list.
2. **Browse your lists.** Tap **Followers** or **Following** on your profile. Your Following list can usually be sorted by the date you followed people.
3. **Find old posts.** Your profile grid, your Archive and your Saved folder are all in the app.
4. **Check your activity.** Accounts Center and the Your activity screen show things like likes, comments and time spent.

**The upside:** it's instant and free.
**The downside:** it's one item at a time. To compare full lists or keep a backup, you need the export.

## How to Request the Fastest Possible Instagram Export (Step by Step)

These steps keep the request as small as possible. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Open Download Your Information

1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
2. Tap the menu icon in the top corner, then **Accounts Center**.
3. Tap **Your information and permissions**, then **Download your information**.
4. Choose to download or transfer information and select your Instagram account.

### Step 2: Choose "Some of Your Information" (the Step That Saves the Most Time)

Choose **Some of your information**, then tick only what you need. For a follower check that's **Followers and following**, under connections. For a backup of your posts, tick your posts and Reels and leave messages and everything else unticked.

> **Pro tip:** If you need two different things, such as your followers and your posts, two small requests can be easier to handle than one huge one. You get the small one first and aren't stuck waiting for both.

### Step 3: Pick the Date Range, Format and Media Quality

- **Date range:** choose **All time** for a complete follower list. For a backup, a shorter range means a smaller file.
- **Format:** **JSON** is best if a tool will read the file, such as our [unfollowers checker](/tools/instagram-unfollowers-checker). **HTML** is easier to read in a browser. The format changes how the file looks more than how long it takes.
- **Media quality:** if you included photos and videos, lower quality makes the files smaller. Choose high quality only for a real backup.

### Step 4: Submit and Note the Date

Choose to download to your device and submit the request. Make a note of the date, so you know how long it's been if you need to follow up.

### Instagram Export Taking Too Long? 6 Things to Check

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| No email yet | Check the Download your information screen in the app, plus your spam and promotions folders | The status shows in the app even if the email is delayed or filtered |
| The email went to an old address | Check the email address on your account in Accounts Center | Instagram sends the notice to the address on file |
| It's been a long time on a full archive | Cancel it, if the option is there, and request only what you need | A smaller request has less to package |
| You submitted the same request several times | Wait for one to finish before asking again | Duplicates don't jump the queue |
| The status says it's ready but nothing downloads | Try a browser on a computer, signed in to the same account | Large zip files are easier to save on desktop |
| It's been several days on a small request | Request it again with only Followers and following ticked | A fresh, minimal request rules out a stuck job |

### Download It Before the Link Expires

When the export is ready, the download link only works for a limited time, usually a few days at the time of writing. If you miss it, you have to request the export again and wait from the start. Instagram may also ask for your password before the download begins, which is a normal security step on Instagram's own site, not a third party.

Large accounts may get several files. Download all of them, then unzip each one.

## "Instant Export" Apps and Sites: No Wait, Big Risk (Use With Caution)

If you search for a faster way, you'll find apps and sites that promise your followers list, unfollowers or a full backup "instantly". There's no secret fast lane into Instagram's export. Most of these services skip it entirely and log in to your account themselves, which means they need your username and password.

### The Real Risks

- **They get full access to your account**, including posting, messaging and changing settings.
- **They can get you restricted.** Logins from unfamiliar servers and automated scraping can look like a takeover, which can lead to locked logins, action blocks or a disabled account. If that happens, our guide to the [disabled account appeal](/blog/instagram-disabled-account-appeal) can help.
- **They break Instagram's terms** on automated access.
- **Your data may be stored, sold or leaked.**

### If You Still Want to Use One

- Never enter your Instagram password into a third party's own login form.
- Check recent reviews for lockouts and hacked accounts.
- Afterwards, review the apps connected to your account in Instagram's security settings and remove anything you don't recognize.
- If you've already used one, change your password and turn on two-factor authentication.

> **Bottom line:** Waiting a few hours for the official export is cheaper than losing your account to save the wait.

## How to Keep Your Instagram Export Safe

![Checklist of safe habits for an Instagram data export, next to mistakes that put your account or your data at risk](/images/blog/how-long-does-instagram-data-export-take-safety-1200x675.webp)

Your export contains personal information about you and the people you're connected to: usernames, messages if you included them, and more. Treat it like a document with your address on it.

Safe habits:

- ✓ Request only the categories you need
- ✓ Download it as soon as it's ready, on a device you trust
- ✓ Use tools that read files in your browser without uploading them
- ✓ Delete old exports you no longer need

Avoid:

- ✗ Giving your password to an "instant export" app
- ✗ Uploading your full archive to sites you don't know
- ✗ Sharing the download link, or the zip file, with anyone
- ✗ Leaving full archives on a shared computer

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to export Instagram data?

There's no fixed time. A small request, such as followers and following only, is often ready within minutes to a few hours. A full archive with all your media can take a day or more.

### Why is my Instagram export taking so long?

Usually because the request is large, with all your information or high-quality media, or because your account has a lot of content. Requesting only what you need is the fastest fix.

### How do I know when my Instagram data is ready?

Instagram sends an email and shows the status in Accounts Center, under Download your information. Check the app if the email doesn't arrive.

### How long is the Instagram download link valid?

Only for a limited time, usually a few days at the time of writing. If it expires, request the export again.

### Does JSON or HTML make the export faster?

Not noticeably, in our experience. Choose JSON if a tool will read the file and HTML if you want to read it yourself.

### Can I cancel an Instagram export and start again?

Often, yes. If a large request is taking too long, cancel it if the option is shown and submit a smaller one.

### Does downloading my data notify my followers?

No. Requesting or downloading your data is private. Nobody is told.

### Is there a faster way to get my Instagram followers list?

Not safely. Apps that promise it instantly usually need your password. The official export, limited to followers and following, is the safe and usually quick option.
