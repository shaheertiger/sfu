Your post was meant to go out at 9 a.m. It's now lunchtime, the grid hasn't changed, and you're looking at "Couldn't post" or a failed scheduled post.

When Instagram is failing to post, the cause is usually one of a few things: the image or video doesn't meet Instagram's rules, the video hasn't finished processing, the connection between Instagram and the app posting for you has expired, or the account is no longer set up the way Instagram's publishing system expects. Each has a specific fix, and most take only a few minutes.

Instagram rarely tells you which of these it is in plain words, and media that works in the Instagram app can still fail from a scheduling tool, because the app crops and converts for you and the publishing system doesn't.

This guide covers posts that fail in the Instagram app and scheduled posts that fail in a tool like Schedule For You, with a troubleshooting table you can work through from top to bottom.

### What Most People Don't Know

Instagram is stricter about media coming from scheduling tools than about media you edit in its own app. In the app, you're asked to crop an image that's too tall. Through the publishing system that third-party tools use, the same image is simply rejected. That's why a post can fail from a scheduler but work fine when you post it by hand.

> **Key point:** If a scheduled post failed but your account looks fine, check the media first. Shape, missing files and video format cause most failures.

## Media Problems: The Most Common Reason a Scheduled Post Fails

### 1. Image Aspect Ratio Outside 4:5 to 1.91:1

Instagram's feed accepts images between 4:5 portrait and 1.91:1 landscape, at the time of writing. A scheduling tool can't crop on your behalf the way the app does, so a 9:16 phone screenshot or a very wide banner is rejected.

Schedule For You doesn't crop or resize images. It sends your file as it is, which means you keep control of the framing, but you need to export images at a supported size first:

- 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait)
- 1080×1080 (1:1 square)
- 1080×566 (1.91:1 landscape)

### 2. No Media Attached

Instagram doesn't support text-only feed posts. If you wrote one caption for several platforms, the Instagram version fails if there's nothing attached, even though the same post works on X or Threads. Add an image, or remove Instagram from that post.

### 3. Video Processing Time

Videos aren't published instantly. Instagram first uploads and processes the file, and only then publishes it as a Reel. Long or very large files take longer, and files in unusual formats can fail processing altogether.

1. Export as MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, which Instagram handles reliably.
2. Use a vertical 9:16 frame at 1080×1920 for Reels.
3. Keep the length within Instagram's current Reel limit. Check the upload screen in the app if you're unsure, as the limit has changed several times.
4. Avoid enormous files: export at a sensible bitrate rather than straight from a camera master.

> **Pro tip:** If a video post fails once, retry it before changing anything. Processing failures are sometimes temporary, and re-exporting first can waste half an hour.

### 4. Carousel Item Limits

Scheduled carousels can hold 2 to 10 photos or videos, and you can mix the two. The Instagram app has allowed more slides for posts made in the app, but the publishing system that third-party tools use caps carousels at 10. If you have more, split the set into two posts with the [Instagram carousel splitter](/tools/instagram-carousel-splitter).

### 5. Caption Length

Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters. Anything longer is rejected. Schedule For You checks each platform's limit live as you type, so you'll see the Instagram version go over before you schedule it.

## Connection and Account Problems: Session Invalid and More

If your media is fine and posts still fail, the problem is usually the connection between Instagram and the tool, or a change to the account itself.

![How to fix Instagram connection problems: reconnect, keep the account professional and linked to its Facebook Page](/images/blog/instagram-not-posting-connection-1200x675.webp)

### 6. Token Expired or "Session Invalid"

Scheduling tools don't store your Instagram password. When you connect an account, Meta issues an access token that lets the tool publish for you. Meta can cancel that token at any time, most often after a password change, a security check, logging out of all sessions, or removing the app's permissions.

The only fix is to reconnect. Open the Accounts screen in Schedule For You, start the Instagram connection again, log in through Meta's window and approve every permission. Our guide to the [400 session invalid error](/blog/instagram-session-invalid-error) has more detail.

### 7. Account No Longer Business or Creator

Scheduled posting only works with Instagram Business or Creator accounts. If someone switches the account to personal, even briefly, publishing access is removed. Switch back to a professional account in the Instagram app's settings, then reconnect.

### 8. Account Unlinked From Its Facebook Page

Instagram accounts used for scheduling must be linked to a Facebook Page. If the link is removed, or the account is linked to a different Page, existing connections stop working. Relink the account to the right Page (look for "Linked accounts" or "Instagram" in the Page's settings, or use Meta Business Suite), make sure the Facebook login you use has full control of that Page, and then reconnect.

### When the Account Is Restricted

If Instagram has restricted or disabled the account, nothing can post to it until that's resolved through Instagram's own appeal flow. Use the in-app options and the [Instagram Help Center](https://help.instagram.com).

## Instagram Failing to Post in the App Itself? 7 Fixes in Order

![What to do when the Instagram app says Couldn’t post: check your connection, retry once, update and restart](/images/blog/instagram-not-posting-app-1200x675.webp)

If you're posting by hand and the app shows "Couldn't post. Tap to retry" or hangs on "Posting…", try these in order. Menu labels change between app versions.

1. **Check your connection.** Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Large videos often stall on weak connections.
2. **Tap retry once.** Then wait. Retrying repeatedly can create duplicate posts.
3. **Update the app.** Older versions sometimes fail on newer features.
4. **Restart the app and your phone.** This clears stuck uploads more often than you'd expect.
5. **Free up storage.** Instagram needs space on your device to prepare uploads.
6. **Check for an outage.** If many people report problems at once, waiting is the fix.
7. **Look for a restriction notice.** Instagram may limit actions if it suspects automated or unusual activity. The notice explains what's affected.

| | Posting in the app | Posting from a scheduler |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong image shape | You're asked to crop | Rejected, so resize first |
| More than 10 carousel slides | Allowed in the app | Rejected above 10 |
| Password change | No effect | Connection needs renewing |
| Weak connection | Upload stalls | Not a factor: the tool uploads |

## "Recovery Services" for Restricted Accounts (Use With Caution)

If your account is restricted and nothing will post, you'll find people offering to "unlock" it for a fee.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't do it.** Only Instagram can lift its own restrictions, through its appeal flow.
- **They want your login.** Handing over your password can turn a restriction into a lost account.
- **You pay for nothing.** Appeals are free.

> **Bottom line:** Use Instagram's in-app options and Help Center, and never pay a third-party "recovery service".

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is Instagram not posting my scheduled post?

The most common reasons are an image outside Instagram's 4:5 to 1.91:1 range, a post with no image or video, a video that failed processing, or an expired connection. Check the failed-post alert for the error, fix the cause and retry.

### Why does Instagram say my session is invalid?

Meta has cancelled the access token your scheduling tool was using, usually after a password change, a security check or a permission change. Reconnect the account from the Accounts screen to get a new token, then retry failed posts.

### Can I schedule Instagram posts without a Facebook Page?

Not with Schedule For You. Instagram accounts must be Business or Creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page, which is how Meta grants publishing access to scheduling tools.

### Why did my Instagram carousel fail?

Check the number of items and their shapes. Scheduled carousels allow 2 to 10 photos or videos, and every image must sit within Instagram's supported aspect ratio range.

### Will a failed Instagram post publish later on its own?

No. A failed post stays failed until you retry it or reschedule it. Fix the cause first, then retry from the post or choose a new time.

### Why does a post work in the app but fail from a scheduler?

The app crops and adjusts media for you, and allows more carousel slides. The publishing system tools use rejects media outside the rules instead.

### What video format does Instagram handle best?

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, in a 9:16 frame at 1080×1920 for Reels.

### Why is my Instagram post stuck on "Posting…"?

Usually a weak connection or a large video. Switch networks, tap retry once, and restart the app if it's still stuck.
