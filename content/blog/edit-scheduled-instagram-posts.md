You scheduled a post, spotted a typo an hour later, and now you can't find it anywhere on your profile. Scheduled Instagram posts don't appear on your profile until they publish. In the Instagram app they live under **Scheduled content** in your profile menu; in Meta Business Suite they're in **Planner** and **Content → Scheduled**. From either place you can edit the caption, change the time, publish now or delete the post.

![How to edit scheduled Instagram posts: find them in Scheduled content or Planner, then edit, reschedule, share now or delete](/images/blog/edit-scheduled-instagram-posts-hero-1200x675.webp)

What isn't obvious is that the two tools don't do the same things. The app can't swap a photo; Business Suite can. Business Suite can't add Instagram's music; the app can.

This guide covers where scheduled posts hide, how to edit, reschedule or cancel them in each tool, the music question, and what to do when a post disappears.

## Why Scheduled Instagram Posts Seem to Disappear

![Where scheduled Instagram posts live: the app's Scheduled content screen versus Meta Business Suite's Planner](/images/blog/edit-scheduled-instagram-posts-where-1200x675.webp)

A scheduled post is a draft with a timer. It sits outside your grid until the moment it publishes, so it's invisible to you on your own profile and to everyone else.

Where you see it depends on where you scheduled it:

| Where you scheduled | Where to find it | What you can change |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram app | Profile menu → Scheduled content | Caption, time, share now, delete |
| Meta Business Suite | Planner, or Content filtered by Scheduled | Text, media, time, publish now, delete, move to drafts |
| A third-party scheduler | That tool's queue or calendar | Whatever the tool allows, usually everything |

### What Most People Don't Know

If you scheduled in a third-party tool, the post waits in that tool until it publishes. Instagram only receives it at publishing time, so it won't appear in the app's Scheduled content list, and you edit it in the tool, not in Instagram.

> **Key point:** Always edit a scheduled post in the place you scheduled it. That's where it lives until it goes out.

## How to Edit a Scheduled Post in the Instagram App (Step by Step)

![Steps to edit a scheduled Instagram post in the app: open the menu, tap Scheduled content, open the post, then edit or reschedule](/images/blog/edit-scheduled-instagram-posts-app-steps-1200x675.webp)

Scheduling in the app is available to professional (Business or Creator) accounts. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Open Your Profile Menu

Go to your profile and tap the menu (three lines) in the top corner.

### Step 2: Tap Scheduled Content

Tap **Scheduled content**. In some versions it sits inside the professional dashboard instead.

### Step 3: Open the Post

Your scheduled posts and Reels are listed with their dates. Tap the one you want.

### Step 4: Choose Reschedule, Share Now or Delete

- **Reschedule** changes the date and time.
- **Share now** publishes it immediately.
- **Delete** removes it.

To change the caption, open the post and edit the text.

### Step 5: Swapping a Photo? Delete and Recreate (the Step People Get Wrong)

The app can't swap the media in a scheduled post. To change a photo or video, copy the caption, delete the scheduled post, and create it again with the new media.

> **Pro tip:** Before you delete, long-press the caption and copy it into your notes. The app has no "move to drafts" option for scheduled posts, so a deleted caption is gone.

## How to Edit Scheduled Posts in Meta Business Suite (Media Too)

![Steps to edit a scheduled Instagram post in Meta Business Suite: open Planner or Content, click the post, then edit text, media or time](/images/blog/edit-scheduled-instagram-posts-business-suite-1200x675.webp)

Business Suite is the more flexible of Meta's two tools.

1. Go to business.facebook.com and pick the account.
2. Open **Planner** for a calendar view, or **Content** and filter by **Scheduled**.
3. Click a post to edit text or media, change the date, publish now or delete.

Business Suite is also the only Meta tool that lists scheduled Stories.

### How to Cancel a Scheduled Instagram Post

Delete it from Scheduled content in the app or from Business Suite. If you only want to hold it, Business Suite can move it to drafts; the app can't, so copy the caption first.

### Instagram App vs Business Suite: What Each Can Change

| Change | Instagram app | Meta Business Suite |
|---|---|---|
| Edit caption | Yes | Yes |
| Change the time | Yes | Yes |
| Swap photo or video | No, delete and recreate | Yes |
| Move to drafts | No | Yes |
| Add Instagram music | Yes, before scheduling | No, at the time of writing |
| See scheduled Stories | No | Yes |

## Can You Add Music to a Scheduled Instagram Post? (Depends Where)

![Where you can add music to a scheduled Instagram post: in the app yes, in Business Suite and third-party schedulers no](/images/blog/edit-scheduled-instagram-posts-music-1200x675.webp)

It depends on where you schedule:

- **Instagram app:** when you schedule a Reel in the app, you add music in the editor before scheduling, the same as a live Reel. Feed photos and carousels can get music the same way.
- **Meta Business Suite:** at the time of writing, Business Suite doesn't add Instagram's licensed music to scheduled posts. Add a licensed track to the video in an editor first, or schedule in the app.
- **Third-party schedulers:** Instagram's API doesn't expose the music library, so no scheduler can attach a track from it. Upload a video that already has its audio.

Instagram's licensing rules for business accounts are stricter than for personal ones, so a track available on a personal account may not appear for yours.

## Baking a Song Into Your Video Before Uploading (Use With Caution)

![Safe audio choices for scheduled Instagram videos compared with risky ones like popular songs you don't have a licence for](/images/blog/edit-scheduled-instagram-posts-audio-risk-1200x675.webp)

Since schedulers can't attach Instagram's music, the obvious workaround is to add a popular song to the video in an editor and upload that. It works technically. Whether it's allowed is another matter.

### The Real Risks

- **The audio can be muted or the video blocked.** Instagram matches copyrighted music, and a track you don't have rights to can get your post muted or taken down.
- **Business accounts face stricter rules.** Music in commercial posts generally needs a licence that a personal download doesn't give you.
- **Repeat issues can limit your account.** Instagram can restrict accounts with repeated copyright problems.

### A Safer Checklist

- Use royalty-free or properly licensed tracks, or your own original audio.
- Keep the licence or receipt for any track you pay for.
- If you want a trending song, schedule that Reel in the Instagram app and add it from the music library.

> **Bottom line:** Schedule Reels with your own or licensed audio from any tool, and save trending songs for posts you schedule in the app.

## What to Do Next: 4 Common Situations

- **Typo in a caption:** edit it wherever you scheduled the post. No need to delete.
- **Wrong photo:** in Business Suite or a scheduler, swap it. In the app, copy the caption, delete and recreate.
- **Need a trending song:** schedule that Reel in the Instagram app and add music before scheduling.
- **Post vanished:** check your grid first, then whether your account switched away from a professional account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I see my scheduled posts on Instagram?

Open your profile menu and tap **Scheduled content**. If you scheduled in Meta Business Suite, look under **Planner** or **Content → Scheduled** there instead.

### Can I edit the caption of a scheduled Instagram post?

Yes, in both the app and Business Suite. Media can only be changed in Business Suite; in the app you delete and recreate the post.

### Can you schedule posts with music on Instagram?

Yes, in the Instagram app, by adding the track before scheduling. Business Suite and third-party tools can't attach Instagram's music library, so use a video that already has its audio.

### Why did my scheduled post disappear?

It either published (check your profile) or was deleted. Instagram also drops scheduled posts if the account type changes from professional back to personal.

### Can I move a scheduled Instagram post back to drafts?

In Meta Business Suite, yes. The Instagram app doesn't offer it, so copy the caption before you delete.

### Can I edit an Instagram post after it's published?

You can edit the caption of a published post, but you generally can't swap the photo or video. To change media, delete and repost.

### Why can't I see Scheduled content in my menu?

Scheduling in the app is for professional accounts. If you're on a personal account, switch to Creator or Business first, and check inside the professional dashboard in case your version moved it.

### Can I schedule Instagram Stories?

In Meta Business Suite, yes. The Instagram app's Scheduled content list covers posts and Reels, and Schedule For You doesn't schedule Stories.
