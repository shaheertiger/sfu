You've edited photos on your laptop, and the last thing you want is to send them to your phone just to post them.

Yes, you can post on Instagram from desktop. Instagram's website has supported creating posts and Reels from a browser for several years, Meta Business Suite adds scheduling and Stories for professional accounts, and a scheduler lets you post from your computer to Instagram and your other accounts in one go. Which one to use depends on whether you want to publish now, schedule, or post to several platforms.

![How to post on Instagram from desktop: instagram.com, Meta Business Suite or a scheduler](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-instagram-from-desktop-hero-1200x675.webp)

Many people still think Instagram is phone-only, because for years it was, and the website still lacks a few app features. This guide covers what each desktop option can and can't do, step-by-step instructions for all three, how to get image sizes right, and which option fits which situation.

## Why Instagram Feels Phone-Only (and What Desktop Can Do Now)

Instagram started as a mobile app and kept posting in the app for a long time. That's changed: the website now handles the post types most people need.

![What you can post on Instagram from desktop with each of the three options](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-instagram-from-desktop-options-1200x675.webp)

| | instagram.com | Meta Business Suite | A scheduler |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photos and carousels | Yes | Yes | Usually |
| Reels | Yes | Yes | Usually |
| Stories | No, at the time of writing | Yes | Varies by tool |
| Schedule for later | No | Yes | Yes |
| Other platforms | No | Facebook | Several |
| Account type | Any | Professional, linked to a Facebook Page | Usually professional |

### What Most People Don't Know

Instagram doesn't rank posts by the device they came from. A photo posted from your laptop gets the same treatment as one posted from your phone. Image quality matters more: upload at 1080 pixels wide so Instagram doesn't compress a larger file more than it needs to.

> **Key point:** Posting from desktop doesn't cost you reach. Getting the size and crop right matters far more than which device you use.

## Option 1: How to Post From instagram.com in a Browser (Free Method)

![Six steps to post on Instagram from a browser on instagram.com](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-instagram-from-desktop-browser-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Sign In and Click Create

Go to instagram.com and sign in. Click **Create** (the plus icon) in the left menu.

### Step 2: Add Your Photo or Video

Drag in a photo or video, or click **Select from computer**. Several photos become a carousel; a video becomes a Reel.

### Step 3: Choose the Crop, Don't Skip It

Pick the crop (original, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9), then click **Next**. This is the step people rush, and then the top of someone's head is cut off in the feed.

### Step 4: Edit, Then Write the Caption

Apply filters or adjustments if you want, then **Next**. Write the caption, add a location, alt text and collaborators.

### Step 5: Share

Click **Share**. Menu labels change between versions, so look for similar wording if yours differs.

> **Pro tip:** Size your images before you upload. The free [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) makes 1080×1350, 1080×1080 and 1080×566 versions in your browser, so the crop step becomes a check rather than a fix.

What you can't do from the website at the time of writing is add music the way the app does, or post a Story, though Instagram has tested Story creation on desktop.

**Best for:** publishing a single post right now, especially photos you edited on your computer.

## Option 2: How to Post and Schedule With Meta Business Suite

If your account is a professional (Business or Creator) account connected to a Facebook Page, Meta Business Suite at business.facebook.com can post and schedule to Instagram from desktop, including Stories.

1. Open Business Suite and click **Create post** or **Create story**.
2. Choose Instagram (and the Facebook Page too if you want both).
3. Add media and the caption, and tailor the text per platform.
4. Click **Publish**, or open the arrow and choose **Schedule**.

**Best for:** brands that post to Instagram and Facebook only, and anyone who needs Stories from desktop.

## Edge Cases: Personal Accounts, Music and Multiple Accounts

- **Personal accounts.** instagram.com works for any account. Business Suite and most schedulers need a professional account.
- **Music.** Adding music the way the app does isn't available on the website at the time of writing. Post Reels that need a trending sound from the app.
- **Several accounts.** You can switch accounts on instagram.com, but posting to several at once needs a scheduler. See [how to connect multiple Instagram accounts](/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts).

## Browser Extensions and "Mobile View" Tricks (Use With Caution)

Before Instagram's website could post, people used browser extensions or switched their browser to mobile view to unlock the app's upload button. Some still recommend it, along with third-party "post to Instagram" apps.

![Official ways to post on Instagram from desktop compared with risky extensions and apps](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-instagram-from-desktop-caution-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Password sharing.** Apps that ask for your Instagram password, rather than using Meta's login screen, put your account at risk and can trigger security checks.
- **Extensions can read your pages.** An extension with access to instagram.com can see what you see.
- **You don't need them any more.** The website does what most of these tricks were for.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Post from instagram.com, Business Suite or a scheduler that connects through Meta's own login.
- Never type your Instagram password into a third-party site.
- Remove extensions you no longer use.

> **Bottom line:** The official website already posts photos, carousels and Reels. Workarounds add risk and little else.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you post on Instagram from a desktop without the app?

Yes. The website handles photos, carousels and Reels in any modern browser, and no app is needed.

### Can you post an Instagram Story from desktop?

Not from instagram.com at the time of writing. Meta Business Suite can post and schedule Stories for professional accounts, and Schedule For You can schedule them too (one 9:16 image or video, without a caption or stickers).

### Does posting from desktop affect reach?

No. Instagram doesn't rank posts by the device they came from. Image quality matters more, so upload at 1080 pixels wide.

### Can I post to multiple Instagram accounts from desktop?

Yes, with a scheduler. Schedule For You can connect several Instagram accounts and post to them from one composer. See [how to connect multiple Instagram accounts](/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts).

### Can I post Reels from my computer?

Yes. Upload a video on instagram.com and it becomes a Reel. Business Suite and schedulers can post Reels too.

### Can I schedule Instagram posts from desktop for free?

Yes. Meta Business Suite is free for professional accounts. Schedule For You's Free plan covers 3 accounts and up to 10 scheduled posts at a time.

### Why can't I see the Create button on instagram.com?

Update your browser or sign out and back in. Features sometimes reach accounts at different times.

### Do I need a Business account to post from desktop?

Not for instagram.com. You do for Meta Business Suite and for schedulers that use Instagram's API.
