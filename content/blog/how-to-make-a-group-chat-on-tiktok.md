You keep sending the same TikToks to three friends one at a time, and you'd rather drop them into one chat where everyone can react.

To make a group chat on TikTok, open **Inbox**, tap the **new message** icon (a pencil or plus in the top corner), select **two or more people**, then tap **Chat** or **Create group chat**. TikTok group chats hold up to **32 people**, and you need to be **16 or older** to use direct messages at all.

![How to make a group chat on TikTok: Inbox, new message, pick two or more friends, then Chat](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-group-chat-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

The option isn't always where people expect it. You'll usually only see friends in the list, teens have extra limits, and group chats haven't reached every region. You can also start a group from a video: tap **Share**, then **Create group**, and the video becomes the first message.

This guide covers the rules, the steps, adding and removing people, sending pictures, deleted messages, and the privacy settings that stop strangers adding you.

## TikTok Group Chat Rules: 32 Members, Age 16+

| What | At the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum members | 32 |
| Minimum age for DMs and group chats | 16 |
| Who you can add | Usually friends (people who follow each other) |
| Teens aged 16–17 | Can only be added by mutual follows. New joiners need approval by default |
| Photos and videos | Up to 9 at once in a chat |
| Voice messages | Up to 60 seconds |

![The rules for TikTok group chats: up to 32 members, age 16 or older, usually friends only](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-group-chat-on-tiktok-rules-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Group chats aren't available in every region yet. If there's no option to create a group, update the app. If it's still missing, it hasn't reached your account, and no setting will switch it on.

The other common surprise is the list of people you can pick. It's usually limited to friends (people who follow each other) and people you already message, so someone who follows you but whom you don't follow back may not appear.

> **Key point:** If someone is missing from the list, follow each other first. Most "I can't add them" problems are a mutual-follow problem, not a bug.

## How to Create a Group Chat on TikTok in 5 Taps

Menu labels change between app versions, so yours may differ slightly.

![Five taps to create a TikTok group chat from your Inbox](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-group-chat-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Inbox

Tap **Inbox** at the bottom of the screen.

### Step 2: Tap the New Message Icon

It's at the top of the screen, usually a pencil or plus.

### Step 3: Tick Two or More People

Tick the people you want to add. This is the step people get wrong: picking one person starts a normal one-to-one chat, not a group.

### Step 4: Tap Chat

Tap **Chat** (or **Create group chat**).

### Step 5: Name the Group (Optional)

Open the chat, tap the **three dots** or the group name, and set a name and photo.

> **Pro tip:** Name the group straight away. A chat called "Ana, Ben, Cara and 2 others" is hard to find in a busy inbox a week later.

### Start a Group From a Video Instead

Tap **Share** on any video, then **Create group**, pick the people, and the video becomes the first message.

## How to Add, Remove or Invite People

- **Add members.** Open the group, tap the **three dots** or group name, and choose **Add members**.
- **Remove someone.** In the same menu, tap a member and choose **Remove**. The person who created the group manages it.
- **Leave.** Anyone can leave at any time with **Leave group**. The chat usually shows a note that you left.
- **Invite with a link.** Some versions let you share an invite link to the group. Teens can only join through a link if the group already includes someone they follow mutually.

| Action | Who can do it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Create a group | Anyone 16+ where available | Inbox → new message |
| Add members | The group's creator, who manages the group | Group menu → Add members |
| Remove members | The group's creator | Group menu → tap a member |
| Leave | Anyone | Group menu → Leave group |
| Share an invite link | Some app versions | Group menu |

## How to Send Pictures and Videos in TikTok DMs

Since 2025 you can send photos and videos in one-to-one and group chats.

![How to send pictures and videos in a TikTok chat](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-group-chat-on-tiktok-photos-1200x675.webp)

1. Open the chat.
2. Tap the **image** or **camera** icon next to the message box.
3. Pick up to nine photos or videos from your camera roll, or take one with the camera. You can edit before sending.
4. Tap **Send**.

You can't send a photo or video as the first message to someone who hasn't accepted your message request. Sharing a TikTok video is different: tap **Share** on the video and pick the person or group.

## Can You See Deleted Messages on TikTok? (No)

There's no way to recover a message once it's deleted, either yours or someone else's. What deleting does depends on how fast you act:

| Option | What it does | Time limit |
|---|---|---|
| Delete for everyone | Removes the message from both sides | Only a short window after sending |
| Delete for me | Removes it from your inbox only | Any time, but the other person still sees it |

So treat anything you send as permanent once that window has passed.

## Privacy Controls That Stop Strangers Adding You

In **Settings and privacy → Privacy → Direct messages** you can choose who can message you and who can add you to groups. If someone keeps adding you to a group you don't want, leave it and change that setting. You can also turn off read status here. See [what nudge means on TikTok](/blog/what-does-nudge-mean-on-tiktok) for read receipts and other chat features.

## "Deleted Message Recovery" Apps (Use With Caution)

Search for a way to read deleted TikTok messages and you'll find apps and sites that promise to recover them.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't do it.** TikTok doesn't support recovering deleted messages, so these tools have nothing to recover from.
- **They want your login.** Giving a third party your TikTok password puts your account and your chats at risk.
- **Data and ads.** Many collect your details or push you through surveys and ads.

> **Bottom line:** Deleted means deleted. Avoid any app that asks for your TikTok login to "recover" messages.
