You've written a Facebook post, but you want it to go out tomorrow morning, not now.

To schedule a post on Facebook, write the post in Meta Business Suite, choose **Schedule** instead of **Publish**, and pick a date and time. This works for Facebook Pages. Personal profiles can't schedule posts at the time of writing, and neither can Groups unless an admin uses Business Suite for a Page that posts there.

![How to schedule a post on Facebook: write it in Meta Business Suite, choose Schedule, then pick a time](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-a-post-on-facebook-hero-1200x675.webp)

It trips people up because the Schedule option is hidden behind a small arrow next to Publish, and because scheduled posts don't appear on your Page until they go out. This guide covers who can schedule, the Business Suite steps, where scheduled posts live, how to edit or cancel one, and how a scheduler handles Facebook alongside your other platforms.

## Why You Can Only Schedule Posts on Facebook Pages

Facebook built scheduling for businesses and creators, so it lives in the tools for Pages. A personal profile is for posting as yourself, and it has no scheduling option at the time of writing.

![Facebook scheduling works for Pages, not personal profiles or most Groups](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-a-post-on-facebook-who-1200x675.webp)

| Where you post | Can you schedule? | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook Page | Yes | Meta Business Suite, or a scheduler using Meta's API |
| Personal profile | No, at the time of writing | Post live, or set a reminder |
| Group | Only in limited cases | An admin using Business Suite for a Page that posts there |
| Instagram (connected to the Page) | Yes | Business Suite, in the same post |

### What Most People Don't Know

Scheduled posts don't show on the Page until they publish. People schedule a post, look at their Page, don't see it, and schedule it again, and then it goes out twice. Scheduled posts live in Business Suite, not on the Page.

> **Key point:** If you want to schedule as yourself, you need a Page. Profiles post live only.

## How to Schedule a Facebook Post in Meta Business Suite (6 Steps)

Meta Business Suite is Meta's free tool for Pages, at business.facebook.com and in its mobile app. Menu names shift between versions, but the flow stays the same.

![Six steps to schedule a Facebook post in Meta Business Suite](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-a-post-on-facebook-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Pick the Right Page

Open Business Suite and pick the Page in the top-left selector. If you manage more than one, this is the step that sends a post to the wrong Page.

### Step 2: Click Create Post

Click **Create post**.

### Step 3: Choose Where It Goes

Choose the Page only, or the Page and a connected Instagram account.

### Step 4: Add Text and Media

Add text, photos, a video or a link. Business Suite builds the link preview automatically.

### Step 5: Open the Arrow Next to Publish, Don't Click Publish

Next to **Publish**, open the small arrow and choose **Schedule**. Clicking Publish itself posts straight away.

### Step 6: Set the Date and Time

Set the date and time, then confirm **Schedule post**.

> **Pro tip:** Business Suite suggests times when it has enough data on your audience. Try its suggestion for a few weeks before you settle on your own slot.

Business Suite lets you schedule up to about 29 days ahead for most post types. Reels and Stories can be scheduled too, though the time window and options differ from feed posts.

## Where Do Scheduled Facebook Posts Go?

Scheduled posts sit in Business Suite under **Planner** (a calendar view) and under **Content → Scheduled**. From either place you can open a post to edit the text or media, move it to another time, publish it now, or delete it.

If you can't find a post you scheduled, check that you're looking at the right Page, and that the post wasn't created in the older Creator Studio or in a third-party tool, which keep their own queues.

## How to Edit, Reschedule or Cancel a Scheduled Post

| You want to | How | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edit | Open the post from Planner or Content, change it and save | Media changes may need a re-upload |
| Reschedule | Drag it to another day in Planner, or change the date in the editor | Check the time zone |
| Cancel | Delete the post | It's gone; copy the text first |
| Keep it for later | Change it to a draft | It won't publish until you schedule it again |
| Change a published post | Edit or delete it like any other post | Published posts can't be rescheduled |

## Tips That Make Scheduled Facebook Posts Perform Better

1. **Schedule for when your audience is online.** Page Insights shows this under **Audience**, and our guide to the [best time to post on Facebook](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-facebook) is a starting point.
2. **Keep link posts and photo posts separate.** A photo post with a link in the text gets the photo, not a preview card. See [how to add links to a Facebook post](/blog/add-links-to-facebook-post).
3. **Write the first line for the feed.** Facebook truncates long text behind "See more".
4. **Leave a gap** between a scheduled post and anything you post live, so they don't bury each other.

## Tools That Post to Profiles or Groups (Use With Caution)

Because profiles can't schedule, some browser extensions and apps promise to post to your personal profile or to Groups automatically.

![Official ways to schedule Facebook posts compared with risky tools that automate profiles and Groups](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-a-post-on-facebook-caution-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **They work by controlling your logged-in account,** often with your password or browser session, rather than through Meta's official API for Pages.
- **Automated posting from a profile can look like spam** and may get your account restricted. See [Facebook shadowban](/blog/facebook-shadowban) for how limits work.
- **Group admins may remove automated posts** or the members who make them.

### A Safer Checklist

- Schedule from a Page with Business Suite or a tool that uses Meta's own login.
- For profiles, write the post ahead of time and set a reminder.
- Never give an app your Facebook password.

> **Bottom line:** If it promises scheduling for personal profiles, it's working around Facebook, not with it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you schedule a post on a personal Facebook profile?

No. Scheduling is available for Facebook Pages through Meta Business Suite and tools that use Meta's API. A personal profile has no scheduling option at the time of writing.

### How far ahead can you schedule a Facebook post?

Meta Business Suite allows scheduling up to about 29 days ahead for feed posts. Third-party schedulers set their own limits; Schedule For You lets you schedule months ahead.

### Why can't I see my scheduled post on Facebook?

Scheduled posts don't appear on the Page until they publish. Open Meta Business Suite and look under **Planner** or **Content → Scheduled**. If it isn't there, it may have been scheduled from another tool or for a different Page.

### Does scheduling a Facebook post reduce reach?

No. Facebook treats a scheduled post like one published by hand at the same moment. Reach depends on the content and when it goes out. See [do scheduled posts get less views](/blog/do-scheduled-posts-get-less-views).

### Can I schedule a post in a Facebook Group?

Only in limited cases, when an admin uses Business Suite for a Page that posts there. Most members can't schedule Group posts.

### Can I schedule Facebook Reels?

Yes, in Business Suite. The time window and options differ from feed posts.

### How do I cancel a scheduled Facebook post?

Open it from Planner or Content → Scheduled in Business Suite and delete it, or change it to a draft to keep the text.

### Can I schedule Facebook posts from my phone?

Yes, in the Meta Business Suite mobile app, for Pages.
