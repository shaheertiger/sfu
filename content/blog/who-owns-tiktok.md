You've heard TikTok was sold, then heard it wasn't, and now you're not sure who's behind the app on your phone. TikTok is owned by **ByteDance**, the Beijing-founded tech company, everywhere except the United States. In the US, TikTok has been run since **22 January 2026** by **TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC**, whose managing investors are **Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX** (15% each). ByteDance keeps a **19.9%** stake in the US business.

![Who owns TikTok in 2026: ByteDance worldwide, and in the US a joint venture led by Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX with ByteDance at 19.9%](/images/blog/who-owns-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

So the honest answer depends on where you are. The rest of the US venture is held by other investors, many of them existing ByteDance shareholders, which is why "TikTok was sold to America" and "ByteDance still owns TikTok" are both partly true.

This guide covers ownership at a glance, the US joint venture's stakes, who bought what, who owns ByteDance, where TikTok is based and what changed for users. These details come from the deal announcement and reporting at the time of writing; ownership has been in the news for years, so check recent coverage if you need the latest.

## Why TikTok's Ownership Is So Confusing

![TikTok ownership by region: ByteDance owns TikTok outside the US, Douyin, CapCut and Lemon8, while a US joint venture runs TikTok in America](/images/blog/who-owns-tiktok-split-1200x675.webp)

The confusion comes from a 2024 US law that required ByteDance to sell TikTok's American business or face a ban. Rather than a single buyer taking over TikTok, the US business was split off into a new company. Everywhere else, nothing changed.

| | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok worldwide (outside the US) | ByteDance |
| TikTok in the US | TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC |
| Douyin (China's version of TikTok) | ByteDance |
| CapCut, Lemon8 | ByteDance |

### What Most People Don't Know

Reporting describes the US venture as roughly 80% owned by non-Chinese investors, with a board where Americans hold most of the seats. But ByteDance didn't leave: it kept 19.9%, and some of the other investors are affiliates of existing ByteDance investors.

> **Key point:** "Who owns TikTok?" has two answers in 2026: ByteDance for the world, and a US joint venture, with ByteDance as a minority shareholder, for America.

## Who Owns TikTok in the US? The Joint Venture's Stakes

![Ownership stakes in TikTok's US joint venture: Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX at 15% each, ByteDance at 19.9% and other investors at about 35%](/images/blog/who-owns-tiktok-us-stakes-1200x675.webp)

Its ownership, as announced:

| Investor | Stake |
| --- | --- |
| Oracle | 15% |
| Silver Lake | 15% |
| MGX (Abu Dhabi) | 15% |
| ByteDance | 19.9% |
| Other investors, including affiliates of existing ByteDance investors | About 35% |

Other named investors include the Dell family office, Alpha Wave Partners, an affiliate of General Atlantic and Xavier Niel's NJJ Capital. **Adam Presser** is the joint venture's CEO.

### What Does Oracle Do?

Oracle is the security partner: US user data is stored in Oracle's cloud, and the recommendation algorithm is being retrained on US data and overseen by the joint venture. Oracle's own filings value its stake at a little over $2 billion, and reports put the US business at around $14 billion.

## Who Bought TikTok? (No Single Buyer)

No single company bought TikTok. The US business was effectively sold to the joint venture's investor group, led by Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX, in a deal approved by the US government in 2025 and closed in January 2026. ByteDance still owns TikTok in every other country.

## Who Owns ByteDance?

ByteDance was founded in 2012 by **Zhang Yiming**, who still holds a large stake. Most of the company is owned by global investors, including US firms, plus employees. It's a private company, so it isn't listed on a stock exchange, and you can't buy TikTok or ByteDance shares directly.

## Where Is TikTok's Headquarters? (Not One Place)

TikTok doesn't have one single headquarters. Its main offices are in **Los Angeles** (Culver City) and **Singapore**, with large teams in London, Dublin and New York. ByteDance is headquartered in **Beijing**, with major offices around the world. The US joint venture runs American operations from the US.

| Company | Base |
|---|---|
| TikTok (global) | Los Angeles (Culver City) and Singapore |
| ByteDance | Beijing |
| TikTok USDS Joint Venture | United States |

## What Changed for TikTok Users and Creators?

![What changed for US TikTok users after the joint venture compared with what stayed the same](/images/blog/who-owns-tiktok-what-changed-1200x675.webp)

For most people in the US, not much you'd notice day to day. The app, your account, followers and videos stayed the same. The changes are behind the scenes: where data is stored, who audits the algorithm and who sits on the board.

A legal challenge to the government's approval of the deal was filed in March 2026, seeking a renegotiated deal rather than a ban, and was still pending at the time of writing. No ban is in effect in the US at the time of writing; see [is TikTok getting banned](/blog/is-tiktok-getting-banned) for the full story.

## "TikTok Stock" and Pre-IPO Share Offers (Use With Caution)

![Legitimate ways to invest near TikTok compared with risky pre-IPO TikTok share offers](/images/blog/who-owns-tiktok-stock-offers-1200x675.webp)

Every ownership headline brings ads and messages offering "TikTok shares", "ByteDance pre-IPO stock" or tokens tied to TikTok.

### The Real Risks

- **There's no public TikTok stock.** Neither TikTok, ByteDance nor the US joint venture is publicly traded.
- **Unregulated offers are hard to check.** Private shares sold through social media ads or DMs may not exist, or may not be what they claim.
- **Crypto tokens named after TikTok** have nothing to do with the company.

### A Safer Checklist

- If you want exposure, Oracle is publicly traded and owns part of TikTok's US business.
- Ignore unsolicited offers of private TikTok or ByteDance shares.
- Check that anyone selling investments is licensed with your country's financial regulator.

> **Bottom line:** You can't buy TikTok stock. Any offer that says you can is the thing to be careful of, not the opportunity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is TikTok owned by China?

TikTok outside the US is owned by ByteDance, a company founded in China. TikTok in the US is owned by a joint venture in which ByteDance holds less than 20% and the managing investors are Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX.

### Does Elon Musk or Larry Ellison own TikTok?

Elon Musk doesn't. Larry Ellison co-founded Oracle, which owns 15% of the US joint venture, so he's linked to it through Oracle, but he doesn't own TikTok personally.

### Who is the CEO of TikTok?

Shou Zi Chew is CEO of TikTok globally. Adam Presser is CEO of the US joint venture.

### Can I buy TikTok stock?

No. Neither TikTok, ByteDance nor the US joint venture is publicly traded. You can buy shares in Oracle, which owns part of TikTok's US business.

### Is TikTok still getting banned?

No ban is in effect in the US at the time of writing. See [is TikTok getting banned](/blog/is-tiktok-getting-banned) for the full story.

### When did the US joint venture take over TikTok?

On 22 January 2026, after the US government approved the deal in 2025.

### Who founded ByteDance?

Zhang Yiming, in 2012. He still holds a large stake.

### How much is TikTok's US business worth?

Reports put it at around $14 billion, and Oracle's filings value its 15% stake at a little over $2 billion.
