To cross post to multiple platforms, write one core message, adapt the caption to each platform's character limit and style, export your media in the sizes each feed needs, and publish from a scheduler that sends a separate version to every account. The aim is one idea, posted everywhere, that still looks native on each platform rather than copied and pasted.

If you're working out how to cross post to multiple platforms for the first time, this guide covers the limits you're writing for, the media formats to prepare, how to time posts, and a step-by-step workflow.

## Platform limits for cross-posting at a glance

These are the limits Schedule For You checks when you publish, which match the platforms' own limits at the time of writing.

| Platform | Text limit | Media per post | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | 280 characters | Up to 4 images, a GIF or a video | Links count as 23 characters; most emoji count as 2 |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Up to 4 images or a video | Connects with an app password |
| Threads | 500 characters | Text only, one image or video, or a carousel of up to 10 | |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | One image, one video (posted as a Reel), or a 2–10 item carousel | Media required; Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page |
| Facebook Pages | 63,206 characters | Text, up to 10 images, or a video | Pages only, not personal profiles or Groups |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | Up to 9 images or a video | |
| TikTok | 4,000 characters | A video, or a photo post of up to 35 images | Choose privacy per post |

| Pinterest | Pin description, optional title and link | An image is required | Choose a board |

The tightest limits (X, Bluesky and Threads) shape your short version. If you're unsure how X will count a draft, paste it into our [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter), which applies X's own weighting.

## Adapting captions when you post to multiple platforms at once

Cross-posting fails when the same block of text lands everywhere. Plan three lengths of the same message.

### Short version: X, Bluesky and Threads

One clear point, plain language, and a link if it matters. Cut hashtags to one or two, or none.

### Medium version: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Google Business Profile

Lead with a strong first line, since Instagram and TikTok truncate captions behind a "more" link. On Instagram, links in captions aren't clickable, so point to your bio instead. On Google Business Profile, put the action in the optional button rather than in the text.

### Long version: LinkedIn and YouTube

LinkedIn rewards a clear takeaway in short paragraphs. On YouTube, the first line becomes your title (up to 100 characters) and the rest is the description, so write a searchable title and a useful description with links. Our guide to [customizing captions for each platform](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) has examples of one message rewritten for each network.

### Details that give away a copy-paste post

- **@mentions.** Handles differ between platforms. A tag that works on Instagram may point to a stranger on X.
- **Hashtags.** A block of 20 hashtags fits Instagram's norms but looks out of place on LinkedIn or Threads.
- **Platform words.** "Link in bio," "swipe," "retweet" and "subscribe" only make sense in one place.
- **Watermarks.** Upload your original video file to every platform instead of saving and re-uploading a video exported from another app.

## Preparing media once for every platform

Export each image or video in a few standard sizes and reuse them across platforms.

| Format | Size | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vertical video, 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube (YouTube decides whether a vertical video is a Short) |
| Portrait image, 4:5 | 1080×1350 | Instagram and Facebook feeds, LinkedIn |
| Square image, 1:1 | 1080×1080 | Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Bluesky |
| Landscape image, 1.91:1 | 1200×627 or 1080×566 | LinkedIn landscape image posts, Instagram landscape posts |
| Pin, 2:3 | 1000×1500 | Pinterest |

Instagram accepts feed images between 4:5 portrait and 1.91:1 landscape at the time of writing, and Schedule For You doesn't crop or resize images for you. Prepare images that fit first; our [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) crops or pads an image to these sizes in your browser.

## Timing your cross-posts

Posting everywhere at the same minute is simplest, but not always best.

- **Stagger when audiences differ.** Your LinkedIn audience may be most active on weekday mornings, while Instagram and TikTok viewers may peak in the evening.
- **Account for time zones** if your followers are spread across regions.
- **Keep announcements simultaneous.** For a launch or time-sensitive news, publishing everywhere at once keeps the message consistent.
- **Test and adjust.** Our guide to the [best days to post on social media](/blog/best-days-to-post-on-social-media) explains how to run a simple test.

## Is cross-posting bad?

Cross-posting isn't bad in itself. It becomes a problem when posts look out of place: wrong length, broken mentions, mismatched hashtags or another app's watermark. Platforms also discourage spammy duplication. X, for example, restricts posting identical content across multiple accounts. Adapting each version keeps your posts native and keeps you within the rules.

## How to cross post to multiple platforms with Schedule For You

Here's the workflow from start to finish. You can see an overview on the [cross-posting](/cross-posting) page.

1. **Connect your accounts.** Add each platform from the Accounts screen. You can connect several accounts on the same platform, such as two Instagram accounts, and keep different brands in separate workspaces.
2. **Create a post and choose its targets.** Select every account the post should go to.
3. **Write the master caption.** This is the version every platform starts with.
4. **Edit each platform's version.** Switch to a platform's version and adjust its caption without changing the others. Live character-limit checks show when a version is over the limit.
5. **Add media and platform settings.** Attach your images or video, then fill in what each platform needs: a title for YouTube and its privacy setting, a privacy setting for TikTok, a board for Pinterest, and an optional button link for Google Business Profile.
6. **Schedule it.** Drop the post into your next queue slot, pick a time on the drag-and-drop calendar, or publish now.
7. **Check the results.** If any target fails, for example because a platform revoked access after a password change, you'll get an alert and can reconnect or retry that target without reposting to the others.

### What this workflow doesn't cover

Each post goes out as a single post, so multi-post threads on X or Threads aren't supported. Instagram and Facebook Stories, first comments, and LinkedIn company pages aren't supported either. Plan those separately in each platform's own app.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I post on all social media at once?

Use a scheduler that publishes to every platform you use from one post. Write a master caption, adjust each platform's version to fit its limits, attach media in the right sizes, and schedule once. Native tools can only post to their own platform.

### Is cross-posting bad for reach?

Not if each version fits its platform. Posts that look copied, with broken mentions, the wrong length or another app's watermark, tend to perform worse. Tailor the caption and upload original media to each platform.

### Can I cross post from Instagram to TikTok?

Yes, if you upload the original video file to both rather than downloading your Reel and re-uploading it. That avoids watermarks and quality loss. Adjust the caption too, since the platforms have different norms and limits.

### What's the best cross-posting tool?

Look for a tool that covers all your platforms, lets you edit each platform's caption separately, checks character limits live, and alerts you when a post fails. Compare how many connected accounts each plan includes.

### Can I cross-post to multiple accounts on the same platform?

Yes. In Schedule For You you can connect several accounts on one platform and send one post to all of them. On X, avoid posting identical text to several of your own accounts, since its automation rules restrict duplicate content.
