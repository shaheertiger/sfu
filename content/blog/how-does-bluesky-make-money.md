You've noticed Bluesky has no ads and no paid tier, and you're wondering how it pays its bills. The short answer: it barely makes money yet. Bluesky runs mainly on **venture funding**, about $123 million disclosed so far, plus small amounts from selling **custom domains** through a registrar partnership.

![How does Bluesky make money? Mostly venture funding, a little from custom domains, and no ads](/images/blog/how-does-bluesky-make-money-hero-1200x675.webp)

Its stated plans are an **optional paid subscription** with extras such as higher-quality video and profile customization, and paid services built on its open network, rather than an ad-driven feed. Bluesky is **not publicly traded**: it's a private company, so there's no Bluesky stock or ticker to buy.

This guide covers who owns Bluesky, how much it has raised, where its money comes from today, what it plans to sell, and how to avoid the scams that use its name.

## Who Owns Bluesky? A Public Benefit Corporation, Not a Nonprofit

Bluesky Social, PBC is a US **public benefit corporation**: a for-profit company whose charter also commits it to a public mission, in Bluesky's case an open, decentralized social web.

![Bluesky timeline: a Twitter-funded project in 2019, independent in 2021, private and venture-funded today](/images/blog/how-does-bluesky-make-money-ownership-1200x675.webp)

| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 2019 | Started as a project funded by Twitter, begun by Jack Dorsey |
| 2021 | Became an independent company |
| 2024 | Jack Dorsey left Bluesky's board |
| Early 2026 | Jay Graber, CEO from the start, moved to a Chief Innovation Officer role; Toni Schneider became interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent one |

### What Most People Don't Know

A public benefit corporation isn't a charity. It can take venture money, pay investors and one day sell shares or be acquired. The mission is written into its charter, but investors who own part of the company still expect a return. That's why the revenue question matters, even while the app is free.

> **Key point:** Bluesky is free today because investors are paying for it. Its plans are to make money from optional extras and services, not by selling your attention to advertisers.

## Is Bluesky Publicly Traded? No, and Watch for These Scams (Use With Caution)

Bluesky Social, PBC is privately held by its founders, staff and investors. It has no stock ticker and no announced IPO. That gap is exactly what scammers fill.

### The Real Risks

- **Crypto tokens using the Bluesky name.** Bluesky has no token or coin.
- **Similarly named companies.** Other businesses called "Blue Sky" or "Bluesky", in software, energy and finance, are unrelated.
- **"Pre-IPO" share offers.** Unsolicited offers to sell Bluesky shares are a common scam pattern.

### A Quick Safety Checklist

- ✓ Check any announcement against Bluesky's official account and blog.
- ✓ Treat any "Bluesky stock" or "Bluesky coin" ad as false until proven otherwise.
- ✗ Don't send money or connect a crypto wallet to anything claiming to be Bluesky.

> **Bottom line:** You can't buy Bluesky today. Anyone offering you a piece of it is either unrelated or running a scam.
