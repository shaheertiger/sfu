You've hit the numbers, or you're close, and you want to know when money actually lands in your bank account.

YouTube starts paying you once three things are true: your channel has been accepted into the YouTube Partner Program (YPP), you've linked an AdSense for YouTube account, and your finalized earnings have reached the payment threshold ($100 in the US at the time of writing). Each month's earnings are finalized early the following month and paid later that month, so your first payment usually arrives one to two months after you start earning, and later if your balance is still below the threshold.

![When does YouTube start paying you? After YPP, AdSense and a $100 balance, usually one to two months after you start earning](/images/blog/when-does-youtube-start-paying-you-hero-1200x675.webp)

It isn't obvious because being accepted into YPP feels like the finish line, but it's really the start of a monthly cycle with its own threshold and one-time checks. This guide covers the requirements, the application, the payment cycle, the holds that delay a first payout, worked scenarios, and how YouTube compares with other platforms.

## How YouTube Pays Creators: The 6-Step Path to Your First Payment

| Step | What happens | Typical timing (at the time of writing) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Become eligible | Reach the subscriber and watch-time or Shorts-view thresholds | Depends on your channel |
| 2. Apply | Accept the YPP terms and link AdSense for YouTube in YouTube Studio | Same day |
| 3. Review | YouTube reviews your channel against its policies | Often about a month, sometimes longer |
| 4. Earn | Monetization is turned on and ads can run on your videos | From acceptance onward |
| 5. Finalize | Last month's estimated earnings are finalized and added to your AdSense balance | Early the following month |
| 6. Get paid | AdSense issues payment if your balance is over the threshold | Around the 21st–26th of that month |

![The path to your first YouTube payment: eligible, apply, review, earn, finalize and get paid](/images/blog/when-does-youtube-start-paying-you-path-1200x675.webp)

Timing varies, and YouTube doesn't guarantee review times. The official details are in [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube).

### What Most People Don't Know

There's no back pay. Views from before your channel joined YPP help you qualify, but they're never paid retroactively. And at the time of writing, creators receive 55% of net ad revenue on long-form videos and 45% of the Shorts revenue allocated to them.

> **Key point:** Acceptance starts the clock, it doesn't stop it. Plan for your first payout one to two months after ads start running, or longer on a small channel.

## What Are the YouTube Partner Program Requirements in 2026?

YPP has two levels at the time of writing. Both require you to live in a country where the program is available.

![YouTube Partner Program tiers: fan funding at 500 subscribers and ad revenue sharing at 1,000](/images/blog/when-does-youtube-start-paying-you-tiers-1200x675.webp)

| Level | Subscribers | Plus one of | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fan funding tier | 500 | 3,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 3 million public Shorts views in 90 days (plus 3 public uploads in the last 90 days) | Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, some shopping features |
| Full ad revenue sharing | 1,000 | 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 10 million public Shorts views in 90 days | Ad revenue on long-form videos and Shorts, plus fan funding |

The lower tier isn't available in every YPP country, so check what YouTube Studio shows for your channel.

### Other Conditions

- Follow YouTube's channel monetization policies, including the Community Guidelines.
- Have no active Community Guidelines strikes.
- Turn on 2-Step Verification for the Google account that owns the channel.
- Have, or be ready to create, one AdSense for YouTube account. Each person can have only one.

Track your progress in YouTube Studio under **Earn**. If you're working toward the Shorts-views route, see [how to post YouTube Shorts](/blog/post-youtube-shorts).

## How to Apply and Link AdSense in 4 Steps

Menu labels change between YouTube Studio versions.

### Step 1: Open Earn in YouTube Studio

Click **Earn** in the left menu.

### Step 2: Accept the Base Terms

Click **Apply** (or **Get started**) and review and accept the terms.

### Step 3: Link AdSense for YouTube

Click **Start** next to the AdSense step, sign in with a Google account, and create or link your AdSense for YouTube account.

### Step 4: Wait for Review

Return to YouTube Studio. Your application now goes into review.

> **Pro tip:** Set up your AdSense address, tax info and bank details the day you're accepted, not the day you expect a payment. Those one-time checks are what delay most first payouts.

If your channel isn't accepted, YouTube tells you which policies it was reviewed against. At the time of writing, you can usually reapply after 30 days once you've fixed the issues, especially reused or repetitive content.

## How YouTube's Monthly Payment Cycle Works

### Estimated First, Then Finalized

During the month, YouTube Studio shows **estimated** revenue. Early the next month, those earnings are finalized and added to your AdSense balance. The finalized amount can differ slightly, for example after YouTube removes invalid traffic.

### One Payment a Month

If your balance is above the payment threshold after finalization, AdSense issues a payment, typically between the 21st and 26th of the month at the time of writing. It can take several more days to reach your bank.

### The $100 Payment Threshold

AdSense only pays once your balance reaches the threshold: $100 in the US at the time of writing, and it varies by currency. Below that, the balance carries over until it gets there.

## 4 Payment Holds That Delay Your First Payout

1. **Address verification.** When your balance reaches a lower verification threshold, AdSense mails a PIN to your payment address. Enter it in AdSense to unlock payments.
2. **Identity verification.** AdSense may ask you to confirm your name and address.
3. **Tax information.** Every monetizing creator must provide US tax info in AdSense, even outside the US, because Google may withhold tax on earnings from US viewers. Missing tax info can lead to higher withholding.
4. **A payment method**, such as a bank account, with a name that matches your AdSense account.

Payment holds are shown on the AdSense home page.

| Why your payment is late | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Balance under the threshold | Wait; it carries over |
| PIN not entered | Enter the mailed PIN in AdSense |
| Tax or identity checks incomplete | Finish them in AdSense |
| No or mismatched payment method | Add a bank account in your AdSense name |
| Accepted late in the month | The first month is small; the next will be fuller |
| Monetization paused | Check YouTube Studio for a review or demonetization |

## What Doesn't Get Paid

- **Views before you joined YPP.** No back pay.
- **Videos with ads turned off** or not suitable for most advertisers.
- **Invalid traffic**, such as purchased views or automated clicks, which is removed before earnings are finalized.

## Buying Views, Subscribers or Watch Hours (Use With Caution)

When you're a few hundred subscribers or watch hours short, services that sell them can look like a shortcut.

![Real growth toward YouTube monetization compared with risky purchased views, subscribers and watch hours](/images/blog/when-does-youtube-start-paying-you-caution-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Invalid traffic is removed** before earnings are finalized, and can risk your YPP status.
- **Fake subscribers don't watch,** so they drag down how your videos perform.
- **Policy violations** can get an application rejected or a channel demonetized.

### A Safer Checklist

- Grow watch time with a regular upload rhythm.
- Turn your best long videos into Shorts that point back to the full version.
- Never pay for views, subscribers or watch hours.

> **Bottom line:** Bought numbers can cost you the very monetization they're meant to unlock.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long after monetization does YouTube pay you?

Usually one to two months. Earnings for a month are finalized early the next month and paid around the 21st–26th, at the time of writing, as long as your balance is above the threshold and there are no payment holds.

### Does YouTube pay you for views before monetization?

No. You only earn once your channel is in YPP and monetization is active. Earlier views help you qualify but aren't paid retroactively.

### What is the minimum payout on YouTube?

YouTube pays through AdSense, which has a payment threshold: $100 in the US at the time of writing, and it varies by currency. Smaller balances roll over.

### Can I get paid by YouTube without AdSense?

No. AdSense for YouTube is how YPP creators are paid. It holds your balance, tax info and payment method.

### Do I need 1,000 subscribers to make money on YouTube?

Not for every feature. At the time of writing, the fan funding tier opens at 500 subscribers in available countries. Ad revenue sharing needs 1,000 subscribers plus the watch-hour or Shorts-view threshold.

### How long does YPP review take?

Often about a month, sometimes longer. YouTube doesn't guarantee review times.

### What day does YouTube pay?

AdSense typically issues payments between the 21st and 26th of the month, at the time of writing.

### Can I reapply if I'm rejected?

Usually after 30 days, at the time of writing, once you've fixed the issues YouTube named.
