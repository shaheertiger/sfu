You've edited a long video, and TikTok won't take it. Or you're planning a series and want to know how much room you have.

A TikTok can be up to **10 minutes** long when you record it in the app, and **up to 60 minutes** when you upload a finished video on many accounts, especially from a computer, at the time of writing. TikTok rolls longer limits out in stages, so your account may see a lower upload limit, such as 30 minutes, until it reaches you. The upload screen always shows your account's current maximum.

![How long can a TikTok be? Up to 10 minutes recorded in the app and up to 60 minutes uploaded on many accounts](/images/blog/how-long-can-a-tiktok-be-hero-1200x675.webp)

The answer is confusing because TikTok has several limits at once (camera, phone upload, desktop upload, Stories, LIVE) and changes them often. This guide lists every limit, explains why your account may differ, shows how to post a long video step by step, and covers the length that actually gets watched.

## TikTok Video Length Limits in 2026 (Every Format)

| Format | Maximum length, at the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded in the TikTok camera | 10 minutes (presets for shorter clips) |
| Uploaded from your phone | Up to 10 minutes on most accounts; longer on some |
| Uploaded on desktop (TikTok Studio) | Up to 60 minutes on many accounts |
| Stories | Short clips; launched at 15 seconds, so check the Story camera for your limit |
| Photo posts | Up to 35 images; no length limit as such |
| LIVE | No fixed limit; long streams are normal |
| Caption | 4,000 characters in the TikTok app |

![TikTok length limits compared: camera recording, phone upload, desktop upload, Stories and LIVE](/images/blog/how-long-can-a-tiktok-be-limits-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

The limits aren't the same for everyone. TikTok tests and rolls out longer upload limits gradually, so two creators can see different maximums on the same day. The camera limit and the upload limit are also separate: you can't record 60 minutes in the app, but you may be able to upload it.

> **Key point:** Don't trust a number you read online, including ours. The upload screen in your own TikTok app or TikTok Studio shows your account's real limit.

## How to Post a Long TikTok in 4 Steps (Desktop Method)

Long uploads are easiest from **TikTok Studio** on a computer. Menu labels change between versions, so look for "Upload" if the wording differs.

![Four steps to post a long TikTok video from a computer using TikTok Studio](/images/blog/how-long-can-a-tiktok-be-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Export the Right File

Export as MP4 (H.264) at 1080×1920, 9:16. See our guide to [TikTok video size and ratio](/blog/tiktok-video-size-ratio) for why that's the safe choice.

### Step 2: Open TikTok Studio and Check Your Limit

Sign in to TikTok on a computer, open TikTok Studio and go to upload. The screen tells you the maximum length and file size for your account. This is the step people skip, and then they wonder why a 45-minute file fails.

### Step 3: Upload, Then Write the Caption

Drop the file in, choose a cover, and write a caption. The first line matters most, since viewers see only that before "more".

### Step 4: Choose Who Can See It and Post or Schedule

Set the privacy, comment and duet options, then post now or schedule it from TikTok Studio.

> **Pro tip:** If a long upload keeps failing, try a smaller file first: shorten it or lower the bitrate. If the short version uploads, the problem is size or length, not your account.

## Recording vs Uploading: Which Gives You More Time?

| | Recording in the app | Uploading a file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum length | 10 minutes | Up to 60 minutes on many accounts |
| Effects, filters, sounds | Yes, in the camera | Add some after upload |
| Edit elsewhere first | No | Yes |
| Best from | Phone | Computer (TikTok Studio) |
| Schedule it | No | Yes, from TikTok Studio |

Recording in TikTok's camera gives you the effects, filters, sounds and stickers, but caps at 10 minutes. You can stop and start to record in segments, and the presets (such as 15 seconds, 60 seconds and 10 minutes, depending on version) set the timer.

Uploading a finished file is how creators post anything longer, or anything edited elsewhere.

## How Long Should a TikTok Be? (By Content Type)

The maximum isn't a target. What matters is whether people watch to the end, so the right length is however long the idea needs.

![How long a TikTok should be by content type, from quick jokes to long explainers](/images/blog/how-long-can-a-tiktok-be-length-1200x675.webp)

| Length | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 to 30 seconds | Jokes, reactions, quick tips, trends | Easy to rewatch, which helps completion rate |
| 30 to 90 seconds | Tutorials, stories, product demos | Long enough to deliver something useful |
| Over 1 minute | Creator Rewards Program eligibility at the time of writing | Needs a strong hook and pace |
| Several minutes or more | Explainers, commentary, podcasts, long tutorials | Works best for an audience that already knows you |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a TikTok be longer than 10 minutes?

Yes, if you upload it rather than record it in the app. Many accounts can upload up to 60 minutes, mostly from desktop; others see a lower limit until the rollout reaches them.

### Why can't I upload a long video to TikTok?

Your account may not have the longer upload limit yet, or the file is too large. Try uploading from TikTok Studio on a computer, and export as MP4 (H.264).

### How long can a TikTok Story be?

Stories launched with 15-second clips, and the limit has been reported differently since, so check the Story option in the camera for yours. Stories disappear after 24 hours.

### Do longer TikToks get more views?

Not automatically. Longer videos can earn more watch time, but only if people keep watching. A short video watched to the end often does better than a long one people leave.

### How long does a TikTok need to be for Creator Rewards?

Over 1 minute at the time of writing. TikTok sets and changes the rules, so check the program's own page.

### How long can a TikTok LIVE be?

There's no fixed limit at the time of writing, and long streams are normal.

### How long can a TikTok caption be?

4,000 characters in the TikTok app at the time of writing. See the [TikTok caption character limit](/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit).

### Can I record a 10-minute TikTok in one take?

Yes, if your camera offers the 10-minute preset. You can also stop and start to record in segments.
