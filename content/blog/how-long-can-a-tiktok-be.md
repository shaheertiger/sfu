A TikTok can be up to **10 minutes** long when you record it in the app, and **up to 60 minutes** when you upload a finished video on many accounts, especially from a computer, at the time of writing. TikTok rolls longer limits out in stages, so your account may see a lower upload limit, such as 30 minutes, until it reaches you. The upload screen always shows your account's current maximum.

## TikTok length limits at a glance

| Format | Maximum length, at the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded in the TikTok camera | 10 minutes (presets for shorter clips) |
| Uploaded from your phone | Up to 10 minutes on most accounts; longer on some |
| Uploaded on desktop (TikTok Studio) | Up to 60 minutes on many accounts |
| Stories | Short clips; launched at 15 seconds, check the Story camera for your limit |
| Photo posts | Up to 35 images; no length limit as such |
| LIVE | No fixed limit; long streams are normal |
| Caption | 4,000 characters |

TikTok changes these figures regularly. If an upload fails, the file is probably longer or larger than your account accepts; check the limit on the upload screen.

## Recording vs uploading

Recording in TikTok's camera gives you the effects, filters, sounds and stickers, but caps at 10 minutes. You can stop and start to record in segments, and the presets (such as 15 seconds, 60 seconds and 10 minutes, depending on version) set the timer.

Uploading a finished file is how creators post anything longer, or anything edited elsewhere. Long uploads are easiest from **TikTok Studio** on a computer, which also lets you schedule. ## How long should a TikTok be?

The maximum isn't a target. What matters is whether people watch to the end, so the right length is however long the idea needs.

- **7 to 30 seconds:** jokes, reactions, quick tips, trends. Easy to rewatch, which helps completion rate.
- **30 to 90 seconds:** tutorials, stories, product demos. Long enough to deliver something useful.
- **Over 1 minute:** required for TikTok's Creator Rewards Program at the time of writing, so creators aiming to earn post more of these. They need a strong hook and pace to hold people.
- **Several minutes or more:** explainers, commentary, podcasts and long tutorials for an audience that already knows you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a TikTok be longer than 10 minutes?
Yes, if you upload it rather than record it in the app. Many accounts can upload up to 60 minutes, mostly from desktop; others see a lower limit until the rollout reaches them.

### Why can't I upload a long video to TikTok?
Your account may not have the longer upload limit yet, or the file is too large. Try uploading from TikTok Studio on a computer, and export as MP4 (H.264).

### How long can a TikTok Story be?
Stories launched with 15-second clips, and the limit has been reported differently since, so check the Story option in the camera for yours. Stories disappear after 24 hours; see [how long TikTok Stories last](/blog/how-long-do-tiktok-stories-last).

### Do longer TikToks get more views?
Not automatically. Longer videos can earn more watch time, but only if people keep watching. A short video watched to the end often does better than a long one people leave.
