You've decided you're done with TikTok, or at least done with this account. To delete it, go to your **Profile**, tap the **menu** (three lines), then **Settings and privacy**, **Account**, **Deactivate or delete account**, and choose **Delete account permanently**. TikTok deactivates it straight away and deletes it for good after **30 days**, unless you log back in.

![How to delete a TikTok account: Settings and privacy, Account, Delete account permanently, then a 30-day window](/images/blog/how-to-delete-tiktok-account-hero-1200x675.webp)

The steps take a minute. The part people regret is what they didn't save first: videos, drafts, earnings and coins don't come back.

This guide covers the steps in the app and on a computer, deactivating instead, the five things to save before you go, and what happens during the 30 days.

## Delete or Deactivate? Why the Choice Matters More Than the Steps

![Deactivating a TikTok account compared with deleting it: deactivation is reversible, deletion erases the account after 30 days](/images/blog/how-to-delete-tiktok-account-delete-vs-deactivate-1200x675.webp)

TikTok gives you two options under the same menu, and they're very different.

| | Deactivate | Delete permanently |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile and videos | Hidden from everyone | Hidden, then erased after 30 days |
| Can you come back? | Yes, any time, by logging in | Only within 30 days |
| Username | Kept | Released after deletion |
| Purchases, coins, earnings | Kept | Lost: no refunds for coins or gifts |

If you only need a break, deactivate. If you're sure, delete.

### What Most People Don't Know

Uninstalling the app doesn't delete anything. Your account and videos stay live until you delete the account in Settings. And during the 30-day window, simply logging in cancels the deletion, which is easy to do by accident on a second phone or tablet that's still signed in.

> **Key point:** The 30 days is a safety net, not a countdown you can ignore. Log out on every device so you don't cancel the deletion by accident.

## How to Delete a TikTok Account in the App (7 Steps)

![Four steps to delete a TikTok account in the app: open Settings and privacy, Account, Delete account permanently, verify](/images/blog/how-to-delete-tiktok-account-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so yours may read slightly differently.

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile**.

### Step 2: Tap the Menu

Tap the **menu** icon (three lines) at the top right.

### Step 3: Open Settings and Privacy, Then Account

Tap **Settings and privacy**, then **Account**.

### Step 4: Tap Deactivate or Delete Account

This one screen holds both options.

### Step 5: Choose Delete Account Permanently (Not Deactivate)

Pick a reason if asked and tap **Continue**. This is where people tap the wrong option, so read the label before you continue.

### Step 6: Verify It's You

Enter the code TikTok sends to your phone or email, or your password. If you signed up with Apple, Google or another app, TikTok asks you to verify through that account instead.

### Step 7: Confirm

Tap **Delete account** to confirm.

> **Pro tip:** Request your data download (below) before step 1. It can take a few days to prepare, and you need to be logged in to collect it.

## How to Delete a TikTok Account on a Computer

On tiktok.com, sign in, hover over your profile picture, choose **Settings**, then **Manage account**, and select **Delete account**. The verification steps are the same as in the app.

## Before You Delete: 5 Things to Save First

![Checklist of what to save before deleting TikTok: your data, videos, drafts, earnings and subscriptions](/images/blog/how-to-delete-tiktok-account-save-first-1200x675.webp)

1. **Download your data.** In **Settings and privacy > Account**, use **Download your data** to request a copy of your profile, videos and activity. The file can take a few days to prepare.
2. **Save your videos and drafts.** Download any videos you want to keep, or check the originals are still in your camera roll. Drafts are deleted with the account: see [how to save TikTok drafts to your camera roll](/blog/how-to-save-tiktok-drafts-to-camera-roll).
3. **Withdraw earnings.** Cash out any Creator Rewards or LIVE gift balance first.
4. **Cancel subscriptions.** Subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play must be cancelled there, not in TikTok.
5. **Check linked logins.** If you sign in to other apps with TikTok, switch them to another login.

| What | Where | Lost if you skip it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your data | Settings and privacy > Account > Download your data | Yes, after 30 days |
| Videos | Each video, or your camera roll | Yes, unless you have the originals |
| Drafts | Save each draft to your device | Yes |
| Earnings and gift balance | Your creator or LIVE balance | Yes |
| Coins | Can't be refunded | Yes |

## What Happens After You Delete Your TikTok Account?

![What happens during and after TikTok's 30-day deletion window](/images/blog/how-to-delete-tiktok-account-after-1200x675.webp)

For the first 30 days the account is deactivated: nobody can see your profile or videos, but logging in cancels the deletion. After that, TikTok deletes the account and you can't recover it.

A few things outlast the account:

- **Some information may stay with TikTok** where the law requires it.
- **Messages you sent** can remain visible in other people's inboxes.
- **Your username** is released after deletion is final, and someone else can eventually claim it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to delete a TikTok account?

The account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days, as long as you don't log in during that time.

### Can I recover a deleted TikTok account?

Yes, within 30 days. Log in and choose to reactivate it. After 30 days it's gone and can't be restored.

### Does deleting the TikTok app delete my account?

No. Uninstalling the app leaves your account and videos live. Delete the account in Settings first.

### Can I delete a TikTok account without the password?

You need to log in to delete it. If you've forgotten the password, reset it with your phone number or email first. If you've lost access completely, report the problem to TikTok support.

### Will my username be available again after I delete?

After deletion is final, the username can eventually be claimed by someone else, so don't count on getting it back. Our [TikTok username checker](/tools/tiktok-username-checker) shows whether a handle is free.

### Do I get a refund for coins when I delete TikTok?

No. Coins and gifts aren't refunded, so spend or withdraw what you can first.

### What's the difference between deactivating and deleting?

Deactivating hides your account until you log back in. Deleting hides it for 30 days, then erases it permanently.

### Can I delete TikTok on a computer?

Yes. On tiktok.com, go to Settings, then Manage account, then Delete account.
