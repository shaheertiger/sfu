You found a TikTok your followers need to see, but you don't want to download it and re-upload someone else's work. To repost on TikTok, open the video, tap the **Share** arrow on the right, and tap **Repost** (a yellow circular-arrows icon). The video is shared with your followers and saved to the **Reposts** tab on your profile.

![How to repost on TikTok: tap Share, then Repost, and the original creator keeps the views and credit](/images/blog/how-to-repost-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Your followers may see it in their feeds with a "[name] reposted" label, and the original creator keeps the views and credit. You can add a short comment in the box that appears.

If there's no Repost button, the creator has turned reposts off for that video, the account is private, or your app hasn't got the feature yet. This guide covers the steps, what "reposted" means, every reason the button goes missing, and what to do with your own videos, which you can't repost.

## What Does "Reposted" Mean on TikTok? (Repost vs Re-Upload)

![A TikTok repost compared with re-uploading someone's video: reposts keep the creator's credit, re-uploads take it](/images/blog/how-to-repost-on-tiktok-versus-1200x675.webp)

When you see "[name] reposted" above a video, someone you follow, or are connected to, shared that video with their followers. It isn't their video; it's a share that points to the original creator.

| | Repost | Downloading and re-uploading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it appears | Your followers' feeds and your Reposts tab | Your main grid, as if it were yours |
| Who gets the views | The original creator | You |
| Credit | Kept automatically | Easy to lose |
| Risk | None | Copyright problems and reduced reach |

### What Most People Don't Know

Reposts don't copy the video to your grid. They live in a separate Reposts tab on your profile, so your own videos stay front and center. And the views and engagement count for the original creator, which is why creators generally welcome reposts.

> **Key point:** A repost is a share, not a copy. It's the in-app way to pass a video on while the person who made it keeps the credit.

## How to Repost a TikTok Video (Step by Step)

![Steps to repost on TikTok: open the video, tap Share, tap Repost, and optionally add your thoughts](/images/blog/how-to-repost-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Find the Video

Open it from your For You feed, Following feed or a profile.

### Step 2: Tap the Share Arrow

It's on the right side of the screen.

### Step 3: Tap Repost

It's at the top of the share sheet, with a yellow circular-arrows icon.

### Step 4: Add Your Thoughts (Optional)

Tap **Add thoughts**, or the comment box that appears, to say why you're sharing it. This is the step people skip, and it's what makes a repost feel like a recommendation rather than noise.

> **Pro tip:** A one-line reason ("this saved my sourdough") gives your followers a reason to watch. Keep it short; the video does the work.

### How to Undo a Repost

Tap Share again on the same video and choose **Remove repost**. Our guide to [removing reposts on TikTok](/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok) covers removing them in bulk.

## Why Can't I Repost on TikTok? 6 Causes and Fixes

| Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The creator turned off reposts for the video | Nothing; share the link instead |
| The video is from a private account | Only the account's followers can watch, and reposting isn't offered |
| It's your own video | You can't repost yourself; pin it to your profile instead |
| The video is restricted by age, region or review | It can't be reposted while restricted |
| Old app version or a rollout that hasn't reached you | Update TikTok, then log out and back in |
| VPN or a poor connection | Turn off the VPN and retry on another network |

If the button disappears for every video, reinstalling the app usually brings it back. If it's missing on one creator's videos only, that's their setting.

## Can People See What You Repost on TikTok?

Yes. Reposts are visible to your followers and appear in your profile's Reposts tab. Making your account private limits them to approved followers. If you don't want someone to see a repost, remove it.

| Who | Can they see your reposts? |
| --- | --- |
| Your followers | Yes, in their feeds and your Reposts tab |
| The original creator | They can see repost counts |
| Strangers, if your account is public | Generally yes, through your profile |
| Strangers, if your account is private | No |

## How to "Repost" Your Own TikTok (4 Alternatives)

![Ways to resurface your own TikTok: pin it, stitch or duet it, post it again with a new hook, or share it to Reels and Shorts](/images/blog/how-to-repost-on-tiktok-own-video-1200x675.webp)

TikTok doesn't let you repost your own video. Options instead:

1. **Pin it** to the top of your profile from the video's **...** menu.
2. **Stitch or duet it** to add a follow-up.
3. **Post it again** as a new video if it's been a while, with a new hook or caption. Duplicate uploads can be held back from recommendations if they look like spam, so change something meaningful.
4. **Share it to other platforms.** Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts take the same vertical video.

## Repost Apps and Re-Uploading Other People's Videos (Use With Caution)

Some apps promise to "repost" TikToks for you by downloading the video and uploading it again from your account. Others encourage cropping the watermark and posting the file as new.

### The Real Risks

- **It strips the creator's credit.** Re-uploading someone else's video as yours can lead to copyright removals.
- **Reach can suffer.** Platforms reduce the reach of content that's simply reposted from elsewhere.
- **Some apps want your login.** Never give a third-party app your TikTok password.

### If You Want to Share Someone's Video Beyond a Repost

- Use Repost, Stitch or Duet inside TikTok.
- Ask the creator for permission and the original file, and credit them by name.
- Don't crop out watermarks or usernames.

> **Bottom line:** The Repost button already does what these apps promise, without risking your account or the creator's credit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I repost on TikTok?

Tap the Share arrow on the video, then tap Repost. You can add a short comment in the box that appears.

### Where do I find my reposts?

On your profile, in the tab with the circular-arrows icon.

### Why is the Repost button not showing?

The creator has turned reposts off, the account is private, the video is restricted, or your app needs an update.

### Does the creator know I reposted their video?

Creators can see repost counts, and reposts appear to your followers with your name, so it's visible.

### Does reposting help the creator?

Yes. Reposts send the video to more people, and the views count toward the original creator.

### Can I repost my own TikTok?

No. Pin it to your profile, stitch or duet it, or post it again with a new hook.

### How do I remove a repost?

Tap Share on the video again and choose Remove repost.

### Can I turn off reposts on my videos?

The creator controls whether a video can be reposted, so check your privacy settings and each video's settings in your version of the app.
