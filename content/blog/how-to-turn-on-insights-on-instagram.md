You've looked through every Instagram setting for an "Insights" toggle and can't find one, while other accounts talk about their reach and watch time.

To turn on Insights on Instagram, switch your account to a professional account (either Creator or Business). Open your profile, tap the menu, go to the account type settings and choose **Switch to professional account**. Insights then appear on your profile as the **Professional dashboard**, and under each new post as **View insights**.

![How to turn on Insights on Instagram: switch to a Creator or Business account, then open the Professional dashboard](/images/blog/how-to-turn-on-insights-on-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

There's no separate "Insights" switch, which is why people miss it. Insights come with a professional account, and they only start tracking from the moment you switch, so it's worth doing early.

This guide covers what you need to know first, Creator versus Business, the switching steps, where Insights live, what each metric means, how far back data goes and how to use it.

## Why There's No Insights Toggle on Instagram

Insights are a feature of professional accounts, not a setting on personal ones. Switching account type is what turns them on.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is there an Insights switch? | No. Insights come with a professional account |
| Which account types get Insights? | Creator and Business |
| Does it cost anything? | No, switching is free |
| Will I see stats for old posts? | Generally no. Data starts after you switch |
| Can I switch back? | Yes, but you lose access to Insights |
| Where do I find Insights? | Professional dashboard on your profile, or View insights on a post |

![Personal Instagram accounts compared with professional accounts that have Insights](/images/blog/how-to-turn-on-insights-on-instagram-why-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Insights start from the moment you switch. Posts published while you had a personal account usually won't show stats, even after you go professional. Audience details such as locations and most active times also only appear once you reach a minimum follower count (100 followers at the time of writing).

> **Key point:** Switch early. Every week you stay on a personal account is a week of posts with no data behind them.

## Creator vs Business Account: Which One Gets You Insights?

Both do. The differences are mostly about the tools around them.

| | Creator | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Insights | Yes | Yes |
| Built for | Influencers, artists, public figures, creators | Brands, shops, local businesses, organizations |
| Extra tools | Inbox organizing, creator-focused tools | Contact buttons, a connected Facebook Page, shopping where available |
| Can be private | Instagram tells you when you switch | No, at the time of writing |

- **Creator** suits an individual building an audience, with extra options for organizing your inbox and creator-focused tools.
- **Business** suits a company or anyone selling products, with contact buttons, a connected Facebook Page and business tools.

### 3 Things to Check Before You Switch

- **Privacy.** Business accounts can't be private at the time of writing. If your account is private, Instagram will tell you whether the account type you've chosen requires it to be public.
- **Category label.** You'll pick a category, such as "Artist" or "Restaurant", and choose whether it's shown on your profile.
- **Third-party tools.** Social media schedulers and analytics tools that use Instagram's official API generally need a professional account to connect.

You can change between Creator and Business later, so don't overthink the choice.

## How to Switch to a Professional Account in 10 Steps

Instagram renames and moves menu items between app versions, so the labels below may be slightly different on your phone. The overall path stays similar.

![The steps to switch an Instagram account to a professional account and turn on Insights](/images/blog/how-to-turn-on-insights-on-instagram-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Open the Instagram app and go to your **profile**.

### Step 2: Tap the Menu

Tap the **menu** icon (three lines) in the top-right corner.

### Step 3: Open Settings and Activity

Tap **Settings and activity** (or **Settings**).

### Step 4: Find Account Type and Tools

Look for **Account type and tools** (sometimes under **For professionals** or **Account**). This is the step people get stuck on, because the name and position move between versions. Use the search bar in Settings and type "professional" if you can't find it.

### Step 5: Tap Switch to Professional Account

Read the screens that explain what you'll get and tap **Continue**.

### Steps 6–10: Category, Type, Contact Details, Done

1. **Pick a category** that describes you or your business, and decide whether to show it.
2. Choose **Creator** or **Business** when asked.
3. Review or add **contact details** if you chose Business. You can skip this.
4. Tap **Done**.
5. You may be prompted to connect a Facebook Page or set up your profile. These steps are optional.

> **Pro tip:** If you plan to use a scheduler for Instagram, connect a Facebook Page when you're offered the option. Many tools that publish through Instagram's API need it, and it's quicker to do now than later.

On a computer, you can usually find the same option in Instagram's settings on the web, but the app has historically had the most complete set of options. Instagram's own steps are in the [Instagram Help Center](https://help.instagram.com) if the menus look different.

## Where to Find Instagram Insights After Switching (2 Places)

### Account-Level Insights

1. Go to your profile.
2. Tap **Professional dashboard** (or **Insights**) below your bio.
3. Choose a time range at the top, then open a section such as views, interactions or followers.

### Insights for a Single Post, Reel or Story

- **Posts and Reels:** open the post and tap **View insights** below it.
- **Stories:** open your Story while it's live and swipe up, or open it later from your Archive or Highlights.

## What Instagram Insights Show: 5 Groups of Metrics

Exact names change as Instagram updates the dashboard, but these are the main types of data at the time of writing.

![The five groups of Instagram Insights metrics: reach, interactions, Reels, Stories and audience](/images/blog/how-to-turn-on-insights-on-instagram-metrics-1200x675.webp)

| Group | Metrics | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Reach and views | Views, accounts reached, followers vs non-followers | How far your content travels |
| Interactions | Likes, comments, shares, saves, Story replies, profile activity | What people do with it |
| Reels | Plays or views, watch time, average watch time, skip rate or similar | Whether your opening holds people |
| Story navigation | Forward taps, back taps, next story, exits | Which frames lose viewers |
| Audience | Follower growth, top locations, age ranges, gender, most active times | Who follows you and when they're online |

**Views** have become a main metric across formats. **Most active times** is the data you need to work out when to post: see our guide to the [best time to post on Instagram on Monday](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram-on-monday) for how to use it.

## How Far Back Do Instagram Insights Go?

Instagram's dashboard lets you choose time ranges, such as the last 7, 30 or 90 days, and in some views a custom range or previous months. Historically, the in-app window has been around 90 days for many account-level views, but Instagram has extended this in some areas. Check the date range menu in your own dashboard.

- **Note key numbers regularly.** For a long-term record, write down monthly totals or take screenshots, because in-app windows are limited.
- **Remember the starting line.** Posts published before you switched usually won't show data.

## How to Use Insights Well: A 5-Step Habit

1. **Set a review rhythm.** Once a week or once a month, look at the same metrics in the same way.
2. **Compare like with like.** Reels with Reels, carousels with carousels.
3. **Look at saves and shares**, not just likes. They often show which content people found useful.
4. **Test one change at a time**, such as posting time, hook or format.
5. **Write down what you learn**, so next month's content is based on evidence rather than memory.

## Third-Party Analytics Apps (Use With Caution)

Once you care about numbers, you'll see apps that promise deeper stats, "who viewed your profile" lists or follower tracking.

### The Real Risks

- **Password requests.** Apps that ask for your Instagram login instead of using Instagram's official connection put your account at risk.
- **Data Instagram doesn't share.** No app can show who viewed your profile, so any that claims to is guessing or scamming.
- **Rule breaks.** Automated access outside Instagram's API can lead to restrictions.

> **Bottom line:** Instagram's own Insights are free and accurate. Only connect tools that use Instagram's official sign-in, and never type your password into a third-party site.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I see Insights on my Instagram account?

You're most likely on a personal account. Insights are only available on Creator and Business accounts. Switch to a professional account in your settings, then check your profile for the Professional dashboard.

### Is switching to a professional account free?

Yes. Switching to a Creator or Business account doesn't cost anything. Boosting posts and running ads are separate and optional.

### Will I get Insights for posts I made before switching?

Generally no. Instagram starts collecting Insights from the time you switch, which is why it helps to switch early.

### Can I switch back to a personal account?

Yes, from the same account type settings at any time. You'll lose access to Insights and professional tools, and data from your professional period may not be available if you later switch again.

### Why is my audience data empty?

Instagram only shows audience details, such as locations, age ranges and most active times, once your account reaches a minimum number of followers (100 at the time of writing).

### Do I need a Facebook Page to use Instagram Insights?

No. You can see Insights without connecting a Facebook Page. Connecting one is optional, and mainly useful for Meta Business Suite, ads and scheduling tools.

### Should I choose Creator or Business?

Creator for individuals building an audience, Business for companies and shops. Both get Insights, and you can change later.

### Can I see Insights on a computer?

Often, yes, through Instagram on the web, but the app has historically had the most complete set of options.
