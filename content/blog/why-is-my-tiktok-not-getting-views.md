You spent an evening on a video and it's sitting at 200 views, or zero. When your TikTok isn't getting views, the cause is usually one of two things: something is stopping the video from being shown (a privacy setting, a restriction or a guideline issue), or people are being shown it and swiping away in the first few seconds.

![Why is my TikTok not getting views? Either it isn't being shown, or people swipe away early](/images/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views-hero-1200x675.webp)

Check the first group in your settings and TikTok Studio, then work on the second with a stronger hook, a length that fits the idea, and original content.

This guide covers how to tell which problem you have, the 5 causes that stop TikTok showing your video, the 7 that stop people watching, the "hacks" that make things worse, and a weekly routine that steadily improves your numbers.

## Why TikTok Views Drop: Shown Less or Watched Less?

Every low-view problem falls into one of two buckets, and they need opposite fixes. If TikTok isn't showing the video, editing won't help. If it is showing it and people leave, settings won't help.

![Two reasons TikTok videos get few views: not being shown, or not being watched](/images/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views-buckets-1200x675.webp)

| What you're seeing | Likely cause | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| 0 views for a long time after posting | Video still under review, privacy not set to public, or a restriction | The video's privacy setting, Account status |
| Views stuck at a few hundred on every video | Viewers swipe away early, or it isn't reaching the right people | Average watch time and retention in analytics |
| One video flagged, others fine | That video is ineligible for the For You feed | Notifications and Account status |
| Sudden drop across all videos | A guideline warning or a change in what you post | Account status, recent changes to your content |
| Most views from your profile or Following | Little For You distribution | Traffic source in video analytics |
| Good views but almost no followers | No reason to see more | Profile, bio, consistency of topic |

### What Most People Don't Know

TikTok has an **Account status** page that lists guideline violations, restricted features, and videos that are ineligible for the For You feed. Most people who think they're "shadowbanned" never open it. It's the only place TikTok tells you directly.

> **Key point:** Rule out the boring causes first: privacy, account status, processing. Only then rework the hook.

## 5 Problems That Stop TikTok Showing Your Video

Start here. No amount of editing helps if the video isn't eligible to be recommended. Menu paths vary between app versions, so use the search in Settings if a label has moved.

![Four checks to run before blaming your content for low TikTok views: privacy, Account status, For You eligibility and processing](/images/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views-shown-1200x675.webp)

### 1. Check the Video Is Public

It sounds obvious, but it's common. Open the video, tap the three dots, and check **Privacy settings**. If it's set to **Friends** or **Only me**, only those people can see it. Also check whether your whole account is private under **Settings and privacy → Privacy**. If you schedule TikToks with a tool, check the privacy chosen there too.

### 2. Open Account Status for Violations

Go to **Settings and privacy → Account → Account status**. This page shows guideline violations, restricted features, and whether any videos are ineligible for the For You feed. If you think a decision was wrong, you can appeal from there.

### 3. Check For You Eligibility

A video can stay up on your profile but not be pushed to new viewers, for example if TikTok considers it unsuitable for broad recommendation, unoriginal, low quality or misleading. You'll usually get a notification, and it shows in Account status.

### 4. Consider Account Age and Your Birthday

Very new accounts have little history, so early videos may reach few people. TikTok also applies extra protections to younger users, including limits on recommending their content, so make sure your account's birthday is accurate.

### 5. Give It Time to Process

New uploads can sit at zero views while they're checked. Don't delete and re-upload the same video to "reset" it; that doesn't help, and repeated duplicates can look like spam.

> **Pro tip:** Run checks 1 and 2 on every video that's stuck at zero. They take a minute and explain more "dead" videos than anything else.

## 7 Problems That Stop People Watching

If everything above is fine, the video is being shown but not holding attention. Open its analytics in TikTok Studio (or tap **More data** under the video) and look at average watch time, the retention graph and traffic sources.

![What keeps people watching a TikTok compared with what makes them swipe away](/images/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views-watched-1200x675.webp)

### 6. The Hook Is Weak

The first second or two decides whether people stay. Strong openings start with the payoff, problem or surprise, not a greeting or logo; put a clear line of on-screen text in the first frame; show movement straight away; and make a promise the rest of the video keeps. If the retention graph drops steeply at the start, rework the hook first.

### 7. The Length Doesn't Fit the Idea

A video should be as long as the idea needs. If people leave halfway, cut the middle. If they watch to the end and rewatch, the idea might support a longer version. Videos over one minute matter for TikTok's Creator Rewards Program, but only if they hold attention. See [how long a TikTok can be](/blog/how-long-can-a-tiktok-be).

### 8. It Isn't Original

Reposted clips, reactions that add little, and videos with another app's watermark are less likely to be recommended. Film your own footage, add your own voice, and export cleanly.

### 9. The Audio Isn't Working

Check that your voice is clear and the music isn't drowning it out. Business accounts can only use sounds from TikTok's commercial library, and unlicensed music can get audio muted, which hurts watch time. Add captions, since many people watch with the sound off. See our guide to [TikTok music copyright](/blog/tiktok-music-copyright).

### 10. TikTok Can't Tell What It's About

TikTok is used as a search engine for many topics. Say the topic out loud early, put the main phrase in on-screen text, write a caption in plain words someone might search for, and use a few specific hashtags rather than a long generic list.

### 11. You Post Inconsistently

Posting in bursts and disappearing makes it harder to build a returning audience and to learn what works. You don't need to post every day; you need a pace you can keep.

### 12. Your Topic Changes Every Video

If one video is cooking, the next is gaming and the next is a vlog, it's hard for TikTok to find the people who'll like your next post, and hard for viewers to decide to follow. Pick two or three related themes and stay with them for a while.

## TikTok "Growth Hacks" That Backfire (Use With Caution)

Search for a fix and you'll find plenty of hacks. Most are against the rules or simply don't work.

![TikTok habits that build views compared with growth hacks that backfire](/images/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views-hacks-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Bought views, likes or followers** break TikTok's rules and fill your audience with accounts that never watch.
- **Engagement groups**, where people like and comment on each other's videos for the sake of it, count as fake engagement.
- **"Growth" apps that want your password** put your account at risk.
- **"Unban" or "unshadowban" services** can't do anything; only TikTok can change your account status.

### Also Avoid

- Spamming hashtags like #fyp in place of real keywords.
- Deleting videos that start slowly; some pick up later, especially through search.

> **Bottom line:** There's no shortcut TikTok can't see. Fix the settings, then the first two seconds.

## What to Do Next: A Weekly Routine

1. **Review last week** in TikTok Studio: note the two videos with the best average watch time and the two with the worst.
2. **Compare their first two seconds.** What's different?
3. **Plan the next batch** around the format that held attention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my TikTok have 0 views?

Usually the video is still being reviewed, its privacy is set to Friends or Only me, your account is private, or there's a restriction on your account. Check the video's privacy settings and your Account status page first.

### Why did my TikTok views suddenly drop?

Check Account status first, since a guideline warning or ineligible videos can cut For You distribution. If nothing is listed, look at what changed: a new topic, a different format, weaker hooks or a gap in posting.

### Is my TikTok account shadowbanned?

TikTok doesn't use the word, but it can make videos ineligible for the For You feed or restrict features after a guideline violation. Account status lists violations and restrictions and lets you appeal; see our [TikTok shadowban guide](/blog/tiktok-shadowban) for more.

### Does deleting and reposting a TikTok help?

Generally, no. Reposting the same video rarely changes how it performs, and repeated duplicate uploads can look like spam, so make a new version with a stronger hook instead.

### How long does it take for a TikTok to get views?

It varies. Some videos get most of their views in the first day or two, and others pick up later through search or shares, so look at patterns across several posts rather than judging in the first hour.

### Do hashtags still matter on TikTok?

A little. A few relevant hashtags help describe your video, but they matter less than what you say, show and write in the caption.

### Do scheduled TikToks get fewer views?

There's no good evidence that they do when posted through TikTok's official tools. See [do scheduled posts get less views](/blog/do-scheduled-posts-get-less-views) for the details.

### How many times a week should I post on TikTok?

There's no official number. Pick a pace you can keep for months; a steady rhythm teaches you more than bursts followed by silence.
