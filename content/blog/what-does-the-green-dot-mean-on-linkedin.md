You sent a message, the person has a green dot on their photo, and there's no reply. A solid green dot on LinkedIn means the person is **active on LinkedIn right now**. It's part of LinkedIn's **Active Status** feature, which shows who's online so you know when a message is likely to get a quick reply. A hollow green circle generally means they aren't active at the moment but can still be reached, often on the mobile app.

![What the green dot means on LinkedIn: a solid dot is active now, a hollow circle is away but reachable, no dot is offline or hidden](/images/blog/what-does-the-green-dot-mean-on-linkedin-hero-1200x675.webp)

What the dot doesn't mean is that they've seen your message, or that they're free to talk. That's where most of the frustration comes from.

This guide explains each indicator, where it appears, what it does and doesn't tell you, and how to limit or hide your own status. LinkedIn updates its interface regularly, so labels are described as they appear at the time of writing.

## What Do LinkedIn's Green Dot and Circle Mean? (3 Indicators)

![The three LinkedIn Active Status indicators compared: solid green dot, hollow green circle and no dot](/images/blog/what-does-the-green-dot-mean-on-linkedin-indicators-1200x675.webp)

| Indicator | What it usually means | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solid green dot | Active now | The person is using LinkedIn at this moment |
| Hollow green circle (green outline, white center) | Away, or reachable on mobile | Usually not active right now, but likely to get notifications |
| No dot | Offline, or Active Status is hidden | You can't tell which, by design |

LinkedIn's help pages are the best source for the exact meaning of the hollow circle, which has been described in slightly different ways over time. In practice, treat a solid dot as "online now" and a hollow circle as "not online right now, but may see your message soon".

### What Most People Don't Know

The green dot and the green **#OpenToWork** frame are unrelated. The frame is a green ring with text around a profile photo that tells recruiters, or everyone, that someone is looking for a job. The small dot is only about whether they're online. The two can appear at the same time.

> **Key point:** A green dot is a hint about timing, not proof of attention. It says someone is on LinkedIn, not that they've seen your message.

## Where You'll See the Green Dot on LinkedIn

The indicator appears in the bottom corner of a member's profile photo in a few places:

- **Messaging:** in your conversation list and at the top of an open conversation.
- **Search results and My Network:** next to people's names and photos in some views.
- **Profile cards and pop-ups:** when you hover over or tap someone in certain parts of LinkedIn.

You won't see the dot on everyone. It only appears if the other person's Active Status settings let you see it, and if your own settings allow you to see others.

## What the Green Dot Tells You (and 3 Things It Doesn't)

![What the LinkedIn green dot tells you, that someone is online, versus what it doesn't, like read messages or profile views](/images/blog/what-does-the-green-dot-mean-on-linkedin-tells-1200x675.webp)

### What It Tells You

- The person is currently active on LinkedIn, on desktop or in the app.
- If you message them, there's a good chance they'll see it soon.

### What It Doesn't Tell You

1. **That they've read your message.** Read receipts are a separate setting.
2. **That they're available to talk.** They may be scrolling the feed, posting or answering someone else.
3. **That they viewed your profile.** Active Status doesn't reveal that. Profile viewing is controlled by separate settings.

If someone is active and doesn't reply, give them time. Sending several follow-ups because the dot is green rarely helps.

## How to Turn Off the Green Dot (Active Status) on LinkedIn (5 Steps)

![Five steps to turn off LinkedIn Active Status: Me, Settings and Privacy, Visibility, Manage active status, choose No one](/images/blog/what-does-the-green-dot-mean-on-linkedin-turn-off-1200x675.webp)

Option names may differ slightly depending on your app version.

### Step 1: Open Settings & Privacy

On a computer, click the **Me** icon (your photo) at the top of the homepage and select **Settings & Privacy**. In the app, tap your **profile photo** in the top-left corner, then **Settings**.

### Step 2: Go to Visibility

Click or tap **Visibility**.

### Step 3: Find Manage Active Status

On a computer it sits under "Visibility of your LinkedIn activity". Click or tap **Manage active status**.

### Step 4: Read the Note Under the Setting (the Step People Skip)

LinkedIn has said that if you hide your status, you may not be able to see other people's Active Status either. The description under the setting explains the current effect, so read it before you switch.

### Step 5: Choose Who Sees Your Dot

| Setting | Who sees your green dot |
| --- | --- |
| Your connections only | First-degree connections |
| All LinkedIn members | Any member, including people you aren't connected to |
| No one | Nobody: your status is hidden |

> **Pro tip:** Can't find the page? Use the search box at the top of Settings & Privacy and type "active status".

### Read Receipts and Typing Indicators Are Separate

To stop people seeing when you've read their messages, go to **Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Messaging experience → Read receipts and typing indicators** (at the time of writing) and switch it off. As with Active Status, turning it off may mean you can't see other people's read receipts either.

## Should You Hide Your Green Dot? (Job Hunting vs Researching)

| Your situation | Keep it visible? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job hunting or selling | Yes | Recruiters and clients can see you're responsive |
| Networking actively | Yes | Contacts may reach out when they see you online |
| Browsing during work hours | Hide it | Your manager or colleagues won't notice |
| Researching a company or job move | Hide it | Keeps a lower profile |
| Feeling pressure to reply instantly | Hide it | Removes the expectation |

There's no penalty for hiding your status. It doesn't affect how your posts or profile appear in search or the feed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a solid green dot mean on LinkedIn?

The person is active on LinkedIn right now. It's part of Active Status, and it suggests a message you send will probably be seen soon.

### What does a hollow green circle mean on LinkedIn?

A green circle with a white center generally means the person isn't active at the moment but can still be reached, often through the mobile app. LinkedIn has described it slightly differently over time, so treat it as "away but reachable".

### Can people see when I'm online on LinkedIn?

By default, some or all members can see a green dot when you're active, depending on your settings. You can limit it to connections or hide it under Settings & Privacy → Visibility → Manage active status.

### Does the green dot mean someone viewed my profile?

No. Active Status only shows whether someone is online. Profile views are tracked separately.

### Does the green dot mean they read my message?

No. Read receipts are a separate setting, and the person may have them turned off.

### If I turn off Active Status, can I still see others?

Usually not. LinkedIn has said hiding your own status can also hide other members' status from you. Check the description under the setting for the current behavior.

### Does the green dot show when I'm only using the mobile app?

Yes, you can appear active while using the app. When you're not active but have the app installed, some members may see a hollow circle instead.

### Is the green dot the same as Open to Work?

No. Open to Work is a green frame with text around the photo. The dot only shows whether someone is online.
