You posted a TikTok and now you want it gone: a typo in the caption, the wrong clip, or something you'd rather not have on your profile.

To delete a TikTok video, open your profile, tap the video, tap the **three dots** (or the **…** menu) and choose **Delete**, then confirm. The video, its views, likes and comments are removed for good. If you only want it off your profile, set it to **Only me** instead: it disappears for everyone else, you keep it and its stats, and you can make it public again later.

![How to delete a TikTok video: three dots, then Delete, or set it to Only me to hide it without losing views](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-tiktok-video-hero-1200x675.webp)

It's easy to delete something you'll later want back, because Delete sits right next to the privacy options and there's no undo. This guide covers why hiding is often better, how to delete on a phone and a computer, how to unpost without deleting, what happens to reposts and duets, and how to avoid deleting in the first place. TikTok moves menus between app versions, so labels may differ slightly on your phone.

## Delete or Hide? Why "Only Me" Is Usually the Safer Choice

Deleting is permanent. Hiding a video with **Only me** gets the same result for your viewers, and keeps everything for you.

![Deleting a TikTok compared with setting it to Only me: permanent loss versus a reversible hide](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-tiktok-video-delete-vs-hide-1200x675.webp)

| Option | Who can see it | Keeps views and comments | Can you undo it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delete | No one | No | No |
| Only me | Just you | Yes | Yes, switch back to Everyone |
| Friends | Followers you follow back | Yes | Yes |

### What Most People Don't Know

"Unposting" isn't a TikTok button. When people say they want to unpost a TikTok, they usually mean hiding it, and **Only me** does exactly that. A deleted post can't be brought back from TikTok's normal menus, but a hidden one is one setting away from being public again.

> **Key point:** If there's any chance you'll want the video back, set it to Only me. Delete only what you're sure you never want.

## How to Delete a TikTok Video on Your Phone (5 Steps)

![Five steps to delete a TikTok video on your phone](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-tiktok-video-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile** in the bottom right.

### Step 2: Tap the Video

Tap the video or photo post you want to remove.

### Step 3: Open the Three-Dot Menu

Tap the **three dots** on the right side of the screen. On some versions, the menu is under the share arrow.

### Step 4: Tap Delete, Not Privacy

Scroll the row of options and tap **Delete** (the trash can icon). This is where people slip: **Privacy settings** and **Delete** sit close together. If you only want to hide the video, stop here and use Privacy settings instead.

### Step 5: Confirm

Tap **Delete** again to confirm. The post is gone from your profile, the For You feed and anywhere it was shared.

> **Pro tip:** Before you delete, save the video to your phone from the same menu if you have the option. Once it's deleted, the only copy is whatever you saved.

## How to Delete a TikTok Video on a Computer

1. Go to tiktok.com and sign in.
2. Open your profile and click the video.
3. Click the **…** menu next to the caption.
4. Click **Delete** and confirm.

## How to Unpost a TikTok Without Deleting It (Only Me)

1. Open the video and tap the **three dots**.
2. Tap **Privacy settings**.
3. Under **Who can watch this video**, choose **Only me**.

To bring it back, repeat the steps and choose **Everyone**. On some versions TikTok also lets you change privacy for many videos at once from the activity tools in settings. There's no "delete all" button at the time of writing.

## What Happens When You Delete a TikTok

| | After deleting |
| --- | --- |
| The video on your profile and For You | Gone |
| Views, likes and comments | Gone for good |
| Reposts and shared links | Stop working |
| Duets and stitches others made | May stay up, since those are their posts |
| Followers | Not notified |

### Deleting a Post You Just Uploaded

If you spot a typo right after posting, some versions of the app offer **Delete and re-edit** for recent uploads, which removes the post and sends the video back to the editor. Your caption and edits may not all carry over, so copy the caption first.

### Will Deleting and Reposting Help Views?

Usually not. Deleting removes whatever momentum the video had, and a reposted copy starts from zero. If a video stalls, check [why your TikTok isn't getting views](/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views) before deleting anything. Mass-deleting old videos also doesn't "reset" your account.

## Bulk-Delete Apps and Scripts (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to clear old TikToks compared with risky bulk-delete apps that need your login](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-tiktok-video-caution-1200x675.webp)

Since TikTok has no "delete all" button, some websites, browser extensions and scripts offer to wipe your profile in one go.

### The Real Risks

- **They need your login.** Handing over your password or session gives a stranger full control of your account.
- **Mass actions can look like spam** and may trigger limits on your account.
- **There's no undo.** A script that deletes the wrong videos can't put them back.

### A Safer Checklist

- Set videos to **Only me** in batches instead of deleting them.
- If you want everything gone, consider whether [deleting your TikTok account](/blog/how-to-delete-tiktok-account) is what you really mean.
- Never give an app or extension your TikTok password.

> **Bottom line:** Slow and manual is safe. Anything that deletes "everything at once" for you also gets access to everything.

## How to Stop Needing to Delete TikToks

Most deletes come from typos, the wrong cover or the wrong time. A few habits help:

![Habits that prevent deleting TikTok videos: check captions, post privately first, and fix posts before they go live](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-tiktok-video-prevent-1200x675.webp)

- **Check the caption before it goes live.** TikTok captions can run to 4,000 characters, and long ones hide mistakes. Our guide to the [TikTok caption limit](/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit) covers what shows before "more."

- **Fix it before it's published**, not after.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I recover a deleted TikTok video?

Not from TikTok's normal menus. Once deleted, the post and its stats are gone. If you saved the original file or a draft, you can post it again as a new video. Check [how to see TikTok drafts](/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-drafts) for unposted versions.

### Does deleting a TikTok notify anyone?

No. Followers aren't told. They'll just find the video missing.

### Why can't I delete a TikTok video?

The usual causes are a weak connection, an old app version or a video that's still processing. Update the app, wait a few minutes and try again. You can only delete your own videos.

### Does deleting a TikTok remove reposts and shares?

Yes, reposts and shared links stop working. Duets and stitches that other people made may remain on their accounts.

### How do I unpost a TikTok without deleting it?

Open the video, tap the three dots, tap Privacy settings and choose Only me. You can switch it back to Everyone later.

### Can I delete all my TikTok videos at once?

There's no "delete all" button at the time of writing. You can change privacy for many videos at once on some versions, or delete them one by one.

### Does deleting old TikToks reset my account?

No. Mass-deleting old videos doesn't reset how TikTok treats your account.

### Can I delete someone else's TikTok?

No. You can only delete your own videos. Report someone else's video if it breaks TikTok's rules.
