You manage more than one Instagram account, maybe your own brand plus a shop account, or five clients, and switching between them in the app is wearing thin. To connect multiple Instagram accounts to Schedule For You, make sure each one is a **Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page**, open the Accounts screen, choose Instagram, log in through Meta and **tick every account you want in one pass**.

![How to connect multiple Instagram accounts: professional accounts, one Page each, and one Meta login where you tick them all](/images/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts-hero-1200x675.webp)

Once connected, each account can have its own posts, or share one post with a caption version for each.

The catch is Meta's login window. It only shows accounts that meet every requirement, and it only remembers the ones you ticked. This guide covers the requirements, the exact steps, plan limits and workspaces, and how to fix an account that doesn't show up.

## Why Some Instagram Accounts Won't Connect: The 5 Requirements

![Instagram accounts that connect to a scheduler compared with ones that don't, like personal accounts and accounts with no Facebook Page](/images/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts-requirements-1200x675.webp)

Every account you want to connect needs to meet the same conditions. Check them before you start, because one missing link is the most common reason an account doesn't appear.

| Requirement | How to check |
|---|---|
| Business or Creator (professional) account | In the Instagram app, open Settings and look at your account type |
| Linked to a Facebook Page | Check the Page's linked accounts settings or Meta Business Suite |
| You have full control of that Page | Look at the people with access in the Page's settings |
| Logged in to the right Facebook account | The Meta window only shows Pages that login can manage |
| Room on your plan | Free connects 3 accounts, Solo 15, Studio 100 |

### What Most People Don't Know

Each Instagram professional account links to one Facebook Page. If you manage five brands, you'll normally have five Pages, each linked to its own Instagram account. If a brand doesn't have a Page yet, create a simple one. It doesn't need to be active; it just has to exist and be linked.

Personal accounts can't be connected to any scheduler that uses Instagram's official publishing system, because Meta only gives publishing access to professional accounts linked to a Page. Switching to a professional account is free and takes a minute in the app.

> **Key point:** Meta's login window only offers the Pages and Instagram accounts that the Facebook login you're using can fully manage. If an account is missing, the cause is almost always on Meta's side, not the scheduler's.

If you're setting up a single account for the first time, our guide on [how to connect Instagram](/blog/connect-instagram) explains how to switch to a professional account and link a Page in detail.

## How to Connect Multiple Instagram Accounts in One Go (8 Steps)

![Four steps to connect several Instagram accounts: open Accounts, choose Instagram, tick every account in Meta's window, then confirm](/images/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts-steps-1200x675.webp)

Because you approve all your Pages in one pass, connecting ten accounts takes about as long as connecting one. Meta's screens change from time to time, so look for the closest match if a label is worded differently.

### Step 1: Sign In and Choose the Workspace

If you keep brands or clients separate, open the workspace these accounts belong to first. Accounts connect to the workspace you're in.

### Step 2: Open the Accounts Screen and Choose Instagram

The Accounts screen is where you add and manage connected accounts. Choose Instagram and a Meta login window opens.

### Step 3: Log In With the Facebook Account That Controls the Pages

Use the Facebook account that has full control of the Pages linked to your Instagram accounts. If you're already logged in, check it's the right one.

### Step 4: Tick Every Page and Instagram Account, Not Just the First

Meta asks which Pages and Instagram accounts the app can access. Tick all the ones you plan to schedule for now. This is the step people get wrong: anything left unticked won't be available later until you run the connection again.

> **Pro tip:** Tick everything you might schedule for in the next few months, even if you'll only add some accounts today. You still choose which ones to connect in the next step.

### Step 5: Leave Every Permission Turned On

Meta lists the permissions the app needs. Leave them all on. Turning one off can stop posts from publishing.

### Step 6: Choose Which Accounts to Add

Back in Schedule For You, pick the Instagram accounts to connect to this workspace.

### Step 7: Confirm

The accounts appear on the Accounts screen and in the composer.

### Step 8: Send a Test Post

Schedule one post to each new account a few minutes ahead. It's the quickest way to confirm the connection and permissions are right.

### Accounts Managed by Different Facebook Logins

If some accounts sit under another person's Facebook login, such as a client's, the cleanest fix is for the owner to give your Facebook account full control of their Page in Meta Business Suite. You can then select it the next time you run the connection. Don't ask clients for their passwords.

## Separate Workspaces for Brands and Clients (Agency Setup)

If you manage Instagram for several brands or clients, you can give each its own workspace. A workspace keeps that brand's connected accounts, queue and calendar together, so you don't post a client's content to the wrong account by accident.

Each workspace has its own plan and its own account limit, so a client in a separate workspace is billed as a separate subscription. To keep several brands on one plan, connect them in the same workspace.

| Setup | Best for | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| One workspace, many accounts | Your own brands, regional accounts, a main account plus a shop account | Everyone with access sees every account |
| One workspace per client | Agencies and freelancers with separate clients | Each workspace has its own plan |

On the paid plans, each workspace can also use approvals and client preview links, so clients can approve or comment on upcoming posts without creating an account.

## Account Doesn't Appear? 7 Fixes That Work

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| It's still a personal account | Switch it to Business or Creator in the app, then reconnect | Only professional accounts get publishing access |
| It isn't linked to a Facebook Page | Link it in the Page's settings or Meta Business Suite (look for "Linked accounts" or "Instagram"), then reconnect | Meta finds Instagram accounts through their Page |
| You didn't tick it in Meta's dialog | Run the connection again, choose to edit your previous settings and tick it, keeping the others ticked | Meta remembers your earlier choices |
| Logged in to the wrong Facebook account | Log out of Facebook in that browser, or use a private window, and connect again | The Meta window uses whichever login your browser has |
| You don't have full control of the Page | Ask someone with full control to upgrade your access, including in the business portfolio | Partial or task-only access can't grant publishing permissions |
| You've reached your plan's limit | Remove accounts you no longer use, or upgrade | Extra Pages are skipped at the limit |
| A connected account stopped posting | Reconnect from the Accounts screen and retry the post | Password changes, security checks and removed permissions end connections |

You'll get a failed-post alert if a connection breaks. See the [session invalid error guide](/blog/instagram-session-invalid-error) for details.

## Password-Sharing and Unofficial Login Tools (Use With Caution)

Some tools ask you to type each Instagram username and password into their own form instead of using Meta's login window. Some agencies also collect client passwords in a spreadsheet so anyone on the team can log in.

### The Real Risks

- **Full control goes to whoever holds the password.** They can post, message and change settings as the account.
- **Unfamiliar logins can trigger security checks.** Logins from new servers can look like a takeover and lead to locked accounts.
- **Clients lose track of who has access.** When someone leaves the team, the password stays with them unless it's changed.

### A Safer Checklist

- Connect through Meta's own login window, which shows you exactly which Pages and accounts you're granting.
- Ask clients to give your Facebook account access to their Page instead of sharing a password.
- Turn on two-factor authentication on every account you manage.
- Review connected apps in each account's security settings now and then, and remove what you don't recognize.

> **Bottom line:** Meta's official connection gives you the same publishing access without anyone typing a password into a third-party form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Instagram accounts can I connect to Schedule For You?

As many as your plan allows: 3 connected accounts on Free, 15 on Solo and 100 on Studio, counting every platform. There's no separate Instagram limit.

### Do I need a separate Facebook Page for each Instagram account?

Yes. Each Instagram professional account must be linked to its own Facebook Page to connect to a scheduler. If a brand has no Page, create a basic one and link it.

### Can I connect multiple Instagram accounts with one Facebook login?

Yes, as long as that Facebook account has full control of each linked Page. Select all the Pages and Instagram accounts in Meta's dialog during one connection.

### Why is my Instagram account not showing up when I connect?

Usually it's a personal account, it isn't linked to a Facebook Page, it wasn't ticked in Meta's dialog, or you're logged in to the wrong Facebook account. Fix the cause and run the connection again.

### Can I post the same content to several Instagram accounts at once?

Yes. Select each account in one post and they all publish it. For accounts that share an audience, edit the caption for each so followers of both don't see the exact same words twice.

### Can I connect a personal Instagram account?

No. Meta only allows scheduling tools to publish to Business or Creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page. Switching is free in the Instagram app.

### What happens to extra accounts if I downgrade?

Your oldest accounts keep publishing and the newest ones past the new limit are paused until you upgrade or disconnect some. Your posts and drafts stay.

### Do I need my clients' Instagram passwords?

No. Ask them to give your Facebook account full control of their Page in Meta Business Suite, then connect through Meta's login window.
