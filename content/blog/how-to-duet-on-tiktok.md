You've found a TikTok you want to react to, sing along with or answer, side by side. To do a duet on TikTok, open the video, tap the **Share** arrow, then tap **Duet**. TikTok opens the camera with the original next to yours. Choose a layout, record your half (or upload a clip), add a caption and post.

![How to duet on TikTok: tap Share, tap Duet, pick a layout, record and post](/images/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Your duet appears on your profile and links back to the original creator. If you don't see Duet, it isn't a bug: the creator has turned duets off for that video, the video is from a private account, or your account is under the age limit.

This guide covers the six steps, the split-screen layouts, duetting with friends and camera-roll clips, why Duet goes missing, and how to control who can duet your own videos.

## What a TikTok Duet Actually Is (and Why the Button Hides)

![A duet plays your video next to the original at the same time, while a stitch plays a clip of the original first](/images/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok-duet-vs-stitch-1200x675.webp)

Duet is TikTok's split-screen feature. Your video plays next to the original at the same time, using the original's sound, and the post credits and links to the creator.

| | Duet | Stitch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How it plays | Side by side, at the same time | A short clip of the original first, then your video |
| Sound | The original's sound, which you can turn down | Your own after the clip |
| Best for | Reactions, sing-alongs, harmonies, play-alongs | Answering a question or adding a punchline |
| Credit | Links to the original creator | Links to the original creator |

### What Most People Don't Know

Duet is a permission, not a fixed button. Every creator decides who can duet each video, and TikTok also limits Duet by age. So a missing button usually tells you something about the video or your account, not about your app.

> **Key point:** If Duet is missing on one video but not others, the creator turned it off. If it's missing everywhere, check your app version and your account's age setting.

## How to Duet on TikTok, Step by Step (6 Steps)

![The TikTok duet steps grouped in three stages: tap Share then Duet, pick a layout and record, then edit and post](/images/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something reads differently.

### Step 1: Tap the Share Arrow

Find the video and tap the **Share** arrow on the right.

### Step 2: Tap Duet in the Bottom Row

**Duet** sits in the bottom row of options, next to Stitch and other actions.

### Step 3: Choose a Layout Before You Record

Tap **Layout** to choose how the two videos sit together. See the table below.

### Step 4: Record Your Half

Tap the record button and react, sing along or add your part. The original plays as you record.

### Step 5: Edit, Then Tap Next

Tap the check mark, add effects, text or sounds if you like, then tap **Next**.

### Step 6: Caption, Choose Who Sees It, Post

Write a caption, choose who can see it, and tap **Post**.

> **Pro tip:** Your duet keeps the original video's sound, and it can drown you out. Lower the original's volume in the editing screen before you post. That's the step most people skip, and it's why so many duets are hard to hear.

## TikTok Duet Layouts: Which Split Screen to Pick

At the time of writing the layout options include:

| Layout | What it looks like | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left and right | Your video beside the original, the classic split screen | Sing-alongs, side-by-side comparisons |
| Top and bottom | Stacked | A reaction under a tutorial |
| React | The original full-screen, with you in a small movable window | Commentary where the original matters most |
| Green screen | The original as your background | Talking over the original |

Names and options shift between app versions, but they're all under **Layout** on the duet camera.

## How to Duet With Friends, Chains and Camera-Roll Clips

- **Duet a friend's video.** It works the same as any duet, as long as they allow duets.
- **Duet a duet.** You can duet a video that's already a duet, making a chain where more people appear in turn.
- **Record together in person.** If you want two people in one shot, film together on one phone. That's a normal video, not a duet.
- **Use a video from your camera roll.** On the duet camera, tap **Upload** to use a clip you've already recorded instead of filming live.

## Why Can't I Duet on TikTok? 5 Causes and Fixes

![The most common reasons the Duet button is missing on TikTok: duets turned off, a private account or an age limit](/images/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok-missing-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The creator turned duets off | Use Stitch if it's allowed, or ask the creator | Creators choose who can duet each video |
| The video is private or from a private account | Nothing to fix; duet only works where allowed | The creator controls access |
| Your account is under the age limit | Wait until you're eligible | Accounts under 16 can't duet at the time of writing |
| The post isn't a video | Find a video post instead | Photo posts and some LIVE replays don't offer Duet |
| Out-of-date app | Update TikTok and restart it | Older versions can hide newer options |

## How to Turn Duets On or Off for Your Own Videos

Go to **Profile > menu > Settings and privacy > Privacy > Duet** and choose who can duet your videos: everyone, people you follow back, or only you.

You can also change it for a single video from that video's **...** menu under **Privacy settings**. That's useful when you want duets on most videos but not on one personal post.

## Reuploading Someone's Video Instead of Dueting (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to react to TikTok videos, like using Duet, next to risky ones like reuploading someone else's video](/images/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok-safety-1200x675.webp)

When Duet is turned off, some people download the video, edit it into their own split screen with another app, and post it as if it were a duet.

### The Real Risks

- **The creator said no.** Turning off Duet is a clear signal they don't want their video reused that way.
- **Copyright and reused-content rules.** Reposting someone else's video can get your post removed, and repeated reports can affect your account. Our guide to [TikTok music copyright](/blog/tiktok-music-copyright) covers how this works for sound.
- **No credit or link.** A fake duet doesn't link to the original, so the creator loses the credit a real duet gives them.

### A Quick Safety Checklist

- Use TikTok's own Duet or Stitch whenever they're available.
- If they're off, ask the creator for permission.
- Only reuse your own footage outside TikTok.

> **Bottom line:** If the button isn't there, the creator decided. Respect it or ask.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you do a duet on TikTok?

Open the video, tap Share, tap Duet, choose a layout, record your side, then post.

### Can I duet with a video from my camera roll?

Yes. On the duet camera, tap Upload and pick a clip from your gallery.

### Why is there no Duet button on a video?

The creator has turned duets off, the account is private, your account is under the age limit, or the post is a photo post.

### Does the original creator get notified of a duet?

Yes. Creators are notified when someone duets their video, and your duet credits and links to the original.

### What's the difference between a duet and a stitch?

A duet plays your video next to the original at the same time. A stitch plays a short clip of the original first, then your video.

### Can I duet with two people?

Yes, in two ways. Duet a video that's already a duet to make a chain, or film two people together on one phone as a normal video.

### How do I stop people from duetting my videos?

Go to Settings and privacy, then Privacy, then Duet, and choose only you. You can also change it per video from its privacy settings.

### Can I schedule a duet?

Not from outside TikTok. Duets are made with TikTok's own camera, so you post them from the app.
