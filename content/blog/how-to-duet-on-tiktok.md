To do a duet on TikTok, open the video you want to duet, tap the **Share** arrow, then tap **Duet**. TikTok opens the camera with the original video next to yours. Choose a layout, record your half (or upload a clip), add a caption and post. Your duet appears on your profile and links back to the original creator.

If you don't see Duet, the creator has turned duets off for that video, the video is from a private account, or your account is under the age limit.

## How to duet on TikTok, step by step

1. Find the video and tap the **Share** arrow on the right.
2. Tap **Duet** in the bottom row of options.
3. Tap **Layout** to choose how the two videos sit together (see below).
4. Tap the record button and react, sing along or add your part. The original plays as you record.
5. Tap the check mark, add effects, text or sounds if you like, then tap **Next**.
6. Write a caption, choose who can see it, and tap **Post**.

Your duet keeps the original video's sound. You can lower the original's volume in the editing screen if you want your voice to come through.

## Split-screen layouts

Duet is TikTok's split-screen feature. At the time of writing the layout options include:

| Layout | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Left and right | Your video beside the original, the classic split screen |
| Top and bottom | Stacked, useful for showing a reaction under a tutorial |
| React | The original full-screen, with you in a small movable window |
| Green screen | The original as your background |

Names and options shift between app versions, but they're all under Layout on the duet camera.

## How to duet with others

- **Duet a friend's video.** It works the same as any duet, as long as they allow duets.
- **Duet a duet.** You can duet a video that's already a duet, making a chain where more people appear in turn.
- **Record together in person.** If you want two people in one shot, simply film together on one phone. That's a normal video, not a duet.
- **Use a video from your camera roll.** On the duet camera, tap **Upload** to use a clip you've already recorded instead of filming live.

## Why can't I duet on TikTok?

- **The creator turned duets off.** Creators choose who can duet each video, and some turn it off entirely.
- **The video is private or from a private account.** Duet only works on videos the creator allows.
- **Age restrictions.** TikTok limits Duet for younger users; accounts under 16 can't duet at the time of writing.
- **The post isn't a video.** Photo posts and some LIVE replays don't offer Duet.
- **Out-of-date app.** Update TikTok and restart it.

## How to turn duets on or off for your own videos

Go to **Profile > menu > Settings and privacy > Privacy > Duet** and choose who can duet your videos: everyone, people you follow back, or only you. You can also change it for a single video from that video's **...** menu under **Privacy settings**.

## Posting your duets elsewhere

Duets are created inside TikTok, so they're posted from the TikTok app. If you also want the reaction on Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts, save a copy of your own side and publish it there. Schedule For You can schedule finished videos to TikTok, Reels and Shorts from one queue; it doesn't create duets or stitches, which need TikTok's own camera.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you do a duet on TikTok?
Open the video, tap Share, tap Duet, choose a layout, record your side, then post.

### Can I duet with a video from my camera roll?
Yes. On the duet camera, tap Upload and pick a clip from your gallery.

### Why is there no Duet button on a video?
The creator has turned duets off, the account is private, your account is under the age limit, or the post is a photo post.

### Does the original creator get notified of a duet?
Yes, creators are notified when someone duets their video, and your duet credits and links to the original.

### What's the difference between a duet and a stitch?
A duet plays your video next to the original at the same time. A stitch plays a short clip of the original first, then your video.
