What is shadowban on TikTok? It's when your videos stop being shown to new people, usually in the For You feed, without TikTok telling you directly. TikTok doesn't use the word "shadowban". It says some videos, and sometimes whole accounts, are **ineligible for the For You feed**, and it lists violations and restrictions on your **Account status** page. So the quickest check is to open Account status, then look at how much of your traffic still comes from For You in your video analytics.

This guide explains what a shadowban looks like in practice, how to check for one, how long it tends to last, and what to do about it.

## What is a shadowban on TikTok?

When people ask "what is shadow banned on TikTok", they usually mean one or more of these:

- **Videos stall at a few views** even though you posted at your usual time.
- **For You traffic drops to almost nothing**, while views from your profile and followers carry on.
- **Your account is hard to find** when someone who doesn't follow you searches your username.
- **New uploads sit at 0 views** for hours.

What TikTok actually does is closer to a recommendation limit than a hidden ban. Under its Community Guidelines, content can stay on the platform but not be recommended in the For You feed: for example videos that are borderline, unoriginal, low quality, or that show risky behavior. If an account repeatedly posts content like this, TikTok says the account itself can become temporarily ineligible for recommendation and harder to find in search. Your followers can usually still see your videos; it's the reach to new viewers that falls.

That's why shadowbanning on TikTok feels invisible. Your video is live, you can watch it, your friends can watch it, but the For You feed has stopped distributing it.

## How to check if you're shadowbanned on TikTok

### 1. Open Account status

At the time of writing, go to **Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Account → Account status**. This page lists guideline violations, any features you can't use (such as going LIVE or posting comments), and whether content or your account has been restricted. If it shows "No violations" or "Good standing", an account-wide restriction is unlikely.

This is the only check that comes from TikTok itself, so it counts more than anything else here.

### 2. Look for "ineligible for the For You feed" notices

A video that TikTok won't recommend may carry a notice in its details or in the notification TikTok sent you, saying it isn't eligible for the For You feed and naming the guideline. Open your most recent videos and check for a banner or label, and look in your inbox under system or activity notifications.

If only one or two videos are flagged, the problem is those videos, not your whole account.

### 3. Check your For You traffic share

Open a recent video, tap the analytics (the bar chart or "More data" in TikTok Studio at the time of writing) and find **Traffic source**. Normally a large share of views on a public video comes from "For You". If several new videos show close to 0% from For You, while "Personal profile" and "Following" make up nearly everything, your videos aren't being recommended.

One quiet video means very little. A pattern across a week of uploads is worth investigating.

### 4. Search for yourself from another account

Ask a friend who doesn't follow you to search your exact @username. If your account doesn't come up, or your recent videos don't appear when they search a phrase from your caption, that supports a limit. New accounts can take a while to show in search, so don't read too much into this alone.

### 5. Rule out processing and privacy

Videos stuck at 0 views are sometimes still processing or under review; check again after a few hours. Also confirm the video's audience is set to Everyone, your account isn't private, and the video isn't restricted by age settings. ## What causes a TikTok shadowban?

TikTok doesn't always explain why a video isn't recommended, but these are the usual reasons:

- **Guideline violations.** Nudity, dangerous acts, hate speech, harassment, misleading health claims and similar content can be removed or kept out of For You.
- **Unoriginal or reposted content.** Re-uploads of other people's videos, compilations with no added value, and clips with another app's watermark tend not to be recommended.
- **Spam-like behavior.** Mass following and unfollowing, copy-paste comments, posting the same video several times, or running many accounts with duplicate content.
- **Bots and fake engagement.** Bought followers, likes or views, and apps that log in with your password to automate actions.
- **Misleading or engagement-bait content.** "Follow for part 2" with no part 2, fake giveaways, or links pushing people off the platform aggressively.
- **Copyrighted music in some contexts.** Business accounts that use tracks outside the Commercial Music Library may have audio muted or reach limited.
- **Age and safety signals.** Content that appears to involve minors in risky situations, or a creator who seems to be under the minimum age, gets restricted quickly.

Scheduling isn't on that list. Posting your own videos through TikTok's official API at a normal pace is a standard use; our guide to [social media posting bots](/blog/social-media-posting-bots) explains where the line between scheduling and rule-breaking automation sits.

## How long does a shadowban last on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a duration for For You restrictions, so anyone who quotes an exact number is guessing. What's known:

- **It depends on the cause.** A single video that's ineligible for For You stays that way unless you appeal and win, but it doesn't drag down your next upload by itself. An account-level limit after repeated violations is temporary, and its length isn't published.
- **People commonly report about two weeks.** Creators often say reach came back after roughly 14 days of posting clean content. Treat that as anecdote, not a rule. Some recover in days, some take longer.
- **Strikes expire.** TikTok's strike system removes strikes from your account after a period TikTok sets, and Account status shows your current strikes. Severe violations can lead to a ban without a warning period.

If Account status is clear and your For You traffic is low anyway, there may be no restriction at all. Reach on TikTok swings a lot from video to video, and a weaker hook or a less popular topic can look just like a shadowban.

## How to recover from a TikTok shadowban

1. **Read Account status first.** If it names a video or rule, start there. Don't guess.
2. **Appeal anything you think is wrong.** Tap the violation in Account status, or the notice on the video, and choose to appeal. Add a short, factual reason. TikTok reviews appeals and reverses some decisions; timing varies.
3. **Delete or edit clear violations.** If a video does break a guideline, remove it rather than leaving it up.
4. **Stop the spam signals.** Uninstall follower or view apps, revoke third-party access you don't recognize, stop reposting the same video, and pause mass follow, like or comment sprees.
5. **Post original videos at a steady pace.** Don't delete and re-upload the same video hoping it does better, and don't post ten videos in an hour. One to a few strong, original videos a day is plenty.
6. **Watch For You traffic, not just views.** When the For You share in Traffic source climbs back on new uploads, the limit is lifting.
7. **Ignore "unban" services.** Anyone selling a shadowban fix or asking for your login is running a scam. Only TikTok can change your account status, and appeals are free.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is shadowban on TikTok?

It's the common name for when TikTok stops recommending your videos in the For You feed, so views from new people collapse while the videos stay online. TikTok calls this being ineligible for the For You feed, and lists restrictions on the Account status page.

### How do I know if I'm shadowbanned on TikTok?

Check Settings and privacy → Account → Account status for violations or restrictions, look for an "ineligible for the For You feed" notice on recent videos, and check whether For You has dropped to near 0% in each video's Traffic source.

### How long does a shadowban last on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a duration. Creators commonly report around two weeks of posting clean content, but it depends on the cause. Strikes expire after a period TikTok sets, and appeals can lift a restriction sooner if TikTok agrees it was a mistake.

### Is there a TikTok shadow ban checker that works?

No outside site can see TikTok's systems, so automated checkers are guesses. A guided test like our [shadowban checker](/tools/shadowban-checker) walks you through the checks you can run yourself, with Account status as the deciding one. Never give a checker your password.

### Can deleting videos fix a TikTok shadowban?

Deleting a video that breaks the guidelines can help, but mass-deleting and re-uploading content tends to look like spam. Remove clear violations, appeal mistakes, and keep posting original videos.

### Does scheduling TikTok videos cause a shadowban?

No. Scheduling your own videos through TikTok's official API is normal. Reach problems come from guideline issues, reposted content, bursts of duplicate uploads and fake engagement, not from when or how a video was published.
