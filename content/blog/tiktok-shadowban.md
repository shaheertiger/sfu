Your last five TikToks stalled at a few hundred views, when you used to get thousands. What is shadowban on TikTok? It's when your videos stop being shown to new people, usually in the For You feed, without TikTok telling you directly. TikTok doesn't use the word "shadowban". It says some videos, and sometimes whole accounts, are **ineligible for the For You feed**, and it lists violations and restrictions on your **Account status** page.

![What is shadowban on TikTok: check Account status first, then your For You traffic share](/images/blog/tiktok-shadowban-hero-1200x675.webp)

So the quickest check is to open Account status, then look at how much of your traffic still comes from For You in your video analytics. If both look normal, you're probably not restricted at all.

This guide explains what a shadowban looks like in practice, five checks that work, what causes it, how long it lasts, and a step-by-step recovery plan.

## What Is a Shadowban on TikTok (and What TikTok Actually Calls It)?

When people ask "what is shadow banned on TikTok", they usually mean one or more of these:

![Signs of a normal TikTok account compared with signs that videos aren't being recommended](/images/blog/tiktok-shadowban-signs-1200x675.webp)

| What you notice | Normal variation | Possible For You restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views | One or two weak videos | Videos stall at a few views, week after week |
| Traffic source | For You makes up a large share | For You close to 0% on several new videos |
| Search | Non-followers find your @username | Your account doesn't come up |
| New uploads | Views start within an hour or two | Uploads sit at 0 views for hours |
| Account status | No violations, good standing | Violations or restrictions listed |

What TikTok actually does is closer to a recommendation limit than a hidden ban. Under its Community Guidelines, content can stay on the platform but not be recommended in the For You feed: for example videos that are borderline, unoriginal, low quality, or that show risky behavior. If an account repeatedly posts content like this, TikTok says the account itself can become temporarily ineligible for recommendation and harder to find in search.

### What Most People Don't Know

Your followers can usually still see your videos during a restriction; it's the reach to new viewers that falls. That's why shadowbanning on TikTok feels invisible: your video is live, your friends can watch it, but the For You feed has stopped distributing it. And the reverse trap is common too: reach on TikTok swings a lot from video to video, and a weaker hook or a less popular topic can look just like a shadowban.

> **Key point:** Account status is the only check that comes from TikTok itself. If it says "No violations" or "Good standing", an account-wide restriction is unlikely, and the fix is probably the videos, not an appeal.

## How to Check If You're Shadowbanned on TikTok: 5 Checks

Menu names in TikTok change between app versions. If a label doesn't match, search Settings or look for the closest one.

![Five checks for a TikTok shadowban, starting with Account status](/images/blog/tiktok-shadowban-checks-1200x675.webp)

### Check 1: Open Account Status (the Only Official Answer)

At the time of writing, go to **Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Account → Account status**. This page lists guideline violations, any features you can't use (such as going LIVE or posting comments), and whether content or your account has been restricted.

### Check 2: Look for "Ineligible for the For You Feed" Notices

A video that TikTok won't recommend may carry a notice in its details or in the notification TikTok sent you, saying it isn't eligible for the For You feed and naming the guideline. Open your most recent videos and check for a banner or label, and look in your inbox under system or activity notifications. If only one or two videos are flagged, the problem is those videos, not your whole account.

### Check 3: Check Your For You Traffic Share, Not Just Views

Open a recent video, tap the analytics (the bar chart or "More data" in TikTok Studio at the time of writing) and find **Traffic source**. Normally a large share of views on a public video comes from "For You". If several new videos show close to 0% from For You, while "Personal profile" and "Following" make up nearly everything, your videos aren't being recommended.

> **Pro tip:** The mistake people make is judging by total views. One quiet video means very little. Write down the For You percentage for your last seven uploads; a pattern across a week is what's worth investigating.

### Check 4: Search for Yourself From Another Account

Ask a friend who doesn't follow you to search your exact @username. If your account doesn't come up, or your recent videos don't appear when they search a phrase from your caption, that supports a limit. New accounts can take a while to show in search, so don't read too much into this alone.

### Check 5: Rule Out Processing and Privacy

Videos stuck at 0 views are sometimes still processing or under review; check again after a few hours. Also confirm the video's audience is set to Everyone, your account isn't private, and the video isn't restricted by age settings. ## How Long Does a Shadowban Last on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a duration for For You restrictions, so anyone who quotes an exact number is guessing. What's known:

- **It depends on the cause.** A single video that's ineligible for For You stays that way unless you appeal and win, but it doesn't drag down your next upload by itself. An account-level limit after repeated violations is temporary, and its length isn't published.
- **People commonly report about two weeks.** Creators often say reach came back after roughly 14 days of posting clean content. Treat that as anecdote, not a rule. Some recover in days, some take longer.
- **Strikes expire.** TikTok's strike system removes strikes from your account after a period TikTok sets, and Account status shows your current strikes. Severe violations can lead to a ban without a warning period.

## "Unban" Services and Follower Apps (Use With Caution)

When reach drops, you'll find services that promise to remove a shadowban, and apps that promise followers or views to "restart" the algorithm.

### The Real Risks

- **Only TikTok can change your account status.** Anyone selling a shadowban fix is running a scam, and appeals are free.
- **Follower and view apps are fake engagement.** They're one of the causes of restrictions, not a cure.
- **Login requests.** Apps that ask for your password can take over the account.

### Safety Checklist

- Uninstall follower or view apps.
- Revoke third-party access you don't recognize.
- Appeal only through Account status or the notice on the video.

> **Bottom line:** Account status and your own analytics are the only reliable sources. Anything that promises a shortcut usually makes the restriction worse.

## How to Recover From a TikTok Shadowban: 7-Step Plan

![Four moves to recover reach on TikTok: read Account status, appeal or fix, stop the spam signals, post steadily](/images/blog/tiktok-shadowban-recovery-1200x675.webp)

1. **Read Account status first.** If it names a video or rule, start there. Don't guess.
2. **Appeal anything you think is wrong.** Tap the violation in Account status, or the notice on the video, and choose to appeal. Add a short, factual reason. TikTok reviews appeals and reverses some decisions; timing varies.
3. **Delete or edit clear violations.** If a video does break a guideline, remove it rather than leaving it up.
4. **Stop the spam signals.** Uninstall follower or view apps, revoke third-party access you don't recognize, stop reposting the same video, and pause mass follow, like or comment sprees.
5. **Post original videos at a steady pace.** Don't delete and re-upload the same video hoping it does better, and don't post ten videos in an hour. One to a few strong, original videos a day is plenty.
6. **Watch For You traffic, not just views.** When the For You share in Traffic source climbs back on new uploads, the limit is lifting.
7. **Ignore "unban" services.** Only TikTok can change your account status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is shadowban on TikTok?

It's the common name for when TikTok stops recommending your videos in the For You feed, so views from new people collapse while the videos stay online. TikTok calls this being ineligible for the For You feed, and lists restrictions on the Account status page.

### How do I know if I'm shadowbanned on TikTok?

Check Settings and privacy → Account → Account status for violations or restrictions, look for an "ineligible for the For You feed" notice on recent videos, and check whether For You has dropped to near 0% in each video's Traffic source.

### How long does a shadowban last on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a duration. Creators commonly report around two weeks of posting clean content, but it depends on the cause. Strikes expire after a period TikTok sets, and appeals can lift a restriction sooner if TikTok agrees it was a mistake.

### Is there a TikTok shadow ban checker that works?

No outside site can see TikTok's systems, so automated checkers are guesses. A guided test like our [shadowban checker](/tools/shadowban-checker) walks you through the checks you can run yourself, with Account status as the deciding one. Never give a checker your password.

### Can deleting videos fix a TikTok shadowban?

Deleting a video that breaks the guidelines can help, but mass-deleting and re-uploading content tends to look like spam. Remove clear violations, appeal mistakes, and keep posting original videos.

### Does scheduling TikTok videos cause a shadowban?

No. Scheduling your own videos through TikTok's official API is normal. Reach problems come from guideline issues, reposted content, bursts of duplicate uploads and fake engagement, not from when or how a video was published.

### Can followers still see my videos if I'm shadowbanned?

Usually, yes. A For You restriction mainly cuts reach to new viewers; your followers can still find your videos on your profile and in their Following feed.

### How do I appeal a TikTok restriction?

Tap the violation in Account status, or the notice on the video, and choose to appeal. Add a short, factual reason. Appeals are free.
