To change your profile picture on TikTok, tap **Profile**, then **Edit profile**, then **Change photo** (tap your current picture). Choose **Take photo** or **Upload photo**, crop the image to the circle and tap **Save** or **Confirm**. The new picture shows on your profile straight away, though it can take a little while to update everywhere for other people.

## Change your TikTok profile picture on iPhone or Android

1. Open TikTok and tap **Profile** in the bottom right.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap your current profile photo, or **Change photo**.
4. Choose **Take photo** to use the camera, or **Upload photo** / **Select from gallery** to pick an image.
5. Drag and pinch to fit it in the circle.
6. Tap **Save** (or **Confirm**).

If TikTok asks for photo access, allow it, or choose **Select photos** on iPhone to share only the image you want.

## Change it on a computer

1. Go to tiktok.com and log in.
2. Click your profile picture in the top right, then **View profile**.
3. Click **Edit profile**.
4. Click the pencil icon on your photo and upload an image from your computer.
5. Adjust the crop and click **Apply**, then **Save**.

## Best size and format

| Setting | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Square, shown as a circle |
| Minimum size | 200 × 200 pixels |
| Good size | 400 × 400 to 1080 × 1080 pixels |
| Format | JPG or PNG |
| What fits | Keep faces and logos in the center; the corners get cut off |

TikTok shows your picture very small in comments and the For You feed, so pick something that still reads at the size of a fingertip: a close-up of your face, or a simple logo on a plain background. Our [TikTok photo resizer](/tools/tiktok-photo-resizer) crops images to the right shape before you upload.

TikTok used to let you set a short **profile video** instead of a photo. That option has disappeared from most accounts; if you don't see it, it isn't available on your version of the app.

## Why your new TikTok profile picture isn't showing

- **It's still updating.** Close and reopen the app. Other people may see the old one for a while because of caching.
- **The upload was rejected.** Images that break TikTok's Community Guidelines, such as nudity, violence or someone else's copyrighted logo, can be removed and replaced with the default avatar.
- **You recently changed it.** TikTok sometimes limits how often you can change profile details in a short period. Wait a day and try again.
- **The app is out of date.** Update TikTok from the App Store or Google Play.
- **Slow connection.** Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data and try again.

If none of that works, see our guide to [fixing TikTok when it isn't working](/blog/is-tiktok-down).

## Tips for a profile picture that works

- **Use the same picture on every platform.** People recognize you faster when your TikTok, Instagram and YouTube look alike. It helps cross-promotion.
- **Brands: use the logo mark, not the full wordmark.** Long text is unreadable at small sizes.
- **High contrast.** A bright background stands out in a busy comments section.
- **Update the rest of your profile at the same time.** A clear name and bio help people decide to follow. Try the [TikTok bio generator](/tools/tiktok-bio-generator) for ideas.

If you manage several brand accounts, keeping profile pictures consistent makes it obvious which account is which in a scheduler. In Schedule For You, each connected account shows its own profile picture, so you can see at a glance where a post is going.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my TikTok profile picture without anyone knowing?
Yes. TikTok doesn't notify your followers when you change your profile picture. People will simply see the new one next time they view your profile or comments.

### Can I remove my TikTok profile picture?
There's no remove button at the time of writing. To go back to a plain look, upload a blank or simple image instead.

### Why is my TikTok profile picture blurry?
The image was too small or heavily compressed. Upload a square image of at least 400 × 400 pixels, and avoid screenshots of screenshots.

### Can I use a GIF or video as my TikTok profile picture?
GIFs aren't supported, and profile videos have been removed from most accounts. Use a still JPG or PNG.
