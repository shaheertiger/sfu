You've got a better photo, a new logo or just a fresh look, and your TikTok still shows the old picture. To change your profile picture on TikTok, tap **Profile**, then **Edit profile**, then **Change photo** (or tap your current picture). Choose **Take photo** or **Upload photo**, fit the image in the circle and tap **Save** or **Confirm**.

![How to change your TikTok profile picture: Profile, Edit profile, Change photo, then crop and save](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

The new picture shows on your profile straight away, though it can take a little while to update everywhere for other people. That delay is what makes most people think the change failed.

This guide covers the steps on iPhone, Android and a computer, the size and format that stay sharp, and what to check when your new picture won't show.

## Why Your TikTok Profile Picture Matters More Than You Think

![Profile pictures that work on TikTok, like close-ups and simple logos, compared with ones that don't, like group shots and long wordmarks](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-tiktok-what-works-1200x675.webp)

Your profile picture appears next to every comment you leave, on your videos in the For You feed and in search results. It's shown very small, often about the size of a fingertip, so a photo that looks great full-screen can turn into a blur.

### What Most People Don't Know

TikTok crops your picture to a circle, so anything in the corners of a square image gets cut off. Faces and logos placed off-center are the most common reason a new picture looks wrong.

> **Key point:** Pick something that still reads at fingertip size: a close-up of your face, or a simple logo on a plain background. Keep it in the center of a square image.

## How to Change Your TikTok Profile Picture on iPhone or Android (6 Steps)

![Three steps to change a TikTok profile picture in the app: open Edit profile, choose a photo, fit it in the circle and save](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Tap Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile** in the bottom right.

### Step 2: Tap Edit Profile

It's the button under your name and follower count.

### Step 3: Tap Your Current Photo or Change Photo

Either opens the photo options.

### Step 4: Choose Take Photo or Upload Photo

**Take photo** opens the camera. **Upload photo** or **Select from gallery** lets you pick an existing image. If TikTok asks for photo access, allow it, or choose **Select photos** on iPhone to share only the image you want.

### Step 5: Drag and Pinch to Fit the Circle

This is the step people rush. Center your face or logo, and check nothing important sits near the edge.

> **Pro tip:** Crop the image to a square before you upload. Our [TikTok photo resizer](/tools/tiktok-photo-resizer) does it in your browser, so you only need a small adjustment inside TikTok.

### Step 6: Tap Save or Confirm

The new picture appears on your profile straight away.

## How to Change Your TikTok Profile Picture on a Computer (Web)

1. Go to tiktok.com and log in.
2. Click your profile picture in the top right, then **View profile**.
3. Click **Edit profile**.
4. Click the pencil icon on your photo and upload an image from your computer.
5. Adjust the crop and click **Apply**, then **Save**.

Desktop is handy when your logo or headshot lives on your computer rather than your phone.

## Best TikTok Profile Picture Size and Format (200 × 200 Minimum)

![Checklist of TikTok profile picture settings: square image, at least 200 by 200 pixels, JPG or PNG, subject centered](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-tiktok-size-1200x675.webp)

| Setting | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Square, shown as a circle |
| Minimum size | 200 × 200 pixels |
| Good size | 400 × 400 to 1080 × 1080 pixels |
| Format | JPG or PNG |
| What fits | Keep faces and logos in the center; the corners get cut off |

### Can You Still Use a Profile Video?

TikTok used to let you set a short **profile video** instead of a photo. That option has disappeared from most accounts. If you don't see it, it isn't available on your version of the app. GIFs aren't supported either, so use a still JPG or PNG.

## New Profile Picture Not Showing? 5 Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| It's still updating | Close and reopen the app; give others a while | Caching means people may see the old picture for a time |
| The upload was rejected | Use an image that follows the Community Guidelines | Nudity, violence or someone else's copyrighted logo can be removed and replaced with the default avatar |
| You changed it recently | Wait a day and try again | TikTok sometimes limits how often you can change profile details |
| The app is out of date | Update TikTok from the App Store or Google Play | Old versions can fail to save changes |
| Slow connection | Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data and retry | The upload may have timed out |

If none of that works, see [whether TikTok is down](/blog/is-tiktok-down).

## Tips for a Profile Picture People Recognize

- ✓ **Use the same picture on every platform.** People recognize you faster when your TikTok, Instagram and YouTube look alike.
- ✓ **Brands: use the logo mark, not the full wordmark.** Long text is unreadable at small sizes.
- ✓ **Go for high contrast.** A bright background stands out in a busy comments section.
- ✓ **Update the rest of your profile at the same time.** A clear name and bio help people decide to follow. Try the [TikTok bio generator](/tools/tiktok-bio-generator) for ideas.
- ✗ Don't use group photos; nobody can tell which one is you.
- ✗ Don't upload a logo with text that runs to the edges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I change my profile picture on TikTok?

Tap Profile, then Edit profile, then Change photo. Choose Take photo or Upload photo, fit it in the circle and tap Save or Confirm.

### Can I change my TikTok profile picture without anyone knowing?

Yes. TikTok doesn't notify your followers when you change your profile picture. People just see the new one next time they view your profile or comments.

### Can I remove my TikTok profile picture?

There's no remove button at the time of writing. For a plain look, upload a blank or simple image instead.

### Why is my TikTok profile picture blurry?

The image was too small or heavily compressed. Upload a square image of at least 400 × 400 pixels, and avoid screenshots of screenshots.

### Can I use a GIF or video as my TikTok profile picture?

No. GIFs aren't supported, and profile videos have been removed from most accounts. Use a still JPG or PNG.

### Why won't my new TikTok profile picture save?

Usually it broke the Community Guidelines, you changed it too recently, or the app needs an update. Wait a day, update the app and try again.

### What size should a TikTok profile picture be?

At least 200 × 200 pixels, and ideally 400 × 400 to 1080 × 1080, in a square JPG or PNG.

### Why do other people still see my old picture?

Caching. The change shows on your profile straight away, but it can take a while to update everywhere for others.
