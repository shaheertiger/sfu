You tap Follow, the button flips back, and nothing happens, again and again. If TikTok won't let you follow people, the most common reason is a **temporary follow limit**: you followed a lot of accounts in a short time, and TikTok paused follows to stop spam. It usually lifts **within a few hours to a day**.

![Why can't I follow people on TikTok: usually a temporary follow limit that lifts within a day](/images/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Other causes are hitting the **10,000-account following cap**, a **new account** with tighter limits, the other person's **privacy settings or a block**, or a plain **app glitch**. The button may bounce back to "Follow", or show an error like "You're following too fast".

This guide helps you work out which cause it is, then fix each one, and explains why following lots of people isn't a good way to grow anyway.

## Why TikTok Stops You Following People (It's Anti-Spam)

Spam and bot accounts tend to follow hundreds of people quickly, hoping some follow back. TikTok's limits are there to stop that, and they catch real people who follow fast too. TikTok doesn't publish its limits, and it doesn't always say which one you've hit.

![Normal following on TikTok compared with patterns that trigger a follow limit](/images/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok-why-1200x675.webp)

| What you see | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "You're following too fast" or the button resets | Temporary follow limit | Stop following for 24 hours |
| Can't follow anyone, following count near 10,000 | Following cap | Unfollow accounts first |
| Brand-new account can't follow | New-account limits | Use the app normally for a few days |
| Can't follow one specific person | Their privacy, a block or a request pending | Check their profile |
| Nothing happens when you tap | App glitch or connection | Update, clear cache, restart |

### What Most People Don't Know

Trying again and again during a block can make it last longer. Every tap on Follow while you're limited looks like more of the behaviour that triggered the limit. The fastest fix is often to put the phone down.

> **Key point:** If you can't follow anyone at all, it's almost always a temporary limit or the 10,000 cap. If you can't follow one person, it's about them: privacy, a pending request or a block.

## How to Find the Cause: 4-Step Check

Menu names in TikTok change between app versions. If a label doesn't match, look for the closest one.

![Four steps to find out why you can't follow people on TikTok](/images/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok-check-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Try Following a Different Account

If that works, the problem is the first account (privacy, a request or a block). If it doesn't, the problem is your account.

### Step 2: Check Your Following Count

Near 10,000? That's the cap.

### Step 3: Think About the Last 24 Hours, Not Just Today

This is the step people skip. Did you follow lots of accounts, or follow and unfollow quickly, yesterday or last night? That's the most common trigger, and the limit can kick in hours later.

### Step 4: Check Account Status

Look under **Settings and privacy > Account > Account status** (where available) for any restriction.

> **Pro tip:** If you were following quickly, stop completely for 24 hours, then follow a handful of accounts at a time with normal use (watching, liking, commenting) in between. Don't test the limit every hour.

## 8 Reasons You Can't Follow People on TikTok (and the Fix for Each)

### 1. You Hit a Temporary Follow Limit

TikTok doesn't publish its limits, but following a few hundred accounts in a day, or following and unfollowing quickly, often triggers a pause. Reports put the daily limit around 200 follows, lower for new accounts. Wait at least 24 hours, then follow at a normal pace.

### 2. You're Following 10,000 Accounts

You can follow up to **10,000 accounts** at the time of writing. Once you're there, the button stops working until you unfollow some. See [how to unfollow on TikTok](/blog/how-to-unfollow-on-tiktok) for the quickest way to trim your list.

### 3. Your Account Is New

Can't follow people on a new TikTok account? New accounts get stricter limits because spam accounts tend to follow hundreds of people on day one. Add a profile photo and bio, verify your email or phone, watch and like videos for a few days, and follow a handful of accounts at a time.

### 4. The Account Is Private, or You've Sent a Request

With a private account, tapping Follow sends a **request**. The button shows **Requested** until they approve it. You can't follow them until they do.

### 5. You're Blocked, or You Blocked Them

If someone blocked you, you can't follow them and their profile may look empty or show "Account not found". If you blocked them, unblock them first. Our guide to [how to know if someone blocked you on TikTok](/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-blocked-you-on-tiktok) covers the signs.

### 6. Age or Account Restrictions

Accounts registered as under 16 have extra restrictions on some features, and an account that broke the Community Guidelines can have features limited for a while. Check Account status for any restriction.

### 7. An App or Connection Glitch

![Quick fixes for a TikTok follow button that won't work, from updating the app to clearing the cache](/images/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok-glitch-1200x675.webp)

- Update TikTok from the App Store or Google Play.
- Clear the cache: **Settings and privacy > Free up space > Cache > Clear**.
- Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or turn a VPN off.
- Log out and back in.
- Check whether TikTok is having a wider problem: see [is TikTok down](/blog/is-tiktok-down).

### 8. You Used a Follow-for-Follow or Automation App

Apps that follow accounts for you can get your account action-blocked for longer. See the section below.

## Follow-for-Follow and Automation Apps (Use With Caution)

When you're trying to grow, apps that follow and unfollow accounts for you, or follow-for-follow groups, look like a shortcut.

![Safe ways to grow on TikTok compared with follow apps that get accounts blocked](/images/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok-apps-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Longer blocks.** Automated following is exactly what the limits are built to catch.
- **Your password.** Many of these apps log in as you, which puts the account at risk.
- **Hollow followers.** People who follow back for a follow rarely watch your videos.

### Safety Checklist

- Revoke app access under **Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions** (or the equivalent in your version).
- Change your password if you gave it to an app.
- Follow by hand, a few accounts at a time.

> **Bottom line:** Follow apps trade a short-term number for a blocked account. They're not worth it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does the TikTok follow block last?

Usually a few hours to a day. Repeating the action during the block can extend it.

### How many people can you follow on TikTok per day?

TikTok doesn't publish a number. Reports suggest around 200 a day, with lower limits on new accounts.

### What is the maximum number of accounts you can follow on TikTok?

10,000 at the time of writing. You'll need to unfollow some accounts to follow anyone new.

### Why does my follow button keep changing back on TikTok?

That's usually a temporary follow limit or a sync glitch. Wait a day, update the app and clear the cache.

### Why can't I follow people on a new TikTok account?

New accounts have stricter limits. Add a profile photo and bio, verify your email or phone, use the app normally for a few days, and follow a handful of accounts at a time.

### Why does it say Requested instead of Following?

The account is private, so your follow is a request until they approve it.

### Can I follow someone who blocked me on TikTok?

No. If someone blocked you, you can't follow them, and their profile may look empty or show "Account not found".

### Do follow-for-follow apps work on TikTok?

They can get your account blocked from following for longer, and the followers they bring rarely watch your videos. Revoke their access and grow by posting instead.
