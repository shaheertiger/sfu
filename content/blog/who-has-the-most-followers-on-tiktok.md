## How to Check Any TikTok Account's Follower Count (App and Web)

Rankings go out of date, but the count on a profile is always current. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### In the App

1. Tap **Search** (the magnifying glass) and type the account's name or handle.
2. Open the profile. Check the handle carefully, because fan and parody accounts often use similar names.
3. Read the **Followers** number under the profile name.

### On a Computer

Go to tiktok.com, search for the account and open its profile. The follower count sits under the name, next to Following and Likes.

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A rounded number, like 163M | Large counts are shown rounded, so small daily changes don't show |
| A different number on a tracker site | The site took its snapshot at another time |
| A similar handle with far fewer followers | Probably a fan or copycat account |

## Why Brands Trail the Top Creators

Apart from TikTok's own account, no brand is close to the top creators. Large media and sports brands lead among brands, but the accounts at the very top are people with a recognizable format. On TikTok, people follow a personality or a format they want more of, which is easier for one creator to build than for a company. For brands, the takeaway is the same as for small creators: a repeatable format and a face or voice people recognize.

## Live Follower Counters and Ranking Sites (Use With Caution)

Live counters and ranking sites are handy for a quick look, but they're snapshots and estimates, not TikTok's own data.

### The Real Risks

- **Numbers disagree.** Sites update at different times and round differently.
- **Some "counters" are ad-heavy pages** that push app downloads or ask you to log in.
- **Lists go stale.** A ranking written months ago can have the wrong order today.

### How to Check Safely

- Open the account in the TikTok app for the current count.
- Never log in to a counter site with your TikTok account.
- Check the date on any ranking before you quote it.

> **Bottom line:** For one account, TikTok itself is the source. For rankings, treat every list as approximate.
