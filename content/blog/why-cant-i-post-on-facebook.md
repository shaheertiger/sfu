You've written the post, tapped Post, and nothing happens, or Facebook tells you that you "can't post right now". The cause is usually one of a handful of things: **a glitch in the app or browser**, **a restriction on your account**, **missing permissions on a Page**, **rules set by a Group's admins**, **a temporary limit for posting too fast**, or **a link Facebook blocks**. Less often, Facebook itself is having problems.

![Why you can't post on Facebook: check Account Status first, then match the symptom to one of nine causes](/images/blog/why-cant-i-post-on-facebook-hero-1200x675.webp)

Start by checking **Account Status** in your settings. It's the one place Facebook tells you directly whether you're restricted.

The fix depends on where Facebook won't let you post (your profile, a Page or a Group) and what you see when it fails. This guide matches each symptom to its cause, then walks through the fixes.

## Why Facebook Stops You Posting (Match the Symptom to the Cause)

![Signs of a local glitch compared with signs of a Facebook restriction when you can't post](/images/blog/why-cant-i-post-on-facebook-symptoms-1200x675.webp)

| What you see | Likely cause | Where to look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Post button does nothing, or the app freezes | App or browser glitch | Update or reinstall the app; try another browser |
| "You can't post right now" or "You're temporarily blocked" | Spam-related limit or restriction | Account Status in Settings |
| No option to post as your Page | Missing Page access or wrong profile selected | Page settings → Page access |
| Your Group post says "pending" | Admin approval is required | The Group's rules and your pending posts |
| No box to write in a Group | Admins have turned off member posts, or you're muted | Group rules; contact an admin |
| A message that your post goes against Community Standards | Blocked content or link | The notice itself; Account Status |
| Nobody can post, including friends | A Facebook outage | Wait and try again later |

### What Most People Don't Know

Many posting failures aren't about your account at all. If the Post button simply does nothing, with no message, it's usually the app, the browser or your connection. Restrictions almost always come with a notice.

> **Key point:** No message usually means a local glitch. A message about limits, blocks or Community Standards means a restriction, and Account Status will say how long it lasts.

## Cause 1: An App or Browser Problem (7-Step Fix)

![Steps to fix a Facebook app or browser glitch: restart, update, clear the cache, then try another device](/images/blog/why-cant-i-post-on-facebook-glitch-1200x675.webp)

Try these in order. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Restart the App or Refresh the Page

Close the app fully, or reload facebook.com, and try the post again.

### Step 2: Update the Facebook App

From the App Store or Google Play.

### Step 3: Clear the Cache

On Android: Settings → Apps → Facebook → Storage. In a browser, clear cookies and cache for facebook.com.

### Step 4: Try a Different Device or Browser

If it works on a computer but not your phone, the problem is local. This is the quickest test there is, and the one people skip.

> **Pro tip:** Try posting the same thing from a private browser window. If it works there, an extension or cached data in your normal browser is the culprit.

### Step 5: Turn Off Browser Extensions

Ad blockers and script blockers can break the post composer.

### Step 6: Check Your Connection

Large photos and videos can time out on weak mobile data. Try Wi-Fi.

### Step 7: Reduce the Upload

If a video fails, try a shorter or smaller file to see whether size is the issue.

## Cause 2: Your Account Has a Restriction (Check Account Status)

Facebook can temporarily stop you from posting, commenting or using certain features if it believes you broke its Community Standards or behaved like spam.

### How to Check Account Status

1. Open the Facebook menu and go to **Settings & privacy → Settings**.
2. Find **Account Status**. At the time of writing it appears near the top of settings; Facebook moves it occasionally, so use the settings search if needed.
3. Review any removed content, restrictions and how long they last.

If you disagree with a decision, Account Status usually shows a **Request review** or **Disagree with decision** option. Use that official route. Reviews take varying amounts of time and nothing guarantees a reversal.

### For Pages and Businesses

Pages and ad accounts have their own health screens. For a Page, look for **Page status** or **Account quality** in Meta Business Suite or the Business Support Home. Restrictions on a Page can stop you posting even when your personal profile is fine.

## Cause 3: You Don't Have the Right Role on a Page

- **Switch to the Page profile.** In the newer Pages experience, you post by switching from your personal profile to the Page from your profile menu. Posting while still in your personal profile won't publish as the Page.
- **Check your access.** Ask an admin to open the Page's **Settings → Page setup → Page access** and confirm you have Facebook access with permission to create content, or the matching task access in Meta Business Suite.
- **Check for an expired invite.** If you were invited to manage the Page, accept the invitation from your notifications first.

## Cause 4: Group Rules or Admin Approval

Groups are run by their admins, who control who posts and when.

| Group setting | What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post approval is on | Your post is "pending" | Check Your content or Pending posts and wait |
| Member posting is off | No box to write in | Only admins post, or only on certain days |
| New-member waiting period | Can't post yet after joining | Wait out the period |
| You've been muted or restricted | Can't post or comment | Contact an admin |
| Your post broke a rule | Declined or removed | Read the rules in the Group's About section |

If you're unsure, message one of the Group's admins or moderators politely. Only they can change Group-level settings.

## Cause 5: Spam Limits for Posting Too Fast

Facebook limits activity that looks automated or spammy. You might see a "temporarily blocked" message if you:

- Post the same message in many Groups in a short time.
- Share lots of links rapidly.
- Send many friend requests or messages to people you don't know.
- Tag lots of people who don't interact with you.

These blocks usually expire after a period stated in the notice. Waiting it out is often the only fix. Avoid repeating the behavior, and spread your posting out.

## Causes 6–9: Blocked Links, Media, Outages and Hacked Accounts

### Cause 6: A Blocked or Flagged Link

If the problem only happens with a particular link, Facebook may be blocking that site. You'll often see a message that the content goes against the Community Standards or looks like spam.

- **Remove the link and try again.** If the post goes through, the link was the problem.
- **Check the destination.** Link shorteners, recently hacked sites and some redirect services are commonly blocked.
- **Fix your own site.** If it's your website that's blocked, make sure it's secure and not hosting malware, then use the review option Facebook offers in the notice.

For the right way to add links to different post types, see our guide to [adding links to a Facebook post](/blog/add-links-to-facebook-post).

### Cause 7: The Media Isn't Supported

Facebook posts can be up to 63,206 characters, so text length is rarely the issue. Media causes more trouble: unsupported file types, very large videos or corrupted files can fail to upload. Re-export in a common format, such as MP4 for video and JPG or PNG for images.

### Cause 8: A Facebook Outage

Signs include feeds that won't load, messages that won't send and friends reporting the same problem. There's nothing to fix on your side: wait and try again later. Meta publishes status information for businesses and developers, and the news usually covers major outages quickly.

### Cause 9: Your Account Is Hacked or Locked

If you see unfamiliar posts, can't change your settings or get logged out repeatedly, your account may be compromised. Go to the [Facebook Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/help) and use the official hacked-account process.

## "Account Recovery Services" and Mass-Posting Tools (Use With Caution)

![Checklist of safe ways to get posting again on Facebook, like the official review, and risky ones, like paid recovery services and mass-posting tools](/images/blog/why-cant-i-post-on-facebook-caution-1200x675.webp)

When you're blocked, two kinds of "help" show up fast: people offering to recover or unblock your account for a fee, and tools that promise to post to dozens of Groups at once.

### The Real Risks

- **Paid recovery services** can't do anything the official process can't, and some are scams that take your money or your login.
- **Messages claiming your account will be deleted** unless you click a link are a common phishing trick.
- **Mass-posting tools** do exactly what triggers spam limits: the same message in many Groups in a short time.
- **Sharing login codes** with anyone gives them your account.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Use Account Status and the Help Center's official processes only.
- Never pay "recovery services" or share your password or login codes.
- Ignore messages threatening deletion unless you click a link.
- Post to Groups by hand, spaced out, following each Group's rules.

> **Bottom line:** The official route is slower than a stranger's promise, but it's the only one that can actually lift a restriction without putting your account at more risk.

## What to Do Next

1. **Check Account Status** for restrictions and their end dates.
2. **No restriction?** Restart, update, clear the cache and try another device.
3. **Posting as a Page?** Switch to the Page profile and confirm your access.
4. **In a Group?** Check pending posts and the Group's rules, then ask an admin.
5. **Got a block?** Wait it out, then spread your posting more evenly.
6. **Link problem?** Remove it, test, and fix or replace the link.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I post on Facebook all of a sudden?

Usually an app glitch, a temporary block for activity that looks like spam, or a restriction after a post was flagged. Check Account Status in Settings & privacy, update the app, and try a different device.

### Why does Facebook say I can't post right now?

It usually means a temporary limit on your account, often after activity that looked like spam, such as sharing the same link in many places quickly. The notice or Account Status normally says how long it lasts.

### How do I know if Facebook has restricted my account?

Go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Account Status. It shows removed content, current restrictions and how long they last, with an option to request a review.

### Why can't I post in a Facebook Group?

The admins may require approval for new posts, allow only admins to post, have a waiting period for new members, or have muted you. Check the Group's rules and your pending posts, or contact an admin.

### Why can't I post as my Facebook Page?

You may still be posting as your personal profile, or you may not have permission to create content on the Page. Switch to the Page profile, and ask an admin to check Page access.

### How long does a Facebook posting block last?

It depends on the reason. Temporary blocks for spam-like activity often show an end date or duration in the notice. Facebook doesn't publish fixed lengths, and repeated issues can lead to longer restrictions.

### Can a scheduling tool post to Facebook Groups or profiles?

It depends on the tool. Schedule For You posts to Facebook Pages only, not personal profiles or Groups. For Groups, post in Facebook itself and follow each Group's rules.

### Is Facebook down or is it just me?

If feeds won't load and friends report the same problem, it's probably an outage. If only posting fails for you, work through the causes above.
