It's Thursday night and you want tomorrow's 7:30 a.m. Story to go out without you holding your phone. You can schedule Instagram Stories in two ways: in **Meta Business Suite**, Meta's free tool for professional accounts, or with a scheduler that publishes Stories through Meta's API, such as Schedule For You. Both need an Instagram **Business or Creator** account linked to a **Facebook Page**, and both can schedule **Facebook Page Stories** too.

![Two ways to schedule Instagram and Facebook Stories: Meta Business Suite or a scheduler, both needing a professional account linked to a Facebook Page](/images/blog/schedule-instagram-and-facebook-stories-hero-1200x675.webp)

The catch is stickers. Polls, quizzes, links and music are where Stories come alive, and they're also what scheduling takes away, partly or completely depending on the method.

This guide covers the native method step by step, what each option can't do, a fair comparison, and how to schedule a Story alongside the rest of your week. Personal Instagram accounts and personal Facebook profiles can't schedule Stories either way.

## Why Scheduled Stories Lose Their Stickers (and When That Matters)

![Stories that schedule well, like product shots and announcements, next to Stories that need the app, like polls, links and music](/images/blog/schedule-instagram-and-facebook-stories-stickers-1200x675.webp)

Scheduled Stories go out through Meta's tools rather than the Instagram app's full editor. Meta Business Suite's editor has fewer creative tools and stickers than the app, and Meta's publishing API, which every scheduler uses, posts the image or video only.

| Story type | Schedule it? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A product photo or daily special | Yes | Nothing interactive needed |
| An announcement with text on the image | Yes | Add the text in your editor first |
| A poll, quiz or question | Post live | Interactive stickers may not be offered when scheduling |
| A link sticker | Meta Business Suite at best, or post live | Schedulers can't add it |
| Music from Instagram's library | Post live | Not available through the API |

### What Most People Don't Know

A scheduled Story still disappears 24 hours after it goes live, like any other. Scheduling changes when the clock starts, not how long it runs. Add it to a Highlight if you want it to stay.

> **Key point:** Schedule the Stories that are just a picture or a clip. Post the interactive ones live, where the stickers are.

## How to Schedule Instagram Stories in Meta Business Suite (8 Steps, Free)

![Four stages to schedule a Story in Meta Business Suite: Create story, pick accounts, add 9:16 media, choose Schedule](/images/blog/schedule-instagram-and-facebook-stories-mbs-1200x675.webp)

Before you start, check that your Instagram account is a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page you manage, and have your media ready at 9:16 (1080×1920). Meta renames menus often, so look for the closest match; [Instagram's help center](https://help.instagram.com) has the current labels.

### Step 1: Open Meta Business Suite

On a computer (business.facebook.com) or in the Meta Business Suite mobile app.

### Step 2: Choose Create Story

It's on the home screen or in the Planner, sometimes under a general Create button.

### Step 3: Pick Where It Posts

Tick your Instagram account, your Facebook Page or both. For Facebook Stories, tick the Page; scheduling works for Pages, not personal profiles.

### Step 4: Add Your Media

Upload images or videos. Each one becomes a separate Story frame.

### Step 5: Edit the Frames

Crop to 9:16 and add text or simple elements.

### Step 6: Choose Schedule, Not Share Now (the Step People Miss)

Open the publishing options and choose **Schedule**. It's easy to hit the default share button out of habit.

### Step 7: Pick the Date and Time

Then confirm with **Schedule story**.

### Step 8: Check the Planner

The Story appears there, where you can change or delete it before it goes live.

> **Pro tip:** Keep text and faces in the middle of the frame. The profile name sits at the top and the reply bar at the bottom, and both cover whatever is underneath.

## Where the Native Method Gets Awkward: a Café's Friday

![A café's Friday: a 7:30 Story on Instagram and Facebook, then a noon Reel, TikTok, YouTube Short and Google Business Profile photo](/images/blog/schedule-instagram-and-facebook-stories-cafe-1200x675.webp)

Picture a café that runs the same routine every Friday. At 7:30 a.m. a short video of the day's special goes out as an Instagram Story and a Facebook Page Story. At noon the longer cut goes out as a Reel, a TikTok and a YouTube Short, and a photo goes to the Google Business Profile.

Meta Business Suite handles the 7:30 Stories and the Reel well. It doesn't publish to TikTok, YouTube or Google Business Profile, so the owner schedules those somewhere else, keeps two calendars in step and checks two places when something fails. On a busy morning, that's how the TikTok gets forgotten.

## How to Schedule Stories With Schedule For You (5 Steps)

Here's the café's Friday in one place.

1. **Connect Instagram and your Facebook Page.** Instagram needs a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Our guide to [connecting Instagram](/blog/connect-instagram) covers the setup, and [connecting a Facebook Page](/blog/connect-facebook-page) covers the Page.
2. **Create a post and choose the accounts.** Select the Instagram account and the Facebook Page.
3. **Set "Post as" to Story.** Each Instagram or Facebook account can post as a feed post, a Reel or a Story, so one account can post a Story while another posts the same file as a feed post.
4. **Attach exactly one image or one video.** A Story is one frame: one image or video, 9:16, with no caption. The composer flags anything else before you can schedule it. For a three-frame sequence, schedule three Stories a minute or two apart.
5. **Pick the time** and schedule it, or add it to the queue.

![The Schedule For You composer with an Instagram account selected and Post as set to Story, showing that a Story needs one 9:16 image or video with no caption](/images/blog/composer-instagram-story-1440x900.webp)

Then the noon posts: create a second post with the longer video, select the Instagram account (Post as: Reel), TikTok and YouTube, and give each its own caption. Both posts sit in the same queue and calendar, and a failed Story shows up next to everything else with the reason and a retry button.

![The Schedule For You queue showing scheduled posts by time, with failed posts listed with reasons and a Retry button](/images/blog/queue-1440x900.webp)

Where we're not the best fit: Stories go out exactly as uploaded, so add text in your editor first. Interactive stickers (polls, questions, links) and music from Instagram's library aren't available through Meta's publishing API, so post those Stories live from the app. And if you only use Instagram and Facebook, Meta Business Suite is free and offers some stickers. The café's Instagram, Facebook Page and TikTok fit the Free plan's 3 accounts; adding YouTube and Google Business Profile needs Solo.

## Tips for Stories People Actually Watch

![Story habits that work, like 9:16 at 1080 by 1920, next to habits that make Stories look soft or get skipped](/images/blog/schedule-instagram-and-facebook-stories-tips-1200x675.webp)

- **Use 9:16 at 1080×1920.** Small or heavily compressed files look soft after Instagram processes them; see [why Instagram Stories look blurry](/blog/why-does-my-instagram-story-look-blurry).
- **Respect the safe zones.** Keep text and faces away from the top and bottom.
- **One idea per frame.** People tap through fast, so put the point in the first frame.
- **Save the good ones to Highlights.** Our guide to [Instagram highlights](/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram) shows how.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you schedule Instagram Stories?

Yes. Professional Instagram accounts linked to a Facebook Page can schedule Stories in Meta Business Suite or with a scheduler that uses Meta's API, such as Schedule For You. Some stickers and music may not be available when scheduling.

### How do I schedule Facebook Stories?

In Meta Business Suite, choose Create story, select your Facebook Page, add media, then choose Schedule. In Schedule For You, select the Page, set Post as to Story and attach one image or video. Both work for Pages, not personal profiles.

### Is there a free way to schedule Instagram Stories?

Yes. Meta Business Suite is free for professional accounts. Schedule For You's Free plan also schedules Stories, for up to 3 accounts and 10 scheduled posts at a time, with no card.

### Can I add link stickers or polls to a scheduled Story?

Not through a scheduler: Meta's publishing API posts the image or video only. Meta Business Suite offers some stickers when scheduling at the time of writing. Stories that rely on polls, questions or music are best posted live.

### Can a scheduled Story have several frames?

In Meta Business Suite, yes: each image or video you add becomes a frame. In Schedule For You each Story is one image or video, so schedule a sequence as separate Stories a minute or two apart.

### Can a scheduled Story have a caption?

No. A Story published through a scheduler is one image or video with no caption, so put any text on the image itself.

### Can personal Instagram accounts schedule Stories?

No. You need a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page.

### How long does a scheduled Story stay up?

24 hours from when it goes live, like any Story. Save it to a Highlight to keep it.
