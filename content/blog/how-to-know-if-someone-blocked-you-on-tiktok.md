You tap on someone's TikTok profile and get "Account not found". Did they block you, or did something else happen? TikTok never tells you when someone blocks you. The clearest sign is that their profile shows **"Account not found"** (or "Couldn't find this account") for you, while it loads normally from a different account or while logged out.

![How to know if someone blocked you on TikTok: compare what you see with what another account sees](/images/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-blocked-you-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

If the profile is missing everywhere, the account was more likely deleted, banned or renamed. That's the whole test: blocked accounts are hidden from you only.

This guide covers how blocking works on TikTok, the signs, a 4-step check that settles it, what "Account not found" means, why "who blocked me" apps can't help, and what to do next.

## Why TikTok Doesn't Tell You When You're Blocked

Blocking is a safety feature. If TikTok sent a notification, people who were blocked for harassment would know to come back with a new account. So the block is silent: the other person simply disappears from your view, and you disappear from theirs.

![What changes for you when someone blocks you on TikTok, and what stays the same](/images/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-blocked-you-on-tiktok-why-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

"Account not found" is not a block message. It's the same message TikTok shows for a deleted, banned or renamed account, and sometimes for a temporary glitch. That's why one look at a missing profile can't tell you anything on its own. You need to compare it with what another account sees.

> **Key point:** If a profile is missing for you but visible to everyone else, you're blocked. If it's missing for everyone, it isn't about you.

## 6 Signs Someone Blocked You on TikTok

| What you notice | Blocked? |
|---|---|
| Their profile shows "Account not found" for you only | Very likely |
| You can't find them in search, but a friend can | Very likely |
| Your old chat with them won't let you send messages | Likely |
| Their comments and videos disappeared from your feed | Possibly |
| You're no longer following each other | Possibly (blocking removes the follow both ways) |
| You can still see their profile but no videos | Probably not: they may be private, or have no public videos |

![Signs that point to a TikTok block compared with signs that point to something else](/images/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-blocked-you-on-tiktok-signs-1200x675.webp)

No single sign proves it, because a glitch or a slow connection can cause the same message. Try a couple of checks before you decide.

### Private Account or Block? How to Tell

A private account still shows up for you: you can see the profile, the username and usually the follower counts, but not the videos, unless they approve your follow request. A block hides the profile completely. So if you can see their name and photo but no videos, you're looking at a private account, not a block. If you were following a private account and suddenly can't see their videos, they may have removed you as a follower, which is different from blocking you.

## How to Check If You're Blocked on TikTok in 4 Steps

TikTok's menus change between app versions, but these checks don't depend on any menu.

![Four steps to check if someone blocked you on TikTok, ending with the check from another account](/images/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-blocked-you-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Search Their Exact @Username

Search for their @handle in the app, not their display name. Display names aren't unique, so searching the name can show you the wrong person.

### Step 2: Open an Old Link or Chat

Open a link to their profile from an old chat or a saved video. If you're blocked, it won't load for you.

### Step 3: Check From Another Account or Logged Out (the Deciding Test)

This is the step people skip, and it's the only one that settles it. Ask a friend to search for them, switch to a second account, or open tiktok.com/@username in a private browser window. If the profile loads there and not for you, you've been blocked.

### Step 4: Look at Your Following List

If they used to be there and aren't now, and you didn't unfollow, a block is one explanation. Blocking removes the follow in both directions.

> **Pro tip:** Use the logged-out browser check rather than asking a mutual friend. It's private, it takes ten seconds, and nobody else needs to know you were checking.

## What Does "Account Not Found" Mean on TikTok? 5 Possible Reasons

| Reason | Missing for you only? | Missing for everyone? | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| They blocked you | Yes | No | Nothing; respect the block |
| They changed their username | Old links and @handle fail for everyone | Under the old name, yes | Search their display name or ask a mutual friend |

| TikTok banned or suspended the account | No | Yes | Nothing to do on your side |
| A temporary error | Maybe | Maybe | Restart the app or check whether [TikTok is down](/blog/is-tiktok-down) |

The test is always the same: if the profile is missing for everyone, it's not about you.

## "Who Blocked Me" Apps for TikTok (Use With Caution)

Search for "who blocked me on TikTok" and you'll find apps and sites that promise a list. They can't deliver it.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't know.** TikTok doesn't share block lists with anyone, so no outside app can see who blocked you.
- **They want your password.** Many ask you to sign in with your TikTok login, which gives a stranger access to your account.
- **Your account can be restricted.** Logging in through unofficial apps can trigger security checks or limits on your account.

### Safety Checklist

- Never enter your TikTok password anywhere except TikTok itself.
- Use the logged-out browser check instead; it's free and private.
- If you already signed in to one of these apps, change your password and review your logged-in devices in TikTok's security settings.

> **Bottom line:** The only reliable test is comparing what you see with what another account sees. No app can do better.

## What to Do If Someone Blocked You on TikTok

Not much, and that's the point of blocking.

- ✓ Accept it and move on, or reach out somewhere you're already in touch if you think it was a mistake
- ✓ Block or restrict accounts you don't want to see yourself
- ✗ Don't make new accounts to get around a block: it can break TikTok's rules on harassment and get your own account restricted
- ✗ Don't keep checking their profile logged out to watch their videos

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you see who blocked you on TikTok?

No. TikTok doesn't show a list of people who blocked you, and no app can see one either, so you can only work it out profile by profile.

### Does "Account not found" always mean blocked?

No. It also appears when an account is deleted, banned or renamed. If the profile is missing from every account you check, you weren't blocked.

### Can I still see someone's TikTok if they blocked me?

Not while signed in to the blocked account. Their public videos may still be visible logged out, but trying to get around a block can count as harassment.

### Do my messages disappear when someone blocks me?

The old chat may stay in your inbox, but you can't send new messages to them while the block is in place.

### Does blocking someone on TikTok unfollow them?

Yes. Blocking removes the follow in both directions, which is why a person missing from your following list can be a sign of a block.

### Does TikTok notify someone when you block them?

No. TikTok doesn't send a notification when you block someone, and it doesn't notify you when someone blocks you.

### If someone unblocks me, will I follow them again?

Usually not. The follow removed by the block doesn't come back on its own at the time of writing, so you'd need to follow them again.

### Can a deactivated TikTok account look like a block?

Yes. A deactivated account shows "Account not found" too, but for everyone, and it can come back if the owner logs in within 30 days.
