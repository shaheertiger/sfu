You left a comment you regret, or someone left one under your video that you don't want there. To delete a comment on TikTok, **press and hold the comment** and tap **Delete**. You can delete your own comments on any video, and any comment left on your own videos. On a computer, hover over the comment, click the **…** menu and choose **Delete**.

![How to delete comments on TikTok: press and hold the comment, tap Delete, or manage up to 100 at once on your own videos](/images/blog/how-to-delete-comments-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Deleted comments are removed for everyone and can't be restored, and the person who wrote the comment isn't notified.

This guide covers who can delete what, deleting many comments at once, finding your old comments, pinning a comment, what folded comments are, and how to keep your comment section manageable. TikTok changes menu labels between versions, so yours may look slightly different.

## Who Can Delete Which Comments on TikTok (4 Cases)

![Which TikTok comments you can delete, and which you can only report](/images/blog/how-to-delete-comments-on-tiktok-who-1200x675.webp)

| Comment | Can you delete it? |
| --- | --- |
| Your comment on your video | Yes |
| Your comment on someone else's video | Yes |
| Someone else's comment on your video | Yes |
| Someone else's comment on someone else's video | No, but you can report it |

### What Most People Don't Know

Deleting isn't your only option on your own videos. You can also **pin** a good comment to the top, let TikTok **fold** likely spam out of sight, and set **keyword filters** so certain comments never show up. For most creators, filters do more of the work than deleting one by one.

> **Key point:** Deleting a comment doesn't notify the person who wrote it, and it can't be undone. If you might want it later, take a screenshot first.

## How to Delete a Comment on TikTok in 3 Steps (Phone and Desktop)

![Three steps to delete a TikTok comment: open comments, press and hold, tap Delete](/images/blog/how-to-delete-comments-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open the Comments

Open the video and tap the **comment** icon.

### Step 2: Press and Hold, Don't Tap

Press and hold the comment for a second. A quick tap opens a reply instead, which is the mistake most people make.

### Step 3: Tap Delete

Tap **Delete**, then confirm if asked.

On a computer, open the video on tiktok.com, hover over the comment, click **…** and choose **Delete**.

> **Pro tip:** If Delete is missing, check whose comment and whose video it is. You can only delete your own comments, or any comment on your own videos. If it should be there, update the app and try again.

## How to Delete Many Comments at Once (Up to 100)

On your own videos, TikTok lets you manage comments in bulk:

1. Open the comments on your video.
2. Press and hold one comment, or tap the **pencil / edit** icon at the top of the comment panel.
3. Tap **Manage multiple comments**, select up to 100 comments, then tap **Delete**.

The same screen can **Block** the accounts that wrote them, which is useful after a wave of spam.

| Method | Best for | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press and hold | One comment | One at a time |
| Manage multiple comments | Spam waves on your video | Up to 100 at a time, your videos only |
| Keyword filters | Stopping repeats before they appear | Your videos only |
| Report | Others' comments on others' videos | Doesn't delete, TikTok reviews it |

## How to See Your Comments on TikTok (2 Ways)

TikTok doesn't have a single "my comments" list in every region. Two ways that work:

- **Activity center or comment history.** On recent versions, go to **Profile → menu (three lines) → Activity center** and look for **Comment history**. If you don't see it, it hasn't reached your account yet.
- **Download your data.** In **Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data**, request a file. It includes the comments you've posted.

## How to Pin a Comment on TikTok (One Per Video)

You can pin one comment to the top of each of your own videos.

- **Phone:** press and hold the comment, then tap **Pin comment**. Pinning another one replaces it.
- **Computer:** open the video on tiktok.com, hover over the comment, click **…** and choose **Pin**. If it's not offered on the website, pin from the app.

"Pinned" on TikTok means one of two things. A **pinned comment** sits at the top of a video's comments with a "Pinned" or "Pinned by creator" label. A **pinned video** sits at the top of a profile grid, and you can pin up to three videos on your profile from the video's **three dots → Pin**.

## What Are Folded Comments on TikTok? (Hidden, Not Deleted)

![Folded TikTok comments compared with deleted comments: folded are collapsed and can be read, deleted are gone](/images/blog/how-to-delete-comments-on-tiktok-folded-1200x675.webp)

Folded comments are collapsed rather than deleted. They sit behind a link at the bottom of the comment section, and you tap it to read them. TikTok folds comments it predicts are spam, off-topic or likely to break its guidelines, and comments can also be folded after reports or by your own keyword filters. Folding a comment doesn't hurt the video's reach.

To control what appears under your videos, go to **Settings and privacy → Privacy → Comments**. You can filter spam and offensive comments, add keywords to filter, or choose who can comment.

### How to Post GIFs or Pictures in Comments

Some accounts see a GIF or image button next to the emoji button in the comment box; others don't, as TikTok rolls these features out gradually. If yours doesn't have one, a keyboard with built-in GIF search (such as Gboard or the GIPHY keyboard) may let you insert one, depending on your phone. Plain emoji always work. ## Comment-Cleaner Apps and Browser Extensions (Use With Caution)

Search for "delete all TikTok comments" and you'll find apps and browser extensions that promise to wipe your comment history in one click. TikTok's own bulk tool only works on your videos, so the appeal is obvious.

### The Real Risks

- **They need your login** or a logged-in browser session, which gives them full access to your account.
- **Mass automated deletions can look like bot activity**, which can trigger security checks or limits.
- **There's no undo.** If the tool deletes the wrong comments, they're gone.

### If You Use One Anyway

- Never type your TikTok password into the tool's own form.
- Test it on a handful of comments first.
- Afterwards, check **Manage devices** in Security and remove anything you don't recognize, and change your password.

> **Bottom line:** For comments on your own videos, TikTok's Manage multiple comments does the job safely. For your comments elsewhere, delete them by hand from your comment history.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I delete a comment on TikTok?

Press and hold the comment and tap Delete. On a computer, hover over it, click the … menu and choose Delete.

### Does the person know if I delete their comment?

No. TikTok doesn't notify them, though they may notice it's gone.

### Can I delete a comment I left on someone else's TikTok?

Yes. Press and hold your comment and tap Delete.

### Can I recover a deleted TikTok comment?

No. Deleted comments are gone for good.

### How many comments can I delete at once?

Up to 100 at a time on your own videos, using Manage multiple comments. Comments you left on other people's videos have to be deleted one by one.

### How many comments can I pin on a TikTok video?

One per video. Pinning a new comment replaces the old one.

### Why can't I delete a comment on TikTok?

You can only delete your own comments, or any comment on your own videos. If the option is missing, update the app and try again.

### What does a folded comment mean on TikTok?

It's collapsed, not deleted. TikTok folds comments it predicts are spam or off-topic, and you can still tap to read them.
