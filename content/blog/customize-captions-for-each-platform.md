To customize social media captions for each platform in Schedule For You, you write one main caption, then open each platform's version and edit it separately. Changes to one platform's version don't affect the others, and a live character-limit check for each platform shows when a version is too long. That way you can keep one idea across every network while still writing for how each platform reads.

This guide walks through the workflow step by step, then gives practical caption tips for each of the 10 supported platforms.

## Why one social media caption rarely fits every platform

Copying the same text everywhere is quick, but it often backfires:

- **Length limits differ a lot.** A caption that fits Instagram's 2,200 characters is far too long for X's 280 or Bluesky's 300.
- **Conventions differ.** Hashtag blocks feel normal on Instagram, look cluttered on LinkedIn, and use up precious characters on X.
- **Links behave differently.** Links in Instagram captions aren't clickable, while on LinkedIn, Facebook or Bluesky they are.
- **Audiences differ.** The same announcement might need a casual tone on Threads and a more professional one on LinkedIn or Google Business Profile.

Tailoring social media captions for each platform takes a few minutes per post and avoids truncated text, failed posts and copy that feels out of place.

## Caption length limits by platform

These are the limits Schedule For You checks against when you write. Platform limits can change, so treat them as correct at the time of writing.

| Platform | Caption or text limit | What else to know |
|---|---|---|

| Bluesky | 300 characters | Up to 4 images or a video |
| Threads | 500 characters | Text only, one image or video, or a carousel of up to 10 |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 characters | One image, optional button link, no video |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | One image, a Reel, or a carousel of 2–10 items |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | Up to 9 images or a video |

| Facebook Pages | 63,206 characters | Up to 10 images or a video |
| Pinterest | Short description works best | Image required, pick a board, optional title and link |

## How to customize captions in Schedule For You

1. **Open the composer.** Start a new post in the workspace you want to publish from.
2. **Select your accounts.** Choose every connected account the post should go to. Each selected platform gets its own version of the caption.
3. **Write the main caption.** Write the full version of your message first. Think of it as the master copy: the idea, the key details and your call to action.
4. **Add your media.** Upload images or video, or pick them from the media library. The media you add affects what each platform will accept, for example a video is required for YouTube and an image for Pinterest.
5. **Open a platform's version.** Switch to the version for one platform, such as X.
6. **Edit it for that platform.** Shorten, rephrase, add or remove hashtags and links. Your edits apply only to that platform's version. The main caption and the other platforms stay as they were.
7. **Watch the character check.** The live count shows how close you are to that platform's limit and flags any version that's over it, so you can fix it before scheduling.
8. **Repeat for the platforms that need it.** You don't have to edit every version. Platforms where the main caption already works can use it as written.
9. **Schedule or queue the post.** Pick a time, drop the post on the calendar, or add it to your next open queue slot.

### Start long, then trim

It's usually easier to write the longest version first and cut it down than to stretch a short caption. Write the main caption at the length you'd use on Instagram or LinkedIn, then trim for X, Bluesky and Threads.

### Keep the core message identical

Change the packaging, not the facts. Dates, prices, offers and links should match across every version, so nobody sees conflicting information. Edit wording, length, hashtags and calls to action instead.

## Social media caption tips for each platform

### X

With 280 characters, lead with the point. Links count as 23 characters no matter how long they are, and most emoji count as 2. One or two hashtags are plenty. Our [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter) uses X's own weighting if you want to draft outside the composer.

### Bluesky

Bluesky's 300 characters reward the same punchy style as X. Write in plain sentences, keep hashtags minimal, and put the link where it reads naturally.

### Threads

At 500 characters you have room for a short story or a question. Threads leans conversational, so asking for opinions often works better than a hard call to action.

### Instagram

The first line or two show before the "more" cut-off in the feed, so put the hook there. Links in captions aren't clickable, so point people to your bio instead. Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post, but a smaller, relevant set is easier to read. To keep line breaks and spacing, prepare the text with our [Instagram line break generator](/tools/instagram-line-break-generator).

### LinkedIn

LinkedIn shows the first few lines before "…more", so open with a clear statement or result. Short paragraphs with white space are easier to read than one block. Use a few specific hashtags at the end rather than many inside the text.

### TikTok

The caption supports the video rather than carrying it. Keep it short, add a few topic hashtags, and use the space for context such as a question or what to watch for.

### YouTube

The first line of your caption becomes the title if you don't fill in the title field, so either fill in the title or make that first line a strong title under 100 characters. The rest becomes the description: summarize the video, add links and chapters, and put the most important information near the top.

### Facebook Pages

You have plenty of room, but long posts get cut off in the feed. Lead with the key point, keep paragraphs short, and add a link where it's useful.

### Pinterest

Pinterest works like a visual search engine. Describe what's in the image with words people would search for, and add a pin title and destination link so people can click through.

### Google Business Profile

Write for local customers: what's new, the offer or event, and the details they need. Keep within 1,500 characters and use the button link to send people to a booking page, menu or product.

## Common mistakes to avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pasting the Instagram hashtag block into X or LinkedIn | Remove or cut it down to one to three hashtags in those versions |
| Writing "link in bio" on platforms with clickable links | Add the real link in the Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Bluesky versions |
| Forgetting the YouTube title | Fill in the title field or write a clear first line |
| Editing the main caption after tailoring versions | Check the platform versions again before scheduling |
| Using Unicode "fonts" for whole captions | Use them sparingly; screen readers may struggle and search may not match them |

## Frequently asked questions

### Does editing one platform's caption change the others?

No. Each platform has its own version. When you edit the X version, for example, the main caption and every other platform's version stay the same.

### What happens if a caption is over a platform's limit?

The live character-limit check flags that platform's version as too long, so you can shorten it before you schedule. Fixing it in the composer avoids a failed post later.

### Do I have to customize every platform?

No. If the main caption already fits a platform's limit and style, you can leave that version as it is. Most people only adjust the shortest-limit platforms and anywhere hashtags or links need to change.

### How long should a social media caption be?

As long as the idea needs and within the platform's limit. Most feeds show only the first line or two before a "more" cut-off, so put the hook first. Short platforms like X and Bluesky need a tight version; Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook leave room for more detail.

### Can I use different hashtags on each platform?

Yes. Hashtags are part of the caption, so you can add, remove or change them in each platform's version independently.

### Can Schedule For You write the platform versions for me?

No. Schedule For You doesn't include AI writing features. You write and edit each version yourself, with live character checks to help.
