To get more followers on TikTok, post a steady run of videos on one clear topic, so someone who likes one video expects more of the same if they follow. Views come from the For You feed; follows come when a viewer taps your profile and sees a page full of videos they want. Fix your profile, post three to five times a week, make series people want to finish, and reply to comments. Don't buy followers: fake accounts don't watch, which drags down how your videos perform.

## Why views don't always turn into followers

A video can get 50,000 views and 20 follows. That usually means one of three things:

- **The video was a one-off.** Viewers liked it, but your profile doesn't show more like it.
- **Your profile is unclear.** No bio, no photo, or a name that doesn't hint at what you post.
- **There's no reason to come back.** Nothing tells people a part two, a next tip or a weekly series is coming.

## 7 ways to get more followers on TikTok

1. **Stick to one niche.** One audience, one kind of value. It's fine to show personality, but the core should stay the same.
2. **Make series.** "Part 1 of 5" gives viewers a reason to follow so they don't miss part 2.
3. **Say why to follow, briefly.** "Follow for a new one every Tuesday" at the end works better than "like and follow."
4. **Reply to comments with videos.** It turns your audience's questions into new videos and shows others you engage.
5. **Post consistently.** Three to five videos a week is a sustainable pace for most people. Use the [best time to post on TikTok](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) as a starting point.
6. **Collaborate.** Duets, stitches and joint LIVEs put you in front of someone else's followers.
7. **Go LIVE once you can.** LIVE viewers who chat with you are likely to follow.

## How to get your first 1,000 followers on TikTok

The first 1,000 are the slowest. A simple 30-day plan:

- **Week 1:** Pick your topic, fix your profile and post five videos that each answer one question your audience has.
- **Week 2:** Look at which video kept people watching longest. Make three more on that angle.
- **Week 3:** Turn the best one into a series. Reply to every comment, and answer one with a video.
- **Week 4:** Keep the pace and try one trend in your niche.

Most accounts get there by posting 30 to 60 videos, not by one lucky hit. To keep the pace without filming daily, batch-film on one day and schedule the rest. ## How many hashtags should you use on TikTok?

Three to five relevant hashtags is a sensible default. TikTok doesn't publish an ideal number; hashtags help it understand your topic, but they don't unlock reach on their own. A good mix:

- One or two **specific** tags for your niche (#mealprepideas, not #food)
- One **community** tag your audience follows (#booktok, #smallbusiness)
- One **trend** tag only if the video belongs to that trend

Skip strings of generic tags like #fyp #viral #foryou; there's no evidence they help. Our [TikTok caption generator](/tools/tiktok-caption-generator) can suggest a caption, and the [TikTok caption character limit](/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit) guide covers how much room you have.

## What not to do

- **Don't buy followers or use follow-for-follow apps.** Fake or uninterested followers lower your engagement rate and can break the Terms of Service.
- **Don't delete and repost underperforming videos.** It rarely helps and can look like spam.
- **Don't mass-follow strangers.** It can trigger a follow limit; see [why you can't follow people on TikTok](/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok).

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to get 1,000 followers on TikTok?
It varies widely. Accounts posting several times a week on a clear topic often reach 1,000 within a few months; a video that takes off can do it in a day.

### Does TikTok give followers for posting more?
Not directly. More videos give you more chances to reach new people on the For You feed, and a profile full of good videos converts more of them.

### Is it worth buying TikTok followers?
No. Bought followers don't watch or engage, which hurts how your videos perform, and buying them can break TikTok's rules.

### Do hashtags get you more followers on TikTok?
Only indirectly. A few relevant hashtags help TikTok show the video to the right people; the video and your profile decide whether they follow.
