Your last video got thousands of views and a handful of follows, and you're wondering what you're doing wrong. To get more followers on TikTok, post a steady run of videos on one clear topic, so someone who likes one video expects more of the same if they follow. Fix your profile, post three to five times a week, make series people want to finish, and reply to comments.

![How to get more TikTok followers: one clear topic, a profile that converts, series and a steady posting pace](/images/blog/how-to-get-more-followers-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Views come from the For You feed. Follows come later, when a viewer taps your profile and decides whether there's more they want. Most advice focuses on the first part and ignores the second.

This guide covers why views don't turn into follows, the profile fixes that change that, seven ways to grow, how many hashtags to use, and a 30-day plan for your first 1,000.

## Why 50,000 Views Can Bring Only 20 Followers

![Why TikTok views don't become followers: a one-off video, an unclear profile, and no reason to come back](/images/blog/how-to-get-more-followers-on-tiktok-why-1200x675.webp)

A video can get 50,000 views and 20 follows. That usually means one of three things:

- **The video was a one-off.** Viewers liked it, but your profile doesn't show more like it.
- **Your profile is unclear.** No bio, no photo, or a name that doesn't hint at what you post.
- **There's no reason to come back.** Nothing tells people a part two, a next tip or a weekly series is coming.

### What Most People Don't Know

A follow is a bet on your next video, not a reward for your last one. People follow when your profile makes the next video predictable: same topic, same kind of value, on a pace they can count on. A viral video on a random topic often brings views and very few follows for exactly that reason.

> **Key point:** Views measure one video. Follows measure your profile. If views are fine and follows aren't, fix the profile and the pattern, not the video.

## 7 Ways to Get More Followers on TikTok

![Seven ways to grow on TikTok, from sticking to one niche to going LIVE](/images/blog/how-to-get-more-followers-on-tiktok-ways-1200x675.webp)

### 1. Stick to One Niche

One audience, one kind of value. It's fine to show personality, but the core should stay the same.

### 2. Make Series

"Part 1 of 5" gives viewers a reason to follow so they don't miss part 2.

### 3. Say Why to Follow, Briefly

"Follow for a new one every Tuesday" at the end works better than "like and follow".

### 4. Reply to Comments With Videos

It turns your audience's questions into new videos and shows others you engage.

### 5. Post Consistently

Three to five videos a week is a sustainable pace for most people. Use the [best time to post on TikTok](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) as a starting point.

### 6. Collaborate

Duets, stitches and joint LIVEs put you in front of someone else's followers. See [how to duet on TikTok](/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok).

### 7. Go LIVE Once You Can

LIVE viewers who chat with you are likely to follow. Our guide on [how to go live on TikTok](/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok) covers the requirements.

## How to Get Your First 1,000 Followers on TikTok (30-Day Plan)

The first 1,000 are the slowest. A simple plan:

| Week | Focus | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Foundation | Pick your topic, fix your profile and post five videos that each answer one question your audience has |
| Week 2 | Learn | Find which video kept people watching longest and make three more on that angle |
| Week 3 | Series | Turn the best one into a series, reply to every comment, and answer one with a video |
| Week 4 | Pace | Keep posting and try one trend in your niche |

Most accounts get there by posting 30 to 60 videos, not by one lucky hit.

## How Many Hashtags Should You Use on TikTok? (3 to 5)

Three to five relevant hashtags is a sensible default. TikTok doesn't publish an ideal number; hashtags help it understand your topic, but they don't unlock reach on their own. A good mix:

- One or two **specific** tags for your niche (#mealprepideas, not #food)
- One **community** tag your audience follows (#booktok, #smallbusiness)
- One **trend** tag only if the video belongs to that trend

Skip strings of generic tags like #fyp #viral #foryou; there's no evidence they help. The [TikTok caption generator](/tools/tiktok-caption-generator) can suggest a caption, and the [TikTok caption character limit](/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit) guide covers how much room you have.

## Bought Followers and Follow-for-Follow Apps (Use With Caution)

![Checklist of TikTok growth habits that work, like series and replies, and shortcuts that backfire, like bought followers](/images/blog/how-to-get-more-followers-on-tiktok-shortcuts-1200x675.webp)

Services that sell followers and apps that trade follows promise a bigger number overnight. The number does go up. That's the only thing that does.

### The Real Risks

- **Fake followers don't watch.** They lower your engagement rate, which drags down how your videos perform.
- **It can break TikTok's Terms of Service.** Buying followers or using follow-trading apps puts your account at risk.
- **Mass-following can trigger limits.** Following strangers in bulk can hit a follow limit; see [why you can't follow people on TikTok](/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok).
- **Deleting and reposting flops rarely helps** and can look like spam.

### A Safer Checklist

- Grow from videos, replies and collaborations.
- Never give your TikTok login to a growth service.
- Follow people you actually want to see, at a normal pace.
- Leave underperforming videos up and learn from them.

> **Bottom line:** A bought follower is a viewer who will never watch. It makes the number bigger and the account weaker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to get 1,000 followers on TikTok?

It varies widely. Accounts posting several times a week on a clear topic often reach 1,000 within a few months; a video that takes off can do it in a day.

### Does TikTok give followers for posting more?

Not directly. More videos give you more chances to reach new people on the For You feed, and a profile full of good videos converts more of them.

### Is it worth buying TikTok followers?

No. Bought followers don't watch or engage, which hurts how your videos perform, and buying them can break TikTok's rules.

### Do hashtags get you more followers on TikTok?

Only indirectly. A few relevant hashtags help TikTok show the video to the right people; the video and your profile decide whether they follow.

### How many hashtags should I use on TikTok?

Three to five relevant ones is a sensible default. TikTok doesn't publish an ideal number.

### Why do I get views but no followers?

Usually because the video was a one-off, your profile doesn't show what you post, or nothing gives viewers a reason to come back. Fix the profile and start a series.

### How often should I post on TikTok to grow?

Three to five videos a week is sustainable for most people. Consistency matters more than any single day.

### Should I delete TikTok videos that flopped?

Usually not. Deleting and reposting rarely helps and can look like spam. Learn from them and post the next one.
