You posted a great story yesterday and now it's gone, or you want a "Prices" circle under your bio that never disappears. To make a highlight on Instagram, open your profile and tap **New** (the **+** circle under your bio), choose stories from your archive, tap **Next**, name the highlight and pick a cover, then tap **Add** or **Done**. You can also tap **Highlight** at the bottom of a story while it's live.

![How to make highlights on Instagram: turn on the story archive, tap New on your profile, pick stories, name it and add a cover](/images/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

Highlights stay on your profile until you remove them, unlike stories, which disappear after 24 hours. The part nobody mentions is that highlights are built from your **story archive**, so if archiving was off, the stories you want may not be there.

This guide covers the archive setting, both ways to create a highlight, the trick for making one without a public story, editing and covers, and what to avoid.

## Why Highlights Depend on Your Story Archive

![Stories disappear after 24 hours while highlights stay on your profile, as long as the story archive is on](/images/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram-archive-1200x675.webp)

A highlight isn't a separate upload. It's a saved collection of stories pulled from your archive. Instagram saves expired stories to your archive automatically unless you turned that off.

| | Stories | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| How long they show | 24 hours | Until you remove them |
| Where they appear | The Stories bar | The circles under your bio |
| Where they come from | What you post | Your story archive |

### What Most People Don't Know

Stories you posted while the archive was off aren't saved, so they can't be added to highlights later unless you saved them to your phone. Checking the setting takes 20 seconds:

1. Go to your profile and open the menu.
2. Tap **Archiving and downloading** (under Settings and activity).
3. Make sure **Save story to archive** is on.

> **Key point:** If the archive is on, any story you've ever posted can become a highlight, even one from years ago.

## How to Make a Highlight on Instagram From Your Profile (7 Steps)

![Seven steps to make an Instagram highlight from your profile, with picking stories from the archive highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram-profile-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Tap your profile picture in the bottom corner.

### Step 2: Tap New

Tap **New** (the circle with a **+** below your bio). If you don't see it, tap **+** at the top and choose **Story highlight**.

### Step 3: Select Stories From Your Archive

Tap one or more stories. They're listed by date, newest first.

### Step 4: Tap Next

The order you tapped them in is the order they'll play.

### Step 5: Name It Short

Type a name. Short names fit best under the circle; long ones get cut off.

### Step 6: Tap Edit Cover

Choose a cover image, or keep the first story as the cover. This is the step people skip, and it's why so many profiles end up with a row of random thumbnails.

### Step 7: Tap Add or Done

> **Pro tip:** Plan your highlight names before you start: **Start here**, **Prices**, **Reviews**, **How it works**, **FAQ**, **New**, and one per product or service work well for businesses and creators.

## How to Add a Live Story to a Highlight (Fastest Way)

1. Open your current story.
2. Tap **Highlight** (the heart icon) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Pick an existing highlight, or tap **New** to start one.

This is the quickest way to keep a story you just posted.

## How to Make a Highlight Without Posting a Public Story

![Two ways to make an Instagram highlight without a new public story: use an old archived story or post to a private Close Friends list](/images/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram-no-story-1200x675.webp)

Highlights have to come from stories, but the story doesn't have to be visible to everyone:

- **Use an old story from your archive.** The profile method works with any story in your archive.
- **Post to Close Friends.** Share the story to a Close Friends list with only yourself or one trusted person on it, then add it to a highlight. Once it's in a highlight, it's visible to everyone who can see your profile.

## How to Edit, Reorder or Delete Instagram Highlights

| What you want | How |
| --- | --- |
| Add more stories | Long-press the highlight, tap **Edit highlight**, then **Stories** or **Archive** |
| Remove one story | Open the highlight, go to that story, tap **More** (three dots), then **Remove from highlight** |
| Change the name or cover | Long-press the highlight, tap **Edit highlight** |
| Reorder highlights | Add a new story to a highlight and it moves to the front; Instagram has no drag-to-reorder |
| Delete a highlight | Long-press it and tap **Delete highlight** (the stories stay in your archive) |

## Instagram Highlight Covers That Look Clean (1080 × 1920)

![What makes clean Instagram highlight covers compared with covers that look messy in small circles](/images/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram-covers-1200x675.webp)

Covers show as small circles, so simple works best: one icon or one word on a plain background in your brand colors.

1. Make each cover as a 1080 × 1920 image with the design in the center.
2. Post it to Close Friends, or upload it directly in **Edit cover**.
3. Set it as the cover.
4. Keep a matching set so your profile looks consistent.

| Works well | Looks messy |
|---|---|
| One icon or one word | Full sentences |
| Plain background in brand colors | Busy photos |
| Design centered in the frame | Text near the edges, cropped by the circle |

## "Anonymous Highlight Viewer" Sites (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to view Instagram highlights compared with anonymous story viewer sites](/images/blog/how-to-make-highlights-on-instagram-viewers-1200x675.webp)

Once you're making highlights, you'll notice sites that promise to show anyone's stories and highlights anonymously. They're popular, and they come with real downsides.

### The Real Risks

- **Some ask for your Instagram login.** With it, they can post, message and change settings as you.
- **They break Instagram's terms.** Automated scraping isn't allowed, and accounts linked to it can be restricted.
- **They don't work on private accounts.** Anything claiming otherwise is a scam.
- **Ads and malware.** Many are covered in pop-ups and fake download buttons.

### A Safer Checklist

- View public highlights in the Instagram app or on the web.
- Never type your Instagram password into another site.
- Remember that viewers of a story in a highlight are only visible to the owner for the first 48 hours after it was posted.

> **Bottom line:** There's nothing a viewer site shows that's worth your password. Public highlights are already visible in Instagram.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you make a highlight without posting a story?

Not directly, but you can use any story already in your archive, or post a story to a Close Friends list with only you on it and then highlight it.

### Can people see who viewed my highlights?

You can see viewers of a story in a highlight only during the first 48 hours after the story was posted. After that, Instagram doesn't show who viewed it.

### How many highlights can you have on Instagram?

Instagram doesn't set a public limit on the number of highlights. Each highlight can hold up to 100 stories at the time of writing.

### Why can't I see my archived stories when making a highlight?

Story archiving was probably off when you posted them. Turn on Save story to archive so future stories are kept.

### Do highlights notify followers?

No. Creating or editing a highlight doesn't send a notification.

### How do I reorder highlights on Instagram?

Add a new story to the highlight you want first; it moves to the front. Instagram has no drag-to-reorder at the time of writing.

### Does deleting a highlight delete the stories?

No. The stories stay in your archive, and you can add them to another highlight later.

### What size should Instagram highlight covers be?

Make them 1080 × 1920 with the design in the center, since Instagram crops covers to a small circle.
