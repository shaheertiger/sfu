You entered the wrong birthday when you signed up, and now TikTok thinks you're younger than you are, with LIVE, DMs or gifts blocked. **You can't change your age on TikTok from your profile or settings, because TikTok locks the date of birth you entered at sign-up.** To fix it, you have to ask TikTok, either through an **age appeal** (offered when a feature is blocked or your account is restricted because of your age) or through **Settings and privacy → Report a problem**.

![How to change your age on TikTok: there's no birthday field, so use an age appeal or Report a problem and verify your age](/images/blog/how-to-change-age-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Either way, TikTok asks you to confirm your age with a method such as an ID, a selfie for age estimation or a card check, and updates your birthday if the check passes.

This guide covers why the birthday is locked, both routes step by step, what to have ready, whether you can change your region, and what to avoid so you don't lose the account.

## Why TikTok Won't Let You Edit Your Birthday (Age Decides Your Features)

![What TikTok features change with age, from private-by-default under 16 to LIVE hosting and gifts at 18](/images/blog/how-to-change-age-on-tiktok-ages-1200x675.webp)

Age decides which features you get. Accounts for under-16s are private by default and can't use direct messages, under-18s can't host LIVE or send and receive gifts, and in the US people under 13 get a separate, limited experience. If anyone could edit their birthday, those protections would mean nothing.

| Age (at the time of writing) | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Under 13 (US) | Separate limited experience, no posting or comments |
| 13 to 15 | Private by default, no DMs, videos not recommended in some places |
| 16 to 17 | DMs allowed, but no LIVE hosting and no gifts |
| 18 and over | LIVE hosting, gifts, creator monetization, TikTok Shop selling |

Thresholds differ by country and change over time, so check TikTok's Safety Center for your region.

### What Most People Don't Know

An age check works in both directions. If verification shows you're under 13, TikTok will remove the account rather than correct it. So only appeal with your true date of birth, and don't ask for a change on an account someone else set up for you with a different birthday.

> **Key point:** TikTok only changes a date of birth after checking it. There's no setting, trick or support script that skips the verification step.

## Route 1: Change Your Age Through an Age Appeal (Fastest When Something's Blocked)

![Four steps to change your age on TikTok with an age appeal: open the notice, tap Appeal, verify, wait](/images/blog/how-to-change-age-on-tiktok-appeal-1200x675.webp)

If TikTok blocks a feature or restricts your account because of your age, the notice usually includes an **Appeal** option. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Open the Age Notice

Open the notification in your inbox, or the banner on the blocked feature.

### Step 2: Tap Appeal, Not Back

Tap **Appeal** (sometimes **Verify age**). This is the step people miss: they close the notice and then can't find the option again.

### Step 3: Choose a Verification Method

Depending on your country, the options include a government ID, a selfie video for age estimation, or a credit card authorization.

### Step 4: Submit and Wait

Appeals usually take a few days. If the appeal succeeds, TikTok updates your date of birth.

> **Pro tip:** You have a limited window to appeal after losing access, so don't leave it for months. Appeal the day you see the notice.

## Route 2: Ask Through Report a Problem (When Nothing Is Blocked Yet)

If your age is wrong but nothing is blocked:

1. Go to **Profile**, tap the menu (three lines) and open **Settings and privacy**.
2. Tap **Report a problem**, then pick the account and profile category. Labels vary by version; look for something like **Account and profile → Editing profile → Other**.
3. Explain that your date of birth was entered incorrectly and give the right one.
4. Follow the steps TikTok sends you to verify your age.

Some versions show **Chat with us** or a support form instead. Either way, someone at TikTok has to approve the change.

### Age Appeal vs Report a Problem

| | Age appeal | Report a problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When to use it | A feature is blocked or the account restricted | Your age is wrong, nothing blocked yet |
| Where to find it | The notice or the blocked feature | Settings and privacy |
| Verification | ID, selfie or card, by country | The same, sent after your report |
| Time limit | A limited window after losing access | None stated |
| Typical wait | A few days | A few days, sometimes longer |

## What to Have Ready Before You Verify (3 Things)

- **A clear photo of a government ID** that shows your date of birth. Cover anything TikTok doesn't ask for, if the flow allows it.
- **Good lighting for a selfie check**, if you choose age estimation.
- **Access to the email or phone number on the account**, since TikTok may contact you there.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Appeal button | Use Report a problem instead | Both reach the same review |
| Selfie check fails | Retry in daylight, face the camera, or use ID | Age estimation needs a clear face |
| No ID option in your country | Try the selfie or card option | Options differ by region |
| No reply after a week | Check the email on the account, then report again | TikTok may have asked for more |

## Can You Change Your Region on TikTok? (No, Not in Settings)

![What changes what you see on TikTok, like language settings, next to region workarounds that put your account at risk](/images/blog/how-to-change-age-on-tiktok-region-1200x675.webp)

TikTok decides your region from signals such as your SIM card, IP address and device location, and there's no **Change region** option. You can change your **app language** and **content language preferences**, which shift some of what you see. Moving to another country, with a local SIM, moves your region over time.

## VPNs, Foreign SIMs and Borrowed Accounts (Use With Caution)

When the official route is slow, workarounds look tempting: a VPN to change region, a foreign SIM, or an account someone else set up with an older birthday.

### The Real Risks

- **VPNs and foreign SIMs** break TikTok's terms in some cases and often don't work anyway.
- **Accounts set up with someone else's date of birth** can't be fixed: age disputes on those can't be resolved.
- **A failed check can cost you the account**, if it shows you're under 13.

### A Safer Checklist

- Enter your real birthday at sign-up. It's the one detail you can't fix yourself.
- For a business, sign up as the person who will run it, aged 18 or over, then switch to a **Business account** in settings.
- Don't buy or share accounts.

> **Bottom line:** None of these workarounds is worth the risk to an account you care about. Appeal with your real date of birth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I change my birthday on TikTok?

You can't edit it directly. Use the age appeal on a blocked feature, or go to Settings and privacy → Report a problem and ask for a date-of-birth correction, then verify your age.

### How long does an age change take on TikTok?

Usually a few days once you've submitted your verification, though it can take longer during busy periods.

### What ID does TikTok accept to verify age?

At the time of writing, TikTok accepts government-issued IDs and, in many countries, a selfie for age estimation or a credit card authorization. The options depend on where you live.

### Can I change my age on TikTok without ID?

In some countries, yes, using the selfie age-estimation or card option. Where those aren't available, an ID is the only route.

### Will changing my age delete my account?

Not if you're 13 or older. If verification shows you're under 13, TikTok removes the account.

### Can I change my region on TikTok?

Not in settings. TikTok sets your region from your SIM, IP address and device location. You can change your app and content language, which shifts some of what you see.

### Why can't I go LIVE on TikTok?

Hosting a LIVE needs you to be 18 or over at the time of writing, along with TikTok's other requirements. If your age is wrong, fix it with an age appeal first.

### Can I make a new account with the right age instead?

You can create a new account with your real birthday, but you'd start from zero followers. Never create one to get around a restriction, which breaks TikTok's rules.
