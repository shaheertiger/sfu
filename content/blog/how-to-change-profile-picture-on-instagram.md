To change your DP (display picture) on Instagram, open your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then **Edit picture or avatar** and choose **New profile picture**. Pick a photo, move and zoom it inside the circle, and tap **Done** or the tick. On a computer, go to instagram.com, open your profile and click your current picture. The new photo appears on your profile, comments and messages right away, though other people's apps may show the old one for a little while.

Here are the steps for each device, the size that looks sharp, and fixes for common problems.

## Change your profile picture in the Instagram app

1. Tap your profile picture in the bottom-right corner to open your profile.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap **Edit picture or avatar** (on some versions, tap the picture itself).
4. Choose **New profile picture** to pick from your gallery, or **Take photo**.
5. Drag and pinch to frame your face or logo inside the circle.
6. Tap **Next** or the tick to save.

Some versions ask whether to **share your profile picture as a post**. Turn it off if you only want the picture changed.

The other options in the same menu:

- **Import from Facebook** uses your Facebook profile photo, if your accounts are linked in Accounts Center.
- **Create avatar** builds a cartoon avatar to use instead of a photo.
- **Remove current picture** goes back to the default grey silhouette.

## Change your profile picture on desktop

1. Go to instagram.com and log in.
2. Click **Profile** in the left menu.
3. Click your current profile picture (or **Edit profile**, then **Change photo**).
4. Choose **Upload photo** and pick a file from your computer.

The photo saves as soon as you pick it. There's no cropping tool on desktop, so crop to a square before uploading.

## Best size for an Instagram profile picture

| Setting | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Square file, shown as a circle |
| Size | At least 320 × 320 px; 1080 × 1080 px is safe |
| Displayed at | About 110 × 110 px on phones |
| Format | JPG or PNG |
| Safe zone | Keep the face or logo in the middle 70%, the corners are cut off |

A logo with fine text becomes unreadable at that size. Use the symbol or a short wordmark, with plenty of space around it. The [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) crops an image to a square at the right size.

## When the new picture won't show

- **The old photo still shows.** Instagram's servers and other people's apps cache pictures. Give it up to a few hours, then close and reopen the app.
- **"Couldn't change profile photo" or an endless spinner.** Check your connection, update the app, and try a smaller JPG. Large HEIC files from iPhones sometimes fail; converting to JPG fixes it.
- **The photo looks blurry.** Upload a sharper, larger square image. A photo zoomed in a lot inside the circle loses detail.
- **The option is greyed out.** You may have changed it many times in a short period. Wait a while and try again.

## Keep your profile picture the same across platforms

A consistent picture helps people recognize you when they see you on TikTok, Threads or LinkedIn. If you run several accounts, update each platform the same day, and make sure the picture works small, because most people see it next to a caption in their feed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Instagram notify people when I change my profile picture?
No. There's no notification. The only way followers see it as an update is if you choose to share the new picture as a post.

### Can I see someone's Instagram profile picture in full size?
In the app it only shows as a small circle; long-pressing it shows a slightly larger preview on some versions. Instagram doesn't offer a full-size view, and sites that claim to are unofficial.

### Why can't I change my Instagram profile picture?
Usually a connection or file problem. Update the app, try a JPG under a few megabytes, or change it from instagram.com instead.

### How do I remove my profile picture?
Go to Edit profile, tap Edit picture or avatar, and choose Remove current picture.

### What does DP mean on Instagram?
DP stands for display picture, another name for your profile picture.
