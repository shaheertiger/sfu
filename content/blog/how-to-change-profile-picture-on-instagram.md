You've got a better photo, a new logo or just a fresh haircut, and you want it on your profile. To change your DP (display picture) on Instagram, open your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then **Edit picture or avatar** and choose **New profile picture**. Pick a photo, move and zoom it inside the circle, and tap **Done** or the tick.

![How to change your profile picture on Instagram: Edit profile, Edit picture, New profile picture, then frame and save](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

On a computer, go to instagram.com, open your profile and click your current picture. The new photo appears on your profile, comments and messages right away, though other people's apps may show the old one for a little while.

The steps are easy. What isn't obvious is why a sharp photo can end up blurry, or why the old picture keeps showing. This guide covers the app and desktop steps, the size that stays sharp, fixes for common problems, and how to remove a picture.

## Why Your Instagram Profile Picture Matters More Than Its Size Suggests

Your profile picture is shown tiny, about 110 × 110 px on phones, but it appears next to everything you do: every post in the feed, every comment, every message and every Story ring. For most people it's the first thing they recognize you by.

![Where your Instagram profile picture appears, and why it has to work at a small size](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-instagram-why-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Instagram doesn't notify anyone when you change your picture. Nobody gets a "new profile photo" alert unless you choose to share the new picture as a post. And because the photo is cropped to a circle, the corners of your square image are cut off, which is where a lot of logos lose their edges.

> **Key point:** Design the picture for a small circle, not a big square. Keep the face or logo in the middle, skip fine text, and use a sharp square file so Instagram has enough detail to work with.

## How to Change Your Profile Picture in the Instagram App (6 Steps)

Menu labels change between app versions, so if a button reads slightly differently, pick the closest match.

![Six steps to change your profile picture in the Instagram app](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-instagram-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Tap your profile picture in the bottom-right corner.

### Step 2: Tap Edit Profile

It's the button under your bio.

### Step 3: Tap Edit Picture or Avatar

On some versions, tap the picture itself.

### Step 4: Choose New Profile Picture

Pick from your gallery, or choose **Take photo**.

### Step 5: Pinch to Frame, Don't Over-Zoom

This is the step people get wrong. Drag and pinch to put your face or logo in the middle of the circle, but don't zoom in a lot: every bit of zoom throws away detail, and that's the most common cause of a blurry DP.

### Step 6: Tap Next or the Tick to Save

Some versions ask whether to **share your profile picture as a post**. Turn it off if you only want the picture changed.

> **Pro tip:** Crop your photo to a square in your phone's photo editor first, with your face already centered. Then you barely need to zoom in Instagram, and the picture stays sharp.

### The Other Options in the Same Menu

- **Import from Facebook** uses your Facebook profile photo, if your accounts are linked in Accounts Center.
- **Create avatar** builds a cartoon avatar to use instead of a photo.
- **Remove current picture** goes back to the default grey silhouette.

## How to Change Your Instagram Profile Picture on Desktop (4 Steps)

1. Go to instagram.com and log in.
2. Click **Profile** in the left menu.
3. Click your current profile picture (or **Edit profile**, then **Change photo**).
4. Choose **Upload photo** and pick a file from your computer.

The photo saves as soon as you pick it. There's no cropping tool on desktop, so crop to a square before uploading.

| | App | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crop and zoom inside the circle | Yes | No: crop before uploading |
| Take a new photo | Yes | No |
| Import from Facebook or create an avatar | Yes | Not always |
| Option to share the picture as a post | On some versions | No |
| Best for | Quick changes | Uploading a carefully prepared file |

## Best Size for an Instagram Profile Picture (Sharp, Not Blurry)

![What makes an Instagram profile picture sharp compared with what makes it blurry](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-instagram-size-1200x675.webp)

| Setting | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Square file, shown as a circle |
| Size | At least 320 × 320 px; 1080 × 1080 px is safe |
| Displayed at | About 110 × 110 px on phones |
| Format | JPG or PNG |
| Safe zone | Keep the face or logo in the middle 70%, the corners are cut off |

A logo with fine text becomes unreadable at that size. Use the symbol or a short wordmark, with plenty of space around it. The [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) crops an image to a square at the right size.

## New Profile Picture Won't Show? 4 Fixes

![Common problems when changing an Instagram profile picture and the fix for each one](/images/blog/how-to-change-profile-picture-on-instagram-fixes-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The old photo still shows | Wait up to a few hours, then close and reopen the app | Instagram's servers and other people's apps cache pictures |
| "Couldn't change profile photo" or an endless spinner | Check your connection, update the app, try a smaller JPG | Large HEIC files from iPhones sometimes fail; converting to JPG fixes it |
| The photo looks blurry | Upload a sharper, larger square image and zoom less | A photo zoomed in a lot inside the circle loses detail |
| The option is greyed out | Wait a while and try again | You may have changed it many times in a short period |

If the app keeps failing, change the picture from instagram.com instead. It uses a different upload path and often works when the app doesn't.

## How to Remove Your Instagram Profile Picture

1. Open your profile and tap **Edit profile**.
2. Tap **Edit picture or avatar**.
3. Choose **Remove current picture**.

Your profile goes back to the default grey silhouette. You can add a new picture at any time.

## Full-Size DP Viewers and Downloaders (Use With Caution)

Search for an Instagram profile picture and you'll find sites that promise to show anyone's DP in full size, or download it.

### The Real Risks

- **They're unofficial.** Instagram doesn't offer a full-size view, so these sites scrape images in ways Instagram doesn't allow.
- **Login requests.** Some ask you to log in with Instagram. Never give a third-party site your password.
- **Ads and malware.** Many are packed with pop-ups, fake download buttons and redirects.

### Safety Checklist

- Don't enter your Instagram login on any viewer site.
- Don't install apps that promise to show "who viewed your profile" or full-size pictures.
- If you've already logged in to one, change your password and turn on two-factor authentication.

> **Bottom line:** Instagram shows profile pictures as small circles on purpose. Sites that get around that aren't worth the risk to your account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Instagram notify people when I change my profile picture?

No. There's no notification. The only way followers see it as an update is if you choose to share the new picture as a post.

### Can I see someone's Instagram profile picture in full size?

Not officially. In the app it only shows as a small circle; long-pressing it shows a slightly larger preview on some versions. Sites that claim to show full size are unofficial.

### Why can't I change my Instagram profile picture?

Usually a connection or file problem. Update the app, try a JPG under a few megabytes, or change it from instagram.com instead.

### How do I remove my profile picture?

Go to Edit profile, tap Edit picture or avatar, and choose Remove current picture.

### What does DP mean on Instagram?

DP stands for display picture, another name for your profile picture.

### Why does my Instagram profile picture look blurry?

Usually because the file is small or you zoomed in a lot inside the circle. Upload a sharper square image of at least 320 × 320 px (1080 × 1080 px is safe) and zoom less.

### What size should an Instagram profile picture be?

A square file of at least 320 × 320 px, with 1080 × 1080 px as a safe choice. It's shown at about 110 × 110 px on phones.

### How long does it take for a new profile picture to show?

It updates on your profile right away. Other people's apps may show the old picture for up to a few hours because of caching.
