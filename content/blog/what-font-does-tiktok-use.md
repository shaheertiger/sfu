You're designing a thumbnail or a slide and want it to look like it belongs on TikTok. TikTok uses **TikTok Sans**, its own custom typeface. TikTok introduced it in May 2023, designed with the Swiss type foundry Grilli Type, and it runs through the whole app: menus, captions, and the default text you add on top of videos.

![What font does TikTok use? TikTok Sans, introduced in 2023 and open source since 2025](/images/blog/what-font-does-tiktok-use-hero-1200x675.webp)

The surprise is that you can use it too. In July 2025 TikTok released TikTok Sans as an open-source font under the SIL Open Font License, so you can download it free and use it in your own designs.

This guide covers where TikTok Sans appears, how to tell it from the old font, where to download it safely, the text styles in TikTok's editor, and how "fonts" in bios really work.

## Where TikTok Sans Shows Up (and What It Replaced)

![Where TikTok Sans appears compared with Proxima Nova, the font TikTok used before 2023](/images/blog/what-font-does-tiktok-use-where-1200x675.webp)

| Place | Font |
| --- | --- |
| App interface, buttons and menus | TikTok Sans |
| Default "Classic" text on videos | TikTok Sans |
| Captions under videos | TikTok Sans (your device may substitute a system font) |
| Auto-captions | TikTok Sans |
| TikTok logo wordmark | A custom logotype, not a font you can type |

### What Most People Don't Know

Before TikTok Sans, the app used **Proxima Nova**, a commercial font by Mark Simonson. If you're matching an older screenshot or a template made before 2023, that's the font you're looking at. The quickest way to tell them apart is the zero: TikTok Sans has a slashed **0**, so it doesn't get mixed up with the letter O.

| | TikTok Sans | Proxima Nova |
|---|---|---|
| Used by TikTok | Since May 2023 | Before 2023 |
| Zero | Slashed | Plain |
| Price | Free, SIL Open Font License | Commercial license |
| Where to get it | TikTok's GitHub, Google Fonts | The foundry or licensed resellers |

> **Key point:** If it's TikTok after 2023, it's TikTok Sans, and it's free for personal and commercial work.

## How to Download TikTok Sans (Free, 2 Official Sources)

![How to get TikTok Sans: download it from TikTok's GitHub or Google Fonts, install it, then pick it in your design app](/images/blog/what-font-does-tiktok-use-download-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Go to an Official Source

TikTok publishes the font in its official GitHub repository (tiktok/TikTokSans), and it's also on Google Fonts. Skip third-party "free font download" sites.

### Step 2: Install It

Download and install it on your computer, or check whether your design app already lists it.

### Step 3: Pick It in Your Design App

In Canva, CapCut or Figma, install TikTok Sans or pick it from the font list if the app already includes it.

### Step 4: Use the Right Weight and Width

It's a variable font with weight, width, slant and optical size axes, and covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic scripts. This is the step people miss: one font file covers many weights, so pick a heavier weight for bold headlines and a regular one for body text instead of using the default for everything.

> **Pro tip:** Because it's under the SIL Open Font License, you can use TikTok Sans in thumbnails, ads and slides, including paid work. You just can't sell the font file itself.

## The Text Styles Inside TikTok's Editor (5 Common Ones)

When you tap **Aa** (Text) in the TikTok editor, you'll see a handful of font styles. Names change between versions, but they usually include:

| Style | What it looks like |

## Fancy Fonts in Your TikTok Bio and Captions (Unicode, Not Fonts)

![How fancy fonts in TikTok bios work: Unicode characters that look styled, with accessibility and search trade-offs](/images/blog/what-font-does-tiktok-use-bio-fonts-1200x675.webp)

TikTok doesn't let you change the font of your bio, username or caption. The "fonts" you see in other people's bios are Unicode characters: letters from mathematical and special symbol sets that look like bold, italic or script text and can be pasted anywhere.

Our free [TikTok font generator](/tools/tiktok-font-generator) turns plain text into those styles so you can copy and paste them into your bio or caption.

| Use styled text for | Keep plain text for |
|---|---|
| One or two words of emphasis | Your name and key words people search |
| A short bio tagline | Anything screen-reader users need to hear |
| A caption hook | Your @username, which only accepts letters, numbers, underscores and periods |

Screen readers read many of these characters letter by letter or skip them, and search doesn't always match them. For bio ideas, try the [TikTok bio generator](/tools/tiktok-bio-generator).

## "Free Font Download" Sites and Unlicensed Proxima Nova (Use With Caution)

![Safe places to get TikTok's fonts compared with risky free font download sites](/images/blog/what-font-does-tiktok-use-download-risks-1200x675.webp)

Search "TikTok font download" and you'll find sites offering TikTok Sans, or "TikTok font" files that are really Proxima Nova, behind download buttons and pop-ups.

### The Real Risks

- **Malware.** Fake download buttons and bundled installers are common on free font sites.
- **Unlicensed fonts.** Proxima Nova is commercial; a free download of it doesn't give you a license, and using it in client or brand work can be a problem.
- **Wrong or outdated files.** You may get an old or incomplete version without the variable axes.

### A Safer Checklist

- Download TikTok Sans only from TikTok's GitHub or Google Fonts.
- Buy Proxima Nova from the foundry or a licensed reseller if you need the old look, or use a similar free sans-serif.
- Don't run installers that come with a font; a font file is all you need.

> **Bottom line:** TikTok Sans is free from official sources, so there's no reason to download it anywhere else.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What font are TikTok captions in?

Captions use TikTok Sans in the app. On some devices or browsers, your system's default sans-serif font may stand in.

### Is TikTok Sans free to use commercially?

Yes. It's released under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial use in designs and videos. You can't sell the font file itself.

### What font did TikTok use before 2023?

Proxima Nova, a commercial font by Mark Simonson. It's not free, so most people who want the old look use a similar free sans-serif instead.

### Can I change the font of my TikTok username?

Not with a setting. You can paste Unicode-styled text into your display name, but your @username only accepts letters, numbers, underscores and periods.

### Where can I download TikTok Sans?

From TikTok's official GitHub repository (tiktok/TikTokSans) or Google Fonts.

### Is the TikTok logo a font?

No. The wordmark is a custom logotype, not a font you can type.

### How do I tell TikTok Sans from Proxima Nova?

Look at the zero. TikTok Sans has a slashed 0; Proxima Nova doesn't.
