A pointing-finger emoji just appeared in one of your TikTok chats, or a notification says someone "nudged" you, and you're not sure whether you're meant to reply.

A **nudge** on TikTok is a quick poke to get someone's attention. In direct messages, tapping **Nudge** sends the other person a notification that you're waiting for a reply. On some creators' profiles, a nudge lets them know you'd like them to **go LIVE**. It's a light reminder, not a message, and the other person can ignore it.

![What does nudge mean on TikTok? A quick poke to ask for a reply or a LIVE, not a written message](/images/blog/what-does-nudge-mean-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

It's confusing because the word covers more than one feature, and TikTok tests features with some accounts before others, so you may not see every kind of nudge in your app.

This guide covers the different nudges, how to send one, how to ask a creator to go live, how to turn off read receipts, and what to do if someone keeps nudging you.

## The 3 Kinds of Nudge on TikTok (and What Each One Does)

"Nudging" simply means sending one of these.

| Where | What a nudge does | What the other person sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A chat in your inbox | Reminds them to reply | A notification and a pointing-finger emoji in the chat |
| A creator's profile (bell or LIVE option) | Asks them to go live | An alert that a viewer wants them to go LIVE |
| A reposted video (in some versions) | Lets the original creator know it was reposted | A notification about the repost |

![The three kinds of TikTok nudge: in chats, on creator profiles and on reposts](/images/blog/what-does-nudge-mean-on-tiktok-kinds-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

A nudge doesn't carry any words you've written. In a chat it's a notification plus a short nudge marker, so the other person knows you're waiting, but not what about. That makes it easy to ignore, and also easy to overuse.

> **Key point:** A nudge is a reminder, not a message. If you need something specific, write it. If you've already written it, one nudge is plenty.

## How to Nudge Someone in TikTok Messages (3 Steps)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the nearest match.

![Three steps to nudge someone in a TikTok chat](/images/blog/what-does-nudge-mean-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open the Conversation

Open **Inbox** and tap the conversation.

### Step 2: Find the Nudge Button

Look for **Nudge** above the message box, with a pointing-finger emoji. This is where people get stuck: it usually only appears when the other person hasn't replied in a while, so a fresh chat won't show it.

### Step 3: Tap It Once

TikTok sends the reminder.

> **Pro tip:** Use it sparingly. Repeated nudges can come across as pushy, and the person can mute or block you.

If you don't see the button, the feature isn't available on your account yet, or the chat doesn't qualify.

## Nudge vs Message vs Mention: How They Differ

A nudge sits somewhere between doing nothing and writing a message. Here's how it compares with the other ways to get someone's attention on TikTok.

| | Nudge | Direct message | Mention in a comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carries your own words | No | Yes | Yes |
| Where it appears | In the chat, with a notification | In the chat | Under a video, with a notification |
| Who else can see it | Only the two of you | Only the two of you | Anyone who reads the comments |
| Best for | A gentle reminder about an unanswered chat | Anything specific | Pointing a friend to a video |
| Can feel pushy if repeated | Yes | Yes | Less so |

If you're waiting on an answer to a question, a short follow-up message ("Any thoughts on Saturday?") usually works better than a nudge, because it reminds the person what you asked. Save the nudge for chats where your last message already says everything.

## How to Turn Off Read Receipts on TikTok (Read Status)

TikTok calls read receipts **read status**. Turning it off means people won't see when you've read their messages.

![How to turn off read status on TikTok in Direct messages settings](/images/blog/what-does-nudge-mean-on-tiktok-read-status-1200x675.webp)

1. Go to **Profile** and tap the **menu** (three lines).
2. Tap **Settings and privacy → Privacy → Direct messages**.
3. Turn off **Read status** (it may sit under "Message preferences").

Read status works both ways: if you turn yours off, you also can't see when others have read your messages. It only shows when both people have it on.

| Setting | What it controls | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Read status | Whether people see when you've read their messages | Privacy → Direct messages |
| Who can message you | Who can start a chat with you | Privacy → Direct messages |
| Group chat invites | Who can add you to group chats | Privacy → Direct messages |
| Activity status | Whether people see when you're online | Privacy → Activity status |

For more on chats, see [how to make a group chat on TikTok](/blog/how-to-make-a-group-chat-on-tiktok).

## Can You Turn Off Nudges? 3 Workarounds

There's no dedicated "turn off nudges" switch at the time of writing. If someone keeps nudging you:

1. **Mute the conversation.** Tap their name in the chat, then **Mute notifications**.
2. **Restrict who can message you** in Direct messages settings.
3. **Block them**, if it's unwanted.

| Situation | Best option |
|---|---|
| A friend who nudges a lot | Mute the chat |
| Strangers nudging you | Restrict who can message you |
| Someone who won't stop | Block them |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a nudge on TikTok send a message?

It sends a notification and a short nudge marker in the chat, not a text message you've written.

### Can someone see if I ignore a nudge?

Only through read status. If you both have read status on, they can see that you've opened the chat.

### Why can't I nudge someone on TikTok?

The button only appears in some chats and on some accounts while TikTok rolls the feature out. Update the app and check again later.

### Does turning off read status hide when I'm online?

No. Activity status is a separate setting in Settings and privacy, Privacy, Activity status.

### What does the pointing-finger emoji mean in a TikTok chat?

It marks a nudge: the other person wanted to remind you they're waiting for a reply.

### What does a LIVE nudge do?

It sends the creator an alert that a viewer would like them to go LIVE. They decide whether to start a stream.

### Can I turn off nudges completely?

Not at the time of writing. Mute the chat, restrict who can message you, or block the person.

### Is nudging someone rude?

Not once. Repeated nudges can feel pushy, so send one and wait.
