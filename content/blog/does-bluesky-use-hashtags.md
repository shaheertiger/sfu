You've moved some of your posting to Bluesky and you're not sure whether to keep your hashtags or drop them like many people do on X.

Yes, Bluesky uses hashtags. Type `#` followed by a word in a post and it becomes a clickable tag. Tapping it shows recent and top posts with that tag, and you can search for tags too. One or two relevant tags work best.

![Does Bluesky use hashtags? Yes: tags are clickable, searchable and power custom feeds](/images/blog/does-bluesky-use-hashtags-hero-1200x675.webp)

What isn't obvious is why tags matter more on Bluesky than on X or Threads. Many **custom feeds** are built around hashtags, so the right tag can put your post in front of everyone who subscribes to that feed, while the wrong ones get you muted.

This guide covers how hashtags work on Bluesky, where they help reach and where they don't, how many to use, how to find the right ones, and how to handle tags when you cross-post.

## Why Hashtags Matter on Bluesky (It's the Feeds)

Bluesky has no single algorithm that rewards hashtags. Its reach comes from feeds, reposts and replies. Feeds are where tags earn their place.

| Where | Do hashtags help? |
| --- | --- |
| Custom feeds built on tags (art, science, books, news) | Yes, the biggest effect |
| Search and tag pages | Yes, people browse popular tags |
| Following feed | No, it's chronological, so your followers see you either way |
| Discover feed | A little. Topic matters more than the tag itself |

![Where hashtags help reach on Bluesky and where they make no difference](/images/blog/does-bluesky-use-hashtags-where-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Communities on Bluesky agree on tags, such as `#ArtistsOnBluesky`, `#BookSky`, `#SciSky` or `#Photography`, and feed builders collect posts that use them. A post tagged `#ScienceSky` might show up in a science feed with tens of thousands of subscribers. The same post with ten generic tags looks like spam and gets muted.

Many feeds say in their description which tags they include, and some require a tag for a post to appear at all.

> **Key point:** On Bluesky, a hashtag is less a label and more an address. Use the tag a community's feed actually collects, and your post lands in front of that whole community.

## How Hashtags Work on Bluesky: 5 Rules

![Five rules for how hashtags work on Bluesky](/images/blog/does-bluesky-use-hashtags-rules-1200x675.webp)

### Rule 1: Any #Word Becomes Clickable

Any `#word` in a post links to a tag page. Tags can include letters, numbers and underscores, but not spaces or punctuation.

### Rule 2: Tags Are Searchable

Searching `#photography` returns posts with that tag. You can switch between Top and Latest.

### Rule 3: Tags Count Toward 300 Characters

The `#` and the word count in full against the [Bluesky character limit](/blog/bluesky-character-limit). Three long tags can take a real bite out of a 300-character post, which is the step people forget when they paste tags from Instagram.

> **Pro tip:** Write CamelCase tags (`#BookSky`, not `#booksky`). Case doesn't matter for search, but CamelCase is easier to read and works better with screen readers.

### Rule 4: People Can Mute Tags

People can add tags to their muted words to hide them, so a tag can also keep you out of some feeds. Off-topic or overused tags are the ones most often muted.

### Rule 5: Case Doesn't Change the Tag

`#BookSky` and `#booksky` lead to the same results, so pick the readable version. If you've used a lowercase tag for months, you don't need to change old posts: they already appear in the same search.

## How Many Hashtags to Use on Bluesky (1 to 3, Not 10)

![How many hashtags to use on Bluesky: one to three community tags, not a block of ten](/images/blog/does-bluesky-use-hashtags-how-many-1200x675.webp)

The right number depends on what you're posting and which feeds you want to reach. A few accurate tags beat a long list every time.

- **One or two** relevant tags is the norm. Three is fine for art and photography, where feeds rely on them.
- **Put them at the end** of the post, or work one into the sentence.
- **Use the community's tag**, not a made-up one. Search the tag first to see if people use it.
- **Skip trending tags** that have nothing to do with your post. People mute them and report off-topic tagging.

| Post type | Suggested tags | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Art or photography | 2–3 | Art and photo feeds collect by tag |
| Science, books, news | 1–2 community tags | Topic feeds use agreed tags |
| Personal update | 0–1 | Your followers see it in Following anyway |
| Link to a blog post or product | 1 | More looks promotional |

## How to Find the Right Bluesky Hashtags in 4 Steps

1. **Search your topic** in Bluesky and look at the tags on top posts.
2. **Browse Feeds** for your field and read how each one collects posts.
3. **Look at accounts you admire** in your niche and note which tags they repeat.
4. **Keep a short list** of three to five tags you rotate.

Menu labels in the Bluesky app change between versions, so if you can't find Feeds, look for it in the main navigation or search.

## Hashtag Stuffing and Trending Tags (Use With Caution)

It's tempting to add a block of popular tags, or a trending tag that has nothing to do with your post, to reach more people.

### The Real Risks

- **Muting.** People add overused tags to their muted words, so your post disappears for them.
- **Reports.** Off-topic tagging gets reported as spam.
- **Lost characters.** Ten tags can use a third of your 300 characters.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Every tag describes what the post is actually about.
- You've searched the tag and seen real people using it.
- No more than three tags per post.

> **Bottom line:** One accurate community tag beats ten popular ones on Bluesky.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do hashtags work on Bluesky?

Yes. They're clickable and searchable, and many custom feeds collect posts by hashtag.

### Do hashtags count toward Bluesky's 300-character limit?

Yes. The whole tag, including the `#`, counts.

### Can I follow a hashtag on Bluesky?

Not directly, but you can pin a tag search or subscribe to a custom feed built around that tag.

### Can I mute a hashtag on Bluesky?

Yes. Add it to your muted words and tags, which were under Settings, then Moderation, at the time of writing.

### How many hashtags should I use on Bluesky?

One or two for most posts, and up to three for art and photography. More than that tends to look like spam.

### Do hashtags help on the Following feed?

No. The Following feed is chronological, so your followers see your posts whether you tag them or not.

### Can hashtags contain spaces or punctuation?

No. Tags can include letters, numbers and underscores only.

### Will my X hashtags work on Bluesky?

The syntax works, but the community tags often differ. Search the tag on Bluesky first to see whether people use it there.
