Bluesky is worth it if your audience overlaps with the people who moved there: journalists, academics, developers, artists, writers and anyone who left X for reasons of tone. It is a smaller network than X, Threads or Instagram, with tens of millions of accounts and a few million active daily, but engagement per follower is high and reach doesn't depend on a single algorithm. If your customers are on Instagram and TikTok and nowhere else, you can skip it for now.

Here's what to weigh.

## What Bluesky is

Bluesky is a text-first social network built on the AT Protocol, an open standard. Posts are 300 characters with up to four images or a video. The difference from X and Threads is control: you choose your feeds (the default Following feed is chronological), you can subscribe to community-made feeds and moderation lists, and your handle can be your own domain, which doubles as verification.

## Who gets value from Bluesky

- **Writers, journalists and newsletters.** Link posts aren't suppressed, and the audience reads.
- **Developers and open-source projects.** The tech community is large and active.
- **Academics and researchers.** Many fields rebuilt their networks there.
- **Artists and photographers.** Image posts do well, and custom feeds surface niche work.
- **Political and news accounts in the US and Europe.** The most active segment of the network.

## Who should skip it, for now

- Consumer brands whose customers live on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.
- Local businesses; there's no local discovery and the user base is thin outside big cities.
- Anyone who needs ads. Bluesky has no ad platform at the time of writing.
- Video-first creators. Video exists but the network is built around text and images.

## What's missing

No native scheduling, no ads, no analytics beyond what you count yourself, no DMs with media, and a smaller audience than the big three. Growth comes from feeds and reposts rather than a discovery algorithm, so a new account grows slowly unless it's picked up by a popular feed or a well-followed reposter.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Bluesky still growing in 2026?
It grows in bursts around events on other platforms and is steadier between them. The active base is a few million daily users, concentrated in news, tech, academia and the arts.

### Is Bluesky better than Threads?
Different. Threads has a far bigger audience from Instagram and an algorithmic feed; Bluesky is smaller, chronological by default, with custom feeds and an open protocol. Many accounts post to both from one draft.

### Does Bluesky have ads?
No, not at the time of writing. Reach is organic only.

### Can you schedule posts on Bluesky?
Not in the Bluesky app. A scheduler connected with an app password can, and can post the same text to X and Threads at the same time.
