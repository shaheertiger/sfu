No, you can't edit a Bluesky post once it's published, at least not at the time of writing. The only way to change the text is to delete the post and publish a new one, which resets its likes, reposts and replies. Bluesky's team has said publicly that it is discussing an edit feature, with ideas like a short editing window, but nothing has shipped. Until it does, the practical fix is to catch mistakes before you post.

## Your options when a post has a mistake

| Situation | Best fix | What you lose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typo spotted within a minute, no engagement yet | Delete and repost | Nothing much |
| Small typo on a post that's getting likes | Leave it, or reply with the correction | Nothing |
| Wrong link, date or price | Reply to your own post with the fix, then pin or repost the reply | Nothing |
| Factually wrong or harmful post | Delete it, then post a correction | Likes, reposts and replies |
| Wrong image or missing alt text | Delete and repost | Likes, reposts and replies |

## How to delete and repost a Bluesky post

1. Open the post and tap the **three-dot menu**.
2. Choose **Copy post text** so you don't have to retype it.
3. Choose **Delete post** and confirm.
4. Start a new post, paste the text, fix it and re-attach any images or video.

Deleting removes the post from your profile and feeds, and replies to it lose their parent. People who quoted it will see that the quoted post was deleted. Menu labels can differ slightly between the app and the website.

## Why Bluesky doesn't have editing

Bluesky runs on the AT Protocol, where every post is a record that other apps and feeds can reference. A quote post points at the exact post it quotes, so if the text could change after the fact, someone could get a lot of reposts on a harmless post and then edit it into something else. That's the main reason the team has moved carefully. Browser extensions that claim to add an edit button work by deleting and reposting behind the scenes, so they reset engagement in the same way, and they need an app password to your account.

## Reply instead of deleting

A self-reply keeps everything the original post has earned. Start it with "Correction:" or "Edit:" so it's obvious, and keep it short. Readers who open the thread will see it right under the original. If the mistake matters, quote the post with the correction so your followers see it in their feeds too.

## Catch mistakes before they go live

Since you can't edit after publishing, the time to fix a post is before it goes out.

- **Use drafts.** Bluesky added drafts in early 2026: in the new post window, tap **Drafts** to save or reopen unfinished posts. Read the post again later before you publish.
- **Schedule it.** A scheduled post can be edited as many times as you like until it publishes. In Schedule For You, Bluesky posts sit in the same queue and calendar as X, Threads and LinkedIn, so you can reread a post an hour later, fix the wording or swap the image, and it goes out on time. The [Bluesky scheduler page](/bluesky) explains how to connect with an app password.
- **Check the length.** Bluesky posts are capped at 300 characters. Our guide to the [Bluesky character limit](/blog/bluesky-character-limit) covers how links and mentions count.
- **Write the thread in one go.** If a thought runs long, plan it as a thread rather than adding a reply later. In Schedule For You, a line with `---` splits a caption into thread posts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will Bluesky add an edit button?
Possibly. Bluesky's product team said in 2026 that it had started discussing an edit feature and that it would come with guardrails such as a short editing window. Nothing has launched at the time of writing.

### Can you edit alt text on Bluesky after posting?
No. Alt text is part of the post, so changing it means deleting and reposting with the new description.

### Does deleting a Bluesky post remove it everywhere?
It removes the post from Bluesky's app and feeds, and apps that follow the protocol stop showing it. Screenshots and copies other people made aren't affected.

### Can you edit a scheduled Bluesky post?
Yes, if it's scheduled in a tool rather than published. You can change the text, media or time until it goes live.
