You've just posted on Bluesky, the likes are coming in, and then you spot it: a typo, a wrong date or a broken link. You look for an edit button and there isn't one.

No, you can't edit a Bluesky post once it's published, at least not at the time of writing. The only way to change the text is to delete the post and publish a new one, which resets its likes, reposts and replies. For most mistakes, a reply with the correction is the better fix.

![Can you edit Bluesky posts? No, so reply with a correction or delete and repost, and catch mistakes before posting](/images/blog/can-you-edit-bluesky-posts-hero-1200x675.webp)

It isn't obvious because most other networks have added editing in some form, and some browser extensions claim to add it to Bluesky too. This guide covers why Bluesky has no edit button, which fix to use for which mistake, how to delete and repost safely, and how to stop mistakes reaching your feed in the first place.

## Why Bluesky Doesn't Let You Edit Posts (the Quote-Post Problem)

Bluesky runs on the AT Protocol, where every post is a record that other apps and feeds can reference. A quote post points at the exact post it quotes. If the text could change afterwards, someone could collect reposts on a harmless post and then edit it into something else, and every quote would now appear to endorse the new text.

![Why Bluesky has no edit button: quote posts point at the exact original post](/images/blog/can-you-edit-bluesky-posts-why-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Bluesky's team has said publicly that it is discussing an edit feature, with ideas such as a short editing window and other guardrails, but nothing has shipped at the time of writing. Bluesky did add **drafts** in early 2026, which helps with the other half of the problem: catching the mistake before you post.

> **Key point:** On Bluesky, "editing" always means deleting and reposting. Anything that claims otherwise is doing the same thing behind the scenes.

## Which Fix to Use: 5 Common Mistakes Compared

The right fix depends on how bad the mistake is and how much engagement the post already has.

| Situation | Best fix | What you lose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typo spotted within a minute, no engagement yet | Delete and repost | Nothing much |
| Small typo on a post that's getting likes | Leave it, or reply with the correction | Nothing |
| Wrong link, date or price | Reply to your own post with the fix, then pin or repost the reply | Nothing |
| Factually wrong or harmful post | Delete it, then post a correction | Likes, reposts and replies |
| Wrong image or missing alt text | Delete and repost | Likes, reposts and replies |

![Reply with a correction to keep engagement, or delete and repost when the post is wrong or harmful](/images/blog/can-you-edit-bluesky-posts-fixes-1200x675.webp)

### When a Reply Is Better Than Deleting

A self-reply keeps everything the original post has earned. Start it with "Correction:" or "Edit:" so it's obvious, and keep it short. Readers who open the thread will see it right under the original. If the mistake matters, quote the post with the correction so your followers see it in their feeds too.

### When Deleting Is the Only Option

Delete when the post is wrong in a way a reply can't fix: incorrect facts that could mislead people, something harmful, the wrong image, or missing alt text. Engagement is worth less than accuracy.

## How to Delete and Repost a Bluesky Post in 4 Steps

Menu labels can differ slightly between the app and the website, and they change between versions.

![Four steps to delete and repost a Bluesky post: open the menu, copy the text, delete, then repost](/images/blog/can-you-edit-bluesky-posts-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open the Post's Three-Dot Menu

Find the post on your profile or in your feed and tap the **three-dot menu** on it.

### Step 2: Copy the Text First, Don't Delete First

Choose **Copy post text** before anything else. This is the step people skip, and then they retype the post from memory and make a new mistake.

### Step 3: Delete the Post

Choose **Delete post** and confirm. The post disappears from your profile and feeds. Replies to it lose their parent, and people who quoted it will see that the quoted post was deleted.

### Step 4: Paste, Fix and Repost

Start a new post, paste the text, fix the mistake and re-attach any images or video, with their alt text.

> **Pro tip:** Before you delete, save any images from the original post and copy their alt text somewhere. Alt text is part of the post, so it goes when the post goes.

## What Happens When You Delete a Bluesky Post

| | Before deleting | After deleting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The post on your profile and in feeds | Visible | Gone |
| Likes and reposts | Counted | Lost; the new post starts at zero |
| Replies | Threaded under your post | Lose their parent post |
| Quote posts | Show your post | Show that the quoted post was deleted |
| Screenshots and copies others made | Exist | Still exist |

Apps that follow the protocol stop showing a deleted post. Screenshots and copies other people made aren't affected, so for anything sensitive, assume someone may already have seen it.

## Edge Cases: Alt Text, Threads and Pinned Posts

- **Alt text.** You can't change alt text after posting. Adding or fixing it means deleting and reposting.
- **A post in the middle of a thread.** Deleting it breaks the chain at that point. Often a reply at the end of the thread with the correction is cleaner.
- **A pinned post.** If you delete a pinned post, pin the new version afterwards.
- **Long thoughts.** If an idea runs long, plan it as a thread from the start rather than adding replies later. Bluesky posts are capped at 300 characters; see the [Bluesky character limit](/blog/bluesky-character-limit) for how links and mentions count.

## "Edit Button" Browser Extensions (Use With Caution)

![What Bluesky edit button extensions really do: they delete and repost, and need access to your account](/images/blog/can-you-edit-bluesky-posts-caution-1200x675.webp)

Search for a Bluesky edit button and you'll find browser extensions that claim to add one. They can't change how Bluesky works. They delete your post and publish a new one for you, so likes, reposts and replies reset exactly as if you'd done it by hand.

### The Real Risks

- **They need an app password to your account.** That gives the extension the ability to post and delete on your behalf.
- **Same engagement loss.** You lose the post's likes, reposts and replies anyway.
- **Unknown developers.** Extensions can be updated or sold to someone else after you install them.

### If You Use One Anyway

- Create a separate app password for it, so you can revoke just that one later.
- Revoke the app password in your Bluesky settings as soon as you stop using the extension.
- Never give any extension your main account password.

> **Bottom line:** An edit extension saves you a copy and paste. It doesn't save your likes, and it costs you some control over your account.

## Catch Mistakes Before They Go Live (Drafts and Scheduling)

Since you can't edit after publishing, the time to fix a post is before it goes out.

- **Use drafts.** In the new post window, tap **Drafts** to save or reopen unfinished posts (the label may change between versions). Reread the post later before you publish.
- **Read it out loud.** Typos in dates and prices are easy to miss on screen and easy to hear.
- **Check links in a browser** before you paste them in.
- **Schedule it.** A scheduled post can be changed as many times as you like until it publishes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you edit a Bluesky post after posting?

No. At the time of writing, Bluesky has no edit button. You can delete the post and publish a corrected one, or reply to it with a correction.

### Will Bluesky add an edit button?

Possibly. Bluesky's team said in 2026 that it had started discussing an edit feature with guardrails such as a short editing window. Nothing has launched at the time of writing.

### Can you edit alt text on Bluesky after posting?

No. Alt text is part of the post, so changing it means deleting and reposting with the new description.

### Does deleting a Bluesky post remove it everywhere?

It removes the post from Bluesky's app and feeds, and apps that follow the protocol stop showing it. Screenshots and copies other people made aren't affected.

### Do I lose likes if I delete and repost?

Yes. The new post is a new record, so it starts with no likes, reposts or replies.

### Can you edit a scheduled Bluesky post?

Yes, if it's scheduled in a tool rather than published. You can change the text, media or time until it goes live.

### Do Bluesky edit extensions actually edit posts?

No. They delete the original and publish a new post for you, so engagement resets the same way.

### Does Bluesky have drafts?

Yes. Bluesky added drafts in early 2026. Open the new post window and look for **Drafts** to save or reopen unfinished posts.
