The best free social media schedulers are the ones built into the platforms themselves: Meta Business Suite for Facebook and Instagram, YouTube Studio, and the scheduling options in the LinkedIn, X and TikTok composers. They're free and official, but each schedules to one platform at a time, so you'll repeat the work for every network you use.

This post focuses only on scheduling: getting a post to go live at a set time without you being there. If you want the wider picture of free inboxes, insights and utilities, see our guide to [free social media management tools](/blog/free-social-media-management-tools).

## The best free social media schedulers by platform

Native features change often and can depend on your account type, country and device. Everything below is accurate to the best of our knowledge at the time of writing; check each platform's help center if an option is missing.

### Meta Business Suite: the strongest free scheduler

If Facebook and Instagram are your main channels, Meta Business Suite is the best free scheduler available. It schedules feed posts, Reels and Stories to a Page and its linked Instagram account in one go, which is the closest any native tool gets to cross-posting. For step-by-step Instagram options, read [how to schedule Instagram posts](/blog/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts).

### YouTube Studio

Upload your video, set visibility to **Schedule**, choose the date and time, and YouTube publishes it for you. Premieres add a countdown page and live chat for the first viewing. It's reliable and free, but it only knows about YouTube.

### LinkedIn and X composers

Both let you write a post, tap a scheduling icon and pick a time. They work well for a few posts a week. They aren't built for planning a month at once: there's no bulk upload, no queue of time slots, and no view that shows your other networks. On X, write within the 280-character limit before you schedule; our [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter) counts links and emoji the way X does.

### TikTok on the web

TikTok's desktop upload page includes a schedule option. The scheduling window is limited and has changed over time, so you can't line up content as far ahead as you might on other platforms. Check TikTok's help center for the current window.

## What free native schedulers can't do

Native schedulers share the same set of gaps, because each platform only cares about itself.

- **One platform at a time.** To post the same announcement to six networks, you schedule it six times, in six tools.
- **No shared calendar.** You can't see this week's Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube posts side by side, so gaps and clashes are easy to miss.
- **No posting time slots.** You pick a time for every post instead of dropping it into a queue that fills your usual slots.
- **No evergreen or repeating posts.** Republishing a proven post means recreating it by hand.
- **No bulk scheduling.** There's rarely a way to upload a spreadsheet of posts.
- **No approvals across channels.** If someone needs to sign off, that happens outside the tool.

## How to schedule social media posts for free: a weekly routine

You can run a perfectly good free setup if you're disciplined about it. This routine keeps it manageable.

1. **Plan in one place.** Keep a simple spreadsheet or document with a row per post: date, platforms, caption, media file name. This is your shared calendar, even if the tools don't have one.
2. **Write the master caption first,** then a short version for X, Bluesky and Threads, and a longer one for LinkedIn and Facebook.
3. **Prepare media once.** Export a 9:16 vertical version for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, and 4:5 or 1:1 images for feeds.
4. **Schedule platform by platform.** Do all Meta posts in Meta Business Suite, then YouTube, then LinkedIn, then X, then TikTok. Doing one tool at a time is faster than switching back and forth per post.
5. **Mark each row as scheduled** in your sheet so you know nothing was missed.
6. **Check the next morning** that the first posts went out as planned.

## Free tiers of commercial schedulers

Some third-party schedulers have offered free tiers that let you schedule to a few accounts from one calendar. These can be a step up from native tools, but they change frequently and usually limit connected accounts, queued posts or the platforms you can use. Video networks and newer platforms are often the first things left out. Check the vendor's current pricing page and the exact limits before you rely on one.

Open-source schedulers you host yourself are the other free route. The software is free, but you'll need a server, your own developer apps for each platform, and time to maintain it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free social media scheduler?

For Facebook and Instagram, Meta Business Suite is the best free scheduler because it handles both platforms together. For everything else, the best free option is each platform's own scheduling feature. No free native tool schedules to every network at once.

### Can you schedule posts on multiple platforms for free?

Not from a single native tool. You can schedule on most platforms for free, but you'll do it separately in each one. Some commercial tools offer limited free tiers for multi-platform scheduling, but check their current limits.

### What is the best free scheduling app for Instagram?

Instagram's own scheduling for professional accounts and Meta Business Suite are both free and official. Meta Business Suite is better if you also post to a Facebook Page, since you can schedule to both together.

### Can you schedule posts on Bluesky for free?

At the time of writing, the official Bluesky app doesn't include a scheduling feature. You'd need a third-party tool that supports Bluesky, which usually connects using an app password from Bluesky's settings.

### Is a free post scheduler safe to use?

Native schedulers are the safest because they're run by the platform. For third-party tools, use ones that connect through the platform's official login or app passwords, and never share your main password with an app that asks for it directly.
