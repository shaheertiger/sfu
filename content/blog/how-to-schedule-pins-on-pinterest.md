You've made a month of pins for your shop and you don't want to sit at Pinterest every evening to post them. Pinterest has a built-in scheduler for business accounts: create a pin, choose **Publish at a later date**, and pick a time up to **30 days** ahead. You can hold up to **100 scheduled pins** at once.

![How to schedule pins on Pinterest: create a pin, turn on Publish at a later date, and pick a time up to 30 days ahead](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-pins-on-pinterest-hero-1200x675.webp)

For most casual pinners that's plenty. For a shop with dozens of products or a blog pinning daily, the 30-day window, the 100-pin cap and the one-pin-at-a-time form start to bite within a week.

This guide covers the native method step by step, its limits, how a scheduler tool compares, the best time to pin, and the automation shortcuts that get accounts flagged.

## Why Scheduling Pins Works Better Than Pinning Live

![Why scheduling pins works on Pinterest: pins keep being found for months, so a steady pace beats a burst](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-pins-on-pinterest-why-1200x675.webp)

Pinterest is a search engine as much as a feed. A pin can keep getting found for months after you post it, so the goal isn't to catch one perfect hour. It's to keep a steady stream of fresh pins going, which is exactly what scheduling is good at.

| | Pinning live | Scheduling |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | Bursts when you have time | Steady, spread across days |
| Time spent | A few minutes every day | One batch session |
| Evening and weekend slots | Only if you're free then | Covered while you're offline |
| Gaps during busy weeks | Common | Rare |

### What Most People Don't Know

Personal accounts can't schedule pins at all. The **Publish at a later date** toggle only appears on business accounts, and converting is free. If you can't find the option, that's almost always why.

> **Key point:** On Pinterest, a steady pace matters more than the perfect hour. Scheduling a week of pins in one sitting gets you that pace without being online every evening.

## How to Schedule a Pin on Pinterest (5 Steps, Business Account)

![Five steps to schedule a pin on Pinterest, with turning on Publish at a later date highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-pins-on-pinterest-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change from time to time, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Click Create, Then Create Pin

On pinterest.com, click **Create → Create Pin**.

### Step 2: Upload a Vertical Image

Vertical 2:3, such as 1000 × 1500 pixels, is the standard. Our [Pinterest photo resizer](/tools/pinterest-photo-resizer) crops to it.

### Step 3: Add the Title, Description, Link and Board

A title (up to 100 characters), a description (up to 500), a destination link and a board. The link is the step people forget, and it's the part that sends traffic to your site.

### Step 4: Turn On Publish at a Later Date

Toggle **Publish at a later date** and choose the date and time, up to 30 days ahead.

### Step 5: Click Schedule

Scheduled pins are listed under **Create → Scheduled Pins** (or in your profile's Pins tab with a clock icon), where you can edit or delete them before they publish.

> **Pro tip:** Write the description for search, not for followers. Put the words people would type into Pinterest in the first sentence, then add the detail.

## Pinterest's Native Scheduler Limits: 4 Walls You'll Hit

![The four limits of Pinterest's native scheduler: 30 days ahead, 100 scheduled pins, one pin per form, and no queue](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-pins-on-pinterest-limits-1200x675.webp)

| Limit | What it means | When it bites |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days ahead at most | You can't plan a season in advance | Holiday and seasonal campaigns |
| 100 scheduled pins at once | About 3 a day for a month | Shops and blogs pinning 5+ a day |
| One pin per form | No bulk upload | Large product catalogs |
| No queue or posting slots | Every pin gets its own time by hand | Anyone pinning daily |
| Business accounts only | Personal accounts can't schedule | Until you convert (free) |

For a shop with 50 products or a blog pinning daily, those limits bite within a week.

## Best Time to Post on Pinterest (Evenings and Saturdays to Test)

Timing matters less than on Instagram, but new pins get an initial push. Evenings and weekends, when people plan and browse, tend to do best. Many pinners report peaks around 8 to 11 PM in their audience's time zone and on Saturdays; treat that as a starting point to test, not a rule.

| What to test | Why |
|---|---|
| 8–11 PM, audience's time zone | People browse and plan in the evening |
| Saturday | More time for planning projects |
| Pins spread across the day | A steady pace beats a burst |

A steady five to ten pins a day, spread across the day, is a common target for active accounts. Consistency matters more than volume.

## Auto-Pinning Bots and Mass Repinning (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to keep a steady pin schedule compared with auto-pinning bots and mass repinning](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-pins-on-pinterest-bots-1200x675.webp)

Once you hit the native limits, tools promising "unlimited auto-pinning" look tempting: they repin the same image to dozens of boards, or pin other people's content around the clock.

### The Real Risks

- **Spam signals.** Pinning the same pin to many boards in a short time, or posting at machine pace, looks like spam to Pinterest.
- **Account limits or suspension.** Pinterest acts against accounts that look automated or spammy.
- **Password sharing.** Some bots ask for your Pinterest login rather than using Pinterest's official sign-in.

### A Safer Checklist

- Use tools that connect with Pinterest's own sign-in.
- Schedule your own fresh pins, not hundreds of repins.
- Spread pins across the day instead of in bursts.
- Vary the image and description when you pin the same link again.

> **Bottom line:** Schedule your own pins at a human pace. Anything that pins for you around the clock is a risk to the account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Pinterest have a built-in scheduler?

Yes, for business accounts: up to 30 days ahead and 100 scheduled pins at a time.

### Is there a free Pinterest scheduler?

Pinterest's own scheduler is free with a business account. Schedule For You also has a Free plan with 3 accounts and up to 10 scheduled posts at a time, no card needed. Bulk upload and unlimited scheduling are on the paid plans.

### How many pins should I schedule per day?

Five to ten fresh pins a day is a common target for active accounts, spread across the day. Consistency matters more than volume.

### Can you schedule video pins?

Pinterest's native scheduler supports standard pins and video pins. Idea Pins have been merged into regular pins; check Pinterest's help center for the current options. Schedule For You publishes one image per pin.

### Why can't I see Publish at a later date?

You're probably on a personal account. Convert to a business account for free, and the toggle appears.

### Where do I find my scheduled pins?

Under **Create → Scheduled Pins**, or in your profile's Pins tab with a clock icon. You can edit or delete them before they publish.

### Can I schedule pins more than 30 days ahead?

Not with Pinterest's own scheduler. A third-party scheduler can schedule further ahead.
