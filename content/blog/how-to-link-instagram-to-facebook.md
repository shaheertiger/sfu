You tried to link Instagram to Facebook and ended up in the wrong menu, or you linked them and your scheduler still can't see your account. That's because there are two different links, and they do different things.

![How to link Instagram to Facebook: Accounts Center for your profile, Linked accounts for a Page](/images/blog/how-to-link-instagram-to-facebook-hero-1200x675.webp)

To link your **personal Facebook account**, open Instagram, go to **Accounts Center**, tap **Add accounts** and log in to Facebook. To connect Instagram to a **Facebook Page** (what businesses need for ads, the shared inbox and scheduling tools), open the Page on Facebook, go to **Settings**, then **Linked accounts**, choose **Instagram** and tap **Connect account**. Most businesses want the second one, or both.

This guide covers which link you need, the steps for each, fixes for when it won't connect, and how to unlink.

## Which Link Do You Need? Profile vs Page

Meta uses one word, "link", for two separate connections. The Accounts Center link joins two personal logins. The Page link ties an Instagram professional account to a Facebook Page, which is what Meta's business and publishing tools look for.

![The Accounts Center link compared with the Facebook Page link for Instagram](/images/blog/how-to-link-instagram-to-facebook-which-link-1200x675.webp)

| You want to | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Share stories and posts to your Facebook profile automatically | Accounts Center (personal Facebook account) |
| Log in to Instagram with Facebook | Accounts Center |
| Run ads for your Instagram from Facebook or Ads Manager | Instagram professional account linked to a Facebook Page |
| Answer Instagram and Facebook messages in one inbox | Linked to a Page |
| Use a scheduler to publish to Instagram | Professional account linked to a Page |
| Cross-post Reels and posts to your Page | Linked to a Page |

### What Most People Don't Know

Linking in Accounts Center doesn't connect Instagram to a Page. You can have Instagram and Facebook happily linked in Accounts Center and still see "no Instagram account found" in a scheduler or Ads Manager, because those tools only see Instagram accounts connected to a Page.

> **Key point:** If you run a business account, the link that matters is Instagram → Facebook Page. The Accounts Center link is optional and personal.

## How to Link Instagram to Your Facebook Account (Accounts Center, 5 Steps)

Meta moves these menus often, so labels may differ slightly in your app version.

![Five steps to link Instagram to a personal Facebook account in Accounts Center](/images/blog/how-to-link-instagram-to-facebook-accounts-center-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Profile Menu

In Instagram, go to your profile and open the menu.

### Step 2: Tap Accounts Center

It's near the top of **Settings and activity**.

### Step 3: Add Your Facebook Account

Tap **Add accounts**, then **Add Facebook account**.

### Step 4: Log In to Facebook and Confirm

Use the Facebook account you actually want linked.

### Step 5: Choose What to Share

Pick which features to use across both: shared login, story and post sharing, and so on. Open **Accounts Center**, then **Sharing across profiles**, to turn automatic sharing on or off later.

> **Pro tip:** An Accounts Center can hold only one Facebook account. If you're logged into Facebook on your phone as a partner or a work profile, you'll link the wrong one. Check which account the Facebook app is signed into before step 4.

## How to Connect Instagram to a Facebook Page (2 Ways)

Your Instagram account needs to be a **professional account** (Business or Creator), and you need full control of the Page. Our guide to [connecting a Facebook Page](/blog/connect-facebook-page) covers Page roles if you're not sure.

![Two ways to connect Instagram to a Facebook Page, from Facebook or from Instagram](/images/blog/how-to-link-instagram-to-facebook-page-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Way 1: From Facebook

1. On Facebook, switch into your Page (tap your profile picture, then the Page name).
2. Open **Settings** (on the Page, under the menu or the profile picture).
3. Go to **Linked accounts** (sometimes under **Permissions**).
4. Choose **Instagram**, then **Connect account**.
5. Log in to Instagram and approve the connection.

### Way 2: From Instagram

1. Go to your profile and tap **Edit profile**.
2. Under **Public business information**, tap **Page** (or **Connect or create**).
3. Pick the Facebook Page to connect, or create a new one.

| | From Facebook | From Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| Best when | You manage the Page on desktop | You're on your phone |
| Can create a new Page | No | Yes |
| Shows Page roles clearly | Yes | No |

## Why Won't Instagram Link to Facebook? 5 Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| "Account not eligible" or the Page doesn't appear | Switch Instagram to a professional account and check you have full control of the Page | Page links need both |
| It links to the wrong Facebook account | In Accounts Center, remove the account and add the right one | Only one Facebook account fits |
| The Page is already connected to another Instagram | Disconnect the old one in the Page's Linked accounts first | A Page links to one Instagram account |
| Login loops or errors | Log out of both apps, update them, and try from a browser on a computer | Clears stale sessions |
| A scheduler can't see the account | Remove the app in Facebook's Business Integrations settings and reconnect, approving every permission | Missing permissions hide the account |

## How to Unlink Instagram From Facebook

- **Personal link:** go to **Accounts Center**, tap **Accounts**, then **Remove** next to the account.
- **Page link:** open the Page's **Linked accounts**, choose **Instagram**, then **Disconnect**.

Disconnecting a Page stops ads and scheduling tools that rely on it, so reconnect before your next scheduled post.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a Facebook Page to use Instagram?

No, Instagram works on its own. A Page is only needed for some business features: ads run from Facebook, the shared inbox and most scheduling tools.

### Will linking Instagram to Facebook post everything to both?

Only if you turn sharing on. In Accounts Center, under Sharing across profiles, you choose whether stories and posts share automatically.

### Can I link more than one Instagram account to a Facebook Page?

A Page connects to one Instagram account at a time. Businesses with several Instagram accounts usually give each its own Page.

### Can I link Instagram to Facebook without a professional account?

You can link a personal Instagram to your Facebook account in Accounts Center. Connecting to a Page requires a Business or Creator account.

### Why doesn't my Page show up when I try to link?

Usually your Facebook login doesn't have full control of the Page. Ask an admin to give you full control, then try again.

### Does unlinking delete my posts?

No. Unlinking stops sharing and Page-based tools; posts already on Instagram or Facebook stay where they are.

### Why does my scheduler say no Instagram account was found?

Your Instagram is probably linked in Accounts Center but not to a Page, or it's still a personal account. Connect it to a Page and reconnect the scheduler.
