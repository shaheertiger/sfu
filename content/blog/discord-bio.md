Someone just clicked your name after you said something in a channel. Your Discord bio has a second or two to tell them who you are. A good one says who you are, what you're into and how to reach you, all within the About Me limit of 190 characters.

![How to write a Discord bio: who you are, one personal detail and how to reach you, in 190 characters](/images/blog/discord-bio-hero-1200x675.webp)

Put the most useful detail first and use Discord's markdown (like **bold** and *italics*) sparingly. The limit is tighter than it looks, because formatting symbols count as characters too.

This guide covers the profile limits, a simple three-part formula, how markdown and fancy fonts work, copy-ready examples by audience, and what to keep out of your bio.

## Why Your Discord Bio Matters More Than You Think

Discord is a chat platform, so people usually look at your profile for one reason: you just said something, joined a voice call or sent a friend request. Your About Me answers the question they're asking: who is this, and why are they here?

![A vague Discord bio compared with a useful one that leads with who you are and how to reach you](/images/blog/discord-bio-good-vs-vague-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

The 190-character limit includes everything you type: spaces, emoji, and the symbols you use for formatting. Two asterisks on each side of a bold word add four characters to your count. A bio that looks like 180 characters of text can be over the limit once it's formatted.

> **Key point:** A useful bio beats a clever one. "Community manager at a small indie studio" tells people more than any quote, and it still leaves room for personality.

## How to Edit Your Discord Bio: 5 Steps (Desktop and Mobile)

Discord renames settings from time to time, so labels may differ slightly in your version.

![Five steps to edit your Discord About Me bio from User Settings](/images/blog/discord-bio-edit-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open User Settings

Click the gear icon near your username at the bottom left.

### Step 2: Go to Profiles

The profile section is usually labeled **Profiles**.

### Step 3: Type in the About Me Box

Type or paste your bio into **About Me**.

### Step 4: Check the Preview, Not the Box

The preview on the right shows how the markdown renders. This is the step people skip, and it's where stray asterisks and broken spoilers show up.

### Step 5: Save Changes

Click **Save Changes**. On mobile, tap your avatar, then **Edit Profile**, and look for **About Me**.

> **Pro tip:** Nitro subscribers can set server profiles, with a different avatar, banner and other profile details in specific servers. That's handy if you're a moderator in one community and just a member in another.

## The 3-Part Formula for a Good Discord Bio

### 1. Lead With the Most Useful Detail

Answer "who is this?" first: your role in the server, your main game or what you make.

### 2. Say How You Want to Be Contacted

"DMs open for collabs" or "Ask questions in #help, not DMs" saves you a lot of awkward messages.

### 3. Add One Personal Touch

One or two interests, a running joke or your time zone makes the profile feel human. Pronouns can go in your bio, though Discord also has a separate pronouns field, which keeps your About Me free for other things.

And keep it current. An outdated bio ("streaming every Friday!" from two years ago) is worse than a short one.

## Discord Bio Markdown: Bold, Spoilers and Line Breaks

Discord's About Me supports the same basic markdown as messages. Use it to highlight one or two things, not everything.

![Discord bio markdown you can use, compared with formatting that wastes characters](/images/blog/discord-bio-markdown-1200x675.webp)

| You type | You get | Characters added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `**bold**` | **bold** | 4 |
| `*italic*` | *italic* | 2 |
| `__underline__` | underlined text | 4 |
| `~~strikethrough~~` | ~~strikethrough~~ | 4 |
| `` `code` `` | `code` in a monospace box | 2 |
| `\|\|spoiler\|\|` | Text hidden until clicked | 4 |

- **Line breaks work.** Press Enter (or Shift + Enter on desktop) to start a new line. Short lines are easier to read than one long sentence.
- **Links are clickable.** Paste a full URL to a portfolio or website.
- **Emoji work too.** Standard emoji are fine for everyone. Custom server emoji in your bio generally require Nitro.

### Fancy Fonts: Use Them as an Accent

You can paste styled text, such as 𝓼𝓬𝓻𝓲𝓹𝓽 or 𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱 𝘀𝗮𝗻𝘀, from a tool like our [Discord font generator](/tools/discord-font-generator). These aren't real fonts. They're look-alike Unicode characters, so they work anywhere Discord accepts text. Screen readers may read them letter by letter or skip them, people can't search for them easily, and some devices show them as empty boxes. Use them for a word or two, not your whole bio.

## What to Keep Out of Your Discord Bio: 4 Mistakes

- **Personal information.** Don't include your full name, school, address, phone number or anything that makes you easy to find offline.
- **Walls of emoji or symbols.** They're hard to read and eat into your 190 characters.
- **Anything that breaks Discord's rules.** Bios fall under Discord's Community Guidelines just like messages, and offensive or impersonating content can get your profile reported.
- **Ads and spam links.** A portfolio link is fine. A string of promo codes looks like a bot, and links that ask people to log in somewhere look like phishing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long can a Discord bio be?

The About Me section allows up to 190 characters at the time of writing. That includes spaces, emoji and any markdown symbols you use, such as the asterisks around bold text.

### Can I use bold and italics in my Discord bio?

Yes, the About Me field supports Discord markdown, including `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `__underline__`, `~~strikethrough~~`, inline `code` and `||spoilers||`. Check the preview in your profile settings before you save.

### Do I need Nitro to have a Discord bio?

No, every account can add an About Me. Nitro adds extras such as a custom profile banner, server-specific profiles and custom emoji from other servers in your profile.

### Can I put links in my Discord bio?

Yes, paste a full URL and it becomes clickable. Keep links to trustworthy pages like your portfolio or website, and never share links that ask people to log in or download files.

### Why do fancy fonts look like boxes on some devices?

Styled text uses special Unicode characters, and devices missing those characters show empty boxes or question marks. Stick to common styles like bold or italic, and use them for a few words rather than your whole bio.

### Can I have a different bio in each server?

Nitro subscribers can set server profiles with different details in specific servers. Without Nitro, your About Me is the same everywhere.

### Should I put my pronouns in my Discord bio?

You can, but Discord has a separate pronouns field, which leaves your 190 characters free for other things.
