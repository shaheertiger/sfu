The social media best practices that matter most are simple: keep complete, consistent profiles, make content in each platform's native format, write captions that fit each platform's limits, post on a schedule you can sustain, reply to people, make posts accessible, secure every account with two-factor authentication, and measure a few metrics that match your goals.

Below is a checklist for each area. Work through it once to set things up, then revisit it every few months.

## Social media best practices checklist at a glance

| Area | The one thing to get right |
| --- | --- |
| Profiles | A clear name, photo and bio that say who you are and what to do next |
| Content | Native formats for each platform, with the point made early |
| Captions | Written for each platform's limit and style, not pasted everywhere |
| Cadence | A posting schedule you can keep for months |
| Community | Timely, human replies and clear moderation |
| Accessibility | Alt text, video captions and real text instead of Unicode "fonts" |
| Security | Two-factor authentication and role-based team access |
| Measurement | Two or three metrics tied to a goal, reviewed monthly |

## Profile best practices

Your profile is often the first thing people see after a post catches their attention.

- **Use the same handle everywhere** where possible, so people can find you across networks.
- **Choose a profile photo that works small and in a circle.** Logos with fine text become unreadable at small sizes.
- **Write a bio that says what you do and what to do next.** Space is tight: at the time of writing, X allows 160 characters, Instagram 150 and TikTok 80.
- **Add a working link and contact details,** and test the link after any website change.
- **Keep business details current.** For local businesses, Google Business Profile hours, phone number and category matter as much as any social bio.

## Content best practices

- **Make content native to each platform.** Vertical 9:16 video for Reels, TikTok and Shorts; 4:5 or 1:1 images for feeds; text-first posts for X, Threads, Bluesky and LinkedIn.
- **Make the point early.** The first line of a caption or first seconds of a video decide whether people keep going.
- **Mix formats** (tips, behind the scenes, answers to customer questions, announcements) so your feed isn't all promotion.
- **Repurpose, don't duplicate.** One idea can become a video, a carousel and a text post, each adapted to its platform.

## Caption best practices for each platform

Each network has its own limit and its own norms. Write one master message, then adapt it. Our guide on how to [customize captions for each platform](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) goes further.

| Platform | Caption limit (at the time of writing) | What tends to work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | 280 characters (longer with Premium) | One idea, plain language, a link if needed |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Conversational, similar to X |
| Threads | 500 characters | Casual, question-led |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | Strong first line before the "more" cut-off; hashtags at the end |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | Short paragraphs, a clear takeaway, professional tone |
| TikTok | 4,000 characters | Short; the video does the work |
| Facebook Pages | 63,206 characters | Short to medium; a clear call to action |
| YouTube | Title 100, description 5,000 | Searchable title; useful description with links and chapters |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 characters | Offer or update, plus a button link |

Two practical tips: count characters before you post (X counts each link as 23 characters and most emoji as 2), and keep line breaks intact on Instagram with our [Instagram line break generator](/tools/instagram-line-break-generator).

## Posting cadence best practices

How often should you post on social media? There's no universal number. The best practice is a cadence you can sustain without the quality dropping.

- **Pick a realistic schedule per platform** and stick to it for at least a couple of months before judging results.
- **Batch your work.** Write and schedule a week or month at a time rather than posting on the fly.
- **Use consistent posting times,** then test alternatives. Our guide to the [best days to post on social media](/blog/best-days-to-post-on-social-media) explains how to run that test.
- **Leave room for timely posts** alongside scheduled ones.

## Community best practices

- **Reply to comments and messages** as promptly as you reasonably can, especially questions and complaints.
- **Write replies by hand.** Automated replies to strangers feel spammy and can break platform rules.
- **Publish simple house rules** for comments and apply them consistently: hide spam, remove abuse, don't delete honest criticism.
- **Move complex issues to a private channel** and say so publicly so others see you responded.
- **Engage beyond your own posts.** Thoughtful comments on others' content build relationships.

## Accessibility best practices for social media

Accessible posts reach more people, including anyone using a screen reader or watching without sound.

### Write alt text for images

Most major platforms, including Instagram, X, LinkedIn and Facebook, let you add alt text to images. Describe what matters in the image in a sentence or two. If the image contains text, include that text.

### Caption your videos

Add captions to every video with speech. Many people watch with the sound off, and captions are essential for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers.

### Use plain text, not Unicode "fonts"

Bold, cursive or bubble "fonts" you copy from generators aren't real fonts. They're look-alike Unicode characters, such as mathematical alphanumeric symbols. Screen readers may read them letter by letter or skip them, platform search may not match them, and some platforms block them in names. Use them sparingly for a word or two of emphasis, never for whole sentences, names or key information. If you want emphasis on LinkedIn, our [LinkedIn text formatter](/tools/linkedin-text-formatter) previews how a post will look, but plain text is still the most accessible choice.

### Other accessibility habits

- Capitalize each word in multi-word hashtags (#SmallBusinessTips) so screen readers say them correctly.
- Use emoji sparingly; screen readers read out each one's name.
- Don't put essential information only in an image.
- Use strong color contrast in graphics.

## Security best practices for social media

### Turn on two-factor authentication

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on every account, ideally with an authenticator app rather than text messages. Keep your recovery email and phone number current.

### Give team access through roles, not passwords

Use each platform's own team features, such as roles in Meta Business Suite or permissions in YouTube Studio, instead of sharing a password. Remove access promptly when someone leaves.

### Review connected apps

Check which third-party apps are connected to each account and remove any you no longer use. Never give an app your password or a 2FA code.

### Watch for phishing

Messages claiming your account will be deleted for a copyright violation, or offering verification, are common scams. Go to the platform's help center directly instead of clicking links in messages.

## Measurement best practices

- **Start with a goal** (awareness, website visits, inquiries, sales) and pick two or three metrics that reflect it.
- **Use native analytics,** which are free and the most accurate source for each platform.
- **Tag your links** with UTM parameters so website analytics shows which posts send visitors.
- **Review monthly,** compare like with like, and change one thing at a time. Our guide to [content performance analysis](/blog/content-performance-analysis) walks through the process.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the most important social media best practices for business?

Keep profiles complete and consistent, post in each platform's native format on a schedule you can sustain, reply to customers, and secure accounts with two-factor authentication. Then measure a few metrics tied to a real business goal.

### How often should you post on social media?

There's no single right number. Choose a frequency you can keep up with without lowering quality, stay consistent for a couple of months, and adjust based on your own results rather than generic rules.

### What are social media accessibility best practices?

Add alt text to images, caption videos, capitalize words in hashtags, use emoji sparingly, and avoid Unicode "fonts" for important text because screen readers and search may not handle them.

### What are social media security best practices?

Use two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, give teammates access through official roles rather than shared passwords, review connected apps regularly, and ignore messages that pressure you to log in through a link.

### Should you post the same caption on every platform?

Usually not. Each platform has different limits and norms, so adapt the same message: shorter for X and Bluesky, more detail for LinkedIn, a strong first line for Instagram.
