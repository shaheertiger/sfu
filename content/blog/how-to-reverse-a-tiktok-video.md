You want a clip of a spilled drink jumping back into the glass, or you just want to see a TikTok backwards. To play a TikTok video in reverse, record or upload it, tap **Effects** on the editing screen, open the **Time** tab, and choose **Reverse**. TikTok plays the clip backwards in the preview, and you can post it like any other video.

![How to reverse a TikTok video: open Effects, choose the Time tab, tap Reverse, then add a sound](/images/blog/how-to-reverse-a-tiktok-video-hero-1200x675.webp)

Two things catch people out. Reverse only flips the picture; the sound plays forwards, so you'll usually want to add music afterwards. And TikTok doesn't have a button to play other people's videos backwards while you watch.

This guide covers reversing your own video step by step, the workarounds for someone else's TikTok, why the sound doesn't reverse, and reverse ideas that work.

## Why TikTok Reverses the Picture but Not the Sound

![TikTok's Reverse effect flips the video track while licensed music and original sounds keep playing forwards](/images/blog/how-to-reverse-a-tiktok-video-sound-1200x675.webp)

TikTok's Reverse effect applies to the video track only. Licensed music and original sounds keep playing forwards so they stay recognisable (and licensable).

| | Reversed by TikTok's effect? | How to reverse it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The picture | Yes | Effects, Time, Reverse |
| Licensed music | No | Not possible inside TikTok |
| Your own recorded audio | No | Reverse the clip with sound in a video editor, then upload it as an original sound |

### What Most People Don't Know

Reverse is an editing effect, not a playback setting. It only works on a video you're about to post. There's no way to play a video in your feed backwards, which is why the workarounds for other people's videos all start by making your own copy or remix.

> **Key point:** You can reverse what you post, not what you watch.

## How to Reverse Your Own TikTok Video (6 Steps)

![Three stages to reverse your own TikTok: record or upload, open Effects and Time, tap Reverse and post](/images/blog/how-to-reverse-a-tiktok-video-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Record or Upload

Tap **+** and record, or tap **Upload** and pick a video.

### Step 2: Tap Next

This takes you to the editing screen.

### Step 3: Open Effects

Tap **Effects**. On some versions it's under **Edit** or the scissors icon.

### Step 4: Swipe to the Time Tab

The Time tab holds the time effects.

### Step 5: Tap Reverse

The preview now plays backwards.

### Step 6: Save, Add a Sound, Post

Tap **Save**, add a sound, and post.

> **Pro tip:** You can only apply one time effect at a time. The step people get wrong is adding Slow-mo or Repeat after Reverse and losing the reverse. If you want both, apply one in TikTok and the other in a separate editor first.

The Time tab also has **Repeat** and **Slow-mo**. If you don't see Effects or Time on your version, use the full editor (**Edit**) or reverse the clip in CapCut or another editor first, then upload it.

## How to Play Someone Else's TikTok in Reverse (3 Workarounds)

There's no reverse playback for videos in the feed. Your options:

| Method | Works when | Can you post it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remix it, then reverse your clip | The creator allows Duet, Stitch or use of the sound | Yes, it's your own clip |
| Save it and reverse it in an editor | The creator allows downloads | Personal viewing only, unless you have permission |
| Scrub backwards | Any video | Nothing to post; it's for catching a detail |

- **Remix it, then reverse.** Tap **Share > Duet** or **Stitch**, or use the video's sound. That makes your own clip, which you can reverse. See [how to duet on TikTok](/blog/how-to-duet-on-tiktok).
- **Save it and reverse it in an editor.** Tap **Share > Save video**, then reverse it in your phone's editor or a video app. Our guide to [downloading TikTok videos](/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos) explains the rules.
- **Scrub backwards.** On the website or in the app, drag the progress bar left to step back through a long video. It isn't true reverse, but it's fine for catching a detail.

## How to Reverse the Audio Too (for "Say It Backwards" Trends)

For reversed audio, reverse the clip with its sound in a video editor first and upload the result. TikTok treats it as an original sound. Licensed music can't be reversed inside TikTok.

## Reverse Problems: Fixes That Work

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can't find the Time effect | Look under Edit instead of Effects, or update the app | Some versions and regions place it differently |
| The sound plays forwards | Add music after reversing, or reverse audio in an editor | The effect flips video only |
| Slow-mo replaced Reverse | Apply one effect in TikTok, the other in an editor | Only one time effect at a time |
| Want to reverse a posted video | Save it, reverse a copy, post again | You can't edit a posted video's effects |

Deleting and reposting a video loses the original's views and comments, so think before you swap one for the other.

## Reversing and Reposting Other People's Videos (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to reverse TikToks, like your own clips and remixes, next to risky ones like reposting someone else's reversed video](/images/blog/how-to-reverse-a-tiktok-video-safety-1200x675.webp)

Reversing a saved copy of someone else's TikTok for fun is one thing. Posting it is another.

### The Real Risks

- **It's still their video.** Reversing it doesn't make it yours, and reposting without permission can get it removed.
- **No credit.** A reupload doesn't link to the creator the way a duet or stitch does.
- **Download sites** that bypass a creator's download setting can be full of ads and unsafe redirects.

### A Quick Safety Checklist

- Reverse your own footage, or remix through Duet or Stitch.
- Keep saved copies of other people's videos for personal viewing.
- Ask the creator before posting anything based on their video.

> **Bottom line:** Reverse your own clips freely. For anyone else's, remix through TikTok or ask.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the reverse effect on TikTok?

On the editing screen, under Effects, then the Time tab. Menu names vary by app version; look for Time or Time effects.

### Can you reverse a TikTok after posting it?

Not in place. Save the video, reverse it in the editor or another app, and post it again. Deleting and reposting loses the original's views and comments.

### Can I reverse a TikTok I'm watching?

No. TikTok has no reverse playback. Save or remix the video first, then reverse your copy.

### Why can't I find the Time effect?

Some app versions and regions place it under Edit instead of Effects, and very old versions may not have it. Update TikTok, or reverse the clip in a separate editor before uploading.

### Why doesn't the sound reverse?

TikTok's Reverse effect only flips the video track. Music and sounds keep playing forwards so they stay recognisable and licensable.

### Can I reverse and slow down a TikTok at the same time?

Not with TikTok's time effects alone, because you can only apply one at a time. Apply one in a separate editor first.

### Can I reverse a TikTok on a computer?

Not with TikTok's effect, which is in the app's editor. Reverse the clip in a desktop video editor, then upload it.
