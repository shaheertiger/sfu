## What Is a Galaxy on TikTok (and Why the Price Isn't Obvious)?

A Galaxy is a virtual gift viewers send during a TikTok LIVE. It plays a full-screen animation of a spinning galaxy, shows the sender's name, and adds to the creator's gift total. It's one of the most visible mid-priced gifts, which is why it gets asked about so often.

![Where a TikTok Galaxy's money goes: the viewer pays in coins, TikTok keeps a share, the creator gets Diamonds](/images/blog/how-much-is-a-galaxy-on-tiktok-flow-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Gifts are priced in coins, not dollars, and the dollar price of a coin isn't fixed. It depends on your country, your currency, the bundle size and whether you buy in the app or on the web. Coins bought in the iPhone or Android app usually cost more than coins bought at tiktok.com/coin, because Apple and Google take a cut of in-app purchases.

> **Key point:** The same Galaxy can cost two viewers different amounts of real money, but it's always 1,000 coins, and the creator's side is always roughly half the gift's value.

## How the Coin and Diamond Math Works (4 Steps)

### Step 1: The Viewer Buys Coins

Bundles range from a few dozen coins to thousands, and bigger bundles cost a little less per coin.

### Step 2: The Viewer Sends a Gift

A Galaxy uses 1,000 of those coins. Open the gift icon in a LIVE and the coin cost is shown under each gift.

### Step 3: The Creator Receives Diamonds, Not Coins

This is the step people get wrong. The creator never gets the coins themselves. TikTok converts gifts into Diamonds, keeping a share for itself. Most estimates put the creator's share at about 50% of the gift's value.

### Step 4: The Creator Withdraws

Creators who are 18 or older and eligible for gifts can cash Diamonds out through TikTok's rewards or LIVE earnings section, subject to a minimum and TikTok's terms.

So if a Galaxy costs the sender $13, the creator might see about $6.50. Different sources give slightly different figures because they assume different coin prices.

> **Pro tip:** If you're the one sending gifts, buy coins on TikTok's website rather than in the app. The coins work the same, and the bundle usually costs less.

## Buying Coins From Third-Party Sites (Use With Caution)

Sites that promise cheap or free TikTok coins are everywhere, and many look official.

![Safe places to buy TikTok coins compared with risky reseller and free-coin sites](/images/blog/how-much-is-a-galaxy-on-tiktok-coins-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Scams.** They ask for your login, payment details or a "verification" survey, and the coins never arrive.
- **Account restrictions.** TikTok can restrict accounts that use coins bought through unofficial resellers.
- **Stolen card details.** Paying an unknown site exposes your card.

### Safety Checklist

- Buy coins only in the TikTok app or at TikTok's own website.
- Never give anyone your TikTok password or login code.
- Ignore "free coins" offers and generator sites. There's no legitimate free source.

> **Bottom line:** Buying on TikTok's website is already the cheapest legitimate option. Anything cheaper is either a scam or puts your account at risk.
