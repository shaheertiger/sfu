A Galaxy on TikTok costs **1,000 coins**. Depending on where you buy coins and in what bundle, that works out to roughly **$10 to $15** for the person sending it, at the time of writing. The creator doesn't receive the full amount: the gift turns into Diamonds, and after TikTok's share the creator ends up with roughly **half the value**, around $5 to $7.50 per Galaxy.

TikTok doesn't publish a fixed coin-to-dollar rate, so every figure below is an estimate. The price shown in the coin store at checkout is the one that counts.

## What is a Galaxy on TikTok?

A Galaxy is a virtual gift viewers send during a TikTok LIVE. It plays a full-screen animation of a spinning galaxy, shows the sender's name, and adds to the creator's gift total. It's one of the most visible mid-priced gifts, which is why it gets asked about so often.

Coin prices differ by country, currency, bundle size and platform. Coins bought in the iPhone or Android app usually cost more than coins bought at tiktok.com/coin, because Apple and Google take a cut of in-app purchases.

## How the coin and Diamond math works

1. **The viewer buys coins.** Bundles range from a few dozen coins to thousands, and bigger bundles cost a little less per coin.
2. **The viewer sends a gift.** A Galaxy uses 1,000 of those coins.
3. **The creator receives Diamonds.** TikTok converts gifts into Diamonds, keeping a share for itself. Most estimates put the creator's share at about 50% of the gift's value.
4. **The creator withdraws.** Creators who are 18 or older and eligible for gifts can cash Diamonds out through TikTok's rewards or LIVE earnings section, subject to a minimum and TikTok's terms.

So if a Galaxy costs the sender $13, the creator might see about $6.50. Different sources give slightly different figures because they assume different coin prices.

## How much is a Universe on TikTok?

The Universe has long been TikTok's top gift. It was listed at **34,999 coins**, and some US accounts have reported a higher price of 44,999 coins. In dollars that's somewhere around **$350 to $560** depending on the coin price and where you buy, with the creator receiving roughly half. Because the price has changed before, check the gift panel in a LIVE for the current number.

## Should you buy coins from third-party sites?

Be careful. Sites that promise cheap or free TikTok coins are a common scam: they ask for your login, payment details or a "verification" survey. Buy coins only in the TikTok app or at TikTok's own website. TikTok can also restrict accounts that use coins bought through unofficial resellers.
