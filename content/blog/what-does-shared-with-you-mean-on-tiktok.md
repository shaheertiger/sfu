You open TikTok and see "Shared with you" under a video, or under the name of someone you barely remember. **"Shared with you" on TikTok usually means someone sent that video to you, or, when it appears on an account, that you and that person have shared videos with each other** in direct messages. TikTok uses the label to explain why it's showing you something.

![What "Shared with you" means on TikTok: a video was sent to you, or you've swapped videos with that account](/images/blog/what-does-shared-with-you-mean-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

It doesn't mean your account was shared publicly, and it doesn't tell anyone else what you watched. TikTok hasn't published a detailed explanation of the label, so the meanings below come from how it appears in the app, and they can vary by version and region.

This guide covers where the label appears and what each case means, why it can show an account you don't know, how to cut down the suggestions, and what creators can learn from it.

## Why TikTok Shows "Shared With You" (It's a Reason, Not an Alert)

![What the Shared with you label on TikTok means, compared with what it doesn't mean](/images/blog/what-does-shared-with-you-mean-on-tiktok-meaning-1200x675.webp)

The label is a reason, like "Friends with" or "From your contacts." TikTok shows these reasons so suggestions feel less random.

| Where it appears | Most likely meaning |
| --- | --- |
| On a video in your feed or inbox | A friend sent you this video in a message |
| Under a name in **Suggested accounts** or **Find friends** | You've exchanged videos with this account in DMs, or they've shared with you |
| On a profile you don't recognize | TikTok links the account to you through shares, contacts or mutual connections |

### What Most People Don't Know

The label is **only shown to you**. The other person isn't told that TikTok suggested them to you, and the label doesn't give them access to your watch history. It's TikTok explaining its own suggestion, nothing more.

> **Key point:** "Shared with you" points to video sharing between you and that account. It isn't a sign that someone is tracking you or that your profile was shared publicly.

## Why Is "Shared With You" Showing an Account I Don't Know? (4 Causes)

![Four reasons TikTok shows Shared with you for an account you don't recognize](/images/blog/what-does-shared-with-you-mean-on-tiktok-unknown-1200x675.webp)

This is the confusing case. A few things can cause it:

1. **Old shares.** Someone sent you a video months ago, or you sent them one, and you've forgotten.
2. **A new or renamed account.** A person you've messaged changed their username or profile photo.
3. **Contacts and Facebook friends.** If you've synced contacts, TikTok may suggest accounts tied to phone numbers in your address book.
4. **Shared links.** Opening a TikTok link someone sent outside the app can also count as a share.

It doesn't mean that person can see your watch history or is tracking you.

### "Shared With You" vs Other Suggestion Labels

| Label | What it points to |
| --- | --- |
| Shared with you | Videos sent between you and that account |
| Friends with | Mutual friends |
| From your contacts | A phone number in your synced contacts |

## How to Find a Video Someone Shared With You

Videos friends send you arrive as messages, so the place to look is your **Inbox**:

1. Tap **Inbox** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Open the chat with the person who sent it.
3. Scroll up to find the video and tap it to watch.

If you can't find it, the sender may have shared a link outside TikTok, for example in a text message or another chat app. Opening that link plays the video in TikTok, and as noted above, it can count as a share between you. ## How to Stop Seeing "Shared With You" Suggestions (4 Steps)

You can't turn off the label itself, but you can reduce suggestions. Menu labels change between app versions.

### Step 1: Open Privacy Settings

Go to **Profile → menu (three lines) → Settings and privacy → Privacy**.

### Step 2: Turn Off What You Don't Want Suggested

Tap **Suggest your account to others** and turn off the options you don't want: contacts, Facebook friends, mutual connections, people who open or send links to you.

### Step 3: Remove Single Suggestions

Tap the **X** next to a name in Suggested accounts.

### Step 4: Stop Syncing Contacts

Turn off contact access in **Settings and privacy → Privacy → Sync contacts and Facebook friends**, and remove synced contacts. This is the step most people miss: turning off suggestions doesn't delete contacts you've already synced.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strangers in suggestions | Turn off contact and link suggestions | Removes the signals TikTok uses |
| One account keeps appearing | Tap the X next to it | Dismisses that suggestion |
| A blocked account shows the label | Close and reopen the app, check your block list | The label may be stale |
| Suggestions from old phone contacts | Remove synced contacts | Turning off sync doesn't delete what's synced |

## "Who Shared My TikTok" and Tracker Apps (Use With Caution)

Search for "see who shared my TikTok" and you'll find apps that promise to show who sent your videos, who watched them, or who's behind a "Shared with you" label.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't see TikTok's data.** Who shared what in private messages isn't available to outside apps.
- **They want your TikTok login**, which gives them full access to your account.
- **The results are guesses**, or made up.

### Stay Safe

- Never type your TikTok password into another app's form.
- Check **Manage devices** in Security and remove anything you don't recognize.

> **Bottom line:** The label is TikTok explaining its suggestion. No app can tell you more about it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does "Shared with you" mean on TikTok?

It usually means someone sent you that video, or that you and that account have shared videos with each other in direct messages.

### Does "Shared with you" mean someone shared my profile?

Not necessarily. It usually means a video was sent to you, or you and that account have swapped videos in messages.

### Can the other person see that TikTok suggested them to me?

No. The label is only shown to you.

### Why does "Shared with you" show for someone I blocked?

Blocked accounts shouldn't appear in suggestions. If one does, the label may be stale; close and reopen the app, and check your block list in Settings and privacy → Privacy → Blocked accounts.

### Is "Shared with you" the same as "Friends with"?

No. "Friends with" means you have mutual friends; "Shared with you" points to video sharing between you and that account.

### Can I turn off "Shared with you" on TikTok?

You can't turn off the label itself. You can reduce suggestions in Settings and privacy → Privacy → Suggest your account to others, and dismiss single suggestions with the X.

### Does opening a TikTok link count as a share?

It can. Opening a TikTok link someone sent you outside the app may be counted as a share between you.
