You've picked a scheduler, clicked "Connect Instagram", and your account isn't in the list. To connect Instagram to a scheduler, you need three things: an Instagram **Business or Creator** account, a **Facebook Page linked to it**, and a login through Meta's own window where you approve the scheduler's permissions. In Schedule For You, that last step happens on the Accounts screen and takes a couple of minutes.

![How to connect Instagram to a scheduler: professional account, linked Facebook Page, then approve Meta's permissions](/images/blog/connect-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

The step that trips most people up isn't the scheduler at all. It's the Facebook Page: either it isn't linked, or the Facebook login you use doesn't have full control of it.

This guide covers why Meta works this way, the full setup step by step, what you can schedule once you're connected, and how to fix the errors you're most likely to see.

## Why Instagram Needs a Facebook Page to Connect a Scheduler

Meta only lets outside apps publish to Instagram through its official API, and that access is tied to professional accounts. The connection runs through the Facebook Page linked to your Instagram account, which is why a personal account, or one with no Page, never shows up.

![What you need to connect Instagram to a scheduler compared with what blocks the connection](/images/blog/connect-instagram-why-1200x675.webp)

| Requirement | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Instagram Business or Creator account | Meta only gives publishing access to professional accounts |
| A Facebook Page linked to that account | The connection runs through the Page |
| Full control of the Page | Needed to grant publishing permissions |
| The Facebook login that manages the Page | Meta's window only lists Pages that login can manage |
| A Schedule For You account | The [Free plan](/signup) needs no card |

### What Most People Don't Know

You never give the scheduler your Instagram password. You log in through Meta's own window, and Meta hands the app a token with only the permissions you approve. That's also why the Facebook side matters so much: the token is issued for the Page and the Instagram account you tick in that window, and nothing else.

> **Key point:** If Instagram doesn't appear when you connect, the problem is almost always on Meta's side of the setup (account type or Page link), not in the scheduler. Fix those two things and the connection usually works first time.

## How to Connect Instagram to a Scheduler: 3 Steps

Menu names in Instagram and Facebook change between app versions, so if a label doesn't match exactly, look for the closest one.

![Three steps to connect Instagram to a scheduler: go professional, link a Facebook Page, connect through Meta](/images/blog/connect-instagram-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Switch Instagram to a Professional Account (Free, Keeps Your Posts)

If your account is already Business or Creator, skip ahead. If not, switching is free and you keep your posts and followers.

1. Open the Instagram app and go to your profile.
2. Open the menu and go to **Settings and activity**.
3. Look for **Account type and tools**.
4. Tap **Switch to professional account**.
5. Choose a category that describes you or your business.
6. Choose **Creator** or **Business** and finish the setup.

Both account types work for scheduling. Business suits companies, shops and services, and offers options like contact buttons. Creator suits individuals such as artists, coaches and influencers. You can switch between them later.

### Step 2: Link Instagram to a Facebook Page (the Step People Get Wrong)

If you don't have a Page, create one on Facebook first. It can be simple: a name, a profile picture and a category are enough. Then link it using one of these routes:

- **From Instagram:** go to Edit profile and look for the Page option under your public business information, then connect an existing Page or create one.
- **From Facebook:** open the Page, go to its settings and look for **Linked accounts** or **Instagram**, then connect your account.
- **From Meta Business Suite:** if the Page is in a business portfolio, add the Instagram account in the portfolio's settings.

Once linked, the Page and the Instagram account should each show the other in their settings.

> **Pro tip:** Before you connect, check that the Facebook account you'll log in with has **full control** of the Page (the equivalent of the old admin role). Partial or task-only access isn't enough to grant publishing permissions, and it's the most common reason a connection half-works. Our guide on [connecting a Facebook Page](/blog/connect-facebook-page) shows how to check.

### Step 3: Connect Instagram in Schedule For You

1. **Sign in** and open the workspace you want to use. Each workspace has its own connected accounts.
2. **Go to the Accounts screen.**
3. **Choose Instagram.** A Meta window opens.
4. **Log in with Facebook**, using the account that has full control of the linked Page. If you're already logged in, confirm it's the right one.
5. **Select the Page and Instagram account.** Meta asks which Pages and Instagram accounts to share. Tick the ones you need.
6. **Approve the permissions.** Leave every requested permission on. They cover things like publishing content to your account, and switching any off can stop posts from going out.
7. **Choose the account to add.** Back in Schedule For You, select your Instagram account and confirm.

Your account now appears on the Accounts screen and in the composer. It counts as one connected account: 3 on Free, 15 on Solo, 100 on Studio. Managing several brands? See [how to connect multiple Instagram accounts](/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts).

## What You Can Schedule Once Instagram Is Connected (and What You Can't)

Knowing what's supported saves you from failed posts later.

![What you can schedule on Instagram through a scheduler compared with what isn't supported](/images/blog/connect-instagram-formats-1200x675.webp)

| What you attach | What gets published |
|---|---|
| One image | A feed photo post |
| One video | A Reel |
| 2 to 10 images or videos (mixed is fine) | A carousel |
| No media | Not possible: Instagram needs an image or video |
| One image or video, with "Post as" set to Story | A Story (9:16, no caption) |

Captions can be up to 2,200 characters, and the composer checks the limit live. You can write an Instagram-specific version of a caption while the same post goes to other platforms with their own versions, and add a first comment to feed posts and Reels, posted by your account right after the post goes live.

### What Isn't Supported

- **Story stickers and music.** Stories go out as the one image or video you upload, with no caption, so polls, link stickers and Instagram's music library aren't available through Meta's API.
- **Automatic cropping.** Images aren't cropped or resized, so they must already sit between 4:5 portrait and 1.91:1 landscape (Instagram's feed range at the time of writing). The [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) has presets for each shape.
- **Personal accounts.** Only Business and Creator accounts linked to a Page can be connected.

### Your First Scheduled Post

Open the composer, select your Instagram account, attach your media and write the caption. Then pick a date and time or add the post to your queue, which publishes it in your next free posting time slot. You can see everything on the drag-and-drop calendar and move posts around as plans change.

## Instagram Won't Connect? 4 Errors and How to Fix Them

![Common Instagram connection errors and the fix for each one](/images/blog/connect-instagram-fixes-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram doesn't appear in Meta's window | Switch to Business or Creator, and link a Page | Meta only lists professional accounts with a linked Page |
| The Page is missing from the list | Reconnect, edit previous settings in Meta's dialog, tick the Page | It was left unticked in an earlier connection |
| Permissions error | Reconnect and approve everything, or get full control of the Page | A permission was switched off, or your Page role is too limited |
| Posts worked, then stopped | Reconnect from the Accounts screen, then retry | Meta cancelled the connection after a change |

### Instagram Doesn't Appear in Meta's Window

Almost always, the account is still personal or isn't linked to a Page. Check both in the Instagram app, fix whichever is missing, and start the connection again.

### The Page Is Missing From the List

You may have left it unticked in an earlier connection. Run the connection again, choose the option to edit your previous settings in Meta's dialog, and select the Page. Also make sure you're logged in to the Facebook account that manages it; a private browser window helps if your browser keeps choosing another login.

### You See a Permissions Error

Either a permission was switched off during setup, or your Page access isn't full control. Reconnect and approve everything, or ask a Page admin to upgrade your access.

### Posts Were Working and Then Stopped

Meta can cancel a connection after a password change, a security check or a change to the account type or Page link. You'll get a failed-post alert with a prompt to reconnect. Reconnect from the Accounts screen, then retry the failed posts. Our guide to [Instagram failing to post](/blog/instagram-not-posting) covers other causes, such as image shapes and video processing.

## Apps That Connect Instagram Without a Facebook Page (Use With Caution)

Some apps promise to schedule to a personal Instagram account, or to skip the Facebook Page, by asking for your Instagram username and password directly instead of using Meta's login window.

### The Real Risks

- **Your password leaves Meta.** Any app that stores it can log in as you, and a leak exposes the account.
- **Unofficial logins look suspicious to Instagram.** Logins from unfamiliar servers can trigger security checks, locked sessions or worse.
- **No official publishing access.** Without Meta's API, these apps rely on workarounds that can break without warning.

### Safety Checklist

- Only connect through Meta's own login window.
- Never type your Instagram password into a scheduler's site.
- Review apps with access in Meta's settings and remove anything you don't use.
- Turn on two-factor authentication for Instagram and Facebook.

> **Bottom line:** Linking a Facebook Page takes a few minutes and is free. It's a much smaller cost than handing your password to an app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I connect a personal Instagram account to a scheduler?

No. Meta only allows scheduling tools to publish to Business or Creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page. Switching to a professional account is free and keeps your posts and followers.

### Can I schedule Instagram posts without a Facebook Page?

Not with Schedule For You. The Instagram account must be linked to a Facebook Page, because that's how Meta grants a scheduler permission to publish.

### Does connecting Instagram give the scheduler my password?

No. You log in through Meta's own window, and Meta gives the app a token with the permissions you approve. Your password isn't shared with the scheduler.

### How long does it take to connect Instagram?

If your account is already professional and linked to a Page, a couple of minutes. Switching account type and linking a Page adds a few more minutes the first time.

### Why does Instagram keep disconnecting from my scheduler?

Usually because something changed on Meta's side: a password change, a security check, or a change to the account type or Page link. Reconnect from the Accounts screen and approve every permission.

### Business or Creator: which works better for scheduling?

Both work the same for scheduling. Pick Business for a company or shop and Creator for an individual; you can switch later.

### Can I connect more than one Instagram account?

Yes. Each one counts as a connected account: 3 on Free, 15 on Solo and 100 on Studio. See [how to connect multiple Instagram accounts](/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts).

### Can I schedule Instagram Stories after connecting?

Yes. Set "Post as" to Story in the composer and attach one image or video (9:16, no caption). Stickers, polls and music aren't available through Meta's API. Our guide to [scheduling Instagram Stories](/blog/schedule-instagram-and-facebook-stories) covers the details.

### What can I schedule to Instagram once it's connected?

Feed photo posts, Reels, carousels of up to 10 images or videos and Stories, with captions up to 2,200 characters and an optional first comment on feed posts and Reels.
