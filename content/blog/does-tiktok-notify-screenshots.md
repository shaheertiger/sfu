No, TikTok doesn't notify anyone when you take a screenshot. That goes for videos, profiles, comments, LIVEs and direct messages, and for screen recordings too. Unlike Snapchat, TikTok has no screenshot alert at the time of writing. What TikTok *can* show other people is a different set of things: profile visits and video views (if you both turn those features on), likes, comments, shares and reposts.

## Screenshots on TikTok at a glance

| What you screenshot or record | Does the other person get notified? |
| --- | --- |
| A video in your feed | No |
| Someone's profile | No |
| Comments | No |
| A LIVE | No |
| A direct message chat | No |
| A screen recording of any of the above | No |

You may see a **Share** prompt pop up after you take a screenshot of a video. That's TikTok suggesting you share the link; it's only shown to you.

## Why people think TikTok notifies screenshots

- **Snapchat does.** Many people assume other apps work the same way.
- **Viral videos claim it.** "TikTok now tells creators when you screenshot" videos come up regularly; they're engagement bait.
- **Third-party apps claim it.** Apps that say they can detect screenshots of your TikToks can't see what happens on other people's phones. Don't give them your login.

## What TikTok does show other people

| Action | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Visiting a profile | The owner, if you both have **Profile view history** on. See [how to turn off profile views](/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok) |
| Watching a video | The creator, only if you both have **Post view history** on |
| Liking a video | The creator, and anyone who can see your Liked tab if it's public |
| Commenting, sharing or reposting | The creator, and often your followers |

## Is it OK to screenshot someone's TikTok?

Taking a screenshot for your own reference is common and isn't flagged. Reposting it is different: other people's videos, photos and words are their work. If you want to show someone else a TikTok, use **Share** to send the link, or use TikTok's **Repost** button. For using another creator's video in your own content, ask first and credit them.

If you're a creator worried about people screenshotting your posts, the only real control is who sees them. You can make your account private, limit who can comment or message you, and set individual videos to **Friends** or **Only me**.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does TikTok notify when you screenshot a DM?
No. The other person isn't told when you screenshot or record a TikTok conversation.

### Does TikTok notify when you screen record?
No. Screen recordings aren't detected or reported to other users.

### Can TikTok creators see who saved their video?
Creators see how many times a video was saved or shared in their analytics, but not a list of who did it.

### Does TikTok notify screenshots on a LIVE?
No. Screenshots and recordings of a LIVE aren't reported to the host.
