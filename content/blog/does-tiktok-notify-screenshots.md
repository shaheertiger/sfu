You've just screenshotted a TikTok, a comment or a DM, and now you're wondering if the other person got an alert. They didn't. **TikTok doesn't notify anyone when you take a screenshot**, whether it's a video, a profile, a comment, a LIVE or a direct message, and screen recordings aren't reported either. Unlike Snapchat, TikTok has no screenshot alert at the time of writing.

![Does TikTok notify screenshots? No: videos, profiles, comments, LIVEs, DMs and screen recordings aren't reported](/images/blog/does-tiktok-notify-screenshots-hero-1200x675.webp)

So why does the question keep coming up? Because TikTok *does* show other people some of what you do, such as profile visits and video views when both of you have those features on, and that's easy to confuse with a screenshot alert.

This guide covers what's never reported, what TikTok actually shows other people, where screenshot-detector apps go wrong, and what creators can do if they don't want their posts saved.

## Why People Think TikTok Notifies Screenshots (It Doesn't)

![Why people believe TikTok sends screenshot alerts, next to what TikTok actually does](/images/blog/does-tiktok-notify-screenshots-myths-1200x675.webp)

Three things keep the myth alive:

- **Snapchat does.** Many people assume other apps work the same way.
- **Viral videos claim it.** "TikTok now tells creators when you screenshot" videos come up regularly. They're engagement bait.
- **Third-party apps claim it.** Apps that say they can detect screenshots of your TikToks can't see what happens on other people's phones.

### What Most People Don't Know

You may see a **Share** prompt pop up after you take a screenshot of a video. That's TikTok suggesting you share the link instead. It's only shown to you, and nothing is sent to the creator. People see the prompt, assume TikTok noticed the screenshot, and conclude the creator was told. They weren't.

> **Key point:** A screenshot happens on your phone, not on TikTok. The app can notice and suggest sharing, but it doesn't tell the person whose video, comment or chat you captured.

## What TikTok Does Show Other People (4 Things to Know)

![What TikTok shows other people: profile visits and video views if both have them on, likes, comments, shares and reposts](/images/blog/does-tiktok-notify-screenshots-visible-1200x675.webp)

Screenshots are invisible, but some actions aren't.

| Action | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Visiting a profile | The owner, if you both have **Profile view history** on |
| Watching a video | The creator, only if you both have **Post view history** on |
| Liking a video | The creator, and anyone who can see your Liked tab if it's public |
| Commenting, sharing or reposting | The creator, and often your followers |

### Step 1: Check Profile View History

Go to **Profile → Menu (☰) → Settings and privacy → Privacy** and look for **Profile views**. If it's on, people whose profiles you visit can see you, as long as they have it on too. Turn it off and you stop appearing (and stop seeing visitors). Our guide on [how to turn off profile views](/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok) has the full steps.

### Step 2: Check Post View History

In the same Privacy menu, look for **Post views**. It works the same way: both people need it on for a view to show.

### Step 3: Hide Your Liked Videos

Under **Privacy → Liked videos**, set who can see your Liked tab. "Only me" keeps your likes off your public profile, although the creator can still see that you liked their video.

### Step 4: Remember That Reposts Are Public

A repost shows the video to your followers. If you want to share quietly, use **Share → Copy link** and send it in a chat instead. See [how to remove reposts on TikTok](/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok) if you've reposted by mistake.

> **Pro tip:** Menu labels change between app versions. If you can't find a setting, use the search box at the top of **Settings and privacy**.

## Is It OK to Screenshot Someone's TikTok? (Saving vs Reposting)

Taking a screenshot for your own reference is common and isn't flagged. Reposting it is different: other people's videos, photos and words are their work.

| What you want to do | Better way |
| --- | --- |
| Show a friend a video | **Share** to send the link |
| Put it on your profile | TikTok's **Repost** button |
| Use it in your own content | Ask the creator first and credit them |
| Keep a recipe or tip for later | Add the video to your **Favorites** |
| Save a funny comment | A screenshot is fine for yourself |

## Screenshot-Detector Apps and "Who Saved My Video" Tools (Use With Caution)

![What TikTok screenshot-detector apps promise compared with what they really do](/images/blog/does-tiktok-notify-screenshots-detector-apps-1200x675.webp)

Search an app store for "TikTok screenshot alert" or "who viewed my TikTok" and you'll find apps that promise to tell you who saved, screenshotted or stalked your posts. They can't. Screenshots happen on other people's phones, which no app on your phone can see.

### The Real Risks

- **They want your login.** With your password, an app can post, message and change settings as you.
- **They can get your account flagged.** Logins from unfamiliar servers and automated activity can look like a takeover.
- **The results are made up.** If an app shows names, it's guessing from public likes and comments, or inventing them.
- **Many charge subscriptions** for information TikTok doesn't share with anyone.

### If You've Already Used One

- Change your TikTok password.
- Turn on **2-step verification** under **Security**.
- Check **Manage devices** and remove anything you don't recognize.

> **Bottom line:** No app can tell you who screenshotted your TikTok. The ones that claim to are after your account or your money.

## How Creators Can Control Who Sees (and Saves) Their Posts

![Settings that control who can see and save your TikToks, from private account to download settings](/images/blog/does-tiktok-notify-screenshots-control-1200x675.webp)

If you're a creator worried about people screenshotting your posts, the only real control is who sees them in the first place.

1. **Make your account private** if you only want approved followers to see your videos.
2. **Set individual videos to Friends or Only me** when you post.
3. **Limit who can comment or message you** in Privacy settings.
4. **Turn off downloads** for your videos, so people can't save the file directly. They can still screen-record, so don't rely on it for anything sensitive.
5. **Don't post what you wouldn't want screenshotted.** Anything visible on a screen can be captured.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TikTok notify when you screenshot a DM?

No. The other person isn't told when you screenshot or record a TikTok conversation.

### Does TikTok notify when you screen record?

No. Screen recordings aren't detected or reported to other users.

### Does TikTok notify screenshots on a LIVE?

No. Screenshots and recordings of a LIVE aren't reported to the host.

### Can TikTok creators see who saved their video?

No. Creators see how many times a video was saved or shared in their analytics, but not a list of who did it.

### Does TikTok notify when you screenshot a profile?

No. Taking a screenshot of a profile isn't reported. A profile visit can show up, but only if you both have Profile view history on.

### Why did a Share prompt appear after my screenshot?

TikTok suggests sharing the link when it notices a screenshot of a video. The prompt is only shown to you, and the creator isn't notified.

### Can apps tell me who screenshotted my TikTok?

No. Screenshots happen on other people's phones, and no app can see them. Apps that claim to are guessing or after your login.

### Can I stop people screenshotting my TikToks?

Not completely. You can limit who sees your posts with a private account or per-video privacy, and turn off downloads, but anything on a screen can be captured.
