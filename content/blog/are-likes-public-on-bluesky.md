You liked a post on Bluesky and now you're wondering who can see that. The short answer: yes, likes are public on Bluesky. Anyone can open a post's like list and see your account in it, and every like you give is stored as public data on the AT Protocol.

![Bluesky likes are public: who can see them, why they can't be hidden, and how bookmarks keep posts private](/images/blog/are-likes-public-on-bluesky-hero-1200x675.webp)

It isn't obvious because the Bluesky app only shows the **Likes** tab on your own profile, so it looks private. It isn't. Third-party tools can read any account's likes, and there's no setting to hide them.

This guide covers who can see your likes, how to check likes on a post or a profile, why you can't hide them, and how to save posts privately instead.

## Why Your Bluesky Likes Aren't Private (Even Though They Look It)

![Where your Bluesky likes are visible: post like lists, notifications and third-party tools, but not your Likes tab in the app](/images/blog/are-likes-public-on-bluesky-visibility-1200x675.webp)

Bluesky runs on the AT Protocol, an open network. Your posts, follows, reposts and likes all live in your public data repository, so any app built on the protocol can read them. The official app just chooses not to show other people's Likes tabs.

| Where | Can others see your like? |
| --- | --- |
| The like list on a post | Yes, your account appears in it |
| The Likes tab on your profile in the Bluesky app | No, only you see it |
| Third-party apps and tools using the API | Yes, likes are public records |
| The author of the post | Yes, in their notifications |

So the person you liked always can, anyone looking at that post's likes can, and anyone determined enough can see your full list.

### What Most People Don't Know

The hidden Likes tab is a design choice in one app, not a privacy feature. Because likes are public records, some third-party Bluesky clients and web tools can show any account's likes in full. That's worth remembering before you like something you'd rather keep quiet.

> **Key point:** On Bluesky, a like is closer to a public post than a private reaction. If you wouldn't repost it, think twice before liking it.

## How to See Who Liked a Post on Bluesky (3 Steps)

![Three steps to see who liked a Bluesky post: open the post, tap the like count, scroll the list](/images/blog/are-likes-public-on-bluesky-steps-1200x675.webp)

This works on any post, yours or someone else's. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something reads differently.

### Step 1: Open the Post Itself

In the feed, tap the post to open it on its own screen. The like list isn't reachable from the small counter in the feed on every version of the app.

### Step 2: Tap the Number Next to "Likes"

Under the post you'll see counts for reposts, quotes and likes. Tap the like count, not the heart. Tapping the heart likes the post, which is exactly what you may not want.

### Step 3: Scroll the List

You'll see every account that liked it, newest first. The same works for reposts and quotes.

> **Pro tip:** If you only want to read the list, tap the number, never the heart icon. That's the step people get wrong, and the author gets a notification as soon as you like.

## How to See Your Own Likes (and Someone Else's)

Go to your profile and open the **Likes** tab. It lists every post you've liked, newest first. The tab only appears on your own profile in the official app.

For someone else's likes, the official app doesn't show their Likes tab. Some third-party Bluesky clients and web tools can display any account's likes, because the data is public.

| What you want | In the Bluesky app | Elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who liked a post | Tap the like count on the post | Any client or tool |
| Your own likes | Profile, **Likes** tab | Any client or tool |
| Someone else's likes | Not shown | Some third-party clients and tools |
| Whether someone liked your post | Notifications, or the post's like list | Any client or tool |

## Can You Hide Likes on Bluesky? (No, But Here Are 3 Workarounds)

![Three ways to keep Bluesky activity private: unlike, bookmark instead, or use a second account](/images/blog/are-likes-public-on-bluesky-options-1200x675.webp)

No. Likes live in your public data repository, the same place as your posts, so there's no private mode for them at the time of writing. Your options:

1. **Unlike it.** Tap the heart again. The like record is deleted, though anyone who saw it already, or a tool that copied it, may still have it.
2. **Bookmark instead.** Tap the bookmark icon on a post to save it. Bookmarks (also called saved posts) are stored off-protocol and are private to you. Find them under **Saved** in the menu.
3. **Use a second account** for things you want to follow quietly. It's still public, just not tied to your name.

| Action | Visible to the author? | Visible to others? | Private? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Like | Yes, notified | Yes | No |
| Repost | Yes | Yes, on your profile | No |
| Bookmark | No | No | Yes |
| Like, then unlike | Possibly, if they saw the notification | Only to anyone who copied it first | Partly |

For the wider picture on what's public, see [how to make your Bluesky account private](/blog/how-to-make-bluesky-account-private).

## Edge Cases: Notifications, Unliking and Logged-Out Viewers

- **Like notifications can be turned off.** The author gets a notification unless they've turned like notifications off, so a missing reaction doesn't mean they didn't see your like.
- **Unliking isn't instant erasure.** The record is deleted from your repository, but anything that already copied it may keep it.
- **Logged-out visibility settings don't cover likes.** Bluesky has a setting that asks apps not to show your account to logged-out visitors. It's a request to apps, not a lock, and your likes stay public data either way.
- **Likes count toward ranking.** Likes feed into Bluesky's Discover feed and many custom feeds, which rank posts partly by early engagement. They don't affect the Following feed, which is chronological.

## Third-Party Like Viewers and Clients (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to use third-party Bluesky tools, like app passwords, next to risky ones, like typing in your main password](/images/blog/are-likes-public-on-bluesky-safety-1200x675.webp)

Since likes are public, some tools let you browse anyone's likes or get extra stats. Reading public data isn't the problem. Logging in is.

### The Real Risks

- **Your main password gives full control.** A tool with your account password can post, follow, block and change settings as you.
- **Data you share may be stored or resold.** You don't know what a small tool keeps.
- **Fake "who viewed your profile" features.** Bluesky doesn't expose profile views, so a tool that claims to show them is guessing or misleading you.

### A Quick Safety Checklist

- Prefer tools that only read public data and don't ask you to log in.
- If a tool needs access, give it a Bluesky **app password**, never your main password. You can revoke an app password at any time in settings.
- Review and delete app passwords you no longer use.

> **Bottom line:** Looking at public likes is harmless. Handing a tool your main password isn't. Use app passwords or skip the login.

Pick the scenario that fits you:

2. **You want to save posts quietly.** Use bookmarks, not likes. They're private.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are likes private on Bluesky?

No. Likes are public data on the AT Protocol. The app shows your Likes tab only to you, but anyone can see that you liked a post by looking at its like list.

### Can people see what I liked on Bluesky?

Yes. They can find you in the like list of any post you liked, and some third-party tools can show your full list of likes.

### Does Bluesky notify people when you like their post?

Yes. The author gets a notification unless they've turned like notifications off.

### Can I see who liked my Bluesky post?

Yes. Open the post and tap the like count to see the full list of accounts.

### Can I hide my likes on Bluesky?

No, not at the time of writing. There's no setting to make likes private. Unlike posts you don't want associated with you, and use bookmarks to save things privately.

### Are Bluesky bookmarks private?

Yes. Saved posts are stored privately and aren't visible to anyone else, including the post's author.

### Does unliking a post remove it completely?

Mostly. Unliking deletes the like record, but anyone who saw it, or a tool that copied it before you unliked, may still have it.

### Do likes help my posts get seen on Bluesky?

Yes, in some feeds. Likes feed into Discover and many custom feeds, which rank partly by early engagement. The Following feed is chronological and ignores them.
