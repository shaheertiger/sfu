You've found the perfect 30 seconds in a two-hour video and want to share just that part. To clip a YouTube video, open it, select **Clip** (the scissors icon under the player), drag the selector to choose a section between 5 and 60 seconds, add a title and select **Share clip**. YouTube creates a link that plays just that moment of the original video.

![How to clip a YouTube video: the Clip button shares 5 to 60 seconds, Studio trims your own upload, and a Short is a new video](/images/blog/how-to-clip-a-youtube-video-hero-1200x675.webp)

What isn't obvious is that "clipping" means three different jobs on YouTube. Sharing a moment from anyone's video, shortening your own upload, and making a vertical highlight that lives in the Shorts feed each use a different tool, and picking the wrong one wastes time.

This guide covers which one you need, the Clip button on computer and phone, why it's sometimes missing, trimming your own video in YouTube Studio, turning a moment into a Short, and what copyright allows.

## Clip, Trim or Short? Which One You Actually Need

| You want to… | Use | Whose videos | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share a funny or useful moment with a friend | Clip button | Any eligible video or live stream | A link that plays 5–60 seconds of the original |
| Remove a mistake or the slow intro from your upload | YouTube Studio Editor (Trim & cut) | Your own | The same video, edited, at the same URL |
| Make a vertical highlight from your long video | Your own editing app, or YouTube's tools for turning a video into a Short | Your own | A new Short with its own URL |
| Reuse someone else's footage in your video | Ask the owner first | Someone else's | Depends on permission and copyright |

![Clip, trim or Short: three different tools for three different jobs on YouTube](/images/blog/how-to-clip-a-youtube-video-which-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

The Clip button doesn't copy, download or re-upload anything. A clip is a pointer to a section of someone's video, and the original creator keeps control of it. If they delete the video or make it private, your clip stops working too. That's exactly why it's the right tool for sharing other people's content.

> **Key point:** Clips are for sharing a moment. Trimming is for fixing your own upload. A Short is a brand-new video. Pick the job first, then the tool.

## How to Clip a YouTube Video With the Clip Button (Computer and Phone)

The Clip feature works in the YouTube app on Android and iPhone and on youtube.com in a desktop browser. You need to be signed in. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the scissors icon if a label has moved.

![Four steps to clip a YouTube video: open it, select Clip, choose 5 to 60 seconds, share the clip](/images/blog/how-to-clip-a-youtube-video-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open the Video and Find Clip

On youtube.com, select **Clip** under the player. If you don't see it, select the three-dot **More** menu next to Share; it's sometimes tucked in there. In the app, swipe along the row of buttons under the video and tap **Clip**.

### Step 2: Drag the Selector to 5–60 Seconds

A clip panel opens. Drag the slider over the timeline, or on a computer type start and end times. This is where people go wrong: the clip has to be at least 5 seconds and no more than 60, so a longer moment won't fit.

### Step 3: Add a Title

Type a title in the text box. It's what people see when you share the link, so make it say what the moment is.

### Step 4: Select Share Clip

Copy the link or pick an app to share to.

> **Pro tip:** If the moment runs longer than 60 seconds, share a timestamped link instead. On desktop, the Share box has a **Start at** checkbox, so the link opens at the moment you choose.

### What Happens After You Share

- Anyone who opens the link sees the clip looping on the original video's watch page, with a button to watch the full video.
- Your clips are listed in your library: on desktop, look for **Your clips** in the left menu; in the app, tap **You** and find **Your clips**.
- You can delete a clip from that list at any time.

## Why the Clip Button Is Missing: 5 Common Reasons

The button only appears on eligible videos, and the rules can change, so treat this as a guide at the time of writing.

![Common reasons the YouTube Clip button is missing and what to do instead](/images/blog/how-to-clip-a-youtube-video-missing-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The creator turned clipping off | Channels can disable it for their content | Share a timestamped link instead |
| The video is set as made for kids | Clipping isn't available on these | Share a timestamped link instead |
| It's a live stream without DVR, or a stream or Premiere in an unsupported state | YouTube doesn't support clipping there | Wait until the stream is saved, or use a timestamp |
| You're signed out, or watching in an embedded player on another site | Clipping needs your account on YouTube itself | Sign in and open the video on YouTube |
| Your app is out of date | Older versions may not show it | Update the YouTube app and check again |

## How to Trim Your Own Video in YouTube Studio (Same URL, Same Views)

If the video is yours, you can cut it down without re-uploading. You keep the same URL, views and comments.

![Trimming your own video in YouTube Studio keeps the URL, views and comments](/images/blog/how-to-clip-a-youtube-video-trim-1200x675.webp)

1. Go to YouTube Studio on a computer and select **Content** in the left menu.
2. Select the video's title or thumbnail to open its details.
3. Select **Editor** in the left menu.
4. Select **Trim & cut**. A blue box appears on the timeline.
5. To shorten the start or end, drag the edges of the blue box. To remove a section from the middle, select **New cut**, drag over the part you want gone and confirm with the check mark.
6. Select **Preview** to watch the result, then **Save**.

Processing can take a while for longer videos, and the original stays live until the edit is ready. YouTube may limit edits on videos with a very high view count, especially for channels outside the YouTube Partner Program, so check the notice in the Editor if saving is blocked. The [YouTube Help Center](https://support.google.com/youtube) has the current rules.

The Editor also handles blurring faces or objects, adding audio from YouTube's library, and setting end screens.

## How to Turn a Moment From Your Video Into a Short (2 Ways)

Clips stay attached to the original video. If you want a vertical highlight that gets its own place in the Shorts feed, you need a new upload.

### Option 1: Use YouTube's Own Tools

On many of your own long videos, the YouTube app offers a way to create a Short from a section of the video (look under the **Remix** or **Create** options below the video). The exact wording and availability vary by country and app version.

### Option 2: Edit It Yourself and Upload

1. Download your own video from YouTube Studio: **Content**, then the three-dot menu next to the video, then **Download**. Better still, start from your original export, which has higher quality.
2. Open it in an editing app such as YouTube Create (available on Android in many countries at the time of writing), CapCut, Premiere or any editor you like.
3. Cut the section you want and reframe it to a 9:16 vertical canvas.
4. Add captions, since many people watch Shorts without sound.
5. Upload it as a new video. YouTube decides whether a vertical or square video counts as a Short; at the time of writing, Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long.

## YouTube Downloader Sites and Re-Uploads (Use With Caution)

The Clip button is designed for sharing, and the clip stays tied to the creator's video, so using it is fine. Taking footage out of YouTube is a different matter.

### The Real Risks

- **Downloader sites break YouTube's Terms of Service**, and many are full of misleading ads and fake download buttons.
- **Re-uploading someone else's footage** as your own Short or video can lead to Content ID claims, copyright strikes, and in some cases the loss of your channel.
- **Fair use is narrow.** Commentary, criticism and education can sometimes use short excerpts, but it depends on the details and on your country's law. "I gave credit" isn't a defense on its own.

### Safety Checklist

- Use the Clip button to share other people's moments.
- Use your own footage for highlights, ideally from your original export.
- When in doubt, ask. Many creators are happy to give permission, especially if you link back to them.

> **Bottom line:** Clip other people's videos, cut highlights only from your own.

## What to Do Next: 4 Quick Scenarios

1. **Sharing a moment with a friend:** use the Clip button, 5–60 seconds.
2. **The moment is longer than a minute:** share a timestamped link with **Start at**.
3. **Your own upload has a slow intro:** trim it in the YouTube Studio Editor and keep your views.
4. **You want highlights in the Shorts feed:** cut them from your original file, reframe to 9:16, add captions, and upload each as a new video.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long can a YouTube clip be?

A clip can be between 5 and 60 seconds long. If you need a longer excerpt from your own video, trim or re-edit the video itself, or share a timestamped link.

### Can I clip any YouTube video?

No. Clipping only works on eligible videos and live streams: creators can turn it off, and it isn't available on videos set as made for kids or on some live streams.

### Do YouTube clips count as views for the creator?

YouTube doesn't publish exactly how clip plays are counted in every report. A clip plays inside the original video's watch page rather than as a separate upload, and viewers can jump to the full video, so creators should check their own YouTube Studio analytics.

### Can I download a clip I made?

No. The Clip feature creates a shareable link, not a file. For a downloadable highlight of your own video, download it from YouTube Studio (or use your original file) and cut the section in an editing app.

### How do I delete a YouTube clip?

Open **Your clips** in your library, find the clip and use its three-dot menu to delete it. The link stops working for anyone you shared it with.

### Will trimming my video in YouTube Studio reset its views?

No. Trimming in the Studio Editor changes the existing video, so it keeps its URL, views, likes and comments, and the edited version replaces the original once processing finishes.

### Can I clip a YouTube live stream?

Sometimes. Clipping works on some live streams, but not on streams without DVR or in states YouTube doesn't support, so check for the Clip button and use a timestamped link if it's missing.

### How long can a YouTube Short be?

At the time of writing, Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long. YouTube decides whether a vertical or square upload counts as a Short.
