You just published a YouTube video and want your Instagram followers to see it. To post a YouTube video on Instagram, upload the original video file (one you own) directly to Instagram as a Reel, ideally re-edited to vertical 9:16. Instagram doesn't embed YouTube players, so if you want people to watch on YouTube itself, share the link with a link sticker in your Stories or add it to the links in your bio.

![How to post a YouTube video on Instagram: re-upload your own file as a Reel, or send people to YouTube with a link](/images/blog/post-a-youtube-video-on-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

Pasting a YouTube link into a caption does nothing, because caption links aren't clickable. That's why so many "how to share" attempts end in "link in bio" with no link in the bio.

This guide covers the four methods and when to use each, the rules on whose videos you can re-upload, reformatting horizontal video for Reels, Story links and bio links, and how to cross-post Shorts without losing reach.

## Why You Can't Just Share a YouTube Link on Instagram

Instagram is built to keep people on Instagram. It doesn't embed players from other sites, and links in captions and comments aren't clickable. So you have two real choices: bring the video to Instagram, or give people a tappable link to YouTube in one of the few places Instagram allows it.

![Bringing your YouTube video to Instagram compared with sending Instagram followers to YouTube](/images/blog/post-a-youtube-video-on-instagram-why-1200x675.webp)

| Method | Best for | Clickable link to YouTube? |
|---|---|---|
| Re-upload your own video as a Reel | Reaching new people on Instagram | No |
| Post a short clip or trailer | Teasing a longer YouTube video | Via bio or Stories |
| Story with a link sticker | Sending followers to the full video | Yes |
| Link in bio | A lasting route to your channel or latest video | Yes |

### What Most People Don't Know

Instagram has said it favors original content and may show reposted videos, or videos with other platforms' watermarks, to fewer people. So a download of your own Short with YouTube's branding on it can reach fewer people than the clean original file from your editor.

> **Key point:** Decide your goal first. Views on Instagram means uploading the video. Views on YouTube means a link sticker or bio link.

## Before You Start: Only Re-Upload Videos You Own

These methods are for your own videos, or videos you have clear permission to reuse.

- **Your own videos:** use the original file from your editing project, or download it from YouTube Studio.
- **Someone else's video:** don't download and re-upload it. That can infringe the creator's copyright and break both platforms' rules. Share a link to it in your Story instead, or ask the creator for permission and the original file.
- **Music and licensed clips:** rights that apply on YouTube don't automatically carry over to Instagram. Audio from YouTube's library or a licensed clip may be muted or removed on Instagram. When in doubt, swap the music for something from Instagram's own audio library.

## Method 1: Re-Upload Your Own YouTube Video as a Reel (Most Reach)

This is the best way to get your video in front of new people, because Reels can be recommended to people who don't follow you yet.

![Four steps to re-upload your own YouTube video as an Instagram Reel](/images/blog/post-a-youtube-video-on-instagram-reel-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Get Your Original File

Use the original export from your editing software; it's the highest quality. Or open YouTube Studio, go to **Content**, hover over the video, open the three-dot menu and choose **Download**. Availability can vary, for example for very long videos.

### Step 2: Reformat From 16:9 to 9:16

This is the step that makes or breaks it. YouTube videos are usually horizontal (16:9); Reels are vertical (9:16), 1080×1920 pixels.

| Option | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Re-edit vertically | Set the canvas to 1080×1920 and reframe each shot | Best: fills the screen |
| Stack the video | Put the horizontal video in the middle and add a title and captions above and below | Good: readable, looks deliberate |
| Upload as is | Let Instagram add bars | Works, but the video looks small on a phone |

Our guide to [Instagram Reel size](/blog/reel-size-instagram) covers safe zones and export settings.

### Step 3: Trim to Fit

Instagram's Reel limit has changed several times; at the time of writing, Reels can be up to a few minutes, and the app tells you if your video is too long. Even within the limit, a shorter cut usually holds attention better. Pick the strongest segment, skip the YouTube intro or channel bumper, and add burned-in captions for people watching without sound. For videos well over the limit, see [how to post a long video on Instagram](/blog/how-to-post-a-long-video-on-instagram).

### Step 4: Upload, Cover and Caption

1. In the Instagram app, tap **+** and choose **Reel**, then select your edited video.
2. Choose a cover. Design it at 1080×1920 and keep the key text in the center so it survives the 3:4 crop on your profile grid at the time of writing.
3. Write your caption, mention that the full video is on YouTube, and point people to the link in your bio.
4. Share now or schedule it.

> **Pro tip:** Write a new caption rather than pasting your YouTube description. Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters, but only the first line or so shows before "more".

## Method 2: Post a Teaser Clip (Best for Driving YouTube Views)

1. Cut a short, self-contained clip that leaves people wanting more.
2. Upload it as a Reel or a feed video. Feed videos can use a 4:5 frame (1080×1350), which suits clips cut from horizontal footage better than a square.
3. In the caption, say where the full video is: "Full video on YouTube, link in bio."

## Method 3: Share the Link in Your Stories (Tappable)

Stories are the only place on Instagram where you can add a tappable link to a specific video in the content itself. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the sticker icon if a label has moved.

![Four steps to share a YouTube link in an Instagram Story with a link sticker](/images/blog/post-a-youtube-video-on-instagram-story-1200x675.webp)

1. Copy the link to your YouTube video (tap **Share** on the video, then **Copy link**).
2. In Instagram, create a new **Story** with a background: a thumbnail screenshot, a short clip or a still.
3. Tap the **sticker** icon, choose **Link**, paste your YouTube URL and set the sticker text, for example "Watch the full video".
4. Place the sticker where it's easy to tap, away from the top and bottom edges.
5. Share it, and add it to a Highlight so it stays visible after 24 hours.

This is also the right way to recommend another creator's video: link to it rather than re-uploading it. If your Stories look soft, see [why your Instagram Story looks blurry](/blog/why-does-my-instagram-story-look-blurry).

## Method 4: Add the Video to Your Bio Links (Permanent)

At the time of writing, Instagram lets you add several links to your bio.

1. Go to your profile and tap **Edit profile**.
2. Tap **Links**, then **Add external link**.
3. Paste the URL to your video or channel and give it a clear title, such as "New video: How I edit Reels".
4. Save, and update it each time you publish a new video.

## Cross-Posting YouTube Shorts to Instagram Reels (Do It Right)

Shorts are already vertical 9:16, the same shape as a Reel, so they're the easiest content to share across both.

![Cross-posting YouTube Shorts to Instagram Reels: what helps reach and what hurts it](/images/blog/post-a-youtube-video-on-instagram-shorts-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| A YouTube watermark on the video | Upload the original file, not a download | Instagram may show videos with other platforms' watermarks to fewer people |
| Music muted on Instagram | Replace it with Instagram audio or an original sound | YouTube music rights don't carry over |
| On-screen text hidden behind buttons | Move text toward the center | Instagram's buttons and caption overlay sit in different places |
| A pasted YouTube description | Write a new Instagram caption | Hook in the first line, hashtags that suit Instagram |

## YouTube Downloader Sites (Use With Caution)

It's tempting to grab a video, yours or someone else's, from a downloader site rather than finding the original file.

### The Real Risks

- **Other people's videos:** re-uploading them can infringe copyright and get your Instagram content removed.
- **Lower quality:** downloads are compressed versions of your upload, and some add watermarks.
- **Ads and malware:** many downloader sites are full of misleading ads and fake buttons.

### Safety Checklist

- Use your original export, or YouTube Studio's own **Download** for your videos.
- Never upload someone else's video without written permission.
- Keep a folder of your original exports so you never need a downloader.

> **Bottom line:** Your own original file is better quality, safer and better for reach than any download.

1. **Launch day:** a teaser Reel, plus a Story with a link sticker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I post a YouTube link on Instagram?

Yes, but only in certain places. Links in captions and comments aren't clickable, so use a link sticker in your Stories or add the link to your bio.

### Can I embed a YouTube video on Instagram?

No. Instagram doesn't support embedded players from other sites, so you have to upload the video file directly, which you should only do with videos you own or have permission to use.

### Is it legal to repost someone else's YouTube video on Instagram?

Not without permission. Downloading and re-uploading someone else's video can infringe their copyright and break both platforms' terms, so share a link in your Story or ask the creator for permission.

### What size should a YouTube video be for Instagram?

Use 1080×1920 pixels (9:16) for Reels and 1080×1350 (4:5) for feed videos cut from horizontal footage. The [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) has presets for cover images.

### Why was the music removed from my video on Instagram?

Music licensed for YouTube isn't automatically licensed for Instagram. If Instagram detects audio it doesn't have rights to, it may mute the video or limit where it's shown, so replace the track with Instagram audio or an original sound.

### Will reposting my YouTube Short on Instagram hurt its reach?

It can if the file has another platform's watermark. Instagram has said it may show reposted or watermarked content to fewer people, so upload the original file.

### How do I share a YouTube video in my Instagram Story?

Create a Story, add a link sticker with the YouTube URL, and place it where it's easy to tap. Add the Story to a Highlight to keep it visible after 24 hours.

### Can I upload a full-length YouTube video to Instagram?

Usually not as one Reel, because of the length limit. Post a teaser or split it into parts, and link to the full video in your bio.
