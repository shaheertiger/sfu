Your caption is written, and Instagram won't let you add another word. The Instagram caption character limit is **2,200 characters**, including spaces, emoji, line breaks and hashtags, and a caption can hold up to **30 hashtags**. But the feed shows only the first 125 characters or so before **…more**, so the first line carries the post.

![Instagram caption character limit: 2,200 characters, with about the first 125 shown before more](/images/blog/instagram-caption-character-limit-hero-1200x675.webp)

So there are really two limits. The hard one, 2,200, stops you posting. The soft one, about 125, decides whether anyone reads the rest.

This guide covers every Instagram text limit in one table, where captions get cut in the feed and in Reels, what counts toward 2,200, hashtags in the caption versus the first comment, how to structure a long caption, and how to write one draft that fits Instagram and shorter platforms too.

## Why Only the First 125 Characters Matter (Where Captions Truncate)

The feed cuts a caption after roughly 125 characters, or at the first line break if that comes sooner, and shows **…more**. Reels show even less in the full-screen player, where the caption sits over the video.

![What shows before more in an Instagram caption compared with what's hidden](/images/blog/instagram-caption-character-limit-truncate-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

A line break counts as a cut point. If your caption opens with a short line and then a blank line, the feed may show only that first short line before **…more**. That's useful when the first line is a strong hook, and a waste when it's "Hey everyone!".

> **Key point:** Write the first line as if it's the whole caption. Put the hook, the offer or the question first, then the detail.

## What Counts Toward the 2,200 Characters

| Element | Counts? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Letters, numbers, spaces | Yes, 1 each | |
| Emoji | Yes, 1 each | As Instagram counts them |
| Line breaks | Yes | Each one uses a character |
| Hashtags | Yes, in full | `#weekendbaking` is 14, and up to 30 per caption |
| Mentions | Yes, in full | Up to 20 per caption |
| Links | Yes, in full | And they aren't clickable in captions |

Because links aren't clickable in captions, a URL spends characters for nothing. Say "link in bio" instead.

## How to Write an Instagram Caption That Fits in 5 Steps

![Five steps to write an Instagram caption: hook, value, line breaks, call to action, hashtags](/images/blog/instagram-caption-character-limit-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Write the Hook in Under 125 Characters

This is the step people get wrong. The opening line is the only part most people see, so it has to make them tap **…more**: a result, a question, a bold claim, a "here's how".

### Step 2: Add the Value

Give the detail, the story or the steps. Keep sentences short; people read captions on a phone.

### Step 3: Break It Into Short Paragraphs

Line breaks are kept if you add them in the app or through the API. Pasting from some apps collapses them, which is why people use an [Instagram line break generator](/tools/instagram-line-break-generator) to keep paragraphs apart.

### Step 4: End With One Call to Action

Ask a question, point to the link in your bio, or ask people to save the post. One ask, not three.

### Step 5: Add a Few Relevant Hashtags

Instagram suggests three to five relevant hashtags rather than the full 30.

> **Pro tip:** Read your caption on your phone before posting. If the first line doesn't make you want to tap "more", the rest won't get read.

## Instagram Caption Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| The caption is cut off after one line | Move the hook into the first line and remove the blank line under it | The feed cuts at the first line break if it comes before ~125 characters |
| Line breaks disappear after pasting | Type the breaks in the app, or prepare the text with a line break generator | Some apps collapse breaks when you paste |
| Instagram won't accept the caption | Cut it below 2,200 characters, counting hashtags and line breaks | Everything counts toward the limit |
| Hashtags seem to be ignored | Use 30 or fewer, and pick ones that match the post | Instagram may reject a caption or comment with more than 30, and suggests three to five |
| People ask "where's the link?" | Put it in your bio and say so in the caption | Links in captions aren't clickable |

### Long Caption or Short Caption?

Both can work. A short caption suits a photo that speaks for itself or a Reel where the video carries the message. A long caption suits a story, a tutorial or a post people will save to come back to. What doesn't work is a long caption with a weak first line, because almost nobody gets past it. Whichever you choose, the first 125 characters do the same job.

## Hashtags: In the Caption or the First Comment?

Instagram has said hashtags in the caption and in the first comment work the same for discovery.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Instagram caption limit?

The limit is 2,200 characters, including spaces, emoji and hashtags, with up to 30 hashtags and 20 mentions.

### How many characters show before "more" on Instagram?

About 125 show in the feed, or up to the first line break if it comes earlier. Reels show less in the full-screen player.

### What is the Instagram bio character limit?

The bio allows 150 characters. The name field allows 30.

### Does the 2,200 limit include hashtags?

Yes. Hashtags count toward the 2,200 characters whether they're at the start or the end of the caption.

### Do emoji count as one character on Instagram?

Yes, Instagram counts each emoji as one character toward the 2,200.

### How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?

Instagram suggests three to five relevant hashtags, even though a caption can hold up to 30.

### Can I put a link in an Instagram caption?

You can type one, but it won't be clickable and it uses up characters. Put the link in your bio and say "link in bio" in the caption.

### What is the Instagram alt text limit?

Alt text allows 100 characters per image, so describe the main subject briefly.
