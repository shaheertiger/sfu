You've got a set of photos, a recipe or a list of tips, and you want people to swipe through them on TikTok. To make a slideshow on TikTok, tap **+**, open your gallery, tap **Select multiple** (or the photo option), pick up to **35 photos** in the order you want, tap **Next**, and make sure the post is set to **Photo** mode rather than video before you add music and publish.

![How to make a slideshow on TikTok: pick up to 35 photos, check Photo mode, add a sound and post](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

The result is a photo post that viewers swipe through, with your sound playing underneath. The steps are short, but one setting catches most people out: TikTok can quietly turn your photos into a video.

This guide covers the two kinds of slideshow, the photo-mode steps, templates, how to make slides people actually swipe, fixes for when it goes wrong, and how to schedule photo posts ahead.

## Photo Mode vs Templates: Two Kinds of TikTok Slideshow

![TikTok photo mode compared with templates: swipeable photo posts of up to 35 images versus fixed-timing videos](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok-types-1200x675.webp)

| Type | How it plays | Photos | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Photo mode (carousel)** | Viewers swipe; it can also auto-advance | Up to 35 | Tips, tutorials, recipes, before-and-after, product sets |
| **Template** | Plays as a video with fixed timing and effects | Set by the template, often 5 to 15 | Quick montages that match a trending style |

Photo mode is what most people mean by a TikTok slideshow. Templates turn your pictures into a short video instead.

### What Most People Don't Know

After you tap **Next**, TikTok sometimes defaults to a **video** preview of your photos. If you post without switching to **Photo**, you get a video slideshow with fixed timing, not a swipeable post. The dots under the preview are the tell: dots mean it's a swipeable photo post.

> **Key point:** Check for the Photo toggle (and the dots under the preview) on the editing screen every time. It's the one step that decides whether you get a slideshow or a video.

## How to Make a Slideshow in Photo Mode: 8 Steps

![The key steps to make a TikTok slideshow in photo mode, with the Photo toggle highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu names move between app versions, but the flow has stayed the same.

### Step 1: Tap + at the Bottom of the Screen

Open TikTok and tap **+**.

### Step 2: Tap Upload

It's the gallery thumbnail beside the record button.

### Step 3: Choose Select Multiple or the Photos Tab

If no photos appear, TikTok doesn't have access to your library. Allow it in your phone's settings under TikTok, then Photos.

### Step 4: Tap Your Photos in Order

The number on each photo is its slide position, so tap them in the order you want them seen. You can pick up to 35.

### Step 5: Tap Next

This opens the editing screen, where the next step matters most.

### Step 6: Switch to Photo, Not Video

On the editing screen, check that **Photo** is selected. This is the step people miss.

> **Pro tip:** Before you add anything else, swipe the preview. If it swipes, you're in photo mode. If it plays by itself with transitions, you're in video mode.

### Step 7: Add a Sound, Text and Stickers

Tap **Add sound** to pick music, and add text, stickers or filters to individual slides.

### Step 8: Write the Caption and Post

Tap **Next**, write your caption, choose who can see it, and tap **Post**.

## How to Make a Slideshow With a Template (4 Steps)

1. Tap **+**, then **Templates** (on some versions it sits at the bottom of the camera screen).
2. Browse and pick one; each shows how many photos it takes.
3. Tap **Upload photos**, choose the right number, and tap **OK**.
4. Edit text or sound if the template allows, then post.

Templates are quick, but you can't add more photos than the template allows, and the timing is fixed.

## How to Make a TikTok Slideshow People Actually Swipe

![What makes a TikTok slideshow worth swiping, next to the mistakes that make people scroll past](/images/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok-swipe-1200x675.webp)

- **Hook on slide one.** The first image is the cover in the feed. Put the promise there in large text: "7 mistakes with sourdough" beats a plain photo.
- **One idea per slide.** Short text, big type, high contrast. People read slideshows quickly.
- **Use vertical images.** 1080×1920 (9:16) fills the screen; square or landscape photos get bars. Our [TikTok photo resizer](/tools/tiktok-photo-resizer) crops or pads them to fit.
- **Keep text out of the bottom fifth.** The caption and buttons cover it.
- **Pick a sound that fits the pace.** Trending sounds help discovery, but the music shouldn't fight the reading.
- **End with a reason to act.** A final slide with "Save this for later" or a question invites saves and comments.

| Slide | What goes on it | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The promise, in big text | "5 plants you can't kill" |
| 2 to second-last | One idea each | One plant per slide, with one line of care advice |
| Last | A reason to act | "Save this for your next plant shop" |

## Why Your TikTok Slideshow Isn't Working (Problem, Fix, Why)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| It posted as a video | Delete it and post again with **Photo** selected | You missed the toggle in step 6 |
| The photos look cropped | Resize to 9:16 first | TikTok fills a vertical screen |
| No music option | Pick a sound from the Commercial Music Library | Some sounds aren't licensed for business accounts |
| Photos missing in the gallery | Allow photo access in your phone's settings | TikTok can't see your library |
| Few views | Check hook, topic and timing | Photo posts are recommended like videos |

Photo posts don't qualify for the Creator Rewards Program at the time of writing, so if you're posting for payouts, that's worth knowing. See [why your TikTok isn't getting views](/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views) for the usual causes of low views.

## Can You Make a TikTok Slideshow on a Computer?

Yes. On TikTok's website or TikTok Studio, most accounts can upload images as a photo post. Music and effect options are more limited than in the app, so if the sound matters, finish in the app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many photos can a TikTok slideshow have?

Up to 35 photos in photo mode, at the time of writing. Templates take a fixed number set by the template.

### Can you make a TikTok slideshow on a computer?

Yes. On TikTok's website or TikTok Studio you can upload images as a photo post on most accounts. Music and effect options are more limited than in the app.

### How long does each slide show for?

Viewers swipe at their own pace. If they don't swipe, the post can auto-advance with the sound, and you don't set exact timings in photo mode. For exact timings, use a template or edit a video.

### Why did my slideshow turn into a video?

TikTok sometimes switches to video mode after you tap Next. Check that Photo is selected on the editing screen before you post.

### Can you schedule a TikTok slideshow?

Yes. A scheduler that supports TikTok photo posts, such as Schedule For You, publishes up to 35 images at the time you pick. TikTok Studio's support for scheduled photo posts has varied by account.

### What size should TikTok slideshow photos be?

1080×1920 pixels, a 9:16 ratio, fills the screen. Square or landscape photos get bars around them.

### Can I add music to a TikTok slideshow?

Yes, in the app. Tap Add sound on the editing screen. Business accounts choose from the Commercial Music Library.

### Do TikTok slideshows get fewer views?

Not by design: photo posts are recommended like videos. They don't qualify for the Creator Rewards Program at the time of writing.
