You've got a few thousand followers, your videos get decent views, and you're wondering when the money starts. You can make money on TikTok in six main ways: the **Creator Rewards Program** (paid views on videos over one minute), **LIVE gifts**, **Subscriptions**, **TikTok Shop** affiliate commissions, **brand deals**, and selling **your own products or services**.

![How to make money on TikTok: six ways to get paid, from no follower minimum to 10,000 followers](/images/blog/how-to-make-money-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

TikTok's own programs have minimums. At the time of writing, Creator Rewards needs 18+, 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days, and LIVE gifts need 18+ and about 1,000 followers. **Brand deals and your own products have no minimum at all.**

This guide covers how each method works, how many followers you need, why TikTok's pay per view isn't fixed, and how to get monetized faster.

## Why TikTok Earnings Are So Hard to Predict

There's no fixed TikTok pay rate, no public price list and no single "monetization" switch. Each program has its own rules, and those rules differ by country and change often.

![Why TikTok earnings vary: what TikTok controls compared with what you control](/images/blog/how-to-make-money-on-tiktok-why-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Views alone don't pay. Through Creator Rewards, only **original videos longer than one minute** earn, and only **qualified views** count (TikTok filters out very short watches, repeat views and some sources). Duets, stitches, photo posts and ads don't earn. So a million views on a 20-second video earns nothing from that program.

> **Key point:** The creators who earn most from TikTok usually earn it off TikTok: brand deals and their own products. TikTok's programs are a bonus on top, not the whole plan.

Open **TikTok Studio** (or **Creator tools** in your profile menu) to see which programs you can join.

### 1. Creator Rewards Program (Paid Views on Videos Over 1 Minute)

This is how TikTok pays for views. **How much does TikTok pay?** There's no fixed rate. Pay is measured as RPM, earnings per 1,000 qualified views, and it varies by video, audience and country. Our guide to [how much TikTok pays for 1 million views](/blog/how-much-money-do-you-get-on-tiktok-for-1-million-views) works through examples.

### 2. LIVE Gifts (About 1,000 Followers, 18+)

Viewers buy coins and send gifts during your LIVE; you receive Diamonds and cash them out. TikTok keeps a share, roughly half by most estimates. See [how much a Galaxy is worth on TikTok](/blog/how-much-is-a-galaxy-on-tiktok) for the coin math and [how to go live on TikTok](/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok) for the requirements.

### 3. Subscriptions (for a Loyal Core Audience)

Followers pay a monthly fee for perks such as subscriber badges, custom emotes and subscriber-only content or LIVEs. It suits creators with a loyal core audience more than those with big but casual reach.

### 4. TikTok Shop Affiliate (Commission on Sales)

Add products from TikTok Shop sellers to your videos and LIVEs and earn a commission on each sale. Product reviews, demos and "what I bought" videos work well. Sellers can also invite you to paid collaborations. Our guide to [selling on TikTok Shop](/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop) covers the seller side.

### 5. Brand Deals (No Follower Minimum)

Brands pay creators to feature products, either directly or through TikTok's creator marketplace (TikTok One at the time of writing). Small creators with an engaged niche audience often get deals sooner than they expect.

> **Pro tip:** Have a one-page media kit ready before a brand asks: who your audience is, your average views, and two or three results from past posts. The step people get wrong is quoting their follower count instead of what their videos actually do.

### 6. Your Own Products and Services

Courses, coaching, digital downloads, merch, a local business or a newsletter. TikTok brings attention; your link in bio turns it into sales. For many creators this earns more than every TikTok program combined.

## How to Get Monetized Faster: 5 Habits

![Five habits that help you get monetized on TikTok faster](/images/blog/how-to-make-money-on-tiktok-faster-1200x675.webp)

1. **Post consistently.** A steady rhythm teaches the algorithm who your audience is. Pick a realistic schedule and stick to it.
2. **Make some videos over one minute.** They're the only ones that earn from Creator Rewards.
3. **Stay original.** Reposted or lightly edited content from others won't qualify and can get flagged.
4. **Avoid strikes.** Recent Community Guidelines or copyright violations block monetization.
5. **Post the same video elsewhere.** A vertical video can also earn on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

## "Get Paid to Watch" Apps and Bought Followers (Use With Caution)

Search for ways to make money on TikTok faster and you'll find apps that pay you to scroll, services selling followers, and "monetization unlock" offers.

### The Real Risks

- **Bought followers break TikTok's rules.** They can get the account restricted, which blocks monetization, and they don't watch your videos, so your views don't rise.
- **"Pay to scroll" apps** often pay very little, and some collect more data than they need. See [can you get paid to scroll on TikTok](/blog/can-you-get-paid-to-scroll-on-tiktok).
- **Login requests.** Nobody outside TikTok can unlock a program for you, and anything asking for your password can take over the account.

### Safety Checklist

- Check eligibility only in TikTok Studio.
- Grow with your own original videos.
- Never share your password or login code.

> **Bottom line:** Every shortcut to monetization risks the account that's supposed to earn the money. Slow and original is faster in the end.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you get paid on TikTok?

Through Creator Rewards for qualified views, LIVE gifts, Subscriptions and TikTok Shop commissions, paid out from TikTok's earnings section, plus brand deals and your own sales paid directly.

### How many followers do you need to get paid on TikTok?

10,000 for Creator Rewards and about 1,000 for LIVE gifts at the time of writing. Brand deals and your own products have no minimum.

### How much does TikTok pay per view?

There's no fixed rate. Creator Rewards pays per 1,000 qualified views on eligible videos over one minute, and the rate varies by video and audience.

### Can you make money on TikTok without showing your face?

Yes. Voiceovers, tutorials, screen recordings, product demos and photo posts all work, and they can qualify for the same programs if they're original.

### Do you need a business account to make money on TikTok?

No. Creator Rewards requires a personal account at the time of writing. Business accounts are aimed at brands and have different tools.

### Do videos under one minute make money on TikTok?

Not from Creator Rewards, which only pays on original videos longer than one minute. Short videos can still earn through brand deals, TikTok Shop and your own products.

### Can you make money on TikTok under 18?

Not from TikTok's own programs, which need you to be 18 or older at the time of writing.

It depends on your audience and content. Compare the programs in our guide to [which social media platform pays the most](/blog/what-social-media-platform-pays-the-most).
