To schedule a tweet, open x.com in a browser, start a post, click the **calendar icon** at the bottom of the composer, pick a date and time, and click **Schedule**. The X mobile app has no scheduling option at the time of writing, so on a phone you either use the website in a mobile browser or a scheduler app.

Here's the web method step by step, where scheduled posts live, what can't be scheduled, and how a scheduler handles X together with your other accounts.

## Schedule a post on x.com

1. Go to x.com and click **Post**.
2. Write the post, add up to four images, a GIF or a video.
3. Click the **calendar icon** in the toolbar under the text.
4. Choose the date, time and time zone, then click **Confirm**.
5. Click **Schedule** instead of Post.

You can schedule up to 18 months ahead. Polls can't be scheduled, and a thread can only be scheduled as its first post.

## Where scheduled tweets live

Start a new post, click the calendar icon, then **Scheduled posts** at the top of the dialog. From there you can edit the text, change the time or delete the post. Scheduled posts don't appear on your profile until they publish.

## Scheduling tweets on mobile

The X app for iPhone and Android can't schedule. Two options:

- Open x.com in your phone's browser (request the desktop site if the calendar icon is hidden) and schedule there.
- Use a scheduler app or website that connects to X, which also lets you see the queue and edit from your phone.

## Schedule tweets with a scheduler

A scheduler is worth it when X is one of several places you post, when you want a queue with set posting times, or when you want to schedule from a phone. In Schedule For You, X connects with X's own sign-in:

1. Write the post once. The counter counts characters the way X does, with links scored as 23 and most emoji as two, the same as the free [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter).
2. Attach up to four images, a GIF or a video.
3. Select X and any other accounts, and tailor each platform's version; the X version can be the short one.
4. Pick a time, or **Add to queue** to fill the next open slot.

A caption with a line holding only `---` publishes as a thread, each part a reply to the one before, with media on the first post. Failed posts send an alert with the reason and a retry. The [X scheduler page](/x) lists what's supported.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you schedule tweets on the X app?
No. Scheduling is only on x.com at the time of writing. Use the website in a mobile browser or a scheduler app.

### How far ahead can you schedule a tweet?
Up to 18 months on x.com.

### Can you schedule a thread on X?
Not natively; x.com schedules only a single post. A scheduler like Schedule For You publishes a thread from one caption split at `---` lines.

### Is there a free way to schedule tweets?
x.com's built-in scheduler is free for single posts from a browser. Schedulers that add a queue, threads and other platforms are usually paid; Schedule For You has a 7-day free trial with a card.
