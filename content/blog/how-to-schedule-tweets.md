You've written a tweet at midnight and want it to go out at 9 AM instead. To schedule a tweet, open x.com in a browser, start a post, click the **calendar icon** at the bottom of the composer, pick a date and time, and click **Schedule**.

![How to schedule tweets on X: open x.com, click the calendar icon, pick a time, click Schedule](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-tweets-hero-1200x675.webp)

The catch: the X mobile app has no scheduling option at the time of writing. On a phone you either use the website in a mobile browser or a scheduler app. And once a post is scheduled, it doesn't appear on your profile, which makes it easy to lose track of.

This guide covers the web method step by step, where scheduled posts live, scheduling from your phone, what can't be scheduled natively, and how a scheduler handles X with your other accounts.

## Why You Can't Find the Schedule Button on X (Web vs App)

![Where you can schedule tweets: yes on x.com in a browser, no in the X app for iPhone and Android](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-tweets-web-vs-app-1200x675.webp)

Scheduling lives in the composer on x.com. The X app for iPhone and Android doesn't have it at the time of writing.

| Where | Can you schedule? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| x.com on a computer | Yes | Calendar icon in the composer |
| x.com in a phone's browser | Yes | Request the desktop site if the icon is hidden |
| X app for iPhone or Android | No | Use the website or a scheduler |
| A scheduler app or website | Yes | Also shows your queue and lets you edit from a phone |

### What Most People Don't Know

Scheduled posts don't appear on your profile until they publish, and they aren't in a menu you'd look at. They sit behind the calendar icon in the composer, under **Scheduled posts**. People often schedule the same post twice because they can't find the first one.

> **Key point:** X's built-in scheduler is free, but it's web-only and schedules one post at a time.

## How to Schedule a Tweet on x.com (5 Steps)

![Steps to schedule a tweet on x.com: write the post, use the calendar icon, then click Schedule instead of Post](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-tweets-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between versions of the site, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Click Post

Go to x.com and click **Post**.

### Step 2: Write the Post and Add Media

Add up to four images, a GIF or a video.

### Step 3: Click the Calendar Icon

It's in the toolbar under the text.

### Step 4: Pick the Date, Time and Time Zone

Choose them, then click **Confirm**.

### Step 5: Click Schedule, Not Post

The button changes to **Schedule**. Click it.

> **Pro tip:** Check the time zone in Step 4. The step people get wrong is scheduling in their own zone for an audience somewhere else, or after travelling, and the post lands at 3 AM.

You can schedule up to 18 months ahead.

## Where Scheduled Tweets Live (and How to Edit Them)

Start a new post, click the calendar icon, then **Scheduled posts** at the top of the dialog. From there you can edit the text, change the time or delete the post.

| Schedule a poll | No | Post polls live |
| Schedule a whole thread | No, a single post only | A scheduler that publishes threads |
| A queue with set posting times | No | A scheduler with posting slots |
| Post to other platforms too | No | A cross-platform scheduler |

## How to Schedule Tweets From Your Phone (2 Ways)

1. **Use x.com in your phone's browser.** Request the desktop site if the calendar icon is hidden, then follow the five steps above.
2. **Use a scheduler app or website** that connects to X, which also lets you see the queue and edit from your phone.

## Auto-Posting Bots and Password-Based Apps (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to schedule on X, like the built-in scheduler and official sign-in, next to risky ones like bots that want your password](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-tweets-safety-1200x675.webp)

Scheduling a post you wrote is normal. Some tools go further: auto-follow, auto-reply, auto-DM, or posting the same text again and again.

### The Real Risks

- **X's automation rules** restrict bulk following, automated replies and duplicate posts, and accounts that break them can be limited or suspended.
- **Password-based apps** get full control of your account.
- **Repeated identical posts** can look like spam.

### A Quick Safety Checklist

- Connect tools through X's own sign-in screen, never by typing your password into their form.
- Schedule posts you wrote; skip auto-follow and auto-DM features.
- Vary repeated posts instead of sending the same text.

> **Bottom line:** Scheduling is fine. Automating your conversations isn't.

## Schedule Tweets, Threads and Other Platforms From One Queue

![The Schedule For You composer with X, Bluesky, Instagram and LinkedIn selected and a separate caption for each](/images/blog/composer-four-platforms-1440x900.webp)

A scheduler is worth it when X is one of several places you post, when you want a queue with set times, or when you want to schedule from a phone.

Take Omar, a developer who posts a short tip on X every weekday, a longer version on LinkedIn, and the same tip on Bluesky. Scheduling on x.com covers only X, and only from a laptop. Posting the other two by hand adds about 10 minutes a day, close to an hour a week, and the Friday thread never gets scheduled because x.com can't.

In Schedule For You, X connects with X's own sign-in:

1. Write the post once. The counter counts characters the way X does, with links scored as 23 and most emoji as two, the same as the free [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter).
2. Attach up to four images, a GIF or a video.
3. Select X and any other accounts, and tailor each platform's version; the X version can be the short one.
4. Pick a time, or **Add to queue** to fill the next open slot.

A caption with a line holding only `---` publishes as a thread, each part a reply to the one before, with media on the first post. Failed posts send an alert with the reason and a retry.

Where we're not the best fit: if you only post to X now and then from a laptop, x.com's scheduler is free and enough. Neither can schedule polls.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you schedule tweets on the X app?

No. Scheduling is only on x.com at the time of writing. Use the website in a mobile browser or a scheduler app.

### How far ahead can you schedule a tweet?

Up to 18 months on x.com.

### Where do I find my scheduled tweets?

Start a new post on x.com, click the calendar icon, then Scheduled posts. They don't appear on your profile until they publish.

### Can you schedule a thread on X?

Not natively; x.com schedules only a single post. A scheduler like Schedule For You publishes a thread from one caption split at `---` lines.

### Can you schedule a poll on X?

No. Polls can't be scheduled on x.com, so post them live.

### Is there a free way to schedule tweets?

Yes. x.com's built-in scheduler is free for single posts from a browser. Schedule For You also has a Free plan for 3 accounts and up to 10 scheduled posts at a time, no card needed; paid plans add unlimited scheduling and start with a 7-day free trial that needs a card.

### Can I edit a scheduled tweet?

Yes. Open Scheduled posts from the calendar icon, then edit the text, change the time or delete it.

### Do scheduled tweets get less reach?

There's no evidence X treats scheduled posts differently. See [do scheduled posts get less views](/blog/do-scheduled-posts-get-less-views).
