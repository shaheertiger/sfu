You've planned your first stream, opened TikTok, and there's no LIVE button anywhere. To go live on TikTok, tap **+** at the bottom of the app, swipe along the bottom menu to **LIVE**, add a title and a cover, and tap **Go LIVE**. At the time of writing, most accounts need about **1,000 followers** and must be **18 or older** to see the LIVE option, and the account has to be in good standing.

![How to go live on TikTok: tap plus, swipe to LIVE, add a title, and go live if you meet the follower, age and standing rules](/images/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

If LIVE isn't in the menu, one of those three rules is usually the reason. TikTok doesn't always tell you which one.

This guide covers the requirements, the steps on phone and computer, how to stream a console, how to end a stream on PC, the LIVE rules, and what to do if you're not eligible yet.

## Why the LIVE Button Is Missing: TikTok's 3 Requirements

TikTok gates LIVE so that new and young accounts can't stream straight away. If the option isn't in your menu, the app hides it instead of explaining why.

![TikTok LIVE requirements: followers, age and account standing, and what blocks each one](/images/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok-requirements-1200x675.webp)

| Requirement | At the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Followers | About 1,000 for most accounts; TikTok says the threshold can vary by region |
| Age to go live | 18+ |
| Age to receive gifts | 18+ |
| Account standing | No recent Community Guidelines violations |
| Desktop streaming (LIVE Studio or stream key) | Offered to some eligible accounts, not all |

### What Most People Don't Know

The follower number isn't the same everywhere. TikTok says the threshold can vary by region, and it tests and changes the rules, so two creators with the same follower count can see different options. The place to check what applies to your account is the **LIVE Center** in the app (Profile > menu > Creator tools > LIVE Center, at the time of writing).

> **Key point:** No LIVE button usually means one of three things: too few followers, under 18, or a recent violation. Check the LIVE Center before you try anything else.

## How to Go Live on TikTok From Your Phone (5 Steps)

Menu labels change between app versions, so if a name doesn't match, look for the closest one.

![Five steps to go live on TikTok from your phone](/images/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Tap + at the Bottom of the Screen

It's the same button you use to post a video.

### Step 2: Swipe the Bottom Menu to LIVE

If you don't see LIVE, you aren't eligible yet. Updating the app sometimes helps if you've only just crossed the threshold.

### Step 3: Add a Title That Says What's Happening

Pick a short title that tells people what they'll see, and choose a cover image.

### Step 4: Set Up Goals, Filters and Moderators (Optional)

You can set a LIVE goal, add filters, turn on comment filters, or add a moderator.

### Step 5: Tap Go LIVE

TikTok counts down and you're on. Your followers get a notification and the stream can show up in the LIVE feed and on For You.

> **Pro tip:** The step people get wrong is the first minute. Start with something already happening, not "waiting for people to join". A clear title and an immediate start help you keep the first viewers who tap in.

## How to Go Live on TikTok Without 1,000 Followers (What Actually Works)

There's no supported shortcut. Accounts below the threshold don't get the LIVE option, and the old in-app "request access" form is no longer shown to most users. What does work:

- **Grow to the threshold.** Our guides on [getting more TikTok followers](/blog/how-to-get-more-followers-on-tiktok) and [going viral on TikTok](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok) cover the habits that move the number.
- **Join someone else's LIVE.** A host can invite you as a guest (multi-guest), which needs no follower count from you.
- **Check your region.** The threshold varies, so some accounts see LIVE earlier.

## How to Go Live on TikTok From a Computer (2 Ways)

Eligible accounts can stream from a computer in two ways.

![Two ways to go live on TikTok from a computer: TikTok LIVE Studio or a stream key in OBS or Streamlabs](/images/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok-desktop-1200x675.webp)

| | TikTok LIVE Studio | Stream key in OBS or Streamlabs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | TikTok's own desktop app for Windows | Your streaming software sends the stream to TikTok |
| Who gets it | Eligible accounts | Some eligible accounts; not every account is offered a key |
| Setup | Sign in, add camera, screen or game, click Go LIVE | Paste the server URL and key, start streaming |
| Good for | A simple desktop stream | Custom layouts and multistreaming |

Turn the canvas to vertical (for example 720×1280 or 1080×1920) so the stream fills phone screens.

### How to Stream on TikTok From a PS5 (Capture Card Needed)

The PS5 has no TikTok option in its broadcast menu (it supports Twitch and YouTube). To stream PS5 games to TikTok, connect the console to a **capture card**, plug the card into a computer, and add it as a video source in TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS. From there it's the same as any desktop stream.

### Does Restream Show TikTok Chat?

Restream and similar multistreaming tools can send one stream to TikTok along with YouTube or Twitch if you have a TikTok stream key. Whether TikTok's chat appears in the tool's combined chat depends on the tool's current TikTok support, so check its help pages. Many streamers keep TikTok open on a phone or in LIVE Studio to read and moderate comments.

### How to End a TikTok LIVE on PC

In TikTok LIVE Studio, click **End LIVE**. If you stream with a key from OBS or Streamlabs, click **Stop Streaming**, and end the session on TikTok's side too if your LIVE setup page still shows it as active. On a phone, tap the **X** or power icon in the top corner and confirm.

## TikTok LIVE Rules: 3 That Catch Streamers Out

LIVE follows the same Community Guidelines as videos, plus a few LIVE-specific ones.

![What's fine on TikTok LIVE compared with what gets a stream restricted](/images/blog/how-to-go-live-on-tiktok-rules-1200x675.webp)

- **Can you smoke on TikTok LIVE?** Tobacco, vaping and alcohol are treated as regulated goods. Showing them can get a LIVE restricted or kept out of recommendations, and promoting or selling them isn't allowed. The safe choice is not to.
- **No minors alone on camera** and no streams that are just a static image or a rerun of a recording.
- **Gifts need 18+** and TikTok takes a share of their value; our guide to [what a Galaxy is worth on TikTok](/blog/how-much-is-a-galaxy-on-tiktok) explains the coin math.

## Can't Go Live? Troubleshooting Table

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |

| No LIVE option, plenty of followers | Under 18, or a recent violation | Check the LIVE Center and your account status |
| LIVE option disappeared | A recent Community Guidelines violation | Wait out the restriction, appeal if it's a mistake |
| No stream key on desktop | Not offered to every account | Use TikTok LIVE Studio instead |
| Stream still "live" after OBS stops | The session is still open on TikTok's side | End it on TikTok's LIVE setup page |

## Buying Followers or "LIVE Access" (Use With Caution)

If you're stuck below the threshold, you'll find services selling followers, or claiming to unlock LIVE for a fee.

### The Real Risks

- **Bought followers break TikTok's rules** and can get the account restricted, which can take LIVE away for longer.
- **Nobody outside TikTok can grant LIVE access.** Services that promise it are guessing or scamming.
- **Login requests.** Anything that asks for your TikTok password can take over the account.

### Safety Checklist

- Grow with your own videos, or join others' LIVEs as a guest.
- Check eligibility only in TikTok's LIVE Center.
- Never share your password or login code.

> **Bottom line:** The only reliable way to get LIVE is to meet TikTok's rules. Shortcuts usually push the button further away.

## What to Do Next: 3 Scenarios

- **You see the LIVE option.** Plan a title, start with something happening, and add a moderator for your first stream.
- **You don't, and you're under about 1,000 followers.** Post steadily, join other creators' LIVEs as a guest, and check the LIVE Center as you grow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many followers do you need to go live on TikTok?

About 1,000 for most accounts at the time of writing. TikTok says the threshold can vary by region, so some accounts qualify earlier.

### How old do you have to be to go live on TikTok?

18 or older to go live and to receive gifts, at the time of writing.

### Why can't I go live on TikTok?

Usually because the account is under the follower threshold, the account holder is under 18, or there's a recent Community Guidelines violation. Updating the app and checking the LIVE Center can also help.

### Can you go live on TikTok from a computer?

Yes, if your account is eligible: use TikTok LIVE Studio or a stream key with OBS or Streamlabs.

### Can I schedule a TikTok LIVE?

TikTok lets eligible creators create a LIVE Event so followers get a reminder. The stream itself still has to be started by you at that time.

### Can you stream a PS5 to TikTok?

Not directly. The PS5's broadcast menu doesn't include TikTok, so use a capture card with a computer running TikTok LIVE Studio or OBS.

### How do I end a TikTok LIVE?

On a phone, tap the X or power icon and confirm. In LIVE Studio, click End LIVE; in OBS or Streamlabs, click Stop Streaming and end the session on TikTok's side if it still shows as active.

### Can I go live with someone who has fewer followers?

Yes. A host can invite a guest to a multi-guest LIVE, and the guest doesn't need to meet the follower threshold.
