Your comment has 12 likes and you want to know who they are. To see who liked a comment on Instagram, open the comments, find the comment, and **press and hold the small heart next to it** (or tap the like count shown with it). A list of every account that liked it opens. A quick tap on the heart doesn't show the list. It just likes or unlikes the comment yourself.

![How to see who liked a comment on Instagram: press and hold the heart instead of tapping it](/images/blog/how-to-see-who-liked-a-comment-on-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

Instagram never explains this in the app, which is why so many people end up accidentally liking comments while looking for the list.

This guide covers the exact steps on iPhone and Android, what to do when the list won't open, who gets notified, and what stays hidden even with the list open.

## Why You Can't Find Who Liked Your Instagram Comment

Instagram used to show a "3 likes" label under each comment that you could tap. Newer versions moved the count next to a small heart on the right of the comment, and the heart does two jobs: a tap likes the comment, and a press-and-hold (or a tap on the count, depending on your version) opens the list.

![A quick tap on the heart likes the comment, while pressing and holding opens the list of who liked it](/images/blog/how-to-see-who-liked-a-comment-on-instagram-gesture-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

The feature never went away. It's just hidden behind a gesture. It also works on **any comment you can see**, not only yours: comments on your posts, your comments on other posts, and other people's comments on posts you can view.

> **Key point:** Comment likes tell you something post likes don't. Someone who likes your comment agreed with what *you* said, not just the original post. Those are often the people worth getting to know.

## How to See Who Liked a Comment on Instagram (Step-by-Step)

These steps work in the Instagram app on iPhone and Android. Menu labels and icons change between app versions, so look for the closest match if your screen differs.

![Four steps to see who liked an Instagram comment: open the comments, find the heart, press and hold, and browse the list](/images/blog/how-to-see-who-liked-a-comment-on-instagram-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open the Comments

Open the post or Reel and tap the speech-bubble icon, or **View all comments**. On Reels, comments open in a panel over the video.

### Step 2: Find the Heart and the Count

Each comment has a small heart on the right. If anyone has liked it, a number appears with the heart. No number means no likes yet, so there's no list to open.

### Step 3: Press and Hold, Don't Tap

Press and hold the heart for about a second. In some versions, tapping the number itself opens the list instead. A quick tap on the heart only likes or unlikes the comment.

> **Pro tip:** Liked it by accident? Tap the heart again to unlike it. The commenter may already have been notified, but the like disappears from the list.

### Step 4: Browse the List

A list of accounts that liked the comment opens. Scroll through it and tap any name to open that profile.

## Long-Press Not Working? 5 Fixes That Usually Do

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing happens when you press and hold | Update the Instagram app | The gesture depends on your app version |
| The comment gets liked instead | Hold for a full second, or tap the number instead of the heart | A short press counts as a tap |
| No list appears | Check that a like count is showing | A comment with no likes has no list |
| The app freezes or the list is empty | Close and reopen Instagram, then try again | Clears a temporary glitch |
| The count and the list don't match | Refresh the comments | Likes can arrive faster than the list updates, and some accounts are hidden from you (see below) |

## Can You See Comment Likes on Instagram Desktop?

Sometimes. On instagram.com, comments show a like count you can click on some layouts, which opens the same list. Desktop layouts change often, though, and the app is the reliable way to do it.

| | Instagram app | instagram.com |
|---|---|---|
| See who liked a comment | Yes: press and hold the heart, or tap the count | Sometimes: click the like count where shown |
| Like or unlike a comment | Tap the heart | Click the heart |
| Reliable across versions | Yes | Varies |

Skip browser extensions and "viewer" sites that claim to show more. They aren't official and often ask you to log in.

## Who Gets Notified When You Like a Comment (and Who Can See It)

Liking a comment isn't a secret, but it isn't announced to everyone either.

![Who is notified when you like an Instagram comment, and who can still see your like in the list](/images/blog/how-to-see-who-liked-a-comment-on-instagram-notified-1200x675.webp)

- **The person who wrote the comment** usually gets a notification that you liked it.
- **Your followers** aren't told. Comment likes don't appear in anyone's feed.
- **Anyone who can see the comment** can open its like list and find your name.
- **Opening a like list** sends no notification. Nobody knows you looked.

If you posted it, your own comment likes show up in your notifications as "username liked your comment", often grouped as "username and 4 others". Tap the notification to jump straight to the comment.

## What You Can't See, Even With the List Open

- **Accounts that blocked you, and accounts you blocked.** They're left out of the list for you.
- **Deactivated accounts.** Their likes can drop out while the account is hidden.
- **When someone liked it.** The list shows who, not when.
- **Who unliked it.** There's no record of removed likes.
- **Who read the comment.** Instagram only shows likes and replies, not views.
- **Comments that were deleted.** The likes go with them.

Some comments are also hidden by Instagram's filters or the post owner's settings. Look for a **View hidden comments** option at the bottom of the comments.

## Comment Insight Apps: Red Flags to Watch For (Use With Caution)

Some apps and extensions claim to show extra Instagram engagement data, such as who likes your comments across every post. Instagram doesn't offer that data to outside apps in a way that would make this possible for a personal account, so these tools usually rely on your login.

### The Real Risks

- **They ask for your Instagram password.** That gives them full control of your account.
- **They can get your account restricted.** Automated activity from unfamiliar servers can trigger security checks or action blocks. If it goes further, see our guide to [suspended Instagram accounts](/blog/suspended-instagram-account).
- **They promise data Instagram doesn't share**, which is a sign they're scraping or making it up.

### Before You Try One

- Never type your password into an app's own login form.
- Check recent reviews for reports of lockouts.
- Remove any connected apps you don't recognize in your Instagram security settings.

> **Bottom line:** The press-and-hold in the official app shows you everything there is to see. Nothing else is worth the risk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I see who liked someone else's comment on Instagram?

Yes, on any post you can see. Open the comments, find the comment, and press and hold the heart (or tap its like count) to open the list.

### Why does tapping the heart like the comment instead of showing the list?

Because a tap is the like button. Press and hold the heart for about a second, or tap the number instead of the heart if your version shows one.

### Does Instagram tell someone I looked at who liked their comment?

No. Opening a like list sends no notification. Only actions like liking, replying or mentioning someone notify them.

### Does the commenter know if I like their comment?

Usually, yes. Instagram typically notifies the person who wrote a comment when someone likes it.

### Can I hide that I liked a comment?

No. Anyone who can see the comment can open its like list. The only way to remove your name is to unlike the comment.

### Can I see who liked a comment on a private account?

Only if you follow that account and can see its posts. Comment like lists follow the same privacy as the post.

### How do I know if the creator liked my comment?

You'll usually get a notification, and on many posts the comment shows a small label or heart showing that the author liked it.

### Can I see who liked a comment after it's deleted?

No. When a comment is deleted, its likes go with it.

### Can I see when someone liked my comment?

Not exactly. The list shows who liked it, but not when. Your notifications show roughly when each like arrived.
