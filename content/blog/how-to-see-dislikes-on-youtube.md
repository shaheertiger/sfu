You want to know whether a video was badly received, but the dislike number under it is gone.

If it's your own video, you can see its dislikes in YouTube Studio: open **Analytics**, switch to **Advanced mode** and add the **Dislikes** metric, or check the likes-versus-dislikes figure in the **Content** list. If it's someone else's video, you can't see the exact dislike count. YouTube hid public dislike counts in November 2021, and only the creator can see the real number.

![How to see dislikes on YouTube: creators see exact counts in YouTube Studio, viewers only get estimates](/images/blog/how-to-see-dislikes-on-youtube-hero-1200x675.webp)

It's confusing because the dislike button is still there, and browser extensions put a number back under videos that looks official but isn't. This guide covers why YouTube hid the counts, how creators find the exact number, what extensions really show, and better ways for viewers to judge a video.

## Why YouTube Hid Dislike Counts in 2021

In November 2021, YouTube made dislike counts private on all videos after testing the change. The dislike button didn't go away: viewers can still dislike a video, and the creator still sees the total.

![Who can see YouTube dislikes: the creator sees the exact count, everyone else sees an estimate or nothing](/images/blog/how-to-see-dislikes-on-youtube-who-1200x675.webp)

| Who you are | Can you see dislikes? | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The video's creator | Yes, exact count | YouTube Studio (Analytics and Content list) |
| Channel manager or editor | Yes, if your role includes analytics | YouTube Studio for that channel |
| Any other viewer | No official count | Only the dislike button, which shows your own vote |
| Viewer with a dislike extension | An estimate | Shown by the extension, not by YouTube |

### What Most People Don't Know

YouTube's stated reason was to reduce "dislike attacks", where groups coordinate to pile dislikes onto a video, which it said hit smaller creators hardest. Shortly afterwards, the public dislike count was also removed from the data YouTube provides to developers. That's why no site or app can pull official dislike numbers any more.

> **Key point:** Disliking a video still does something. It tunes your own recommendations, and the creator still sees it as private feedback.

## How to See Dislikes on Your Own YouTube Videos (2 Methods)

Menu labels change between YouTube Studio versions, so look for similar wording if yours differs.

![Two ways for creators to see dislikes in YouTube Studio: the Content list and Advanced mode](/images/blog/how-to-see-dislikes-on-youtube-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Method 1: The Content List (Quick Ratio)

1. Go to studio.youtube.com and click **Content**.
2. Find the **Likes (vs. dislikes)** column. It shows the percentage of ratings that were likes, with the number of likes underneath.
3. Work out the balance from the percentage. For example, if a video has 90 likes at 90%, it has about 10 dislikes.

### Method 2: Analytics Advanced Mode (Exact Numbers)

1. In YouTube Studio, click **Analytics**.
2. Click **Advanced mode** in the top right.
3. Open the metric picker and add **Dislikes**, plus **Likes** and **Likes (vs. dislikes)** if you want them side by side.
4. Pick **Lifetime** as the date range, not the default. This is the step people miss: the default range covers only a recent period, so older dislikes may not show.
5. Filter by a single video, or group by **Content** to compare videos.

> **Pro tip:** Group by Content and sort by Dislikes to find the videos that got the most negative reactions, then read the comments on those first.

### In the YouTube Studio App

Open the **YouTube Studio** app, tap **Content**, tap a video, then open its analytics. The engagement section shows likes and the like-to-dislike ratio. The layout varies between app versions; for full numbers, use Studio on a computer.

### Shorts and Live Streams

The same steps work for Shorts and past live streams. In the Content list, switch to the **Shorts** or **Live** tab; in Advanced mode, filter by content type.

## What Creators Should Do With Dislike Data

Dislikes are most useful in context, alongside other numbers in YouTube Studio.

| Signal | What it might mean |
| --- | --- |
| High dislikes and short average view duration | The video didn't deliver what the title or thumbnail promised |
| High dislikes but strong retention | The topic is divisive; people watched but disagreed |
| A sudden spike in dislikes after publishing | The video was shared somewhere with a hostile audience |
| A low, steady dislike rate | Normal; every video gets some |

1. **Compare the video to your channel's usual like ratio** rather than looking at the raw number.
2. **Read the comments** posted around the same time as the dislikes.
3. **Check your packaging.** A mismatch between title and content is a common cause of dislikes.
4. **Look at audience retention** to find the moment viewers left.

## How Viewers Can Judge a Video Without Dislikes

If you used dislikes to decide whether a tutorial or review was worth your time, these signals still work:

- **The comments.** Look for specific feedback, like "step 4 didn't work for me", rather than general praise.
- **Pinned comments and corrections** from the creator.
- **The like count relative to views.** It's not a ratio with dislikes, but it shows whether people found the video worth rewarding.
- **Chapters and the description**, which show whether the video covers what you need before you watch.

## Dislike Extensions: What the Number Really Is (Use With Caution)

You may have seen browser extensions that add a dislike number back under YouTube videos. It's worth knowing where that number comes from.

![What YouTube dislike extensions show compared with the real count only creators can see](/images/blog/how-to-see-dislikes-on-youtube-caution-1200x675.webp)

### How the Estimates Work

Because YouTube no longer publishes dislike counts, these extensions build their own numbers. In general they combine:

- **Archived counts** collected before YouTube hid them, for older videos.
- **Votes from their own users**, scaled up to estimate the total.

The figure can be fairly close on popular videos with lots of extension users and far off on new, niche or small videos where few extension users have voted. YouTube doesn't confirm or support these numbers.

### The Real Risks

- **Permissions.** Browser extensions can read the pages you visit. Check what an extension asks for, who makes it and how it handles your data.
- **Accuracy.** Treat the number as a rough signal, not a fact. Don't make decisions, or accuse a creator of anything, based on it.
- **No official source.** No extension can show the real count; only the creator can, in YouTube Studio.

We don't recommend any particular extension.

> **Bottom line:** An extension's dislike count is an estimate from other users. The real number lives only in the creator's YouTube Studio.

## Fewer Dislikes Start With the Title: Plan Uploads Ahead

The most common fixable cause of dislikes is a title or thumbnail that promises something the video doesn't deliver. That usually happens when a video is titled in a rush at upload time.

Take Leah, who posts two cooking videos a week on YouTube and clips from each to Instagram and TikTok. Her dislikes spiked on a video titled "The Only Bread Recipe You'll Ever Need", which turned out to be a quick flatbread. The comments said the same thing the dislikes did.

Now Leah uploads both videos on Sunday in Schedule For You, writes the title (up to 100 characters) and description (up to 5,000) while she still remembers exactly what's in each video, reads them back on Monday, and schedules them as public, unlisted or private. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I see who disliked my YouTube video?

No. YouTube shows creators how many dislikes a video has, but not who left them. Likes are anonymous in the same way.

### Do dislikes hurt a YouTube video?

YouTube doesn't publish exactly how ratings affect recommendations. Dislikes are one of many signals, and YouTube puts more emphasis on what viewers watch and how satisfied they are. A video can perform well with a fair number of dislikes if people keep watching.

### Can I hide the like count on my videos?

Yes. When uploading or editing a video in YouTube Studio, you can untick the option that shows viewers how many people liked it. Dislike counts are already hidden from the public.

### Are dislike extensions accurate?

They're estimates based on archived data and on votes from their own users, so accuracy varies, especially on newer or smaller videos. Only the creator can see the real number.

### Can I see dislikes on YouTube Shorts?

On your own Shorts, yes, in YouTube Studio. On other people's Shorts, no; the count is private just as it is for regular videos.

### When did YouTube remove dislikes?

YouTube made dislike counts private in November 2021. The dislike button still works.

### Does disliking a video do anything?

Yes. It helps tune your own recommendations, and the creator sees the total in YouTube Studio.

### Can a channel manager see dislikes?

Yes, if their role on the channel includes access to analytics in YouTube Studio.
