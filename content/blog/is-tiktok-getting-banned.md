You've seen another video saying TikTok is about to disappear, and you're wondering whether to start saving your drafts.

No, TikTok isn't getting banned in the US. The 2024 law that could have banned it took effect in January 2025 but was never enforced, and on **22 January 2026** TikTok's US business was handed to **TikTok USDS Joint Venture**, a new company majority-owned by non-Chinese investors. That sale is what the law asked for, so there's no ban date coming up at the time of writing.

![Is TikTok getting banned? No: the US business moved to a joint venture in January 2026, which the law required](/images/blog/is-tiktok-getting-banned-hero-1200x675.webp)

The question keeps coming back because the story ran for two years, TikTok really did go dark for about a day in January 2025, and old articles about the deadlines still circulate. This guide covers why TikTok isn't banned, the full timeline, the one open lawsuit, the countries that do ban it, and what creators should do anyway.

## Why TikTok Isn't Banned in the US (the Law Asked for a Sale)

The law didn't ban TikTok outright. It banned app stores and hosting companies from supporting TikTok in the US unless ByteDance sold control of the US business.

![Why TikTok isn't banned: the law required a sale of the US business, and the sale closed in January 2026](/images/blog/is-tiktok-getting-banned-why-1200x675.webp)

The joint venture is led by Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX, with ByteDance keeping 19.9%, which the government accepted as meeting that requirement. Our guide to [who owns TikTok](/blog/who-owns-tiktok) breaks down the new ownership.

### What Most People Don't Know

A "ban" was always the fallback, not the goal. US lawmakers worried that the Chinese government could access American users' data or influence what TikTok recommends through ByteDance. The law required a sale to non-Chinese owners to address that. Once the sale closed, the reason for a ban fell away.

> **Key point:** Rumors of a new ban date come back every few months, often from old articles about the 2025 deadlines. If you see one, check whether it's dated before January 2026.

## Is TikTok Getting Banned Again? 3 Things to Watch

### 1. The March 2026 Lawsuit

The one open question is a lawsuit filed in March 2026 by two shareholders of rival tech companies, arguing that ByteDance still controls too much of TikTok. It asks for the deal to be renegotiated, not for TikTok to be banned, and was still pending at the time of writing.

### 2. Government-Device Bans

Individual states and government agencies ban TikTok on government-issued phones. That doesn't affect personal use.

### 3. Old Headlines Recycled as New

Most "TikTok ban next month" posts are recycled from 2024 and 2025. Check the date before you share or act on one.

> **Pro tip:** If you see a new ban claim, check two things: the date of the article, and whether it names a specific court ruling or law after January 2026. If it doesn't, it's almost certainly old news.

## VPNs and Unofficial App Downloads (Use With Caution)

Every ban scare brings advice to "just use a VPN" or download TikTok from a site other than your app store.

### The Real Risks

- **Legal risk.** In countries where TikTok is blocked, getting around the block may break local law.
- **Malware.** App files from outside official app stores can be modified to steal logins or data.
- **Free VPNs** may log or sell your browsing data.
- **Account issues.** Logging in from unexpected locations can trigger security checks.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Download TikTok only from your phone's official app store.
- Check local law before using a VPN to reach a blocked service.
- Turn on two-step verification on your TikTok account.

> **Bottom line:** In the US, you don't need a workaround: TikTok works normally at the time of writing.
