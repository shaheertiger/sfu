You've got a product people stop scrolling for, and you've seen other sellers turn a single video into a pile of orders. To sell on TikTok Shop, sign up at **TikTok Shop Seller Center**, verify your identity or business, connect a bank account, list your products and link a TikTok account to sell from.

![How to sell on TikTok Shop: set up in Seller Center, then sell through videos, LIVE and affiliate creators](/images/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop-hero-1200x675.webp)

You then sell three ways: **product links in your videos**, **selling on LIVE**, and **paying other creators an affiliate commission** to promote your products. TikTok takes a referral fee on each order, around 6% in most US categories at the time of writing, with reports of an increase in 2026, and there's no monthly subscription.

The setup is the easy part. What decides whether you make money is understanding what TikTok and creators take per order, and posting often enough to be seen. This guide covers all of it.

## Why TikTok Shop Sells Differently From a Website Store

![A TikTok Shop compared with a regular online store: buyers discover products in videos and LIVE instead of searching for them](/images/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop-why-1200x675.webp)

On a website, buyers arrive already looking for something. On TikTok, most buyers didn't plan to shop: they see the product in a video or a LIVE stream and buy without leaving the app. That changes what works.

| | Website store | TikTok Shop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How buyers find you | Search, ads, links | Videos, LIVE, the Shop tab and creators |
| What sells | Product pages | Showing the product in use |
| Who promotes | You | You, plus affiliate creators |
| Monthly fee | Depends on your platform | None; a referral fee per order |

### What Most People Don't Know

You don't need followers to open a seller account. There's no follower minimum for sellers. The follower minimum people talk about applies to creators who want to earn affiliate commissions promoting other shops' products, which has been 5,000 followers in the US at the time of writing.

> **Key point:** On TikTok Shop, content is the storefront. A seller who posts product videos consistently gets far more chances at the For You feed than one with a perfect listing and no videos.

## What You Need to Sign Up for TikTok Shop (US Checklist)

- **Age 18 or over** and US residency for an individual seller, or a registered business (with an EIN) for a company account.
- **ID:** a driver's license, passport or state ID.
- **Tax details:** your SSN or ITIN for individuals, EIN for businesses.
- **Proof of address** and a **US bank account** in the same name for payouts.
- **Products you're allowed to sell.** Some categories (supplements, cosmetics, electronics) need extra approval; some are banned.

Requirements differ in the UK, EU and Southeast Asia, so start from the Seller Center for your country.

## How to Set Up TikTok Shop Step by Step (7 Steps)

![Steps to set up TikTok Shop: register and verify in Seller Center, add shipping and a bank account, then list products and link your TikTok account](/images/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop-setup-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels in Seller Center change from time to time, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Register in Seller Center

Go to TikTok Shop Seller Center, sign up with a phone number or email, and choose individual or business.

### Step 2: Verify Your Identity or Business

Upload your ID or business documents. Approval usually takes from a day to a few business days. Missing or blurry documents are the most common reason for delays, which is the step people rush.

### Step 3: Add a Warehouse Address and Shipping

In the US, TikTok's own logistics options now handle most shipping. Follow the shipping setup in Seller Center.

### Step 4: Connect Your Bank Account

Use an account in the same name as your seller account, for payouts.

### Step 5: List Products

Use clear photos on a white or simple background, a short title with the key search words, size and material details, and honest shipping times.

### Step 6: Link Your TikTok Account

Connect the account you'll post from as an official shop account, so product links appear in your videos and LIVE.

### Step 7: Set Up Affiliate Commissions (Optional but Powerful)

Choose the commission rate creators earn per sale, so other people can promote your products.

> **Pro tip:** Write your product titles the way a buyer would search: "linen tote bag with zip pocket" beats a brand name nobody knows yet.

### How Much Does TikTok Take If You Sell on LIVE?

The same referral fee applies whether the sale happens from a video, the Shop tab or a LIVE stream; there's no separate LIVE cut. If another creator sells your product on **their** LIVE, you also pay them your affiliate commission. If you're the creator selling someone else's product, you earn that commission and don't pay the referral fee. ## What Is GMV on TikTok? (And Why It Isn't Profit)

**GMV** means **gross merchandise value**: the total value of orders placed through your shop, video or LIVE before fees, refunds and returns. A LIVE that "did $5,000 GMV" sold $5,000 of products at checkout, not $5,000 of profit.

TikTok uses GMV in Seller Center dashboards, in affiliate programs (creators are ranked by the GMV they drive) and in its ad product **GMV Max**, which optimizes ads for total sales value. To see what you actually keep, subtract the referral fee, commissions, shipping, returns and product cost.

- **Proof.** Showing the product used, unboxed or compared is more convincing than photos.
- **Reach.** Popular streams are recommended to people who don't follow you.

## Bought Reviews and Unvetted Suppliers (Use With Caution)

![Checklist for building trust on TikTok Shop: test products and collect real reviews, and avoid bought reviews and untested suppliers](/images/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop-caution-1200x675.webp)

New shops are tempted by two shortcuts: paying for reviews, and listing products from suppliers they've never tested.

### The Real Risks

- **Fake reviews break the rules.** Platforms remove them and can penalize the shop. Buyers also spot them.
- **Untested products lead to returns.** Returns eat into GMV and hurt your shop's standing with buyers.
- **Shipping promises you can't keep** lead to cancellations and complaints.
- **Restricted categories** such as supplements, cosmetics and electronics need approval; listing without it can get products removed.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Order and test a sample of every product before you list it.
- Collect reviews from real buyers, starting with free samples to creators.
- Quote honest shipping times.
- Check category rules in Seller Center before you list.

> **Bottom line:** A shop with ten honest reviews outsells one with none, and keeps selling. Shortcuts that fake trust tend to cost more than they bring in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you need followers to sell on TikTok Shop?

No. A seller account has no follower minimum. Creators who want affiliate commissions for promoting other shops' products do need a minimum, which has been 5,000 followers in the US at the time of writing; the app shows the current requirement.

### Is it free to sell on TikTok Shop?

There's no listing or monthly fee. TikTok takes a referral fee per order, plus whatever affiliate commissions and shipping you pay.

### How much does TikTok Shop take per sale?

About 6% of the order in most US categories at the time of writing, with reports of an increase in 2026. Check your current rate in Seller Center.

### How long does TikTok Shop approval take?

Usually one to a few business days after you submit documents. Missing or blurry documents are the most common reason for delays.

### What does GMV mean on TikTok?

Gross merchandise value: the total value of products sold before fees, returns and costs.

### Does TikTok take a bigger cut on LIVE sales?

No. The same referral fee applies to sales from a video, the Shop tab or a LIVE. If another creator sells your product on their LIVE, you also pay their affiliate commission.

### Can I sell on TikTok Shop from outside the US?

To sell to US buyers you generally need a US entity or residency; otherwise use TikTok Shop's cross-border program or the Seller Center for your own country.

### Can I schedule TikTok Shop videos?

You can schedule the videos in TikTok Studio up to 10 days ahead, or with a scheduler. Add Shop product links in TikTok.
