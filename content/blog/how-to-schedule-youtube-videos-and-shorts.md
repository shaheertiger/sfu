You've finished the edit and want it live on Thursday at 5 p.m., not right now. To schedule a video on YouTube, upload it in YouTube Studio, go to the **Visibility** step, choose **Schedule**, and set the date and time. The video stays private until then and goes public on its own. Shorts work the same way.

![How to schedule a video on YouTube: upload, add details, choose Schedule under Visibility, pick a date and time](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-youtube-videos-and-shorts-hero-1200x675.webp)

The part that catches people out is the pinned comment. You can't pin one before the video is public, because comments don't exist until then. There are two simple workarounds.

This guide covers scheduling on desktop and mobile, how Shorts are classified, the pinned-comment workaround, how to change a scheduled video, and how to schedule a week of Shorts (or the same clip on TikTok and Instagram) without uploading each one by hand.

## Why Schedule YouTube Videos at All?

Scheduling separates making a video from publishing it. You upload when the edit is done and let YouTube publish when your audience is around, whether or not you're at your desk.

![Publishing a YouTube video live compared with scheduling it](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-youtube-videos-and-shorts-why-1200x675.webp)

It also gives you a buffer. A video uploaded on Monday for a Thursday release has three days of processing time, so higher-quality versions are ready by the time it goes public, and you have time to catch a typo in the title.

### What Most People Don't Know

YouTube decides what counts as a Short. At the time of writing, a vertical or square video of 3 minutes or less is treated as a Short, whether you schedule it or not. Adding `#Shorts` to the title or description is optional; YouTube no longer requires it.

> **Key point:** Scheduling on YouTube is just a visibility setting. The video sits as private until the time you choose, then goes public on its own.

## How to Schedule a Video in YouTube Studio in 4 Steps (Desktop)

Studio's labels change from time to time, but the flow has stayed the same for years.

![Four steps to schedule a video in YouTube Studio on desktop](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-youtube-videos-and-shorts-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Upload the File

Go to studio.youtube.com and click **Create → Upload videos**, then choose the file.

### Step 2: Fill In the Details

Add the title (up to 100 characters), description (up to 5,000), thumbnail, playlist and audience settings. The free [YouTube title checker](/tools/youtube-title-checker) helps you fit the title.

### Step 3: Click Through Video Elements and Checks

Add end screens and cards if you use them, and wait for the copyright and policy checks.

### Step 4: Choose Schedule, Not Public

This is the step people miss: on the **Visibility** screen, select **Schedule** instead of Public, pick the date and time, and click **Schedule**. If you click Publish with Public selected, it goes live immediately.

> **Pro tip:** Check the time zone shown next to the date picker. If your audience is in a different time zone from you, convert the time first, or your "5 p.m." release lands at the wrong hour for them.

A scheduled video shows in **Content** with a clock icon and its date. Click it to change the time or make it public now. Premieres are an option on the same screen if you want a countdown and live chat.

## How to Schedule a Short (Same Steps, Different Format)

Shorts are scheduled exactly like videos. Upload a vertical or square video of 3 minutes or less (at the time of writing), and YouTube classifies it as a Short. For tips on the format itself, see our guide to [posting YouTube Shorts](/blog/post-youtube-shorts).

| Upload | YouTube treats it as | How to schedule |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical or square, 3 minutes or less | A Short | Visibility → Schedule |
| Horizontal, any length | A regular video | Visibility → Schedule |
| Vertical, over 3 minutes | A regular video | Visibility → Schedule |

## How to Schedule From the YouTube Mobile App

1. Tap **+ → Upload a video** (or **Create a Short**).
2. Add details, then under **Visibility** choose **Schedule** and set the time.

The mobile app's options are fewer than Studio's. Thumbnails and end screens are easier on desktop, so many creators upload from the phone and finish the details in Studio.

## What Time Should You Schedule a YouTube Video For?

Viewing often increases in the evenings and on weekends, when people have more free time. Our own guide suggests publishing two to three hours before your audience's peak, so the video has time to reach subscribers and recommendations before the busiest viewing hours. A regular release day and time also helps subscribers know when to expect new videos.

Your own audience is the best guide: YouTube Studio's analytics show when your viewers are on YouTube. Our guide to the [best time to post on YouTube](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube) covers how to read it.

## The Pinned Comment Catch: 2 Workarounds

You can't pin a comment before the video is public, because comments don't exist yet.

![Two workarounds for pinning a comment on a scheduled YouTube video](/images/blog/how-to-schedule-youtube-videos-and-shorts-pinned-1200x675.webp)

| Workaround | How | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Post and pin at publish time | Set a reminder for the publish time, then post and pin the comment right after | Questions that start discussion, sponsor notes |
| Use the first line of the description | Put the link or note there instead | Links and key info that should be visible immediately |

The first line of the description shows under the video straight away, so for links it's often the better choice anyway.

## Scheduled Video Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| It went public immediately | Check you picked **Schedule**, not Public | Public publishes on save |
| It went live at the wrong hour | Check the time zone next to the date picker | The time is in the shown zone, not your viewers' |
| A vertical video wasn't treated as a Short | Check it's 3 minutes or less and vertical or square | YouTube decides by format |
| You need to change the date | Open it in **Content** and edit the schedule | Scheduled videos stay editable until they go live |
| Only lower quality is available at first | Upload earlier | Higher-quality versions take longer to process |

## What to Do Next

1. **Pick a release time** your audience is awake for, and note it in their time zone.
2. **Upload two or three days early** so processing finishes before release.
3. **Schedule, don't publish:** choose Schedule on the Visibility screen.
4. **Put links in the first line** of the description instead of relying on a pinned comment.
5. **Batch your Shorts** if you post daily, and reuse the same clips on TikTok and Instagram with captions written for each.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you schedule YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Upload the vertical video in YouTube Studio or the app and set Visibility to Schedule. YouTube classes it as a Short by its format.

### Can you schedule a YouTube video from your phone?

Yes. The YouTube app's upload flow has a Schedule option under Visibility, though thumbnails and end screens are easier in Studio on desktop.

### How far in advance can you schedule YouTube videos?

YouTube doesn't state a maximum, and videos have been scheduled a year or more ahead.

### Can you pin a comment when scheduling a video?

No. Pin it right after the video goes public, or put the note in the first line of the description.

### Can I change the time of a scheduled YouTube video?

Yes. Open the video in **Content** in YouTube Studio and edit the schedule, or make it public right away.

### Do I need #Shorts in the title?

No. YouTube classifies a Short by its format, vertical or square and 3 minutes or less at the time of writing. The hashtag is optional.

### What's the difference between scheduling and a Premiere?

A scheduled video simply goes public at the set time. A Premiere adds a countdown and live chat so viewers can watch it together the first time.

### Can I schedule the same video to YouTube and TikTok?

Not from YouTube Studio. A scheduler like Schedule For You can post one video to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with a separate caption for each.
