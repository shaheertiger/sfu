Your boss, your dentist and your ex keep showing up in TikTok's suggested accounts, and you'd like that to stop. To turn off contacts on TikTok, go to **Profile > menu (three lines) > Settings and privacy > Privacy > Sync contacts and Facebook friends**, switch off **Sync contacts**, then tap **Remove previously synced contacts**. Then go to **Privacy > Suggest your account to others** and switch off **Contacts**.

![How to turn off contacts on TikTok: stop syncing your contacts, then stop suggesting your account to them](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-contacts-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

Both switches matter. The first stops TikTok reading your contacts. The second stops your account being shown to people who have your number, which is the part most people actually care about.

This guide covers what each setting does, the step-by-step for both, the phone-level permission, why suggestions can linger, and how to quiet notifications and see less AI-generated content while you're in Settings.

## Why Turning Off Sync Isn't Enough (What Each Setting Does)

TikTok treats your contacts in two directions. **Sync contacts** controls whether TikTok reads your phone book to suggest people to you. **Suggest your account to others** controls whether TikTok shows your account to people who have you in their phone book. Turning off one doesn't turn off the other.

![What syncing contacts does on TikTok compared with suggesting your account to others](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-contacts-on-tiktok-two-ways-1200x675.webp)

| Setting | What it stops |
|---|---|
| Sync contacts | TikTok reading your phone contacts |
| Remove previously synced contacts | Contacts TikTok already stored |
| Suggest your account to others > Contacts | Your account appearing to people with your number |
| Private account | Non-followers seeing your videos |

### What Most People Don't Know

Other people can still find you if *they* synced a contact list that includes your number. Your own settings can't stop someone else's phone book from matching you. TikTok also says it may still suggest accounts based on contacts that were synced in the past. Turning everything off reduces suggestions; it doesn't make you invisible.

> **Key point:** Turn off syncing, remove what's already stored, and turn off suggestions. Only all three together keep your contacts out of it.

## How to Turn Off Contact Syncing on TikTok in 5 Steps

Labels change between app versions and regions. If a toggle isn't where we say, use the search bar at the top of **Settings and privacy**.

![Five steps to turn off contact syncing on TikTok and remove stored contacts](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-contacts-on-tiktok-sync-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile**.

### Step 2: Open Settings and Privacy

Tap the **menu** icon in the top right, then **Settings and privacy**.

### Step 3: Go to Privacy

Tap **Privacy**, then **Sync contacts and Facebook friends**.

### Step 4: Turn Off Sync Contacts

Switch off **Sync contacts**.

### Step 5: Remove Previously Synced Contacts

This is the step people skip. Turning off sync stops new uploads, but TikTok keeps what it already has until you tap **Remove previously synced contacts** and confirm.

> **Pro tip:** Revoke the permission on your phone too, so TikTok can't ask again after an update. It takes 20 seconds and works even if TikTok moves its own menus.

### Revoke the Permission at the Phone Level

- **iPhone:** Settings > Apps > TikTok > Contacts, set to None (on older iOS versions, Settings > TikTok > Contacts).
- **Android:** Settings > Apps > TikTok > Permissions > Contacts > Don't allow.

## How to Stop TikTok Suggesting Your Account to People You Know

![Four toggles under Suggest your account to others that control who TikTok shows your account to](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-contacts-on-tiktok-suggest-1200x675.webp)

1. Go to **Settings and privacy > Privacy**.
2. Tap **Suggest your account to others**.
3. Switch off the sources you don't want:

| Toggle | Who stops seeing you as a suggestion |
|---|---|
| Contacts | People who have your number |
| Facebook friends | People you're connected to on Facebook |
| People with mutual connections | Friends of your followers and people you follow |
| People who open or send links to you | People you've exchanged TikTok links with |

For the strongest privacy, switch your account to **private** under **Privacy > Private account**. Then only followers you approve can see your videos, whoever finds your profile.

## Contacts Still Showing Up? 4 Reasons and Fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Friends still appear in your suggestions | TikTok also uses mutual connections, Facebook friends and links | Turn off each toggle under Suggest your account to others |
| People you know still find you | They synced a contact list that includes your number | Go private if that matters |
| Suggestions linger after you changed settings | Past matches may stay for a while | Give it time, and make sure stored contacts are removed |
| TikTok asks for contacts again | The phone permission is still on | Revoke it in your phone's settings |

## How to Turn Off TikTok Notifications (While You're in Settings)

1. Go to **Settings and privacy > Notifications**.
2. Turn off the categories you don't want: likes, comments, new followers, mentions, direct messages, videos from accounts you follow, LIVE, and suggestions.
3. To silence TikTok completely, turn off notifications for the app in your phone settings (iPhone: Settings > Notifications > TikTok; Android: Settings > Apps > TikTok > Notifications).

If you run a brand or creator account, keep comments and mentions on and turn off the rest. Those are the notifications that need a reply.

## How to See Less AI-Generated Content on TikTok

TikTok added an **AI-generated content** slider to its topic controls. At the time of writing it's at **Settings and privacy > Content preferences > Manage topics** (on some versions under **Content and display** first). Move the slider toward less and save.

![What the TikTok AI-generated content slider reduces and what it can't remove](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-contacts-on-tiktok-ai-1200x675.webp)

Two limits: it reduces AI videos rather than removing them, and it only works on videos labeled as AI-generated. TikTok asks creators to label realistic AI content and reads Content Credentials (C2PA) data to label some videos automatically.

You can't switch off TikTok's own AI features, such as AI-made effects or search summaries, with one toggle. You can simply not use them, and you can mute keywords you're tired of with **Content preferences > Filter keywords**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TikTok tell people when I sync contacts?

No message is sent to your contacts. But if you leave suggestions on, people in your contacts may see your account under "Suggested accounts".

### Will turning off contacts remove the people I already follow?

No. Follows stay as they are; turning off contacts only stops TikTok using your address book to make new suggestions.

### Why does TikTok still suggest my friends after I turned off contacts?

TikTok also uses mutual connections, Facebook friends, links you open or send, and contacts other people synced. Turn off each toggle under Suggest your account to others, and note that past matches may linger.

### Can I stop people who have my number from finding me?

Not completely. If they synced a contact list that includes your number, TikTok can still match you. Turning off Contacts under Suggest your account to others reduces it, and a private account limits what they can see.

### Can I turn off the For You page?

No. You can switch to the Following feed, refresh your For You feed in Content preferences, and filter keywords, but there's no setting that removes For You.

### Does making my account private stop contact suggestions?

Not by itself. A private account controls who sees your videos; the Suggest your account to others toggles control who sees your account as a suggestion. Use both for the most privacy.

### Can I turn contact syncing back on later?

Yes. Go back to **Privacy > Sync contacts and Facebook friends** and switch Sync contacts on again. TikTok will ask for permission to read your contacts, and your phone may ask too if you revoked it there.

### Can I turn off AI-generated videos on TikTok completely?

No. The AI-generated content slider reduces labelled AI videos rather than removing them, and it can't catch videos that aren't labelled.
