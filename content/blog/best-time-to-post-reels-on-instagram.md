The best time to post Reels on Instagram is **just before your followers' most active hours**, which for most accounts means **weekday mornings (7 to 9 AM), lunchtime (12 to 1 PM) and evenings (7 to 9 PM)** in your audience's time zone, with **late mornings** at weekends. Reels keep being recommended for days, so a strong first hour sets up a long tail.

Here's the pattern by day, why Reels differ from feed posts, and how to find your own time in Insights.

## Best time to post Reels by day

| Day | Good windows (audience's local time) |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | 7 to 9 AM, 7 to 8 PM |
| Tuesday | 8 to 10 AM, 12 to 1 PM |
| Wednesday | 8 to 10 AM, 7 to 9 PM |
| Thursday | 9 to 11 AM, 7 to 9 PM |
| Friday | 9 AM to 12 PM |
| Saturday | 10 AM to 12 PM, 6 to 8 PM |
| Sunday | 10 AM to 1 PM |

Midweek evenings are the strongest for most accounts. These windows come from aggregate studies of Reels engagement and from Instagram's own activity charts; your account will differ, so treat them as a starting grid.

## Why Reels timing is different from feed posts

A feed photo is mostly shown to your followers in the hours after you post. A Reel is shown to non-followers through the Reels tab and Explore for days, and Instagram decides how far to push it based on how the first viewers respond: watch time, replays, shares and saves. Posting when your followers are online gives the Reel a strong first audience, which is what earns the wider push.

That also means a Reel posted at a dead hour isn't ruined; it just starts slower.

## Find your own best time in Insights

1. On your professional account, open **Insights** (or **Professional dashboard**).
2. Tap **Total followers**.
3. Scroll to **Most active times** and switch between hours and days.

Post 30 to 60 minutes before the busiest block. If you manage several accounts, check each one; a bakery's followers and a B2B consultant's followers keep different hours. Our general guide to the [best time to post on Instagram](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram) covers feed posts and Stories.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best time to post Reels on Instagram?
Just before your followers' most active hours. For most accounts that's weekday mornings and evenings and late mornings at weekends, but Insights shows your own peak.

### Does the best time to post Reels change in 2026?
The pattern has been stable for a few years: mornings, lunch and evenings on weekdays. What changes is your audience, so recheck Insights every few months.

### How often should I post Reels?
Three to five a week is a common target for growth. A steady rhythm matters more than a daily grind.

### Does posting a Reel at the wrong time hurt it permanently?
No. Reels are recommended for days. A bad hour means a slower start, not a lost post.
