You've got a vertical clip ready and you want it in the Shorts feed, not buried as a regular video on your channel.

To post a video to YouTube as a Short, upload a vertical or square video that's 3 minutes or shorter, add a title, choose public, unlisted or private, and publish or schedule it. There's no special "Shorts" switch: YouTube treats the upload as a Short based on its shape and length, at the time of writing.

![How to post a video to YouTube as a Short: upload a vertical or square video of up to 3 minutes](/images/blog/post-youtube-shorts-hero-1200x675.webp)

It's confusing because the YouTube app has a Shorts camera, YouTube Studio has a normal upload button, and many people assume they need the #Shorts hashtag. This guide explains how YouTube classifies Shorts, how to post one from the app or YouTube Studio, how to schedule it alongside Reels and TikTok, and what to check when a video doesn't show up as a Short.

## What Makes a Video a YouTube Short (Shape and Length, Not a Hashtag)

YouTube doesn't have a separate upload type for Shorts. It looks at the video and treats it as a Short when it meets two conditions, at the time of writing.

![What makes a YouTube Short: vertical or square, up to 3 minutes, compared with a regular video](/images/blog/post-youtube-shorts-rules-1200x675.webp)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Shape | Vertical (taller than it is wide) or square |
| Length | Up to 3 minutes |
| Recommended size | 1080×1920 (9:16) |
| Title | Up to 100 characters |
| Description | Up to 5,000 characters |
| Hashtag #Shorts | Optional |

A horizontal video stays a regular video regardless of length, and a vertical video longer than 3 minutes is treated as a regular long-form upload. YouTube has changed these rules before, so check [YouTube's help center](https://support.google.com/youtube) if you're working near the limits.

### What Most People Don't Know

You don't need #Shorts in the title. YouTube classifies Shorts by the video's shape and length, not by the hashtag. Some creators still add it as a label for viewers, which does no harm, but it uses up characters in your title.

> **Key point:** The export decides whether it's a Short. A vertical clip inside a 16:9 frame with black bars is a horizontal video to YouTube.

## How to Post a Short From the YouTube App or YouTube Studio (Free Method)

Menu labels change between versions, so look for similar wording if yours differs.

![Steps to post a YouTube Short: export vertical, upload, add a title, choose privacy and publish or schedule](/images/blog/post-youtube-shorts-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Export at 9:16, Not Inside a 16:9 Frame

Export your clip at 1080×1920 if you can, and keep it to 3 minutes or less. This is the step people get wrong: some editors place a vertical clip inside a horizontal frame with black bars, and YouTube then sees a horizontal video. See [TikTok video size and ratio](/blog/tiktok-video-size-ratio) for export settings that work everywhere.

### Step 2: Upload the Video

In the YouTube app, tap **Create** (the plus) and choose to upload a Short, or record one in the Shorts camera. On a computer, open YouTube Studio and click **Create → Upload videos**.

### Step 3: Write a Title That Says What Happens

Keep it under 100 characters, and put the main topic in the first few words. Shorts are watched quickly, so a clear title beats a clever one.

### Step 4: Choose Privacy and Audience

Pick public, unlisted or private, and set whether the video is made for kids.

### Step 5: Publish or Schedule

Publish now, or schedule it. In YouTube Studio, choose **Schedule** on the visibility step and pick a date and time.

> **Pro tip:** Run the title through our free [YouTube title checker](/tools/youtube-title-checker), which checks length, keyword position, numbers and punctuation.

After it's published, YouTube processes the video. It can take a little while to appear in the Shorts feed, and YouTube's own systems decide where it's shown.

## How to Write a Title That Works for Shorts

- Put the main topic in the first few words.
- Keep it well under 100 characters so it isn't cut off on small screens.
- Avoid ALL CAPS and strings of punctuation.
- Use a number when it fits ("3 ways to…").

## Posted as a Regular Video, Not a Short? 4 Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Video is horizontal | Export vertical (9:16) or square | YouTube needs taller-than-wide or square |
| Vertical clip shows black bars | Export at 9:16, not inside a 16:9 frame | The frame, not the clip, sets the shape |
| Longer than 3 minutes | Trim to 3 minutes or less | Longer vertical videos count as long-form |
| Not in the Shorts feed yet | Wait for processing | YouTube decides where and when it's shown |

## Watermark Removers and Reuploaded Clips (Use With Caution)

Many Shorts start life as TikToks, and it's tempting to grab a "watermark remover" site or reupload clips other people made.

![Clean exports from your own editor compared with risky watermark removers and reuploaded clips](/images/blog/post-youtube-shorts-caution-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Copyright.** Reuploading someone else's clip can lead to a copyright claim or strike on your channel.
- **Low-quality files.** Downloaded and re-encoded clips often look worse than your original export.
- **Shady sites.** Some downloader and remover sites push ads, extensions or downloads you didn't ask for.

### A Safer Checklist

- Export a clean version from your own editor, without another app's watermark or end screen.
- Only post clips you made or have the rights to.
- Keep your original files so you never need to download your own video back.

> **Bottom line:** The cleanest Short is the one you export yourself, once, at 9:16.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a Shorts toggle when uploading?

No. YouTube decides whether an upload is a Short based on its shape and length. A vertical or square video of up to 3 minutes is treated as a Short at the time of writing.

### How do I post a video to YouTube as a Short instead of a regular video?

Export it vertical (ideally 9:16) or square, keep it to 3 minutes or less at the time of writing, and upload it as usual. A horizontal video, or a vertical clip inside a 16:9 frame with black bars, stays a regular video.

### Do I need to add #Shorts to the title?

No, it's optional. YouTube classifies Shorts by the video's format, not the hashtag.

### How long can a YouTube Short be?

At the time of writing, up to 3 minutes. Anything longer is treated as a regular video, even if it's vertical.

### Can I schedule a Short as unlisted or private?

Yes. YouTube Studio and Schedule For You both let you choose public, unlisted or private.

### Can I post the same video to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok at once?

Yes, with a scheduler. Select all three accounts, upload the video once, tailor each caption and schedule.

### Why did my Short get a strange title?

If the title field is empty, the first line of your caption becomes the title in Schedule For You. Fill in the title field, or rewrite the first line.

### How long until my Short appears in the Shorts feed?

It can take a little while after processing, and YouTube's systems decide where it's shown.
