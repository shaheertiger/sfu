Your For You feed won't load, or a video has been "uploading" for ten minutes. To check if TikTok is down right now, look at an outage tracker such as **Downdetector** or **IsDown**: a sudden spike in reports means the problem is on TikTok's side, and the only fix is to wait.

![Is TikTok down? Check an outage tracker first, then fix your app, phone or connection](/images/blog/is-tiktok-down-hero-1200x675.webp)

TikTok has no official status page, which is why it's hard to tell an outage from a problem on your phone. If outage reports are quiet, the problem is almost certainly your app, phone or connection, and the fixes below usually sort it out in a few minutes.

This guide covers five checks to tell whether it's TikTok or you, seven fixes in order, and what's behind specific problems like search, comments and auto scroll.

## Is TikTok Down, or Is It Just You? 5 Quick Checks

![Signs TikTok is down for everyone compared with signs the problem is on your phone](/images/blog/is-tiktok-down-everyone-vs-you-1200x675.webp)

| Check | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Downdetector, IsDown or StatusGator | A spike in reports means a wide outage |
| Search "TikTok down" on X, Threads or Bluesky | Lots of fresh posts mean it's not just you |
| Open tiktok.com in a browser | If the website works but the app doesn't, it's your app |
| Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data | If one works, it's your network |
| Ask someone nearby to open TikTok | If theirs works, it's your account or device |

### What Most People Don't Know

Outages rarely take down all of TikTok at once. They tend to hit one feature first: the For You feed won't load, uploads fail, comments won't open or LIVE drops. That's normal for a partial outage, and it usually clears within an hour or two. So "everything else works" doesn't rule out an outage.

> **Key point:** No official status page means outage trackers and other users are your best signal. Check them before you reinstall anything.

## Why Is My TikTok Not Working? 7 Fixes to Try in Order

Start at the top; most problems are solved by step 3. Menu labels change between app versions.

![Seven fixes for TikTok not working, from restarting the app to reinstalling](/images/blog/is-tiktok-down-fixes-1200x675.webp)

### Fix 1: Close and Restart TikTok

Swipe the app away from your recent apps and open it again. It fixes most freezes.

### Fix 2: Check Your Connection

Turn Wi-Fi off and on, or try mobile data. A VPN can also block TikTok in some regions, so turn it off.

### Fix 3: Update the App

Old versions stop working when TikTok changes its servers. Check the App Store or Google Play.

### Fix 4: Clear the Cache

In TikTok, go to Profile, the menu, **Settings and privacy**, then **Free up space**, and tap **Clear** next to Cache.

### Fix 5: Restart Your Phone

It clears stuck network settings and memory.

### Fix 6: Log Out and Back In

This refreshes your session if the app keeps saying something went wrong.

### Fix 7: Reinstall the App (Save Drafts First)

Delete TikTok and install it again. Your account and posted videos are safe, but unposted drafts are stored on your phone and are lost when you delete the app. Save them first: see [how to save TikTok drafts to your camera roll](/blog/how-to-save-tiktok-drafts-to-camera-roll).

> **Pro tip:** Reinstalling is the step people jump to first, and it's the only one that can cost you something. Clear the cache before you reinstall; it fixes most of the same problems without touching your drafts.

## What Does Clearing the Cache Do on TikTok?

Clearing the cache deletes temporary files TikTok stores on your phone, such as preloaded videos and thumbnails. It frees space and often fixes videos that won't play or a feed that won't refresh.

| Cleared | Kept |
|---|---|
| Preloaded videos | Your account |
| Thumbnails and temporary files | Posted videos and drafts |
| | Likes and messages |

The first few minutes afterwards may feel a little slower while TikTok downloads fresh files.

## Specific TikTok Problems and What Causes Them

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Search returns nothing | Partial outage, old app or corrupt cache; some terms are blocked on purpose | Update and clear the cache; check your inbox for a restriction notice |
| Comments won't scroll | A loading problem, or the creator limited comments | Restart, check connection, update |
| Feed scrolls by itself | Auto scroll is on, or the screen is faulty | Turn off Auto scroll; clean the screen |
| "No internet connection" on working internet | VPN, data saver or stuck network | Airplane mode on and off, VPN off, then reinstall |
| Works on Wi-Fi, not on data | Mobile data blocked for TikTok | Allow data for TikTok; turn off low data mode |

### Why Can't I Search on TikTok?

Search breaks most often during a partial outage, on an old version of the app, or when the cache is corrupted, so update and clear the cache first. Some search terms are blocked by TikTok on purpose and return no results or a safety message. If search fails only on your account, it may be a temporary restriction: check your inbox for a notice from TikTok.

### Why Can't I Scroll Comments on TikTok?

Comments that won't scroll or load are usually a loading problem: restart the app, check your connection and update. Creators can also limit or turn off comments, and some comments are folded away by TikTok's filters, so a short or missing comment list isn't always a bug.

### Why Does TikTok Auto Scroll Sometimes?

TikTok has an **Auto scroll** option that moves to the next video when one finishes. If it's on by accident, press and hold on a video and look for Auto scroll in the menu to turn it off. If the feed jumps without that setting, a stuck or sensitive screen is the likely cause: clean the screen, remove a cracked protector and restart the phone.

## "TikTok Fix" Apps and Modded Versions (Use With Caution)

When TikTok won't work, search results fill up with "TikTok fixer" apps, modded APKs and VPN offers that promise to get it running again.

![Safe TikTok fixes compared with risky fixer apps and modded versions](/images/blog/is-tiktok-down-caution-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **Modded or unofficial apps** can steal your login and break TikTok's terms, which can get your account banned.
- **"Cleaner" and "fixer" apps** rarely do anything TikTok's own Clear cache button doesn't.
- **VPNs** can cause the problem rather than fix it, since TikTok may block some VPN connections.

### Safety Checklist

- ✓ Only install TikTok from the App Store or Google Play.
- ✓ Use TikTok's own cache button.
- ✗ Never log in to TikTok through a third-party "fix" app.

> **Bottom line:** If an outage tracker shows a spike, no app can fix it. Wait it out.

## How Long Do TikTok Outages Last?

Most last from a few minutes to a couple of hours. Big ones are rare and usually come with news coverage. If TikTok is still broken for you hours after reports have dropped back to normal, go back to the fixes above.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if TikTok is down for everyone?

Check Downdetector or a similar tracker, and search "TikTok down" on another social network. A sudden jump in reports from many places means it's down for everyone.

### How long do TikTok outages last?

Most last from a few minutes to a couple of hours. Big ones are rare and usually make the news.

### Will clearing my cache delete my TikTok videos?

No. Clearing the cache removes temporary files only; your posted videos, drafts, likes and messages stay.

### Why does TikTok work on Wi-Fi but not on data?

Check that mobile data is allowed for TikTok in your phone's settings and that low data mode is off. If it still fails, your carrier may be having problems.

### Is TikTok being shut down?

No, TikTok is running normally in the US at the time of writing. Our guide on [whether TikTok is getting banned](/blog/is-tiktok-getting-banned) has the details.

### Does TikTok have an official status page?

No, not at the time of writing. Outage trackers and other users' reports are the quickest signal.

### Will reinstalling TikTok delete my drafts?

Yes. Drafts live on your phone, so save them to your camera roll before you delete the app.
