One of your videos just crossed a million views, or you're hoping the next one will, and you want to know what that's worth in actual money. There's no fixed TikTok payout per view, so there's no set amount for 1 million views. Through the **Creator Rewards Program**, TikTok pays eligible creators for **qualified views** of eligible videos **longer than one minute**, at a rate per 1,000 qualified views (RPM) that varies from video to video.

![How much TikTok pays for 1 million views: $0 for videos under a minute, and qualified views times RPM for eligible videos](/images/blog/how-much-money-do-you-get-on-tiktok-for-1-million-views-hero-1200x675.webp)

So a million views on a 20-second video earns **$0** from that program, while a million views on a qualifying video can earn a modest or a meaningful amount, depending on how many of those views qualify and the RPM.

The view counter on your video isn't the number TikTok pays on, which is why "how much is 1 million views" has no single answer. This guide explains how the payout works, walks through clearly hypothetical examples you can redo with your own numbers, and covers the other ways TikTok creators earn.

TikTok doesn't pay for views in general. It pays through specific programs, and only for views that meet that program's rules.

### What Most People Don't Know

Not every view on an eligible video counts. TikTok's criteria for qualified views have included the view coming from the For You feed, a minimum watch time, and the view not being flagged as fraudulent or invalid. So your qualified views are usually lower than the count shown on the video. TikTok shows qualified views in your Creator Rewards dashboard, and that's the number to use.

> **Key point:** Two creators with "1 million views" can see very different payouts. The share of views that qualify and the RPM matter as much as the headline view count, and videos under a minute earn nothing from the program at all.

Program rules change, and they differ by country. Check TikTok's own help pages and the Creator Rewards section of the app for the current details where you live.

## How Does the TikTok Creator Rewards Program Pay Creators?

The Creator Rewards Program is TikTok's main way of paying creators for views. It replaced the older Creator Fund, and it focuses on longer, original content.

### 1. Who Is Eligible?

At the time of writing, you need to:

- Be at least 18 years old.
- Have at least 10,000 followers.
- Have at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days.
- Have an account in good standing, in a country where the program is available.

Once you're eligible, you apply from the app's creator tools. Joining doesn't mean every video earns: each video also has to meet the program's criteria.

### 2. Which Videos Earn?

Videos need to be longer than one minute and original. TikTok also applies its content guidelines and its standards for what can be recommended in the For You feed. Reposted clips, content made mostly of other people's material, and videos that break the Community Guidelines won't earn.

### 3. What Affects Your TikTok RPM?

RPM here means rewards per 1,000 qualified views. TikTok has said rewards depend on factors including originality, how long people watch, how much search value the video has, and how much people engage with it. Outside TikTok's own formula, the forces that shape ad-funded payouts everywhere matter too: where your audience lives, your niche, and the time of year.

| Factor | Pushes RPM up when | Pushes RPM down when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Originality | You film and voice your own content | You reuse other people's clips |
| Watch time | People watch most of the video | People swipe away early |
| Search value | The video answers something people search for | Nobody would search for it |
| Engagement | People comment, share and save | Views with no interaction |

## How to Calculate What 1 Million Views Pays You (Free Method)

![Four steps to estimate TikTok earnings: open the Creator Rewards dashboard, note qualified views, work out your RPM, repeat for several videos](/images/blog/how-much-money-do-you-get-on-tiktok-for-1-million-views-calculate-1200x675.webp)

The formula is simple:

**Earnings = (qualified views ÷ 1,000) × RPM**

The hard part is using your real inputs instead of someone else's. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Open Your Creator Rewards Dashboard

Find it in TikTok Studio or the Creator tools section of the app, once you've joined the program.

### Step 2: Note a Recent Video's Qualified Views and Estimated Rewards

Pick an eligible video, longer than a minute, and write down both numbers. Ignore the public view count.

### Step 3: Work Out Your RPM

Divide the rewards by (qualified views ÷ 1,000). That's your RPM for that video.

### Step 4: Repeat for Several Videos

One video is a data point. Five or ten give you a range, and your own range is far more useful than anyone else's number.

> **Pro tip:** Write your RPM next to each video's length, topic and qualified-view share in a simple spreadsheet. Patterns show up fast: the topics with the best RPM are the ones to make more of.

## 5 Other Ways to Earn on TikTok (Often Worth More Than Views)

For many creators, view-based rewards are only part of the income. These are the main official options. Eligibility, availability and fees vary by country and change over time, so check the requirements in the app.

### 1. LIVE Gifts

When you go LIVE, viewers can send virtual Gifts bought with TikTok Coins. Gifts convert into Diamonds, which eligible creators can cash out. You need to be an adult to receive gifts, and TikTok sets its own requirements for who can go LIVE.

### 2. Subscriptions

Eligible creators can offer a monthly subscription. Subscribers pay for perks such as badges, custom emotes or subscriber-only content, and the creator receives a share of the revenue.

### 3. Brand Deals

Brands pay creators to feature products or services, through direct contact, an agency or TikTok's own marketplace for brands and creators. Paid partnerships must be disclosed with TikTok's content disclosure setting, and the price is negotiated, not set by TikTok.

### 4. TikTok Shop Affiliate

Where TikTok Shop is available, creators can tag products in videos and LIVE streams and earn a commission on sales. Sellers set commission rates per product, and there are eligibility requirements to join.

### 5. Off-Platform Income

Many creators use TikTok to grow something they own: a newsletter, a course, a product line, a service business or a YouTube channel. A link in your bio can send people there once your account qualifies for one.

## Bought Views and "TikTok Money Calculators" (Use With Caution)

Two shortcuts promise faster answers. Both can cost you.

### The Real Risks

- **Bought views don't qualify.** Views flagged as fraudulent or invalid are excluded from qualified views, so they add nothing to rewards.
- **They can hurt your standing.** Fake engagement breaks TikTok's rules, and an account that isn't in good standing can lose eligibility.
- **Calculator sites guess the RPM.** Online "TikTok money calculators" plug in a rate they chose, not yours. The result can be far off in either direction.
- **Some ask you to log in.** Never give your TikTok password to a calculator or growth site.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Use only your Creator Rewards dashboard for real figures.
- Treat any calculator result as a rough guess, not a forecast.
- Never buy views, likes or followers.
- Check your Account status in settings if earnings suddenly stop.

> **Bottom line:** Your dashboard is the only reliable source for what your views earn. Everything else is someone else's guess.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does TikTok pay per view?

TikTok doesn't publish a fixed rate per view. Through the Creator Rewards Program, eligible creators earn based on qualified views of eligible videos, at an RPM that varies. Your Creator Rewards dashboard shows your actual figures.

### How much does TikTok pay for 1,000 views?

There's no standard figure. For 1,000 qualified views, you earn your RPM for that video. As a hypothetical example, an RPM of $0.80 would mean about $0.80 for those 1,000 qualified views. Views that don't qualify earn nothing from the program.

### How much money do you get for 1 million views on TikTok?

It depends on the video's length, how many views qualify and your RPM. A video under a minute earns $0 from Creator Rewards. For an eligible video, multiply qualified views ÷ 1,000 by your RPM.

### Do TikTok videos under one minute earn money?

Not from the Creator Rewards Program, which requires videos longer than one minute at the time of writing. Shorter videos can still earn through brand deals, TikTok Shop affiliate sales, or by growing an audience for LIVEs and Subscriptions.

### How many followers do you need to get paid on TikTok?

For Creator Rewards, at least 10,000 followers, plus 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, being 18 or older and living in an eligible country, at the time of writing. LIVE gifts and Subscriptions have their own requirements.

### Why are my qualified views lower than my total views?

Only some views count. TikTok's criteria have included where the view came from (such as the For You feed), how long it lasted, and whether it was genuine. Views from other sources, very short plays and invalid traffic don't count.

### Is 1 million views on TikTok worth more than on YouTube?

It depends on format, length, how many views qualify, the audience and the niche. Each platform calculates payouts differently, so compare your own RPM on each rather than general claims.

### Are TikTok money calculators accurate?

Not for your account. They use an RPM someone chose, not yours. Use the formula with numbers from your own dashboard instead.
