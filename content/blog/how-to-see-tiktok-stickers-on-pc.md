If TikTok stickers don't show on your PC or laptop, it's usually not your computer: TikTok's website doesn't render every sticker the mobile app does. Stickers people add to **comments** often appear as a placeholder such as "[Sticker]", or as an empty comment, on the desktop site. The reliable way to see them is to open the same video in the TikTok app on a phone or tablet, or in an Android emulator on your computer.

Stickers that are part of the **video itself** (text, GIF and emoji stickers added in the editor) always show on PC, because they're baked into the video.

## Which stickers show on the TikTok website

| Sticker type | On the app | On the website (PC/laptop) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stickers added to the video in the editor | Yes | Yes, they're part of the video |
| Stickers and GIFs in comments | Yes | Often a placeholder or missing, at the time of writing |
| Interactive stickers (polls, questions, links) | Yes, tappable | Visible in the video; tapping may not work |
| Stickers in direct messages | Yes | May not display |

TikTok adds new comment features to the app first and the website later, so this changes over time. If an update adds full sticker support to the web, comments will start displaying them without you doing anything.

## How to see TikTok stickers on a laptop

1. **Sign in.** TikTok's website hides or trims comments when you're logged out. Log in, then reopen the video.
2. **Refresh and update your browser.** Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) for a hard refresh, and make sure Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox is up to date.
3. **Turn off extensions for TikTok.** Ad blockers and privacy extensions sometimes block the images stickers load from. Try a private window with extensions off.
4. **Try a different browser.** If it works in one browser and not another, it's an extension or cache issue.
5. **Open it on your phone.** Tap **Share > Copy link** on the PC (or copy the URL from the address bar), send it to your phone, and open it in the TikTok app. This is the guaranteed way to see comment stickers.
6. **Use an emulator.** If you have no phone handy, an Android emulator on Windows or Mac runs the full TikTok app, stickers included. It's heavy for a quick look, but it works.

## Why you can't add stickers on PC either

The TikTok website and the desktop upload tools don't offer the in-app sticker library. If you want stickers in your video, add them in the TikTok app before posting, or in your video editor before uploading. On desktop, TikTok Studio lets you upload, caption and schedule, but it isn't a full editor.

That's also how most creators who work from a computer handle it: edit the video (stickers, text, captions) in an editor, then upload the finished file. Schedule For You works the same way: upload the finished video or a photo post, write the caption, set who can see it, and pick a time; the same file can go to Reels and Shorts in the same draft. The [TikTok scheduler page](/tiktok) lists what's supported.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do TikTok comments say [Sticker] on my computer?
The commenter used a sticker or GIF the website can't display. Open the video in the TikTok app to see it.

### Can you see TikTok stickers on a Chromebook?
On the website, the same limits apply. Many Chromebooks can install the Android TikTok app from the Google Play Store, which shows stickers normally.

### Can you send stickers in TikTok comments from a PC?
Not at the time of writing; the sticker and GIF options in comments are in the mobile app.

### Do stickers in the video show on PC?
Yes. Stickers added in the editor are part of the video file, so they show everywhere the video plays.
