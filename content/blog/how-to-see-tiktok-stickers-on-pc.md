You're reading TikTok comments on your laptop and half of them just say "[Sticker]" or look empty. It's usually not your computer: TikTok's website doesn't render every sticker the mobile app does. The reliable way to see comment stickers is to open the same video in the TikTok app on a phone or tablet, or in an Android emulator on your computer.

![TikTok stickers on PC: video stickers always show, comment stickers often appear as a placeholder, so open the app to see them](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-stickers-on-pc-hero-1200x675.webp)

Stickers that are part of the **video itself**, like text, GIF and emoji stickers added in the editor, always show on PC, because they're baked into the video. It's the ones in **comments** that go missing.

This guide explains which stickers work on the website, six ways to see the rest, why you can't add stickers on desktop either, and how creators who work from a laptop handle it.

## Why TikTok Stickers Don't Show on PC (App First, Website Later)

![Which TikTok stickers show on PC: stickers in the video always show, comment and message stickers often don't](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-stickers-on-pc-which-1200x675.webp)

TikTok adds new comment features to the app first and the website later. Comment stickers and GIFs are one of those features, so the desktop site often shows a placeholder where the app shows the sticker.

| Sticker type | On the app | On the website (PC/laptop) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stickers added to the video in the editor | Yes | Yes, they're part of the video |
| Stickers and GIFs in comments | Yes | Often a placeholder or missing, at the time of writing |
| Interactive stickers (polls, questions, links) | Yes, tappable | Visible in the video; tapping may not work |
| Stickers in direct messages | Yes | May not display |

### What Most People Don't Know

There are two completely different kinds of "sticker". A sticker in the video is pixels in the file, so it plays everywhere. A sticker in a comment is a separate image the page has to load and draw, and the website doesn't always know how. That's why the same video can look perfect on PC while its comments look broken.

> **Key point:** If the sticker is in the video, your PC shows it. If it's in a comment and you see "[Sticker]", the website can't draw it yet. Open the app.

If an update adds full sticker support to the web, comments will start showing them without you doing anything.

## How to See TikTok Stickers on a Laptop (6 Fixes, Easiest First)

![Six ways to see TikTok stickers on a laptop, from signing in and refreshing to opening the app on your phone](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-stickers-on-pc-fixes-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app and website versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Sign In

TikTok's website hides or trims comments when you're logged out. Log in, then reopen the video.

### Step 2: Hard-Refresh and Update Your Browser

Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) for a hard refresh, and make sure Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox is up to date.

### Step 3: Turn Off Extensions for TikTok

Ad blockers and privacy extensions sometimes block the images stickers load from. Try a private window with extensions off.

### Step 4: Try a Different Browser

If it works in one browser and not another, it's an extension or cache issue.

### Step 5: Open It on Your Phone (the One That Always Works)

Click **Share > Copy link** on the PC, or copy the URL from the address bar, send it to your phone and open it in the TikTok app. This is the guaranteed way to see comment stickers.

> **Pro tip:** Send the link to yourself in a chat app you have on both devices, or scan it as a QR code. You'll land on the exact video and can scroll straight to the comments.

### Step 6: Use an Emulator

No phone handy? An Android emulator on Windows or Mac runs the full TikTok app, stickers included. It's heavy for a quick look, but it works.

| Fix | Time | Works for comment stickers? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign in and refresh | Seconds | Only if the website supports that sticker |
| Extensions off, new browser | 1–2 minutes | Only if something was blocking it |
| Open in the phone app | 30 seconds | Yes |
| Android emulator | 10+ minutes to set up | Yes |

## On a Chromebook? Use the Android App

On the website, Chromebooks have the same limits. Many Chromebooks can install the Android TikTok app from the Google Play Store, which shows stickers normally.

## Why You Can't Add Stickers on PC Either

![Adding TikTok stickers from a computer: add them in the app or your video editor before uploading](/images/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-stickers-on-pc-add-1200x675.webp)

The TikTok website and desktop upload tools don't offer the in-app sticker library, and the sticker and GIF options for comments are in the mobile app at the time of writing.

If you want stickers in your video, add them:

1. **In the TikTok app** before posting, or
2. **In your video editor** before uploading.

On desktop, TikTok Studio lets you upload, caption and schedule, but it isn't a full editor.

## Unofficial TikTok Viewer Sites and Modded Apps (Use With Caution)

Search for a fix and you'll find "TikTok viewer" sites and modified versions of the app that promise to show everything on any device.

### The Real Risks

- **Your login.** Some ask you to sign in with TikTok, which hands your account to a stranger.
- **Malware.** Modified app files from outside an official store can carry it.
- **Your account.** Using unofficial clients can break TikTok's terms.

### Stay Safe

- Use the official app, the official website, or a well-known emulator with the app from an official store.
- Never type your TikTok password into a third-party site.
- If you already did, change your password and turn on two-step verification.

> **Bottom line:** Your phone already shows every sticker. There's no need to risk your account to see one on a laptop.
