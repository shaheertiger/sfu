You uploaded a GIF to Instagram and it came out frozen. That's expected: Instagram doesn't accept animated GIF files as feed posts or Reels, so upload a `.gif` and it posts a single still frame. To post a GIF on Instagram, convert it to a short MP4 video first, then post it as a Reel or feed video.

![How to post a GIF on Instagram: convert it to an MP4, loop it to 3 seconds or more, and post it as a Reel](/images/blog/how-to-post-a-gif-on-instagram-hero-1200x675.webp)

Stories, comments and direct messages are different: they support GIFs through Instagram's built-in GIPHY search, but you can't upload your own file there.

This guide covers converting and posting a GIF to the feed or Reels, GIF stickers in Stories, GIFs in comments and DMs, fixes for common problems, and how to schedule a GIF post across platforms.

## Why Instagram Turns Your GIF Into a Still Image

![Where GIFs work on Instagram: Stories, comments and DMs through GIPHY, but not as files in the feed or Reels](/images/blog/how-to-post-a-gif-on-instagram-where-1200x675.webp)

Instagram only reads the first frame of a GIF file in the feed. Posting it as a video keeps the motion.

| Where on Instagram | GIFs supported? | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed post | Not as a GIF file | Convert to MP4, post as a video |
| Reels | Not as a GIF file | Convert to MP4, at least 3 seconds |
| Stories | Yes, GIPHY stickers | Sticker icon, then GIF |
| Comments | Yes, GIPHY only | GIF button in the comment box |
| DMs | Yes, GIPHY only | Sticker icon, then GIF |

### What Most People Don't Know

A converted GIF is just an ordinary video. Once it's an MP4, every video rule applies: Reels need to be at least 3 seconds, Instagram adds compression, and you can schedule it like any other clip.

> **Key point:** Instagram has two kinds of GIF: GIPHY stickers you pick inside the app, and your own GIF files, which have to become videos first.

## How to Post an Animated GIF to the Feed or as a Reel (4 Steps)

![Steps to post a GIF on Instagram: convert to a looped MP4, tap plus and choose Reel, then select and share](/images/blog/how-to-post-a-gif-on-instagram-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Convert the GIF to an MP4 (and Loop It)

On a phone, apps like GIPHY, ImgPlay or GIF to Video export an MP4. On a computer, a free converter site or a video editor does it. Export at 1080 pixels wide, with the GIF looped a few times so the clip runs at least 3 seconds.

### Step 2: Open Instagram and Tap +

Choose **Reel** for a vertical clip or **Post** for a square or landscape one.

### Step 3: Select the MP4

Trim it, and add music or text if you like.

### Step 4: Write the Caption and Share

Post it like any other video.

> **Pro tip:** Reels need at least 3 seconds, and most GIFs are shorter. The step people get wrong is converting a 1-second GIF once and getting an error. Loop it three or four times before you export.

Instagram adds compression, so a clean, high-contrast GIF converts better than a grainy one. Check the frame size first: our guides to [Reel size](/blog/reel-size-instagram) and [Instagram post size](/blog/instagram-post-size) have the specs.

## How to Add a GIF to an Instagram Story

1. Open the Story camera and pick a photo or video.
2. Tap the **sticker icon** and choose **GIF**.
3. Search GIPHY, tap a GIF, then resize and place it.

You can add several GIF stickers to one Story. To use your own GIF as a Story background, convert it to a video first, as above.

## How to Send a GIF in a Comment or DM

- **Comments:** tap the **GIF** button in the comment box, search, and tap to post.
- **DMs:** tap the sticker icon in the message bar and choose **GIF**.

Both use GIPHY; you can't upload your own file there.

## GIF Problems on Instagram: Fixes That Work

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The GIF posts as a still image | Convert it to MP4 | Instagram only reads a GIF file's first frame |
| "Video too short" for a Reel | Loop the GIF to 3 seconds or more | Reels need at least 3 seconds |
| It looks blurry or blocky | Start from a clean, high-contrast GIF and export at 1080 pixels wide | Instagram compresses every video |
| The edges get cropped | Resize to 4:5 or 9:16 before posting | Instagram crops other ratios |
| Can't find your own GIF in Stories or comments | Convert it to a video and post that | Stickers and comments only search GIPHY |

## Other People's GIFs and Unknown Converters (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to post GIFs on Instagram, like GIPHY stickers and your own GIFs, next to risky ones like reposting someone else's GIF](/images/blog/how-to-post-a-gif-on-instagram-safety-1200x675.webp)

GIPHY stickers in Stories, comments and DMs are built into Instagram. Downloading someone else's GIF and posting it as your own Reel is different.

### The Real Risks

- **Copyright.** Many GIFs are clips from films, shows or other creators' work. Posting them as your own video can get the post removed.
- **Unknown converter apps and sites** may add watermarks, ask for permissions they don't need, or keep what you upload.

### A Quick Safety Checklist

- Convert GIFs you made or have the right to use.
- Use GIPHY stickers inside Instagram for reaction GIFs.
- Pick converters that don't ask you to log in with your Instagram account.

> **Bottom line:** Your own GIFs as videos, GIPHY's inside the app. Skip the rest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you post a GIF on Instagram?

Not as a GIF file in the feed; Instagram posts a still frame. Convert it to a short MP4 and post it as a Reel or video. Stories, comments and DMs support GIFs through GIPHY.

### How do I turn a GIF into a video for Instagram?

Use a converter app (GIPHY, ImgPlay) or a video editor to export an MP4 at 1080 pixels wide, looped so it runs at least 3 seconds.

### Why does my GIF post as a still image?

Instagram only reads the first frame of a GIF file. Posting it as a video keeps the motion.

### Can you schedule a GIF on Instagram?

Yes, once it's an MP4. Instagram's app can schedule Reels for professional accounts, and schedulers can post the video to Instagram and other platforms at a set time.

### Can I upload my own GIF as a Story sticker?

No. Story stickers search GIPHY. To use your own GIF, convert it to a video and post that as the Story.

### How long does a GIF need to be for a Reel?

At least 3 seconds. Loop a short GIF three or four times before you export.

### Why does my converted GIF look blurry?

Instagram compresses every video. Start with a clean, high-contrast GIF and export at 1080 pixels wide.
