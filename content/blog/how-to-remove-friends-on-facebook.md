Your friends list has filled up with old coworkers, people you met once and an ex you'd rather not see. To get rid of a friend on Facebook, go to their profile, tap the **Friends** button under their name, choose **Unfriend** and confirm. Facebook doesn't notify them.

![Ways to get rid of friends on Facebook: unfriend, unfollow, restrict, take a break or block](/images/blog/how-to-remove-friends-on-facebook-hero-1200x675.webp)

Unfriending isn't always the best fit, though. If cutting ties would be awkward, you can **unfollow** them, add them to your **Restricted** list, use **Take a break**, or **block** them if you want no contact at all.

This guide covers the steps on each device, how to clear several friends, which option suits which situation, and what to do if your profile has turned into a business page.

## Unfriend, Unfollow, Restrict or Block? 6 Options Compared

![Unfriending compared with quieter options on Facebook: who stays a friend and who still sees your posts](/images/blog/how-to-remove-friends-on-facebook-options-1200x675.webp)

| Option | You stay friends? | They see your friends-only posts? | You see their posts? | They're notified? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unfriend | No | No | Only public posts, if you follow | No |
| Unfollow | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Snooze for 30 days | Yes | Yes | Not for 30 days | No |
| Restricted list | Yes | No, only public posts | Yes | No |
| Take a break | Yes | You choose | Less often | No |
| Block | No | No | No | No, but they can't find you |

### What Most People Don't Know

Unfriending doesn't stop someone seeing your **public** posts, and it doesn't close your Messenger conversation. If the goal is privacy, the Restricted list often does more with less drama: they stay a friend on paper but only see what you post as Public. If the goal is no contact, only blocking does that.

> **Key point:** Pick the option by what you want to stop. Their posts in your feed? Unfollow. Them seeing your posts? Restricted. Any contact? Block.

## How to Unfriend Someone in the Facebook App (3 Steps)

![Three steps to unfriend on Facebook: open their profile, tap Friends, tap Unfriend and confirm](/images/blog/how-to-remove-friends-on-facebook-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Open Their Profile

Search their name or tap it on one of their posts.

### Step 2: Tap the Friends Button

It's the button below their name that shows you're already friends.

### Step 3: Tap Unfriend, Then Confirm

That's it. They're removed from your friends list and aren't notified.

> **Pro tip:** Clearing out many people? Go to your own profile, tap **Friends** (or **See all friends**), and use the three dots next to each name. You can work down the list without opening every profile.

## How to Unfriend Someone on Desktop

1. Go to facebook.com and open the person's profile.
2. Click **Friends** under their cover photo.
3. Click **Unfriend** and confirm.

From your own profile's **Friends** tab, the three dots next to each friend do the same.

## How to Remove Several Friends at Once (No Bulk Button)

Facebook doesn't have a bulk unfriend button. The fastest built-in way is to go to your profile, open **Friends**, and work down the list using the three dots next to each name. Spread a big clear-out over a few sessions rather than doing hundreds in one go.

## 4 Quieter Options Than Unfriending

![Four quieter Facebook options: unfollow or snooze, Restricted list, Take a break, and block](/images/blog/how-to-remove-friends-on-facebook-quiet-1200x675.webp)

### 1. Unfollow or Snooze

On their profile, tap **Friends**, then **Unfollow** to stop seeing their posts permanently. Or open the three dots on one of their posts and choose **Snooze for 30 days**.

### 2. Restricted List

On their profile, tap **Friends**, then **Edit friend list** and choose **Restricted**. They stay a friend but only see your posts set to Public.

### 3. Take a Break

Built for exes and other tricky situations. Open their profile, tap **Friends**, then **Take a break**, and choose how much you see of each other and who can see past posts.

### 4. Block

On their profile, tap the three dots, then **Block**. They can't see your profile, message you or find you in search.

| Situation | Best option |
| --- | --- |
| Their posts annoy you, but you like them | Unfollow |
| You need a short break from someone | Snooze for 30 days |
| A boss or relative you can't unfriend | Restricted list |
| A breakup | Take a break |
| Someone you've lost touch with | Unfriend |
| Harassment or someone you want no contact with | Block |

## Mass-Unfriend Browser Extensions (Use With Caution)

Browser extensions and scripts promise to unfriend hundreds of people in one click. When you're facing a list of 2,000 names, that's tempting.

### The Real Risks

- **They need access to your account.** An extension that can unfriend for you can usually read and do much more.
- **They break Facebook's terms**, which don't allow automated activity on your account.
- **They can trigger a temporary block** for unusual activity, locking you out of features you actually use.

### If You Still Want to Clear Your List Fast

- Use the three-dot menu on your Friends tab instead.
- Do a few dozen at a time, over several days.
- Never enter your Facebook password into an extension or third-party site.
- Afterwards, check **Settings > Apps and websites** and remove anything you don't recognise.

> **Bottom line:** A few minutes a day on your Friends tab gets the same result without handing your account to an extension.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Facebook tell someone when you unfriend them?

No. Facebook doesn't send a notification, but they may notice if they look for you in their friends list or your profile.

### Can someone tell I unfollowed them on Facebook?

No. Unfollowing is invisible to them. You stay friends and they can still see your posts.

### What happens to messages after you unfriend someone?

Your Messenger conversation stays. They can still message you, though new messages may go to message requests depending on your settings. Blocking stops messages.

### How do I re-add someone I unfriended?

Go to their profile and tap Add friend. They have to accept the request again.

### Can I unfriend lots of people at once on Facebook?

There's no bulk button. Use the three dots next to each name on your Friends tab, and avoid mass-unfriend extensions.

### What's the difference between Restricted and unfriending?

Restricted keeps them as a friend but shows them only your public posts. Unfriending removes them from your list entirely.

### Is there a limit to how many friends you can have on Facebook?

Yes, 5,000 friends per profile. Unfriending frees up space; followers aren't capped the same way.

### Can I remove someone from my Facebook Page?

Yes. In the Page's settings under People, you can remove or ban someone and hide comments from specific people.
