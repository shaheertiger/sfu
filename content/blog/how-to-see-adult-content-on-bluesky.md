You keep seeing blurred images on Bluesky, or posts you know exist don't show up at all. To see adult content on Bluesky, go to **Settings > Moderation**, switch on **Enable adult content**, then set each content filter (Adult, Suggestive, Graphic media, Non-sexual nudity) to **Show** or **Warn**. You need to be 18 or over.

![How to see adult content on Bluesky: enable it in Moderation, on the web if your iPhone app hides it](/images/blog/how-to-see-adult-content-on-bluesky-hero-1200x675.webp)

If the switch is missing or greyed out in the iPhone app, change it on [bsky.app](https://bsky.app) in a browser instead, then close and reopen the app so the setting syncs. In some places, including the UK and a few US states, Bluesky also asks you to verify your age first.

This guide covers the full walkthrough, what each option does, why the toggle goes missing, and what Bluesky actually allows.

## Why Adult Content Is Hidden on Bluesky by Default

Adult content is off by default for every account. Bluesky's approach is labels plus choice: content gets labeled, and each adult decides what to show, blur or hide.

![Bluesky's default settings compared with what changes when an adult turns adult content on](/images/blog/how-to-see-adult-content-on-bluesky-default-vs-on-1200x675.webp)

| | Default (off) | After you enable it |
|---|---|---|
| Adult-labeled posts | Hidden | Shown, blurred or hidden, per your choice |
| Graphic media | Warned or hidden | Your choice per label |
| Who can switch it | — | Accounts registered as 18 or over |
| Where you switch it | — | Settings > Moderation (on the web for iPhone users) |

### What Most People Don't Know

The setting isn't stored in the app; it's saved to your account. That's why changing it on the web fixes the iPhone app: once you set it at bsky.app, it applies everywhere you're signed in. The iPhone app just doesn't show the switch.

> **Key point:** If the toggle is missing on iPhone, you haven't done anything wrong. Change it in a browser, then restart the app.

## How to Turn On Adult Content on Bluesky: 5 Steps

Menu labels move between app versions, so the names below may differ slightly.

![Five steps to turn on adult content on Bluesky in Settings and Moderation](/images/blog/how-to-see-adult-content-on-bluesky-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Settings

Open Bluesky and go to **Settings**.

### Step 2: Tap Moderation

In some versions it's under **Privacy and security**, then **Moderation**.

### Step 3: Switch On Enable Adult Content

Find **Content filters** and switch on **Enable adult content**.

### Step 4: Choose Show, Warn or Hide for Each Label

Set Adult, Suggestive, Graphic media and Non-sexual nudity separately.

### Step 5: On iPhone, Use the Web Instead

If the switch isn't there, sign in at bsky.app in Safari or any browser, make the change there and restart the app.

> **Pro tip:** Start with **Warn** rather than **Show**. You'll see what's there behind a tap-through blur, and you can switch individual labels to Show once you know what you're comfortable with.

## Show, Warn or Hide: What Each Option Does

| Option | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Show | Labeled posts appear normally, images unblurred |
| Warn | Labeled posts appear blurred behind a warning you can tap through |
| Hide | Labeled posts are filtered out of your feeds and search |

Bluesky's labels cover four kinds of content:

| Label | Covers |
|---|---|
| Adult | Sexual content |
| Suggestive | Sexualized but not explicit |
| Graphic media | Violence, gore or injury |
| Non-sexual nudity | Art, medical or educational nudity |

You can set each one separately, so you could show art nudity, warn on suggestive posts and hide graphic media.

## Why Is the Adult Content Setting Missing or Greyed Out? 4 Reasons

![Four reasons the Bluesky adult content toggle is missing, and the fix for each](/images/blog/how-to-see-adult-content-on-bluesky-missing-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No toggle in the iPhone app | Apple doesn't let social apps switch on adult content inside the iOS app | Change it at bsky.app, then restart the app |
| Toggle greyed out | The account is registered as under 18 | It can't be enabled |
| Content still hidden after enabling | Age verification is required where you live | Complete Bluesky's age check |
| Changed on web, app still blurs | Settings haven't synced | Sign out of the app and back in |

Where local law requires age checks, Bluesky uses Epic Games' Kids Web Services to confirm age with a face scan, ID scan or payment card. At the time of writing that applies in the UK and some US states. Until you verify, adult content stays hidden and some features, such as DMs, may be limited.

## Still Seeing Blurred Images? How Labels Work

Images carry the label, not the whole post. With **Warn** on, you'll see a blurred image with a "Content warning" note; tap it to reveal the image. With **Show**, it appears without the blur.

If an image is still hidden after you've chosen Show, the account itself may carry a label, or a moderation service you subscribe to may be hiding it. Check **Moderation > Advanced** for the labelers you use.

## Third-Party Clients and Labelers (Use With Caution)

Because Bluesky is an open network, you'll find other apps and moderation services (labelers) that change what you see. Some promise to "unlock" content the official app hides.

### The Real Risks

- **Your password.** Any third-party client should use an app password, never your main one.
- **Bypassing age checks.** Tools that claim to skip age verification may break the law where you live and Bluesky's terms.
- **Unexpected filters.** A labeler you subscribed to and forgot about can hide or reveal more than you expect.

### Quick Safety Checklist

- ✓ Use the official app or bsky.app for moderation settings.
- ✓ Review your labelers under Moderation > Advanced now and then.
- ✗ Don't enter your main password in a third-party client.

> **Bottom line:** The official setting on the web does everything you need. Anything promising more is a risk to your account.

## Does Bluesky Allow NSFW and Adult Content?

Yes, within limits. Bluesky's community guidelines allow consensual adult content as long as it's labeled, either by the poster or by Bluesky's moderation. What isn't allowed:

- Unlabeled adult content (it gets labeled, and repeat offenders face action).
- Adult content in profile pictures or banners.
- Anything involving minors, non-consensual imagery or illegal content, which is removed and reported.

Correctly labeled posts reach people who opted in and stay out of everyone else's feed. Text-only posts can't carry these labels, since they apply to media.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I turn on adult content in the Bluesky app?

On iPhone the toggle isn't offered in the app because of App Store rules. Sign in at bsky.app in a browser, enable adult content under Settings, then Moderation, and restart the app.

### Do I need to verify my age to see adult content on Bluesky?

Only where the law requires it, such as the UK and some US states at the time of writing. Bluesky prompts you if it applies to you.

### Does Bluesky have porn?

Bluesky allows labeled adult content from adults, and it's hidden by default. You only see it if you're 18 or over and switch it on.

### Can I hide adult content again later?

Yes, switch off Enable adult content, or set each label to Hide, under Settings, then Moderation.

### Can other people see that I enabled adult content?

No. Your moderation preferences are private to your account.

### Why are images still blurred after I chose Show?

The account may carry its own label, or a labeler you subscribe to may be hiding it. Check Moderation > Advanced.

### Does the setting apply on every device?

Yes. It's saved to your account, so it applies wherever you're signed in once the app syncs.
