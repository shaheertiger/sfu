To see your scheduled posts on LinkedIn, start a new post, click the **clock icon** next to the Post button, and choose **View all scheduled posts**. That screen lists every post waiting to publish, with options to edit, reschedule or delete. The route is the same on desktop and in the mobile app, which is why it's easy to miss: the list only appears from inside the post composer.

## Find scheduled posts on desktop

1. On the LinkedIn home page, click **Start a post**.
2. In the composer, click the **clock icon** at the bottom right, beside **Post**.
3. Click **View all scheduled posts**.

You'll see each post with its date and time. Click the three dots on a post for **Edit post**, **Reschedule** or **Delete post**.

## Find scheduled posts in the mobile app

1. Tap **Post** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap the **clock icon** next to the Post button.
3. Tap **View all scheduled posts**.

## What you can and can't edit

- **Text:** editable up to the scheduled time.
- **Time:** reschedule to any slot from one hour to three months ahead, at the time of writing.
- **Media:** images and video can't be swapped on a scheduled post. Delete and recreate it if the file is wrong.
- **Who sees it:** the audience setting (Anyone or Connections only) is fixed once scheduled.

LinkedIn schedules only on your personal profile and on Company Pages you manage. Polls, events and documents can't be scheduled natively at the time of writing.

## Managing LinkedIn with your other platforms

The clock-icon list only shows LinkedIn. If you also schedule to X, Instagram, Threads or Bluesky, a scheduler puts every post in one queue and calendar so you can see the whole week at once.

In Schedule For You, your personal LinkedIn profile connects with LinkedIn's own sign-in. You write once, tailor the LinkedIn version against its 3,000-character limit, attach up to nine images or a video, and schedule. Scheduled posts sit in the queue with the rest; drag one on the calendar to move it, open it to edit the text or swap media from the library, or delete it. The [LinkedIn scheduler page](/linkedin) lists what's supported, including the formatting that the [LinkedIn text formatter](/tools/linkedin-text-formatter) adds for bold and italic text.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are my scheduled posts on LinkedIn?
Start a new post, click the clock icon next to Post, then **View all scheduled posts**. They aren't shown on your profile until they publish.

### Can you edit a scheduled post on LinkedIn?
Yes. Open the scheduled posts list, click the three dots on the post and choose **Edit post**. Text can be changed; media and the audience setting can't.

### How far ahead can you schedule on LinkedIn?
From one hour to three months ahead at the time of writing.

### Why isn't my scheduled LinkedIn post getting impressions?
A scheduled post is ranked like any other. If a post underperforms, timing and format are the usual causes; our guide to a [LinkedIn post not getting impressions](/blog/linkedin-post-not-getting-impressions) goes through the checks.
