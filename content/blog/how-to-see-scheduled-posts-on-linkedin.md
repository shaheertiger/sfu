You scheduled a LinkedIn post last week, and now you can't find it anywhere: not on your profile, not in your activity, not in your notifications.

To see your scheduled posts on LinkedIn, click **Start a post**, click the **clock icon** next to the Post button, then click **View all scheduled posts**. The list shows every post waiting to publish with its date and time. For a company page, do the same from the page's **admin view**, so the composer opens as the page.

![How to see scheduled posts on LinkedIn: Start a post, the clock icon, then View all scheduled posts](/images/blog/how-to-see-scheduled-posts-on-linkedin-hero-1200x675.webp)

Scheduled posts don't appear on your profile or page until they publish, which is why they're easy to lose track of. The list lives inside the composer, where few people think to look.

This guide covers the steps for profiles and pages on desktop and mobile, how to edit, reschedule or cancel a post, why a post can seem missing, and what to do when you're tracking more than one account.

## Why You Can't See Scheduled LinkedIn Posts on Your Profile

A scheduled post isn't a post yet. LinkedIn keeps it in a private list inside the composer, and it only appears on your profile or page once it publishes.

![Where LinkedIn scheduled posts are, and where they don't appear](/images/blog/how-to-see-scheduled-posts-on-linkedin-where-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Profiles and company pages keep **separate** scheduled lists. If you scheduled a post as the company page and then look from your own profile, the list looks empty. The same happens the other way round. Other admins of a company page can see the page's scheduled posts too, and can reschedule or delete them. Nobody else can see a personal profile's scheduled posts.

> **Key point:** Before you click the clock icon, check whose name or logo is at the top of the composer. Most "missing" scheduled posts are in the other list.

## How to Find Scheduled Posts on Your LinkedIn Profile (3 Steps)

LinkedIn rearranges the composer from time to time, so labels and the icon's position may differ slightly.

### Step 1: Click Start a Post

On the LinkedIn home page, click **Start a post**. In the mobile app, tap **Post** in the bottom bar.

### Step 2: Click the Clock Icon, Not Post

Click the **clock icon** beside **Post**. Depending on your version it's at the bottom or the top of the composer. This is the step people miss, because the clock is small and sits right next to the button that publishes.

### Step 3: Click View All Scheduled Posts

The list shows each scheduled post with its date and time.

> **Pro tip:** You don't need to write anything in the composer to open the list. Click Start a post, then the clock, and close the empty draft afterwards.

| Where | Steps |
|---|---|
| Desktop, profile | Start a post → clock icon → View all scheduled posts |
| Mobile app, profile | Post → clock icon → View all scheduled posts |
| Company page | Admin view → Create or Start a post → clock icon → View all scheduled posts |

## How to Find Scheduled Posts on a LinkedIn Company Page

![Three steps to see scheduled posts on a LinkedIn company page from the admin view](/images/blog/how-to-see-scheduled-posts-on-linkedin-page-1200x675.webp)

1. Open the page's **admin view** (you need a super admin or content admin role).
2. Click **Create** or **Start a post**. Check that the page's logo, not your photo, shows at the top of the composer.
3. Click the **clock icon**, then **View all scheduled posts**.

If a post you scheduled for the page is missing, you're probably looking at your profile's list.

## How to Edit, Reschedule or Cancel a Scheduled LinkedIn Post

![How to edit, reschedule or cancel a scheduled LinkedIn post from the three dots menu](/images/blog/how-to-see-scheduled-posts-on-linkedin-edit-1200x675.webp)

From the **View all scheduled posts** list, open the **three dots** (More) on the post:

- **Edit the text:** choose **Edit post**, change it and save.
- **Change the time:** choose **Modify schedule** (or **Reschedule**), pick a new date and time up to about three months ahead, and save.
- **Cancel it:** choose **Delete** (or the bin icon) and confirm. The post is gone, and nothing is published.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| The list is empty | Switch between profile and page | They keep separate lists |
| No Edit post option on a page post | Delete it and schedule a corrected one | Some guides report text edits are sometimes missing on pages |
| Wrong image or video | Recreate the post | Swapping media on a scheduled post isn't reliable |
| The launch moved a day | Modify schedule on each post | Each post keeps its own time |

## Where This Gets Awkward: Three Profiles and a Page in Launch Week

Say you're the marketing lead at a small company. Launch week has a post on the company page, an announcement from the founder's profile, and a follow-up from the head of product's profile, plus the same news on X and Bluesky.

To answer "what's going out on Tuesday?", you'd have to:

- open the page's admin view and check its scheduled list,
- ask the founder and the head of product to check theirs, because nobody else can see a personal profile's scheduled posts,
- then look in whatever tool holds the X and Bluesky posts.

If launch moves a day, every person reschedules their own post. Nobody sees the whole week until it has already happened.

## Sharing LinkedIn Logins to Check Posts (Use With Caution)

The quick fix is for the founder to hand over their LinkedIn password so you can check their scheduled list yourself.

### The Real Risks

- **Security checks.** Logging in from a new device or location can trigger LinkedIn's verification and lock the owner out at the worst moment.
- **Full access.** A password gives access to messages, connections and settings, not just scheduled posts.
- **No record.** If something goes out wrong, there's no way to tell who changed it.

> **Bottom line:** Ask each person to check their own list, or move the posts somewhere everyone can see without sharing passwords.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where are my scheduled posts on LinkedIn?

Click **Start a post**, then the clock icon next to Post, then **View all scheduled posts**. For a company page, start from the page's admin view. Scheduled posts don't show on your profile or page until they publish.

### Why can't I see my scheduled LinkedIn post?

Usually because you're looking in the wrong list. Posts scheduled as a company page only appear in the page's list, and profile posts only in yours.

### Can you edit a scheduled post on LinkedIn?

Yes. In the scheduled posts list, open the three dots and choose **Edit post** for the text or **Modify schedule** for the time. If editing isn't offered on a page post, delete it and schedule it again.

### Can I see someone else's scheduled LinkedIn posts?

Not for personal profiles: only the account owner can see them. Admins of a company page can see the page's scheduled posts. In a shared Schedule For You workspace, members see the posts scheduled to the connected profiles.

### How do I cancel a scheduled LinkedIn post?

Open **View all scheduled posts**, click the three dots or the bin icon on the post, and confirm the delete. Nothing is published.

### How far ahead can I schedule a LinkedIn post?

Up to about three months, at the time of writing.

### Do scheduled LinkedIn posts appear on my profile?

No, not until they publish.

### Why isn't my scheduled LinkedIn post getting impressions?

A scheduled post is ranked like any other, so timing and the first lines matter more than how it was posted. Our guide to a [LinkedIn post not getting impressions](/blog/linkedin-post-not-getting-impressions) goes through the checks.
