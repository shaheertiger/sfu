You exported a video, uploaded it to TikTok, and it's sitting in a thin strip with empty space above and below. The best size for a TikTok video is **1080×1920 pixels**, which is a **9:16 vertical aspect ratio**. That fills the whole phone screen with no black bars.

![TikTok video size in 2026: 1080 by 1920 pixels at 9:16 fills the screen, while 1:1 and 16:9 show with bars](/images/blog/tiktok-video-size-ratio-hero-1200x675.webp)

TikTok also accepts square (1:1) and horizontal (16:9) videos, but they appear smaller, with empty space above and below. And even a perfect 9:16 video can lose text under TikTok's buttons and caption if you don't leave a safe zone.

This guide covers the specs, what happens with other ratios, length and file limits, safe zones for text, the export settings that keep your video sharp, and how to reuse the same file elsewhere.

## Why 9:16 Is the Right TikTok Ratio (Specs at a Glance)

![Bar chart of how much of a TikTok screen each ratio fills: 9:16 full screen, 4:5 and 1:1 with bars, and 16:9 a thin strip](/images/blog/tiktok-video-size-ratio-ratios-1200x675.webp)

TikTok is built for phones held upright. A 9:16 video uses the full height and width of most phone screens, so your content takes up the whole view in the For You feed. Anything else leaves unused space, which makes your video look smaller and less polished next to the ones around it.

| Spec | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 (vertical) |
| Resolution | 1080×1920 pixels |
| Minimum resolution | 720×1280 pixels |
| Other accepted ratios | 1:1 (square), 16:9 (horizontal) and others, shown with bars |
| File formats | MP4 or MOV (MP4 or WebM on desktop, at the time of writing) |
| Video codec | H.264 is the safest choice |
| Frame rate | 30 fps (24–60 fps all work) |
| Photo posts | 1080×1920 recommended (9:16) |
| Profile photo | 200×200 minimum recommended |

TikTok updates its upload limits regularly, so treat any length or file-size figure as a snapshot. The upload screen in TikTok Studio on desktop lists the current maximums.

### What Most People Don't Know

Getting the size right is only half of it. TikTok places its username, caption, buttons and tabs on top of your video, so a correctly sized 9:16 video can still hide your text. The safe zone matters as much as the ratio.

> **Key point:** Set your project to 1080×1920 before you edit, and keep key text slightly above the middle of the frame.

### How to Fix Horizontal Footage

If you only have horizontal footage, reframe it rather than uploading it as-is. Most editing apps let you crop to 9:16 and pan to follow the action, or place the horizontal clip in the middle with a caption above and below so the frame is still filled.

### Photo Posts and Carousels

TikTok's photo mode lets you post a swipeable set of images. Use 1080×1920 for full-screen images; square and landscape photos get letterboxed like videos. ## TikTok Safe Zones: Where to Put Text (4 Rules)

![TikTok safe zones: keep text away from the bottom caption area, the right-side buttons and the top tabs](/images/blog/tiktok-video-size-ratio-safe-zones-1200x675.webp)

TikTok places your username and caption at the bottom, the like, comment, share and profile buttons down the right side, and the Following / For You tabs across the top.

1. **Keep text and faces away from the bottom.** The caption, sound name and username cover roughly the lower fifth of the screen, more when your caption is long.
2. **Leave a margin on the right side.** The engagement buttons run down the right edge.
3. **Avoid the very top.** Navigation tabs and the status bar sit there.
4. **Center key text slightly above the middle.** That area is clear on almost every phone.

Screen shapes vary, so the exact covered area differs between devices. Before publishing, preview the video in TikTok's editor or save a draft and watch it on your own phone with the interface showing.

### Text and Captions That Stay Readable

- Use large, bold text with good contrast, such as white text with a dark outline or a semi-transparent background.
- Keep on-screen text to a few words per frame. Viewers are scrolling quickly.
- Turn on TikTok's auto captions or add your own for people watching without sound.
- For styled text in your written caption, the [TikTok font generator](/tools/tiktok-font-generator) creates Unicode bold, italic and script styles. Use it for a word or two, since styled characters aren't searchable and screen readers may struggle with them.

## Export Settings for a Sharp TikTok Video (6 Steps)

![Export settings for a sharp TikTok video: a 1080 by 1920 canvas, H.264 MP4 at 1080p, and a high-quality upload over Wi-Fi](/images/blog/tiktok-video-size-ratio-export-1200x675.webp)

TikTok compresses every upload. Starting from a clean, high-quality file gives the compression less to damage.

### Step 1: Set the Canvas to 1080×1920 Before Editing

Changing the canvas size at the end can stretch or crop your work.

### Step 2: Export at 1080p

TikTok supports higher resolutions for some uploads, but 1080×1920 is the standard and plays well everywhere.

### Step 3: Use H.264 in an MP4 File

It's the most widely compatible combination. Use AAC for audio.

### Step 4: Match the Frame Rate to Your Footage

If you shot at 30 fps, export at 30 fps. Mixing rates can cause stutter.

### Step 5: Use a Reasonably High Bitrate

Most editors have a "high quality" or "social media" preset that works well.

### Step 6: Turn On High-Quality Uploads

Check the TikTok app's data or upload settings for an option to upload in high quality, and upload over Wi-Fi when you can.

> **Pro tip:** Step 1 is the one people get wrong. Editing on a 16:9 timeline and switching to 9:16 at export either stretches the picture or crops out the action. Start vertical.

## Common Problems and Fixes

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black bars above and below | Video isn't 9:16 | Reframe or crop to 1080×1920 |
| Video looks blurry | Low-resolution source or heavy compression | Export at 1080×1920, upload in high quality over Wi-Fi |
| Text cut off | Text is in the safe-zone margins | Move text toward the center |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best TikTok video size?

1080×1920 pixels in a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio. It fills the full phone screen and gives TikTok enough detail to keep your video sharp after compression.

### Can I post a horizontal video on TikTok?

Yes, but 16:9 videos appear as a band across the middle of the screen with empty space above and below. Reframe horizontal footage to 9:16 before uploading.

### What is the minimum resolution for TikTok videos?

720×1280 pixels is the practical minimum for a vertical video. Anything lower will look soft on most phones. Aim for 1080×1920 whenever your source footage allows.

### How long can a TikTok video be?

At the time of writing, you can record up to 10 minutes in the app, and longer uploads (up to around 60 minutes) are available for many accounts, especially from desktop. Check the upload screen for your account's current maximum.

### Does TikTok support 4K uploads?

TikTok accepts higher-resolution files, but it compresses videos for playback, and 1080×1920 is the standard most viewers see. Exporting at 1080p gives a smaller file that uploads faster, with little visible difference on a phone.

### What size should TikTok photo posts be?

1080×1920 for full-screen images. Square and landscape photos work but get letterboxed.

### Why does my TikTok look blurry?

Usually a low-resolution source or heavy compression. Export at 1080×1920 and upload in high quality over Wi-Fi.

### Can I use the same video for Reels and Shorts?

Yes. A 9:16 video at 1080×1920 fits Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts. Check each platform's length limits and safe zones.
