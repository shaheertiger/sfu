Your comments disappear, your live chat messages get no replies, or your new videos stall at a fraction of their usual views. A YouTube shadowban is when your videos, comments or chat messages stop reaching people without a clear notice. But YouTube doesn't use the word "shadowban", and it doesn't have one hidden, site-wide switch.

![YouTube shadowban: check YouTube Studio first, then test your comments in a private window](/images/blog/youtube-shadowban-hero-1200x675.webp)

What people experience is usually one of three things: a **strike or lost feature** shown in YouTube Studio, **comments held for review or hidden by a particular channel**, or a plain **drop in impressions**. Start by checking YouTube Studio for notices, then test your comments in a private window.

This guide covers each kind of YouTube "shadowban", five tests that tell them apart, and who can actually lift each one.

## What a YouTube Shadowban Actually Is (4 Different Problems)

"Shadowbanned on YouTube" gets used for very different situations, and who decided matters more than what it looks like.

![Problems YouTube decides compared with problems a single channel decides](/images/blog/youtube-shadowban-who-decides-1200x675.webp)

| What you notice | Who usually decided | Do you get a notice? |
|---|---|---|
| Your comments vanish for everyone else | The channel you commented on, or YouTube's spam filter | No |
| Your live chat messages don't show to others | That channel's moderators | No |
| Your channel lost features (uploads, live, custom thumbnails) | YouTube | Yes, in Studio and by email |
| Your videos get far fewer impressions | Nobody: usually normal variation | No |

### What Most People Don't Know

Most comment and chat "shadowbans" are per channel. A channel owner or moderator can hide a user, and after that every comment you post on that channel is invisible to everyone but you. Your comments on other channels are unaffected, and YouTube can't overrule it.

> **Key point:** If YouTube restricts your own channel, it tells you in Studio. If nothing's there, you're dealing with one channel's moderation, spam filtering or ordinary reach, not a site-wide ban.

## How to Tell If You're Shadowbanned on YouTube: 5 Tests

Menu names in YouTube Studio change between versions, so use Studio's search if you can't find a setting.

![Five tests for a YouTube shadowban, starting with YouTube Studio](/images/blog/youtube-shadowban-tests-1200x675.webp)

### Test 1: Check YouTube Studio (the Only Official Answer)

If YouTube has taken action on your channel, it tells you. At the time of writing, look in three places:

1. **The YouTube Studio dashboard.** Community Guidelines warnings, strikes and copyright strikes appear here with the video and policy named.
2. **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility.** This shows which features your channel can use and whether any are limited.
3. **The email linked to your channel.** YouTube sends notices for removals, strikes and age restrictions. Only trust messages from YouTube's own domains, and never enter your password from an unexpected link.

No warnings, no strikes and full feature eligibility means YouTube hasn't restricted your channel.

### Test 2: The Private-Window Comment Test

1. Post a normal comment, without a link, on a video.
2. Open the same video in a private or incognito window where you're not logged in.
3. Sort comments by **Newest first** and look for yours. Give it a minute or two.
4. Repeat on two or three different channels.

> **Pro tip:** Missing on one channel but visible on others means that channel is holding or hiding you. Missing everywhere usually means YouTube's spam filter. Testing on a single channel is the mistake that makes people think YouTube banned them.

### Test 3: The Live Chat Test

Ask a friend in the same stream whether they can see your messages, then try another channel's stream. If you're visible elsewhere, the first channel's moderators made the call.

### Test 4: The Logged-Out Search Test

In a private window, search for your channel name and the exact title of a recent video. New videos can take a while to appear for their title, so check again after a day.

### Test 5: Compare Impressions Across Similar Videos

In YouTube Analytics, compare impressions and click-through rate across several similar videos. A drop on one video is normal; a sudden drop on every new video is worth looking into.

### The 5 Tests Compared

| Test | What it checks | How reliable | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Studio | Strikes, warnings, lost features | Highest: YouTube's own record | 2 minutes |
| Private-window comments | Whether others see your comments | High, if run on several channels | 5 minutes |
| Live chat | Whether others see your chat | High, with a friend's help | 5 minutes |
| Logged-out search | Whether your channel and videos appear | Medium: new videos lag | 3 minutes |
| Impressions | Whether reach really dropped | Medium: reach varies naturally | 10 minutes |

## Why Your YouTube Comments Disappear: Held, Filtered or Hidden

![Why YouTube comments disappear: held for review, caught as spam, or hidden on one channel](/images/blog/youtube-shadowban-comments-1200x675.webp)

| Cause | What's happening | Who can fix it |
|---|---|---|
| Held for review | The channel holds comments with links, blocked words or possibly inappropriate content until approved; many are never reviewed | The channel owner |
| YouTube's spam filter | Links, repeated text across many videos, or bursts of comments send them to "Likely spam" or remove them | You, by changing the pattern |
| Hidden user | The channel added you to its Hidden users list | Only that channel |

## Did YouTube Shadowban Me From Stream Chats?

Probably not YouTube itself. In live chat, a channel's moderators can **hide a user on the channel** or put them in a **timeout**, and both are per channel. A hidden user still sees their own messages, which is why it feels like a shadowban. Live chats can also run blocked-word lists, hold messages, or limit chat to subscribers or members.

If chat is missing everywhere, or you can't type at all, check Studio and your email for a notice about your own account.

## Why Your Videos Get Fewer Views: 6 Reach Problems That Aren't Bans

If Studio is clear and your comments show, a feeling of being shadowbanned usually comes from reach.

1. **Lower click-through rate.** Fewer clicks on your thumbnail means fewer impressions next time.
2. **Weaker retention.** Videos that lose viewers early get recommended less.
3. **Search takes time.** New videos can take a while to appear for their exact title.
4. **Reused or repetitive content.** Channels that mainly reupload other people's material can be limited, especially for monetization.
5. **Age restrictions.** Age-restricted videos aren't shown to signed-out viewers or younger users.
6. **Misleading metadata.** Titles, tags or thumbnails that don't match the video break YouTube's spam policies. Our [YouTube title checker](/tools/youtube-title-checker) scores a title on length, keyword placement, caps and punctuation.

## Shadowban Extensions and "Removal" Services (Use With Caution)

You'll find browser extensions that claim to detect a YouTube comment shadowban, often with a "check" button next to your comments. The most they can do is the private-window test, automated: reload the comments as a logged-out visitor and report whether yours appears.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't tell you why.** Held, filtered and hidden all look the same from outside, and no extension can see YouTube's systems.
- **They can read a lot.** Extensions that read the pages you visit can see far more than your comments.
- **"Shadowban removal" for money is a scam.** Only YouTube can lift its own actions, and only a channel can unhide you.

### If You Use One Anyway

- ✓ Treat the result as a hint, then run the manual test.
- ✗ Never give an extension or website your Google password.
- ✗ Never sign in to YouTube through a third party to "scan" your account.

> **Bottom line:** We don't recommend any specific extension. The manual test takes two minutes and is just as reliable.

## Can You Contact YouTube to Remove a Shadowban?

| Situation | Can YouTube help? | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Strike or warning | Yes | Appeal from the notice in Studio; strikes expire after a period YouTube sets, and the notice says when |
| Lost features or a channel-wide limit | Yes | Use the appeal link and the YouTube Help Center; eligible channels, such as those in the YouTube Partner Program, may reach Creator support from Studio's help menu |
| Comments held or hidden on a channel | No | Message the creator through their listed business email or social links |
| Spam-filtered comments | No form | Fewer links, no repeated text, slower commenting |
| Lower impressions, no notice | Nothing to remove | Work on titles, thumbnails and retention |

## What to Do Next

1. **Studio shows a strike?** Read it, fix what it names, and appeal if it's wrong.
2. **Comments missing on one channel?** That channel's moderation; message the creator or move on.
3. **Comments missing everywhere?** Cut links, repeated text and bursts of comments.
4. **Views down, nothing in Studio?** Work on thumbnails, titles and retention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does YouTube shadowban channels?

YouTube doesn't use the term and doesn't run a hidden site-wide ban. It can issue strikes, remove features, age-restrict videos and filter spam comments, and it tells you about the first three in YouTube Studio and by email.

### Why can't anyone see my YouTube comments?

Your comment is probably held for review, caught by YouTube's spam filter, or you've been hidden on that channel. Test it in a private window sorted by newest, on several channels.

### How do I know if I'm shadowbanned in YouTube live chat?

Ask someone in the same stream whether they can see your messages, then try another channel's stream. Moderators can hide a user or put them in timeout on their own channel only.

### Do YouTube shadowban extensions work?

They can automate a logged-out visibility check for your comments, and that's all. They can't see YouTube's systems or tell you why a comment is missing.

### How do I contact YouTube to remove a shadowban?

Appeal strikes and removals from the notice in YouTube Studio, and use the Help Center or Creator support if your channel is eligible. A channel that has hidden you is the only one that can unhide you.

### How long does a YouTube shadowban last?

There's no set length, because there's no single shadowban. Strikes expire after a period YouTube sets, a channel's hidden users list lasts until that channel removes you, and spam filtering eases once you stop the behavior that triggered it.

### Can a YouTube channel see that it hid me?

Yes. Hidden users appear in the channel's Hidden users list in its YouTube Studio settings.

### Does deleting and reposting a comment help?

Usually not. Repeating the same text can look more like spam, so rewrite it without links instead.
