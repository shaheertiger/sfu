You found a TikTok you want to keep, send to a friend who isn't on the app, or watch on a plane. To download a TikTok video, open it, tap the **Share** arrow and choose **Save video**. It goes to your camera roll (Photos on iPhone, Gallery on Android) with TikTok's watermark and the creator's username on it.

![How to download TikTok videos: tap Share, then Save video, and keep clean copies of your own videos](/images/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos-hero-1200x675.webp)

If there's no Save video button, the creator has turned downloads off. Your **own** videos are easier: you can save them from your profile at any time, and keep a clean copy without the watermark when you post.

This guide covers the steps on iPhone, Android and a computer, how to keep your originals clean, why the watermark-remover sites are a bad idea, and what's fair to do with other people's videos.

## Why Some TikToks Download With a Watermark and Some Won't Download at All

![TikTok download options compared: other people's videos save with a watermark, your own originals can stay clean](/images/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos-options-1200x675.webp)

TikTok controls downloads to keep creators credited. Every saved copy of someone else's video carries the watermark and their username, and creators can switch downloads off completely.

| What you want | How | Watermark? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Someone else's TikTok, to watch later | Share > Save video | Yes |
| Someone else's TikTok, without saving a file | Tap the bookmark (Favorites) | No file |
| Your own posted video | Profile > video > ... > Save video | Yes, in most versions |
| Your own video, clean copy | Turn on **Save to device** when posting, or keep your original edit | No |
| All your videos | Settings > Account > Download your data | Depends on the export |
| A video with downloads turned off | Ask the creator for the file | Their choice |

### What Most People Don't Know

You often don't need a file at all. Tapping the bookmark adds a video to your Favorites, so you can find it again without filling your camera roll. And for your own videos, the cleanest copy isn't on TikTok: it's the file you exported before uploading.

> **Key point:** The watermark is how creators stay credited when videos travel. TikTok's own Save button keeps it on other people's videos on purpose, and you can keep your own videos clean without any outside site.

## How to Download TikTok Videos on iPhone (4 Steps)

![Three steps to download a TikTok on a phone: open the video, tap Share, then tap Save video and wait for it to finish](/images/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos-steps-1200x675.webp)

Labels change between app versions, though the options have stayed similar for years.

### Step 1: Open the Video in the TikTok App

Play the video you want to save.

### Step 2: Tap the Share Arrow

It's on the right side of the screen.

### Step 3: Tap Save Video and Wait

Wait for the progress circle to finish. Leaving the screen too early is the step that makes downloads fail.

### Step 4: Open Photos

The video is in Recents.

The first time, iOS asks for permission to add to Photos. If saving fails, go to **iPhone Settings > TikTok > Photos** and set it to **Add Photos Only** or **Full Access**. You can also use **Live Photo** in the share menu to save a TikTok as a Live Photo for a wallpaper.

> **Pro tip:** Not sure you'll want it in a month? Tap the bookmark instead. It keeps the video in your Favorites without using storage.

## How to Download TikTok Videos on Android

The steps are the same: **Share > Save video**. The file lands in your Gallery or in the **Movies/TikTok** or **DCIM** folder, depending on your phone. If it fails, check that TikTok has storage or media permission in Android settings.

## How to Download TikTok Videos on a Computer

On tiktok.com, open a video, then right-click it or open the **...** menu and look for **Download video**. It appears only when the creator allows downloads, and the file carries the watermark.

## How to Save Your Own TikToks Without a Watermark (3 Clean Methods)

| Method | When to use it | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep your original edit | Every time you edit in CapCut or your phone's editor | Export the final file before you upload |
| Save to device while posting | When you edit inside TikTok | On the post screen, open **More options** and turn on **Save to device** (some versions also offer saving without the watermark) |
| Download your data | To back up everything you've posted | In **Settings and privacy > Account > Download your data**, request an export that includes your videos |

The first method is the one to make a habit. The exported file is your clean master for every other platform.

## Watermark Remover Sites (Use With Caution)

![Checklist of safe ways to get a clean TikTok file, like keeping your original, compared with risky remover sites](/images/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos-remover-sites-1200x675.webp)

Search for TikTok downloads and the top results are sites that promise "no watermark" copies if you paste a link.

### The Real Risks

- **Many are packed with ads**, pop-ups and redirects that are easy to tap by mistake.
- **Some ask for your TikTok login.** Never give a download site your password.
- **They strip creator credit.** Removing the watermark from someone else's video and reposting it is exactly what gets content taken down and accounts penalized.

### If You Use One Anyway

- Never enter your TikTok username or password.
- Don't install extensions or apps they push.
- Only use it on videos you own or have permission to reuse.

> **Bottom line:** For your own videos, the clean file is the one you exported before posting. For other people's videos, the watermark should stay.

## Is It Okay to Download Other People's TikToks? (Fair Use vs Reposting)

Saving a video to watch offline or send to a friend is what the Save button is for. Reposting someone else's video as your own, cropping out the watermark or using it in an ad is different: the creator owns the copyright, and platforms remove reposted content and can penalize the account.

| Fine | Not fine |
| --- | --- |
| Saving to watch offline | Reposting it as your own |
| Sending it to a friend | Cropping out the watermark |
| Using TikTok's Repost or Stitch | Using it in an ad without permission |
| Asking the creator for the file and crediting them | Re-uploading to another platform as yours |

To share a video with your followers, use TikTok's **Repost** button or **Stitch**, or ask the creator for permission and credit them. Our guide on [how to repost on TikTok](/blog/how-to-repost-on-tiktok) covers the in-app way.

## Can't Download a TikTok? 5 Reasons and Fixes

| Problem | Fix | Why it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No Save video button | Ask the creator for the file | They turned downloads off in Settings and privacy > Privacy > Downloads |
| Save is greyed out | Bookmark it instead | The video has licensed music TikTok doesn't let you save in your region |
| You can't see the video | Follow, or ask the creator | The account is private, or the video is Friends only |
| Saving fails partway | Allow photo or storage access in your phone's settings | TikTok lacks permission to save |
| Brand-new video won't save | Try again in a few minutes | It's still processing |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I download a TikTok video to my iPhone camera roll?

Open the video, tap Share, then Save video. It appears in Photos. If it doesn't, allow TikTok to add photos in iPhone Settings.

### Can I download TikTok videos without the watermark?

Your own, yes: keep your original file or turn on Save to device when posting. Other people's videos carry the watermark so the creator stays credited.

### How do I stop people from downloading my TikToks?

Go to Settings and privacy > Privacy > Downloads and turn it off. You can also turn it off per video in the post settings.

### Does the creator know if I download their TikTok?

No notification is sent for a download, though creators can see the number of saves or shares in their analytics.

### Why is there no Save video button?

The creator turned downloads off, the video uses music you can't save in your region, or you can't access it because it's private or Friends only.

### Where do downloaded TikToks go on Android?

To your Gallery, or the Movies/TikTok or DCIM folder, depending on your phone.

### Can I download TikTok videos on a computer?

Yes, when the creator allows it. On tiktok.com, right-click the video or open the ... menu and choose Download video.

### Are TikTok watermark remover sites safe?

Many are full of ads and some ask for your login. Never give them your password, and don't use them to strip credit from other people's videos.
