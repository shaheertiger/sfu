Your posts have a lock icon, people have to request to follow you, and nobody outside your followers can see or repost what you share. To make your Twitter account public, open **Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Audience and tagging** and uncheck (or switch off) **Protect your posts**, then confirm.

![How to make your X account public: turn off Protect your posts, but review your old posts first](/images/blog/how-to-make-twitter-account-public-hero-1200x675.webp)

Your account becomes public right away, and anyone can see your posts, **including the ones you shared while your account was protected**. That last part is what catches people out.

The steps are almost the same on X (formerly Twitter) in a browser and in the mobile app. This guide covers both, what changes once you're public, a checklist to run first, and how to switch back.

## What Changes When Your X Account Goes Public (Protected vs Public)

![A protected X account compared with a public one: approved followers only versus anyone, and no reposts versus reposts and quotes](/images/blog/how-to-make-twitter-account-public-versus-1200x675.webp)

Making your account public changes who can see and interact with what you post. Here's what to expect at the time of writing.

| | Protected account | Public account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who sees your posts | Only approved followers | Anyone on X |
| New followers | Must request, and you approve | Can follow instantly |
| Reposts | Others can't repost your posts | Others can repost and quote you |
| Search | Your posts only appear in your followers' search results | Your posts can appear in X search for anyone |
| Older posts | Visible to followers only | Become public too |
| Replies to non-followers | Only your followers see them | Visible to anyone |

### What Most People Don't Know

Switching to public doesn't only affect future posts. Everything you posted while protected becomes visible to anyone. Your name, handle, bio, profile photo and follower counts were already visible while protected; it's the posts that change.

> **Key point:** Before you flip the switch, scroll through your profile and delete anything you wouldn't want strangers, employers or clients to read. Once it's public, people can screenshot or quote it.

## How to Make Your X Account Public on the Web (6 Steps)

![Steps to make an X account public: open Settings and privacy, go to Privacy and safety, then Audience and tagging, and turn off Protect your posts](/images/blog/how-to-make-twitter-account-public-steps-1200x675.webp)

X renames menu items from time to time, so if a label looks slightly different on your screen, look for the closest match. The names below are the ones in use at the time of writing.

### Step 1: Sign In at x.com

Go to x.com and sign in to the account you want to make public.

### Step 2: Open Settings and Privacy

In the left-hand menu, click **More** (the three-dot icon), then **Settings and privacy**.

### Step 3: Click Privacy and Safety

It's in the list of settings sections.

### Step 4: Open Audience and Tagging

Under "Your X activity", click **Audience and tagging**. On some versions it's called **Audience, media and tagging**; it's the same place.

### Step 5: Uncheck Protect Your Posts

Uncheck the box next to **Protect your posts**.

### Step 6: Confirm

Confirm in the pop-up if X asks. The lock icon next to your name disappears.

> **Pro tip:** Open your profile in a private browser window to see how it looks to people who don't follow you. Depending on X's current rules for logged-out visitors, you may need to sign in with a second account to see the full public view.

## How to Make Your X Account Public in the iPhone or Android App

The mobile apps follow the same structure.

1. Open the X app and tap your **profile picture** in the top-left corner.
2. Tap **Settings and privacy**.
3. Tap **Privacy and safety**.
4. Tap **Audience and tagging**.
5. Turn off the **Protect your posts** toggle.
6. Confirm if prompted.

| Where you are | Path to the setting | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web (x.com) | More → Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Audience and tagging | Uncheck "Protect your posts" and confirm |
| iPhone app | Profile picture → Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Audience and tagging | Switch off "Protect your posts" |
| Android app | Profile picture → Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Audience and tagging | Switch off "Protect your posts" |

If you can't find the setting, use the search bar at the top of the Settings screen and type "protect". It usually takes you straight there.

## Before You Go Public: A 6-Point Checklist

![Checklist before making an X account public: review old posts, handle follow requests and tidy your bio, and what to avoid](/images/blog/how-to-make-twitter-account-public-checklist-1200x675.webp)

Going public is a good moment to tidy up so first impressions are strong.

1. **Review old posts and media.** Delete anything you don't want public.
2. **Handle pending follow requests.** Approve the ones you want first. X's help center explains how pending requests are handled when you unprotect, and the behavior has changed before, so it's safest to deal with them first.
3. **Rewrite your bio.** You have 160 characters to say who you are and why people should follow. The free [X bio generator](/tools/x-bio-generator) gives ideas that fit.
4. **Check your display name and handle.** Display names can be up to 50 characters and handles 4 to 15 characters (letters, numbers and underscores).
5. **Update your profile and header images**, and pin a post that explains what you share.
6. **Check what personal details are visible**, such as your location or birth date, in your profile and Your account settings.

## Search, Replies and Strangers: How to Stay in Control Once You're Public

Public posts can show up in X search, on hashtag pages and in recommendations for people who don't follow you. That's why brands and creators keep accounts public. It also means replies, quote posts and mentions from people you don't know. You can still control a lot:

- **Limit who can reply** to a post when you write it: everyone, accounts you follow, verified accounts, or only accounts you mention.
- **Mute or block** accounts that bother you.
- **Turn on quality filters** in notification settings to reduce low-quality mentions.
- **Restrict who can tag you in photos** in the same Audience and tagging section.
- **Turn off direct messages from strangers** under Privacy and safety → Direct messages.

## How to Make Your X Account Private Again

You can switch back at any time:

1. Go to **Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Audience and tagging**.
2. Check or switch on **Protect your posts**.

Your posts are then visible only to your current followers, and new followers have to request access. Anything people already saw, screenshotted or quoted while you were public may still exist elsewhere; protecting your account later doesn't remove copies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make my Twitter account public?

Go to Settings and privacy → Privacy and safety → Audience and tagging and uncheck "Protect your posts". The same path works on x.com and in the iPhone and Android apps.

### Will my old protected posts become public?

Yes. When you turn off "Protect your posts", the posts you shared while protected become visible to anyone. Review and delete anything you don't want public first.

### Why can't I find "Protect your posts" on X?

The section may be labeled "Audience and tagging" or "Audience, media and tagging". Update the app, try x.com, or search "protect" from the Settings screen.

### Can people still see my profile if my account is protected?

Yes. Your name, handle, bio, profile photo and follower counts are still visible. Only your posts and some activity are hidden from people who don't follow you.

### Does making my X account public notify my followers?

No. X doesn't announce the change. Followers will just notice the lock icon is gone, and new people can follow without a request.

### How long does it take for my account to become public?

It's immediate once you confirm. Your posts may take a little time to show up in X search for other people.

### What happens to pending follow requests when I go public?

X's help center explains how they're handled, and the behavior has changed before. Approve the requests you want before you switch.

### Can I make individual posts public on X?

No. Protection applies to the whole account. To limit a single post, restrict who can reply to it instead.
