Your last three Reels reached a fraction of what they used to, and you're wondering whether Instagram has quietly buried you. The fastest answer is in your own settings. Instagram doesn't use the word "shadowban", but it does stop recommending some posts and accounts to people who don't follow you, and it says so on your **Account Status** page. Open Settings, search for "Account Status", and check whether your account is eligible to be recommended.

![Am I shadowbanned on Instagram? Check Account Status first, then test hashtags, search and non-follower reach](/images/blog/instagram-shadowban-hero-1200x675.webp)

If it is, and nothing is listed, a drop in reach is more likely timing, format or topic than a restriction. That's the part most "shadowban" advice skips.

This guide covers what a shadowban on Instagram actually is, how to read Account Status, a five-step test you can run with a friend, the common causes, and an eight-step recovery plan.

## What a Shadowban on Instagram Actually Is

![Signs of normal Instagram reach compared with signs of an account that isn't being recommended](/images/blog/instagram-shadowban-signs-1200x675.webp)

People use "shadowbanned on Instagram" for a few different experiences:

| What you notice | Normal account | Possibly not recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Hashtag results | Your new post appears for non-followers | Missing from the tag for non-followers |
| Reach to non-followers | Some on most posts | Close to zero on every post |
| Username search | You appear when someone types your handle | You don't come up |
| Reach overall | Swings post to post | Falls sharply overnight across every post |

### What Most People Don't Know

Instagram's own explanation is about **recommendations**. Your followers can still see your posts in their feed and Stories, but Instagram has recommendation guidelines for what it suggests to everyone else in Explore, Reels, search and hashtag pages. Content that's borderline, sensitive, low quality or misleading can stay up and simply not be recommended. If an account posts content like that repeatedly, the whole account can become ineligible for recommendation for a while.

> **Key point:** A shadowban on Instagram is less a hidden ban and more a quiet loss of reach to non-followers, and Account Status tells you whether it's happening.

## How to Check Account Status on Instagram (the Only Official Answer)

![How to check Instagram Account Status: open Settings and activity, search Account Status, read recommendation eligibility](/images/blog/instagram-shadowban-account-status-1200x675.webp)

At the time of writing, go to **Profile → Menu → Settings and activity → Account Status**. If the menu has moved, use the search bar at the top of Settings. Menu labels change between app versions; if you can't find it, update the app and search again.

| What Account Status shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| Whether your account can be recommended | If yes, Instagram isn't limiting your reach to non-followers |
| Content that was removed or can't be recommended | The post and the guideline it relates to |
| Features you can't use | Going live or sending messages, if restricted |

If Account Status says your account is eligible to be recommended and lists nothing, Instagram isn't limiting you. If it shows a problem, it usually names the post and gives you a way to request a review. This is the only check that comes from Instagram itself, so it outranks every other test.

## Instagram Shadowban Test: 5 Steps With a Friend (15 Minutes)

If Account Status is clear but reach still feels wrong, run this test with a friend who doesn't follow you.

### Step 1: Pick a Niche Hashtag

Choose a small, specific hashtag you used on a new post, not a giant one like #love where thousands of posts arrive every minute. This is where most tests go wrong: on a huge tag, your post is buried in seconds whether you're restricted or not.

### Step 2: Ask a Non-Follower to Search It

Have them search the hashtag and, if their app shows a Recent tab or recent results, look for your post. If it's there, hashtag visibility is fine.

### Step 3: Search Your Username

Have the same friend type your exact username into search. Your account should appear. Very new accounts can take a little while.

### Step 4: Check Non-Follower Reach in Insights

Open Insights on your last few posts and Reels. Under **Accounts reached**, look at the split between followers and non-followers. A healthy public account usually reaches some non-followers; close to zero on every post is a warning sign.

### Step 5: Compare Like With Like

Line up similar posts, same format and topic, and see whether reach dropped by more than half overnight across several of them. One weak post is normal.

> **Pro tip:** Note the date and what each test showed. If you request a review or retest in two weeks, you'll know exactly what changed.

## Banned and Broken Hashtags: The Hidden Reach Killer

Some hashtags are restricted. When you open one, Instagram may show only a few top posts, or a message saying recent posts are hidden because of reports that content may not meet the Community Guidelines. Using one doesn't ban your account, but the post won't be found through that tag, and repeatedly using hashtags tied to spam or adult content can weigh on recommendations.

Before you post, tap each hashtag you plan to use and check that it shows recent content normally. Drop any that don't. Hashtags that sound harmless can be restricted because spammers took them over.

## What Causes an Instagram Shadowban? 7 Common Triggers

![Behaviors that keep Instagram accounts recommended compared with behaviors that trigger restrictions](/images/blog/instagram-shadowban-causes-1200x675.webp)

| Trigger | Red flag | Safe approach |
|---|---|---|
| Hashtags | Banned or broken tags, or the same block of unrelated tags on every post | A handful of relevant, checked tags |
| Automation | Apps that log in with your password to auto-like, auto-follow, auto-comment or DM; bot-run engagement pods | Only schedule your own posts through the official API |
| Fake engagement | Bought followers or likes | Grow slowly with real posts |
| Recycled content | Reposting other people's videos, or Reels with another app's watermark | Original content; Instagram has said it favors it |

| Spam-like behavior | Mass following and unfollowing, copy-paste comments, many DMs to strangers | A normal, human pace |
| Forgotten apps | Old follower trackers or growth tools still connected | Remove anything you don't use |

## How to Fix an Instagram Shadowban: 8-Step Recovery Plan

![Four moves to recover Instagram reach: read Account Status, request a review, remove the trigger, then post normally](/images/blog/instagram-shadowban-recovery-1200x675.webp)

1. **Read Account Status.** Find out what, if anything, Instagram flagged.
2. **Request a review if it's a mistake.** On Account Status, open the flagged post or restriction and use the option to request a review or disagree with the decision. Keep your explanation short and factual. Reviews take varying amounts of time and aren't guaranteed.
3. **Delete or edit clear violations.** If a post does break the guidelines, remove it.
4. **Disconnect suspicious apps.** At the time of writing, search Settings for "Apps and websites" to see what's connected, and remove anything you don't recognize or no longer use. Change your password if a growth app ever had it.
5. **Stop automation and fake engagement.** Leave pods, cancel follower services and stop mass follow or comment sprees.
6. **Clean up your hashtags.** Use a handful of relevant, specific tags and check each one.
7. **Post original content at a steady pace.** Keep posting normally rather than going silent or flooding your feed. Many people find recommendations return within a couple of weeks once the cause is gone, but Instagram doesn't publish a timeline.
8. **Re-run the test.** Check non-follower reach in Insights after a week or two.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Am I shadowbanned on Instagram?

Check Settings → Account Status. If it says your account is eligible to be recommended and lists no removed or restricted content, you're not shadowbanned; lower reach is more likely format, timing or topic. If it shows a problem, it names the cause and offers a review.

### How do you know if you're shadowbanned on Instagram?

Look at Account Status first, then test: ask a non-follower to search a niche hashtag from your new post and your username, and check the non-follower share of Accounts reached in Insights. Near-zero non-follower reach across several posts is the clearest sign.

### Is there a reliable Instagram shadowban test?

Account Status is the reliable one, because it's Instagram's own answer. The hashtag, search and Insights checks support it. Our [shadowban checker](/tools/shadowban-checker) guides you through all of them without needing your login.

### How long does an Instagram shadowban last?

Instagram doesn't publish a duration. People commonly report reach returning after a couple of weeks once they remove the cause, but it varies. A successful review can restore recommendation eligibility sooner.

### Can using a scheduler get me shadowbanned on Instagram?

Not by itself. Schedulers that post through Instagram's official API are approved. Restrictions come from tools that log in with your password to automate likes, follows or comments, and from spammy or rule-breaking content.

### Do shadowban checker apps work for Instagram?

Apps that claim to scan your account can't see Instagram's systems, and many ask for your password, which risks your account. Use Account Status and the manual tests instead.

### Can I pay someone to remove an Instagram shadowban?

No. Only Instagram can change your Account Status, and reviews are free. Anyone charging for removal, or asking for your login or two-factor codes, is running a scam.

### Do banned hashtags get your account shadowbanned?

Using one doesn't ban your account, but the post won't be found through that tag. Repeatedly using hashtags tied to spam or adult content can weigh on recommendations.
