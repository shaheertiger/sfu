If you're asking "am I shadowbanned on Instagram?", the fastest answer is in your own settings. Instagram doesn't use the word "shadowban", but it does stop recommending some posts and accounts to people who don't follow you, and it says so on your **Account Status** page. Open Settings, search for "Account Status", and check whether your account is eligible to be recommended. If it is, and nothing is listed, a drop in reach is more likely timing, format or topic than a restriction.

This guide shows how to know if you're shadowbanned on Instagram, how to run a simple test, what causes it and how to recover.

## What a shadowban on Instagram actually is

People use "shadowbanned on Instagram" for a few different experiences:

- Posts stop appearing under hashtags for people who don't follow you.
- Reels and posts reach almost no one outside your followers.
- Your account doesn't come up when someone searches your username.
- Reach falls sharply overnight across every post.

Instagram's own explanation is about **recommendations**. Your followers can still see your posts in their feed and Stories, but Instagram has recommendation guidelines for what it suggests to everyone else in Explore, Reels, search and hashtag pages. Content that's borderline, sensitive, low quality or misleading can stay up and simply not be recommended. If an account posts content like that repeatedly, the whole account can become ineligible for recommendation for a while.

So a shadowban on Instagram is less a hidden ban and more a quiet loss of reach to non-followers.

## Am I shadowbanned on Instagram? Check Account Status first

At the time of writing, go to **Profile → Menu → Settings and activity → Account Status**. If the menu has moved, use the search bar at the top of Settings. The page shows:

- **Whether your account can be recommended** to people who don't follow you.
- **Content that was removed** or that can't be recommended, with the guideline it relates to.
- **Features you can't use**, such as going live or sending messages, if any are restricted.

If Account Status says your account is eligible to be recommended and lists nothing, Instagram isn't limiting you. If it shows a problem, it usually names the post and gives you a way to request a review. This is the only check that comes from Instagram itself, so it outranks every other test.

If you can't find it, update the app and search Settings again.

## Instagram shadowban test: step by step

If Account Status is clear but reach still feels wrong, run this test. It takes about 15 minutes with a friend who doesn't follow you.

1. **Pick a niche hashtag.** Choose a small, specific hashtag you used on a new post, not a giant one like #love where thousands of posts arrive every minute.
2. **Ask a non-follower to search it.** Have them search the hashtag and, if their app shows a Recent tab or recent results, look for your post. If it's there, hashtag visibility is fine.
3. **Search your username.** Have the same friend type your exact username into search. Your account should appear. Very new accounts can take a little while.
4. **Check non-follower reach in Insights.** Open Insights on your last few posts and Reels. Under **Accounts reached**, look at the split between followers and non-followers. A healthy public account usually reaches some non-followers; close to zero on every post is a warning sign.
5. **Compare like with like.** Line up similar posts, same format and topic, and see whether reach dropped by more than half overnight across several of them. One weak post is normal.

## A note on banned and broken hashtags

Some hashtags are restricted. When you open one, Instagram may show only a few top posts, or a message saying recent posts are hidden because of reports that content may not meet the Community Guidelines. Using one of these doesn't ban your account, but the post won't be found through that tag, and repeatedly using hashtags tied to spam or adult content can weigh on recommendations.

Before you post, tap each hashtag you plan to use and check that it shows recent content normally. Drop any that don't. Hashtags that sound harmless can be restricted because spammers took them over.

## What causes an Instagram shadowban?

These are the most common reasons an account or post stops being recommended:

- **Banned or broken hashtags**, or stuffing every post with the same block of unrelated tags.
- **Automation and bots.** Apps that log in with your password to auto-like, auto-follow, auto-comment or send DMs break Instagram's terms and are a classic trigger. So are "engagement pods" run by bots.
- **Buying followers or engagement.** Fake followers don't engage, which drags your reach down, and Instagram removes fake accounts and fake activity it detects.
- **Recycled content.** Reposting other people's videos, or Reels with another app's watermark, tends not to be recommended. Instagram has said it favors original content over reposts.
- **Guideline violations.** Nudity, violence, hate speech, misinformation and regulated goods can get posts removed or kept out of recommendations, even when they stay up.
- **Spam-like behavior.** Mass following and unfollowing, copy-paste comments, and lots of DMs to strangers in a short time.
- **Third-party apps you forgot about.** Old follower trackers or growth tools still connected to your account can act on your behalf.

Scheduling isn't one of them. Publishing your own posts through Instagram's official API is a normal, approved way to post; our guide to [social media posting bots](/blog/social-media-posting-bots) explains where the line is. Schedule For You publishes your own posts and Reels to Instagram at the times you choose. It doesn't like, follow or comment on your behalf, and it won't fix a restriction by itself.

## How to recover and request a review

1. **Read Account Status.** Find out what, if anything, Instagram flagged.
2. **Request a review if it's a mistake.** On Account Status, open the flagged post or restriction and use the option to request a review or disagree with the decision. Keep your explanation short and factual. Reviews take varying amounts of time and aren't guaranteed.
3. **Delete or edit clear violations.** If a post does break the guidelines, remove it.
4. **Disconnect suspicious apps.** At the time of writing, search Settings for "Apps and websites" to see what's connected, and remove anything you don't recognize or no longer use. Change your password if a growth app ever had it.
5. **Stop automation and fake engagement.** Leave pods, cancel follower services and stop mass follow or comment sprees.
6. **Clean up your hashtags.** Use a handful of relevant, specific tags and check each one.
7. **Post original content at a steady pace.** Keep posting normally rather than going silent or flooding your feed. Many people find recommendations return within a couple of weeks once the cause is gone, but Instagram doesn't publish a timeline.
8. **Re-run the test.** Check non-follower reach in Insights after a week or two.

### Avoid "unshadowban" scams

Anyone offering to remove your Instagram shadowban for a fee, or asking for your login or two-factor codes, is running a scam. Only Instagram can change your Account Status, and reviews are free. ## Frequently asked questions

### Am I shadowbanned on Instagram?

Check Settings → Account Status. If it says your account is eligible to be recommended and lists no removed or restricted content, you're not shadowbanned; lower reach is more likely format, timing or topic. If it shows a problem, it names the cause and offers a review.

### How do you know if you're shadowbanned on Instagram?

Look at Account Status first, then test: ask a non-follower to search a niche hashtag from your new post and your username, and check the non-follower share of Accounts reached in Insights. Near-zero non-follower reach across several posts is the clearest sign.

### Is there a reliable Instagram shadowban test?

Account Status is the reliable one, because it's Instagram's own answer. The hashtag, search and Insights checks support it. Our [shadowban checker](/tools/shadowban-checker) guides you through all of them without needing your login.

### How long does an Instagram shadowban last?

Instagram doesn't publish a duration. People commonly report reach returning after a couple of weeks once they remove the cause, but it varies. A successful review can restore recommendation eligibility sooner.

### Can using a scheduler get me shadowbanned on Instagram?

Not by itself. Schedulers that post through Instagram's official API are approved. Restrictions come from tools that log in with your password to automate likes, follows or comments, and from spammy or rule-breaking content.

### Do shadowban checker apps work for Instagram?

Apps that claim to scan your account can't see Instagram's systems, and many ask for your password, which risks your account. Use Account Status and the manual tests instead.
