TikTok is worth it for most businesses and creators who can make short vertical video on a regular schedule. It reaches somewhere around 1.9 to 2 billion people a month worldwide and well over 130 million in the US by third-party estimates (TikTok doesn't publish audited figures), and its For You feed shows videos from accounts people don't follow, so a new account can reach strangers from its first post. It's less worth it if you can't produce video, sell mainly to older B2B buyers, or need a guaranteed return from the first month.

Here's how to decide.

## Who gets the most from TikTok

- **Consumer brands with something to show.** Food, beauty, fashion, home, fitness, gadgets: anything that looks good in 15 to 60 seconds.
- **Shops and ecommerce.** TikTok Shop lets people buy without leaving the app. See [how to sell on TikTok Shop](/blog/how-to-sell-on-tiktok-shop).
- **Local businesses with personality.** Restaurants, salons, gyms and trades do well with behind-the-scenes clips, and location tags help nearby discovery.
- **Educators and experts.** Short how-to videos travel far; "explain it in 30 seconds" is a format TikTok rewards.
- **Creators building an audience.** Reach is easier to get than on Instagram or YouTube for new accounts.

## Who might skip it, for now

- **Teams with no time for video.** TikTok runs on consistent posting. One video a month won't tell you much.
- **Niche B2B sellers.** If your buyers are procurement teams, LinkedIn is usually the better first channel.
- **Regulated industries** that can't approve fast, informal content.
- **Anyone who needs guaranteed reach.** A video can get 200 views or 200,000. That variance is normal.

## What TikTok costs you

| Cost | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Money | Free to post; ads optional |
| Time per video | 20 minutes to 2 hours, depending on editing |
| Posting rhythm | 3 to 5 videos a week to learn what works |
| Time to judge | About 60 to 90 days |
| Gear | A phone, decent light and a cheap microphone |

The biggest cost is ideas, not equipment. Repurposing helps: one vertical video can go out as a TikTok, an Instagram Reel and a YouTube Short.

## The cheapest way to find out

Make TikTok part of a workflow you already run instead of a new one. Film vertical, post the same video to TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and compare which platform responds. Schedule For You publishes [TikTok videos and photo posts](/tiktok) from the same queue as Instagram, YouTube and the rest, with the caption tailored for each platform and the privacy set per post. Plans start at $19 a month for 15 connected accounts.

A fair 60-day test:

1. **Post 3 to 5 times a week** at the times your audience is active. Our guide to the [best time to post on TikTok](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) has a starting schedule.
2. **Hook in the first two seconds.** Show the result, then explain it.
3. **Try three formats:** a tutorial, a behind-the-scenes clip and a photo slideshow ([how to make one](/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok)).
4. **Judge by saves, shares and profile visits,** not just views or followers.
5. **Double down on whatever format gets shares.**

If you see flat numbers after a few weeks, check [why your TikTok isn't getting views](/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views) before deciding it doesn't work.

## Is TikTok worth it for creators who want to earn?

It can be, but pay from views alone is modest. The Creator Rewards Program pays for longer videos once you meet its thresholds, and most creators earn more from brand deals, LIVE gifts, TikTok Shop affiliate commissions and their own products. See [how to make money on TikTok](/blog/how-to-make-money-on-tiktok) and [what TikTok pays per 1 million views](/blog/how-much-money-do-you-get-on-tiktok-for-1-million-views).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is TikTok worth it for a small business?
Usually, if you can post a few short videos a week and your customers are consumers. It's one of the few platforms where a brand-new account can reach people who don't follow it.

### Is TikTok worth it in 2026, or is it too late?
It isn't too late. The For You feed still shows videos from small accounts, and new niches open up every year. Competition is higher than in 2020, so consistency and clear hooks matter more.

### Do I need to show my face on TikTok?
No. Product demos, screen recordings, voiceovers and photo slideshows all work. Faces tend to help with trust, but they aren't required.

### Should I post the same video on TikTok, Reels and Shorts?
Yes, it's the most efficient way to test. Remove other platforms' watermarks, tailor the caption, and post the clean file to each.
