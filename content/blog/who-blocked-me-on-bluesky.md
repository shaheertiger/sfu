Someone's replies in a thread now say "Blocked post", or you tap a profile you used to follow and it's empty. You're fairly sure you've been blocked, and you'd like to know by whom.

Bluesky doesn't show a "who blocked me" list in its app, but blocks on Bluesky are public records, so you can find out. Open the person's profile: if they blocked you, you'll see a notice that you're blocked and can't see their posts. To see everyone who blocked you, third-party lookup tools such as **Clearsky** read the public block data and list it.

![Who blocked me on Bluesky? Check the profile for a blocked notice, or use a lookup tool that reads public block data](/images/blog/who-blocked-me-on-bluesky-hero-1200x675.webp)

That surprises people coming from X or Instagram, where blocks are private. On Bluesky they're public by design, while mutes stay private.

This guide covers how to tell if one person blocked you, how to see everyone who did, what a block actually does, how to remove a follower, and how to see who you've blocked.

## Why Bluesky Blocks Are Public (and Mutes Aren't)

Because blocks are stored in each user's public data repository, anyone can read them through the AT Protocol. Bluesky chose this design on purpose: blocks must be visible to every server so they're enforced across the network.

![Bluesky blocks compared with mutes: blocks are public data, mutes are private](/images/blog/who-blocked-me-on-bluesky-public-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

A mute is the private option. If someone is simply quiet in your feed, they may have muted you, which can't be detected by anyone. A block, by contrast, can be seen by anyone who looks it up, including the person you blocked.

> **Key point:** If you don't want someone to be able to find out, mute instead of block. Blocks on Bluesky are public data.

## How to Tell If One Person Blocked You on Bluesky: 3 Signs

![Three signs someone blocked you on Bluesky](/images/blog/who-blocked-me-on-bluesky-signs-1200x675.webp)

### Sign 1: Their Profile Says You're Blocked

It shows **You are blocked** (or "This user has blocked you") and no posts. Wording changes between app versions.

### Sign 2: Their Replies Show as Blocked Posts

In threads, their replies appear as **Blocked post** instead of the text.

### Sign 3: You Can't Interact

You can't follow, reply to, quote or message them.

### What Isn't a Block

This is where people jump to conclusions. If the profile says **Account not found** or **This account has been deactivated**, they deleted or deactivated it, or changed their handle. That's not a block.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "You are blocked" on their profile | They blocked you |
| "Blocked post" in a thread | One of you blocked the other |
| "Account not found" | Deleted, or the handle changed |
| "This account has been deactivated" | Deactivated |
| They just seem quiet in your feed | Possibly a mute, which you can't detect |

> **Pro tip:** Before assuming a block, search for the person's display name. If they changed their handle, their profile will turn up under the new one.

## How to See Everyone Who Blocked You on Bluesky

**Clearsky** (clearsky.app) is the best-known lookup. Enter your handle and it shows:

- Accounts that block you
- Accounts you block
- Moderation lists you've been added to

You don't need to sign in to search a handle.

## What a Block Does on Bluesky: 5 Effects

| After a block | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Seeing posts | Neither of you sees the other's posts while signed in |
| Replies and quotes | Both are stopped. Existing ones show as blocked |
| Following | The follow stops working in both directions |
| DMs | You can't message each other |
| Visibility of the block | Public data, visible through tools |

Blocks are enforced while people are logged in to Bluesky. Public posts can still be seen logged out or through other apps, which is why blocking isn't the same as being private. See [how to make your Bluesky account private](/blog/how-to-make-bluesky-account-private) for what else you can do.

## How to Remove Followers on Bluesky (There's No Button)

There's no **Remove follower** button on Bluesky at the time of writing. It's been a long-standing feature request.

![How to deal with an unwanted follower on Bluesky: block, mute or add them to a moderation list](/images/blog/who-blocked-me-on-bluesky-remove-1200x675.webp)

- **Block them.** A block stops the follow from working: they won't see your posts in their feed while signed in. This is the only reliable method.
- **Don't count on a soft block.** On X, blocking then unblocking removes a follower. On Bluesky, the follow record is stored in the follower's own account, so after you unblock, the follow can come back. Keep the block if you want them gone.
- **Mute them** if you just don't want to see them, without them knowing.
- **Add them to a moderation list** if you're dealing with many accounts, such as a wave of spam followers.

| Option | Stops them seeing you while signed in | Private | Reversible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block | Yes | No, blocks are public | Yes, but the follow can return |
| Soft block (block, then unblock) | No, the follow can come back | No | Not useful on Bluesky |
| Mute | No | Yes | Yes |

## How to See Who You Blocked

Go to **Settings → Moderation → Blocked accounts**. You can unblock anyone from there. Mutes are under **Muted accounts**, and lists you subscribe to are under **Moderation lists**. Menu labels change between app versions.

## Block Lookup Tools (Use With Caution)

Clearsky and similar tools are third-party sites, not part of Bluesky.

### The Real Risks

- **Password requests.** You don't need to sign in to search a handle. Never give a lookup tool your password.
- **Arguments.** Seeing a block doesn't mean you should act on it. Looking someone up to argue about a block usually ends in more blocks.
- **Look-alike sites.** Check you're on the site you meant to visit before typing anything.

> **Bottom line:** Lookup tools read public data, so they never need your login. Use them to understand, not to confront.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Bluesky notify you when someone blocks you?

No. There's no notification. You find out when you visit their profile or use a lookup tool.

### Can someone see that I blocked them on Bluesky?

Yes, potentially. Blocks are public data, so tools like Clearsky can show it. Mutes are private.

### Can a blocked user still see my posts?

Not while signed in to Bluesky. Public posts can still be read logged out or through other apps.

### Is there a way to remove a follower without blocking?

Not at the time of writing. Blocking is the only way to stop a follower, and unblocking may let the follow return.

### Why are Bluesky blocks public?

Blocks are stored in each user's public data repository so that every server on the network can enforce them.

### Does a soft block work on Bluesky?

Not reliably. The follow record lives in the follower's account, so the follow can come back after you unblock.

### Is Clearsky safe to use?

Searching a handle doesn't require signing in. Treat it like any outside site and never give it your password.

### How do I see my muted accounts?

Under Settings, Moderation, Muted accounts.
