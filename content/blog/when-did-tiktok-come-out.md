You remember TikTok arriving around 2018, but you've seen 2016 and 2017 quoted too. TikTok came out in **September 2017**, when ByteDance launched it outside China. **ByteDance** had launched the Chinese version, **Douyin**, a year earlier in September 2016.

![When did TikTok come out? Douyin in 2016, TikTok in September 2017, the Musical.ly merger in August 2018](/images/blog/when-did-tiktok-come-out-hero-1200x675.webp)

The reason so many people say 2018 is the Musical.ly merger. TikTok became popular in the US after **2 August 2018**, when ByteDance merged it with Musical.ly, the lip-sync app it had bought, and moved every Musical.ly account into TikTok.

This guide covers the full timeline, why the dates get mixed up, how TikTok grew so fast, and where it stands in 2026.

## Why Everyone Remembers a Different Launch Date

There are three dates that all sound like "TikTok came out", and each one is true for a different audience.

![Three TikTok launch dates compared: Douyin in 2016, TikTok in 2017 and the Musical.ly merger in 2018](/images/blog/when-did-tiktok-come-out-three-dates-1200x675.webp)

| Date | What launched | Who remembers it |
|---|---|---|
| September 2016 | Douyin, in China | People who follow ByteDance's history |
| September 2017 | TikTok, outside China | Early users, mostly in Asia |
| 2 August 2018 | Musical.ly becomes TikTok | Most American and European users |

### What Most People Don't Know

In August 2018 the Musical.ly app simply updated into TikTok, keeping users' accounts and videos. Millions of people woke up with TikTok on their phones without downloading anything, which is why 2018 feels like the launch for so many.

> **Key point:** TikTok launched in September 2017. August 2018 is when it arrived on most American phones, through the Musical.ly merger.

## When Was TikTok Made? (2016 to 2017)

TikTok was built in 2016 and 2017 by ByteDance, based on Douyin, which was developed in about 200 days and launched in September 2016. TikTok was designed as the international version, with separate servers and content from Douyin. ByteDance's founder, Zhang Yiming, wanted a short-video app that could grow outside China, which Douyin, built for the Chinese market, couldn't.

## Why Did TikTok and Musical.ly Merge?

Musical.ly was already big with teenagers in the US and Europe, while TikTok was strong in Asia. Buying Musical.ly gave ByteDance an instant Western audience. Merging the apps meant one global app instead of two competing ones.

> **Pro tip:** If you're citing TikTok's age in an article or presentation, say "launched internationally in September 2017". It's the accurate date, and it avoids the 2016 and 2018 mix-ups.

## How TikTok Grew So Fast: 3 Reasons

1. **The For You feed.** Instead of showing posts from people you follow, it learns from what you watch and serves videos from anyone. A brand-new account could get millions of views, which pulled in creators fast.
2. **The Musical.ly audience.** The 2018 merger brought a ready-made Western user base.
3. **2020 lockdowns.** They accelerated everything, and TikTok has been one of the most downloaded apps in the world in most years since.

## How Many Videos Are on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a total, but it's in the many billions. Its quarterly transparency reports give a sense of scale: in recent years TikTok has removed well over 100 million videos a quarter for breaking its rules, and those are a small share of everything uploaded. TikTok says it has more than 1 billion monthly users worldwide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When did TikTok come out in the US?

TikTok was available in the US from 2017, but most Americans first used it after Musical.ly merged into it on 2 August 2018.

### Who created TikTok?

ByteDance, the company founded by Zhang Yiming in 2012. Its team built Douyin first and then TikTok as the international version.

### Is TikTok the same as Musical.ly?

TikTok absorbed Musical.ly in 2018. Musical.ly accounts became TikTok accounts and the Musical.ly app stopped existing.

### Is TikTok the same as Douyin?

No. They look similar and are both made by ByteDance, but they're separate apps with separate content and rules. Douyin is only for China; TikTok isn't available there.

### How old is TikTok?

TikTok launched in September 2017, so it turned nine in 2026. Douyin is a year older.

### When did TikTok reach 1 billion users?

TikTok passed 1 billion monthly users in September 2021.

### How long were TikTok videos at launch?

15 seconds. The limit later grew to 60 seconds, then 3 and 10 minutes.
