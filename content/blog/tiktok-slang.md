TikTok slang moves fast, and most of it is short because comments and captions are short. The terms people search for most are: **PMO** (put me on, or pisses me off), **ASL** (as hell), **IB** (inspired by), **TS** (this sh\*t), **moots** (mutual followers), **LMR** (like my recent) and **FYP** (For You page). The full list, with examples, is below.

Meanings shift with context, and many acronyms have a second meaning outside TikTok. When in doubt, read the comments under the video: the replies usually make it clear.

## What does PMO mean on TikTok?

PMO has two common meanings. **"Put me on"** asks someone to share or recommend something: "pmo to the song in this video." **"Pisses me off"** vents annoyance: "ts pmo." Which one is meant is usually obvious from the tone.

## What does ASL mean on TikTok?

On TikTok, ASL almost always means **"as hell"**: "tired asl," "funny asl." Elsewhere it can mean American Sign Language, or the old chat-room question "age, sex, location?"

## What does IB mean on TikTok?

IB means **"inspired by."** Creators write "ib: @username" in a caption to credit the person whose idea, format or dance they copied. It's often seen next to "dc" (dance credit). Under student content, #ib can also refer to the International Baccalaureate.

## What does OAT mean on TikTok?

OAT usually means **"of all time,"** a casual cut-down of GOAT (greatest of all time): "best album oat." You'll see it most in gaming and sports comments.

## What does SYFM mean on TikTok?

SYFM stands for **"shut your f\*\*\*ing mouth."** It's blunt and often used jokingly between friends, sometimes as a reaction to a viral audio clip of someone yelling it. Between strangers it reads as hostile.

## What does TEST mean on TikTok?

TEST isn't a settled acronym on TikTok. Most of the time it's literal: someone is testing a feature, a sound or a post. In gym and fitness content, "test" is short for testosterone. If it's in all capitals under a trend, it's usually an inside joke tied to that trend, and the comments will explain it.

## What does TS mean on TikTok?

TS usually means **"this sh\*t,"** as in "ts so funny" or "ts pmo" (this pisses me off). Under music content, TS can also mean Taylor Swift.

## What does moot or moots mean on TikTok?

A moot is a **mutual**: someone who follows you and whom you follow back. "Moots only" means a post or live is meant for mutual followers. The term came from Twitter and spread to TikTok.

## What does LMR mean on TikTok?

LMR means **"like my recent,"** a request to like the person's newest post, usually in comments or messages to boost engagement.

## What does OD mean on TikTok?

OD comes from "overdose" but on TikTok means **overdoing it** or simply **very**: "this is OD funny" (extremely funny), "stop OD'ing" (stop being dramatic).

## What does bop mean on TikTok?

A bop is a **catchy, fun song**: "this sound is a bop." More recently some creators use "bop" as an insult for a person, mostly aimed at girls, so it can be negative depending on who's talking about whom.

## What does BMF mean on TikTok?

BMF most often means **"bad mother\*\*\*er,"** praise for someone confident or impressive. It's also the name of a TV series and a rap track, so music comments may mean those instead.

## What does EYP mean on TikTok?

EYP is a **sexual acronym** used to get past TikTok's filters. It shows up in crude jokes and meme comments. It isn't something to use in brand or professional content.

## What does HB mean on TikTok?

HB usually means **"happy birthday."** In a dating or appearance context it can also be shorthand for "hot boy."

## What does MYF mean on TikTok?

MYF most often means **"my fault,"** a quick apology. Some people use it for "mind your f\*\*\*ing business," so check the tone.

## What does SSA mean on TikTok?

On TikTok, SSA is usually **"ass" spelled backwards**, used to dodge word filters. In news or finance content it can still mean the US Social Security Administration.

## What does WLW mean on TikTok?

WLW stands for **"women loving women,"** an umbrella term for women attracted to women, used in LGBTQ+ content and tags.

## What does pink font mean on TikTok?

Pink font is a **caption trend** that started in late 2025. Creators write romantic, longing or vulnerable thoughts in pink text: crushes, situationships, people who won't text back. It's the softer sibling of the **yellow font** trend, which is used for raw, introspective confessions. It isn't a TikTok feature, just a text colour people agreed on.

## What does FYP mean on TikTok?

FYP is the **For You page**, TikTok's main recommendation feed. People add #fyp to captions hoping to land there, though the hashtag itself doesn't get you onto it. Our guide to [going viral on TikTok](/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok) covers what does.

## Using slang in brand content

Slang lands when it sounds like you and badly when it sounds borrowed. A few rules for businesses and creators:

- Use terms your audience already uses in your comments.
- Skip anything crude or filter-dodging (EYP, SSA, SYFM) in brand posts.
- Don't rely on a term in the hook if half your audience won't know it.
- Check meanings before posting; they change quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does "ts pmo" mean on TikTok?
Roughly "this stuff pisses me off" (TS is the cruder version of "this stuff"). It's used to complain about something in the video or in life.

### What does "ib" mean in a TikTok caption?
"Inspired by." It credits the creator whose idea or format the video is based on, usually followed by their handle.

### What are moots on TikTok?
Mutual followers: accounts that follow you and that you follow back.

### What does pink font mean versus yellow font?
Pink font is for romantic or longing thoughts; yellow font is for raw, personal confessions. Both are creator-made trends, not TikTok features.

### Why does TikTok slang spell words backwards or use letters?
To fit short comments and to avoid TikTok's automatic filters, which can hide or limit posts with certain words.
