You can't sign in to YouTube, or someone has taken over your channel and renamed it. **To recover a YouTube account, recover the Google account it belongs to.** Go to Google's account recovery page at [accounts.google.com/signin/recovery](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery), enter your email or phone number and follow the steps. If your channel was hacked, secure the Google account first, then use the hacked-channel troubleshooter in [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube) to report the takeover and restore your channel.

![How to recover a YouTube account: recover the Google account, secure it, then restore the channel through YouTube Help](/images/blog/how-to-recover-youtube-account-hero-1200x675.webp)

Most people start in the wrong place. They search YouTube's settings or message "YouTube support" on social media, when the account that's actually locked is a Google account.

This guide covers the order that works: recover the Google account, secure it, restore the channel, sort out Brand Accounts, and avoid the scammers who target people in exactly this situation. Everything uses official Google and YouTube routes only.

## Why YouTube Recovery Starts With Google (Your Situation in One Table)

![Your YouTube recovery path depends on the situation: forgot password, lost phone, hacked, Brand Account or terminated](/images/blog/how-to-recover-youtube-account-situations-1200x675.webp)

Every YouTube channel belongs to a Google account. YouTube can help undo what happened to the channel, but it can't let you in to the Google account.

| Situation | What to do first |
| --- | --- |
| Forgot your password | Google account recovery |
| Lost your phone or 2-Step Verification method | Google account recovery, using a backup code or "Try another way" |
| Hacked, but you can still sign in | Change your password and run Google's Security Checkup, then use YouTube's hacked-channel help |
| Hacked, and you're locked out | Google account recovery, then YouTube's hacked-channel help |
| Channel is on a Brand Account and you lost access | Recover a Google account that owns it, or ask another owner to re-add you |
| Channel was terminated | Appeal through YouTube's official appeal process |
| Google account was deleted | Try Google account recovery quickly; recent deletions can sometimes be restored |

### What Most People Don't Know

Where and how you try matters. Google's automated recovery is the only route for most personal accounts, and it relies on signals it recognizes: a device you've used before, your usual location and network, and your answers. Trying from a borrowed phone on hotel Wi-Fi makes it harder for Google to confirm it's you.

> **Key point:** No one at YouTube can bypass Google's account recovery. Anyone who says they can, for a fee, is running a scam.

## Step 1: Recover Your Google Account (7 Steps)

![Steps to recover the Google account behind your YouTube channel from a familiar device, then secure it and restore the channel](/images/blog/how-to-recover-youtube-account-recover-1200x675.webp)

1. Go to [accounts.google.com/signin/recovery](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery).
2. Enter the email address or phone number linked to the account.
3. Answer the questions as fully as you can. If you're asked for the last password you remember, give your best guess rather than skipping it.
4. Use a device and location you've signed in from before, such as your usual laptop on your home Wi-Fi.
5. Enter the codes sent to your recovery phone or recovery email.
6. If you don't have access to an option, click **Try another way** to see the alternatives.
7. Once you're in, create a new, strong password you haven't used anywhere else.

> **Pro tip:** If recovery fails, don't retry ten times in a row. Wait a while and try again from a familiar device, with as many accurate answers as possible.

### If You Lost Your 2-Step Verification Phone

Use a backup code if you saved one, or another second step you set up, such as a security key or a prompt on another signed-in device. If none of those are available, go through the account recovery flow above.

## Step 2: Secure Your Google Account (6 Checks)

Getting back in isn't the end. If someone else had access, close every door they might have left open.

1. **Run Google's Security Checkup** at myaccount.google.com and fix every warning.
2. **Sign out of unknown devices** under **Security → Your devices**.
3. **Check recovery info.** Remove any phone number or email you don't recognize, and add your own.
4. **Review third-party access** under **Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services** and remove anything you don't recognize.
5. **Check Gmail** for forwarding rules, filters or delegated access you didn't create.
6. **Turn on 2-Step Verification**, ideally with a passkey or security key, which resists phishing better than text messages.

Google changes these menu names from time to time, so use the search box in your Google Account if a label is different.

## Step 3: Restore Your YouTube Channel After a Hack

Once the Google account is secure, deal with what happened to the channel itself. Hijacked channels are often renamed, used to stream scams, or have their videos deleted.

1. Go to [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube) and search for help with a hacked channel.
2. Follow YouTube's hacked-channel troubleshooter. It asks what happened and guides you through securing the account and reporting the takeover.
3. If prompted, submit the request to YouTube while signed in with the recovered account.
4. YouTube may be able to reverse changes the attacker made, such as a changed channel name, uploaded videos or removals. What can be restored depends on the case.

Meanwhile, check YouTube Studio for:

- **Videos uploaded or deleted** by the attacker. If videos are missing, see [how to find deleted YouTube videos](/blog/how-to-find-deleted-youtube-videos).
- **Channel name, handle, picture and links** that were changed.
- **Live streams** scheduled by someone else.
- **Channel permissions** (Settings → Permissions) for people you didn't invite.
- **Your AdSense account**, if you're monetized, for changed payment details. Our guide to [when YouTube starts paying you](/blog/when-does-youtube-start-paying-you) explains how AdSense payments work.

If the channel was terminated because of what the attacker posted, mention the hack when you go through the hacked-channel flow or YouTube's appeal process, so the takeover can be taken into account.

## Brand Account Channels: How to Recover Ownership

Many channels sit on a Brand Account, which can have several owners and managers, each signing in with their own Google account.

| Your role | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Removed as an owner or manager | Ask the primary owner or another owner to re-add you in YouTube Studio permissions, or at myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts |
| Every legitimate owner was removed | Recover the Google account that was the primary owner (Step 1), then use YouTube's hacked-channel help (Step 3) |
| You're the primary owner and back in | Review everyone with access and remove anyone you don't recognize |

Google applies a waiting period before a newly added owner can become primary owner, which helps protect against quick takeovers. Going forward, give editors and managers their own access through YouTube Studio permissions instead of sharing a password, so you can remove one person without changing anything for everyone else.

## Deleted, Hidden and Terminated Channels: What Can Come Back

| Status | Can it come back? |
| --- | --- |
| Deleted Google account | Sometimes, if it was deleted recently; after a period of time deletion is permanent |
| Hidden channel | Yes: sign in and create your channel again with the same account |
| Deleted channel | Not normally; deleting a channel permanently removes its content |
| Terminated channel | Only through YouTube's official appeal; YouTube explains the reason by email and in YouTube Help |

## Paid "Recovery Services" and YouTube Support Impersonators (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to recover a YouTube account compared with recovery scams that target locked-out creators](/images/blog/how-to-recover-youtube-account-scams-1200x675.webp)

People who lose a channel are prime targets. Within hours of posting "I got hacked", you may get DMs and comments from "recovery experts", "YouTube insiders" or hackers for hire.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't do what they promise.** No outside company can bypass Google's recovery process.
- **They take money and disappear**, or ask for more once you've paid.
- **They ask for your password or codes**, and use them to take the account again.
- **Fake support accounts** copy YouTube's name and logo. Official help lives at support.google.com/youtube.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Never pay a recovery service.
- Never share your password, verification codes or backup codes. Google and YouTube staff won't ask for them.
- Ignore DMs and comments that claim to be from YouTube support.
- Don't sign in through links in unexpected emails. Type the address yourself.

> **Bottom line:** Timing varies for every recovery and appeal, and no one can guarantee a result or a date. Anyone who does is lying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I recover my YouTube account?

Recover the Google account it belongs to at accounts.google.com/signin/recovery. If the channel was hacked, secure the account, then use YouTube Help's hacked-channel troubleshooter.

### Can I recover my YouTube account without my email or phone number?

Sometimes. Go through Google account recovery and answer as accurately as you can from a familiar device and location. Without a recovery phone or email, it's harder for Google to confirm ownership, and recovery isn't guaranteed.

### How long does YouTube account recovery take?

It varies. Google account recovery can be quick if you have your recovery details, but can take longer if Google needs more checks. Neither Google nor YouTube guarantees a timeline.

### Can YouTube support recover my channel for me?

YouTube can help restore a hacked channel through its hacked-channel help flow, but you must first recover the Google account yourself. No one at YouTube can bypass Google's account recovery.

### Can I recover a deleted YouTube channel?

A hidden channel can be restored, and a recently deleted Google account may be recoverable. A channel you permanently deleted can't normally be brought back.

### Someone offered to recover my channel for a fee. Is it legitimate?

No. Paid recovery services and "account hackers for hire" are scams that often steal money, passwords or the channel itself. Use only Google account recovery and YouTube Help.

### My channel was terminated while it was hacked. What now?

Mention the hack in the hacked-channel flow or YouTube's appeal process, so the takeover can be taken into account.

### Why did my scheduled YouTube uploads stop after recovery?

Changing your password and signing out other sessions disconnects apps that publish for you. Reconnect YouTube in each app you use.
