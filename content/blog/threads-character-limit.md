You've written a post for Threads and the counter has turned red. **The Threads character limit is 500 characters per post**, including spaces, emoji, links and hashtags. A longer thought becomes a thread of 500-character posts, each one a reply to the one before.

![Threads character limit 2026: 500 characters per post, up to 20 photos or videos, and longer posts become threads](/images/blog/threads-character-limit-hero-1200x675.webp)

Media is more generous than text: up to 20 photos or videos in a carousel, and videos up to five minutes. What trips people up is that links count at full length, and that a thread only works if the first post makes sense on its own.

This guide covers every Threads limit in one table, how links and tags count, how to write a thread that gets opened, how Threads compares with X, Bluesky and LinkedIn, the best time to post, and how to schedule.

## Why 500 Characters Feels Different on Threads

![What counts toward the 500-character Threads limit: spaces, emoji, full-length links and the topic tag](/images/blog/threads-character-limit-counts-1200x675.webp)

500 characters is roughly 80 to 100 words: enough for a full thought, a short story or a list of tips, but not an essay. It sits between X's 280 and LinkedIn's 3,000, which is why posts copied from either one rarely fit as they are.

| What | Limit (at the time of writing) |
| --- | --- |
| Post text | 500 characters |
| Photos or videos per post | Up to 20 in a carousel |
| Video length | Up to 5 minutes |
| Links | Allowed in the text; the first one gets a preview card |
| Topic tag | One per post (Threads' version of hashtags) |
| Bio | 150 characters |
| Alt text | Supported on images |

Threads is run by Meta and changes often; its help center has the current numbers.

### What Most People Don't Know

**Links count toward the 500 characters at their full length.** There's no automatic shortening like X's, so a long tracking URL can eat a fifth of your post. Threads also uses a **single topic tag per post** instead of many hashtags: a `#` in the text is turned into a tag only for that one term.

> **Key point:** Write for 500 characters including the link. If the URL is long, use a shorter link or put it in a reply.

## How to Write a Thread Longer Than 500 Characters (4 Steps)

![Four steps to write a Threads thread longer than 500 characters, with a strong first post](/images/blog/threads-character-limit-thread-steps-1200x675.webp)

Write past 500 characters and Threads prompts you to continue in a reply. Each part is a separate post that readers open from the first one.

### Step 1: Write the Whole Thing First

Draft it in one go, then decide where the breaks fall.

### Step 2: Make the Opening Post Stand Alone

This is the step people get wrong. The first post is all most people see in their feed, so it needs to make sense, and make a promise, without the rest.

### Step 3: Break at Natural Points

One idea per post. Media can go on any part.

### Step 4: End With a Question or Next Step

Replies in the first hours help a post travel, so give people something to answer.

> **Pro tip:** Read only the first post aloud. If it doesn't make you want to open the rest, rewrite it before you post.

## How to Cut a Post Down to 500 Characters (5 Quick Edits)

When a post is only a little over, a thread is overkill. These edits usually get you under the limit without losing the point:

1. **Cut the warm-up.** Openers like "I've been thinking a lot lately about" add characters and delay the point. Start with the point.
2. **Shorten the link.** A long tracking URL can be the biggest thing in the post. Use a shorter link, or move it to a reply.
3. **Drop the extra tags.** Threads only turns one term into a topic tag, so a row of hashtags is just text using up space.
4. **Swap phrases for words.** "In order to" becomes "to", "at this point in time" becomes "now".
5. **Move detail into a carousel.** A list of steps can live on images, with the post text as the hook.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counter is red by a few characters | Cut the opener or a filler phrase | Small edits usually cover a small overrun |
| The link takes up half the post | Use a shorter link or post it in a reply | Links count at full length |
| Tags won't all work | Keep one topic tag | Threads tags one term per post |
| The post is really three ideas | Make it a thread, one idea per part | Each part gets its own 500 characters |
| The text is a list of steps | Put the steps on carousel images | Up to 20 images per post |

## Best Time to Post on Threads (Weekday Mornings and Early Evenings)

![When to post on Threads: weekday mornings and early evenings, with midweek strongest](/images/blog/threads-character-limit-timing-1200x675.webp)

Threads inherits Instagram's audience, so activity peaks on weekday mornings and early evenings in your followers' time zones, and midweek tends to do better than weekends. The For You feed favors replies and reposts in the first hours, so post when you can answer. Two or three posts a day at those times is a workable rhythm for most accounts.

## How to Schedule Threads Posts: Native vs a Scheduler

Threads added native scheduling in 2025 for posts created in the app on desktop and mobile. Start a post, open the **…** menu in the composer, choose **Schedule** and pick a time up to 75 days ahead. Scheduled posts are listed from your profile. Menu labels change between app versions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the character limit on Threads?

500 characters per post, including links, spaces and emoji.

### How many photos can you post on Threads?

Up to 20 photos or videos in one post, shown as a carousel.

### How long can a Threads video be?

Up to 5 minutes at the time of writing.

### Do links count toward the Threads character limit?

Yes, at their full length. The first link in a post gets a preview card.

### How many hashtags can you use on Threads?

Threads uses one topic tag per post. A `#` in the text becomes a tag only for that one term.

### Can you schedule posts on Threads?

Yes. Threads has a built-in scheduler in its composer, up to 75 days ahead, and third-party schedulers connect through Meta's API to schedule alongside other platforms.

### What is the Threads bio character limit?

150 characters.

### Do links in Threads posts hurt reach?

Threads has said links aren't penalized. The first link gets a preview card; keep the post itself readable without it.
