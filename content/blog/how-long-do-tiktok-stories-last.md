You posted a Story this morning and want to know when it disappears, or whether that clip from yesterday is gone for good.

TikTok Stories last **24 hours**. Once you post a Story, it stays visible on your profile and in your followers' feeds for one day, then disappears from public view automatically. You can delete it sooner, and at the time of writing TikTok also lets you keep Stories on your profile for longer by adding them to **Highlights**.

![How long do TikTok Stories last? 24 hours, unless you add them to a Highlight or save the video](/images/blog/how-long-do-tiktok-stories-last-hero-1200x675.webp)

The confusing parts are everything around that number. The clip length has been reported differently over the years, an expired Story isn't the same as a deleted one, and viewing a Story isn't anonymous.

This guide covers how long a Story stays up and how long each clip can be, what happens when it expires, who can see it and who viewed it, how to save one in time, and how TikTok Stories compare with Instagram's.

### What Most People Don't Know

Every Story runs on its own 24-hour clock, counted from the moment it's posted. If you post three Stories in a day, the first one disappears first. TikTok has used the same window since it first tested Stories: [TechCrunch reported in 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/17/tiktok-stories-expanded-pilot-feature/) that Stories "last for 24 hours" during the expanded pilot.

You can't extend the timer on a live Story. To keep it visible, add it to a Highlight, post it again as a new Story, or publish it as a regular TikTok video.

> **Key point:** An expired Story isn't deleted. TikTok keeps it privately for you, which is what Highlights draw from. A Story you delete yourself is a different matter, so save it first.

## How Long Can a TikTok Story Clip Be? (15 Seconds at Launch)

TikTok launched Stories with clips of up to 15 seconds, and many guides still quote that limit. Some 2026 guides report longer Story clips, and TikTok doesn't put one clear number in front of everyone.

![How to check how long your TikTok Story clips can be](/images/blog/how-long-do-tiktok-stories-last-length-1200x675.webp)

The reliable way to check is the **Story** option in the TikTok camera: it shows the recording lengths available on your account. If a clip is longer than your limit, trim it or split it into several Stories.

## How to Post a TikTok Story in 5 Steps

Menu labels change between app versions, so if a button looks different, look for the same option nearby.

### Step 1: Tap the + Button

It's at the bottom of the screen.

### Step 2: Choose Story, Not Post

Pick **Story** from the options at the bottom of the camera. This is the step people get wrong: if you leave it on the regular post option, the video goes to your profile grid permanently instead of disappearing after 24 hours.

### Step 3: Record or Upload

Record a video, take a photo, or upload one from your phone.

### Step 4: Add Text, Stickers or Sounds

Add text, stickers, sounds or effects if you want.

### Step 5: Check the Audience, Then Post

Check the audience setting, then tap the button to post to your Story.

> **Pro tip:** Check the audience and comment settings on every Story, especially personal ones. Don't rely on whatever you chose last time.

### Where People Watch Stories

- **On a profile.** An account with an active Story shows a colored ring around its profile picture. Tap the picture to watch.
- **In feeds.** Stories from accounts you follow can appear in the For You and Following feeds, usually marked as a Story.
- **In the inbox.** Depending on your app version, Stories from people you follow may also show at the top of your inbox.

Yes, Stories still exist. TikTok has reshaped them several times since its 2021 test, but at the time of writing they're still part of the app.

## What Happens When a TikTok Story Expires? Archive, Highlights, Deleted

After 24 hours, the Story is no longer visible to other people. It isn't simply gone from your account, though.

| What happened | Can others see it? | Can you get it back? |
|---|---|---|
| The Story expired after 24 hours | No | Yes, from your private Story archive |
| You added it to a Highlight | Yes, on your profile | It's still there |
| You deleted it yourself | No | Don't count on it |
| You saved the video to your phone | Not on TikTok | Yes, in your camera roll |

- **Highlights.** At the time of writing, TikTok's Help Center describes Story Highlights, which let you pick older Stories from your Story archive and keep them on your profile beyond 24 hours. Highlights follow the visibility setting of the original Story.
- **The archive.** Expired Stories are kept privately for you. Where it sits in the menus can vary by app version, so if you can't find it, start the Highlight flow from your profile and you'll be offered your past Stories.
- **Deleted Stories.** If you delete a Story yourself, add it to a Highlight or save the video first.

## Who Can See Your TikTok Story, and Who Viewed It?

Who sees a Story depends on two things: whether your account is public or private, and the audience you choose when you post.

![Who can see a TikTok Story on a public account compared with a private account](/images/blog/how-long-do-tiktok-stories-last-audience-1200x675.webp)

- **Public accounts.** A Story can be shown to your followers and may also appear to other people in their feeds, depending on the audience setting you pick.
- **Private accounts.** Only followers you've approved can see your Stories.
- **Per-Story settings.** When you post, you can limit who can see the Story and whether people can comment.

### How to See Who Viewed Your Story

1. Go to your **profile** and tap your profile picture while your Story is live.
2. Look at the bottom of the screen for the view count.
3. Tap it to see the list of viewers, along with any comments or reactions.

Early test versions showed only a view count, not names, as [TechCrunch noted at the time](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/17/tiktok-stories-expanded-pilot-feature/). If you only see a number, your app version or region may differ. Check the list while the Story is live, and don't expect it to stay available after it expires.

Unlike Instagram, comments on a TikTok Story can be public. Direct message replies land in your inbox, and your message settings control who can send them.

## How to Save a TikTok Story Before It Expires (3 Ways)

1. **Save the video to your phone.** Open your Story from your profile, tap the **…** (more) or share button and choose **Save video** (sometimes labeled **Download**). The file goes to your camera roll.
2. **Keep the original file.** If you edited the video outside TikTok, keep the original: it's higher quality than a re-download. If you edit inside TikTok, save a copy before posting. Our guide on [how to see TikTok drafts](/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-drafts) explains why drafts live on your phone.
3. **Add it to a Highlight.** If the goal is to keep the Story visible rather than own a file, add it to a Highlight. That's useful for FAQs, product demos and event recaps. A Story that did well can also be re-uploaded as a regular TikTok video with a full caption.

## "Anonymous Story Viewer" Sites (Use With Caution)

Search for a way to watch TikTok Stories without being seen and you'll find sites that promise anonymous viewing.

### The Real Risks

- **They aren't TikTok services.** TikTok's Help Center notes that people can see that you viewed their Story even if your account is private or you've turned off profile view history. Sites claiming otherwise work outside TikTok's rules.
- **Logins and data.** Some ask you to sign in with TikTok or enter a username, then fill the page with ads or redirects.
- **Unreliable results.** They can break or show the wrong content whenever TikTok changes.

> **Bottom line:** Assume anyone can see that you watched their Story. If you don't want to show up in a viewer list, don't open the Story.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do TikTok Stories last?

24 hours from the moment you post them. After that, they disappear from your profile and feeds. You can keep a Story on your profile longer by adding it to a Highlight, where that feature is available.

### How long are TikTok Stories allowed to be?

TikTok launched Stories with clips of up to 15 seconds. The limit has been reported differently since, so open the Story option in the TikTok camera to see the lengths available on your account.

### Can people see that I viewed their TikTok Story?

Yes. Viewing a Story isn't anonymous. TikTok's Help Center says people can see that you viewed their Story even if your account is private or you've turned off profile view history.

### Can I get back a TikTok Story after 24 hours?

Usually, yes. Expired Stories are kept privately for you, and you can add them to a Highlight. A Story you deleted yourself may not be recoverable.

### Do multiple Stories expire at the same time?

No. Each Story has its own 24-hour clock from when it was posted, so they disappear in the order you posted them.

### Can I extend a TikTok Story past 24 hours?

Not the live timer. Add it to a Highlight, repost it as a new Story, or publish it as a regular video.

### What size should a TikTok Story be?

1080×1920 pixels, a 9:16 vertical frame, fills the screen.

### Does Schedule For You post TikTok Stories?

No. It schedules TikTok videos and photo posts. Post Stories directly in the TikTok app.
