Your Following feed is full of accounts you don't care about any more, and you want them gone without any awkwardness. To unfollow someone on TikTok, open their profile and tap **Following** (or **Friends** if you follow each other), then confirm **Unfollow** if asked. To unfollow several people, go to your own profile, tap your **Following** count and tap the button next to each name.

![How to unfollow on TikTok: tap Following on their profile or in your Following list; they aren't notified](/images/blog/how-to-unfollow-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

**TikTok doesn't notify people when you unfollow them**, and it has no built-in way to unfollow everyone at once. That second part is where people get into trouble, with apps that promise bulk unfollows.

This guide covers both ways to unfollow, how to clear a big list safely, how to unfriend and remove followers, what the other person sees, and gentler ways to clean up your feed.

## Why Unfollowing on TikTok Is Quiet (What the Other Person Sees)

![What happens when you unfollow someone on TikTok, and what doesn't](/images/blog/how-to-unfollow-on-tiktok-what-happens-1200x675.webp)

- **They aren't notified.** They might notice if they check their followers list.
- **Their videos leave your Following feed**, but can still show up in For You.
- **Your Friends status ends** if you followed each other, which can affect messaging if they only allow messages from friends.
- **You can follow again at any time.**

### What Most People Don't Know

Unfollowing and re-following the same accounts repeatedly, or unfollowing hundreds of accounts in minutes, can trigger follow limits. TikTok treats very fast follow and unfollow activity as possibly automated, and may temporarily stop you following or unfollowing anyone.

> **Key point:** Unfollowing is private and reversible. Unfollowing fast is what causes problems, so pace a big clean-up over several sessions.

## How to Unfollow Someone on TikTok: 2 Ways

![Two ways to unfollow on TikTok: from their profile or from your Following list](/images/blog/how-to-unfollow-on-tiktok-two-ways-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

### Way 1: From Their Profile

1. Search for the account or tap their name on a video.
2. Tap the **Following** or **Friends** button under their bio.
3. Confirm **Unfollow** if TikTok asks.

### Way 2: From Your Following List (Faster for Clean-Ups)

1. Go to **Profile** and tap the **Following** number.
2. Find the account. Use the search bar at the top for long lists.
3. Tap **Following** or **Friends** next to their name so it changes to **Follow**.

> **Pro tip:** In your Following list, the search bar is the quickest way to find one account among hundreds. Type a few letters of the username.

### On a Computer

On tiktok.com, open your profile, click **Following**, and click the button next to each account, or click **Following** on their profile.

## Can You Unfollow Everyone on TikTok at Once? (No, Here Are Your Options)

No, not in the TikTok app at the time of writing. There's no "unfollow all" or select-many option.

| Method | Speed | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tap through your Following list | A few hundred per session | None, if you pace it |
| Third-party "mass unfollow" apps or extensions | Fast | They need your login or account access, can break TikTok's Terms of Service and can get your account limited |
| Start a new account | Instant | You lose your followers, videos and username |

If you're clearing out a big list, unfollow in batches of a few dozen with breaks in between.

### A Safe Clean-Up Plan for a Big Following List

1. **Session 1:** unfollow accounts that no longer post.
2. **Session 2, another day:** unfollow accounts whose topics you've lost interest in.
3. **Session 3:** review the rest and keep the ones you'd miss.
4. **Between sessions:** take breaks, and stop if TikTok shows a limit warning.

## How to Unfriend Someone on TikTok (and Remove Them as a Follower)

On TikTok, **Friends** are people you follow who also follow you back. To unfriend someone, unfollow them using either method above. They'll stay as one of your followers unless you also remove them:

1. Go to **Profile** and tap **Followers**.
2. Tap the three dots (or **Remove**) next to their name and confirm.

| You want to | Do this | They can still |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stop seeing their posts in Following | Unfollow | Follow you and see your posts |
| End the Friends status | Unfollow | Follow you |
| Stop them following you | Remove them as a follower | Follow you again, if your account is public |
| Stop all contact | [Block them](/blog/how-to-block-someone-on-tiktok) | Nothing on your account |

## Clean Up Your Feed Without Unfollowing (3 Softer Options)

![Softer ways to clean up a TikTok feed without unfollowing anyone](/images/blog/how-to-unfollow-on-tiktok-softer-1200x675.webp)

If you'd rather not unfollow, there are gentler options:

- Long-press a video and tap **Not interested**.
- Add keyword filters under **Settings and privacy → Content preferences**.
- Turn off post or LIVE notifications for an account from the bell icon on their profile, if you've switched them on.

## Mass-Unfollow Apps and Browser Extensions (Use With Caution)

They promise to clear hundreds of accounts in one click. TikTok doesn't offer that, so the demand is real.

### The Real Risks

- **They need your login or account access**, which gives them control of your account.
- **They can break TikTok's Terms of Service.**
- **Fast automated unfollowing can get your account limited**, sometimes from following or unfollowing anyone at all.

### If You're Tempted

- Never give your TikTok password to an app that promises bulk unfollows.
- Check **Manage devices** in Security and remove anything you don't recognize.
- Change your password if you've already used one.

> **Bottom line:** A few sessions of tapping is slower, but it's the only way that doesn't put your account at risk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TikTok notify someone when you unfollow them?

No. TikTok doesn't send a notification, though they can see it if they look through their followers list.

### Can you unfollow everyone on TikTok at once?

No. There's no unfollow-all option in the app at the time of writing. Tap through your Following list in batches, and avoid third-party apps that ask for your login.

### Why can't I unfollow someone on TikTok?

Usually it's a temporary limit after lots of follow and unfollow actions, a weak connection or an app glitch. Wait a few hours, update the app and try again.

### Is there a limit on how many people you can unfollow on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish one, but very fast unfollowing can trigger a temporary block. Pace yourself.

### How do I unfollow on TikTok on a computer?

On tiktok.com, open your profile, click Following, and click the button next to each account, or click Following on their profile.

### How do I unfriend someone on TikTok?

Unfollow them. Friends are mutual follows, so unfollowing ends it. To stop them following you too, remove them from your Followers list.

### Will they still see my videos if I unfollow them?

Yes, if your account is public. Unfollowing only changes what you see. Remove them as a follower or block them to change what they see.

### Why can't I follow someone again after unfollowing?

You may have hit a temporary follow limit. See [why you can't follow people on TikTok](/blog/why-cant-i-follow-people-on-tiktok) for the usual causes.
