Someone keeps showing up in your comments or your messages on TikTok, and you want them gone.

To block someone on TikTok, open their profile, tap the **share arrow** (or the **three dots**) in the top corner, then tap **Block** and confirm. They aren't told, they can no longer see your videos or message you, and you won't see theirs. You can also block from a comment or a chat, or block up to 100 commenters in one go.

![How to block someone on TikTok: open their profile, tap the share arrow or three dots, then tap Block](/images/blog/how-to-block-someone-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

It isn't always obvious because TikTok tucks Block inside the share menu rather than showing a button, and it moves menus between versions, so the labels below may differ slightly in your app. This guide covers every way to block, what blocking really does (and doesn't), how to unblock, lighter options, and how to block the TikTok app itself on an iPhone.

## What Happens When You Block Someone on TikTok (and What Doesn't)

Blocking cuts the connection between two accounts in both directions. It doesn't make your public videos invisible to the whole internet.

![What blocking on TikTok does and doesn't do](/images/blog/how-to-block-someone-on-tiktok-what-happens-1200x675.webp)

| Blocking does | Blocking doesn't |
| --- | --- |
| Stops them seeing your profile, videos, likes and comments while signed in to that account | Stop them viewing public videos while logged out or on another account |
| Stops them messaging you, commenting, duetting or stitching | Erase screenshots, reposts or downloads they already made |
| Removes them from your followers, and you from theirs | Notify them |
| Hides their videos from you | Delete old messages (those stay until you delete the chat) |

If they look for you, your profile usually shows as unavailable or "Account not found".

### What Most People Don't Know

Blocking is tied to one account. Someone determined can still see your public videos from a second account or while logged out. If that's a concern, switch your account to private as well, so only followers you approve can see your videos. Not sure whether someone has blocked you? See [how to know if someone blocked you on TikTok](/blog/how-to-know-if-someone-blocked-you-on-tiktok).

> **Key point:** Blocking stops contact from one account. For real privacy, pair it with a private account and comment controls.

## How to Block Someone on TikTok From Their Profile (3 Steps)

![Three steps to block someone on TikTok from their profile](/images/blog/how-to-block-someone-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Their Profile

Search for the account, or tap their name on a video or comment.

### Step 2: Tap the Share Arrow, Not the Follow Button

On their profile, tap the **share arrow** or **three dots** at the top right. This is where people get stuck: Block isn't on the profile itself, it's inside this menu.

### Step 3: Tap Block and Confirm

Tap **Block**, then **Block** again to confirm. The button on their profile now reads **Unblock**, which is how you know it worked.

> **Pro tip:** If someone is breaking TikTok's rules, tap **Report** in the same menu before you block. Once blocked, they're harder to find again.

## How to Block on TikTok From Comments, Chats and LIVE

| Where you are | How to block |
| --- | --- |
| A comment on your video | Press and hold the comment, choose **Manage multiple comments** (or tap the pencil icon), select it, tap **More**, then **Block accounts** |
| Many comments at once | Same menu: select up to 100 comments, then block all of those accounts together |
| A direct message | Open the chat, tap the **three dots** or their name at the top, then **Block** |
| A live stream you're hosting | Tap the viewer's name in the chat or viewer list and choose **Block** |

### How to Block up to 100 Accounts at Once

Bulk blocking only works on people who have commented on your videos. Press and hold a comment, choose **Manage multiple comments**, tick up to 100, then tap **More** and **Block accounts**. There's no way to paste in a list of usernames, so anyone else has to be blocked one profile at a time.

## How to Unblock Someone on TikTok (4 Steps)

1. Go to your profile and tap the **menu** (three lines) at the top right.
2. Tap **Settings and privacy**, then **Privacy**.
3. Tap **Blocked accounts**.
4. Tap **Unblock** next to the person.

You can also visit their profile and tap **Unblock**. Unblocking doesn't restore the follow: if you were following each other before, you'll both need to follow again.

## Block or Restrict? 4 Lighter Options to Try First

Blocking is the right call for harassment, spam and anyone you never want to hear from. For people who are just annoying, TikTok has softer tools.

![Block someone on TikTok or try lighter options such as comment filters and removing a follower](/images/blog/how-to-block-someone-on-tiktok-options-1200x675.webp)

| Option | What it does | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restrict comments | In Settings and privacy, then Privacy, then Comments, choose who can comment and add keyword filters | Waves of rude comments |
| Remove a follower | Open your follower list, tap the three dots next to someone and remove them | Someone you don't want following, without a block |
| Not interested | Press and hold a video on your For You feed to see less from that creator | Content you don't want to see |
| Report | Report the account or video from the same menu as Block | Rule-breaking behaviour |
| Block | Cuts contact in both directions | Harassment, spam, persistent contact |

## How to Block the TikTok App on an iPhone (Screen Time)

If you want to block the TikTok app itself, for yourself or a child, use the iPhone's **Screen Time** settings. Labels can change between iOS versions.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Screen Time**.
2. Tap **App Limits**, then **Add Limit**.
3. Choose **Social**, tick **TikTok**, and set the limit to one minute a day.
4. Turn on **Block at End of Limit** so it can't be extended without the Screen Time passcode.

Set a Screen Time passcode the child doesn't know, or the limit can simply be ignored. To stop the app being installed again, go to **Content & Privacy Restrictions**, then **iTunes & App Store Purchases**, and set **Installing Apps** to **Don't Allow**. For teens who keep the app, TikTok's own **Family Pairing** lets a parent set screen-time limits and restrict DMs from their phone.

## Mass-Block Apps and Shared Block Lists (Use With Caution)

![TikTok's own blocking tools compared with risky mass-block apps that ask for your password](/images/blog/how-to-block-someone-on-tiktok-caution-1200x675.webp)

If you're dealing with a pile-on, third-party apps and websites that promise to block hundreds of accounts for you can look tempting.

### The Real Risks

- **They want your login.** Tools like this usually work by asking for your TikTok password or login session, which puts your account at risk.
- **Automated actions can look like spam** and may get your own account limited.
- **Shared block lists block people you've never met**, based on someone else's judgement.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Use TikTok's own bulk block from comments first (up to 100 at once).
- Tighten comment settings and keyword filters.
- Never give an app your TikTok password.
- Report serious harassment to TikTok so it can act.

> **Bottom line:** TikTok's own tools are slower but safe. Anything that needs your password to "help" isn't worth it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TikTok tell someone when you block them?

No. TikTok doesn't send a notification. They may notice because your profile and videos disappear for them and they can no longer message you.

### Can a blocked person see my TikTok videos?

Not from the account you blocked. They could still see public videos while logged out or on a different account, which is why some people also make their account private.

### How many people can you block on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a limit for blocking profiles one by one. From comments, you can block up to 100 accounts in one action.

### If I unblock someone, will they follow me again?

No. Blocking removes the follow in both directions, and unblocking doesn't bring it back. They'd have to follow you again.

### Can I block someone on TikTok without them knowing?

Yes. There's no alert, and blocking is private. The only signs on their side are that your profile looks unavailable and they can't contact you.

### Can I block someone who hasn't commented on my videos in bulk?

No. Bulk blocking only works on commenters. Anyone else has to be blocked from their profile, one at a time.

### Do messages disappear when I block someone?

No. Old messages stay in your inbox until you delete the chat.

### How do I see who I've blocked on TikTok?

Go to **Settings and privacy**, then **Privacy**, then **Blocked accounts**.
