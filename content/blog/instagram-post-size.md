You've exported a photo for Instagram and the app crops off the top of someone's head, or the post looks tiny in the feed. **The best Instagram post size is 1080×1350 pixels (4:5 portrait)**, because it takes up the most space in the feed. Square posts work at **1080×1080 (1:1)** and landscape at **1080×566 (1.91:1)**. Reels and Stories are **1080×1920 (9:16)**, and your profile photo should be at least **320×320**.

## Why Instagram Crops Your Photos: Aspect Ratio vs Pixels

![Instagram aspect ratios compared: 4:5 portrait, 1:1 square, 1.91:1 landscape and 9:16 full screen](/images/blog/instagram-post-size-ratios-1200x675.webp)

**Aspect ratio** is the shape of an image: width compared to height. 1:1 is square, 4:5 is slightly taller than wide, and 9:16 is a full phone screen. **Pixels** are the actual size. Instagram displays images at up to 1080 pixels wide, so 1080 is the width to export at. A larger image gets scaled down, and a smaller one gets scaled up and can look soft.

To check an image's ratio, divide width by height. Feed posts must sit between **0.8 (4:5)** and **1.91 (landscape)**. Anything taller or wider gets cropped in the app.

### What Most People Don't Know

At the time of writing, the Instagram app also accepts **3:4 portrait photos** (1080×1440), the native shape of most phone cameras. But posts published by scheduling tools go through Instagram's publishing API, which still requires the 4:5 to 1.91:1 range. If you schedule posts, export at 4:5. Our guide on [why Instagram crops photos](/blog/instagram-aspect-ratio-cropping) explains what happens to other shapes.

> **Key point:** Export at 1080 pixels wide and 4:5 for feed posts. It's the one size that works in the app, in schedulers and in the feed.

## Instagram Feed Post Size: 4:5 vs 1:1 vs 1.91:1

### Portrait: 1080×1350 (4:5), the Best Default

Portrait takes up more of the screen as people scroll, so your post gets more attention than a square or landscape one. It suits people, products, food, fashion and text-heavy graphics.

### Square: 1080×1080 (1:1) for Graphics

The classic Instagram shape. It's a safe choice for logos, quotes, product shots on a plain background and anything with a centered subject.

### Landscape: 1080×566 (1.91:1), Use Sparingly

Landscape works for wide scenes, group photos and wide screenshots, but it shows up small in a vertical feed. If a wide photo matters, split it into a seamless carousel with the [Instagram carousel splitter](/tools/instagram-carousel-splitter).

> **Pro tip:** If you only remember one size, make it 1080×1350. It fills the feed, works in schedulers and loses only a thin strip on the grid.

## Instagram Profile Grid Size: Why 4:5 Posts Show as 3:4

![How a 4:5 Instagram post is cropped to a 3:4 thumbnail on the profile grid](/images/blog/instagram-post-size-grid-1200x675.webp)

Your profile grid shows each post as a **3:4 thumbnail**, taller than a square. A 4:5 post loses a small strip at the top and bottom on the grid. A square or landscape post is cropped at the sides. The full post still shows in the feed.

- **Keep faces, logos and text in the center** of the image.
- **Leave space at the edges** of square and landscape posts, since they're cropped at the sides.
- **Plan multi-post layouts with the 3:4 crop in mind.** The [Instagram grid maker](/tools/instagram-grid-maker) splits one image into tiles for a seamless grid.

## Instagram Carousel Size: The First Slide Sets the Shape

Every slide in a carousel is shown in the same shape, set by the **first slide**. If the first slide is 4:5, the rest are shown at 4:5, and slides of other shapes are cropped to fit.

1. **Export every slide at the same size**, ideally 1080×1350.
2. **Crop images yourself before uploading**, so you choose what's trimmed.
3. **Watch the slide limit.** Scheduled carousels can have up to 10 items. Our guide to the [Instagram carousel limit](/blog/instagram-carousel-limit) explains why the app allows more.

## Instagram Reels and Story Size: 1080×1920 (9:16)

![Safe zones for Instagram Reels and Stories at 1080×1920, keeping text away from the top, bottom and right side](/images/blog/instagram-post-size-vertical-1200x675.webp)

**Reels** should be 1080×1920 pixels, the full height of a phone screen. A Reel in another shape gets black bars or is cropped. The cover image should be the same size; on your profile grid it's cropped to 3:4, so keep the title and faces near the middle. Instagram's buttons, username and caption sit over the bottom and right side, so keep important text away from those areas. Our [Instagram Reel size guide](/blog/reel-size-instagram) covers safe zones and export settings.

**Stories** are also 1080×1920. Instagram shows your profile picture and name at the top and the reply bar at the bottom, so keep text and logos out of roughly the **top and bottom 250 pixels**. Have a square or landscape photo? Put it in the middle and use the space above and below for text or a background color.

## Instagram Profile Picture Size and File Types

Upload a square profile image of at least **320×320 pixels**. Instagram displays it as a circle everywhere, so keep your face or logo centered with a little space around it. A bigger upload, such as 1080×1080, stays sharp.

| File | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos | JPG | The safest format; Instagram's publishing API accepts JPG only |
| Graphics in the app | PNG works | The app accepts other common types |
| Videos | MP4 or MOV, H.264 video and AAC audio | The standard Instagram accepts |
| Width | 1080 pixels | Very large files are compressed anyway, a common cause of blurry posts |

Ads use the same shapes as organic posts: **1:1 or 4:5** for feed ads and **9:16 (1080×1920)** for Story and Reels ads. For full ad specs, check Meta's Ads Guide, since ad requirements change more often than post sizes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Instagram post size?

1080×1350 pixels at a 4:5 ratio. Portrait posts take up the most space in the feed. Square (1080×1080) and landscape (1080×566) posts also work.

### What size is an Instagram post in pixels?

Instagram displays posts at up to 1080 pixels wide. Use 1080×1350 for portrait, 1080×1080 for square and 1080×566 for landscape.

### Is the Instagram grid 4:5 or 3:4?

The profile grid previews posts at 3:4. Your post itself can be 4:5, 1:1 or 1.91:1, but keep the important part in the center so it isn't cut off on the grid.

### What size are Instagram Reels and Stories?

Both are 1080×1920 pixels at a 9:16 ratio. Keep text away from the top and bottom, where Instagram places its buttons and profile details.

### Can I post 3:4 photos on Instagram?

In the app, yes, at the time of writing. Scheduling tools use Instagram's publishing API, which still needs 4:5 to 1.91:1, so export at 4:5 if you schedule.

### How do I post a picture on Instagram without cropping?

Resize it to a supported shape before you upload: 4:5, 1:1 or 1.91:1 for feed posts. To keep the whole image, use the "fit" option in the [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer), which adds a border instead of cutting anything off.

### What size should an Instagram profile picture be?

At least 320×320 pixels, square. Instagram shows it as a circle, so keep the subject centered.

### Why do my Instagram posts look blurry?

Usually the file was much larger or smaller than 1080 pixels wide, and Instagram's compression or upscaling softened it. Export at 1080 wide. Our guide on [why Instagram Stories look blurry](/blog/why-does-my-instagram-story-look-blurry) covers more causes.
