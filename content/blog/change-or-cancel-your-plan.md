You've decided you don't need your paid plan any more, or you need a different one, and you want it done without losing the posts you've already scheduled. To cancel your social media scheduler subscription in Schedule For You, sign in as the **workspace owner**, open **Billing** from the workspace menu, and choose to cancel in the secure Stripe customer portal that opens. Cancelling stops your plan from renewing.

![How to cancel a social media scheduler subscription in Schedule For You: open Billing, cancel in Stripe's portal, keep your posts](/images/blog/change-or-cancel-your-plan-hero-1200x675.webp)

The same Billing screen is where you change plan, upgrade or downgrade, update your card and download invoices. What trips people up is that only the owner sees it, and that cancelling isn't the same as deleting your work.

This guide covers each billing task step by step, what happens to your posts and accounts when a paid plan ends, what to check before you move from Studio to Solo, and how to cancel during the free trial so you aren't charged.

## Why Cancelling Doesn't Delete Your Posts (What Actually Happens)

![What stays and what stops when a paid Schedule For You plan ends and the workspace goes back to Free](/images/blog/change-or-cancel-your-plan-what-happens-1200x675.webp)

Every Schedule For You workspace starts on the Free plan, and that's where it goes back to when a paid plan ends. You don't lose your account or your work.

When a paid plan ends:

- **Your posts, drafts and media stay.**
- **Scheduled posts keep publishing.**
- **Accounts beyond the first 3 are paused.** Your oldest accounts keep publishing, and the newest ones past the limit are paused until you upgrade or disconnect some.
- **Paid features stop**, such as bulk scheduling, RSS, webhooks and the API, until you upgrade again.

### What Most People Don't Know

Payments are handled by Stripe, a widely used payment provider. When you open Billing on a paid plan, you're taken to Stripe's secure customer portal for your subscription, and any card details are entered there, not in Schedule For You. That's also why the cancel, change-plan and invoice buttons live in the portal rather than in the dashboard itself.

> **Key point:** Only the workspace owner can open Billing. If you don't see it in the workspace menu, you're probably a team member. Ask the owner, or contact support if the owner has left your organization.

## How to Cancel Your Subscription in 6 Steps (About 2 Minutes)

![Six steps to cancel a Schedule For You subscription, from signing in as the owner to saving the confirmation](/images/blog/change-or-cancel-your-plan-cancel-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Sign In as the Workspace Owner

Billing belongs to the owner. Team members on Studio can create and schedule posts, but they can't change the plan.

### Step 2: Open the Workspace Menu and Choose Billing

On Free, Billing shows the plans to upgrade to. On a paid plan, it opens Stripe's customer portal.

### Step 3: Wait for the Stripe Customer Portal

It opens in your browser. If nothing happens, check that your browser isn't blocking the new page.

### Step 4: Choose to Cancel

Labels in the portal can change over time, so look for the option to cancel your plan or subscription.

### Step 5: Review the Details, Then Confirm

Read what the portal shows about your plan before you confirm.

### Step 6: Save the Confirmation

Check that the portal shows your plan as cancelled, and save a screenshot for your records.

> **Pro tip:** Before step 4, open your calendar and queue. Anything scheduled keeps publishing after the plan ends, so you don't need to rush, but it's worth knowing what's coming.

Cancelling stops your subscription from renewing, so you won't be charged for future billing periods. If you have a question about a charge, contact support rather than guessing.

### Before You Cancel: A 4-Point Checklist

- **Check your calendar and queue** for posts in the coming days and weeks.
- **Copy anything you want to keep elsewhere**, such as evergreen posts you plan to reuse in another tool.
- **Tell your team and clients** if they rely on approvals or client preview links in your workspace.
- **Consider downgrading instead.** If cost is the issue, Studio to Solo, or Solo to Free, may cover what you need.

## How to Change Plan: Solo vs Studio (and When Free Is Enough)

![Free, Solo and Studio compared by connected accounts, scheduled posts and team members](/images/blog/change-or-cancel-your-plan-plans-1200x675.webp)

There are two paid plans, and both include unlimited scheduling and every paid feature. Free is always there underneath.

| Plan | Price | Connected accounts | Scheduled posts at a time | Team members | Priority support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0, no card | 3 | 10 | No | No |
| Solo | $19/month | 15 | Unlimited | No | No |
| Studio | $59/month | 100 | Unlimited | Yes | Yes |

Paying yearly gets you 2 months free: Solo is $190 a year and Studio is $590 a year.

To switch between Solo and Studio:

1. Open **Billing** from the workspace menu.
2. In the Stripe customer portal, choose to **update or change your plan**.
3. Select **Solo** or **Studio**.
4. **Review the details** Stripe shows for the change, then confirm.

The new plan appears on your subscription in the portal once confirmed. For a full side-by-side of what each plan includes, see [pricing](/pricing).

### How to Downgrade From Studio to Solo Without Surprises

1. **Count your connected accounts.** Solo includes 15 in total, across every platform. If you have more, decide which to remove from the Accounts screen.
2. **Review your team.** Team members are part of Studio. If colleagues or freelancers log in to schedule posts, plan how you'll work without them.
3. **Check the brands you manage.** Make sure the accounts you keep cover the clients you still need.
4. **Then switch plan** in Billing as described above.

If you're unsure how the change will affect your account, contact support before you confirm.

### When to Upgrade From Solo to Studio

Upgrade when you need more than 15 connected accounts, want to invite team members, or want priority support. The steps are the same: Billing, change plan, choose Studio, confirm.

## How to Update Your Card or Download Invoices

To update your card, open **Billing**, find the payment method section in the Stripe portal, add the new card and make it the default, then remove the old one if you don't want it on file. Do it before your card expires so renewals don't fail.

Invoices are listed in the same portal. Open one to view or download it for your accounts team or tax records.

## How to Cancel the 7-Day Free Trial Before Day 8 (No Charge)

![Timeline of the 7-day Schedule For You trial: card added on day 1, cancel by day 7, first charge on day 8](/images/blog/change-or-cancel-your-plan-trial-1200x675.webp)

Solo and Studio start with a 7-day free trial. A card is required to start it, and you aren't charged until day 8. If it isn't right for you, cancel before day 8 through **Billing**, using the same steps as above, and you won't be charged. The Free plan never needs a card.

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| You cancel on day 1–7 | No charge. The workspace stays on Free |
| You do nothing | Your chosen plan starts and the first charge is on day 8 |
| You signed up through a referral link | The trial is 14 days and the first charge is on day 15 |
| You cancel after being charged | The plan stops renewing |

For any trial:

- **Set a reminder** a day or two before it ends.
- **Test the workflows you'll actually use**: connect your accounts, schedule a week of posts, and try the calendar and queue. Our guide to [planning content in advance](/blog/plan-content-in-advance) suggests a routine.
- **Check the confirmation** after cancelling so you know it went through.

## How to Cancel Any Social Media Scheduler Subscription (6 Rules)

If you're cancelling a different tool, the process is usually similar:

1. **Find the billing or subscription settings.** They're often in an account or workspace menu, and may only be visible to the owner or admin.
2. **Check where you subscribed.** If you paid through an app store on your phone, you usually cancel in that store's subscription settings, not in the tool.
3. **Note the renewal date.** Cancel before it to avoid the next charge.
4. **Deleting the app isn't cancelling.** Uninstalling or disconnecting accounts doesn't stop billing on its own.
5. **Save the confirmation** email or screenshot.
6. **Contact the vendor's support** if you can't find the option.

## Disputing a Charge With Your Bank Instead (Use With Caution)

When a cancel button is hard to find, it's tempting to skip the vendor and ask your bank to reverse the charge. Sometimes that's the right move, but it's rarely the fastest.

### The Real Risks

- **It's slower.** Bank disputes often take longer than a support reply.
- **It can affect your account with the vendor**, depending on their policy, while the dispute is open.
- **It doesn't always cancel the subscription.** A reversed charge and a cancelled plan are separate things.

### Before You Dispute

- Cancel in the billing settings first, so no further charges happen.
- Contact the vendor's support with the date and amount of the charge.
- Keep screenshots of your cancellation and any replies.

> **Bottom line:** Cancel first, ask support second, and keep a bank dispute for when a vendor won't answer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my Schedule For You subscription?

Sign in as the workspace owner, open Billing from the workspace menu, and choose to cancel in the Stripe customer portal that opens. Cancelling stops your plan from renewing.

### Will I lose my posts if I cancel?

No. When a paid plan ends, the workspace goes back to Free and your posts, drafts and media stay. Scheduled posts keep publishing, and accounts beyond the first 3 are paused.

### Can I cancel the free trial without being charged?

Yes. The 7-day trial needs a card, but you aren't charged until day 8. Cancel before day 8 through Billing and you won't be charged.

### How do I downgrade my plan?

Open Billing, choose to change your plan in the Stripe customer portal and select Solo. First make sure you're within Solo's 15 connected accounts, and plan for losing team members, which are part of Studio.

### Why can't I see the Billing option?

Only the workspace owner can open Billing. If you're a team member, ask the owner to make the change, or contact support if the owner is no longer available.

### Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

You choose monthly or yearly when you start your trial. Yearly costs the same as 10 months. For a change to an existing subscription, check the options in the Stripe portal or contact support.

### What happens to my referral credit if I cancel?

Unused referral credit stays on your account for when you come back. Credits apply to future invoices and aren't paid out in cash.

### Who do I contact about a billing question?

Contact Schedule For You support with any question about charges, invoices or your subscription. Include the email address on the account so the team can find it quickly.
