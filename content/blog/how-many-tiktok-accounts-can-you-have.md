You want a second TikTok account, maybe for a side project, a brand or a niche you don't want on your main profile. Good news: **TikTok doesn't publish a limit on how many accounts one person can have, and running more than one is allowed.** The practical limits are elsewhere: each account needs its **own email address or phone number**, and the in-app account switcher only holds **a handful of accounts per device** (three is the number most often reported, and some versions allow more).

![How many TikTok accounts can you have? No published cap, one email or phone each, and a few per device in the switcher](/images/blog/how-many-tiktok-accounts-can-you-have-hero-1200x675.webp)

What catches people out isn't the number of accounts. It's the switcher filling up, security checks when you switch too often, and the one rule that does get accounts banned: creating new accounts to get around a ban, or to spam.

This guide covers the limits, how to create and switch accounts, how to log out and remove other devices, and how to run several accounts without switching all day.

## Why There's No Official Limit (and What Limits You Instead)

![What limits how many TikTok accounts you can run: one email or phone each and the switcher, not a published cap](/images/blog/how-many-tiktok-accounts-can-you-have-limits-1200x675.webp)

TikTok treats each account as its own profile, tied to its own sign-in. It doesn't publish a cap per person, so the real limits come from how you sign in and how you switch.

| Question | Answer, at the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Can you have more than one TikTok account? | Yes |
| Official cap on accounts per person | None published |
| Accounts in the in-app switcher | A few per device; the exact number varies by version |
| Same email or phone for two accounts? | No, each needs its own |
| Accounts on one phone | As many as the switcher allows; more means logging out and in |

### What Most People Don't Know

Other users can't see that your accounts belong to the same person unless you tell them. TikTok can, from your device and sign-in details. That's why making a new account to get around a ban doesn't work: TikTok's rules treat it as a violation in itself, and the new account can be removed too.

> **Key point:** Having several accounts is fine. Using extra accounts to dodge a ban, or to spam, breaks TikTok's Community Guidelines.

## How to Create a Second TikTok Account in 5 Steps

![The steps to create a second TikTok account from the account switcher](/images/blog/how-many-tiktok-accounts-can-you-have-create-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if yours differ.

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile** in the bottom bar.

### Step 2: Tap Your Username at the Top

A list of your accounts appears. This is the account switcher.

### Step 3: Tap Add Account, Then Create New Account

On some versions it reads **Sign up** instead of **Create new account**.

### Step 4: Sign Up With a New Email or Phone (the Step People Get Wrong)

Use an email, phone number or Google/Apple sign-in that **isn't already linked to TikTok**. If you use the same email as your main account, TikTok will try to log you in to that account instead of creating a new one.

> **Pro tip:** A Gmail address with a different name, or a work email, is the simplest way to get a second sign-in. Note which one goes with which account.

The new account starts as a personal account. If it's for a brand, switch it to a business account in **Settings and privacy → Account**.

## How to Switch Accounts on TikTok (App and Desktop)

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Phone app | Profile → tap your username at the top → pick the account |
| Website | Hover over your profile picture → **Switch account** |
| Switcher full | Log one account out, then add the other |

If switching fails with a security message, TikTok has seen unusual activity. Wait a while, update the app, and avoid switching back and forth many times in a row.

## How to Log Out of TikTok (One Account or All)

1. **Profile → Menu (☰) → Settings and privacy.**
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Log out**.
3. Choose whether to save login info for that device.

With several accounts in the switcher, TikTok asks whether to log out of the current account or all of them. Log out of one when you need a free slot in the switcher; log out of all on a shared or old device.

## How to Log Someone Else Out of Your TikTok Account

![Four steps to log someone out of your TikTok account: manage devices, remove, change password, turn on 2-step verification](/images/blog/how-many-tiktok-accounts-can-you-have-log-out-1200x675.webp)

If someone else has access to your account, such as an old phone, a former employee or a shared tablet:

1. Go to **Settings and privacy → Security and permissions** (on some versions just **Security**) **→ Manage devices**.
2. Find the device you don't recognize and tap the **trash** icon or **Remove**.
3. **Change your password** straight after, so they can't sign back in.
4. Turn on **2-step verification** in the same Security menu.

TikTok also sends security alerts when a new device logs in. Check them under **Security alerts**.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Switcher is full" | Log out one account | Frees a slot on this device |
| Security check when switching | Wait, update the app, switch less often | Rapid switching looks unusual |
| Can't create a new account | Use an email or phone not linked to TikTok | Each account needs its own sign-in |
| Ex-employee still has access | Remove the device, change the password | Ends their session and blocks a new one |

## Sharing One TikTok Login With a Team (Use With Caution)

When a brand account has several people posting, the quick fix is to share the password. It works, but it's the setup most likely to go wrong.

### The Real Risks

- **You can't tell who did what.** Every post and reply looks like it came from the same person.
- **Removing someone means changing the password for everyone.**
- **Logins from many devices and places can trigger security checks**, which lock everyone out at the worst moment.
- **2-step verification codes go to one phone**, so someone always has to be on hand.

### If You Share a Login Anyway

- Keep the password in a shared password manager, not a chat.
- Check **Manage devices** every month and remove anything unknown.
- Change the password whenever someone leaves.

> **Bottom line:** Shared passwords are fine for two people who trust each other. Past that, give people access through a tool that doesn't need the password.

## What to Do Next

1. **Decide what each account is for** before you create it. One clear topic per account is easier to grow.
2. **Pick a separate email or phone** for each one and write down which is which.
3. **Turn on 2-step verification** on every account.
4. **Check Manage devices** after any shared use.
5. **Use a scheduler** once you're past the switcher's limit or posting for a team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many TikTok accounts can you have?

TikTok doesn't publish a cap on how many accounts one person can have. Each needs its own email or phone number, and the in-app switcher holds only a few per device.

### Can I have two TikTok accounts with the same email?

No. Each account needs a unique email address or phone number. You can use different sign-in methods, such as email for one and phone for another.

### Will TikTok ban me for having multiple accounts?

Not for having them. Accounts get banned for breaking the Community Guidelines, and creating new accounts to get around a ban is itself a violation.

### How many TikTok accounts can I switch between on one phone?

A few; three is the number most often reported, and some versions allow more. If you hit the limit, log one out to add another.

### Do my TikTok accounts know they're linked?

Other users can't see that your accounts belong to the same person unless you tell them. TikTok can, from your device and sign-in details.

### How do I log someone out of my TikTok?

Go to Settings and privacy, then Security (or Security and permissions), then Manage devices, and remove the device. Change your password straight after.

### Can I make a second account a business account?

Yes. New accounts start as personal accounts, and you can switch one to a business account in Settings and privacy under Account.

### Why does TikTok show a security check when I switch accounts?

TikTok has noticed unusual activity, often from switching many times in a row. Wait a while, update the app and switch less often.
