You checked someone's TikTok profile and then remembered: TikTok can show people who visited them.

To turn off profile views on TikTok, go to **Profile > menu (three lines) > Settings and privacy > Privacy > Profile views** and switch off **Profile view history**. With it off, other people can't see that you viewed their profile, and you can't see who viewed yours. TikTok only shows a visit when *both* people have the setting on.

![How to turn off profile views on TikTok: Settings and privacy, Privacy, Profile views, then switch it off](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

The feature is confusing because it's mutual, it's not available to every account, and it's easy to mix up with post views, liked videos and reposts. This guide covers how profile views work, how to turn them off, what private browsing does and doesn't hide, and the other privacy settings worth checking. Watching someone's videos in your feed isn't the same as visiting their profile and doesn't show up in this list.

## How TikTok Profile Views Work (It's a Two-Way Switch)

![TikTok profile views only appear when both people have the setting on](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok-how-1200x675.webp)

| Question | Answer, at the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Is it on by default? | Reported as off by default for most accounts |
| Who appears in the list? | People who viewed your profile in the past 30 days and also have the setting on |
| Who can use it? | Accounts 16 and older with fewer than 5,000 followers, according to TikTok's rollout notes and most guides |
| Does turning it off hide past visits? | It stops new visits being recorded and hides the list; visits you made while it was on may already have been seen |

### What Most People Don't Know

Profile view history is separate from **post view history**, which (where available) shows people who watched your videos in the past seven days, again only if they have the setting on too. You'll find it under **Privacy > Post views**. Turning off one doesn't turn off the other.

> **Key point:** You can't see who viewed your profile while hiding your own visits. TikTok makes it all or nothing.

## How to Turn Off Profile View History in 4 Taps

Menu labels move between app versions, so the wording may differ slightly on your phone.

![Four taps to turn off profile view history on TikTok](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Profile Menu

Open TikTok, tap **Profile**, then tap the **menu** (three lines) at the top right.

### Step 2: Go to Settings and Privacy, Then Privacy

Choose **Settings and privacy**, then **Privacy**.

### Step 3: Tap Profile Views

Tap **Profile views**. If you don't see it, your account may not have the feature; see the troubleshooting table below.

### Step 4: Switch Off Profile View History

Switch off **Profile view history**. Then go back to **Privacy** and check **Post views** too. That's the step people miss: they turn off profile views but their video views still show up in creators' lists.

**From your inbox:** if you've had a "viewed your profile" notification, tap it, tap the settings icon on the Profile views page and switch the feature off.

> **Pro tip:** Visit a profile only after you've switched the setting off. Visits made while it was on may already be in the other person's list.

## Does a Private Browser Hide Your TikTok Views?

Mostly, yes. If you open a profile in a private or incognito browser window **without logging in**, TikTok has no account to link the visit to, so it can't appear in anyone's profile view history. If you log in inside that private window, the visit counts as your account's, the same as in the app. Watching a video still adds to its public view count either way. ## Reposts, Likes and Following Lists: Who Sees What

| Activity | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Visiting a profile | The owner, only if you both have profile views on |
| Watching a video | The creator, only if you both have post views on |
| Watching a video someone reposted | No one; reposts don't appear in profile view history |
| Reposting a video | Your followers and friends |
| Your Liked videos tab | Everyone or only you, depending on your setting |
| Liking someone's video | The creator, by tapping the like count |
| Your Following list | Everyone or only you, depending on your setting |

### Do People Know If You Watch Their TikTok Repost?

No. TikTok doesn't tell anyone that you watched a video they reposted. The exception is post view history: if the original creator and you both have post views on, your view of their video can appear in their list.

### Can People See Your Likes on TikTok?

It depends on one setting. Your **Liked videos** tab can show to everyone or only you, and recent guides, including Consumer Reports, say it's private by default. To check, go to **Settings and privacy > Privacy > Liked videos** and choose **Only me**. A creator can always see which accounts liked their own videos, and hiding your Liked tab doesn't change that.

### Can You See a Hidden Following List?

No, not through any legitimate method. When someone sets **Following list** to **Only me**, other people only see the accounts you both follow. To hide your own list, change **Following list** to **Only me** in the same menu.

## Other TikTok Privacy Settings Worth 2 Minutes

- **Private account:** only followers you approve see your videos.
- **Suggest your account to others:** stop TikTok suggesting you to contacts or people with mutual connections.
- **Activity status:** hide when you're active in messages.

## "Who Viewed My Profile" and Following-List Apps (Use With Caution)

Search for TikTok privacy and you'll find apps and sites that promise to show who viewed your profile without the setting, or to reveal someone's hidden following list.

![TikTok's own privacy settings compared with risky apps that claim to reveal viewers or hidden lists](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok-caution-1200x675.webp)

### The Real Risks

- **They don't work.** TikTok only shows profile visits when both people have the setting on, and a hidden following list stays hidden.
- **They ask for your login,** which puts your account at risk.
- **They may collect your data** or push you to install more apps.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Use the settings in **Settings and privacy > Privacy** only.
- Never enter your TikTok password on a third-party site.
- Remove apps you've connected to TikTok and no longer use.

> **Bottom line:** TikTok's own switch is the only way to see or hide profile visits. Everything else is guesswork or a scam.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If I turn off profile views, can people still see I viewed their profile?

No new visits will be shown. Visits that were already shown while the setting was on may still have been seen before you turned it off.

### Why don't I have the profile views option on TikTok?

It's usually because your account has 5,000 or more followers, you're under 16, or your app needs updating. Availability also varies by region.

### Does TikTok tell people when you watch their videos?

Only through post view history, and only when both of you have it turned on. Otherwise watching a video is anonymous apart from the view count.

### Can people see who viewed their TikTok profile without the setting?

No. A visit appears only when both people have Profile view history turned on.

### How long does TikTok keep profile views?

The list shows visits from the past 30 days, at the time of writing.

### Is profile view history on by default?

It's reported as off by default for most accounts. Check under Settings and privacy > Privacy > Profile views.

### Can someone see I viewed their profile if I'm logged out?

No. Without a logged-in account, TikTok can't link the visit to you.

### Does turning off profile views hide my likes?

No. Liked videos is a separate setting under Privacy. Creators can always see who liked their own videos.
