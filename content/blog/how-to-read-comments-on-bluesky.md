A post says 40 replies, you tap the little speech bubble, and you're not sure whether you're seeing all of them, or in what order.

To read comments on Bluesky, **tap the post** (or the speech-bubble reply count under it). That opens the post's thread, with the replies, which are Bluesky's version of comments, listed underneath. Tap any reply to open the conversation that branches from it. The number next to the speech bubble is how many replies the post has.

![How to read comments on Bluesky: tap the post to open its thread, then sort replies and open hidden ones](/images/blog/how-to-read-comments-on-bluesky-hero-1200x675.webp)

What isn't obvious is that the replies you see are already sorted and filtered. Bluesky puts popular replies first by default, and some replies are tucked away because the author hid them, you muted someone, or a moderation setting filtered them.

This guide covers how to open a thread, how to sort replies and change the view, why some comments are missing, how to see replies to your own posts, and how to keep up with replies when you post on a schedule.

## Why Bluesky Comments Are Called Replies (and Why Some Are Missing)

On Bluesky, every comment is a reply: a post of its own, attached to the one above it. That's why tapping a reply opens its own conversation, with the chain above and below it.

![Bluesky replies you can see compared with replies that are hidden or filtered](/images/blog/how-to-read-comments-on-bluesky-why-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

The reply count and the replies you can see often don't match. Replies hidden by the author, replies from accounts you muted or blocked, replies with moderation labels you've set to hide, and replies containing your muted words are all counted but not shown in the main list. The count can also lag for a while after someone deletes a reply.

> **Key point:** If the count is higher than what you can see, scroll to the very end of the thread. Bluesky tucks hidden and filtered replies behind **Show more replies** or **Hidden replies** there.

## How to Read Comments on Bluesky in 5 Steps

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the nearest match if yours differ.

![Five steps to read comments on a Bluesky post](/images/blog/how-to-read-comments-on-bluesky-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Find the Post

Find it in your feed, on a profile or in search.

### Step 2: Tap the Post Text or the Reply Icon

The thread opens with the original post at the top. This is the step people get wrong: tapping the heart or repost icon reacts to the post instead of opening it, so tap the text or the speech bubble.

### Step 3: Scroll Down to Read Replies

Replies to replies are indented or shown as "View more replies".

### Step 4: Tap a Reply to Focus on It

You'll see the full chain above and below it.

### Step 5: Go Back

Use the arrow at the top left.

On bsky.app in a browser it works the same way: click the post to open its thread.

> **Pro tip:** In a long argument, switch to threaded view before you scroll. You'll see who's answering whom instead of one flat list.

## How to Sort Bluesky Replies: Hot, Newest, Oldest or Most Liked

Bluesky sorts replies with popular ones first by default, which means recent replies with more likes rise to the top. You can change that:

- In a thread, tap the **sort or options control** near the top of the replies to choose an order, or
- Go to **Settings → Content and media → Thread preferences** to set your default.

| Option | What it does | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot (default) | Recent replies with the most likes first | Getting the gist quickly |
| Newest | Latest replies first | Following a live conversation |
| Oldest | Replies in the order they were posted | Reading a discussion from the start |
| Most liked | Highest like count first | Finding the best answers |
| Threaded view | Shows replies nested under the reply they answer | Long conversations |
| Linear view | One reply after another, not nested | Short threads |

![Bluesky reply sort options from Hot to Most liked, and threaded versus linear view](/images/blog/how-to-read-comments-on-bluesky-sort-1200x675.webp)

## Missing Comments? 6 Reasons Replies Are Hidden

If the reply count says 40 and you only see 30, some replies are filtered.

| Reason | What you see | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden by the author | Nothing in the main list | Open **Hidden replies** at the bottom of the thread |
| You muted or blocked the account, or they blocked you | "Blocked post", or nothing | Unmute or unblock, if it's your choice. See [who blocked you on Bluesky](/blog/who-blocked-me-on-bluesky) |
| A moderation label you set to hide | Nothing | Change the label setting to warn |
| A label set to warn | A "show" button | Tap to reveal |
| Your muted words | Nothing | Remove the word from your muted words |
| A deleted reply | The count lags for a while | Nothing to read |

## How to See Replies to Your Own Bluesky Posts

Open **Notifications** and switch to the **Mentions** tab to see replies, mentions and quotes without likes and follows in the way.

You can also limit who can reply to your posts. When writing a post, tap the reply settings ("Anyone can interact") and choose followers, people you follow, people you mention, a list, or nobody.

### How to Hide a Reply on Your Own Post

Open the reply, tap the three-dot menu and choose **Hide reply for everyone**. It moves to the hidden replies section of your thread. People can still open it there.

## Reading Bluesky Comments on Desktop (and Without an Account)

On a computer, open bsky.app and click a post to open its thread. Everything above, from sorting to hidden replies, works the same as in the app.

Posts are public, so bsky.app usually shows replies to logged-out visitors. An account can choose to discourage apps from showing it to logged-out visitors, and in that case you need to sign in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Bluesky have comments?

Yes. They're called replies. Tap a post to open it and read the replies underneath.

### Can you see Bluesky comments without an account?

Usually yes, on bsky.app, since posts are public. An account can choose to discourage apps from showing it to logged-out visitors, and in that case you need to sign in.

### Why can't I reply to a post on Bluesky?

The author limited who can reply, for example to followers or mentioned accounts only, or one of you has blocked the other.

### How do I hide comments on my Bluesky post?

Open the reply, tap the three-dot menu and choose **Hide reply for everyone**. It moves to the hidden replies section of your thread. You can also limit who can reply before or after posting.

### Why does the reply count not match what I see?

Some replies are hidden by the author, filtered by your mutes, blocks or moderation settings, or recently deleted.

### How do I change the order of replies on Bluesky?

Tap the sort control near the top of the replies, or set a default under Settings, Content and media, Thread preferences.

### What's the difference between threaded and linear view?

Threaded view nests each reply under the one it answers. Linear view shows them one after another.

### Where do I see replies to my own posts?

In Notifications, on the Mentions tab.
