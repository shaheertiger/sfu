Every time you pause a TikTok, a "Find Similar" bubble pops up over the video. TikTok doesn't have a setting called "Turn off Find Similar" at the time of writing. On many app versions, though, you can hide it: go to **Profile > Menu (☰) > Settings and privacy > Playback** and turn off the option to show object tags or visual search prompts.

![How to turn off Find Similar on TikTok: turn off object tags in Playback settings](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-find-similar-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

If you don't see that option, the prompt can't be switched off on your version, but you can make it appear less often. "You May Like" in search works the same way: there's no off switch, but you can clear what feeds it.

This guide covers what Find Similar actually is, four ways to hide it, how to reset You May Like, and the setting other guides mix it up with.

## What Is Find Similar on TikTok? (It's Visual Search)

Find Similar is part of TikTok's visual search. When you pause or long-press a video, TikTok scans the frame and offers videos, products or creators that look alike. On some versions it's a small bubble or a magnifying-glass button near the top of the video; on others it's in the long-press menu.

![When TikTok's Find Similar is useful compared with when it gets in the way](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-find-similar-on-tiktok-useful-vs-annoying-1200x675.webp)

| When it helps | When it gets in the way |
|---|---|
| Finding where to buy a jacket in a video | Pops up every time you pause |
| Finding more videos of a dish or place | Covers part of the video |
| Discovering creators with a similar style | Appears on videos with nothing to shop |

### What Most People Don't Know

Many guides send you to **Privacy > Suggest your account to others** to turn off Find Similar. That's a different setting: it controls whether TikTok recommends your profile to other people. Turning it off won't remove the bubble, and it can cut how often new people find your account.

> **Key point:** Find Similar is controlled from Playback settings, not Privacy. If Playback has no visual-search or object-tag toggle on your version, there's no way to switch it off completely.

## How to Hide Find Similar on TikTok: 4 Methods

Try these in order. Labels differ between app versions and regions.

![Four ways to hide or reduce Find Similar on TikTok, starting with Playback settings](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-find-similar-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Method 1: Turn Off Object Tags in Playback

1. Go to **Profile** and tap the menu (☰).
2. Tap **Settings and privacy**.
3. Tap **Playback**.
4. Look for a toggle about displaying object tags, visual search or product recognition, and turn it off.

### Method 2: Update the App

TikTok has moved this feature between menus. The newest version is the most likely to have the toggle, so update from the App Store or Google Play, then check Playback again.

> **Pro tip:** If the toggle appears after an update and later disappears, check again after the next update. TikTok tests features with some accounts and regions before others.

### Method 3: Tap "Not Interested" on Videos You Don't Want

Long-press a video and tap **Not interested**. It doesn't remove the button, but it teaches your feed what to show less of.

### Method 4: Watch on the Web

TikTok's website shows fewer of these prompts. See [how to watch TikTok without the app](/blog/how-to-watch-tiktok-without-app).

| Method | Removes the bubble? | Time | Works on every version? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Object tags off in Playback | Yes, where the toggle exists | 1 minute | No |
| Update the app | Sometimes adds the toggle | 2 minutes | No |
| Not interested | No, but shapes the feed | Seconds per video | Yes |
| Watch on the web | Mostly | — | Yes |

## How to Clear "You May Like" on TikTok (5 Steps)

"You May Like" is the list of suggested searches under the search bar. TikTok builds it from your search history, watch history and what's trending. You can't turn it off, but you can reset it.

![Five ways to clear or change TikTok's You May Like search suggestions](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-find-similar-on-tiktok-you-may-like-1200x675.webp)

1. **Clear search history.** Tap the search bar, then **Clear all** (or the X next to each past search). Or go to Settings and privacy > **Activity center** > **Search history**.
2. **Remove a single suggestion.** Long-press a suggestion; on many versions you can remove or report it.
3. **Clear watch history.** Settings and privacy > Activity center > **Watch history** > select and delete.
4. **Refresh your For You feed.** Settings and privacy > **Content preferences** > **Refresh your For You feed**. This resets recommendations as if you were a new user, which also changes your suggestions.
5. **Filter keywords.** Content preferences > **Filter keywords** hides videos containing words you choose.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Embarrassing search suggestions | Clear search history | Suggestions draw on past searches |
| Suggestions about one topic everywhere | Clear watch history and filter keywords | Removes the signal behind them |
| Whole feed feels stuck | Refresh your For You feed | Resets recommendations |

## "Suggest Your Account to Others": the Setting People Mix Up

![Find Similar compared with the Suggest your account to others privacy setting](/images/blog/how-to-turn-off-find-similar-on-tiktok-mixup-1200x675.webp)

| | Find Similar | Suggest your account to others |
|---|---|---|
| What it controls | Visual search prompts on videos you watch | Whether TikTok recommends your profile |
| Where it lives | Playback (on some versions) | Privacy |

Leave **Suggest your account to others** on unless you specifically want fewer strangers finding you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does Find Similar keep popping up on TikTok?

It appears when TikTok's visual search recognises something in the frame, usually when you pause or long-press. Turning off object tags in Playback settings hides it on many versions.

### Can I get rid of Find Similar for good?

Not on every version. If your Playback settings don't have the toggle, the feature can't be switched off, though updating the app sometimes adds it.

### How do I delete "You May Like" on TikTok?

You can't delete the section, but clearing your search and watch history and refreshing your For You feed changes what it suggests.

### Does Find Similar tell creators I searched for their video?

No. Searches and visual-search taps aren't shown to the creator.

### Does turning off Suggest your account to others remove Find Similar?

No, that setting controls whether TikTok recommends your profile to other people. It doesn't affect visual search prompts.

### Will refreshing my For You feed delete my account data?

No. It resets recommendations, but your videos, followers and settings stay as they are.
