You've got a draft you love and you don't want to lose it, or you want to post it somewhere else first. TikTok has no button that exports a draft straight to your camera roll, so you use a private post instead: open the draft, tap **Next**, set **Who can watch this video** to **Only me**, turn on **Save to device** under **More options**, then tap **Post**.

![How to save a TikTok draft to your camera roll: post it as Only me with Save to device on](/images/blog/how-to-save-tiktok-drafts-to-camera-roll-hero-1200x675.webp)

The finished video lands in your camera roll, and on TikTok it sits in your private videos where nobody else can see it. You can delete the private post afterward and keep the file.

This guide covers that method step by step, two alternatives, how to back up and delete drafts, and whether anyone can see them.

## Why TikTok Drafts Are Easy to Lose

TikTok drafts are stored in the app on your phone, not in your account. That's the detail most people don't realize until it's too late.

![Where TikTok drafts live compared with videos saved to your camera roll](/images/blog/how-to-save-tiktok-drafts-to-camera-roll-why-1200x675.webp)

| | TikTok draft | Saved to camera roll |
|---|---|---|
| Where it lives | Inside the TikTok app on one phone | Your phone's photo library |
| Survives reinstalling TikTok | No | Yes |
| Moves to a new phone | No | Yes, with your photo backup |
| In TikTok's Download your data | No | Not needed |

### What Most People Don't Know

Drafts don't appear in **Download your data** and don't sync to another phone. Delete the app, reset your phone or switch devices, and your drafts are gone. Our guide on [how to see TikTok drafts](/blog/how-to-see-tiktok-drafts) explains why drafts disappear and how to prevent it.

> **Key point:** A draft is one app away from deletion. If it matters, get it into your camera roll, where your normal phone backup protects it.

## Method 1: Post as Only Me With Save to Device (Best Quality)

Menu labels change between app versions, so yours may differ slightly.

![Six steps to save a TikTok draft to your camera roll with an Only me post](/images/blog/how-to-save-tiktok-drafts-to-camera-roll-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Your Drafts Folder

Go to **Profile** and tap the **Drafts** folder.

### Step 2: Open the Draft and Tap Next

Don't change anything in the editor unless you mean to.

### Step 3: Set Who Can Watch to Only Me

Tap **Who can watch this video** and choose **Only me**. This is the step people get wrong: forget it, and the video goes public.

### Step 4: Turn On Save to Device

Open **More options** (or **Advanced settings**) and turn on **Save to device**.

### Step 5: Tap Post

The video posts privately and saves at the same time.

### Step 6: Check Your Camera Roll

Look in Photos on iPhone or Gallery on Android. The full edit, with effects, text and sound, is there.

> **Pro tip:** Before deleting the private post, open the saved file and play it with sound. Some licensed music may not save with the video. If the file is silent or the option is greyed out, swap to a sound from the Commercial Music Library or an original sound.

The video now shows in your profile's private tab (the lock icon). Leave it, or delete it once you've confirmed the file is saved. Deleting the post doesn't remove the copy on your phone.

### Method 2: Save From Your Private Videos

If you already posted a draft as Only me without saving it:

1. Go to **Profile** and tap the **lock icon** tab.
2. Open the video and tap **...** (or the Share arrow).
3. Tap **Save video**.

### Method 3: Screen-Record the Preview

Open the draft, start your phone's screen recording and play the preview. It's quick, but it's lower quality, includes the on-screen buttons unless you crop, and isn't worth it for anything you plan to post elsewhere.

## How to Back Up TikTok Drafts in Bulk

There's no bulk option. To back up many drafts, repeat Method 1 for each one, starting with the ones you'd most hate to lose. Once they're in your camera roll, your phone's normal photo backup covers them.

## How to Delete Drafts on TikTok

1. Open **Profile** > **Drafts**.
2. Tap **Select** in the top corner.
3. Tick the drafts you want to remove (or **Select all**).
4. Tap **Delete** and confirm.

Deleted drafts are gone for good; there's no recently deleted folder for drafts. Save anything you might want first.

## Can People See Your Drafts on TikTok?

No. Drafts are visible only to you, on the phone where you made them. They don't show on your public profile, other people can't see the Drafts folder, and followers aren't notified when you save one. Videos posted as **Only me** are also private until you change the setting to Everyone or Friends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you save a TikTok draft without posting it?

Not with full quality. Post it as Only me with Save to device turned on, then delete the private post if you like; a screen recording works without posting, at lower quality.

### Where do TikTok drafts go when you save them to your camera roll?

Into your phone's main photo library: Recents in Photos on iPhone, or the Gallery or DCIM folder on Android.

### Does posting a draft as Only me notify my followers?

No. Only me posts are private, don't appear in anyone's feed and don't send notifications.

### Why can't I save my TikTok draft to my camera roll?

Usually because of licensed music, missing photo permission for TikTok, or low storage. Check TikTok's photo permission in your phone's settings and try a different sound.

### Do TikTok drafts transfer to a new phone?

No. Drafts live in the app on the phone where you made them, so save them to your camera roll before switching.

### Will deleting the Only me post delete the saved video?

No. The file in your camera roll stays after you delete the private post.

### Can I recover a deleted TikTok draft?

No, there's no recently deleted folder for drafts. Once deleted, they're gone.
