You want to message someone on Bluesky privately instead of replying in public. You can: tap the **chat bubble** icon in the menu (on the web it's **Chat** in the left sidebar), tap the **new message** button, search for the account and type. You can also open someone's profile and tap the chat icon next to Follow.

![How to DM on Bluesky: open Chat, start a new message, pick the account, and check who can message you](/images/blog/how-to-dm-on-bluesky-hero-1200x675.webp)

The catch is the default setting. People can only message you if you follow them, and you can only message people whose settings allow it, which is why the chat icon sometimes isn't there.

This guide covers sending a DM, group chats (up to 50 people since June 2026), who can message you, what DMs support, how private they are, and why a DM won't send.

## Why Bluesky DMs Work Differently From Posts

![Bluesky posts are public on the AT Protocol, while DMs are stored privately on Bluesky's servers](/images/blog/how-to-dm-on-bluesky-private-1200x675.webp)

Almost everything you do on Bluesky, posts, likes, follows, is public data on the AT Protocol. DMs aren't. They're stored on Bluesky's own servers, outside the public protocol, so nobody can browse them the way they can browse your likes.

| | Posts and likes | DMs |
|---|---|---|
| Where they live | The public AT Protocol | Bluesky's servers |
| Who can see them | Anyone | People in the chat |
| End-to-end encrypted | No | No |
| Can Bluesky access them for moderation | Yes | Yes |

### What Most People Don't Know

Bluesky DMs are private, but they aren't end-to-end encrypted. Bluesky can access them for moderation. For sensitive conversations, use an encrypted app. Bluesky has partnered with the third-party app Germ for encrypted messaging, which is separate from the built-in chat.

> **Key point:** Treat Bluesky DMs like a private conversation in a company's building, not a sealed letter. Fine for everyday chat; not for anything you'd need encrypted.

## How to Send a DM on Bluesky (4 Steps)

![Four steps to send a DM on Bluesky: open Chat, tap New chat, pick the account, type and send](/images/blog/how-to-dm-on-bluesky-steps-1200x675.webp)

Labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Open the Chat Tab

Tap the **speech bubble** in the menu, or **Chat** in the left sidebar on the web.

### Step 2: Tap New Chat

Tap the **pencil** or **New chat** button.

### Step 3: Search for the Account

Type a handle or name and select the account.

### Step 4: Type and Send

Write your message and tap send.

**From a profile:** open the profile and tap the **chat** icon. No chat icon, or a "can't be messaged" note? That person only accepts messages from people they follow, or from no one. This is the step people misread as a bug.

**To share a post in a DM:** tap the **share** icon on the post and choose **Send via direct message**.

> **Pro tip:** If you want someone to be able to message you back, follow them. With the default setting, people you follow can message you; everyone else can't.

## What Bluesky DMs Support (and What They Don't Yet)

| Feature | Supported at the time of writing |
| --- | --- |
| Text and emoji | Yes |
| Links and shared Bluesky posts | Yes |
| Emoji reactions | Yes |
| Group chats | Yes, up to 50 people, with invite links |
| Photos, video and voice notes | Not yet |
| End-to-end encryption | Not built in |

Bluesky has said media in DMs will come once its safety and moderation tools for it are ready. Until then, people share an image as a post and send the post, or use another app for photos.

## How to Start a Bluesky Group Chat (Up to 50 People)

![How to start a Bluesky group chat: start a new chat, add several people or share an invite link, and control who joins](/images/blog/how-to-dm-on-bluesky-group-1200x675.webp)

1. Open the Chat tab and start a new chat.
2. Add more than one person, or create a group and share its invite link.
3. As the creator, decide who may join.

Groups hold up to 50 members at the time of writing.

## How to Change Who Can Message You on Bluesky

![Bluesky chat settings compared: Everyone, Users I follow (the default) and No one](/images/blog/how-to-dm-on-bluesky-settings-1200x675.webp)

Go to **Settings > Privacy and security > Chat** (or tap the gear icon in the Chat tab) and choose:

| Setting | Who can start a chat with you | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone | Anyone on Bluesky | Businesses and creators who want inquiries |
| Users I follow (default) | Only accounts you follow | Most people |
| No one | Nobody | Taking a break, or after harassment |

Message requests from people you don't follow appear in a separate **Requests** area, where you can accept, delete or report them. Spam usually lands there.

## Why Can't I DM on Bluesky? 4 Common Reasons

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No chat icon on a profile | Their settings only allow people they follow, or no one | Ask them to follow you, or reply publicly |
| Messages stopped working | One of you blocked the other | Blocks stop DMs; nothing to fix if it's their choice |
| Chat is disabled entirely | Age checks: in places that require them, such as the UK, Bluesky disables DMs for accounts not verified as adults | Complete Bluesky's age verification |
| A third-party app can't see your chats | Its app password wasn't given DM access | Create an app password with "Allow access to your direct messages" ticked, only if you trust the app |

## Third-Party Apps With DM Access (Use With Caution)

![Safe app password habits for Bluesky compared with risky ones like giving DM access to apps that don't need it](/images/blog/how-to-dm-on-bluesky-app-passwords-1200x675.webp)

Bluesky lets other apps sign in with an **app password** instead of your main password. When you create one, there's a box to allow access to your direct messages. Some clients and tools ask you to tick it.

### The Real Risks

- **The app can read every DM.** Including group chats and message requests.
- **It can send messages as you.** Spam sent from your account gets you reported, not the app.
- **You may forget it's there.** App passwords keep working until you delete them.

### A Safer Checklist

- Leave the DM box unticked unless the app is a chat client you trust.
- Create one app password per app, named after the app, so you can revoke just that one.
- Review and delete old app passwords in **Settings > Privacy and security > App passwords**.

> **Bottom line:** Only tick "Allow access to your direct messages" for an app whose whole job is reading your chats.

## What to Do Next: 3 Situations

- **You want inquiries from anyone:** set Chat to Everyone and check Requests daily.
- **You're getting spam:** switch to Users I follow, then report spam from Requests.
- **You need privacy for something sensitive:** move the conversation to an encrypted app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you DM on Bluesky?

Yes. Direct messages launched in 2024 and group chats in 2026. Open the Chat tab to start one.

### Can I send pictures in Bluesky DMs?

Not at the time of writing. You can send text, emoji, links and shared posts.

### Can I delete a Bluesky DM?

You can delete a message or a whole conversation for yourself from its menu. The other person may still have their copy.

### How do I stop strangers messaging me on Bluesky?

Set Chat settings to Users I follow or No one, and report spam from the Requests list.

### Are Bluesky DMs end-to-end encrypted?

No. They're private to the people in the chat but stored on Bluesky's servers, where Bluesky can access them for moderation.

### How many people can be in a Bluesky group chat?

Up to 50 at the time of writing, and the creator decides who may join.

### Why is there no chat button on someone's profile?

Their settings only allow messages from people they follow, or from no one.

### Can blocked users DM me on Bluesky?

No. Blocks stop DMs in both directions.
