You've got an old channel you're embarrassed by, a test channel you never used, or a business channel that's run its course, and you want it gone.

To delete a YouTube channel, open **YouTube Studio** while signed in to that channel, go to **Settings**, then **Channel**, then **Advanced settings**, and click **Remove YouTube content** at the bottom. After you sign in again, choose **I want to permanently delete my content**, tick the boxes and confirm. If you only want it gone for now, choose **I want to hide my channel** instead.

![How to delete a YouTube channel: Settings, Channel, Advanced settings, then Remove YouTube content](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-youtube-channel-hero-1200x675.webp)

The option is buried four levels deep, and the YouTube app doesn't offer it at all. Deletion also removes the channel's videos, comments, playlists and history, and it can't be undone, so the choice between hiding and deleting matters more than the steps.

This guide covers hiding versus deleting, the full desktop steps, how to do it from a phone, Brand Account channels, what to save first, and when a fresh start beats deleting.

## Hide or Delete Your YouTube Channel? 7 Differences

You can only delete channels you own, or manage as an owner. Before you start, decide which of the two options you want.

| | Hide channel | Delete permanently |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Videos | Private, can be restored | Deleted |
| Channel name and URL | Hidden | Removed |
| Subscriber count | Kept for when you unhide | Lost |
| Comments you posted | Deleted | Deleted |
| Playlists | Private | Deleted |
| Google account (Gmail, Drive) | Untouched | Untouched |
| Can you undo it? | Yes, create the channel again with the same account | No |

![Hiding a YouTube channel compared with deleting it permanently](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-youtube-channel-hide-vs-delete-1200x675.webp)

### What Most People Don't Know

Both options delete the comments you've posted. Hiding keeps your videos and subscriber count safe for later, but your comments across YouTube go either way. Your Google account, including Gmail and Drive, is untouched by both.

> **Key point:** If there's any chance you'll want the channel back, hide it. Hiding keeps your subscriber count and videos, and deleting can't be reversed.

## How to Delete a YouTube Channel on Desktop: 8 Steps

Menu labels change from time to time, so look for "Remove YouTube content" if a step is named differently.

![The eight steps to delete a YouTube channel in YouTube Studio](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-youtube-channel-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Switch to the Right Channel

Go to [studio.youtube.com](https://studio.youtube.com) and make sure the channel you want to delete is selected (profile picture, then **Switch account**). This is the step people get wrong: if you manage several channels, Studio may open on a different one.

### Step 2: Open Settings

Click **Settings** at the bottom left.

### Step 3: Go to Channel, Then Advanced Settings

Click **Channel**, then **Advanced settings**.

### Step 4: Click Remove YouTube Content

It's at the bottom of the Advanced settings page.

### Step 5: Sign In Again

YouTube asks you to confirm it's you.

### Step 6: Choose Delete, Not Hide

Choose **I want to permanently delete my content**. To hide the channel instead, choose **I want to hide my channel**.

### Step 7: Tick the Confirmation Boxes

Tick the boxes to confirm what will be deleted.

### Step 8: Confirm the Deletion

Click **Delete my content** and confirm with your channel's name or email if asked.

> **Pro tip:** Before Step 4, check the channel name at the top of Studio once more. It's the only thing standing between you and deleting the wrong channel.

It can take a while for videos and thumbnails to disappear everywhere.

## How to Delete a YouTube Channel on Your Phone (2 Ways)

The YouTube app doesn't offer channel deletion. You have two options:

1. **Use a mobile browser.** Open studio.youtube.com, switch to desktop mode if needed, and follow the desktop steps above.
2. **Use your Google account.** Go to myaccount.google.com, open **Data & privacy**, then **Delete a Google service**, and choose YouTube.

| Method | Works on | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Studio on desktop | Computer | Any channel, with the clearest steps |
| Studio in a mobile browser | Phone, in desktop mode | When you have no computer |
| Delete a Google service | Phone or computer | Removing YouTube from a personal Google account |
| The YouTube app | Not supported | Nothing: it doesn't offer deletion |

## Deleting a Brand Account Channel: Owner Only

Many business channels belong to a Brand Account, which can have several managers. Only the **primary owner** can delete it.

![Who can delete a Brand Account YouTube channel: the primary owner, not managers](/images/blog/how-to-delete-a-youtube-channel-brand-1200x675.webp)

Switch to the Brand Account channel first, then follow the same steps. If you want to remove the whole Brand Account, do it from the Brand Account's Google Account settings. A manager without owner rights needs the owner to do it, or to be promoted.

If you can't find **Remove YouTube content**, you're probably signed in to the wrong channel, or you're a manager rather than the owner.

## Before You Delete: 4 Things to Save First

- **Download your videos.** Use **Google Takeout** (takeout.google.com) and pick YouTube for a copy of your uploads, playlists and channel data.
- **Check monetization.** If the channel is in the YouTube Partner Program, make sure your last AdSense payment has cleared.
- **Remove it from tools.** Disconnect the channel from apps that post to it, such as schedulers, so they stop trying to publish.
- **Check memberships and subscriptions.** Paid subscriptions like YouTube Premium belong to the Google account, and the deletion screen may offer to cancel them.

## Permanent Deletion (Use With Caution)

Deleting is the only option here that can't be undone, which makes it the risky one.

### The Real Risks

- **Everything goes.** Videos, playlists, comments and the subscriber count are gone for good.
- **Wrong channel.** If you manage several channels, it's easy to be switched to the wrong one.
- **No copy.** Without a Takeout download, your original uploads may be lost if you don't have the files elsewhere.

### A Short Safety Checklist

- Download your channel with Google Takeout.
- Confirm the channel name at the top of Studio.
- Make sure any final payment has cleared.
- Disconnect the channel from scheduling tools.
- Ask yourself whether hiding would do.

> **Bottom line:** Hide first if you're unsure. You can always delete a hidden channel later, but you can't undelete one.

## If You Just Want a Fresh Start: Rename Instead

Deleting erases the subscribers and watch history you built. Often it's better to rename the channel, change the handle (the [YouTube handle checker](/tools/youtube-handle-checker) tests new ideas), set old videos to private, and start posting again.

Take Dev, whose cooking channel from five years ago has 600 subscribers and twenty old videos he's not proud of. Deleting means starting from zero. Instead, he sets the old videos to private, renames the channel and plans two new uploads a week, plus a Short on Sundays.

## What to Do Next (Pick Your Situation)

1. **You might want it back.** Hide the channel.
2. **You're sure it should go.** Download it with Takeout, disconnect it from tools, then delete it.
3. **It's a Brand Account and you're a manager.** Ask the primary owner, or ask to be promoted.
4. **You want a fresh start.** Rename it, set old videos to private and schedule new uploads.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I delete a YouTube channel that isn't mine?

No. Only the owner can delete a channel. If a channel copies your content or impersonates you, report it to YouTube instead.

### Does deleting a YouTube channel delete my Google account?

No. Gmail, Drive and other Google services stay. Only the YouTube channel and its content go.

### Can I recover a deleted YouTube channel?

Not after it's permanently deleted. A hidden channel can be restored by creating a channel again with the same account.

### How long does it take to delete a YouTube channel?

The request is immediate, but videos and thumbnails can take some time to disappear from YouTube and search results.

### Why can't I find Remove YouTube content?

You're probably signed in to the wrong channel, or you're a manager rather than the owner of a Brand Account. Switch to the channel, and check your role.

### Can I delete a YouTube channel from the app?

No. The YouTube app doesn't offer deletion. Use YouTube Studio in a browser, or Delete a Google service in your Google account.

### Do my comments disappear if I hide my channel?

Yes. Both hiding and deleting remove the comments you've posted.

### Should I download my videos before deleting?

Yes, if you want to keep them. Google Takeout gives you a copy of your uploads, playlists and channel data.
