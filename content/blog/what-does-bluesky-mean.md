You've heard someone call an idea "blue sky", or you've seen friends move to an app called Bluesky, and you want to know what the words actually mean.

"Blue sky" means **open, unlimited or not yet constrained by practical limits**. Blue-sky thinking is brainstorming without worrying about budgets or rules; blue-sky research is science done for discovery rather than a product. The **Bluesky** social network borrowed the idea: it started as a research project to build an open protocol for social media. It's pronounced exactly like the two words, **"blue sky"**, with the stress on "blue".

![What does blue sky mean? Open and unconstrained, as in blue-sky thinking, and the idea behind the Bluesky app's name](/images/blog/what-does-bluesky-mean-hero-1200x675.webp)

It's confusing because the same two words can sound ambitious or unrealistic depending on who says them, and because the app writes them as one word. This guide covers the phrase and its variations, why the app is called Bluesky, how to say and spell it, what the app is like, and how to start posting there.

## Why "Blue Sky" Means Open and Unconstrained

A clear blue sky has nothing in it: no clouds, no walls, no ceiling. The phrase took on that sense of open space with nothing boxed in, which is why it attaches to ideas that ignore current limits.

![The meanings of blue sky: blue-sky thinking, blue-sky research, a blue-sky scenario and out of a clear blue sky](/images/blog/what-does-bluesky-mean-phrase-1200x675.webp)

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue-sky thinking | Creative ideas without constraints | "Let's do some blue-sky thinking before we set the budget." |
| Blue-sky research | Research without an immediate practical goal | "The lab's funding covers blue-sky research." |
| Blue-sky scenario | The best case, if everything goes right | "In a blue-sky scenario we launch in March." |
| Blue-sky laws (US) | State laws regulating the sale of securities | Named to stop "speculative schemes which have no more basis than so many feet of blue sky" |
| Out of a clear blue sky | Suddenly, unexpectedly | "The offer came out of a clear blue sky." |

### What Most People Don't Know

In business English, "blue sky" can be positive (ambitious, imaginative) or a little sceptical (unrealistic). "That's very blue-sky" from a manager can mean "great idea" or "that will never happen". Context and tone tell you which.

> **Key point:** Blue sky means unconstrained. Whether that's a compliment depends on who's saying it and why.

## Why the Bluesky App Is Called Bluesky (and Why a Butterfly)

Bluesky began in 2019 as a Twitter-funded project to design an open, decentralized standard for social media. The name fit a blue-sky research effort: an open-ended exploration of how social networks could work. When it became an independent company it kept the name, and the open protocol it built is the AT Protocol.

![Where the Bluesky app's name came from: a 2019 research project, an independent company, the AT Protocol and a butterfly logo](/images/blog/what-does-bluesky-mean-name-1200x675.webp)

There's also an obvious visual echo. Twitter's bird was blue and lived in the sky. Bluesky's own logo is a **blue butterfly**, which replaced an earlier cloud-and-sky logo and is meant to suggest freedom and transformation rather than a bird.

| | Detail |
| --- | --- |

| Goal | An open, decentralized standard for social media |
| Protocol | The AT Protocol |
| Logo | A blue butterfly (earlier, a cloud-and-sky logo) |
| Posts are called | "Posts" in the app; "skeets" informally |

## How to Say and Spell Bluesky (One Word or Two?)

Say it as two words run together: **BLOO-sky** (/ˈbluːˌskaɪ/), with the stress on "blue".

| You mean | Write it as |
| --- | --- |
| The social app | **Bluesky** (one word, lowercase "s") |
| The everyday phrase | **blue sky** (two words) |
| The phrase before a noun | **blue-sky** (hyphenated), as in "blue-sky thinking" |

## What Bluesky the App Is Like

Bluesky is a text-first network with 300-character posts, up to four images or a video, custom feeds you can choose, and handles that can be your own domain. It's popular with journalists, academics, developers and artists. If you're deciding whether to join, read [is Bluesky worth it](/blog/is-bluesky-worth-it).

## Edge Cases: Skeets, the Old Logo and Similar Names

- **Skeets.** An informal name for Bluesky posts, coined by users. The app calls them posts.
- **The old logo.** Before the butterfly, Bluesky used a cloud-and-sky logo, so older screenshots look different.
- **Other "Blue Sky" names.** Plenty of businesses and products use the phrase. If a link says "Blue Sky", check that it really points to Bluesky before you sign in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it Bluesky or Blue Sky?

The social app is **Bluesky**, one word. The phrase is **blue sky**, two words, or **blue-sky** with a hyphen when it describes a noun.

### What does blue sky mean in business?

Ideas or plans that ignore current limits, such as budget or technology. "Blue-sky thinking" is brainstorming; a "blue-sky scenario" is the best case.

### What is the Bluesky logo?

A blue butterfly. It replaced an earlier cloud-and-sky logo and is meant to suggest freedom and change.

### What is a skeet?

An informal name for a Bluesky post, coined by users. Bluesky's app calls them posts.

### How do you pronounce Bluesky?

BLOO-sky, like the two words "blue sky", with the stress on "blue".

### What does "out of a clear blue sky" mean?

Suddenly and unexpectedly, like something arriving from an empty sky.

### Who started Bluesky?

It began in 2019 as a project funded by Twitter to build an open standard for social media, and later became an independent company.

### What are blue-sky laws?

US state laws that regulate the sale of securities, named to stop speculative schemes with "no more basis than so many feet of blue sky".
