Your account is growing, people are copying your videos, and you'd like the blue check that proves you're the real one. To get verified on TikTok, go to **Profile > menu (three lines) > Settings and privacy > Account > Verification** and submit a request with proof of who you are and evidence that you're notable, such as news coverage. If TikTok agrees, it adds the blue check next to your name.

![How to get verified on TikTok: be authentic, notable and active, then apply in Settings and privacy under Verification](/images/blog/how-to-get-verified-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

There's no follower number that guarantees verification and, at the time of writing, no way to pay for it. What TikTok looks for is an account that is **authentic, notable and active**, which is why big accounts get turned down and smaller ones sometimes get the badge.

This guide covers what the badge means, the six criteria, how to apply, how long review takes, why requests get denied, and how to spot verification scams.

## What the Blue Check on TikTok Actually Means

![What TikTok verification means compared with what it doesn't: proof of identity, not a reach boost](/images/blog/how-to-get-verified-on-tiktok-meaning-1200x675.webp)

The verified badge tells people an account really belongs to the person, brand or organization it claims to be. It's meant for accounts that people are likely to search for and that might be impersonated: public figures, well-known creators, brands, media outlets, government bodies and nonprofits.

| The badge is | The badge isn't |
|---|---|
| Proof the account is the real one | A reward for popularity |
| For accounts likely to be impersonated | Something you can buy |
| Given after a review by TikTok | A boost on the For You feed |

### What Most People Don't Know

Follower count is not on TikTok's list. Large creators are often verified because they also tend to be notable and widely searched, but the followers aren't what earns it. Independent press coverage counts for more than a big number on your profile.

> **Key point:** TikTok verifies accounts that people search for and might fake. Prove you're real and prove you're notable, and the follower count matters much less.

## What Does TikTok Look For? 6 Verification Criteria

![Six criteria for TikTok verification: authentic, notable, active, unique, secure and in good standing](/images/blog/how-to-get-verified-on-tiktok-criteria-1200x675.webp)

| Criterion | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Authentic | You are who you say you are; ID or business documents prove it |
| Notable | You're in the news or widely searched; coverage in independent media counts, paid posts and press releases don't |
| Active | You post regularly and have been on TikTok for a while (guides commonly cite six months) |
| Unique | The account is the main one for that person or brand, not a fan page, parody or backup |
| Secure | Two-step verification is on and the email or phone number is confirmed |
| In good standing | No recent Community Guidelines strikes |

TikTok says the process is subjective and changes over time, so treat this as a guide rather than a checklist that guarantees a badge.

## How to Apply for TikTok Verification in the App (6 Steps)

![Six steps to apply for TikTok verification, with uploading documents and notability links highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-get-verified-on-tiktok-apply-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Turn On Two-Step Verification

Go to **Settings and privacy > Security**, turn on two-step verification, and confirm your email and phone.

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile

Use your real name or brand name, a clear photo, a bio and a link to your website.

### Step 3: Open Verification

Go to **Settings and privacy > Account > Verification**.

### Step 4: Pick Your Category

Choose the category that fits, for example individual creator, business or government.

### Step 5: Upload Documents and Notability Links (the Step That Decides It)

Upload ID or business documents and add links that show notability: articles about you, your official website, verified accounts on other platforms. This is where most requests are won or lost. Links to your own posts or a paid press release don't count.

> **Pro tip:** Pick your three strongest independent articles and lead with them. A few solid features in real publications are better evidence than a long list of blog mentions.

### Step 6: Submit and Watch Your Inbox

TikTok replies in your inbox once it has reviewed the request.

If you don't see **Verification** in your settings, it isn't available for your account or region yet. Some regions use a web form instead, linked from TikTok's Help Center.

## How Long Does TikTok Verification Take?

Usually up to about 30 days. If you're turned down, TikTok says why in general terms, and you can apply again after 30 days.

| Stage | What happens | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| Submitted | TikTok receives your documents and links | Same day |
| Under review | TikTok checks identity and notability | Up to about 30 days |
| Approved | The blue check appears next to your name | When you get the reply |
| Denied | A general reason in your inbox | Reapply after 30 days |

## Why TikTok Verification Requests Get Denied: 5 Reasons

![Reasons TikTok verification requests get denied compared with what improves your chances](/images/blog/how-to-get-verified-on-tiktok-denied-1200x675.webp)

| Reason | Fix before you reapply |
|---|---|
| Not enough independent coverage | Earn interviews and features in local or industry media; self-published posts, sponsored articles and press releases don't prove notability |
| Incomplete or inconsistent details | Make the account name match your documents, and add a photo and bio |
| Inactive account | Post on a steady schedule for months, not in bursts |
| Recent violations | Wait out strikes and follow the Community Guidelines |
| Duplicate accounts | Make one account the main one for that person or brand |

## Paid "Verification Services" (Use With Caution)

![Safe signs of real TikTok verification compared with scam offers that charge for a blue check](/images/blog/how-to-get-verified-on-tiktok-scams-1200x675.webp)

Search for TikTok verification and you'll find people offering a guaranteed blue check for a fee, often through DMs or emails that look official. TikTok never sells verification and never asks for your password in a DM or email.

### The Real Risks

- **Account takeover.** "Verification forms" outside the app or TikTok's own site are built to capture your login.
- **You pay for nothing.** Nobody outside TikTok can add the badge.
- **Strangers can post as you.** Anyone with your login can post spam or break the rules on your account, and the strikes are yours.

### A Safer Checklist

- Apply only in **Settings and privacy > Account > Verification**, or through a form linked from TikTok's Help Center.
- Ignore DMs offering a badge, even from accounts that look official.
- Never share a password or login code.
- If you already clicked a link, change your password and check **Manage devices**.

> **Bottom line:** Real TikTok verification is free and happens inside TikTok. Anyone charging for a blue check is after your account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many followers do you need to get verified on TikTok?

There's no minimum. Large creators are often verified, but follower count alone isn't enough, and smaller accounts that are notable can be verified.

### Can you pay to get verified on TikTok?

No, not at the time of writing. TikTok doesn't sell the blue check, and anyone offering it for money is running a scam.

### Can a business get verified on TikTok?

Yes. Apply under the business category with registration documents and evidence that the brand is well known. This is separate from verifying a business for TikTok ads.

### Does verification help you go viral?

No. The badge confirms identity; it doesn't change how the For You feed ranks your videos.

### How long does TikTok verification take?

Usually up to about 30 days. If you're denied, you can apply again after 30 days.

### Why don't I see Verification in my TikTok settings?

It isn't available for your account or region yet. Some regions use a web form linked from TikTok's Help Center instead.

### Can I get verified on TikTok with a fan page?

No. TikTok verifies the main account of a person or brand, not fan pages, parodies or backups.
