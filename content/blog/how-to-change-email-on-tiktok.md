You're moving to a new inbox, or the old one belongs to a job you've left, and TikTok is still sending everything there. To change your email address on TikTok, open **Profile > menu (three lines) > Settings and privacy > Account > Account information > Email**, enter the new address and type in the verification code TikTok sends to it. TikTok may ask you to confirm with your current email, phone number or password first.

![How to change your email on TikTok: Settings and privacy, Account, Account information, Email, then verify the code](/images/blog/how-to-change-email-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

The change takes effect as soon as the new address is verified, and you can then use it to log in and reset your password. What trips people up is the verification: the code goes missing, the old inbox is gone, or the account was made with Google or Apple.

This guide covers the app and desktop steps, phone and Google or Apple sign-ups, fixes when the code doesn't arrive, and what to lock down afterwards.

## Why Your TikTok Email Matters More Than You Think

![What your TikTok email controls: logins, password resets and security alerts, compared with what it doesn't affect](/images/blog/how-to-change-email-on-tiktok-why-1200x675.webp)

Your email is how TikTok proves the account is yours. It receives password reset links, login codes and security alerts. An old address you can't open is a locked back door: the day you forget your password, there's nowhere for the reset link to go.

| Your email is used for | It doesn't affect |
|---|---|
| Logging in with email and password | Your username or display name |
| Password resets and login codes | Your followers, videos or drafts |
| Security alerts about new devices | What anyone sees on your profile |
| Account recovery with support | Apps connected through TikTok's sign-in |

### What Most People Don't Know

If you signed up with Google or Apple, TikTok's record of your email is tied to that provider. You can usually add a separate email in Account information, but the address used for "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple" changes only on Google's or Apple's side.

> **Key point:** Changing your TikTok email doesn't log you out, doesn't notify your followers and doesn't touch your videos. It only changes where TikTok sends codes and alerts.

## How to Change Your Email on TikTok in the App (7 Steps)

![Seven steps to change your email on TikTok in the app, with verifying the code from the new inbox highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-change-email-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels differ slightly between app versions and regions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Open Your Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile**.

### Step 2: Open Settings and Privacy

Tap the **menu** (three lines) at the top right, then **Settings and privacy**.

### Step 3: Go to Account Information

Tap **Account**, then **Account information**. Some versions call it **User information**.

### Step 4: Tap Email, Then Change Email

Phone sign-ups will see **Add email** here instead.

### Step 5: Confirm It's You

TikTok may send a code to your current email or phone, or ask for your password. This is where people without the old inbox get stuck: look for an option to verify another way, such as your phone number.

### Step 6: Enter the New Address

Type the new email address and tap **Send code**.

### Step 7: Type In the Code From the New Inbox

Check spam and promotions folders if it isn't there within a few minutes.

> **Pro tip:** Open the new inbox on your computer before you tap Send code. Verification codes expire, so it helps to have the inbox ready and type the code in straight away.

## How to Change Your TikTok Email on a Computer

On tiktok.com, log in, click your profile picture and choose **Settings**, then **Manage account**. If email editing isn't offered on the web for your account, make the change in the mobile app; the web settings don't always include every account option.

## What to Do If You Can't Get the TikTok Code: 5 Fixes

![Fixes for TikTok email verification problems, from a missing code to an email already in use](/images/blog/how-to-change-email-on-tiktok-fixes-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Code never arrives | Check spam and promotions, wait a few minutes, then tap resend | Filters often catch automated emails |
| "Too many attempts" | Wait an hour or so before trying again | TikTok rate-limits code requests |
| You lost access to the old email | Verify with your phone number or password instead, if offered | TikTok accepts other proof of ownership |
| You can't verify any way | **Settings and privacy > Report a problem > Account and profile** | Support can verify you manually |
| Email "already in use" | Change or remove it on the other TikTok account first | One address can belong to one account |

Support may ask for details only the owner would know, like when the account was created or the device you use. Expect replies to take a few days.

## Account Recovery Services and "Email Change" Sites (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to recover TikTok account access compared with risky recovery services](/images/blog/how-to-change-email-on-tiktok-recovery-risk-1200x675.webp)

When the code won't come and support is slow, sites and social accounts offering to "recover" or "unlock" a TikTok account for a fee start to look tempting. They can't do anything you can't, and many are scams.

### The Real Risks

- **They ask for your password and codes.** With those, they can change the email to theirs and take the account.
- **They charge for nothing.** Only TikTok can verify ownership, and its support is free.
- **They can get the account banned.** Logins from unfamiliar devices and locations look like a takeover.

### A Safer Checklist

- Use only TikTok's in-app **Report a problem** flow or its official help center.
- Never share a login code with anyone, including someone claiming to be TikTok staff.
- If you already shared details, change your password and check **Manage devices** straight away.

> **Bottom line:** No outside service can change your TikTok email faster than TikTok's own verification. Use the in-app flow and support, and keep your codes to yourself.

## What to Do After You Change Your TikTok Email

1. **Turn on 2-step verification** under Settings and privacy > Security.
2. **Check Manage devices** and log out any you don't recognize.
3. **Change your password** if you switched email because of a hack, and review linked apps.
4. **Use a shared address for business accounts**, one the team controls.

- ✓ Verify with your phone if the old inbox is gone

- ✗ Don't use a personal inbox for a business account

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my TikTok email without the old email?

Often, yes. TikTok can verify you by phone number or password instead. If none of those work, contact support through Report a problem.

### How many times can you change your email on TikTok?

TikTok doesn't publish a limit, but repeated changes in a short time can trigger a temporary block. Wait a day if you're blocked.

### Does changing your email on TikTok log you out?

No. You stay logged in on your devices. Use the new email the next time you log in.

### Will my followers see that I changed my email?

No. Your email address isn't shown on your profile.

### Why isn't TikTok sending me the code?

Usually it's in spam or promotions, or you've requested too many codes. Wait a few minutes, check every folder, then tap resend once.

### Can I use the same email on two TikTok accounts?

No. If TikTok says the email is already in use, it's linked to another account. Change or remove it there first.

### How do I change my TikTok email if I signed up with Google?

Add a separate email in Account information, or change the address on your Google account itself. The Google sign-in follows Google's address.
