You tapped Repost by accident, or your Reposts tab is full of videos you'd rather your followers didn't see. To remove a repost on TikTok, open the video, tap the **Share** arrow and tap **Remove repost**. The video leaves your Reposts tab and stops being shown to your followers as your repost. The creator's original video isn't affected.

![How to remove reposts on TikTok: open the video, tap Share, tap Remove repost, then swipe to the next one](/images/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

The frustrating part is that there's **no button to remove all reposts at once** at the time of writing: no select-all and no multi-select. Clearing them means going through your Reposts tab one by one, and there's a fast way to do it.

This guide covers undoing one repost, clearing them all at about two seconds each, the bulk-delete apps and their risks, privacy options, and how to stop others reposting your videos.

## Why TikTok Reposts Are So Easy to Add and So Hard to Clear

![Why people remove TikTok reposts: accidental taps, a Reposts tab that no longer fits, and wanting followers to see your own videos first](/images/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok-why-1200x675.webp)

The Repost button sits in the Share menu, one tap from the options people use every day. That makes accidental reposts common. Removing them, though, has no shortcut.

Common reasons to clear them:

- **You reposted something by accident.** It's an easy tap to make.
- **Your Reposts tab no longer matches your brand.** Old reposts can say more about your taste three years ago than your work today.
- **You want followers to see your own videos first**, not shares of other people's.

### What Most People Don't Know

Removing a repost is silent. TikTok doesn't send a notification when you remove one, and the creator's video, its likes and its comments stay exactly as they were. Nothing you do in your Reposts tab affects the original.

> **Key point:** Removing a repost only takes the video off your Reposts tab and out of your followers' feeds as your share. It never deletes anyone's video.

## How to Undo a Repost on TikTok (5 Steps)

![Five steps to undo a TikTok repost, with tapping Remove repost in the Share menu highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok-undo-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Go to Your Profile

Tap **Profile** in the bottom corner.

### Step 2: Open the Reposts Tab

Tap the **Reposts** tab, the circular-arrows icon.

### Step 3: Open the Video

Tap the repost you want to remove.

### Step 4: Tap the Share Arrow (Not Long-Press)

Tap the **Share** arrow on the right of the video. This is where people get stuck: the remove option lives inside the Share menu, not on your profile grid.

### Step 5: Tap Remove Repost

It's where the Repost button was. The yellow Repost icon turns back to normal, which confirms it's gone.

You can also do this from the video in your feed if you reposted it by accident a moment ago.

## How to Delete All Reposts on TikTok (Fastest Manual Method)

![The fastest way to delete all reposts on TikTok: open the newest, remove, swipe up, repeat, at about two seconds each](/images/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok-delete-all-1200x675.webp)

TikTok doesn't offer bulk removal. The fastest manual method:

1. Open the Reposts tab and tap the newest repost.
2. Tap Share, then Remove repost.
3. Swipe up to the next video in your Reposts tab and repeat.

> **Pro tip:** Stay inside the video player and keep swiping. Going back to the grid after each removal doubles the time.

With practice it's about two seconds per video. If you have hundreds, do it in sessions.

| Reposts to clear | At about 2 seconds each | Suggested approach |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | About 1 minute | One sitting |
| 100 | About 3–4 minutes | One sitting |
| 500 | About 17 minutes | Two or three sessions |
| 1,000+ | Over half an hour | Several sessions across days |

## How to Make Reposts Private on TikTok (3 Options)

There's no setting to hide just your reposts at the time of writing, and no way to remove the Reposts tab. Your options:

| Option | What it does | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Remove the reposts | Takes them off your profile completely | One at a time |
| Make your account private (**Settings and privacy > Privacy > Private account**) | Only approved followers see your activity, including reposts | Strangers can't see your videos either |
| Check your app's privacy settings | Look under **Settings and privacy > Privacy** for any repost or activity settings in your version | TikTok adds and renames options often |

## How to Stop Other People Reposting Your Videos

![How to stop other people reposting your TikTok videos: open the video's privacy settings and turn off reposting where shown](/images/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok-stop-others-1200x675.webp)

You can't hide the Repost button from yourself, but you can stop others from reposting **your** videos:

1. Open your video.
2. Tap **...** > **Privacy settings**.
3. Turn off reposting where the option is shown.

You can also restrict who can see the video, which limits reposts too.

## "Delete All Reposts" Apps and Extensions (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to clear TikTok reposts compared with risky bulk-delete apps that need account access](/images/blog/how-to-remove-reposts-on-tiktok-apps-risk-1200x675.webp)

Some third-party apps and browser extensions offer to clear every repost in one go. When you're facing 800 reposts, that's appealing.

### The Real Risks

- **They need access to your TikTok account.** That carries privacy and security risks, and some ask for your password directly.
- **Automated actions can get your account limited.** Hundreds of removals in seconds don't look like a person.
- **You can't easily check what else they do.** An extension that can remove reposts can often like, follow or read your activity too.

### If You Still Want to Use One

- Check recent reviews and the permissions it asks for.
- Never give it your password directly.
- Remove its access afterwards, and check **Manage devices** for anything you don't recognize.

> **Bottom line:** Two seconds a video by hand is slow but safe. A bulk app saves minutes and puts your account at risk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I undo a repost on TikTok?

Open the video, tap the Share arrow and choose Remove repost.

### Can I delete all my reposts on TikTok at once?

Not with TikTok's own tools at the time of writing. Remove them one by one from the Reposts tab.

### Does removing a repost delete the original video?

No. It only removes your share. The creator's video stays up.

### Does the creator know if I remove a repost?

TikTok doesn't send a notification when you remove a repost.

### Can I hide my Reposts tab?

Not at the time of writing. Removing reposts or making your account private are the ways to limit who sees them.

### Can I stop people reposting my TikToks?

Yes, where the option is shown. Open your video, tap ... > Privacy settings, and turn off reposting.

### Where is the Reposts tab on TikTok?

On your profile, next to your videos, marked with a circular-arrows icon.
