You scrolled past a video last night that you now can't stop thinking about, and it's nowhere in your likes. To see your watch history on TikTok, open your **Profile**, tap the **menu** (three lines, top right), go to **Settings and privacy**, then **Activity center**, then **Watch history**. You'll see the videos you've watched recently, newest first, and can tap any of them to play it again.

![How to see your watch history on TikTok: Profile, menu, Settings and privacy, Activity center, Watch history](/images/blog/how-to-see-watch-history-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

It's not obvious because it isn't on your profile with your likes and saves. It sits four menus deep, in the mobile app only; the TikTok website doesn't reliably show the same page.

This guide covers where TikTok keeps each kind of activity, how to open, clear or turn off watch history, how to wipe search history, where saved and liked videos live, and what to do when the list is empty.

## Where TikTok Keeps Your Activity (6 Places)

![Where TikTok keeps your activity: watch history in Activity center, saves and likes on your profile, searches under the search bar](/images/blog/how-to-see-watch-history-on-tiktok-where-1200x675.webp)

TikTok spreads your activity across your profile and Settings. Knowing which list holds what saves a lot of scrolling.

| What you're looking for | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Videos you watched | Profile > menu > Settings and privacy > Activity center > Watch history |
| Things you searched | Tap the search icon; recent searches show under the search bar |
| Videos you saved | Profile > the bookmark tab |
| Videos you liked | Profile > the heart tab |
| Videos you reposted | Profile > the repost tab (two arrows) |
| Comments you left | Activity center > Comment history, where available |

### What Most People Don't Know

Watch history doesn't go back forever. It covers a limited time, around 180 days according to most guides at the time of writing. And if a creator deletes a video or makes it private, it disappears from your history too, so a video you watched yesterday can vanish from the list today.

> **Key point:** Your watch history is visible only to you. Nobody, including the creators you watched, can see the list.

## How to See Your TikTok Watch History (5 Steps)

![Five steps to see your TikTok watch history, with Activity center highlighted](/images/blog/how-to-see-watch-history-on-tiktok-steps-1200x675.webp)

Menus move between app versions. If Watch history isn't where these steps say, update the app and look in **Activity center** first, then any **Content** or **Activity** group in Settings.

### Step 1: Tap Profile

Open TikTok and tap **Profile** in the bottom right.

### Step 2: Open the Menu

Tap the **menu** icon (three lines) in the top right.

### Step 3: Tap Settings and Privacy

It's usually near the top of the menu that opens.

### Step 4: Tap Activity Center (Not Privacy)

This is the step people get wrong. Watch history isn't under Privacy; it's under **Activity center**.

### Step 5: Tap Watch History

The list shows the newest videos first. Tap one to play it.

> **Pro tip:** When you find a video you'll want again, tap Save (the bookmark) or add it to a collection. Saves don't expire the way watch history does.

## How to Clear or Turn Off TikTok Watch History

| What you want | How |
|---|---|
| Clear everything | Open **Watch history**, tap the **gear** icon, choose **Clear history** |
| Remove some videos | Tap **Select**, pick the ones you want gone, delete them |
| Stop recording new videos | Switch off the **Watch history** toggle on the same settings page |

Turning it off hides the list from you; it doesn't stop TikTok using what you watch to shape your For You feed. ## How to Clear Your TikTok Search History (3 Steps)

![Three steps to clear your TikTok search history, from the search icon to Clear all](/images/blog/how-to-see-watch-history-on-tiktok-search-1200x675.webp)

1. Tap the **search** icon (magnifying glass) at the top of the For You feed.
2. Your recent searches appear under the search bar.
3. Tap the **X** next to a single search to remove it, or tap **Clear all** (sometimes **See more** first) to wipe the list.

Clearing search history removes the list on your device and account. It doesn't remove trending suggestions TikTok shows to everyone; those come from "You may like," which you can turn off separately.

## How to Find Saved and Liked Videos on TikTok

Saved videos are on your own profile:

1. Tap **Profile**.
2. Tap the **bookmark** tab, the one with the flag-shaped icon, under your bio.
3. Choose **Posts** to see videos, or switch to collections, sounds, effects or products you've saved.

Saving is private: the creator isn't told and nobody else can see your saved list. To keep a copy of your own video as a file, see [how to download TikTok videos](/blog/how-to-download-tiktok-videos).

Liked videos sit under the **heart** tab. That tab is private by default, but you can make it visible in **Settings and privacy > Privacy > Liked videos**.

| List | Who can see it | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Watch history | Only you | Around 180 days |
| Saved (bookmark tab) | Only you | Until you unsave |
| Liked (heart tab) | Only you by default | Until you unlike |
| Search history | Only you | Until you clear it |

## Watch History Missing or Empty? 4 Fixes

![Fixes for missing TikTok watch history: update the app, check the toggle, use the app instead of a browser, or download your data](/images/blog/how-to-see-watch-history-on-tiktok-fixes-1200x675.webp)

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| No Watch history option | Update the app | The feature and its location have changed several times |
| The list stopped updating | Check the Watch history toggle is on | If it was off, nothing new was recorded |
| Can't find it on a computer | Use the iPhone or Android app | The web versions don't show the same page |
| You need older activity | **Settings and privacy > Account > Download your data** | The export includes viewing activity and arrives as a file within a few days |

## "TikTok History Viewer" Apps and Sites (Use With Caution)

![What TikTok's own tools show compared with what history viewer apps claim and the risks they bring](/images/blog/how-to-see-watch-history-on-tiktok-viewers-1200x675.webp)

Some apps and sites promise to show your full TikTok history, or worse, someone else's: what they watched, what they liked privately, who they viewed. None of that is available outside the person's own account.

### The Real Risks

- **They can't do what they claim.** Another person's watch history is only visible to them.
- **They want your login.** Signing in through a third-party site can hand over your account.
- **Malware and subscriptions.** Many are built to collect payments or install unwanted software.

### A Safer Checklist

- Use Watch history in the app for recent videos.
- Use **Download your data** for older activity.
- Never type your TikTok password into a site that isn't TikTok.

> **Bottom line:** Everything you can legitimately see about your own history is already in TikTok. Nothing shows anyone else's.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can other people see my TikTok watch history?

No. Your watch history is visible only to you. Creators can see who viewed their profile if you both have profile views turned on, but not which of their videos you watched. Our guide on [how to turn off profile views](/blog/how-to-turn-off-profile-views-on-tiktok) covers that setting.

### How far back does TikTok watch history go?

Around 180 days at the time of writing. For anything older, use Download your data, which can include more of your viewing activity.

### Does clearing watch history reset my For You page?

No. Clearing the list hides it from you, but TikTok still uses your activity for recommendations. Use the refresh option in Content preferences if you want a fresh feed.

### Can I see my watch history on a computer?

Not reliably. The full Watch history page is in the iPhone and Android apps. On a computer, use Download your data instead.

### Why did a video disappear from my watch history?

The creator probably deleted it or made it private, or it's older than the history keeps.

### Is my liked videos list public?

It's private by default. You can make it visible in Settings and privacy > Privacy > Liked videos.

### Does TikTok tell creators when I save their video?

No. Saving is private, and the creator isn't told.
