You've built a 15-slide carousel, and your scheduler won't take it. The Instagram carousel limit depends on how you post: posts made directly in the Instagram app have allowed up to 20 photos and videos at the time of writing, but posts published through Instagram's API, which every scheduling tool uses, are limited to 10.

When you schedule a carousel with Schedule For You, you can post 2 to 10 items and mix photos and videos in the same post. If you need more, split it into two carousels or post the long version by hand.

This guide covers the limits side by side, why the numbers differ, how other platforms compare, how to order slides, and which aspect ratio keeps Instagram from cropping them.

## Why the Instagram App Allows More Slides Than Scheduled Posts

Instagram has raised the in-app carousel limit over the years. Third-party tools don't publish through the app, though. They use Instagram's official publishing API, and that API caps carousels at 10 items.

![The Instagram app's carousel limit compared with the API limit that schedulers use](/images/blog/instagram-carousel-limit-app-vs-api-1200x675.webp)

| How you post | Carousel limit at the time of writing |
|---|---|
| By hand in the Instagram app | Up to 20 photos and videos |
| Any scheduler using Instagram's API | Up to 10 |
| Schedule For You | 2 to 10, photos, videos or a mix |

### What Most People Don't Know

The 10-slide cap isn't a choice a scheduler makes; it's the limit of the API that every tool uses. A tool that claims to schedule 20-slide carousels would have to post through some other route, which is worth being wary of.

> **Key point:** Plan scheduled carousels for 10 slides. If the story needs more, it's two carousels or a manual post.

## How to Order Carousel Slides: Hook, One Idea Each, Payoff

The order of a carousel matters more than its length. People decide whether to swipe based on the first slide, and whether to save or share based on the last.

![How to order Instagram carousel slides: a hook first, one idea per slide, a payoff last](/images/blog/instagram-carousel-limit-order-1200x675.webp)

### Slide 1: The Hook

The first slide does a headline's job. Make the promise clear (a result, a question, or a "how to" in a few words) and keep text large enough to read on a phone.

### Slides 2 to 9: One Idea Per Slide

Give each slide one point, step or image. If a slide needs a paragraph to make sense, split it into two. A consistent layout (same margins, font and slide-number position) makes swiping feel smooth.

### Last Slide: The Payoff

End with a summary, the final step, or a call to action such as "save this for later". Since caption links aren't clickable on Instagram, the last slide is a good place to point people to your profile.

### Mixing Photos and Videos

A short video works well as slide 2, right after a strong still cover, or as a demo in the middle of a step-by-step set. Keep videos short so people keep swiping.

> **Pro tip:** Write the last slide first. If you know the payoff, the hook and the steps in between are much easier to cut down to 10.

## Carousel Aspect Ratios: Pick One Shape for Every Slide

Instagram shows a carousel at one shape, so every slide should share the same aspect ratio.

| Shape | Size | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait 4:5 | 1080×1350 | Most feed space; a good default for text slides |
| Square 1:1 | 1080×1080 | Clean grid, product shots, mixed sources |
| Landscape 1.91:1 | 1080×566 | Wide photos where height isn't important |

In the Instagram app, the first item generally sets the shape for the whole carousel, and later items are cropped to match. At the time of writing, published carousels behave the same way, so a mix of portrait and landscape slides can end up cropped. Schedule For You doesn't crop or resize anything, and images outside Instagram's 4:5 to 1.91:1 range will be rejected by Instagram. Export every slide at the same size first; our [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) has presets for each feed shape.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Later slides cropped | Export every slide at the same ratio | The first slide sets the shape |
| Post rejected | Keep images between 4:5 and 1.91:1 | Instagram refuses anything outside the range |
| 11+ slides won't schedule | Split into two carousels | The API caps carousels at 10 |
| Seam visible in a panorama | Keep faces and text off the cut lines | Slices are separate images |

### Seamless Panoramas

A "seamless" carousel spreads one wide image across several slides so it looks continuous as people swipe. Design a single wide image and cut it into equal slices. - Keep faces and text away from the cut lines.
- Make the first slice work on its own, since it's the cover in the feed.

## Scheduling a 10-Slide Carousel to Four Platforms

Take Hana, who runs a travel account and posts a 10-photo city guide every Friday. She wants it on Instagram and Threads as a full carousel, on LinkedIn with nine images, and on X with the best four.

1. **Prepare your slides.** Export every image at the same aspect ratio, inside Instagram's feed range, and number the files.
2. **Open the composer** and select your Instagram account, plus any other platforms.
3. **Upload 2 to 10 items** in order, or pick them from the media library.
4. **Write your caption.** Hook in the first line, within 2,200 characters for Instagram. Adjust other platforms' versions separately.
5. **Check the other platforms.** X and Bluesky take four images and LinkedIn nine. If your carousel is longer, schedule a separate post for those with your strongest images.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many photos can you put in an Instagram post?

In a carousel scheduled with Schedule For You, up to 10 photos and videos. The Instagram app may allow more for posts made in the app (up to 20 at the time of writing), but the publishing API that schedulers use is limited to 10.

### Can I mix photos and videos in one carousel?

Yes, a carousel can contain any mix of photos and videos, up to 10 items in total when scheduled.

### Why does Instagram crop some of my carousel slides?

Instagram displays a carousel at one shape, usually set by the first item, so slides with a different ratio get cropped. Export all slides at the same ratio to avoid it.

### Can I add more than 10 slides by scheduling two posts?

Not as one carousel. Split the content into two carousels and schedule them separately, or post a longer carousel by hand in the Instagram app.

### Can I schedule a carousel from a personal Instagram account?

No, scheduling requires an Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Switching to a professional account is free in Instagram's settings.

### What's the best aspect ratio for an Instagram carousel?

Portrait 4:5 (1080×1350) for most carousels, because it takes up the most feed space. Use it for every slide.

### How many hashtags can a carousel have?

Up to 30 per post, the same as any other Instagram feed post.
