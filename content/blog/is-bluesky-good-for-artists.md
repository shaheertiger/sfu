You're tired of posting a finished illustration to Instagram and watching it sink because it isn't a Reel. **Yes, Bluesky is good for many artists**, especially illustrators, comic artists, photographers and writers. The Following feed is chronological, art communities run their own feeds that surface new work, image posts aren't held back for not being video, and Bluesky has said it doesn't train generative AI on user posts.

![Is Bluesky good for artists: a chronological feed, art feeds and starter packs, and a stated no-AI-training policy, with a smaller audience](/images/blog/is-bluesky-good-for-artists-hero-1200x675.webp)

The trade-offs are real, though: the audience is smaller than Instagram's or TikTok's, there's no shop or commissions feature, and your posts are public on an open network.

Most artists treat Bluesky as one channel among several, not a replacement. This guide covers what works, what's missing, the AI question, content labels, and how to post your art so it's seen.

## Why Artists Are Moving to Bluesky (What Actually Works)

![What works for artists on Bluesky, like a chronological feed and art feeds, compared with what's missing, like a shop and a large audience](/images/blog/is-bluesky-good-for-artists-pros-cons-1200x675.webp)

- **You choose what you see.** The Following feed shows accounts you follow in order, with no algorithm deciding your latest piece isn't worth showing.
- **Art feeds and starter packs.** Community-made feeds pull in posts by hashtag, keyword or member list, so a new piece can reach people who don't follow you yet. Starter packs for illustration, comics, photography and other fields help new followers find you.
- **Images aren't second-class.** Bluesky is built around text and images. Since an April 2026 update, photos display at up to 4,000 pixels (up from 2,000), and multi-image posts show as a swipeable carousel that keeps each image's shape, at the time of writing.
- **Links aren't buried.** A post linking to your shop, Patreon or portfolio isn't hidden for leaving the app.
- **Your own domain as your handle.** A handle like @yourstudio.com doubles as proof the account is really you, which helps against impersonators.

### What Most People Don't Know

Discovery on Bluesky runs through feeds that people in your community build, not one central algorithm. Getting your work into the right art feeds, usually by using the tag or keyword listed in the feed's description, matters more than follower count when you're starting out.

> **Key point:** Bluesky rewards still images and steady posting in the right community feeds. It won't replace Instagram's reach, but it can be a calmer, more direct line to people who care about your work.

## Does Bluesky Train AI on Your Art? (What the Policy Says)

![Comparison of what Bluesky's AI policy covers, its own use, with what it can't control, outside crawlers on an open network](/images/blog/is-bluesky-good-for-artists-ai-1200x675.webp)

Bluesky said in late 2024 that it doesn't use user content to train generative AI and has no plans to; the AI it uses is for moderation and ranking the Discover feed. That's the company's own policy.

Bluesky is an open network, though, so public posts can be read by anyone, including outside crawlers, and the company has said robots.txt can't guarantee others respect your wishes. If that worries you:

- Post smaller versions rather than full-resolution files.
- Watermark your work.
- Keep full-resolution files for clients and your shop.

## Content Labels for Art: Nudity, Gore and Adult Work

If you draw nudity or gore, label the post when you upload images. Bluesky offers self-labels such as **Suggestive**, **Nudity**, **Porn** (sexually explicit) and **Graphic media**; label names can vary slightly by app version.

| Label | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Labeled post | Appears behind a warning or is hidden, depending on each viewer's settings |
| Unlabeled adult content | Can be labeled by moderators, which can affect your reach |
| Profile picture and banner | Must stay safe for work |

Labeling keeps your account in good standing and lets people who want that work see it. Viewers who are 18 or older choose whether to see adult art.

## How to Post Art on Bluesky So It Gets Seen (5 Steps)

![Five steps to post art on Bluesky: lead with the image, write alt text, use the art feed's tag, share works in progress, keep a rhythm](/images/blog/is-bluesky-good-for-artists-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Lead With the Image, Add a Line of Context

Title, medium, a short story about the piece. Bluesky posts are 300 characters; our [Bluesky character limit](/blog/bluesky-character-limit) guide covers the details.

### Step 2: Write Alt Text

Describe the piece for people using screen readers. It's also a courtesy the Bluesky art community notices, and the step people skip most.

### Step 3: Use the Tag the Art Feeds Pick Up

One or two tags, such as the ones listed in each art feed's description. More isn't better.

> **Pro tip:** Before posting, open the art feeds you'd like to appear in and read their descriptions. Many list exactly which tag or keyword gets a post included.

### Step 4: Post Work in Progress

Sketches and process clips get replies and keep followers around between finished pieces.

### Step 5: Keep a Steady Rhythm

Post when your followers are online, and keep it regular. A piece a week you can sustain beats a burst you can't.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Bluesky good for artists?

Yes, for many. It has a chronological Following feed, art feeds and starter packs, and a stated policy of not training generative AI on posts. The audience is smaller than Instagram's, so most artists use both.

### Is Bluesky better than Instagram for artists?

It's different. Instagram has a far bigger audience and pushes Reels; Bluesky is smaller, chronological by default and friendlier to still images and links. Many artists post to both.

### Does Bluesky compress images?

Yes, like every network. At the time of writing, photos display at up to 4,000 pixels on the long side, and large uploads are resized and compressed.

### Can you sell art on Bluesky?

Not inside the app. Link to your shop or commission form in your bio, pinned post and image posts.

### Is Bluesky good for NSFW artists?

Bluesky allows adult art when it's labeled, and viewers who are 18 or older choose whether to see it. Profile pictures and banners must stay safe for work.

### Does Bluesky use my art to train AI?

Bluesky has said it doesn't train generative AI on user posts. Because it's an open network, outside crawlers can still read public posts, so watermark or post smaller versions if that concerns you.

### How do artists get discovered on Bluesky?

Through community feeds, starter packs and reposts. Use the tag a feed lists in its description and post consistently.

### Do hashtags work on Bluesky?

Yes, and many art feeds use them to pick up posts. See [does Bluesky use hashtags](/blog/does-bluesky-use-hashtags) for details.
