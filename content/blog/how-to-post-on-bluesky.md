You've just joined Bluesky and want to get your first post out, or you want to do more than plain text. To post on Bluesky, tap the **pencil** (New post) button at the bottom right of the app, or **New Post** in the sidebar on the web, write up to 300 characters, add images, a video or a GIF if you like, and tap **Post**.

![How to post on Bluesky: tap the pencil, write up to 300 characters, add media and tap Post](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-bluesky-hero-1200x675.webp)

The basics are simple. What trips people up is the detail: posts can't be edited afterwards, GIFs sometimes show as a still frame, and the app has no way to schedule a post for later.

This guide covers each step in the composer, what a post can include, GIFs, threads, reply controls, and how to schedule posts.

## Why Bluesky Posting Feels Different From X

Bluesky looks a lot like X, but a few rules change how you write.

![What carries over from X to Bluesky, and what's different](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-bluesky-vs-x-1200x675.webp)

| | Bluesky | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Text limit | 300 characters | Slightly more room than X's 280 |
| Editing | Not possible after posting | Check before you tap Post |
| Scheduling | Not in the app | Use a third-party tool |
| Feeds | Following, Discover and custom feeds | The post's language label helps language-based feeds |

### What Most People Don't Know

Because there's no edit button, a typo means deleting and reposting, which loses any likes and replies. Our guide to [editing Bluesky posts](/blog/can-you-edit-bluesky-posts) covers the workarounds. That's also why drafting ahead helps: a queued post can be fixed right up until it goes out.

> **Key point:** On Bluesky, the composer is your only chance to get it right. Write, reread, then post.

## How to Post on Bluesky Step by Step (App and Web)

Menu labels change between app versions, so yours may differ slightly.

![Seven steps to post on Bluesky, from the pencil button to Post](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-bluesky-steps-1200x675.webp)

### Step 1: Open Bluesky and Sign In

Use the app or [bsky.app](https://bsky.app) in a browser.

### Step 2: Tap the Pencil (or New Post on the Web)

The pencil button sits at the bottom right on mobile. On the web, it's **New Post** in the left sidebar.

### Step 3: Write Your Post, Watching the Counter

The counter shows how many of the 300 characters you have left.

### Step 4: Add Images, Video, a GIF or Emoji

Tap the **image** icon for photos or a video, the **GIF** button for a GIF, or the **emoji** button.

### Step 5: Set Who Can Reply

Tap **Anyone can interact** to limit replies and quotes, for example to people you follow.

### Step 6: Check the Language Label

Tap the **language** label to set the post's language. It helps language-based feeds show your post to the right people. This is the step most people skip.

### Step 7: Tap Post

The post goes to your profile and your followers' Following feed straight away, and may surface in Discover and custom feeds.

> **Pro tip:** Add alt text to every image. Bluesky allows up to 2,000 characters per image, and many people on the network expect it.

## Can You Upload GIFs to Bluesky? Yes, 2 Ways

![Two ways to post a GIF on Bluesky: the GIF picker or your own file](/images/blog/how-to-post-on-bluesky-gifs-1200x675.webp)

- **GIF picker.** Tap the **GIF** button in the composer and search. The GIF is embedded from Bluesky's GIF provider and plays in the feed.
- **Your own GIF.** Attach a GIF file through the image button. Current versions of the app treat an animated GIF like a short looping video.

Only one GIF or video fits in a post, and it can't be combined with images.

| Problem | Fix | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| GIF shows as a still frame | Convert it to MP4 and attach it as a video | Video always animates |
| Can't add a GIF next to photos | Post the GIF on its own, or in a reply | One GIF or video per post, no mixing |
| Post button greyed out | Trim to 300 characters | The counter has gone negative |
| Upload fails | Check the video's length and size | Over 10 minutes or 300 MB is rejected |

## How to Post a Thread on Bluesky (3 Steps)

For anything over 300 characters, write a thread:

1. Write the first post.
2. Tap **Add another post** (the plus below the text box) to add the next part.
3. Repeat, then tap **Post all**.

You can also reply to your own post later to extend it. Make the first post stand on its own, since that's the one people see in the feed.

## Why Can't I Post on Bluesky? 4 Common Causes

- **Over 300 characters.** Trim the text or split it into a thread.
- **An image that's too large.** Resize it and try again.
- **A video over the length or size limit.** Trim or compress it.
- **An outage.** Check our guide to [whether Bluesky is down](/blog/is-bluesky-down).

## How to Schedule Bluesky Posts (the App Can't)

The Bluesky app has no scheduling. Take Nadia, a freelance illustrator who wants to share a new piece every Tuesday and Thursday at 9 a.m. Eastern, when her US followers are scrolling. She lives in Lisbon, so that's mid-afternoon her time, right when she's deep in client work.

![Writing a Bluesky post with a 300-character check in the Schedule For You composer](/images/blog/composer-bluesky-1440x900.webp)

Schedule For You connects to Bluesky with an **app password** (create one in Bluesky under **Settings > Privacy and security > App passwords**, labels may vary). Nadia writes both posts on Monday, adds her images and alt text, and picks the times. In the composer she can:

- Write a post with up to four images or a video and pick a time.
- Write a thread by putting a line with `---` between the parts; each part is checked against 300 characters before anything goes out.
- Add a first reply that posts right after the main post, such as a link.
- Send the same draft to X and Threads, with a tailored version for each.

![The Schedule For You queue with Bluesky posts lined up for the week](/images/blog/queue-1440x900.webp)

| | Bluesky app | Other schedulers | Schedule For You |

| Threads | Yes, posted live | Varies | Yes, split with `---` |
| Same post to X and Threads | Post each by hand | Varies | One draft, a version per platform |
| Cost | Free | Varies | Free: 3 accounts, 10 scheduled posts at a time, no card |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I post on Bluesky from a computer?

Go to bsky.app, sign in, and click New Post in the left sidebar. The composer works the same as in the app.

### Can you upload GIFs to Bluesky?

Yes, use the GIF button to search, or attach your own GIF file. If it doesn't animate, upload it as an MP4 video instead.

### Why can't I post on Bluesky?

Usually the post is over 300 characters, an image or video is too large, or Bluesky is having an outage. Check [whether Bluesky is down](/blog/is-bluesky-down).

### Can I post the same thing to Bluesky and X?

Yes, most X posts fit Bluesky's 300 characters unchanged. A scheduler can send both from one draft.

### Can you edit a post on Bluesky?

No. You can delete a post and repost it, but not edit it once it's live.

### How many images can I add to a Bluesky post?

Up to four images, or one video or GIF, at the time of writing.

### Can I stop people replying to my Bluesky post?

Yes, tap Anyone can interact in the composer and choose who can reply and quote.
