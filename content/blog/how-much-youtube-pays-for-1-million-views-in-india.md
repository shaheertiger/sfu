Your video just crossed 10 lakh views and you want to know what that's worth. There is no official YouTube view price in India. YouTube pays creators a share of ad revenue, so 1 million views earn **1,000 × your RPM** (revenue per 1,000 views). If your RPM were ₹40, 1 million views would earn about ₹40,000. If it were ₹150, the same views would earn about ₹1,50,000.

![YouTube earnings for 1 million views in India equal 1,000 times your RPM, which depends on audience country, format and niche](/images/blog/how-much-youtube-pays-for-1-million-views-in-india-hero-1200x675.webp)

Those figures are hypothetical. Two Indian channels with a million views each can earn very different amounts, and the reasons are mostly about who is watching and what kind of video they watched. Viral screenshots of "₹X per million" don't tell you much about your own channel.

This guide covers the formula, why RPM in India is often lower, how Shorts and long-form differ, worked scenarios, taxes, and how YouTube compares with other platforms.

## Why YouTube Doesn't Have a Fixed Price Per View in India

![What sets your YouTube RPM: advertiser demand, viewer country, format, niche, season and ad suitability](/images/blog/how-much-youtube-pays-for-1-million-views-in-india-factors-1200x675.webp)

YouTube earns money when advertisers pay to show ads, and it shares part of that with creators. At the time of writing, creators in the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) receive 55% of net ad revenue on long-form videos and 45% of the revenue allocated to them from the Shorts feed. The full rules are in [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube).

Because the money starts with advertisers, the value of a view changes with:

- how much advertisers are paying to reach that viewer,
- whether an ad actually played (not every view shows one),
- the format (long-form, Shorts or live),
- and other income, such as YouTube Premium revenue and memberships, which RPM also includes.

You also earn nothing from ads until your channel is in YPP. See [when YouTube starts paying you](/blog/when-does-youtube-start-paying-you) for the requirements and payment timeline.

### What Most People Don't Know

YouTube's ad rates follow the **viewer**, not the creator. An Indian creator whose audience is largely in India will mostly earn at Indian ad prices. An Indian creator whose English-language tutorials are watched in the US, UK or Canada will earn more like a channel based in those countries, for those views.

> **Key point:** Your passport doesn't set your RPM. Your viewers' countries, your format and your niche do.

## How Much Does YouTube Pay for 1 Million Views in India? (The Formula)

**Estimated earnings = (views ÷ 1,000) × RPM**

For 1 million views, that's 1,000 × your RPM. The RPMs below are invented to show the math. They are not averages for India or for any niche.

| Hypothetical RPM | 1 lakh views (100,000) | 10 lakh views (1 million) | 1 crore views (10 million) |

| ₹5 | ₹500 | ₹5,000 | ₹50,000 |
| ₹25 | ₹2,500 | ₹25,000 | ₹2,50,000 |
| ₹75 | ₹7,500 | ₹75,000 | ₹7,50,000 |

### How to Find Your Real RPM in YouTube Studio (3 Steps)

Menu labels change between versions of YouTube Studio, so look for the closest match.

1. **Open YouTube Studio** on desktop once your channel is monetized.
2. **Go to Analytics, then Revenue.** Your RPM is listed there for the period you pick.
3. **Open Advanced mode and group by Geography or content type.** This shows how much each country's views, and Shorts versus long-form, earn on your channel.

> **Pro tip:** Don't confuse RPM with CPM. CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions before YouTube's share; RPM is what you actually earn per 1,000 views. The step people get wrong is multiplying views by CPM and expecting that money.

## Why RPM in India Is Often Lower Than in the US

Creators in India often notice that their RPM is lower than what creators with US audiences describe. The main reason is advertiser demand: ad prices are set by how much advertisers compete to reach viewers in a market, and in markets like the US, advertisers typically pay more per ad than they do in India.

### More Views Can Still Mean More Money

A lower RPM doesn't make Indian audiences less valuable for a creator. India has an enormous YouTube audience, so channels can reach view counts that make up for lower per-view rates. Many successful Indian channels earn mostly from scale, sponsorships and brand deals rather than a high RPM.

## Shorts vs Long-Form: Why 1 Million Views Aren't Equal

![Long-form YouTube videos keep 55 percent of their own ad revenue while Shorts share a pooled 45 percent](/images/blog/how-much-youtube-pays-for-1-million-views-in-india-formats-1200x675.webp)

| | Long-form videos | Shorts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How ads are served | Before, during or after your video | Between Shorts in the feed |
| How revenue is split | 55% of net ad revenue from your video | Pooled, then allocated by share of Shorts views; you keep 45% |
| Mid-roll ads | Possible on videos 8 minutes or longer | Not applicable |
| RPM | Set by your own video's ads | Usually different from long-form; check your own data |

Many creators find their Shorts RPM much lower than their long-form RPM, which is why a viral Short with a million views might earn less than a long video with a fraction of the views. Filter the Revenue tab by content type to compare yours.

## 5 Things That Move the YouTube View Price in India

### 1. Which Language and Country Is Your Audience In?

Content in Hindi and other Indian languages mostly reaches viewers in India. English content can reach both domestic and international viewers. Neither is "better"; the language you choose shapes which advertisers you reach.

### 2. Which Niches Pay the Most?

Topics such as personal finance, technology, education and business tools tend to attract advertisers who pay more. Comedy, vlogs and general entertainment can draw huge audiences, but often at lower ad prices.

### 3. Does the Season Matter?

Yes. Advertiser budgets in India often rise around major festive and shopping seasons and fall afterward. The same video can earn more in one month than another without any change in views.

### 4. Do Longer Videos Earn More?

Often. Longer videos with good retention give more chances for ads to play, including mid-rolls. Short or clicked-away views earn less.

### 5. Is Your Video Fully Ad-Suitable?

Videos with limited ads (a yellow icon in YouTube Studio) earn less. Strong language, violence or sensitive topics can reduce how many advertisers are willing to appear.

## Taxes and Deductions to Plan For

- **US tax withholding.** Creators outside the US must submit tax info in AdSense. Google may withhold US tax on earnings from US viewers; submitting your tax info can reduce the rate under the India–US tax treaty, and not submitting it can lead to higher withholding. Check AdSense for the current rules.
- **Indian income tax.** YouTube income is taxable in India. Rules can change, so talk to a chartered accountant or tax professional.
- **Payments in rupees.** AdSense pays to your bank account once you pass the payment threshold for your currency.

## "Buy Views" and Monetization Shortcuts (Use With Caution)

Some services sell views, watch hours or subscribers to push a channel into YPP faster or to make videos look popular.

### The Real Risks

- **Fake engagement breaks YouTube's policies**, and YouTube can remove views, reject a YPP application or take action on a channel.
- **Bought views don't earn.** Ad revenue comes from real viewers that advertisers pay to reach.
- **They distort your data**, so you can't tell which videos really work.

> **Bottom line:** Real viewers in the right niche are the only views that raise your earnings. Skip anything that sells them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does YouTube pay for 1 million views in India?

It's 1,000 times your RPM. At a hypothetical RPM of ₹40 that's about ₹40,000; at ₹150 it's about ₹1,50,000. Your actual RPM is in the Revenue tab of YouTube Studio.

### How much does YouTube pay for 1 lakh views in India?

It depends on your RPM. 1 lakh views is 100 × your RPM, so a hypothetical RPM of ₹30 would give about ₹3,000.

### How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views in India?

That amount is your RPM, and it isn't fixed. It differs by channel, niche, audience country, season and format. Check the Revenue tab in YouTube Studio for your own figure.

### Why is YouTube RPM lower in India than in the US?

Advertisers usually pay less per ad to reach viewers in India than in markets like the US, so the same number of views brings in less ad revenue. Views from international audiences on an Indian channel are priced by those viewers' countries.

### Do YouTube Shorts views pay in India?

Yes, once your channel is in YPP with Shorts monetization accepted. Shorts revenue is pooled and shared based on your share of Shorts views, and creators keep 45% of their allocation at the time of writing. Shorts RPM is often lower than long-form RPM.

### Does YouTube pay in rupees?

Yes. AdSense can pay Indian creators in rupees by bank transfer once your balance passes the payment threshold and verification steps are complete.

### How many views do you need to earn ₹10,000 on YouTube?

Divide ₹10,000 by your RPM and multiply by 1,000. At a hypothetical RPM of ₹20, that's 5 lakh views; at ₹100, it's 1 lakh views.

### Is YouTube income taxable in India?

Yes. YouTube income is taxable in India, and US tax may also be withheld on earnings from US viewers unless you submit your tax info. Talk to a chartered accountant for your situation.
