You've finished the edit, the thumbnail is ready, and now you're hovering over the Publish button wondering if 9 PM on a Tuesday is a mistake. The best time to post on YouTube is **two to three hours before your audience's peak viewing time**. For most channels that means **early afternoon on weekdays (around 1 to 4 PM in your audience's time zone)** and **mornings on Saturday and Sunday**.

![Best time to post on YouTube: two to three hours before your viewers peak, weekday afternoons and weekend mornings for most channels](/images/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube-hero-1200x675.webp)

The lead time matters because YouTube needs a little while to process a new video and start recommending it, so publishing just before the peak catches the wave instead of chasing it.

Our [hour-by-hour YouTube heatmap](/best-time-to-post/youtube) shows when viewing activity peaks. Use it the same way: publish two to three hours before the peak it shows, not at the peak itself.

General charts only get you close. The precise answer for your channel is in YouTube Studio, and this guide shows where to find it, how Shorts differ, and how to keep a schedule once you've picked a slot.

## Why Upload Time Matters Less on YouTube Than You Think

![Comparison of what upload time affects on YouTube, the first day, with what matters over a video's life, like thumbnail, title and watch time](/images/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube-why-1200x675.webp)

On Instagram or TikTok, a post that misses its first hour can be gone for good. YouTube works differently. A video keeps earning views for months through search and suggested videos, so a video published at a bad hour isn't lost.

Timing mostly affects the first day. That first day decides how quickly YouTube tests the video with new viewers, which is why it's worth getting right, but it isn't the deciding factor.

### What Most People Don't Know

YouTube Studio already tells you when your own viewers are online. Most creators copy a generic "best time" chart from a blog when the chart for their exact audience is two clicks away in Analytics.

> **Key point:** Thumbnail, title and watch time matter far more over a video's life than the hour you upload. Pick a good slot, then put your energy into the video itself.

## Best Times to Post on YouTube by Day (Audience's Local Time)

![YouTube posting windows by day of the week: weekday afternoons and weekend mornings for most channels](/images/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube-days-1200x675.webp)

These windows are a starting point for a general audience. Use them until your own Studio data says otherwise.

| Day | Good windows (audience's local time) |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Thursday | 1 to 4 PM, with a smaller window at 7 to 9 PM |
| Friday | 12 to 3 PM |
| Saturday | 9 to 11 AM and 3 to 5 PM |
| Sunday | 9 AM to 12 PM |

These come from aggregated studies of viewing activity and line up with YouTube's own "when your viewers are on YouTube" chart for most audiences in North America and Europe. Treat them as a pattern, not a promise.

### Does Your Niche Shift the Window?

Yes. The table above describes a general audience. Two common shifts:

| Channel type | Typical shift | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gaming and entertainment | Later, into the evening | Viewers watch after school or work |
| Business and education | Earlier in the day | Viewers watch during work hours or breaks |
| General interest | Matches the table | Broad audience, broad pattern |

If your channel sits in one of the first two groups, move your test slot in that direction, then check it against your own chart.

## How to Find Your Own Best Time in YouTube Studio (Free Method)

![Three steps to find your best upload time: open Analytics, choose the Audience tab, read the when-your-viewers-are-on-YouTube chart](/images/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube-studio-1200x675.webp)

This is the method that beats every generic chart, including the one above. Menu labels change between Studio versions, so look for the closest match if something is worded differently.

### Step 1: Open Analytics in YouTube Studio

Go to studio.youtube.com and click **Analytics** in the left menu.

### Step 2: Switch to the Audience Tab

Open the **Audience** tab at the top of the Analytics page.

### Step 3: Read the "When Your Viewers Are on YouTube" Chart

Find the **When your viewers are on YouTube** card. It's a grid of days and hours, and the darker the bar, the more of your viewers are online at that time. Small or new channels may not see it until they have enough viewers for YouTube to show the data.

### Step 4: Count Back Two to Three Hours

Find the darkest block and schedule your upload two to three hours earlier. If the darkest block is Thursday at 6 PM, aim for 3 or 4 PM. This is the step people get wrong: they publish at the peak, when it's already a little late.

> **Pro tip:** The chart shows times in your own local time zone, not your viewers'. That's useful: you can read the darkest block and schedule against your own clock without converting anything.

### Step 5: Pick One Slot If Your Audience Spans Time Zones

If your viewers are spread across continents, the chart will show a wider, flatter pattern. Choose the block that covers the most viewers, and keep using it.

## YouTube Shorts vs Long Videos: Different Timing Rules

![Two lists comparing long YouTube videos, which keep earning through search, with Shorts, which depend on their first hours in the feed](/images/blog/best-time-to-post-on-youtube-shorts-1200x675.webp)

Shorts are shown in a feed that refreshes constantly, so the first hours matter more than they do for long videos, and the daily pattern is flatter.

| | Long videos | Shorts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where views come from over time | Search and suggested videos, for months | Mostly the Shorts feed, early on |
| How much the first hours matter | Some | More |
| Good times | Weekday afternoons, weekend mornings | Mornings (7 to 9 AM), lunch and after 7 PM |
| Best rhythm | A consistent weekly slot | A steady daily rhythm |

Post Shorts when your audience is scrolling. A Short published at 2 AM gets its first viewers at 2 AM, and that first batch sets its early performance.

| Mistake | Fix | Why it works |

| Publishing at the exact peak | Publish two to three hours before it | Gives YouTube time to start recommending the video |

## What to Do Next: A 4-Week Timing Test

1. **Week 1:** Open the Audience tab and note your darkest block. If there's no chart yet, start with the table above.
2. **Week 2:** Publish two to three hours before that block, and schedule the upload so you don't have to be online.
3. **Weeks 3 and 4:** Keep the same slot. Change only one thing at a time, or you won't know what worked.
4. **After four weeks:** Compare first-day views across those uploads. If one slot clearly wins, make it your regular slot.

For Shorts, run the same test with a daily rhythm instead of a weekly one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best time to post on YouTube?

Two to three hours before your audience's peak, which for most channels means early afternoon on weekdays and mornings at weekends. The Audience tab in YouTube Studio Analytics has the exact chart for your viewers.

### What is the best time to post YouTube Shorts?

When your audience is scrolling: mornings, lunchtime and evenings, on a daily rhythm. Shorts depend on their first hours more than long videos do.

### What is the best day to post on YouTube?

Weekend mornings and weekday afternoons are strongest for general audiences. A consistent weekly slot matters more than the specific day.

### Is it better to post at the same time every week?

Yes. A regular slot trains viewers to expect you and gives you a fair way to compare uploads.

### Why don't I see the "When your viewers are on YouTube" chart?

Usually because the channel doesn't have enough viewers yet for YouTube to show it. Use the general windows until it appears.

### Does posting at the wrong time ruin a YouTube video?

No. Long videos keep earning views through search and suggestions for months. Timing mostly affects the first day.

### Should I post at the same time in every time zone?

You can only pick one upload time, so choose the block on your Studio chart that covers the most viewers. The chart already accounts for where they are.

If your channel is new and the chart is empty, start with our [YouTube best-time heatmap](/best-time-to-post/youtube) or a country page such as [YouTube in the USA](/best-time-to-post/youtube/in-usa) or [YouTube in Germany](/best-time-to-post/youtube/in-germany).

### Can I schedule YouTube videos in advance?

Yes. In YouTube Studio, set Visibility to Schedule and choose a date and time. Schedulers like Schedule For You can also publish to YouTube alongside other platforms.
