To go viral on TikTok, make videos people watch to the end and watch again. The For You feed (FYP) shows each new video to a small test group, then to bigger groups when people in that group watch most of it, rewatch, comment, share or follow. A strong hook in the first one or two seconds, a short and tight edit, and a topic a specific audience cares about matter far more than hashtags or the exact minute you post. Nobody can promise a viral video, but posting often gives you more chances to land one.

## What counts as viral on TikTok?

There's no official number. A useful rule of thumb is to compare a video with your own normal results:

| Your usual views | A video that has "gone viral" for you |
| --- | --- |
| Under 1,000 | 10,000 to 100,000+ |
| 1,000 to 10,000 | 100,000 to 1 million+ |
| 10,000+ | 1 million+ |

Most people mean a video that reached far beyond their followers, usually 100,000 views or more, mostly from the For You feed. Your video analytics show this: check **Traffic source** and look for a high share from "For You."

## What does FYP mean on TikTok?

**FYP** stands for **For You Page**, the main feed that opens when you launch TikTok. It's personalized for each viewer, so "getting on the FYP" means TikTok is recommending your video to people who don't follow you. Every public, eligible video starts on the FYP for a small group; the question is how far it travels. People add #fyp or #foryou to captions, but there's no evidence those tags change reach.

## How the For You feed decides what spreads

TikTok says recommendations are based on how people interact with videos (watching, rewatching, liking, sharing, commenting, following), on information in the video like captions, sounds and hashtags, and, less strongly, on device and language settings. In practice:

- **Watch time and completion** are the strongest signals. A 15-second video watched to the end beats a 60-second video people leave at 10 seconds.
- **Rewatches and shares** suggest a video is worth showing to more people.
- **Comments** help, especially when people reply to each other.
- **Follower count** matters less than on other apps. New accounts go viral all the time.

Videos that break the Community Guidelines, or that TikTok marks as ineligible for the For You feed, won't be recommended at all. ## 8 ways to raise your odds of going viral

1. **Hook in the first second.** Start with the result, the question or the surprising bit. Cut any intro, logo or "hey guys."
2. **Put text on screen.** Many people watch without sound. A short line at the top tells them why to keep watching.
3. **Keep it as short as the idea allows.** Trim every pause. Shorter videos are easier to finish and rewatch.
4. **Pick a clear audience.** "Budget meal prep for nurses" travels further than "food." TikTok needs to know who to test the video on.
5. **Use trends with your own angle.** A trending sound or format gives you a familiar frame; your niche gives people a reason to follow.
6. **Ask for a comment, not a like.** A question or a "which one would you pick?" ending starts conversations.
7. **Post consistently.** Three to five videos a week gives you more tests than one perfect video a month. Our guide to the [best time to post on TikTok](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) helps you pick slots.
8. **Reply to comments with videos.** A video reply to a top comment is an easy second video on a topic that already worked.

## Can you go viral on TikTok overnight?

Yes, it happens: a video can pick up most of its views in the first 24 to 48 hours. But "overnight" success usually follows weeks of posting. Videos can also take off days or weeks later, when a sound trends or a new audience finds them. Don't delete videos that start slowly; let them sit.

If you want more of those chances without filming every day, batch your work. Film several videos in one session, then schedule them. ## How to reset your FYP on TikTok

If your own For You feed is full of things you don't want, TikTok lets you start fresh:

1. Go to **Profile**, tap the menu (three lines) and open **Settings and privacy**.
2. Tap **Content preferences**.
3. Tap **Refresh your For You feed**, then **Continue** and confirm.

Your feed then acts like a new account's and learns from what you watch next. Your followers, profile and messages aren't affected. Availability can vary by region and app version. For lighter fixes, long-press a video and tap **Not interested**, or add keyword filters under Content preferences.

## What to do after a video goes viral

- **Pin it** to the top of your profile so new visitors see it first.
- **Post a follow-up within a day or two**: part two, a reply to the top comment, or the next step.
- **Update your bio** so new visitors know what to expect. Our [TikTok bio generator](/tools/tiktok-bio-generator) can help.
- **Check the analytics** to learn which part of the video held people.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many views is viral on TikTok?
There's no official threshold. Most people call 100,000 to 1 million views viral, but a video with ten times your normal views has gone viral for your account.

### Do #fyp and #foryou hashtags help you go viral?
There's no evidence they do. Hashtags help TikTok understand a video's topic, so a few specific ones are more useful than generic tags.

### Does posting time affect going viral?
A little. Posting when your audience is active helps the first test group engage quickly, but a strong video can take off at any hour.

### Can a new account go viral on TikTok?
Yes. TikTok shows new accounts' videos on the For You feed too, so follower count matters less than on most platforms.

### How do I get on the FYP on TikTok?
Every public, eligible video is tested on the For You feed. To get further, make videos people finish and share, stay within the Community Guidelines and post regularly.
