You've posted a dozen TikToks, each stuck at a few hundred views, and you want to know what the viral ones do differently. To go viral on TikTok, make videos people watch to the end and watch again. The For You feed (FYP) shows each new video to a small test group, then to bigger groups when people in that group watch most of it, rewatch, comment, share or follow.

![How to go viral on TikTok: a hook in the first two seconds, high watch time and posting often enough to get chances](/images/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok-hero-1200x675.webp)

A strong hook in the first one or two seconds, a short and tight edit, and a topic a specific audience cares about matter far more than hashtags or the exact minute you post. Nobody can promise a viral video, but posting often gives you more chances to land one.

This guide covers what counts as viral, how the For You feed decides, eight ways to raise your odds, how to reset your own FYP, and what to do once a video takes off.

## How the TikTok For You Feed Decides What Goes Viral

![How a TikTok video spreads: a small test group first, then bigger groups when people watch, rewatch and share](/images/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok-fyp-1200x675.webp)

**FYP** stands for **For You Page**, the main feed that opens when you launch TikTok. It's personalized for each viewer, so "getting on the FYP" means TikTok is recommending your video to people who don't follow you. Every public, eligible video starts on the FYP for a small group; the question is how far it travels.

TikTok says recommendations are based on how people interact with videos (watching, rewatching, liking, sharing, commenting, following), on information in the video like captions, sounds and hashtags, and, less strongly, on device and language settings.

| Signal | How much it matters | What it looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watch time and completion | The strongest | A 15-second video watched to the end beats a 60-second video people leave at 10 seconds |
| Rewatches and shares | Strong | Suggests the video is worth showing to more people |
| Comments | Helps | Especially when people reply to each other |
| Follower count | Less than on other apps | New accounts go viral all the time |
| #fyp and #foryou | No evidence they help | Specific hashtags describe the topic better |

### What Most People Don't Know

Videos that break the Community Guidelines, or that TikTok marks as ineligible for the For You feed, won't be recommended at all, however good they are. If your views suddenly drop to almost nothing, see [why your TikTok isn't getting views](/blog/why-is-my-tiktok-not-getting-views) and how a [TikTok shadowban](/blog/tiktok-shadowban) differs from a slow week.

> **Key point:** TikTok tests every eligible video. Going viral isn't about getting in; it's about holding the first test group long enough to earn the next one.

## What Counts as Viral on TikTok? (Views by Account Size)

There's no official number. A useful rule of thumb is to compare a video with your own normal results:

| Your usual views | A video that has "gone viral" for you |
| --- | --- |
| Under 1,000 | 10,000 to 100,000+ |
| 1,000 to 10,000 | 100,000 to 1 million+ |
| 10,000+ | 1 million+ |

Most people mean a video that reached far beyond their followers, usually 100,000 views or more, mostly from the For You feed. Your video analytics show this: check **Traffic source** and look for a high share from "For You."

## 8 Ways to Raise Your Odds of Going Viral

![Bar chart of TikTok signals: watch time strongest, then rewatches and shares, comments, and no evidence for #fyp hashtags](/images/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok-tips-1200x675.webp)

### 1. Hook in the First Second

Start with the result, the question or the surprising bit. Cut any intro, logo or "hey guys."

### 2. Put Text on Screen

Many people watch without sound. A short line at the top tells them why to keep watching.

### 3. Keep It as Short as the Idea Allows

Trim every pause. Shorter videos are easier to finish and rewatch.

### 4. Pick a Clear Audience

"Budget meal prep for nurses" travels further than "food." TikTok needs to know who to test the video on.

### 5. Use Trends With Your Own Angle

A trending sound or format gives you a familiar frame; your niche gives people a reason to follow.

### 6. Ask for a Comment, Not a Like

A question or a "which one would you pick?" ending starts conversations.

### 7. Post Consistently

Three to five videos a week gives you more tests than one perfect video a month. Our guide to the [best time to post on TikTok](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok) helps you pick slots.

### 8. Reply to Comments With Videos

A video reply to a top comment is an easy second video on a topic that already worked.

> **Pro tip:** Watch your own video with the sound off and count to two. If you'd scroll away, so will the test group. The hook is the step people get wrong most often, usually by opening with an intro.

## Can You Go Viral on TikTok Overnight?

Yes, it happens: a video can pick up most of its views in the first 24 to 48 hours. But "overnight" success usually follows weeks of posting. Videos can also take off days or weeks later, when a sound trends or a new audience finds them. Don't delete videos that start slowly; let them sit.

| Myth | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| You need lots of followers | New accounts go viral all the time |
| #fyp gets you on the FYP | No evidence it changes reach |
| The posting minute decides it | It helps a little; a strong video can take off at any hour |
| A slow start means it's dead | Videos can take off days or weeks later |

## How to Reset Your FYP on TikTok (3 Steps)

If your own For You feed is full of things you don't want, TikTok lets you start fresh. Menu labels change between app versions, so look for the closest match.

1. Go to **Profile**, tap the menu (three lines) and open **Settings and privacy**.
2. Tap **Content preferences**.
3. Tap **Refresh your For You feed**, then **Continue** and confirm.

Your feed then acts like a new account's and learns from what you watch next. Your followers, profile and messages aren't affected. Availability can vary by region and app version. For lighter fixes, long-press a video and tap **Not interested**, or add keyword filters under Content preferences.

## Bought Views, Follow Trains and Growth Bots (Use With Caution)

![Safe ways to grow on TikTok, like strong hooks and steady posting, next to risky shortcuts like bought views and growth bots](/images/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok-safety-1200x675.webp)

When a video stalls, it's tempting to buy views, join follow-for-follow groups or use apps that automate likes and comments.

### The Real Risks

- **Fake viewers don't watch to the end.** Watch time is the strongest signal, so views that leave immediately can tell TikTok your video isn't worth pushing.
- **Inauthentic activity breaks TikTok's rules**, and accounts that use it can lose reach or be restricted.
- **Bots often need your login**, which hands control of your account to a stranger.
- **Your analytics stop meaning anything**, so you can't learn what actually works.

> **Bottom line:** The algorithm rewards people who watch. Bought numbers can't do that for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many views is viral on TikTok?

There's no official threshold. Most people call 100,000 to 1 million views viral, but a video with ten times your normal views has gone viral for your account.

### What does FYP mean on TikTok?

FYP stands for For You Page, the personalized main feed. Getting on the FYP means TikTok is recommending your video to people who don't follow you.

### Do #fyp and #foryou hashtags help you go viral?

There's no evidence they do. Hashtags help TikTok understand a video's topic, so a few specific ones are more useful than generic tags.

### Does posting time affect going viral?

A little. Posting when your audience is active helps the first test group engage quickly, but a strong video can take off at any hour.

### Can a new account go viral on TikTok?

Yes. TikTok shows new accounts' videos on the For You feed too, so follower count matters less than on most platforms.

### How do I get on the FYP on TikTok?

Every public, eligible video is tested on the For You feed. To get further, make videos people finish and share, stay within the Community Guidelines and post regularly.

### How long does it take for a TikTok to go viral?

Often within the first 24 to 48 hours, but videos can also take off days or weeks later when a sound trends or a new audience finds them.

### Does resetting my FYP affect my own videos?

No. Refreshing your For You feed only changes what you see. Your followers, profile and messages aren't affected.
