Brandwatch and RecurPost are aimed at very different buyers. Brandwatch is an enterprise consumer intelligence suite known for social listening, with publishing, engagement and reporting in its social media management product (formerly Falcon.io). RecurPost is a scheduler for small businesses and agencies, built around evergreen content libraries that repost on a recurring schedule. If you need listening and enterprise reporting, look at Brandwatch; if you need to keep accounts active without much effort, look at RecurPost.

## Brandwatch overview

Brandwatch is best known for consumer research: tracking conversations about brands, products, competitors and topics across social networks, news, blogs, forums and review sites. Insights teams use it to measure sentiment, spot emerging trends and understand audiences at scale.

Its social media management product, which grew out of the Falcon.io platform Brandwatch acquired, covers the day-to-day side of running social channels.

### What Brandwatch does well

- **Social listening and consumer research.** Broad data coverage, historical search, sentiment analysis and trend detection.
- **Publishing.** A shared content calendar, approval workflows and scheduling across major networks.
- **Engagement.** A unified inbox for comments and messages, built for teams handling high volume.
- **Measurement and benchmarking.** Reporting on owned channels and comparisons against competitors.
- **Influencer marketing.** A separate product for finding and managing creator partnerships.

### Who Brandwatch suits

Large brands and agencies that need listening, publishing, customer care and reporting in one vendor relationship, with the budget and team size to make use of an enterprise suite.

## RecurPost overview

RecurPost is built for people who want consistent posting without rebuilding a schedule every week. You sort content into libraries by theme, such as tips, blog promotions or testimonials, then assign each library to recurring time slots. RecurPost cycles through the libraries and reposts evergreen content automatically.

### What RecurPost does well

- **Content libraries.** Control the mix of topics and how often each appears.
- **Recurring and evergreen posting.** Good content keeps working without manual rescheduling.
- **Bulk import.** Add many posts from a spreadsheet at once.
- **Agency tiers.** Multi-client management with white-label options.
- **Social inbox and reports.** Respond to engagement and review performance in one place.

### Who RecurPost suits

Small and mid-sized businesses, freelancers and agencies with a back catalog of evergreen content and limited time to schedule posts one by one.

## Pricing and plans

**Brandwatch** doesn't publish standard prices. Its products are sold through a sales process, usually as annual contracts, with pricing based on which modules you need (for example, consumer research, publishing, engagement or influencer marketing), the number of users and data volume. Expect an enterprise-level commitment and a demo before purchase.

**RecurPost** publishes self-serve plans on its website, tiered by the number of social accounts, content libraries and users, with dedicated agency plans. You can sign up and start without speaking to sales.

The practical difference is large: Brandwatch is a multi-product contract, while RecurPost is a monthly or annual subscription you can start the same day. Check each vendor's pricing page for current prices and plan details.

## Which should you choose?

### Choose Brandwatch if

- Social listening and consumer insights are a core need, not a nice-to-have.
- You run social for a large brand with several teams, regions or agencies involved.
- You need enterprise reporting, benchmarking, governance and support.
- You have the budget for a sales-led, multi-module contract.

### Choose RecurPost if

- You mainly need to publish consistently, not monitor the wider conversation.
- You have evergreen content you want to recycle automatically.
- You want a self-serve tool you can set up today.
- You post to Google Business Profile as well as social networks.

### Consider using both

Some organizations use an enterprise listening tool for insights and a lighter scheduler for day-to-day publishing on smaller brands or regional accounts. If you only need Brandwatch for listening, ask whether its publishing modules are required in your contract.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Brandwatch a social media scheduler?

Partly. Brandwatch is best known for social listening and consumer research, but its social media management product includes publishing, scheduling, engagement and reporting.

### Does RecurPost do social listening?

No. RecurPost focuses on scheduling, recurring content, a social inbox and reporting on your own accounts. For listening across the web, you'd need a dedicated listening tool.

### Why doesn't Brandwatch list its prices?

Brandwatch sells modular enterprise products, so pricing depends on the modules, users and data volume you need. You get a quote after talking to its sales team.

### Which is better for a small business?

RecurPost, in most cases. It's self-serve, publicly priced and designed for small teams that want consistent posting. Brandwatch's strengths are aimed at large organizations.

### Was Brandwatch's publishing tool called Falcon?

Yes. Brandwatch's social media management product grew out of Falcon.io, which Brandwatch acquired and later brought under the Brandwatch name.

### Do either of them post to Bluesky?

We couldn't confirm Bluesky publishing for either tool at the time of review. Check their current channel lists. Schedule For You publishes to Bluesky.
