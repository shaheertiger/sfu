## What's planned for Google Business Profile
Google Business Profile posts appear on your business listing in Google Search and Maps. They're a simple way to show people who are already looking for you what's new.

> **Coming soon:** Schedule For You doesn't publish to Google Business Profile yet. We're working on it, but we can't promise a date. For now, post your updates directly in Google Business Profile, and schedule your other accounts here.

A Google Business Profile post has three parts. These are Google's own rules, so they're worth knowing whichever tool you use.

### Text
Up to 1,500 characters. Shorter usually works better: the listing shows only the start of the post, so lead with what matters.

### One image
A clear photo of the product, the dish or the space works better than a busy graphic.

### An optional button link
A button can send people to your booking page, menu, product or offer.

## What to do now
1. **Post in Google Business Profile directly.** Search for your business name on Google while signed in, or open the Business Profile manager, and add an update there. Menu labels change from time to time, so look for an option such as **Add update**.
2. **Write the social versions in Schedule For You.** If you're announcing the same news on Facebook and Instagram, write it once here and tailor each version.
3. **Keep the Google version factual and local.** What's happening, when and where, plus the button link. Your social versions can stay more casual.
4. **Pick a time or add the social posts to your queue.** Schedule an announcement for a specific day, or add everyday updates to your queue.

When Google Business Profile publishing arrives, the plan is to connect your profile from the Accounts screen, like any other platform. Our guide to [connecting Google Business Profile](/blog/connect-google-business-profile) will be updated with the steps.

## Tips for Google Business Profile posts
- **Answer "why now?".** Customers see posts while deciding where to go, so make each one about something timely: a new item, an event or a seasonal change.
- **Use your own photos.** Real photos of your business look more trustworthy on a listing than stock images.
- **Pick the right link.** Point the button at the page that completes the action, such as a booking form, rather than your homepage.
- **Keep listing details current.** Posts don't update your hours, phone number or address. Change those in your profile itself, following [Google's Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business).
- **Post regularly.** A listing with recent updates shows the business is active. A simple weekly rhythm, set as a calendar reminder, is easy to keep.

Yes, in Google Business Profile itself. A button link lets customers book, order or learn more.

Yes. Write it in Schedule For You, select those accounts and tailor each version. Facebook can take up to 10 images. Post the Google version in Google Business Profile for now.

### Does posting update my opening hours?
No. Posts are updates on your listing. Change hours, contact details and address in your Business Profile settings.
