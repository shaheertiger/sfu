## What you can schedule on Threads
Threads is built for conversation, so text carries more weight here than on Instagram. Schedule For You supports the formats people actually use.

### Text-only posts
Unlike Instagram, Threads treats a plain text post as completely normal. Write up to 500 characters with no media at all. The composer counts as you type and flags anything over the limit.

### A single image or video
Attach one image or one video when a picture makes the point faster than words.

### Carousels
Attach up to 10 items to publish a carousel. It's a good fit for screenshots, a short tutorial or a set of photos from one event.

## How it works
1. **Connect Threads.** Open the Accounts screen, choose Threads and sign in to authorize Schedule For You.
2. **Write the post.** Start with your idea. If it's also going to X, Bluesky or Instagram, select those accounts too.
3. **Tailor the Threads version.** Threads sits between X and Instagram. You have more room than on X, so add a line of context or a question, but drop the block of hashtags you'd use on Instagram. Your edits stay on the Threads tab.
4. **Pick a time or add it to the queue.** Set an exact time, or add it to your queue and let the next free time slot take it.

## Features that help on Threads
### One draft, several short-form networks
Many people post similar thoughts on Threads, X and Bluesky. Each has a different limit: 500 characters on Threads, 300 on Bluesky and 280 on X. Writing once and tailoring each version with live limit checks saves you from rewriting the same idea three times from scratch.

### A queue for a steady voice
Threads is conversational, which suits frequent, lighter posts. Set a few daily time slots and keep the queue stocked so your account stays active on busy days.

### Repeating posts
Weekly prompts, like a Friday "what are you working on?" post, can be set to repeat so they appear without you remembering each week.

### Workspaces and approvals
If you run Threads for a brand or a client, keep it in its own workspace and use client preview links so they can approve or comment without an account.

## Tips for Threads
- **Ask something.** Posts that invite a reply tend to suit Threads better than announcements. End with an open question when it fits.
- **Lead with text, add media when it helps.** A text-only post is not a lesser post on Threads.
- **Keep carousels in order.** Drag items into the sequence you want before scheduling, and make the first image strong enough to work alone.
- **Reply in person.** Scheduling gets the post out, but the conversation underneath is where Threads builds relationships, so check back after it goes live.
- **Adapt rather than copy.** See our guide to [customizing captions for each platform](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) for ways to reshape one idea for different audiences.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I schedule text-only posts on Threads?
Yes. Threads is one of the platforms where Schedule For You publishes posts with no image or video. Write up to 500 characters.

### How many images can a Threads carousel have?
Up to 10 items in a carousel. You can also post a single image or a single video.

### Can I schedule a batch of Threads posts at once?
Yes. Bulk scheduling lets you upload a CSV of posts, with media, and schedule them in one go. Threads is connected separately from Instagram on the Accounts screen, and each post gets its own Threads version.

### Can I post the same update to Threads and Instagram?
Yes, but remember Instagram needs at least one image or video, while Threads doesn't. If your post is text-only, it can go to Threads, X, Bluesky, LinkedIn and Facebook, but not to Instagram.

### What happens if my Threads post fails?
You get a failed-post alert. If access was revoked, reconnect Threads from the Accounts screen and retry the post.

### Does Schedule For You suggest what to post on Threads?
No. There are no AI writing or suggestion features. You write the posts, and Schedule For You handles tailoring, timing and publishing.
