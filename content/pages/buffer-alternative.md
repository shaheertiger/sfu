If you're looking for a Buffer alternative, first work out what's pushing you to look. Buffer is known for clean, simple scheduling, and if that's all you need, it may already be the right tool. If you've outgrown per-channel pricing, need clients to approve posts, or want an API and RSS auto-posting in the same place, a scheduler such as Schedule For You may fit better.

## Who looks for a Buffer alternative, and why
Buffer has a long track record as an easy, uncluttered scheduler: connect your channels, add posts to a queue, and they go out at the times you set. It also offers Start Page, a simple landing page you can use as a link-in-bio, and it has historically had a free plan for a small number of channels (check whether that's still available and what it includes today).

People start comparing apps like Buffer for a few common reasons:

- **Pricing grows with every channel.** Buffer's paid plans have typically been priced per connected channel. That's fair for two or three profiles, but creators and agencies with ten or more accounts often prefer a flat plan with a set number of accounts. Check Buffer's pricing page for its current model.
- **They work with clients.** Agencies and freelancers need a separate space per client and a way for clients to review posts without buying a seat.
- **They want deeper per-platform control.** One idea, written differently for LinkedIn, X and Instagram, with each version checked against that network's limits.
- **They want to automate.** Developers and operations-minded marketers may want a REST API, webhooks or an RSS feed that adds posts for them.

None of this makes Buffer a bad choice. It means the job has changed.

## What to look for in a Buffer alternative
Write down every network and account you post to, then check each candidate against this list.

### Platform coverage
Confirm the tool publishes directly to each network you use, and check the details. "Supports Facebook" usually means Pages, not personal profiles or Groups. "Supports LinkedIn" can mean personal profiles, company pages or both.

### Pricing structure
Compare how plans are counted: per channel, per user, or a fixed number of accounts. Check whether posts are capped and whether the trial needs a card.

### Per-platform editing
Look for separate versions per network, each with a live character count, so a long LinkedIn post doesn't break on X.

### Scheduling workflow
If you like Buffer's queue, look for posting time slots. Also check for a drag-and-drop calendar, drafts and bulk uploads from a spreadsheet.

### Collaboration and clients
Check for team members, approvals, separate workspaces per brand, and a way for clients to approve posts without an account.

### Reliability
Find out what happens when a post fails. A good scheduler alerts you straight away and makes it easy to reconnect the account and retry.

## Where Buffer may suit you better
Buffer may be the better choice if:

- **You need a free plan.** If you post to a couple of channels and don't want to pay, check Buffer's current free tier. Schedule For You has no free plan.
- **You rely on Start Page.** Schedule For You doesn't offer a link-in-bio or landing page.
- **You want analytics in the same tool.** Schedule For You focuses on planning and publishing and leaves reporting to each platform's own insights.
- **You post to a network Schedule For You doesn't cover.** It publishes to 10 platforms only. If Mastodon or another network is central for you, check Buffer's list.
- **You only manage one or two channels.** Per-channel pricing can work out simpler when your channel count is small.

If Buffer's simplicity is exactly what you value and your account count is small, staying put may be the right call.

## How to switch from Buffer
The goal is to avoid losing or double-posting anything:

1. **Export or list what's queued.** Note every post scheduled in Buffer for the next few weeks, including text, media and times.
2. **Save your media.** Download the images and videos you plan to reuse so you can add them to the Schedule For You media library.
3. **Start your trial and connect accounts.** Connect each network from the Accounts screen. Instagram needs a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page; Bluesky uses your handle and an app password.
4. **Recreate your queue times.** Set up posting time slots that match your Buffer posting schedule.
5. **Rebuild the schedule.** Add posts one by one or, for a long list, prepare a CSV with captions and media and use bulk scheduling.
6. **Prepare Instagram images.** Schedule For You doesn't crop images, so keep feed images between 4:5 and 1.91:1. The free [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) helps.
7. **Pause the Buffer queue.** Remove or pause each post in Buffer once it's scheduled in Schedule For You, so nothing goes out twice.
8. **Sort out Start Page.** If your profile links point to a Start Page, keep it or replace it before you cancel.
9. **Watch the first week.** Check posts as they publish and act on any failed-post alert by reconnecting the account.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is there a free Buffer alternative?
Each platform's own tools, such as Meta Business Suite for Facebook and Instagram or YouTube Studio, are free but separate. Schedule For You isn't free: it has a 7-day free trial (card required) and paid plans from $19 a month. Our roundup of the [best free social media schedulers](/blog/best-free-social-media-schedulers) covers free options.

### Is Schedule For You cheaper than Buffer?
It depends on how many channels you run. Schedule For You charges $19 a month for up to 15 connected accounts or $59 for up to 100, with unlimited posts. Compare that with Buffer's current pricing for your exact account count.

### What are other apps like Buffer?
Later, Hootsuite, Sprout Social, SocialBee, Publer and Metricool are common options, each with a different focus. Our guide to the [best Buffer alternatives](/best-buffer-alternatives) explains what each is known for and how to choose.

### Does Schedule For You have something like Buffer's Start Page?
No. Schedule For You doesn't offer a link-in-bio or landing page, so keep your existing page or choose a dedicated tool before you move.

### Can I post to LinkedIn company pages with Schedule For You?
No. Schedule For You posts to personal LinkedIn profiles only, not company pages. If you publish to a company page, check whether Buffer or another tool covers it.
