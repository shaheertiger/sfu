## How to manage multiple social media accounts without tab-hopping
The simplest way to manage multiple social media accounts is to stop logging in and out of each app. Connect them all to Schedule For You, plan posts in one queue and calendar, and let it publish to each account at the time you choose.

### One calendar for every account
Every scheduled post appears on a single drag-and-drop calendar, whichever accounts it's going to. You can see the week at a glance, spot gaps and move a post to another day in one motion.

### Several accounts on the same platform
You aren't limited to one profile per network. Connect two Facebook Pages, three Instagram accounts and a pair of LinkedIn profiles to the same workspace, and pick exactly which ones each post goes to.

### Room to grow
Solo connects up to 15 accounts and Studio up to 100, across X, Bluesky, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages, Pinterest and Google Business Profile. Both plans include unlimited posts; see [pricing](/pricing) for details.

## Social media management for multiple clients and brands
Agencies and freelancers need clear walls between clients. Workspaces provide them.

### A workspace per brand or client
Create a separate workspace for each brand or client. Its connected accounts, posts, queue and calendar stay inside it, so a post meant for a bakery can't end up on a law firm's profile. Each workspace has its own plan and its own account limit, so a client in a separate workspace is billed as a separate subscription. To keep several brands on one plan, connect them in the same workspace.

### Time slots that match each audience
Set posting time slots for each workspace's queue. A local café might post at breakfast and lunch while a B2B client posts mid-morning on weekdays, and each queue fills its own slots.

### Captions that suit each platform
When one post goes to several accounts, each platform gets its own version of the caption. Shorten it for X, add hashtags for Instagram and keep LinkedIn plain, with live character-limit checks as you type. Our guide to [customizing captions for each platform](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) shows the workflow.

## Working with a team and clients
Managing many accounts usually means more than one person touches each post.

### Team members on Studio
On the Studio plan you can invite team members to a workspace, so writers, designers and account managers work from the same queue instead of passing drafts around in chat.

### Approvals before anything goes live
Turn on approvals and posts wait until they're signed off. It's a simple safeguard when several people schedule for the same brand.

### Client preview links
Send a client a preview link for a post. They can approve it or leave a comment without creating an account, and you see their response next to the post.

### Alerts when something breaks
If a platform revokes access, for example after a password change, you get a failed-post alert with a prompt to reconnect the account from the Accounts screen and retry. Reminder emails and a daily digest keep everyone aware of what's coming up.

## Setting it up in five steps
1. **Create a workspace** for each brand or client you manage.
2. **Connect the accounts.** Add each profile from the Accounts screen. For Instagram, you sign in through Meta and choose which Business or Creator accounts to add; our guide to [connecting multiple Instagram accounts](/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts) covers the details.
3. **Set your time slots** so the queue knows when each workspace posts.
4. **Write and target posts.** Select the accounts, tailor each platform's version and add the post to the queue or a specific time.
5. **Review and approve.** Use approvals and preview links so the right person signs off before publishing.

To fill several calendars quickly, bulk-schedule from a CSV with media, or set up repeating and evergreen posts for content that stays relevant. The guide to [planning content in advance](/blog/plan-content-in-advance) has more on batching.

## Frequently asked questions
### How do you manage multiple social media accounts at once?
Connect them to one scheduler, write each post once and select every account it should go to. Schedule For You publishes to each account at the scheduled time, with a separately editable version for each platform.

### Can I connect several accounts on the same platform?
Yes. A workspace can hold several accounts per platform, such as multiple Instagram accounts or Facebook Pages, and you choose which ones each post targets. To learn more, see how to [post to multiple Instagram accounts](/post-to-multiple-instagram-accounts).

### How many social media accounts can I manage?
Solo includes up to 15 connected accounts and Studio up to 100, per workspace. Every profile, Page or channel you connect counts as one account.

### How do agencies keep client accounts separate?
Use one workspace per client. Each workspace has its own connected accounts, queue, calendar and plan, so posts stay with the right brand and each client can be billed on its own subscription.

### Can my team and clients help with posts?
Team members are available on the Studio plan. Clients don't need an account at all: send a preview link and they can approve or comment on the post.

### Can I post the same content to all my accounts?
Yes. Select every account when you write the post and Schedule For You sends it to all of them, with a version for each platform you can tailor. Read more about our [cross-posting tool](/cross-posting).
