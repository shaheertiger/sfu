## How our social media scheduler pricing works
Schedule For You's social media scheduler pricing has two plans: Solo costs $19 a month for up to 15 connected accounts, and Studio costs $59 a month for up to 100 connected accounts, team members and priority support. Both include unlimited posts and the same publishing features, and both start with a 7-day free trial.

### Solo: $19 a month
Solo suits a creator, freelancer or small business running its own channels. You get 15 connected accounts, which covers one profile on each of the 10 supported platforms with room for a second brand or a few extra profiles.

### Studio: $59 a month
Studio is for agencies, in-house teams and anyone managing many brands. It raises the limit to 100 connected accounts, lets you invite team members to work alongside you, and adds priority support.

### Which plan should you pick?
Count the profiles, Pages and channels you post to today, then add the ones you expect to take on soon. If the total stays under 15 and you work alone, Solo is enough. If you need more accounts or other people need to log in, pick Studio.

## What's included on every plan
The plans differ only in account limits, team members and support. Everything below comes with both.

### Unlimited posts on all 10 platforms
Publish as often as you like to X, Bluesky, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages, Pinterest and Google Business Profile. There are no monthly post caps and no per-platform add-ons.

### Planning tools
- **Per-platform captions:** write once, then edit each platform's version on its own, with live character-limit checks.
- **Queue and calendar:** set posting time slots, add posts to the queue and drag them around a calendar.
- **Bulk scheduling:** upload a CSV with media to schedule weeks of posts in one go.
- Drafts, a media library, and repeating and evergreen posts.

### Review and client tools
Use approvals so nothing publishes until it's signed off, and send client preview links so clients can approve or comment without creating an account.

### Automation and developer tools
Auto-post from RSS feeds, send events to Zapier, Make or n8n with webhooks, and build your own integrations with the [REST API](/api). Every workspace gets these on both plans.

### Alerts that keep you informed
Failed-post alerts tell you when a platform rejects a post or a connection needs renewing. Reminder emails before posts and a daily digest round it out.

## What counts as a connected account?
A connected account is one profile, Page or channel you've linked so Schedule For You can publish to it. Your plan sets how many you can connect: 15 on Solo, 100 on Studio.

### Examples
- One Instagram Business or Creator account counts as one.
- One Facebook Page counts as one.
- One YouTube channel, one Pinterest profile or one Google Business Profile each count as one.

### Several accounts on the same platform
Each account counts on its own. Three Instagram accounts use three of your connected accounts, which is how agencies [post to multiple Instagram accounts](/post-to-multiple-instagram-accounts) from one place.

### Is this a cheap social media scheduler?
At $19 a month with unlimited posts, Solo sits at the affordable end of paid tools, but "cheap" depends on what you need. If you post rarely to one account, a platform's built-in scheduler may be enough; our guide to [free social media schedulers](/blog/best-free-social-media-schedulers) covers those options. Weigh any social media scheduling tool's price against how many accounts and platforms it actually covers.

## How the 7-day free trial works
Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial of the full product.

### A card is required
You add a card when you sign up. You aren't charged during the trial; the first charge happens on day 8 if you keep your subscription.

### Cancel anytime
If it isn't right for you, cancel before day 8 and you won't be charged. You can also cancel later; cancelling stops your plan from renewing.

### There's no free plan
Schedule For You is a paid product. After the trial, you need an active Solo or Studio subscription to keep publishing.

## Changing or cancelling your plan
The workspace owner manages billing. Open **Billing** from the workspace menu and you'll land in Stripe's secure customer portal, where you can switch between Solo and Studio, update your card, download invoices or cancel. For anything else about billing, contact support. The [change or cancel your plan guide](/blog/change-or-cancel-your-plan) walks through each step.
