The best Buffer alternative depends on why you're leaving. If Buffer's per-channel price is the problem, a flat plan such as SocialBee, Later or Schedule For You costs less once you pass about five accounts. If you need a social inbox, listening and reporting for a team, Hootsuite and Sprout Social are built for that and cost far more. For visual Instagram planning, look at Later; for evergreen recycling, SocialBee; for scheduling plus client reports, Metricool.

*Prices checked October 2026 from vendors' published plans and recent pricing reviews. They change often, are in US dollars before tax, and some vendors only publish annual rates, so confirm on each vendor's pricing page before you buy.*

## What Buffer costs, and when it's still the right tool

Buffer is a simple, calm scheduler: connect channels, add posts to a queue, and they go out at your set times. At the time of writing:

- **Free:** up to 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel, one user.
- **Essentials:** about $6 per channel a month, or $5 billed yearly.
- **Team:** about $12 per channel a month, or $10 billed yearly, adding unlimited users and approvals.
- Both paid plans have a 14-day free trial, and per-channel rates are reported to drop past 10 channels.

If you post to two or three channels on your own, Buffer's free plan or Essentials is hard to beat, and each network's own scheduler (Meta Business Suite, YouTube Studio, LinkedIn and TikTok's built-in scheduling) is free too. Our roundup of [free social media schedulers](/blog/best-free-social-media-schedulers) covers those.

## Where per-channel pricing starts to hurt

Take Priya, a freelance social media manager with three clients: a café, a dental practice and a yoga studio. Each client posts to Instagram, a Facebook Page, TikTok and Google Business Profile, so she manages 12 profiles. Her clients want to approve posts before they go out.

On Buffer Essentials, 12 channels cost about $72 a month. Approvals are on the Team plan, which takes that to about $144 a month, and every new client adds four more channels. She also has to keep three brands apart in one queue, and send screenshots to clients who don't have Buffer logins.

That's when people start comparing. The right alternative for Priya isn't the one with the most features; it's the one that counts accounts the way she works, keeps clients separate and lets them approve without a seat.

What one person pays a month at 5, 10 and 15 connected profiles, on the cheapest plan that fits.

| Hootsuite | Per user; Standard covers 10 accounts | $99 | $99 | $199 (Professional) | Each user is another seat |

| SocialBee | Profile tiers: 5, 10, 25 | $29 | $49 | $99 | 3 users on Pro |
| Publer Professional | Per account, about $4–5 | About $20–25 | About $40–50 | About $60–75 | Paid per user |
| Metricool | Brands (one profile per network) | From $25 | $25–45 | $45–67 | Advanced plans only |
| Schedule For You | Connected accounts | $19 | $19 | $19 | Studio, $59 for 100 accounts |

How to read it:

- **Billing.** Prices are monthly billing where the vendor publishes it. Hootsuite publishes annual rates ($99 Standard, $199 Professional per user). Sprout Social is $199, $299 and $399 per seat a month billed yearly, or $249, $399 and $499 monthly. Later, SocialBee and Metricool are cheaper billed yearly; Schedule For You gives 2 months free yearly.
- **Counting quirks.** Later's social set holds one profile per network, so two Instagram accounts need a second set. Metricool counts brands, and Starter has no team members; X is an add-on there. Hootsuite and Sprout multiply by people, so a team of three triples the bill.
- **What the money buys.** Hootsuite and Sprout include a social inbox, listening and deep analytics. Schedule For You is publishing only: no inbox, no engagement analytics, no AI writing and no link-in-bio page.

## How Schedule For You handles Priya's 12 accounts

Here's the same scenario in Schedule For You, with screenshots from the live dashboard's demo.

**One plan for all three clients.** Solo covers 15 connected accounts for $19 a month, so Priya's 12 profiles fit with room for a new client. Accounts are counted individually, so two Instagram accounts are simply two accounts. She saves each client's profiles as an **account group**, so one click selects the café's four accounts and nothing else.

![The Social accounts screen showing seven connected accounts across TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, X, Instagram, LinkedIn and Threads, with Connected, Expires soon and Reconnect needed statuses](/images/blog/social-accounts-1440x900.webp)

**One draft, tailored per platform.** She writes the café's weekend post once, then edits the Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google Business Profile tabs separately. Counters show every platform's limit at once, and the composer flags problems before scheduling, such as a TikTok post with no video.

![The New post screen with four accounts selected, a tab per platform, character counters for each limit and a warning to fix before publishing](/images/blog/composer-four-platforms-1440x900.webp)

**Client approval without a login.** She marks the post as needing approval and sends a **client preview link**. The client approves, requests changes or comments in the browser, and Priya gets an email. Approvals and preview links are on both plans; a read-only **shared calendar** link shows each client their whole schedule.

**A queue she can trust.** Posting slots fill in order, the queue shows what goes out next, and a failed post shows its reason with a retry button, plus an email straight away.

![The Queue screen with a countdown to the next post, labels, account chips on each post and a list of failed posts with reasons and a Retry button](/images/blog/queue-1440x900.webp)

If a client wants to be billed separately, give them their own workspace and subscription. If Priya hires help, Studio ($59 a month) adds team members and raises the limit to 100 accounts.

Where Schedule For You isn't the right fit: LinkedIn posting is for personal profiles only, not company pages, and there's no social inbox or engagement reporting. If a client needs those, a suite or the platform's own tools will serve them better.

## The best Buffer alternatives, tool by tool

Listed in no particular order.

### Later
Built around visual Instagram planning, with a grid preview, link-in-bio page and influencer tools, and now several other networks. Starter is $25 a month for one social set (8 profiles) and 30 posts per profile a month; Growth is $50 for two sets and two users. Best for brands that live on Instagram and care how the grid looks. Our [Later alternative](/later-alternative) page compares it in more detail.

### Hootsuite
One of the longest-running all-in-one suites: scheduling, social inbox, listening, analytics and team workflows. Standard is $99 per user a month (billed yearly) for up to 10 accounts; approvals sit on Advanced at $399. Best for organizations that want publishing, engagement and reporting in one place. See also our [free Hootsuite alternative](/blog/free-hootsuite-alternative) guide.

### Sprout Social
A full suite known for its unified inbox, reporting and collaboration. Priced per seat: Standard covers 5 profiles, and Professional removes the profile cap. Best for businesses and agencies that treat social as a customer channel and have the budget for seats.

### SocialBee
Organizes posts into content categories and recycles evergreen posts, so the queue never runs dry. Bootstrap ($29, 5 profiles), Accelerate ($49, 10) and Pro ($99, 25, 3 users), each with a 14-day trial. Best for small businesses reusing a library of content.

### Publer
Packs a lot of scheduling into per-account pricing: calendar, recycling, bulk scheduling. A free tier covers 3 accounts without X; analytics, best-time posting and its API sit on the Business plan. Best for people who want many publishing options at a low per-account price.

### Metricool
Scheduling plus analytics and client reports, including ads data. Counts brands rather than accounts: Starter from $25 a month for up to 5 brands, Advanced (team, approvals, API) from $67. The free tier covers one brand but excludes LinkedIn and X. Best for freelancers and agencies who send monthly reports.

### Native schedulers
Meta Business Suite, YouTube Studio, LinkedIn and TikTok all schedule posts for free and support their own formats best. Each covers one network (Business Suite covers two), so you upload and write every post separately.

### Schedule For You
A cross-posting scheduler for 10 platforms: Instagram (posts, Reels, carousels and Stories), Facebook Pages, X, LinkedIn personal profiles, Threads, Bluesky, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Google Business Profile. One draft with a tailored version per platform, a queue and drag-and-drop calendar, bulk scheduling and CSV import, first comments, repeating and evergreen posts, approvals, client preview links, RSS auto-post, webhooks, a REST API and an MCP server. Solo is $19 a month for 15 accounts and Studio $59 for 100 accounts with team members; there's also a Free plan (3 accounts, up to 10 scheduled posts at a time, no card), and the paid plans' 7-day trial needs a card. Our [Buffer alternative](/buffer-alternative) page compares the two directly.

## How to choose by need

| If you need… | Start with |
|---|---|
| Free scheduling for one to three channels | Buffer's free plan or native schedulers |
| Visual Instagram grid planning and link-in-bio | Later |
| Social inbox, listening and reporting for a team | Hootsuite or Sprout Social |
| Evergreen content recycling by category | SocialBee |
| Scheduling plus client reports | Metricool |
| Many accounts at a flat price, tailored per platform | Schedule For You or SocialBee |
| Client approvals without giving clients a login | Schedule For You |
| An API, webhooks and RSS auto-posting on both paid plans | Schedule For You; check others' API access by plan |
| LinkedIn company pages | Buffer, the suites or LinkedIn itself, not Schedule For You |

### Run a real week in each trial
Shortlist two tools and schedule a real week, not test posts: tailor one caption per network, move posts on the calendar, add a carousel and send something for approval. A week of real use tells you more than any feature list.

### Count accounts, not just channels
Add up every profile, Page and channel, including clients' accounts and second accounts on the same network, then compare against how each tool counts. Our guide to [managing multiple social media accounts](/manage-multiple-social-media-accounts) helps you plan the setup.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the cheapest Buffer alternative?
For one to three channels, Buffer's own free plan and the native schedulers cost nothing. For 5 to 15 accounts, Schedule For You's Solo plan is $19 a month and Later's Starter is $25 (one profile per network). Publer costs about the same at five accounts and more as you add accounts.

### What is the best free Buffer alternative?
Each platform's native scheduler is free and supports its own formats. Publer and Metricool have free tiers with limits (Publer excludes X, Metricool excludes LinkedIn and X). Schedule For You has a Free plan for 3 accounts with up to 10 scheduled posts at a time and no card; bulk scheduling, approvals and the API need a paid plan.

### Which Buffer alternative is best for agencies?
Look for pricing by account rather than by user, a way to keep clients separate, and client approval without a seat. Schedule For You has account groups, workspaces, approvals and preview links on both plans; Hootsuite and Sprout Social add inbox and reporting at a much higher per-user price.

### Is Hootsuite or Sprout Social better than Buffer?
They're a different kind of tool. Both are full suites with inboxes, listening and reporting priced per user. Buffer and Schedule For You focus on publishing. Pay for a suite only if your team will use the engagement and reporting features.

### Which Buffer alternatives post to LinkedIn company pages?
Buffer, Hootsuite, Sprout Social and most suites do; check each tool's current LinkedIn support. Schedule For You posts to personal LinkedIn profiles only, so use LinkedIn's own scheduler for company pages.
