The best Buffer alternatives depend on why you're leaving. If you want visual Instagram planning, look at Later. If you need a social inbox and reporting for a team, suites like Hootsuite and Sprout Social are built for that. If you want to recycle evergreen content, SocialBee is known for it. And if you mainly want to publish one post to many networks with each version tailored, a cross-posting scheduler such as Schedule For You is worth a trial.

*Last reviewed: October 2026. Features and prices change; check each vendor's site before buying.*

This page isn't a ranking of the best Buffer alternatives. Each tool below is good at different things, so start with your needs, then use the table near the end to narrow the list.

## Why people look for Buffer competitors
Buffer is known for simple, uncluttered scheduling: a queue, a posting schedule and a calm interface. It has historically offered a free plan for a few channels (check what's available today), and its paid plans have typically been priced per channel. It also offers Start Page, a simple landing page that works as a link-in-bio.

People compare Buffer competitors when:

- **Their account count grows.** Per-channel pricing adds up for creators and agencies running many profiles.
- **They need team features.** Shared inboxes, approvals, client review and detailed permissions.
- **They want analytics or listening.** Deeper reporting, competitor tracking or brand monitoring.
- **They post differently.** Visual Instagram planning, content recycling or heavy automation.

## What to check before you switch
Before you look at any list of apps like Buffer, write down:

- **Every network and account you post to,** including whether you need Facebook Pages, LinkedIn company pages or Instagram Stories.
- **How you write posts.** One caption everywhere, or a tailored version per network?
- **Who's involved.** Just you, a team, or clients who need to approve posts?
- **What you need beyond publishing.** Inbox, analytics, link-in-bio, AI writing, an API.
- **How each tool counts plans:** per channel, per user, or a fixed number of accounts.

## The best Buffer alternatives, tool by tool
Listed in no particular order. Where we're unsure about a detail, we say "check".

### Later
Later grew out of visual Instagram planning. It's known for a drag-and-drop grid preview, a link-in-bio page and influencer marketing products, and it now schedules to several other networks too. It suits brands whose main channel is Instagram and who care how the profile grid looks. Our [Later alternative](/later-alternative) page covers its strengths in more detail.

### Hootsuite
Hootsuite is one of the longest-running all-in-one social media suites. Alongside scheduling, it's known for a social inbox, listening, analytics and workflows for larger teams. It suits organizations that want one platform for publishing, engagement and reporting. If budget is the concern, see our [free Hootsuite alternative](/blog/free-hootsuite-alternative) guide.

### Sprout Social
Sprout Social is another full suite, known for its unified inbox, reporting and team collaboration tools. It's aimed at businesses and agencies that treat social as a customer-facing channel, not just a publishing one. Check how its plans are counted, since suites of this kind are often priced per user.

### SocialBee
SocialBee is known for organizing posts into content categories and recycling evergreen posts on a schedule, so your queue never runs dry. It suits small businesses and solo marketers who reuse a library of content. Check its current network list and which AI features it includes.

### Publer
Publer is known for packing a wide set of scheduling features into its plans, such as a calendar, content recycling and bulk scheduling. It suits people who want plenty of publishing options without a full enterprise suite. Check its current features and network list.

### Metricool
Metricool combines planning and scheduling with analytics, and it's known for reporting, including on paid ads (check which ad platforms). It suits freelancers and agencies who want scheduling and client reports in one place.

### Native schedulers
Meta Business Suite schedules Facebook Page and connected Instagram professional account posts. YouTube Studio lets you schedule a video to go public at a set time, and LinkedIn and TikTok have offered scheduling in their own tools at the time of writing (check each help center for current limits). They're free and support the platform's own formats, but each covers only one network, so you write and upload every post separately. Our roundup of the [best free social media schedulers](/blog/best-free-social-media-schedulers) goes deeper.

### Schedule For You
Schedule For You is a hosted cross-posting scheduler. You write one post, tailor a separate version for each network with live character-limit checks, and publish from one queue and drag-and-drop calendar to 10 platforms: Instagram, Facebook Pages, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Google Business Profile. It includes bulk scheduling from a CSV, repeating and evergreen posts, workspaces per brand, approvals, client preview links, RSS auto-post, webhooks and a REST API. Plans are Solo at $19 a month (15 connected accounts) and Studio at $59 a month (100 accounts plus team members), both with unlimited posts.

It doesn't do everything. There's no free plan (the 7-day free trial needs a card), no analytics, no AI writing, no link-in-bio page, no Instagram Stories or first comments, and LinkedIn posting is for personal profiles only, not company pages. Our [Buffer alternative](/buffer-alternative) page compares the two directly.

## How to choose a Buffer alternative by need

| If you need… | Start by looking at |
|---|---|
| Free scheduling for one or two networks | Native schedulers, or check whether Buffer's free tier still fits |
| Visual Instagram grid planning and link-in-bio | Later |
| Social inbox, listening and reporting for a team | Hootsuite or Sprout Social |
| Evergreen content recycling by category | SocialBee, or any tool with evergreen posts |
| Scheduling plus client reports and ad analytics | Metricool |
| Lots of publishing options in one plan | Publer |
| One post tailored per network across many accounts, with flat plans | Schedule For You |
| Client approvals without giving clients a login | Schedule For You; check others for similar review links |
| An API, webhooks and RSS auto-posting | Schedule For You; check others' API access by plan |

### Run a real week in each trial
Shortlist two or three tools and schedule a real week of posts, not test posts. Try tailoring one caption per network, moving posts on the calendar, adding a carousel and inviting a teammate or client. You'll learn more in a week than from any feature list.

### Count accounts, not just channels
Add up every profile, Page and channel, including clients' accounts and any second account on the same network. Then compare against how each tool counts its plans. Our guide to [managing multiple social media accounts](/manage-multiple-social-media-accounts) helps you plan the setup.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the best free Buffer alternative?
For one or two networks, each platform's native scheduler is free and supports its own formats. For several networks, check which multi-platform tools still offer a free tier and what it limits. Schedule For You isn't free: it has a 7-day free trial with a card required.

### What are some apps like Buffer for small businesses?
SocialBee, Publer, Metricool and Later are common picks for small businesses, each with a different focus. Schedule For You suits small businesses that post to several networks and want one queue with tailored versions per platform. See our guide to [social media management tools for small business](/blog/best-social-media-management-tools-for-small-business).

### Is Hootsuite or Sprout Social better than Buffer?
They're different kinds of tool. Hootsuite and Sprout Social are full suites with inboxes and reporting for teams. Buffer focuses on simpler scheduling. Pick based on whether you need those extra features.

### Which Buffer alternative is best for agencies?
Look for workspaces per client, approvals, a way for clients to review without a seat, and pricing that scales with accounts. Schedule For You includes workspaces, approvals and client preview links, and its Studio plan adds team members; suites like Sprout Social and Hootsuite add reporting.

### Which Buffer alternatives post to LinkedIn company pages?
Many suites do, but check each tool's current LinkedIn support. Schedule For You posts to personal LinkedIn profiles only, not company pages.
