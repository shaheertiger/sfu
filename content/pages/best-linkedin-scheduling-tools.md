The best LinkedIn scheduling tools depend on one question first: are you posting from a personal profile, a company page, or both? LinkedIn's own scheduler is free and may be all you need for one profile. If you also post to other networks, a multi-network scheduler saves time, and if you run a brand's company page with a team, check company page support before anything else.

*Last reviewed: October 2026. Features and prices change; check each vendor's site before buying.*

Below: what LinkedIn offers natively, what to look for in a LinkedIn post scheduler, the main tools by type, and an honest note on where Schedule For You fits. It's not a ranking.

## Can you schedule LinkedIn posts natively?
Yes. At the time of writing, LinkedIn lets you schedule a post from its own composer by choosing a date and time instead of posting right away, and this has been available for personal profiles and for pages you manage. Check LinkedIn's help center for the current steps, how far ahead you can schedule, and which post types are included, since these details change.

The native scheduler is a good starting point because it's free and supports LinkedIn's own formats. The trade-offs:

- **One network only.** If the same idea also goes to X, Threads or Bluesky, you'll write and schedule it again elsewhere.
- **Limited planning view.** You won't get a shared calendar across networks, posting time slots or bulk uploads.
- **Little team workflow.** Approvals and client review usually happen outside LinkedIn.

## What to look for in a LinkedIn post scheduler
### Personal profiles vs. company pages
This is the detail most lists skip. Some tools post to personal profiles only, some to company pages only, and some to both. LinkedIn's API treats them differently, so "supports LinkedIn" isn't enough. Confirm each tool supports the exact account types you need.

### Post formats
Check whether the tool can publish text-only posts, multiple images and video. If you rely on other formats, such as document posts or polls, check each tool's current support, as many schedulers don't publish every LinkedIn format.

### Writing and formatting
LinkedIn posts allow up to 3,000 characters, but the feed shows only the first few lines before "…more", so the opening needs to work on its own. LinkedIn has no built-in bold or italic; people use Unicode characters instead, which screen readers may read awkwardly. Our free [LinkedIn text formatter](/tools/linkedin-text-formatter) shows a live count and roughly where the cut-off falls.

### Planning and teamwork
Look for a calendar, posting time slots, drafts, and approvals if anyone else signs off on posts. Agencies and ghostwriters managing several people's profiles should check how many accounts each plan includes.

### Reliability
LinkedIn connections can expire and need reconnecting. A good tool tells you when a post fails and lets you reconnect and retry.

## The best LinkedIn scheduling tools by type
Examples are well-known tools, listed in no particular order. Where we're not confident about profile or company page support, the table says "Check".

### LinkedIn's native scheduler
Free and built in. Best for one person or one page posting only to LinkedIn.

### LinkedIn-focused tools
Tools such as Taplio and AuthoredUp are built for people who post to LinkedIn a lot. They're known for features around writing, formatting, previews and LinkedIn-specific insights, and some include AI features. Check each one's scheduling options and account support.

### Multi-network schedulers
Buffer, Publer, SocialBee, Metricool and Later schedule to LinkedIn alongside other networks. They suit people who repurpose ideas across several platforms. Each has its own focus, from simple queues to content recycling and reporting.

### All-in-one suites
Hootsuite and Sprout Social add social inboxes, analytics and team workflows. They suit marketing teams running brand company pages who need reporting and approvals.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can you schedule posts on LinkedIn without a tool?
Yes. At the time of writing, LinkedIn's composer lets you pick a date and time for a post. Check LinkedIn's help center for current steps and limits.

### What is the best free LinkedIn scheduler?
For LinkedIn alone, the native scheduler is free and supports LinkedIn's own formats. Some third-party tools have free tiers; check their limits. Schedule For You isn't free, but it has a 7-day free trial (card required).

### Can Schedule For You post to a LinkedIn company page?
No. Schedule For You posts to personal LinkedIn profiles only. For a company page, use LinkedIn's own scheduler or a tool that confirms company page support.

### Does scheduling LinkedIn posts reduce reach?
LinkedIn hasn't said that posts published through its approved API are penalized. Reach depends far more on the post itself, how people respond early on, and your audience.

### How do I schedule LinkedIn posts for multiple profiles?
Use a scheduler that connects several LinkedIn accounts. In Schedule For You, each person's profile connects through LinkedIn's login, and you can keep clients in separate workspaces.
