## How to post to all social media at once
To post to all your social media at once, use a cross-posting tool: write the post once, select every account it should go to, adjust the version for each platform, and schedule it. The tool publishes to each account at the same time. Built-in options only cover part of the job: Meta Business Suite posts to a Facebook Page and Instagram together, and Instagram and Facebook can share some posts to Threads, but no native tool reaches X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Bluesky, YouTube and Pinterest in one go.

Schedule For You is a cross-posting tool for 10 platforms: X, Bluesky, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages, Pinterest and Google Business Profile. Each platform gets its own editable version of the post, checked against that platform's limits before anything is published.

## The manual way, and the native shortcuts
### Posting in each app
The free method is to open each app and post by hand: write the caption once in a notes app, then paste it into X, LinkedIn, Instagram and the rest, trimming and re-attaching media each time. It works for one or two platforms. Past that, it takes 20–30 minutes per announcement, and the posts go out minutes apart rather than together.

### Meta Business Suite for Facebook and Instagram
Meta Business Suite can publish one post to a Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account at the same time, with a separate caption for each if you want. It's free and reliable for those two, but it doesn't post to X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky or Pinterest. Reports differ on whether it can schedule to Threads, so check your own Business Suite before relying on it.

### Sharing to Threads from Instagram or Facebook
When you post a photo on Instagram or Facebook, a Threads option can share the same post to your Threads profile, and Instagram has a setting to do it automatically for future posts. At the time of writing it doesn't cover Instagram Reels, and the menu location changes between app versions.

### Everything else
TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky and Pinterest have no built-in way to publish to other networks. You can download your own video and upload it again elsewhere, but that's still one app at a time.

## Where it breaks down: one launch, six platforms
Here's a typical case. A coffee roaster is launching a winter blend at 8 AM on Monday and wants the news on X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and its Google Business Profile at once. The core message is the same, but every platform needs something different:

| Platform | What has to change | Example version |
|---|---|---|
| X (280 characters) | Cut to the essentials, link at the end | "Winter Blend is here ☕ Dark chocolate, orange peel, a little smoke. In shops and online from today: [link]" |
| Bluesky (300 characters) | Same as X, with room for one more line | The X version plus "Roasted in small batches every Thursday." |
| LinkedIn (3,000 characters) | Longer, about the people and process | Three short paragraphs on sourcing the beans and the team that developed the blend |
| Instagram (2,200 characters) | Must have an image or video; hashtags welcome | The photo of the bag, a fuller caption and a hashtag block in the first comment |
| TikTok | Must have a video or photo post | A 20-second roasting clip with a two-line caption |
| Google Business Profile (1,500 characters) | Local call to action, one image, no video | "Pick up a bag at our Main Street shop this week" with the bag photo |

Doing that by hand means six apps, six copies of the caption, two files to re-upload, and a good chance that the X version gets cut off or the Instagram post fails for lack of an image. If one platform rejects the post, you usually find out later, by noticing it's missing.

### If one target fails
When you post to many accounts, one of them occasionally fails while the rest go through. The queue shows which account failed and why, for example an expired connection or a caption that became too long after a link was added, and you get an email right away. Fix the cause, then retry: only the failed accounts are sent again, so the others don't get a duplicate.

## Cross-posting without looking automated
Cross-posting saves hours, but followers notice when every network gets the identical post.

### Write for the platform, not just the limit
Open with a hook on X and Threads, add context on LinkedIn, and give Google Business Profile a clear local call to action. Small edits make one idea feel native in each place. Our [caption customization guide](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) has tips for each network.

### Match the format to the network
A vertical video can be a Reel on Instagram, a TikTok and a YouTube Short in one post, while a photo set becomes a carousel on Instagram and a multi-image post on Facebook.

### Know the gaps
Schedule For You posts to personal LinkedIn profiles, not LinkedIn company pages, which still need LinkedIn's own scheduler. It doesn't do TikTok LIVE or duets, it doesn't publish to Discord, Telegram, Reddit or Slack, and it reports publishing results rather than likes and impressions.

## Frequently asked questions
### How do I post to all social media at once?
Use a cross-posting tool. In Schedule For You, write the post, select every account it should go to, adjust each platform's version and schedule it. Everything publishes at the time you pick, and any account that fails can be retried on its own.

### Is there a free way to post to all social media at once?
Partly. Meta Business Suite posts to Facebook and Instagram together for free, and Instagram and Facebook can share photo posts to Threads. For X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky and Pinterest you'd post in each app by hand, or use a paid tool.

### What is a cross-posting tool?
It's an app that publishes one post to several social accounts and platforms at once. Schedule For You adds a separately editable version for each platform, so you aren't forced to send identical text everywhere.

### Is cross-posting bad for engagement?
Copying the exact same caption everywhere can feel out of place, because each platform has different norms and limits. Tailoring each version keeps the benefits of cross-posting without that downside.

### Can I cross-post to several accounts on the same platform?
Yes. You can select multiple accounts on one platform, such as several Instagram accounts or Facebook Pages, in the same post. See how to [manage multiple social media accounts](/manage-multiple-social-media-accounts).

### What happens if one platform rejects my post?
The queue shows the failed account and the reason, and you get an email. Fix the cause, often a reconnect or a caption that's too long, then retry; only the failed accounts are published again.

### Which platforms can I cross-post to?
X, Bluesky, Instagram, LinkedIn (personal profiles), Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages, Pinterest and Google Business Profile.
