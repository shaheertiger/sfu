## How the cross-posting tool works
Schedule For You is a cross-posting tool for people who publish the same idea in several places. You write one post, select the accounts and platforms it should go to, tailor each platform's version, and schedule it once.

1. **Write the main caption** and attach your images or video.
2. **Select the targets.** Pick any mix of connected accounts across X, Bluesky, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages, Pinterest and Google Business Profile, including several accounts on one platform.
3. **Tailor each version.** Open a platform's version and edit it without touching the others.
4. **Fix anything flagged.** Live checks show when a version is over its platform's limit.
5. **Schedule or queue it.** Pick a time or add it to your next open time slot.

## Cross post to multiple platforms without the copy-paste problems
Pasting the same text everywhere is how captions get cut off, hashtags pile up on LinkedIn and posts fail at publish time. Per-platform versions solve that.

### A separate version for each platform
The main caption is your starting point. Change the X version to fit 280 characters, add a hashtag block to the Instagram version and keep the Facebook version conversational. Every other version stays exactly as it was. Our [caption customization guide](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) has tips for each network.

### Limits checked as you type
Each platform has a different ceiling, and the composer checks them all live.

| Platform | Text limit | Media notes |
|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters | Up to 4 images, a GIF or a video |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Up to 4 images or a video |
| Threads | 500 characters | Text only, one image or video, or a carousel of up to 10 |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 characters | One image, no video |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | One image, a Reel, or a carousel of 2–10 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | Up to 9 images or a video |
| TikTok | 4,000 characters (at the time of writing) | A video, or a photo post with up to 35 images |
| YouTube | Title 100, description 5,000 | Video required |
| Facebook Pages | 63,206 characters | Up to 10 images or a video |
| Pinterest | Keep it short; optional pin title | Image required, pick a board, optional link |

### Media that suits every target
Not every file works everywhere. YouTube needs a video, Pinterest needs an image and Google Business Profile doesn't take video. Check the table before you attach media, and split a post in two when one set of targets needs a video and another needs an image.

## What happens if a target fails
When you cross post to many accounts, one of them occasionally fails while the rest go through.

### Alerts with the reason
You get a failed-post alert telling you which account didn't publish. The most common cause is an expired connection, for example after a password change.

### Retry only what failed
Reconnect the account from the Accounts screen if needed, then retry. Only the failed targets are sent again, so accounts that already published don't get a duplicate.

## Cross-posting social media without looking automated
Cross-posting saves hours, but followers notice when every network gets the identical post.

### Write for the platform, not just the limit
Open with a hook on X and Threads, add context on LinkedIn, and give Google Business Profile a clear local call to action. Small edits make one idea feel native in each place.

### Match the format to the network
A vertical video can be a Reel on Instagram, a TikTok and a YouTube upload in one post, while a photo set becomes a carousel on Instagram and a multi-image post on Facebook.

### Know the gaps
Schedule For You doesn't post Stories, first comments or multi-post threads on X and Threads, and it doesn't publish to Discord, Telegram, Reddit or Slack. Plan those separately.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is a cross-posting tool?
It's an app that publishes one post to several social accounts and platforms at once. Schedule For You adds a separately editable version for each platform, so you aren't forced to send identical text everywhere.

### How do I cross post to multiple platforms at the same time?

### Is cross-posting bad for engagement?
Copying the exact same caption everywhere can feel out of place, because each platform has different norms and limits. Tailoring each version keeps the benefits of cross-posting without that downside.

### Can I cross-post to several accounts on the same platform?
Yes. You can select multiple accounts on one platform, such as several Instagram accounts or Facebook Pages, in the same post.

### What happens if one platform rejects my post?
You get an alert naming the failed account. Fix the cause, often a reconnect, then retry; only the failed targets are published again.
