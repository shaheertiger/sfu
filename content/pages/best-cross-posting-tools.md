The best cross-posting tools let you write one post, adjust a separate version for each network, check every version against that platform's limits, and publish the right media in the right format. Tools that simply copy one caption everywhere save time on day one, then cost you reach and fixes later. Below are the requirements that matter, the main types of cross-posting software with examples, and the free native options.

*Last reviewed: October 2026. Features and prices change; check each vendor's site before buying.*

## What a cross-posting app needs to do well
Cross-posting means publishing the same idea to several networks at once. The hard part is that each network has its own rules.

### Per-platform versions
One caption rarely fits everywhere. A LinkedIn post can run to 3,000 characters; a Bluesky post stops at 300. Hashtags that help on Instagram look out of place on LinkedIn. A good tool lets you write a main caption and then edit each network's version on its own, without touching the others. Our guide to [customizing captions for each platform](/blog/customize-captions-for-each-platform) walks through a practical routine.

### Limit checks before you publish
You want to see a problem while you're writing, not after the post fails. Look for a live character count per network and warnings about media limits.

| Platform | Text limit | Media notes (at the time of writing) |
|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters (standard accounts) | Up to 4 images, a GIF or a video |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Up to 4 images or a video |
| Threads | 500 characters | Text only, one image or video, or a carousel of up to 10 |
| Instagram | 2,200 characters | Feed photo, Reel, or carousel of 2–10 items; feed images between 4:5 and 1.91:1 |
| Facebook Pages | 63,206 characters | Text, up to 10 images, or a video |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 characters | Up to 9 images or a video |
| TikTok | 4,000 characters | A video, or a photo post with multiple images |
| YouTube | Title 100, description 5,000 | Video required |
| Pinterest | Up to 500 characters (pin description) | Image required; pick a board; optional pin title and link |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 characters | One image, no video |

### Media handling
Check what happens to your images and videos. Some networks need a video (YouTube) or an image (Pinterest), and some don't accept video at all (Google Business Profile posts). Instagram rejects feed images outside its aspect-ratio range, so a tool should either warn you or let you prepare the right size. If it doesn't crop for you, a resizer such as our free [Instagram photo resizer](/tools/instagram-photo-resizer) fills the gap.

### Multiple accounts and failure alerts
If you post to several accounts on one network, or for several brands, check how many connected accounts each plan includes. And when one target fails, for example because a network revoked access after a password change, you want an alert and a one-click retry, not a silent gap.

## Native cross-posting options
Before paying for anything, check what the networks already offer.

- **Meta's own tools.** Meta Business Suite lets you create posts for a Facebook Page and its linked Instagram professional account together, and Accounts Center has offered settings to share Instagram posts and Reels to Facebook automatically. Check Meta's help center for the current options.
- **Share buttons inside apps.** Some apps let you share a post to a sister app, such as Instagram and Threads. These are handy but limited to the same company's networks.
- **Each network's own scheduler.** YouTube Studio, LinkedIn and TikTok have offered native scheduling at the time of writing. They're free, but each covers only itself.

Native options are free and support every format the platform offers. The limit is reach: none of them cross-post to a competitor's network, so for X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest together you'll need a third-party tool. Our roundup of the [best free social media schedulers](/blog/best-free-social-media-schedulers) covers the free route in more depth.

## The best cross-posting tools by type, with examples
Tools fall into a few broad groups. Examples are well-known names, not endorsements, and each changes its features over time, so check details before you buy.

### Cross-posting schedulers
Focused tools built around one queue and one calendar for many networks. Buffer is known for simple queue-based scheduling, Publer for a wide set of publishing features, and Schedule For You for per-platform versions with live limit checks across 10 networks. They usually skip social inboxes and listening in exchange for a simpler interface.

### All-in-one suites
Hootsuite and Sprout Social combine publishing with social inboxes, analytics and team workflows. They cross-post well but are built for marketing teams, and you pay for the extras.

### Planners with analytics or visual focus
Metricool pairs scheduling with reporting. Later is known for visual Instagram planning and link-in-bio. Both cross-post, but each has a center of gravity: reports for Metricool, Instagram for Later.

### Automation platforms
Zapier, Make and n8n connect apps with triggers, such as "when a new blog post appears, share it". They're flexible for automated cross-posting from a feed or spreadsheet, but tailoring each network's version is up to you. Many schedulers, including Schedule For You, offer webhooks or an API that plugs into these tools. See [how to automate social media scheduling](/blog/how-to-automate-social-media-scheduling) for patterns.

## Where Schedule For You fits
Schedule For You is a cross-posting scheduler. One post can go to many accounts and platforms at once, each with its own caption version and live limit checks, and any target that fails alerts you so you can retry it. It publishes to Instagram, Facebook Pages, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Google Business Profile, with a queue, drag-and-drop calendar, bulk CSV scheduling, RSS auto-post and a REST API. The [cross-posting](/cross-posting) page shows how it works.

Be clear on the limits too. It doesn't post Stories, first comments or multi-post threads, doesn't crop images, posts to personal LinkedIn profiles only (not company pages), and has no analytics or AI writing. It has no free plan: Solo is $19 a month and Studio $59 a month, with a 7-day free trial that needs a card.

## How to choose the best cross-posting tool for you

| Your situation | Good starting point |
|---|---|
| Facebook and Instagram only | Meta Business Suite |
| Two networks, occasional posts | Each network's native scheduler |
| Many networks, tailored captions, several accounts | A cross-posting scheduler |
| Team inbox, listening and reports | An all-in-one suite |
| Auto-posting from a blog or spreadsheet | An automation platform, or a scheduler with RSS and an API |

Even the best cross-posting tools behave differently with your own accounts, so test your shortlist with a real week of content before you cross-post to multiple platforms for good. ## Frequently asked questions
### What is the best app to post on all social media at once?
No app posts everywhere. Pick the one that covers the networks you actually use and lets you tailor each version. Cross-posting schedulers suit most creators and small businesses; suites suit larger teams.

### Is cross-posting bad for reach?
Posting the identical caption everywhere can look out of place, since each network has different norms and limits. Tailoring each version, and using native formats like Reels or carousels, matters more than which tool sends the post.

### Is there a free cross-posting tool?
Native tools such as Meta Business Suite are free but limited to their own networks. Some third-party tools offer free tiers with limits; check each one. Schedule For You isn't free, but it has a 7-day free trial (card required).

### How do I cross-post from Instagram to Facebook?
Meta lets you link the accounts in Accounts Center and has offered automatic sharing settings, or you can post to both from Meta Business Suite. Check Meta's help center for the current steps.

### Can cross-posting tools post to Discord, Reddit or Telegram?
Some can; check each tool's network list. Schedule For You doesn't post to Discord, Reddit, Telegram, Slack or Snapchat.
