## What we make
Schedule For You is a social media scheduler. You write a post, adjust the version for each platform you're sending it to, and choose when it goes out. Everything sits in one queue and one drag-and-drop calendar, so you can see the week ahead at a glance and move things around when plans change.

Around that core are the tools that make regular posting practical: posting time slots, bulk scheduling from a CSV with media, a media library, repeating and evergreen posts, signatures appended to posts, RSS auto-post, reminder emails and a daily digest.

## Who it's for
### Creators
If you post the same idea to several networks, you shouldn't have to rewrite it from scratch in five apps. Batch your content when you have time, then let the queue publish it while you get on with making the next thing.

### Small businesses
Owners and small marketing teams need their Facebook Page, Instagram and Google Business Profile to stay current without daily logins. A few minutes of planning each week covers the essentials.

### Agencies and freelancers managing clients
Each client can get a workspace with its own accounts, queue, media and plan. Client preview links let them approve or comment on posts without creating an account, and the Studio plan adds team members so colleagues can work in the same space.

## How we build it
A few principles shape what we add and what we leave out.

### One post, tailored per platform
A caption that works on LinkedIn rarely works on X. You write once, then edit any platform's version without touching the others, with live character-limit checks for each network as you type.

### Honest about limits
Platforms decide what third-party tools can do. We say plainly what isn't supported, such as LinkedIn company pages, Facebook personal profiles and Groups, and stickers on Stories, so you can plan around it instead of finding out after a post fails.

### No AI writing that floods feeds
There are no AI writing features. We think feeds have enough generic, generated content, and your audience follows you for your voice. Schedule For You handles the timing and publishing; the words are yours.

### Simple pricing
Two plans, both with unlimited posts. The difference is how many accounts you connect and whether you work with a team.

### Know when something goes wrong
Platforms sometimes revoke access, for example after a password change. When a post can't publish, you get a failed-post alert with a prompt to reconnect the account and retry, rather than discovering a gap in your calendar days later.

### Open to people who automate
Webhooks connect to Zapier, Make or n8n. There's a REST API and an MCP server if you'd rather create and schedule posts from your own tools and workflows.
