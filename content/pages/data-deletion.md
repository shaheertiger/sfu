You can remove the data Schedule For You stores about you in three ways. None of them deletes anything already published on your social accounts; manage published posts on each platform.

## 1. Disconnect a platform

1. Sign in to Schedule For You.
2. Open the **Accounts** screen.
3. Choose the account and disconnect it.

This immediately deletes that account's access token and stored profile details from our database, and we can no longer publish to it.

## 2. Remove our access from Facebook or Instagram

1. On Facebook, go to **Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations** (or **Accounts Center → Apps and websites**).
2. Find **Schedule For You** and choose **Remove**.
3. Then email us (step 3 below) if you also want the data we stored about that account deleted.

Other platforms have similar settings; see [Revoking platform access](/privacy#revoking-platform-access) in our Privacy Policy.

## 3. Delete your whole account

Email **[privacy@scheduleforyou.com](mailto:privacy@scheduleforyou.com)** from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will:

- cancel any active subscription, so you aren't charged again;
- delete your account, workspaces you own, posts, media, connected accounts and tokens, API keys, webhooks and feeds;
- confirm by email once it's done.

We complete deletion within 30 days. Copies in database backups expire on a rolling basis after that. We keep only the billing records we're required to keep for tax and accounting.

If you're a member of someone else's workspace, deleting your account removes you from it, but the posts and media in that workspace belong to its owner.
