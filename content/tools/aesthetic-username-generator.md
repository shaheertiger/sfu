### Aesthetic usernames for Instagram
Instagram is where most people want an aesthetic username, and it's the most flexible: up to 30 characters, with periods and underscores both allowed. Periods can't start or end the name or sit two in a row, so `.moon.glow.` won't work but `moon.glow` will. Many aesthetic accounts use a period in place of an underscore because it looks lighter. Aesthetic boards and Discord servers suit the same names. Discord accepts periods; Pinterest usernames, by our checker's rules, take letters, numbers and underscores. Keep one version of the name across all of them so people can find you.

Hearts, stars, sparkles, flowers and styled letters (♡, ✿, ✧, 𝓂𝑜𝑜𝓃) aren't allowed in usernames on any of these platforms. Put them in your display name and bio instead, where most platforms accept them. The username stays plain and searchable; the profile around it carries the decoration.

## Best Practices for Aesthetic Usernames
### One word, not a sentence
The best aesthetic usernames are two parts at most: a soft word and your word. Three-part names like `rose_fern_diary` are pretty but harder to remember and to tag.

### Skip random numbers
Numbers break the mood. If your favourite is taken, try a different ending, swap the order (`glowmoon` vs `moonglow`), or use a period on platforms that allow it, before adding digits.

### Read it as a link
Picture it on your bio link or a business card: instagram.com/moon.glow. If it reads cleanly with no doubled letters at the joins, it's a good one.

### Watch for awkward joins
Some combinations run letters together in odd ways when the separator is removed. Read every candidate slowly; if a word appears that you didn't intend, use the underscore or period version.

### Keep the decoration in the profile
Symbols, emoji and fancy fonts go in your display name and bio, not the username. Screen readers and search both handle a plain username best, and anyone can type it.

### Claim it everywhere at once
Once you've picked the name, claim it on every platform you might use on the same day, even if you won't post there yet.
