## Why Instagram removes your line breaks
Instagram often collapses empty lines in captions and bios, so a nicely spaced caption turns into one dense paragraph after you post. This generator fixes that by putting an invisible character on each empty line, which Instagram treats as content and keeps.

### Blank lines get merged
When a line has nothing on it, Instagram may decide it isn't needed and join the paragraphs either side. The result is that two or three short paragraphs become one block of text, especially when the caption was written in another app and pasted in.

### Trailing spaces cause breaks to vanish
A space at the end of a line, right before you press return, is one of the most common reasons a break disappears. It's easy to add one by accident and impossible to see. The generator trims those trailing spaces automatically.

### Behavior varies by app version
How strictly Instagram cleans up spacing has changed over time and can differ between the iPhone app, the Android app and the web. A caption that kept its spacing last month may not this month. Instagram can change this at any point, so check your post after publishing.

## How the line break generator works
The tool makes two small changes to your text and leaves everything else exactly as you wrote it.

### It fills empty lines with an invisible character
Each completely blank line gets a single blank Unicode character. It doesn't show up as a dot or a symbol, but Instagram sees that the line isn't empty and keeps it.

### It strips spaces at the ends of lines
Any spaces sitting at the end of a line are removed so they can't interfere with the break that follows.

### Your words, emoji and hashtags stay the same
Nothing else is rewritten. Emoji, mentions, hashtags and links are passed through untouched. The tool runs in your browser, so your caption isn't uploaded anywhere.

## How to use the generator
### 1. Write or paste your caption
Type straight into the box or paste a draft from your notes app. Lay it out the way you want it to look, blank lines included.

### 2. Copy the converted text
Press the copy button. The copied version contains the invisible characters, so paste it as-is and don't retype any of the blank lines.

### 3. Paste into Instagram and preview
Paste into the caption or bio field. Before you share, scroll through the preview to confirm the spacing. If one break still collapses, look for a stray space or an extra empty line you added after pasting.

## Tips for readable captions
### Use spacing to separate ideas, not every sentence
A blank line between a hook, the main point and a call to action makes a caption easy to scan. A blank line after every sentence makes people scroll past it.

### Put the hook before the cut-off
Instagram shows only the first line or two before "more". Make that opening line count, and keep the spacing for what follows.

### Remember invisible characters still count
Each invisible character uses up a character of your limit. Captions can be up to 2,200 characters and bios up to 150, so a bio with several spaced lines has less room for words. Instagram also allows a maximum of 30 hashtags per post.

### Keep hashtags tidy
If you use hashtags, many creators separate them from the caption with a couple of spaced lines so the message reads cleanly. The generator keeps that gap for you.

### Bios need extra care
Bio spacing is the most likely to break, and there's little room to work with. Short lines, one idea per line, and a clear link prompt usually work better than decorative layout. For wording ideas, try the [Instagram bio generator](/tools/instagram-bio-generator).

## Frequently asked questions
### Why does Instagram delete my line breaks?
Instagram tends to collapse empty lines and can drop a break when a line ends in a space. Filling blank lines with an invisible character and removing trailing spaces usually keeps the layout intact.

### What invisible character does the tool use?
It inserts a blank Unicode character that displays as empty space. Instagram treats the line as having content, so it doesn't merge it away.

### Does this work for Instagram bios?
Yes, it works for bios as well as captions. Bios are short, at 150 characters, so each invisible character takes a little of that space.

### Will my line breaks always stay in place?
Usually, but not guaranteed. Instagram changes how it handles text from time to time, so preview the post and check it after publishing.

### Can screen readers handle the invisible characters?
Screen readers generally skip blank characters or read them as a pause. Keep the spacing purposeful so the caption still makes sense when read aloud.

### Is my caption saved or uploaded?
No. Everything happens in your browser, and nothing is stored once you close the page.
