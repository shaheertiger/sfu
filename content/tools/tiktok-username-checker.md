## A rule check, not an availability guarantee
The checker confirms whether a username follows TikTok's format rules and gives you a one-tap link to tiktok.com/@yourname. It can't tell you for certain that a name is free, because TikTok has no public availability lookup and names can stay attached to accounts you can't see. To know for sure, try the name in TikTok under Edit profile, where the app tells you if it's taken.

### The rules it tests
- 2 to 24 characters
- Letters, numbers, underscores and periods only
- No spaces or other symbols
- Doesn't end with a period

- At least one letter, so people can find and remember it

When a name breaks a rule, the checker explains the problem in plain words and lists close variations that pass.

### Check a list of usernames
Switch to **Check a list** to paste up to 200 names, one per line or separated by commas. Each one is marked as valid or gets the first rule it breaks, valid names link straight to their tiktok.com/@ profile, and you can copy only the valid ones. It turns a long brainstorm into a short list worth opening.

### Reading the profile link
Tapping the profile button opens tiktok.com/@ and your name in a new tab. A profile with videos or a bio means the name is in use. A "couldn't find this account" message is encouraging but not proof: banned, deactivated or otherwise hidden accounts may still be holding it.

## How to check if a TikTok username is available
Checking takes two steps: make sure the name follows TikTok's rules and isn't already on a visible profile, then confirm in the app, which is the only place that gives a definite answer.

### 1. Type the name you want
Enter the username exactly as you'd want it to appear, without the @ in front. Leave out spaces; if you'd normally separate words, try an underscore or a period instead.

### 2. Fix what's flagged
Most failures come from three things: the name is over 24 characters, it includes a character TikTok doesn't accept (hyphens, apostrophes, emoji), or it ends with a period. The explanation tells you which.

### 3. Open the profile link
If the name passes, tap the profile button. A profile with videos or a bio means the name is in use, so move on to a variation. A "couldn't find this account" page means it's worth trying.

### 4. Compare the suggestions
If your first choice fails or is clearly taken, scan the suggested variations, or paste several candidates into **Check a list**. Pick the one that's still easy to read aloud and type on a phone keyboard.

### 5. Confirm in the app
Open TikTok, go to Profile, tap Edit profile, then Username. Enter the name and TikTok shows whether you can use it. If it's available, save it before someone else does. You can't swap usernames freely: at the time of writing TikTok only lets you change it once every 30 days, so it's worth getting right.

## TikTok username rules vs. other platforms
### TikTok is shorter than Instagram
TikTok caps usernames at 24 characters, while Instagram allows 30. If you want the same handle everywhere, design it for TikTok's limit first, then check it with the [Instagram handle checker](/tools/instagram-handle-checker).

### Periods can start a name but not end one
TikTok rejects a username that ends with a period. Underscores are allowed anywhere. A name like "plant.lab" works on both TikTok and Instagram; "plantlab." works on neither.

### Username vs. name
Your username is the @ handle in your profile link and the one people tag. Your name is the display text above it, which can include spaces and is set separately in Edit profile. Viewers searching TikTok can find you by either.

## Tips for a TikTok username that sticks
### Say what you post
Viewers often decide to follow from a single video. A username that hints at your topic ("@fixit.with.dana" rather than "@dana8841") tells them what more of your videos will be about.

### Avoid look-alike characters
Mixing a capital I and a lowercase l, or a 0 and an O, makes a name hard to type from memory. Underscores at the end ("dana__") also look like typos when read aloud.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can this checker tell me if a TikTok username is taken?
Not definitively. It validates the format and opens the profile URL so you can look, but TikTok has no public availability check. Entering the name in Edit profile in the TikTok app is the only reliable test.

### How many characters can a TikTok username have?
Between 2 and 24 characters, using letters, numbers, underscores and periods.

### Can a TikTok username end with a period?
No. TikTok doesn't allow a period as the last character. A period in the middle of the name is fine.

### Why does TikTok say a name is taken when no profile shows up?
The name may belong to an account that's banned, deactivated or hidden from public view. Those accounts can keep their usernames, so a missing profile page doesn't mean the name is free.

### How often can I change my TikTok username?
At the time of writing, TikTok lets you change your username once every 30 days. Check TikTok's in-app help if the option is grayed out.

### How do I check if a TikTok username is available?
Type it into the TikTok name checker above to make sure it follows the rules, open the tiktok.com/@ link to see whether a profile exists, then enter it under Profile → Edit profile → Username in the TikTok app. The app's answer is the final one.

### Can I check a list of TikTok usernames at once?
Yes. Use **Check a list** to paste up to 200 names, see which follow TikTok's rules, and copy the valid ones.

### Is anything I type stored?
No. The checker runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or saved.
