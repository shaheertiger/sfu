## What makes a good X bio
Your X (formerly Twitter) bio has 160 characters to convince someone who just read one of your replies that your other posts are worth following. This free X bio generator gives you 12 options inside that limit, each with a character count.

### It answers "why follow?" in one read
People usually reach your profile from a reply, a quote post or a repost. They want to know what they'll get. "Posts about API design and the mistakes I've made with it" answers that faster than a list of job titles.

### It uses @mentions on purpose
Mentioning your employer, publication or project links your profile to theirs. One or two well-chosen handles add credibility; five handles in a row looks like clutter.

### It works with your pinned post
The bio says what you're about; the pinned post shows it. Keep the bio short and let your best thread, launch or portfolio post do the heavy lifting.

### It skips filler
Generic phrases like "thought leader" or "passionate about everything" use characters without telling anyone anything. A concrete detail, such as a city, a specialty or a current project, is more memorable.

## X profile rules and limits
X spreads profile information across several fields, so you don't need to cram everything into the bio.

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Bio | 160 characters |
| Display name | 50 characters |
| @handle | 4–15 characters: letters, numbers, underscores |
| Standard post | 280 characters |

### How characters are counted
In posts, X counts most emoji and CJK characters as two and every URL as 23. Our generator shows a plain count for each bio, so if you add several emoji or non-Latin text, check that X's profile editor accepts it. For posts, the [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter) applies X's weighting.

### Use the location and website fields
Your city and your link have dedicated fields. Moving them out of the bio frees space for a sentence about what you post.

### Handles and display names
If you're rethinking your name too, the [X name generator](/tools/x-name-generator) suggests personal handles and the [X page name generator](/tools/x-page-name-generator) works for brand accounts. Whatever you pick, X's rules on [help.x.com](https://help.x.com) don't allow impersonation and require parody, commentary and fan accounts to say so clearly.

## Frequently asked questions
### How many characters can an X bio have?
An X bio can be up to 160 characters. Your display name (50 characters), handle, location and website are separate fields, so they don't use bio space.

### Do emoji count as two characters in an X bio?
X counts most emoji as two characters in posts. Bio counting isn't always identical, so treat our character count as a guide and rely on X's profile editor if you're within a few characters of 160.

### Can I add hashtags to my X bio?
Yes, hashtags and @mentions in your bio become clickable links. One relevant hashtag, such as a community or event tag, can help; a row of them makes the bio harder to read.

### Is the X bio generator free?
Yes. You can use it free, with no account and no limit on how many sets of bios you generate.

### Can I use bold or fancy fonts in my bio?
You can paste Unicode-styled characters, but they're look-alike symbols rather than a real font. Screen readers may read them oddly and search may not match them, so keep important words in plain text.
