## What makes a good Instagram bio
An Instagram bio has 150 characters to tell a visitor who you are, what they'll see if they follow, and what to do next. This free bio maker gives you 12 drafts inside that limit, so you can pick the closest one and adjust it instead of staring at an empty field.

### It says what you post, not only who you are
"Designer and dog person" describes you. "Small-batch ceramics, studio process and kiln fails" tells a visitor what will show up in their feed. The second version gives people a reason to tap Follow.

### It gives one clear next step
Point visitors to a single action: shop the latest drop, book a session or watch the newest Reel. Competing calls to action tend to cancel each other out.

### It sounds like your captions
If your posts are playful, a stiff corporate bio feels off. If you sell professional services, a string of jokes can undercut trust. Pick the tone you actually write in.

## Instagram bio rules and limits
A few profile fields work together, and knowing which is which saves you characters.

### The bio field holds 150 characters
Letters, spaces, line breaks and emoji all count, and the Instagram app has the final say if you're right at the edge.

### Your name field is separate
The bold name above your bio is its own field, and Instagram search looks at names as well as usernames. Adding a plain keyword there, such as "Maya | Wedding Photographer", can help the right people find you without using bio space.

### Links, mentions and hashtags
Website addresses typed into the bio text aren't clickable. Use the links section in Edit profile instead, which allows up to five links at the time of writing. @mentions and hashtags in the bio do become tappable, so use them for a second account or a branded hashtag, not as filler.

## Frequently asked questions
### How long can an Instagram bio be?
An Instagram bio can be up to 150 characters. That count includes spaces, line breaks and emoji. The name, username, category and links fields sit outside it, so you can spread information across them.

### Do emoji count toward the 150-character limit?
Yes. Every emoji uses at least one character, and some, like flags or skin-tone variants, are made of several characters that each count. If a bio is close to the limit, paste it into Instagram and check whether the app accepts it.

### How do I add line breaks to my Instagram bio?
You can press return in the bio field on most versions of the app, but spacing sometimes collapses when you paste from elsewhere. Our [line break generator](/tools/instagram-line-break-generator) adds an invisible character on empty lines so the layout stays put.

### Can I use fancy fonts in my Instagram bio?
You can paste styled Unicode text from the [Instagram font generator](/tools/instagram-font-generator), but use it sparingly. Those characters aren't a real font, screen readers may read them letter by letter, and search may not match them. Keep your key words in plain text.
