## Slack image sizes at a glance
Slack custom emoji should be 128×128 and under 128 KB, while profile photos and workspace icons work well at 512×512. Square images work best everywhere in Slack, and the resizer outputs square files for all three presets.

| Preset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Custom emoji | 128×128 | Max 128 KB; square works best |
| Profile photo | 512×512 | At least 512×512 recommended |
| Workspace icon | 512×512 | Shown in the workspace switcher and sidebar |

### Custom emoji
Emoji appear inline with text and as reactions, so they're tiny on screen. Slack accepts larger images and scales them down, but starting at 128×128 gives you control over how the emoji looks rather than leaving it to the scaler.

### Profile photo
Your profile photo shows next to every message you send, in DMs and in search results. A square of at least 512×512 keeps it sharp in the larger profile view while still looking clean in the small message thumbnail.

### Workspace icon
The workspace icon helps people who belong to several workspaces tell them apart in the switcher. A bold initial or simple mark tends to work better than a detailed logo at that size.

## How to resize an image for Slack
### 1. Upload your image
Choose a PNG or JPG. The file stays on your device; the resizer works in your browser.

### 2. Choose emoji, profile photo or workspace icon
The canvas switches to the right square size for the preset.

### 3. Crop or pad
**Fill (crop)** zooms in so the image covers the square and trims the excess, which is ideal for headshots. **Fit (add background)** shrinks the image to fit inside the square and fills the gaps with a color you choose, which keeps wide logos or reaction screenshots whole.

### 4. Download
Pick PNG for emoji and icons with crisp edges, or JPG for photos. Then upload through Slack's emoji settings, your profile, or your workspace settings, depending on your permissions.

## Tips for crisp Slack images
### Stay under 128 KB for emoji
A 128×128 PNG of a simple graphic is usually well under the limit. If Slack rejects your file, simplify the artwork, reduce the number of colors, or try exporting as JPG.

### Zoom in on the important part
For reaction emoji, crop tightly around a face or a single object. Wide shots with lots of background become unreadable at emoji size.

### Pick a background that works in both themes
Slack has light and dark themes, and team members choose their own. When using fit, choose a background color that looks intentional on both rather than one that blends into either.

### Use a real photo for your profile
Teammates recognize faces faster than avatars or logos, especially in a large workspace. A well-lit headshot cropped with fill works best.

## Frequently asked questions
### What size should a Slack custom emoji be?
Use 128×128 pixels and keep the file under 128 KB. Square images work best.

### What size is a Slack profile photo?
Upload at least 512×512. Slack crops profile photos to a square, so center your face.

### What size should a Slack workspace icon be?
A 512×512 square works well and stays sharp across the desktop and mobile apps.

### Why won't Slack accept my emoji?
The most common reason is file size: emoji must be 128 KB or smaller. Simplify the image, crop more tightly or export a different format.

### Can I make an animated Slack emoji here?
No. The resizer exports still PNG or JPG files only.

### Are my images stored anywhere?
No. Resizing runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded to a server.
