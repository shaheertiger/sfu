## Styles you can generate for Pinterest

Pinterest shows text in its own typeface, but it displays Unicode characters you paste in. Type a word or phrase, browse 30+ styles and tap to copy. Because Pinterest works like a search engine, the trick is deciding where styling helps and where it costs you clicks.

### Aesthetic styles

Script (𝒲ℯℯ𝓀𝓁𝓎), bold script, small caps, fullwidth vaporwave letters and superscript match the soft, curated look of many mood boards.

### Editorial styles

Serif bold, italic, bold italic and sans-serif variants feel clean next to product and recipe pins. Monospace suits tech and design boards.

### Statement styles

Gothic and bold gothic (𝖂𝖊𝖊𝖐𝖑𝖞), double-struck outline letters, bubble and squared letters. They're eye-catching but harder to read at a glance, so keep them short.

### A quick note on what these are

They aren't fonts. Each style replaces your letters with Unicode symbols that look similar. Pinterest search, screen readers and translation tools don't treat them as the letters they imitate.

## Where styled text works on Pinterest

### Board names and descriptions

A styled accent word at the end of a board name, such as "Kitchen ideas · 𝓼𝓹𝓻𝓲𝓷𝓰," can make your profile grid look coordinated. Keep the main topic of the board in plain text so Pinterest can understand and surface it.

### Profile name and About section

Your profile name and About text accept Unicode. A styled tagline in your About section is low risk. Styling your whole profile name is riskier, because people searching for your brand may not find you. If you also want a matching profile picture, the [Pinterest logo generator](/tools/pinterest-logo-generator) makes a simple lettered icon.

### Pin descriptions

Descriptions accept styled text, but this is where keywords matter most. One short styled call to action, like "𝐒𝐚𝐯𝐞 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐥𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐫," is plenty; the rest should be plain, descriptive sentences.

### Comments

Styled text works in comments on pins, though comments are a small part of Pinterest. Use it for a quick reaction rather than a long reply.

### Text on the pin image

Text overlaid on the image itself, typically on a 1000×1500 (2:3) pin, should be designed in your image editor or Pinterest's own editing tools with real fonts. Unicode styles are for text fields, not graphics. To get the image itself to 1000×1500, use the [Pinterest photo resizer](/tools/pinterest-photo-resizer).

## Best practices for Pinterest

### Treat titles as search queries

Pinterest matches pin titles and descriptions to what people type in the search bar. A title written as "𝔼𝕒𝕤𝕪 𝕨𝕖𝕖𝕜𝕟𝕚𝕘𝕙𝕥 𝕕𝕚𝕟𝕟𝕖𝕣𝕤" may not match "easy weeknight dinners" at all. Keep pin titles plain.

### Don't style the words that describe your content

Product names, ingredients, places and topics should always be readable as normal text. Decorate the extras: a season, a mood word, or a short label.

### Accessibility matters on visual platforms

People using screen readers rely on text to understand pins. Styled characters may be read out as symbols or skipped, so don't put essential details only in a styled phrase. Add alt text to your pins in plain language.

### Check the grid on mobile

Most pinning happens on phones. Some decorative characters show as boxes on older devices, so check your board names in the app after saving.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change the font on Pinterest?

Not for text fields. Pinterest uses its own typeface for titles, descriptions and board names. You can paste Unicode look-alike characters from this generator, or design text with real fonts directly on your pin image.

### Do fancy fonts hurt Pinterest SEO?

They can. Pinterest search reads the actual characters, so styled words may not match what people type. Use styles for decoration and keep descriptive keywords in plain text.

### Can I use styled text in my Pinterest board names?

Yes, board names accept Unicode. Keep the main topic in plain words and style only an accent word, so the board still shows up for relevant searches.

### Why do some styles show up as squares?

The device viewing your board doesn't have a font that covers those characters. Bold and italic styles are the most reliable; bubble and squared letters fail more often.

### Is this Pinterest font generator free?

Yes. It's free, it needs no sign-up, and it runs in your browser without sending your text to a server.

### Which style is best for Pinterest?

Script and small caps fit the aesthetic boards Pinterest is known for, while sans bold works well for short calls to action. Whichever you pick, use one style consistently across your boards.
