## Tips for picking a Roblox username
### Make it easy to read and type
Friends add you by typing your name. Capitals at the start of each word ("LavaKnight") make it readable, and Roblox treats names that differ only in capitals as the same username when it checks availability. Swapping letters for numbers ("L4v4Kn1ght") makes a name harder to share.

### Use the underscore wisely
You get one underscore, so spend it where it helps: between two words ("Rocket_Rex") or before a number. "Rocket__Rex", "_RocketRex" and "RocketRex_" won't work.

### Your display name can be friendlier
Your username is unique and shown with an @ in front. Your display name is what other players see first in games, it doesn't have to be unique, and you can change it separately. If the exact name you wanted is taken as a username, you can often use it as your display name and pick a close variation as your username. Changing your username later costs Robux, so it's worth choosing carefully the first time.

## Keep your Roblox name safe
Roblox is popular with kids, and a username is public. These rules matter more than finding the perfect name.

### Leave out personal details
Don't put a real name, last name, age, birth year, school, city or street in a username. "CozyBunny" tells strangers nothing; "Emma_Leeds2015" tells them a first name, a place and roughly how old someone is. That's also why it's best to type an interest into the generator, not your name.

### Never share your password for a name
No username generator or checker needs your Roblox password, login or cookie. Sites that ask for them, or promise free Robux, rare usernames or "OG" names in exchange, are trying to steal accounts. This generator runs in your browser, doesn't ask you to sign in and doesn't send what you type anywhere.

### Let Roblox's filter and a parent have a say
Roblox blocks names its filter considers inappropriate or that look like personal information, so a name that passes here can still be refused. Parents and younger players can pick a name together, turn on two-step verification and review Roblox's parental controls.
