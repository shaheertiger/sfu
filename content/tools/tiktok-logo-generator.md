### Made for a tiny circle
Your TikTok profile picture shows as a circle on the right side of every video and next to every comment you leave. At that size, a strong letter or a single emoji is easier to spot than a detailed photo.

### Good for faceless accounts
Plenty of accounts don't show a face: tutorials, page accounts, clip channels, small brands. A clear icon gives those accounts a recognizable identity without needing a photo.

### Avoid TikTok's own branding
Don't recreate TikTok's music-note logo or its distinctive cyan-and-red style in your icon. It can make people think your account is official, and it may break TikTok's brand rules. Use your own letters, colors and emoji.

## How to make your TikTok profile icon
### 1. Choose letters or an emoji
Type the initials of your username or brand, or pick one emoji that fits your content.

### 2. Pick a shape
Circle matches how TikTok shows the picture. Choose rounded square or square if you plan to reuse the icon somewhere else.

### 3. Style the background and text
Try a gradient for a punchy look or a solid color for something cleaner. Set a text color that clearly contrasts with the background.

### 4. Download and set it on TikTok
Download the PNG, then go to Edit profile in the TikTok app and change your photo. The icon is generated in your browser and never uploaded.

## Design tips for TikTok profile icons
### Contrast matters more than color choice
TikTok shows your icon over moving video. High contrast between the letters and background keeps it readable whatever is playing behind it.

### Choose a signature color
Pick one color and use it in your icon, your video text and your thumbnails. Viewers start to recognize your content before they read your name.

### Keep the emoji or letters centered
Because the picture is cropped into a circle, anything near the corners disappears. The circle preview shows exactly what will be visible.

### Match your username
If your handle is long, use its first one or two letters. Your username can be up to 24 characters, but your icon should be readable at a glance. For handle ideas, try the [TikTok name generator](/tools/tiktok-name-generator).
