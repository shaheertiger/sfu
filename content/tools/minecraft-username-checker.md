## Minecraft Username Rules at a Glance
A Minecraft Java Edition username has to be 3 to 16 characters long and can only use letters, numbers and underscores. This Minecraft name availability checker tests a name against those rules as you type. When it passes, a button searches NameMC, a third-party lookup site, so you can see whether a player already has it.

## Minecraft Username Rules in Detail (3–16 Characters)
### Length: 3 to 16 characters
Java Edition names need at least 3 characters and can't go past 16. The checker shows the length as a count out of 16 and tells you how far over you are: "Too long: 18 characters, the limit is 16." Two-letter names fail with "Too short: Minecraft needs at least 3 characters." Three-letter names are valid, but nearly all of them were claimed long ago.

### Letters, numbers and underscores only
The underscore is the only symbol Minecraft allows. Hyphens, periods, apostrophes, emoji and accented letters such as ö fail with "Only letters, numbers and underscores are allowed." Block-Builder has to become Block_Builder or BlockBuilder.

### No spaces
A space anywhere in the name fails with "Spaces aren't allowed." To show two words, join them with an underscore or use capitals: Block_Builder or BlockBuilder.

### Capitals are yours to choose, but not unique
Minecraft keeps the capitals you pick, so the name can appear as BlockBuilder in chat. Uniqueness ignores case, though: if blockbuilder exists, you can't register BlockBuilder. The checker shows your name exactly as you typed it, capitals included.

### Underscores can go anywhere
There's no limit on where underscores go or how many there are, so xX_Miner_Xx-style names are fine as long as they stay within 16 characters and use only letters, numbers and underscores.

### Java Edition vs. Bedrock Edition
These rules are for Java Edition profile names, the name that shows in chat, on a server's player list and in commands such as /whitelist add. Bedrock Edition (consoles, phones and Windows) shows your Xbox gamertag from your Microsoft account. Gamertags have their own rules and their own availability check in your Microsoft account settings, so this checker and NameMC don't apply to them.

## What This Minecraft Username Checker Does (and What It Can't)
### It checks the format Minecraft accepts
The checker answers the part you can answer on your own: is this a name Minecraft accepts? It removes spaces around the name (and a leading @, if you paste one), counts the characters, and lists every rule the name breaks in plain words. A valid name shows "Follows Minecraft's rules"; an invalid one shows "Not allowed" with each reason.

### It searches NameMC, a third-party site
When a name passes, the checker shows an **Open namemc.com/search…** button. **NameMC is a third-party lookup site. It isn't run by Minecraft, Mojang or Microsoft.** It keeps its own record of Java Edition profiles, including the names a profile has used before. If a profile with your exact name shows up, ignoring capitals, the name is almost certainly taken. Because NameMC is a separate site, its data can lag behind Minecraft's, and it can't see every reason a name is blocked.

### It can't promise a name is free
Mojang and Microsoft don't offer a public availability check, so no website can confirm a name is free. A missing NameMC profile means "worth trying," not "yours." The name change screen in your Minecraft account gives the final answer.

### It suggests close valid variations
When you type a name, the checker suggests up to 12 variations that already pass the rules, such as steve_, itssteve, realsteve, stevehq or stevelive. They're in lowercase; you can add capitals when you claim the name. A name longer than 12 characters is shortened first so the additions still fit within 16.

### It checks a whole list at once
Switch to **Check a list** and paste up to 200 names, one per line or separated by commas. Duplicates are dropped, each name shows whether it follows the rules or the first rule it breaks, and every valid name links to its NameMC search. A **Copy the valid ones** button copies only the names that pass.

### It adds a tip for names without letters
A name made only of numbers and underscores, such as 2012_2012, is valid but hard for friends to remember or type into a whitelist. The checker adds a tip to include at least one letter. The name still passes, because the tip is advice, not a rule.

### It runs in your browser
Nothing you type is sent to us or stored. The NameMC button is an ordinary link that opens their search in a new tab, and you don't sign in to anything.

## How to Check if a Minecraft Username Is Taken
1. **Type the name you want.** Capitals are fine; the checker shows the name exactly as you typed it.
2. **Read the result.** "Follows Minecraft's rules" means the format is valid. "Not allowed" comes with the reason, such as a hyphen or a 17th character.
3. **Search NameMC.** Tap the **Open namemc.com/search…** button. Look for a profile with exactly your name, ignoring capitals.
4. **Try a variation if needed.** Pick one of the suggestions, or paste your shortlist into **Check a list** and open each NameMC link.
5. **Claim it in your account.** Sign in to your Minecraft account on the official Minecraft website or in the launcher and open the profile name settings (labels change between versions). Minecraft tells you whether the name is available when you try to save it. That's the only definitive check.
6. **Tell your servers.** Servers that use a whitelist or ranks may need to know about your new name, so let the admins know after you change it.

## Best Practices for Picking a Minecraft Username
### Keep it readable in chat
Names show up in small text in chat and above players' heads. Short, pronounceable names are easier for friends to type into /msg or a whitelist. Long runs of underscores and numbers make that harder.

### Avoid personal details
Your username is public on every server you join and on sites like NameMC. Leave out your full real name, birth year, school or town.

### Watch for lookalike letters
Capital I and lowercase l look alike in Minecraft's font, as do O and 0. A name that mixes them is easy to mistype and easy for someone else to copy.

### Plan for the long term
A name that's funny at 12 can be awkward at 20, and Minecraft limits how often you can change it. Something neutral and memorable lasts longer than an in-joke.

### Never give your login to a "checker"
A username checker never needs your Microsoft password, an account code or a login. Sites that ask for them, or promise rare names or free accounts, are trying to steal accounts. This tool works without signing in to anything, and NameMC's search doesn't need your login either.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can this checker tell me if a Minecraft name is taken?
Not with certainty. It checks the name against Minecraft's rules and searches NameMC, a third-party site, for profiles that use it. The name change screen in your Minecraft account gives the final answer.

### Is NameMC an official Minecraft site?
No. NameMC is run independently and isn't part of Minecraft, Mojang or Microsoft. It's a public lookup site for Java profiles, and you don't need to sign in to use its search.

### How many characters can a Minecraft username have?
A Java Edition username can have 3 to 16 characters at the time of writing, using letters, numbers and underscores.

### Can a Minecraft username have spaces or a hyphen?
No. Only letters, numbers and underscores are allowed. Use an underscore or capitals to separate words, like Block_Builder or BlockBuilder.

### Are Minecraft usernames case-sensitive?
No. You can choose how the capitals appear, but Steve and steve count as the same name, so you can't register one if the other exists.

### Does this work for Bedrock Edition gamertags?
No. Bedrock Edition uses your Xbox gamertag, which has different rules and is checked in your Microsoft account settings. This checker covers Java Edition names.

### Can I check several Minecraft names at once?
Yes. Switch to **Check a list** and paste up to 200 names. It marks which follow the rules, links each valid one to a NameMC search, and copies the valid ones for you.

### Does the checker save the names I type?
No. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
