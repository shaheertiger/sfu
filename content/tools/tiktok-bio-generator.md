## What makes a good TikTok bio
A TikTok bio is limited to 80 characters, which is about a dozen words. Most visitors arrive from a single video on the For You feed, so the bio's job is to confirm "yes, there's more of this here." This free TikTok bio generator writes 12 options in that space and shows each one's length.

### The niche comes first
Someone who liked your sourdough video wants to know if the account is all baking or just one lucky clip. Starting with "Sourdough for beginners" settles it in three words.

### It gives people a reason to follow now
Ongoing series, a regular posting day, or "part 2 tomorrow" style hooks turn a one-time viewer into a follower. Even "Daily 60-second Spanish lessons" works as a promise.

### Emoji act as punctuation
At 80 characters, emoji can replace words: a 📍 before a city or a 👇 pointing to your link. Two or three is plenty; a row of them leaves no room for meaning.

### It sounds like your videos
If your videos are deadpan, a bubbly bio feels like a different person. Choose a tone that matches how you talk on camera.

## How to use the TikTok bio generator
It runs in your browser, free and without an account. Nothing you enter is uploaded.

### Say what you make
Type a short phrase such as "nurse sharing night shift life and easy meals." Mention the format too if it's distinctive, like "60-second," "series" or "storytimes."

### Choose a tone
Pick friendly, professional, funny, minimal or aesthetic, and switch emoji on or off.

### Copy and paste
Every bio shows its character count, so you can see what's left at a glance. Tap one to copy it, then open your TikTok profile, tap **Edit profile**, and paste it into Bio.

## TikTok profile rules and limits
Your bio sits alongside a handful of other profile fields, each with its own rules.

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Bio | 80 characters |
| Username | Up to 24 characters: letters, numbers, underscores, periods |
| Video caption | 4,000 characters (at the time of writing) |
| Profile photo | 200×200 minimum recommended |

### Not every account can add a website link
TikTok decides which accounts get a clickable website field, and eligibility can depend on account type and other factors. If you don't see the option in Edit profile, writing a full URL into the bio won't make it clickable, so point people to a pinned video or another platform instead.

### Line breaks change the layout
Breaking the bio into two or three short lines makes it easier to scan. Line breaks are small, but they still use characters on an 80-character budget.

### Community Guidelines apply to your profile
Your bio has to follow TikTok's Community Guidelines like your videos do. Avoid "follow for follow" requests, misleading giveaway claims and anything that implies you're someone you're not.

## Frequently asked questions
### How long can a TikTok bio be?
A TikTok bio can be up to 80 characters, including spaces, emoji and line breaks. Your username and display name are separate fields with their own limits.

### Why can't I add a link to my TikTok bio?
TikTok only offers the clickable website field to eligible accounts, and the rules depend on account type and other factors set by TikTok. Check Edit profile periodically, since eligibility can change.

### Can I use fancy fonts in my TikTok bio?
You can paste Unicode-styled text from the [TikTok font generator](/tools/tiktok-font-generator). Each styled letter is a separate symbol, so it may not show up in search and screen readers can stumble over it. Use it for one word, not the whole bio.

### Is the TikTok bio generator free?
Yes. It's free, needs no sign-up, and you can generate new sets of bios as often as you like.

### Should I put my posting schedule in my bio?
If you post on a reliable rhythm, yes. A line like "New episode every Friday" tells new viewers when to come back. If your schedule is unpredictable, describe your content instead so the bio stays accurate.
