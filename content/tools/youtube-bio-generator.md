## What makes a good YouTube channel description
On YouTube, your "bio" is the channel description: up to 1,000 characters that tell viewers what the channel covers, who it's for and when new videos arrive. This free generator writes 12 drafts from a short summary. Each one leaves room for your own details.

### The first sentence carries the most weight
Viewers often see only a short preview of your description before they tap to expand it. Open with the promise of the channel, such as "Weeknight dinners in 30 minutes or less," rather than "Welcome to my channel!"

### It uses the words viewers search for, naturally
If people would search "beginner woodworking" to find you, say "beginner woodworking" in a real sentence. A pasted list of keywords reads badly to humans and can run against YouTube's spam policies.

### It says when to come back
A line about your upload rhythm, like "New videos every Thursday," gives viewers a reason to subscribe now rather than later. Only promise a schedule you can keep.

### It tells collaborators how to reach you
Brands and other creators look for a contact route. Say where business inquiries should go, and never paste a personal phone number.

## YouTube channel limits and rules
The channel description is only one of several text fields, and they're easy to mix up.

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Channel name | 50 characters (at the time of writing) |
| Handle | 3–30 characters: letters, numbers, underscores, hyphens, periods |
| Channel description | 1,000 characters |
| Video title | 100 characters |
| Video description | 5,000 characters |

### Channel description vs. video description
The channel description introduces the whole channel and rarely changes. Video descriptions are written per upload and hold chapters, credits and links for that video. This tool is for the channel description.

### Links have their own section
Basic info includes a Links section for your website and social profiles. Adding them there keeps your description focused on what the channel is about.

## Frequently asked questions
### How long can a YouTube channel description be?
A YouTube channel description can be up to 1,000 characters. That's enough for three or four short paragraphs. Front-load the most important sentence, since viewers often see only the opening lines before expanding it.

### Where do I edit my YouTube channel description?
Open YouTube Studio, go to Customization, then Basic info. The Description field is there, along with your channel name, handle and links. Click Publish to save the changes.

### Should I add keywords to my channel description?
Use the phrases viewers would search for, written as natural sentences. Repeating a keyword over and over doesn't make the description more helpful and can conflict with YouTube's spam policies.

### Can I use bold text in my channel description?
YouTube doesn't format channel descriptions, but you can paste Unicode-styled letters from the [YouTube bold text generator](/tools/youtube-bold-text-generator). Keep them to a word or two, because screen readers and search may not handle them well.
