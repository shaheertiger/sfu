## What makes a good Telegram name
Telegram has two names worth getting right: the **username**, which becomes your public t.me link, and the **channel or group name**, the title subscribers see in their chat list. A good username is short and easy to type; a good channel name says in a few words why to join. Enter a keyword above to get 30 ideas for either.

### Your username is your link
Anyone can open your chat or channel at t.me/yourname. That link ends up in bios, flyers and email footers, so pick something you'd happily print and nobody will mistype.

### Your channel name competes in a chat list
Subscribers see your channel squeezed between friends and family chats. The first two or three words carry most of the weight, so lead with the topic or brand rather than "The Official…" or "Welcome to…".

### Privacy is a choice, not an afterthought
A personal username is optional on Telegram. If you set one, people can find and message you without your phone number; if you'd rather not be discoverable, you can skip it or remove it later. For public projects, give the channel a username and keep your personal account separate.

## How to use the Telegram name generator
The same keyword can feed both a username and a channel title.

### For a personal username
Enter your name, nickname or a word you like. Pick minimal or professional for a clean handle, or cool and funny for something with personality. Ideas use only a–z, 0–9 and underscores, and are 5 to 32 characters long.

### For a channel or group
Enter your topic ("frontend jobs", "budget travel deals", "chess puzzles"). Aesthetic and cool styles suit lifestyle channels; professional fits news, jobs and company updates; gaming suits clans and game communities. Use the longer, readable ideas as the channel title and a shorter one as its username.

### Copy and claim
Tap an idea to copy it, then paste it into Telegram under **Settings > Username** for your account, or in the channel's settings. Telegram checks availability as you type. The generator itself can't see which names are taken, so keep a couple of backups.

### Check names for business use
For a business or product channel, search trademarks and look for channels with similar names, which make it easier for copycats to confuse subscribers.

## Telegram name rules and limits
Values at the time of writing.

| Field | Limit | Allowed characters |
|---|---|---|
| Username (account, channel or group) | 5–32 characters | a–z, 0–9, underscores |
| Channel or group name | Up to 128 characters | Letters, spaces, emoji, most symbols |
| Bio | 70 characters (140 with Premium) | Text and emoji |

### Usernames aren't case-sensitive
"@ChessPuzzleDaily" and "@chesspuzzledaily" are the same username. Use capitals to make a long username readable where it's displayed, but expect people to type it in lowercase.

### One username, one owner
Each username belongs to one account, channel or group at a time. Rename a public channel's username and the old t.me link stops working, so update bios and pinned posts too.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the Telegram name generator free?
Yes. It works in your browser with no sign-up, and your keywords stay on your device.

### How long can a Telegram username be?
Between 5 and 32 characters at the time of writing, using lowercase letters a–z, numbers and underscores. Spaces, periods and hyphens aren't allowed.

### What's the difference between a channel name and a channel username?
The name is the title shown in chat lists and can be up to 128 characters with spaces and emoji. The username is the unique handle that creates the public t.me link.

### Can I change my Telegram username?
Yes. You can change or remove it in settings at any time. The old link stops working once you change it, so update anywhere you've shared it.

### Does the generator check if a name is available?
No. Telegram checks availability when you type a username in settings. Use the generator for ideas, then confirm in the app.
