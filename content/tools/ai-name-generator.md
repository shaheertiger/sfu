## A faster alternative to AI name generators
Most people searching for an AI name generator want the same thing: a handful of good username ideas, quickly, without thinking too hard. This tool gives you that. It isn't a language model. You don't have to choose. The two work well as steps in one process.

### 1. Brainstorm keywords with a chatbot

## Frequently asked questions
### Is this an AI name generator?
No. It doesn't use a language model. It combines your keyword with word lists and patterns for each style, then keeps only names that use characters every major platform allows, at 15 characters or fewer. That's why it's instant and why the ideas work as handles. If you want a model's help, use a chatbot to brainstorm keywords and bring them here.

### Is it free?
Yes. There's no sign-up and no limit on how many ideas you generate. Everything runs in your browser.

### Why do AI tools suggest usernames I can't use?
Language models write names that sound good, but they don't always respect character limits or allowed characters. A suggestion like "The.Nova-Studio" fails on X, Telegram and Instagram for different reasons. This generator removes anything outside letters, numbers and underscores and keeps names between 3 and 15 characters.

### Does it check whether a name is taken?
No. The tool can't see any platform's accounts. Use the [Instagram handle checker](/tools/instagram-handle-checker) or the [TikTok username checker](/tools/tiktok-username-checker) to confirm the format and open the profile link, then search for the name on each platform.

### What's a good length for a username?
Somewhere around 6 to 12 characters is easy to type from memory and fits even X's 15-character limit.

### Can I use a generated name for a business?
You can, but run a trademark search in the countries where you sell first, and make sure no one else trades under that name in your market.
