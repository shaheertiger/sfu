## What makes a good YouTube comment
YouTube comments can be longer and more thoughtful than on most platforms, and the ones that rise to the top usually add to the video: a timestamp, a tip, a question or a kind correction. This free YouTube comment generator gives you 12 template-based ideas to adapt. You write and post the final comment yourself.

### It points to a timestamp
Typing a time like 4:12 in a comment turns it into a link to that moment in the video. "The comparison at 4:12 finally made this click for me" is useful to the creator and to every viewer who reads it.

### It adds to the topic
On tutorials and explainers, a related tip, a note about what worked for you, or a politely worded correction keeps the conversation valuable. Start corrections with something like "Small note for anyone following along" rather than "Wrong."

### It asks a question the creator could answer
Questions such as "Would this work on a smaller budget?" give creators ideas for future videos. They also tend to collect replies from other viewers who know the answer.

### It leaves self-promotion out
Dropping a link to your own channel under someone else's video is widely seen as spam and can fall foul of YouTube's spam policies. Comment as a viewer, not an advertiser.

## How to use the YouTube comment generator
It runs in your browser. No account is needed, and what you type isn't uploaded.

### Describe the video
Summarize it in a line, for example "tutorial on fixing a leaky faucet" or "vlog about moving to a new city." The tool doesn't watch the video, so add the detail you want to react to.

### Choose a tone
Pick supportive, curious question, funny, professional or hype. Curious question and supportive work well on educational content; funny and hype suit entertainment and music.

### Add your own detail and post
Each of the 12 ideas is a draft. Add a timestamp or a specific observation, adjust the wording, and post it under the video yourself.

## YouTube comment tools for creators
If you run a channel, the comment section is part of your community. YouTube gives you tools to shape it.

### Reply from YouTube Studio
The Comments section of YouTube Studio collects comments from all your videos in one place, with filters for ones you haven't replied to yet. It's the fastest way to answer your audience without opening every video.

### Pin and heart comments
You can pin one comment to the top of a video and heart comments to show appreciation. Pinning a viewer's helpful tip or a correction you agree with shows you're listening.

### Moderation settings
YouTube Studio lets you hold potentially inappropriate comments for review and add blocked words. Setting these up early keeps the thread welcoming as the channel grows.

### Know the rules
YouTube's Community Guidelines and spam policies cover comments as well as videos. Repetitive comments, misleading links and fake engagement can be removed and may lead to restrictions on your account.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the YouTube comment generator free?
Yes. It's free, works in any browser and doesn't require a sign-up or a YouTube login.

### Does it post comments to YouTube?
No. It only suggests text. You choose an idea, edit it and post it from your own account, so every comment is yours.

### How do I add a clickable timestamp to a YouTube comment?
Type the time in minutes and seconds, such as 3:07, or hours, minutes and seconds for long videos, such as 1:12:45. YouTube turns it into a link that jumps to that point in the video.

### Can I use it to reply to comments on my own channel?
Yes, that's one of the best uses. When many viewers ask similar things, the ideas help you vary your replies. Add the viewer's specific question so each answer still reads as personal.

### Can I make text bold in YouTube comments?
YouTube comments support simple formatting at the time of writing: wrap text in asterisks for bold, underscores for italic and hyphens for strikethrough. For channel text that doesn't support formatting, see the [YouTube bold text generator](/tools/youtube-bold-text-generator).
