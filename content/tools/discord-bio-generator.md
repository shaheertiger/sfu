## What makes a good Discord About Me
Your About Me appears when someone clicks your name in a server or DM, and Discord gives you 190 characters for it. This free Discord bio generator turns a short description into 12 bios that fit, so you can grab one and personalize it.

### It answers what server-mates wonder
People open your profile to see who they're talking to. Your games, what you make, your time zone or when you're around for voice chat beat a vague quote.

### It sets expectations for DMs
A line like "DMs open for commissions" or "Please ping in the server instead of DMing" heads off awkward messages. Moderators in particular benefit from saying how they prefer to be contacted.

### It matches the servers you're in
A bio that suits a competitive gaming server may feel odd in a study group. If your communities differ a lot, keep it neutral.

### It keeps personal details out
Your profile is visible to people across every server you share. Leave out your school, address, full name or anything that would help a stranger locate you.

## How to use the Discord bio generator
The generator works in your browser. You don't need to log in, and nothing is sent to a server.

### Describe yourself
Write a phrase like "Valorant player, EU, learning pixel art." Concrete details give you more specific results.

Choose friendly, professional, funny, minimal or aesthetic, and decide whether you want emoji included.

Each of the 12 bios shows its length. Tap one to copy it, then paste it into **User Settings > Profiles > About Me** on desktop, or Edit Profile on mobile. At the time of writing, the About Me field supports Discord's text formatting, such as `**bold**` and `*italic*`. The asterisks count toward the 190 characters, so check the total with the [Discord character counter](/tools/discord-character-counter).

## Discord profile rules and limits
The About Me is one of several profile fields, each with its own limit.

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| About Me | 190 characters |
| Display name | 32 characters |
| Username | 2–32 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, underscores and periods |
| Custom status | 128 characters |
| Profile banner | 680×240 image |

### Per-server profiles
Nitro members can customize their profile for individual servers at the time of writing. Check Discord's profile settings to see which fields your account can change per server.

## Frequently asked questions
### How long can a Discord bio be?
The Discord About Me holds up to 190 characters. Formatting symbols, emoji and line breaks count toward that total, so a heavily formatted bio fits less text.

### Can I use bold or italic text in my About Me?
At the time of writing, yes. Discord's markdown works in the About Me field: wrap text in double asterisks for bold or single asterisks for italic. You can also paste Unicode styles from the [Discord font generator](/tools/discord-font-generator), but keep them short for readability.

### Does my About Me show in every server?
Your main About Me shows everywhere by default. Nitro members can set a different profile per server, which lets you keep a gaming bio in one community and a professional one in another.

### Can I put links in my Discord bio?
You can paste a link, and it's usually clickable. Many servers have rules about self-promotion, though, so a link to your portfolio is fine while a string of referral links may get you warned.

### Is this Discord bio generator free?
Yes. It's free, works in your browser and doesn't need a Discord login or any other account.
