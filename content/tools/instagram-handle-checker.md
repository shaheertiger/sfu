## What is an Instagram handle?
Your Instagram handle is your username: the name after the @ that people use to tag and mention you, and the last part of your profile URL (instagram.com/yourhandle). "IG handle," "handle on Instagram" and "Instagram username" all mean the same thing, so an IG handle checker and an IG username checker do the same job.

### Where to find your handle
Open the Instagram app and go to your profile. Your handle is shown at the top of the screen. On the web, it's the part of your profile URL after instagram.com/.

### Handle vs. display name
Your username is the @handle in your profile URL, and no two accounts can share one. Your display name is the bolder name shown on your profile, which can include spaces and doesn't have to be unique. People can find you by either, so a short handle and a descriptive name often work well together. If someone asks for your Instagram handle, give them the @username.

### How to change your Instagram handle
Go to your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then tap the **Username** field and type the new handle. Instagram checks the name as you type and won't let you save one that's taken. Once you change it, your old profile URL no longer leads to you, so update the link in your other bios.

## Instagram username rules in detail
### Periods and underscores
Periods are allowed in the middle of a handle but not at either end, and never two in a row. Underscores have no such restriction, so "_studio_" is valid while ".studio." is not. Hyphens, apostrophes, ampersands and emoji aren't allowed at all.

### Length and letters
A handle can be up to 30 characters. Very short handles are valid but rarely free, and a name made only of numbers and symbols is hard to find, so the checker asks for at least one letter.

### Keep it easy to say out loud
A handle that people can spell after hearing it once is easier to share on a podcast, a business card or a shop sign. Mixing periods and underscores ("the_bloom.studio") is valid but tends to get mistyped.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can this tool tell me if an Instagram username is available?
No. It checks the format and opens the profile URL, but Instagram doesn't provide a public availability check. The only definitive answer comes from entering the name in Edit profile inside the Instagram app.

### Is this an Instagram name checker or a username checker?
It checks usernames (handles). Your display name doesn't have to be unique, so there's no Instagram name availability to check: any name within Instagram's limits is yours to use. Only the @username has to be unique.

### Why does a handle look unused but Instagram says it's taken?
Deactivated, restricted or otherwise hidden accounts can still hold a username, so the profile URL may show nothing even though the name is reserved. Instagram doesn't release those names on a schedule you can predict.

### Can I check several Instagram names at once?
Yes. Use **Check a list** to paste up to 200 names. The checker shows which follow Instagram's rules, links each valid one to its profile, and lets you copy the valid names.

### How long can an Instagram username be?
Up to 30 characters. The shortest valid handle is one character, though short handles are rarely available.

### Can an Instagram username have a hyphen or space?
No. Only letters, numbers, periods and underscores are allowed. Use an underscore or period where you would put a space or hyphen.

### Are Instagram usernames case-sensitive?
No. Instagram stores usernames in lowercase, so capital letters you type are converted.

### Does the checker save the handles I test?
No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
