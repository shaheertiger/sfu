## What this checker can and can't tell you
This tool tells you whether a handle follows Instagram's username rules and lets you open instagram.com/yourhandle in one tap. It **cannot** confirm that a handle is free. Instagram doesn't offer a public availability check, and deactivated or restricted accounts can still hold names that look unused. The only reliable test is typing the name into Edit profile in the Instagram app.

### What it checks
- Length between 1 and 30 characters
- Only letters, numbers, periods and underscores
- No spaces
- Doesn't start or end with a period
- No two periods in a row

If a rule fails, the checker says which one and why, then suggests close variations that pass every rule.

### What the profile button shows
The profile button opens instagram.com/ followed by your handle in a new tab. If a profile appears, the name is taken. If you see a "page isn't available" message, the name may be free, or it may belong to an account that's deactivated, restricted or private in a way that hides it. Treat it as "worth trying," not "yours."

### Why it runs in your browser
Nothing you type is sent to a server or stored; the profile link is just a normal link to Instagram.

## How to use the handle checker
### 1. Type the handle you want
Enter it without the @. Instagram usernames aren't case-sensitive, so typing in lowercase shows you the handle the way it will actually appear.

### 2. Read the result
A pass means the format is valid. A tip explains what to fix, such as a trailing period or a hyphen, which Instagram doesn't allow.

### 3. Try a suggested variation
If the original fails or is clearly taken, pick one of the suggested variations. They stay close to what you typed and already pass every format rule, so you only need to see whether a profile exists.

### 4. Claim it in the app
Open Instagram, go to your profile, tap Edit profile and enter the handle in the username field. Instagram tells you straight away whether it's available. ## Instagram username rules in detail
### Periods and underscores
Periods are allowed in the middle of a handle but not at either end, and never two in a row. Underscores have no such restriction, so "_studio_" is valid while ".studio." is not. Hyphens, apostrophes, ampersands and emoji aren't allowed at all.

### Handle vs. display name
Your username is the @handle in your profile URL. Your display name is the bolder name shown on your profile, which can include spaces and is limited separately. People can find you by either, so a short handle and a descriptive name often work well together.

### Keep it easy to say out loud
A handle that people can spell after hearing it once is easier to share on a podcast, a business card or a shop sign. Mixing periods and underscores ("the_bloom.studio") is valid but tends to get mistyped.

## Tips for choosing a handle that's likely to be open
### Add a meaningful modifier
Short single words are almost always taken. Adding your city, trade or a short word like "studio," "shop" or "co" often produces a name that's both open and clearer about what you do.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can this tool tell me if an Instagram username is available?
No. It checks the format and opens the profile URL, but Instagram doesn't provide a public availability check. The only definitive answer comes from entering the name in Edit profile inside the Instagram app.

### Why does a handle look unused but Instagram says it's taken?
Deactivated, restricted or otherwise hidden accounts can still hold a username, so the profile URL may show nothing even though the name is reserved. Instagram doesn't release those names on a schedule you can predict.

### How long can an Instagram username be?
Up to 30 characters. The shortest valid handle is one character, though short handles are rarely available.

### Can an Instagram username have a hyphen or space?
No. Only letters, numbers, periods and underscores are allowed. Use an underscore or period where you would put a space or hyphen.

### Are Instagram usernames case-sensitive?
No. Instagram stores usernames in lowercase, so capital letters you type are converted.

### Does the checker save the handles I test?
No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
