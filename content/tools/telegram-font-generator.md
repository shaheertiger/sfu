## Built-in formatting or Unicode?

### Use Telegram's formatting in messages

In chats, channels and groups, select text in a message you're writing and open the formatting menu to apply bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, monospace or spoiler. Many Telegram apps also convert markdown as you type, such as `**bold**`, `__italic__` and `~~strikethrough~~`. Real formatting is readable by screen readers and keeps your posts searchable, so prefer it whenever it's available.

### Use Unicode where formatting can't go

Your first and last name, your bio, and the titles and descriptions of channels and groups don't support formatting. That's where styled Unicode is useful.

### Where neither works

Usernames (the @handle) only allow a–z, 0–9 and underscores, 5–32 characters. Styled characters aren't accepted there, and your public link depends on that handle, so keep it plain.

## Best practices for Telegram

### Keep public channels discoverable

Telegram's global search matches channel titles and usernames. A title written entirely in styled characters may not show up when people search for the plain words. Style a decorative part and leave the key topic readable.

### Help screen reader users

Assistive tech may read styled names as strings of symbols. In a group where people mention each other often, a mostly plain name is easier for everyone.

### Don't imitate official accounts

Styled characters can make a name look like a verified brand or another person. Impersonation breaks Telegram's terms of service, so make sure your name clearly represents you.

### Test across apps

Telegram runs on phones, desktop and the web. A character that looks fine on your phone may show as a box in another client, so check from a second device.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make bold text on Telegram?

In a message, select the text and choose bold from the formatting menu, or type `**text**` in apps that support markdown. For your name, bio or channel title, copy a bold Unicode style from this generator instead, or use the [Telegram bold text generator](/tools/telegram-bold-text-generator).

### Can I use a fancy font in my Telegram username?

No. Usernames only allow lowercase letters, numbers and underscores. Use a styled first name or channel title instead.

### Do styled characters count toward the bio limit?

Yes. Each styled character counts, and some take up more than one character, so plan for the 70-character limit (140 with Premium) and check that nothing gets cut off.

### Why can't my contacts see my styled name?

Their device or Telegram app is missing a font for those characters, so they may see boxes. Bold and italic styles are the most widely supported.
