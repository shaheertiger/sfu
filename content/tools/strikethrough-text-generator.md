### How combining marks work

Unlike bold or cursive styles, these don't replace your letters. They keep each normal letter and add a zero-width "combining" character right after it, which the device draws on top of or under the letter. That has two side effects: the underlying words are still plain letters, and every character you type becomes two.

## Where strikethrough text works

### Platforms that need the Unicode version

Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky and Pinterest have no strikethrough formatting in posts or bios, so a copied Unicode strike is the only way to show it. It pastes into captions, comments and bios on all of them.

### Platforms with built-in strikethrough

Some apps already support it, and their version is better because it's real formatting:

- **Discord**: `~~text~~` (see the [Discord font generator](/tools/discord-font-generator) for when Unicode helps)
- **Telegram**: the formatting menu, or `~~text~~` in many clients
- **YouTube comments**: `-text-`
- **Reddit**: `~~text~~`
- **Slack and WhatsApp**: `~text~`

Use the built-in option in those apps and save the Unicode version for names or fields where formatting isn't rendered.

### Places it won't work

Usernames, hashtags, @mentions, links and search fields need plain characters. A struck-through hashtag won't link anywhere.

## Best practices

### Mind the character count

Because each letter carries a hidden mark, crossing out 20 characters uses about 40. On X, with a 280-character limit, that adds up quickly. Paste your draft into the [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter) before posting.

### Don't rely on the line to carry meaning

Screen readers usually ignore the combining marks and read the letters normally, so "$̶4̶0̶ $25" may be heard as "40 dollars, 25 dollars." Add a plain word to make it clear: "Was $̶4̶0̶, now $25."

### Expect small rendering differences

Some fonts draw the line slightly too high, too low or with gaps between letters. Short strikethrough and slash styles tend to look neater in apps where the long stroke overlaps awkwardly. Check on your phone before publishing.

### Strike whole words, not single letters

A line through one letter often looks like a rendering glitch. Strike complete words or phrases so the intent is obvious.

Underline and double underline are useful for a single word you want to stand out without switching to bold, such as a deadline.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I cross out text on Instagram?

Instagram has no strikethrough option. Type your text here, copy the strikethrough style, and paste it into your caption, bio or comment.

### Why does my strikethrough look broken?

The app or device font doesn't position the combining mark well, so the line may have gaps or sit off-center. Try the short strikethrough or slash style, which often render more cleanly.

### Does strikethrough text count as extra characters?

Yes. Each letter is followed by a combining mark, so the text takes roughly twice as many characters. Leave room when you're close to a platform's limit.

### Can I remove the strikethrough after pasting?

Not with a formatting button. Delete the text and paste or type a plain version instead.
