### Why initials suit Pinterest
Pinterest is a visual search engine, and your profile picture appears next to every pin you save and every board you share. Small business owners, bloggers and shops often use a monogram here: it looks tidy, it's recognizable at tiny sizes, and it doesn't compete with the pins themselves.

### It's shown as a circle
Pinterest displays profile photos as circles. Upload a square of 280×280 or larger; the generator's 512×512 output gives Pinterest plenty to work with.

### Keep your own identity
Don't imitate Pinterest's own logo, such as a red "P" in a circle, or design your icon to look like an official Pinterest account. Doing so can mislead people and may go against Pinterest's brand guidelines. Use your own initials and colors instead.

## Design tips for a Pinterest profile icon
### Let your pins do the decorating
Pinterest feeds are busy with photography and color. A simple, flat icon stands out against that more than another detailed image would.

### Pick colors from your best pins
Look at the pins that represent your account best and borrow one or two colors from them. Your profile picture will then feel connected to the content people discover first.

### Two letters balance best
Monograms with two letters tend to look balanced in a circle. If you use three, choose a lighter background and darker text so the letters stay sharp.

### Keep it consistent with your pins' branding
If you add a small logo or watermark to your pins, match its colors to your profile icon. Pins are usually 1000×1500 (2:3), and a consistent corner mark makes your content easier to recognize when it's repinned.
