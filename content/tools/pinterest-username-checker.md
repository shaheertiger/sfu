## Pinterest Username Rules at a Glance
A Pinterest username has to be 3 to 30 characters long and can only use letters, numbers and underscores. This Pinterest username checker tests a name against those rules as you type. When it passes, a button opens pinterest.com/yourname so you can see whether a profile already uses it.

| Rule | What Pinterest allows (at the time of writing) | Checked here |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 3–30 characters | Yes |
| Characters | Letters A–Z, numbers 0–9 and underscores | Yes |
| Spaces | Not allowed | Yes |
| Periods, hyphens, emoji, accents | Not allowed | Yes |
| Capital letters | Accepted; don't rely on them to tell names apart | Shown as typed |
| Already taken | Can't be claimed | Profile link only |

## Pinterest Username Rules in Detail (3–30 Characters)
### Length: 3 to 30 characters
Pinterest gives you up to 30 characters, twice what X allows, and needs at least 3. The checker shows the length as a count out of 30 and tells you how far over you are: "Too long: 32 characters, the limit is 30." Two-letter names fail with "Too short: Pinterest needs at least 3 characters."

### Letters, numbers and underscores only
The underscore is the only symbol allowed. Periods, hyphens, apostrophes, ampersands, emoji and accented letters fail with "Only letters, numbers and underscores are allowed." That catches people who copy an Instagram handle with a period: cozy.home has to become cozy_home or cozyhome on Pinterest.

### No spaces
A space anywhere fails with "Spaces aren't allowed." Your Pinterest profile name (the display name at the top of your profile) can have spaces; the username in your URL can't.

### Underscores can go anywhere
Pinterest's format doesn't limit where underscores go, so _cozy_ is valid. It isn't easy to type or say, though, so most accounts keep underscores in the middle, if they use them at all.

### Capitals don't make a different name
The checker keeps your capitals so you can see how a name reads, like CozyHomeIdeas. Capitals won't get you a name that's already taken, though: when you check, treat CozyHome and cozyhome as the same name.

### Username vs. profile name
Your username is the unique part of your profile URL, pinterest.com/yourname, and it's what this checker tests. Your profile name is the bigger name on your profile. It can have spaces and doesn't have to be unique, so a business can use "Cozy Home Ideas · Small Space Decor" as the profile name and cozyhomeideas as the username.

## What This Pinterest Name Checker Does (and What It Can't)
### It checks the format Pinterest accepts
The checker removes a leading @ and spaces around the name, counts the characters, and lists every rule the name breaks. A valid name shows "Follows Pinterest's rules"; an invalid one shows "Not allowed" with each reason.

### It opens the profile so you can see if it's used
When a name passes, the checker shows an **Open pinterest.com/…** button. If a profile opens, the name is taken. If Pinterest shows a "not found" page, the name is worth trying.

### It can't promise a name is free
Pinterest doesn't offer a public availability check. Deactivated or suspended accounts can still hold a name, and Pinterest may reserve some. The username field in your Pinterest settings, or the sign-up form, gives the final answer.

### It suggests close valid variations
Whenever you type a name, the checker suggests up to 12 variations that already pass Pinterest's rules, such as cozyhomeideas_, thecozyhomeideas, itscozyhomeideas, cozyhomeideashq or cozyhomeideas_daily. Suggestions use lowercase letters, numbers and underscores, and a name longer than 26 characters is shortened first so the additions still fit within 30.

### It checks a whole list at once
Switch to **Check a list** and paste up to 200 names, one per line or separated by commas. Duplicates are dropped, each name shows whether it follows the rules or the first rule it breaks, and every valid name links to its Pinterest profile. A **Copy the valid ones** button copies only the names that pass.

### It adds a tip for names without letters
A name made only of numbers and underscores, like 123456, is valid but hard to find in search and hard to remember. The checker adds a tip to include at least one letter. The name still passes.

### It runs in your browser
Nothing you type is sent to a server or saved. The profile button is an ordinary link to pinterest.com, and you don't sign in to anything.

## How to Check if a Pinterest Username Is Available
1. **Type the username you want.** Paste it with or without the @; the checker removes it.
2. **Read the result.** "Follows Pinterest's rules" means the format is valid. "Not allowed" tells you why, such as a period or a 31st character.
3. **Open the profile.** Tap the **Open pinterest.com/…** button. A profile means the name is taken; a missing page means it may be free.
4. **Try a variation if needed.** Pick one of the suggestions, or paste your shortlist into **Check a list** and open each link.
5. **Claim it on Pinterest.** On an existing account, open Settings, then Edit profile, and change the Username field (menu labels change between app versions). Pinterest tells you if the name is taken when you save.
6. **Update your links.** Your profile URL changes with your username, so update it on your website, in your other bios and anywhere you've shared it.

## Pinterest Username Checker Use Cases by Audience
### Bloggers and recipe sites
Pinterest sends traffic back to your site, so your username should match your blog name or domain. If the exact name is taken, add a word that fits your topic, like recipes, eats or kitchen, and check the variations in one list.

### Ecommerce and Etsy sellers
Shoppers who find a product Pin often tap through to the profile. A username that matches your shop name makes it obvious they're in the right place. Thirty characters gives you room for "shop" or "studio" at the end.

A florist, salon or home builder can use the city in the username, like portlandflorals, to stand out from businesses with the same name elsewhere. The 30-character limit leaves room for it.

## Best Practices for Picking a Pinterest Username
### Make it match your website or shop
Pinterest is about sending people somewhere. A username that matches your domain or shop name builds trust when someone taps from a Pin to your profile and on to your site.

### Use words people search for, but keep it a name
A username like cozyhomeideas tells people what to expect. Don't stuff it with keywords, though: the profile name, board names and Pin descriptions are better places for those.

### Shorter is still easier
Thirty characters is the limit, not the target. A shorter name is easier to type from a business card and fits better in profile links shown in other apps.

### Skip the period habit
If your handle on other platforms uses a period, decide on one underscore version or a version without separators and use it consistently wherever periods aren't allowed.

### Know what the checker doesn't test
It follows Pinterest's published format, not Pinterest's private records. It can't see names held by deactivated or suspended accounts or names Pinterest reserves. Treat a pass and a missing profile as "worth trying."

### Don't trust tools that ask for your login
No website can see Pinterest's username database. A tool that asks for your Pinterest password can't check availability any better and may be after your account. This checker never asks you to sign in.

### Finish the profile
Once the username is set, a clear profile picture and logo help people recognize you. The [Pinterest logo generator](/tools/pinterest-logo-generator) and [Pinterest photo resizer](/tools/pinterest-photo-resizer) help with images, and the [Pinterest font generator](/tools/pinterest-font-generator) shows styled text for your profile name (screen readers may read styled letters badly, so use it sparingly).

## Frequently asked questions
### Can this checker tell me if a Pinterest username is taken?
Not with certainty. It checks the name against Pinterest's rules and opens the profile URL so you can see whether an account uses it. The username field in your Pinterest settings gives the final answer.

### How many characters can a Pinterest username have?
A Pinterest username can have 3 to 30 characters at the time of writing, using letters, numbers and underscores.

### Can a Pinterest username have a period or a hyphen?
No. Only letters, numbers and underscores are allowed. Use an underscore or leave the separator out.

### What's the difference between a Pinterest username and a profile name?
The username is the unique part of your profile URL and follows strict rules. The profile name is the display name on your profile; it can have spaces and doesn't have to be unique.

### Can I change my Pinterest username?
Yes. Change it in Settings under Edit profile. Your profile URL changes too, so update any links you've shared.

### Why does no profile show up, but Pinterest says the name is taken?
The name may belong to a deactivated or suspended account, or Pinterest may reserve it. Those names can't be claimed even when no profile appears.

### Can I check several Pinterest names at once?
Yes. Switch to **Check a list** and paste up to 200 names. The checker marks which follow Pinterest's rules, links each valid one to its profile, and copies the valid ones.

### Does the checker save the names I type?
No. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
