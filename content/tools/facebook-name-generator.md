## What a Facebook name generator can (and can't) help with
Facebook requires your personal profile to use the name you go by in everyday life, and this tool isn't built to help anyone get around that rule. It is useful for a **nickname** in your profile's "other names", a **Page** for your creative work, or a **Group** you're starting.

### Your profile name stays real
Facebook's name standards ask for the name your friends and family know you by. Accounts that break this can be asked to confirm their name, so a made-up name doesn't belong in that field.

### Nicknames live in "other names"
Your profile details can list other names, such as a nickname or maiden name, and show one at the top of your profile. That's the home for "Bex", as long as people actually call you that.

### Pages are for your public side
If you post as a band, artist, coach or creator, a Facebook Page gives you a separate public presence with its own name, while your profile stays personal.

## Facebook name rules and limits
Summary at the time of writing; see the [Facebook Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/help) for the full name standards.

| Name type | Main rules |
|---|---|
| Profile name | The name you use in everyday life; no symbols, numbers, unusual capitalization, repeated characters, titles or phrases |
| Other names | Nickname, maiden name, alternate spelling and similar; must be real names you go by |
| Page name | Must reflect the Page; no misleading capitals or symbols, no generic word alone, no "official" unless it is |
| Group name | Should describe the group accurately; can be edited by admins |

### Changing your profile name
Facebook limits how often you can change your profile name (once every 60 days at the time of writing) and reviews some changes. Use the official settings, and confirm the name if asked.

### Pages and Groups are easier to adjust
Page name changes go through a review, and Group admins can rename a Group, but choosing well up front avoids confusing members.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the Facebook name generator free?
Yes, and there's nothing to install or sign up for. Ideas are generated in your browser, so your keywords stay private.

### Can I use a fake name on my Facebook profile?
No. Facebook requires the name you use in everyday life on personal profiles, and this tool is not meant to help get around that. Use it for nicknames you actually go by, Page names and Group names.

### Where do I add a nickname on Facebook?
In your profile's About section, under details about you, you can add other names such as a nickname or maiden name, and choose whether to show one at the top of your profile.

### Does the generator check if a Page or Group name is available?
No. Names too close to existing ones can confuse people, so search Facebook and, for anything commercial, check trademarks.
