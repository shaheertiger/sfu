## What makes a good YouTube brand channel name
For a business, the safest YouTube channel name is usually your company name, with a short descriptor only when it adds something people search for. The harder choices are the handle and whether you need more than one channel. Generate 30 ideas above, then use the guidance below.

### Recognition first, creativity second
Customers type the name on your invoice or storefront. A clever channel name that differs from it makes them wonder if they've found the real you.

### A descriptor can match how people search
"Fernway Tools" is fine. "Fernway Tools Workshop" or "Fernway Tools Academy" tells viewers the channel is full of tutorials, which can be exactly what a customer with a half-assembled shelf is looking for. Add a descriptor when it reflects the content, not just to fill space.

### The handle should mirror your domain
If your website is fernwaytools.com, @fernwaytools is the natural handle. Matching domains, handles and other social accounts reassures viewers and makes it easier for partners to tag you correctly.

## YouTube name rules and limits for brands
Values at the time of writing; check [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube) for the latest.

| Field | Limit | Allowed characters |
|---|---|---|
| Channel name | Up to 50 characters | Letters, numbers, spaces, many symbols |
| Handle | 3–30 characters | Letters, numbers, underscores, hyphens, periods |
| Channel description | 1,000 characters | Plain text and links |

### Share access instead of passwords
Brand channels usually have several people uploading and replying. YouTube's channel permissions let you invite colleagues with specific roles, so nobody needs the owner's login.

### One channel or several?
Most businesses do best with one channel and playlists for each content type. Separate channels make sense when the audiences are truly different, such as a consumer channel and a channel for trade partners, or channels in different languages. If you split, keep a shared root in every name and handle: "Fernway Tools" and "Fernway Tools Pro".

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the YouTube page name generator free?
Yes. It works in your browser with no account, and nothing you enter is uploaded.

### Should my business channel use the exact company name?
In most cases, yes. It's what customers search for and what confirms the channel is genuine. Add a descriptor only when it reflects the content, like tutorials or support.

### Will the tool tell me if a brand channel name is free to use?
No. It only suggests names. Search YouTube, test the handle in YouTube Studio, and do a trademark search before committing.

### Can I rename a business channel later?
Yes, the channel name and handle can be edited in YouTube Studio, but YouTube limits how often. A rename also means updating links on your website, packaging and other profiles, so choose for the long term.
