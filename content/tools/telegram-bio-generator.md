## What makes a good Telegram bio
A Telegram bio is tiny: 70 characters for most accounts and 140 with Telegram Premium. That's about one sentence, so the best bios say one useful thing, like what you do or why someone might message you. This free generator writes 12 options and counts every character for you.

### One idea, not three
With 70 characters, trying to fit your job, hobbies and a quote leaves you with a cramped line nobody reads. Pick the single fact that matters most to the people who contact you.

### Context for people who meet you in groups
Many people open your profile after seeing your name in a large group chat. A bio like "Moderator here. DM me about event tickets" answers their question before they ask it.

### A signal about how to reach you
If you prefer email for work requests or only reply in the evenings, the bio is a polite place to say so.

## Telegram bio rules and limits
Telegram keeps profile fields separate, and each has its own rules.

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Bio (standard account) | 70 characters |
| Bio (Telegram Premium) | 140 characters |
| Username | 5–32 characters, a–z, 0–9 and underscores |
| Channel or group name | Up to 128 characters |

### Your username does a different job
The bio describes you; the username lets people find and message you without your phone number. If you're choosing a new one, the [Telegram name generator](/tools/telegram-name-generator) suggests options that follow the 5–32 character rule.

### Channel and group descriptions are separate fields
This tool is for your personal profile bio. Channels and groups have their own description field with different space, so a channel can carry more detail about what you post and how often.

### Privacy settings affect who sees it
Telegram's privacy settings let you control who can see several parts of your profile. Check **Settings > Privacy and Security** if you'd rather show your bio only to contacts.

## Frequently asked questions
### How many characters can a Telegram bio have?
A Telegram bio holds 70 characters on a standard account. Telegram Premium raises that to 140. Spaces, punctuation and emoji all count toward the total.

### Why does Telegram cut off my bio when I paste it?
Your text is probably longer than your account's limit. Check the count shown next to each generated bio and pick one at 70 or under, or trim filler words like "just" and "really" until it fits.

### Is a Telegram bio the same as a channel description?
No. Your bio sits on your personal profile. A channel or group has its own description field that you edit from the channel's settings, and it allows more room.

### Can I use fancy fonts in my Telegram bio?
You can paste Unicode-styled text from the [Telegram font generator](/tools/telegram-font-generator). Those styled letters are separate characters, so screen readers may struggle with them and they still count toward your limit. Use them for one word at most.
