## What makes a good Facebook Page name
A strong Facebook Page name matches the name customers already use for you, says what you do when that isn't obvious, and follows Facebook's Page name standards so it's approved without a fight. Type a keyword above to get 30 ideas, then use these guidelines to narrow them down.

### It matches your real-world name
If your shop sign says "Harbor Street Bakery", your Page should too. People search Facebook for the name they saw on a receipt or a storefront, so a creative variation can make you harder to find.

### It adds context when the name alone is vague
"Juniper" could be a florist, a salon or a café. Adding a short descriptor or location ("Juniper Florals", "Juniper Café Portland") helps the right people recognize you in search results and suggested Pages.

### It looks like a real organization
Facebook's standards reject names that are all capitals, packed with symbols, or that use a generic word on its own (like just "Coffee"). Clean, normally capitalized names get approved faster and look more trustworthy.

Four quick steps take you from a keyword to a shortlist.

### Pick a keyword that describes you
Enter your trade, product or cause: "landscaping", "vintage records", "neighborhood cleanup". If you already have a brand name you love, enter that instead to get variations around it.

### Check before you create the Page
The tool can't see existing Pages. Search Facebook for each finalist, check your local business registry, and run a trademark search. A clash found now costs nothing; one found after a year of posting costs you recognition.

## Facebook Page name rules and limits
Facebook reviews Page names against its Page name standards. Key points at the time of writing are below; see the [Facebook Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/help) for the full list.

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| No misleading capitalization | Avoid ALL CAPS or rAnDoM capitals, except real acronyms |
| No excess symbols | Skip decorative characters and unnecessary punctuation |
| No generic terms alone | "Pizza" won't pass; "Lucia's Pizza" can |
| No "official" unless you are | Don't imply you represent a brand or person you don't |
| No abusive or misleading terms | Names can't be offensive or impersonate others |

### Page name versus Page username
Your Page name is the display name. The Page username is the short @name in your Page's web address, which makes it easy to share. At the time of writing, usernames use letters, numbers and periods and must be at least 5 characters, and each one can only belong to one Page.

### Changing a Page name later
Admins can request a name change, which Facebook reviews. A completely different name may be questioned if it doesn't reflect the same organization, so treat the name as long-term.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the Facebook Page name generator free?
Yes. It's free, runs in your browser and doesn't need an account. Nothing you type leaves your device.

### Can I change my Facebook Page name after creating it?
Usually, yes. Admins can request a new name in the Page settings, and Facebook reviews the request.

### Why was my Page name rejected?
Common reasons include unusual capitalization, extra symbols, a generic word on its own, or a name that suggests you're someone you're not. Rewrite it in plain, normally capitalized words and try again.

### Does the tool check trademarks?
No. It suggests names; it can't check trademarks, business registries or Facebook. Do those checks yourself before printing anything.

### Can I put emoji in a Facebook Page name?
Facebook's standards discourage decorative characters in Page names. Save emoji and styled text from the [Facebook font generator](/tools/facebook-font-generator) for posts.
