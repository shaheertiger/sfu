## What makes a good TikTok username
A good TikTok username is one viewers can catch from a quick glance or a voiceover and type into search without pausing the next video. It should hint at your niche, fit TikTok's 24-character limit, and look good stamped on your clips. Enter a keyword above to get 30 ideas, then check your favorites against the points below.

### It's readable in a split second
Your username shows on the For You feed, in comments and on duets and stitches, and viewers scroll fast. Familiar words beat clever spellings: "@plantsbyrae" reads quicker than "@plnts.x.r4e".

### It sounds right when you say it
Many creators sign off with "follow @..." in their videos. Say the name out loud: if a listener can't tell where the periods or underscores go, they'll search for the wrong thing.

### It looks good as a watermark
When people save a TikTok, the downloaded video carries your @username. That means your handle travels to group chats and other apps. A tidy, recognizable name turns those shares into free introductions.

### It fits your niche without trapping you
"@tinyapartmentcook" tells viewers exactly what you do, which helps early on. If you suspect you'll branch out, keep the niche word at the end so a future rename only swaps one part.

## TikTok name rules and limits
Values below are at the time of writing and can change.

| Field | Limit | Allowed characters |
|---|---|---|
| Username | Up to 24 characters | Letters, numbers, underscores, periods |
| Name (display name) | Up to 30 characters | Letters, spaces, emoji and most symbols |
| Bio | 80 characters | Text and emoji |
| Caption | 4,000 characters | Text, hashtags, mentions |

### The period rule
TikTok allows periods inside a username but not at the very end. "@rae.cooks" works; "@raecooks." doesn't. If you edit an idea by hand, keep any period in the middle.

### How often you can change it
TikTok limits username changes (at the time of writing, once every 30 days). Changing it also changes your profile link, so old links in other bios or videos may stop working. Settle on a name before you start promoting it.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the TikTok name generator free?
Yes. No account or app download is needed, and your keywords never leave your browser.

### How many characters can a TikTok username have?
Up to 24 at the time of writing, using letters, numbers, underscores and periods. Your display name is separate and allows up to 30 characters.

### Does the generator check if a username is taken?
No. The tool can't see TikTok's accounts. Paste your choice into TikTok's Edit profile screen, which tells you right away whether it's available.

### Should I use underscores or periods?
Either works, but both are easy to forget when someone types your name from memory. Use one only when it aids readability, and say it clearly in videos.

### Can I use fancy fonts in my TikTok username?
No. Usernames only accept plain letters, numbers, underscores and periods. You can try styled text in your display name or bio with the [TikTok font generator](/tools/tiktok-font-generator), though some symbols may not display everywhere.
