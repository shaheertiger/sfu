## Styles you can generate for TikTok

TikTok's profile and caption fields only use the app's default typeface, but they'll display Unicode characters that look like other fonts. Type your text, scroll through 30+ styles and tap one to copy it.

### Bold styles for hooks

Serif bold (𝐑𝐞𝐚𝐝 𝐭𝐡𝐢𝐬), sans bold (𝗥𝗲𝗮𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝘀) and bold italic grab attention in the first words of a caption or a pinned comment.

### Expressive styles for bios

Bold script (𝓡𝓮𝓪𝓭 𝓽𝓱𝓲𝓼), gothic, double-struck letters and small caps suit the personality-driven bios that are common on TikTok.

### Fun styles

Bubble (Ⓡⓔⓐⓓ), black bubble, squared, fullwidth vaporwave, superscript, upside-down text and strikethrough. These read best in short bursts, like a one-word reaction.

### Characters, not fonts

Each style is built from Unicode characters that resemble letters. They're not font files, so TikTok, search and assistive tech treat them as different characters from the plain alphabet.

## Where styled text works on TikTok

### Bio

TikTok bios are short: 80 characters at the time of writing. A single styled word or phrase gives you personality without eating the whole limit. The [TikTok bio generator](/tools/tiktok-bio-generator) can suggest wording that fits.

### Display name

Your display name (the name shown above your @handle) accepts most Unicode styles. Your username is stricter: up to 24 characters using letters, numbers, underscores and periods only. Styled letters won't be accepted there. Brainstorming? Try the [TikTok name generator](/tools/tiktok-name-generator).

### Captions

Captions can be long, up to 4,000 characters at the time of writing, but viewers mostly see the first line over the video. Put a short styled hook up front and keep the rest plain.

### Comments and replies

Styled text works in comments and replies. A bold line in a pinned comment, like "𝗣𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝟮 𝗶𝘀 𝘂𝗽", helps viewers find what they're looking for. The [TikTok comment generator](/tools/tiktok-comment-generator) has more ideas for replies you write yourself.

### On-screen text

TikTok's video editor has its own text tool with built-in fonts. Those are real fonts and look sharper on video than pasted Unicode, so use the editor's options for text over your clip.

## Best practices for TikTok

### Keep search terms plain

Many people use TikTok like a search engine, and the app matches words in your caption. If your caption says "𝗲𝗮𝘀𝘆 𝗽𝗮𝘀𝘁𝗮 𝗿𝗲𝗰𝗶𝗽𝗲," TikTok search may not connect it to "easy pasta recipe." Style a separate hook and write the searchable phrase in normal letters.

### Leave hashtags alone

Hashtags only work in plain characters. A styled hashtag won't link to the tag page, so it does nothing for discovery.

### Use styles sparingly for accessibility

Screen readers may read styled characters as individual symbols, which makes a fully styled caption confusing. A few emphasized words are fine; whole paragraphs aren't.

### Preview on a second phone

Rare characters can appear as blank boxes on some Android devices. Check your bio from a friend's phone, or log in on another device, before you settle on a style.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you get different fonts on TikTok?

For text on top of a video, use the fonts in TikTok's editor. For your bio, display name, captions and comments, copy a Unicode style from this generator and paste it in. TikTok has no font picker for those fields.

### Can I put a fancy font in my TikTok username?

No. Usernames are limited to letters, numbers, underscores and periods. Use a styled display name instead and keep your @handle plain so people can find and tag you.

### Do styled fonts count toward the TikTok bio limit?

Yes. Every styled character counts, and some apps count them as more than one character. Leave a little room under the 80-character limit and check that your bio isn't cut off.

### Will TikTok remove posts with fancy text?

Using Unicode characters isn't against TikTok's rules on its own. Just don't use styled text to get around content filters or to impersonate another account.

### Is the TikTok font generator free?

Yes. It works in your browser without an account, and the text you type isn't uploaded anywhere.

### Why does my styled text look different on TikTok?

TikTok uses the fonts on your device to display these characters. Each phone draws them a little differently, and a few characters may not be supported at all.
