## What makes a good YouTube channel name
A good YouTube name tells a new viewer what kind of videos to expect, is easy to say out loud, and still works as a handle. Enter a topic above to get 30 starting points, then use the checks below to pick the one worth keeping.

### It survives being said out loud
Viewers recommend channels in conversation, in comments and in other creators' videos. If you have to spell your name for someone, you lose some of those mentions.

### It leaves room to grow
"BeginnerSourdoughDaily" boxes you in if you later film pasta or travel. A name that hints at you or your point of view ("Crumb Theory", "Flour & Field") lets the channel change direction without a rebrand.

### It works as a handle
Every channel also gets an @handle. If your name can become your handle with little or no change, people searching for you find you faster. Keep a backup spelling ready in case the exact handle is taken.

## YouTube name rules and limits
YouTube separates your channel name (the display name people see) from your handle (the unique @name in your URL). Values below are at the time of writing.

| Field | Limit | Allowed characters |
|---|---|---|
| Channel name | Up to 50 characters | Letters, numbers, spaces and many symbols |
| Handle | 3–30 characters | Letters, numbers, underscores, hyphens, periods |
| Channel description | 1,000 characters | Plain text |

### Names are shared, handles are not
Two channels can have the same name, but only one can hold a given handle. That is why the handle matters more for being found, and why you should claim it as soon as you settle on a name.

### Changing your name later
You can edit both in YouTube Studio, but YouTube limits how often, and links using an old handle may stop pointing to you. See [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube) for the current rules.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the YouTube name generator free?
Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up, and nothing you type is uploaded.

### Does it check if a YouTube name is available?
No. The generator cannot look up YouTube accounts. Search the name on YouTube and try the handle in YouTube Studio. If the handle is taken, try a small variation such as adding "tv", "hq" or your first name.

### Should my channel name and handle match?
Ideally, yes. A matching handle makes you easier to find and tag. If the exact match is gone, keep the handle as close as you can and skip long strings of numbers, which can look like spam.

### How long should a YouTube channel name be?
YouTube allows up to 50 characters at the time of writing, but shorter is better. Two or three words, under about 20 characters, usually shows in full on most screens.

### Can I use fancy fonts or emoji in my channel name?
Some symbols may be accepted, but Unicode "fonts" are hard to search for and screen readers may read them letter by letter.
