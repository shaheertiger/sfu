## What makes a good social media username
The best username is one you can use everywhere: short, easy to spell, and free of characters that some networks reject. This generator gives you 30 ideas at a time from a keyword and a style, so you can find a handle that works on more than one platform.

### One handle, every platform
When your name is the same on Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube, people who find you in one place can find you in the others without guessing. It also makes your handle easier to print on a business card, a podcast outro or a product label.

### Short beats clever
Every extra letter is another chance for a typo when someone tags you. Aim for something you could read to a friend over the phone once and have them type correctly.

### Plain characters travel best
Periods, hyphens and capital letters are each allowed on some networks and rejected on others. A name built from lowercase letters, numbers and at most one underscore is the safest bet if you want the same handle everywhere.

## Username rules and limits by platform
Each network sets its own rules. The table compares username or handle limits on the major platforms (values at the time of writing).

| Platform | Length | Allowed characters |
|---|---|---|
| X | 4–15 | Letters, numbers, underscores |
| Instagram | Up to 30 | Letters, numbers, periods, underscores |
| TikTok | Up to 24 | Letters, numbers, underscores, periods |
| YouTube handle | 3–30 | Letters, numbers, underscores, hyphens, periods |
| Discord | 2–32 | Lowercase letters, numbers, underscores, periods |
| Telegram | 5–32 | a–z, 0–9, underscores |
| Reddit | 3–20 | Letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores |

### The cross-platform sweet spot
Put the rows together and a pattern appears: **5 to 15 characters, lowercase letters and numbers, and an underscore only if you need one**. That range clears X's 15-character cap and Telegram's 5-character minimum, and it avoids periods and hyphens, which are each blocked somewhere.

### When the perfect handle is taken
Add a short, meaningful word rather than random digits. Endings like "studio", "hq", "makes" or "irl", or a prefix like "hey" or "its", keep the name readable. Use the same fallback on every platform so your identity stays consistent.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the social media name generator free?
Yes. It works in your browser with no account, and nothing you type is sent to a server.

### How does the generator come up with names?
It uses templates, word lists and the rules of each platform to assemble names. That keeps results quick and predictable, and you can regenerate as often as you like.

### What's the best length for a username?
Short enough to type from memory and long enough to be unique. Somewhere around 6 to 12 characters is comfortable for most people and fits even X's 15-character limit.

### Should I use underscores or periods?
If you want one handle everywhere, use underscores sparingly and skip periods. X and Telegram don't allow periods, while Instagram, TikTok and Discord do.

### Can I use the same username for a business?
You can, but check trademarks first, and make sure no one else is already trading under that name in your market. A clash can force a rename after you have built an audience.
