## What the X Bold Text Generator Actually Does

Regular posts on X are plain text. There's no bold button in the standard composer, and the X app shows every post in its own font. This X bold text generator gets around that by replacing your letters with bold-looking Unicode characters, so "Big news" becomes 𝐁𝐢𝐠 𝐧𝐞𝐰𝐬 or 𝗕𝗶𝗴 𝗻𝗲𝘄𝘀. X treats them as ordinary characters, so the bold survives copy and paste and shows up for everyone who reads your post.

The bold letters come from Unicode's Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols block, a set of styled letters created for math and science. Phones, browsers and the X apps include fonts for them, which is why they display almost everywhere.

### X bold vs. X's own formatting

X has offered bold and italic formatting to some Premium subscribers, mostly in long posts written on the web. Availability and menus change between versions, so check your composer. If you have it, native formatting is the better choice inside a post because it's real formatting. For everyone else, and for places that never have formatting (your display name, bio, replies on mobile), Unicode bold is the only option.

### What happens to your text

- **Latin letters (A–Z, a–z)** become bold.
- **Digits** turn bold in Bold (serif), Bold (sans), Double-struck and Black bubble. The other bold styles leave numbers plain.
- **Accented letters** keep their accent: "café" becomes 𝐜𝐚𝐟𝐞́.
- **Punctuation, emoji, @mentions' @ sign, # and non-Latin scripts** pass through unchanged.

### The catch: bold letters cost 2 characters on X

X doesn't count every character as 1. It uses a weighted count: most Latin letters, digits and punctuation count as 1, while emoji, CJK characters and most symbols count as 2. **Unicode bold letters are outside X's "light" ranges, so each one counts as 2.**

| Text | Characters you see | X's weighted count |
|---|---|---|
| Big news | 8 | 8 |
| 𝐁𝐢𝐠 𝐧𝐞𝐰𝐬 | 8 | 15 |
| Hot take incoming | 17 | 17 |
| 𝗛𝗼𝘁 𝘁𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗶𝗻𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗴 | 17 | 32 |

The space in the middle still counts as 1, which is why the bold versions come to 15 and 32 rather than 16 and 34. In practice: a fully bold post can only hold about 140 bold characters within the 280 limit. Bold a few words and you'll barely notice; bold a whole post and you'll run out of room fast. Paste your final draft into the [X character counter](/tools/x-character-counter) to see the exact weighted count.

## Best Practices for Bold Text on X

### Bold a few words, not the whole post

A fully bold post is harder to read, uses twice the character budget and looks like spam. One bold hook or label per post is plenty.

### Screen readers read these characters poorly

This is the biggest drawback. Unicode bold isn't formatting; it's a set of math symbols. Many screen readers read each letter out separately, announce it as "mathematical bold capital B", or skip it entirely. Blind and low-vision people use X heavily, so keep every important word, such as the news itself, a date or a link description, in plain text.

### Keep searchable words plain

X search treats 𝗹𝗮𝘂𝗻𝗰𝗵 and "launch" as different words. If you want people to find a post by its keyword or topic, leave that word unbolded.

### Never bold hashtags or cashtags

A bold #𝗹𝗮𝘂𝗻𝗰𝗵 isn't the same tag as #launch, so it won't appear in the hashtag's results. Bold the words around it instead.

### Budget for the double count

Every bold letter costs 2 of your 280. A short bold opener costs a few extra characters; a bold paragraph can cost a third of the post. Check the weighted count before you post.

### Check on another device

Bold (serif) and Bold (sans) display almost everywhere. Black bubble and black squared can look like emoji or show as boxes on older devices.

### Don't impersonate

A bold display name that copies a brand, journalist or public figure misleads people and can get your account suspended. Our guide to [X shadowbans](/blog/am-i-shadowbanned-twitter) covers what else can quietly limit an account.

### Small businesses and ecommerce

Bold the offer, not the whole post: "𝟮𝟬% 𝗼𝗳𝗳 this weekend only." Bold (sans) styles digits, so prices and percentages match the bold words.

### Thread writers and educators

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you bold text on X (Twitter)?

Copy a bold style from this X bold text generator and paste it into your post, reply, bio or display name. Some Premium subscribers also have native bold in the composer on the web, but regular posts don't have a bold button.

### Does bold text count as more characters on X?

Yes. X counts each Unicode bold letter as 2 characters, while ordinary letters count as 1. A fully bold post can hold about 140 bold letters within the 280 limit.

### Can I bold my X display name?

Yes. Paste bold text into the Name field in Edit profile. Your @username can't be bold, and a fully bold name can make it harder for people to find you in search.

### Will bold text hurt my reach on X?

There's no public evidence that X penalizes Unicode bold itself. But bold words don't match searches, bold hashtags don't link to the plain tag, and a fully bold post can read as spam, so use it sparingly.

### Why does my bold text show as boxes for some people?

Their device doesn't have a font with those characters. Bold (serif) and Bold (sans) are supported almost everywhere; black bubble and black squared fail more often on older phones.

### Is bold text bad for accessibility on X?

It can be. Screen readers often read Unicode bold as symbol names or skip it. Use bold for emphasis only, and keep essential information in plain text.

### Can I use italic or other fonts on X too?

Yes. The [font generator](/tools/font-generator) has 24 styles, including italic, script and monospace. They count as 2 characters per letter on X as well, except small caps, tiny and wide, which vary.

### Is this X bold text generator free?

Yes. It's free, needs no account and runs entirely in your browser.
