## What makes a good Instagram username
A good Instagram username is short enough to remember, easy to spell after hearing it once, and clear about who you are or what you post. It's also the @handle in your profile link, in every tag and in every mention, so it does a lot of work. Enter a word above to get 30 ideas, then check your favorites against the points below.

### People can type it from memory
Followers find you by typing your handle into search or tagging you in a Story. Every extra period, underscore or swapped letter is a chance for a typo. "@goldenhourdiary" is easier to type than "@g0ldn.h0ur_d1ary".

### It works with your display name
Instagram shows two names: your **username** (the @handle) and your **name** (the bold line on your profile). Both are searchable. A short, memorable handle plus a descriptive name, like "@rae.bakes" with "Rae | Sourdough & Pastry", covers both the person and the topic.

### It still fits in a year
"@rae.bakes2026" dates itself. A niche word is helpful early on, but if you might branch out, keep it at the end so a later rename only changes one part.

### It matches your other accounts
If you're also on TikTok, YouTube or X, using one handle everywhere helps people find you across platforms. Our [social media name generator](/tools/name-generator) only suggests names that fit every major network's rules.

## Instagram username rules and limits
Values below are at the time of writing and can change.

| Field | Limit | Allowed characters |
|---|---|---|
| Username (@handle) | 1 to 30 characters | Letters, numbers, periods, underscores |
| Name (display name) | Up to 30 characters | Letters, spaces, emoji and most symbols |
| Bio | 150 characters | Text, emoji, @mentions, #hashtags |
| Caption | 2,200 characters | Text, emoji, mentions, hashtags |

### Periods, underscores and capitals
Periods are allowed in the middle of a username but not at the start or end, and never two in a row. Underscores have no such restriction. Instagram stores usernames in lowercase, so "GoldenHour" and "goldenhour" are the same handle. Hyphens, spaces, apostrophes and emoji aren't allowed.

### Changing your username later
You can change your username in **Edit profile**. Your old handle may become available to others, and links or tags that used it stop pointing to you, so pick a name you're happy to keep before printing it anywhere.

## Finsta name ideas
A finsta ("fake Instagram") is a second, usually private account for close friends, unpolished photos and inside jokes. A good finsta name is recognizable to your friends but not obvious to everyone else. Pick the **Finsta** style above and type your nickname, an inside joke or a pet's name. You'll get ideas like "maya_dump", "tiny.maya" or "mayalore".

A few tips:
- **Leave out your full name** if you'd rather the account didn't come up when people search for you.
- **Use a nickname only friends know**, so the right people still recognize it.
- **Set the account to private** in **Settings > Account privacy**, and approve followers one by one.

You can switch between accounts without logging out: tap your username at the top of your profile and choose **Add Instagram account**. Our guide on [connecting multiple Instagram accounts](/blog/connect-multiple-instagram-accounts) covers running more than one.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the Instagram name generator free?
Yes. No account or download is needed, and your keywords never leave your browser.

### Is this an AI Instagram name generator?
No. It combines your word with curated word lists and patterns for each style, then keeps only names that follow Instagram's username rules. That makes it instant, and every idea is a valid handle. Press **More ideas** as often as you like for new combinations.

### How many characters can an Instagram username have?
Up to 30, using letters, numbers, periods and underscores. Periods can't go at the start or end or appear twice in a row.

### Does the generator check if a username is taken?
No, because the tool can't see Instagram's accounts. Paste your choice into **Edit profile** in Instagram, which tells you instantly whether it's free. The [Instagram handle checker](/tools/instagram-handle-checker) confirms the format and opens the profile link so you can see if an account exists.

### What's the difference between an Instagram username and a name?
Your username is your unique @handle and profile link. Your name is the bold display name above your bio. It doesn't have to be unique and can include spaces and emoji. People can find you by searching either.

### Can I use fancy fonts in my Instagram username?
No. Usernames only accept plain letters, numbers, periods and underscores. You can use styled text in your display name and bio with the [Instagram font generator](/tools/instagram-font-generator), though some symbols may not display on every phone.
