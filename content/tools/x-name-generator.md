## What makes a good X username
On X (formerly Twitter), your identity is split in two: an @handle people use to mention you, and a bold display name above every post. A good handle is short and easy to type; a good display name tells strangers who you are at a glance. Enter a keyword above to get 30 ideas for both.

### Short handles get mentioned more easily
Every reply and tag starts with your handle. Seven letters are easy to type from memory; fifteen with underscores invite typos.

### Your handle should outlast your current job
"@maya_at_acme" is awkward the day you leave Acme. Build your handle around your name, nickname or a lasting interest, and put your current role in the display name or bio, which are easy to update.

### Your display name can work harder
The display name allows up to 50 characters and can be changed whenever you like. Many people use it for their name plus a short tag, such as "Maya Lin | Product Design" or "Theo – writes about trains". Keep the name first so it's still readable when the tag gets cut off on small screens.

## X name rules and limits
Values at the time of writing; see [X's help center](https://help.x.com) for the latest.

| Field | Limit | Allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Handle (@username) | 4–15 characters | Letters, numbers, underscores |
| Display name | Up to 50 characters | Letters, numbers, spaces, emoji and most symbols |
| Bio | 160 characters | Text, emoji, links, mentions |
| Post | 280 characters (25,000 with Premium) | URLs count as 23; most emoji count as 2 |

### Handles aren't case-sensitive
On X, "@NightSkyTheo" and "@nightskytheo" are the same account. Capital letters only change how the handle displays, so use them to make a run-together name readable.

### Some words aren't allowed
X reserves certain words and blocks them in handles. If a name you like is rejected even though no account seems to use it, a reserved word may be the reason; the help center lists the current restrictions.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the X name generator free?
Yes. It's free, works in your browser and doesn't need sign-up. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere.

### How long can an X username be?
Handles must be 4 to 15 characters at the time of writing, using only letters, numbers and underscores. Display names can be up to 50 characters.

### Can I change my X handle and display name?
Yes. Both can be changed in your profile settings. The display name is safe to change often; changing your handle can break old links and confuse people who mention you, so do it rarely.

### Can I use emoji or fancy fonts in my X name?
Emoji and Unicode styles work in display names but not in handles. Use them sparingly: screen readers may read styled letters one by one, and search may not match them.

### Does this tool check whether a handle is taken?
No. It suggests ideas only. X's sign-up and settings screens check availability instantly, so keep a few backups ready.
