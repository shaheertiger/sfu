## What makes a good TikTok comment
TikTok comments move fast, and the ones that get noticed are short, specific and in on the joke. This free TikTok comment generator turns a quick description of a video into 12 template-based ideas. You pick one, make it yours, and post it from your own account.

### It reacts to a specific moment
Mentioning the exact beat that made you laugh, such as "the cat walking in at the end," shows you watched to the end. Vague praise blends into hundreds of other comments.

### It's quick to read
Viewers skim comments while the video loops, so one sentence usually beats a paragraph.

### It asks for what comes next
On TikTok, "part 2?" style requests and questions about a recipe, outfit or tool are welcome. They give the creator material for a follow-up video.

### It laughs with the creator, not at them
Comment culture on TikTok is playful, but a joke at someone's expense can sink a thread. If you're unsure how it will read, choose the supportive or curious tone instead.

The tool works in your browser with no sign-up, and the text you type stays on your device.

## TikTok comment features and rules
TikTok gives creators several tools for managing the comment section. They're worth knowing whether you're commenting or being commented on.

### Reply to a comment with a video
Creators can answer a comment with a new video that shows the original comment on screen. Good questions in your comment section can become your next piece of content, and thoughtful comments on someone else's video can become theirs.

### Pin a comment on your own video
You can pin a comment to the top of your own video's comments. Pin the one that answers the most common question, or one that sets a friendly tone for the thread.

### Filter and moderate
TikTok lets creators filter comments containing chosen keywords and hold comments for review. If you manage a brand account, set these up before a post takes off rather than after.

### Community Guidelines on spam and fake engagement
TikTok's Community Guidelines prohibit spam and inauthentic engagement. Posting the same comment across many videos, using multiple accounts, or trading comments to game the algorithm can get your account restricted. Use these ideas one thoughtful comment at a time.

## TikTok comment ideas by situation
### Replying to your own audience
- "Recipe's in the next video, you're the third person to ask so I'm filming it tonight."
- "Thank you! The trick is cold butter, I'll show it slower in part 2."

### Supporting a creator you follow
- "Day 1 to day 30 is genuinely impressive. The landing at 0:40 deserved the slow-mo."
- "The way you explained this in under a minute is better than my entire semester."

### Engaging with peers in your niche
- "Fellow candle maker here. Your wick placement trick just solved my tunneling problem, thank you."

### Brand accounts
- "Love seeing it in your space. Which scent did you go with?"
- "Thanks for the honest review. We've passed your note about the lid to our product team."
