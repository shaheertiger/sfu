## Snapchat Username Rules at a Glance
A Snapchat username has to be 3 to 15 characters long, start with a letter, end with a letter or number, and use only letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores and at most one period. This Snapchat username checker tests a name against all of those rules as you type. When it passes, a button opens snapchat.com/add/yourname so you can see whether a profile already uses it.

|---|---|---|

| Characters | Letters, numbers, hyphens (-), underscores (_) and periods (.) | Yes |

| First character | Must be a letter | Yes |

## Snapchat Username Rules in Detail (3–15 Characters)
### Length: 3 to 15 characters
Snapchat usernames are short: 15 characters at most and 3 at least. The checker shows a count out of 15 and tells you how far over you are: "Too long: 18 characters, the limit is 15." A 20-character Instagram handle won't carry over as it is.

### Allowed characters: letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores, one period
Spaces, emoji, accented letters and symbols such as & or ! fail with "Only letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores and one period are allowed." Snapchat is unusual in allowing hyphens, which X, Instagram and Pinterest don't, so sunny-days is fine here but won't work on those platforms.

### Only one period
Snapchat allows a single period. sunny.days passes; sunny.day.s fails with "Only one period is allowed." If you want a second break, use a hyphen or underscore, like sunny.days-uk.

### Must start with a letter
A username can't start with a number or a symbol. 1sunny and _sunny both fail with "It has to start with a letter." Move the number to the end instead: sunny1.

### Must end with a letter or number
A trailing hyphen, underscore or period isn't allowed. sunny_ fails with "It has to end with a letter or number." This is one of the most common reasons a Snapchat name is rejected, because adding an underscore at the end is the go-to trick on other platforms.

### Lowercase only in practice
Snapchat shows usernames in lowercase, so capitals don't make a name different. The checker accepts capitals while you type, and the profile button turns the name into lowercase, which is how Snapchat's link works. If you want capitals, put them in your display name.

### Username vs. display name
Your username is unique, follows all the rules above, and is how people add you by search or by link. Your display name is what friends see in chats and stories. It can have spaces, capitals and emoji, and it doesn't have to be unique, so there's nothing to check for availability. If the username you want is taken, you can still show the full name in your display name and pick a close variation for the username.

## How to Check if a Snapchat Username Is Available
1. **Type the username you want.** Leave out the @, or paste it with one; the checker removes it.
2. **Read the result.** "Follows Snapchat's rules" means the format is valid. "Not allowed" comes with each reason, so you can fix them all at once.
3. **Open the profile link.** Tap the **Open snapchat.com/add/…** button. If a profile appears, the name is taken.
4. **Try a variation if needed.** Pick one of the suggestions, or paste a shortlist into **Check a list** and open each link.
5. **Claim it in the app.** On a new account, Snapchat asks for a username during sign-up and tells you right away if it's taken. On an existing account, open your profile, tap the settings gear and choose Username (menu labels change between app versions).
6. **Share the new link.** Your add link changes with your username, so update it wherever you've posted it.

## Best Practices for Picking a Snapchat Username
### Keep it easy to type from a sign or a story
People often add a Snapchat account by typing the name they saw somewhere else. Use one separator at most; mixing a hyphen, an underscore and a period in one name is hard to remember.

### Put the number at the end
Since a name can't start with a number and can't end with a symbol, a number at the very end (sunny7) is the simplest way to make a taken name unique.

### Think before you change it
Snapchat limits how often you can change your username, and your add link changes with it. Test the name here and settle on it before you switch.

### Leave out personal details
Your username is visible to anyone who has it or finds you in search. Skip your full name, birth year and school, especially for younger users.

### Watch for lookalikes
Lowercase l and the number 1 look almost the same in many fonts, and so do rn and m. A name that relies on them is easy to mistype and easy for someone else to imitate.

### Don't trust tools that ask for your login
No website can see Snapchat's private list of usernames. A "checker" that asks for your Snapchat password or a code sent to your phone is trying to take over your account. This tool never asks you to sign in.

### Need ideas?

## Frequently asked questions
### Can this checker tell me if a Snapchat username is taken?
Not with certainty. It checks the name against Snapchat's rules and opens the public snapchat.com/add link so you can see whether a profile uses it. Snapchat's sign-up screen or the username setting in the app gives the final answer.

### How many characters can a Snapchat username have?
A Snapchat username can have 3 to 15 characters at the time of writing.

### Can a Snapchat username have a period?
Yes, but only one. Hyphens and underscores are also allowed, as long as the name doesn't end with one.

### Why can't my Snapchat username start with a number?
Snapchat requires every username to start with a letter. Put the number later in the name, like sunny7 instead of 7sunny.

### Can I use capital letters in a Snapchat username?
Snapchat shows usernames in lowercase, so capitals don't make a different name. Use your display name if you want capitals or spaces.

### Why does nothing show up, but Snapchat says the name is taken?
The name may belong to a deleted, locked or banned account, or Snapchat may reserve it. Those names can't be claimed even when no profile appears.

### Can I check several Snapchat names at once?
Yes. Switch to **Check a list** and paste up to 200 names. The checker marks which follow Snapchat's rules, links each valid one to its profile, and copies the valid ones.

### Does the checker save the names I type?
No. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored.
