## 3. The structure they rank with

### Blog posts

See "Writing blog posts" in CLAUDE.md, which is based on Post Bridge's top three posts.

### Tool pages (Postiz)

All six reference pages follow the same layout:

1. **A title with a hook and the specs**: "Discord Character Counter – 2,000 Limit Checker (4,000 with Nitro)", "Discord Font Generator (𝓬𝓸𝓹𝔂 𝕒𝕟𝕕 𝓅𝒶𝓈𝓉𝑒) – 100+ Fonts", "Instagram Bio Generator [100% free]". Our titles already do this.
2. **The H1, a one-line instruction, then the tool**, with nothing between them. Resizers add an "at a glance" strip of sizes (Profile Banner 600 × 240, PFP 512 × 512).
3. **3,400–4,200 words** below the tool, in this order:
   - What it is or why you need it
   - The specs or variants, one H3 each (each font style, each Discord image size, each limit by surface)
   - How to use the tool
   - **Use cases by audience**, 4–7 H3s (creators, ecommerce, local business, coaches, bot builders)
   - Best practices, plus caveats (accessibility, where it doesn't work)
   - FAQ with 6–8 questions
   - "Schedule <platform> content with <product>" (the product pitch comes last)
   - "Check also" and "More free text generators" link lists
4. **A sticky sidebar CTA card** on desktop ("Do you want to grow your social media faster?", 4 bullets, button, video). Phones get a sticky CTA bar instead.

We now have the sidebar card and the phone bar (`components/marketing/GrowCta.jsx`). The remaining gaps are the use-cases-by-audience sections and the overall depth.
