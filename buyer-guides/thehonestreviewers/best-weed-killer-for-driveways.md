## 1. Roundup Max Control 365: Best overall weed killer for driveways

Max Control 365 combines three actives: glyphosate moves to the roots, diquat speeds up visible wilting, and imazapic sits in the soil and stops new weeds sprouting. In heavy rainfall areas you may need to reapply every 6 to 8 months; in heavy clay it may last longer than a year.

Read the restrictions before buying. The label says not to use it on lawns, over the root zones of trees or shrubs, on slopes, or along a fence with plants you want on the other side. For trees and shrubs, it says to stay farther from the trunk than twice the distance to the drip line. Nothing can be planted in treated soil for a year.

## How to choose a weed killer for driveways

### 1. Decide between contact and residual control

- **Contact killers** (diquat, ammonium nonanoate or pelargonic acid, vinegar, flame) kill what they touch, often within hours. No soil activity, so they are the safest choice near trees and lawns, but weeds regrow from roots.
- **Systemic killers** (glyphosate, and glufosinate in some products and states) move to roots for a more complete kill, typically over one to three weeks. They have little or no soil activity on their own.
- **Residual or "extended control" products** add a soil-active herbicide such as imazapic, imazapyr, oxyfluorfen, or in some products indaziflam, to stop new weeds for months. They do the most work per spray and carry the most risk.

Rule of thumb: use residual products only on areas that do not drain toward lawn, beds or trees. Everywhere else, use contact or systemic sprays and repeat as needed.

### 2. Map the roots before you spray

Tree roots routinely run under driveways and patios, well past the drip line, and can take up soil-active herbicides like imazapyr and imazapic. Both year-long labels say to stay farther from the trunk than twice the distance to the drip line. If a tree has a 20-foot canopy radius, that means at least 40 feet. If your driveway is under or near a large tree, skip residual products.

### 3. Watch runoff, slopes and drains

Rain moves residual herbicides across pavement into lawn edges, beds and storm drains. Do not spray before rain or on slopes, and do not let spray puddle. Check the label's rainfast time.

### 4. Match the product to the surface

- **Concrete and asphalt cracks:** any listed product works; a narrow-stream nozzle cuts drift.
- **Pavers:** contact sprays or flame near beds; then refill joints with polymeric sand to block regrowth. See our [best polymeric sand for pavers](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/best-polymeric-sand-for-pavers/).
- **Gravel driveways:** the most likely place a long-lasting product makes sense, if no trees or lawn are close.
- **Moss on asphalt:** weed killers are not the right tool. See [how to remove moss from an asphalt driveway](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/how-to-remove-moss-from-asphalt-driveway/).

### 5. Skip salt

Salt and salt-vinegar mixes kill weeds, but salt stays in the soil, moves to nearby lawn and plants, and can damage concrete and pavers.

### 6. Read the label, because the label is the law

Under federal law, using a pesticide in a way its label does not allow is illegal. The label sets where, how much, how often and how long before replanting. Some products are not registered in every state, and some states and towns restrict certain herbicides, so check local rules. Prevention also helps: a [driveway sealer](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/best-driveway-sealer/) closes the small cracks that weeds use to get started.

### Mistakes to avoid

- Spraying a year-long product on a driveway under trees
- Treating pavers next to a lawn edge with a residual product
- Spraying before rain or on a windy day
- Using salt, which ruins soil and can damage concrete
- Letting pets onto a treated area before the spray has dried

## FAQ

### What is the best weed killer for driveways and patios?

Away from trees and lawns, Roundup Max Control 365 is the most complete option, with up to 12 months of prevention on the label. For patios near beds, trees or lawn, a contact product like Ortho GroundClear Weed & Grass Killer is safer, though you will spray more often.

### What is the best weed killer for pavers?

Near beds, use a contact spray (ammonium nonanoate or 20% vinegar) or a flame weeder. Then sweep out debris and refill the joints with polymeric sand so new seeds have nowhere to root. Test vinegar first on limestone or travertine.

### What is the longest lasting weed killer for a gravel driveway?

Products with imazapyr or imazapic, such as Ortho GroundClear Year Long Vegetation Killer and Roundup Max Control 365, claim up to a year. Heavy rain shortens that, and clay soil can extend it. Use them only where no trees, shrubs or lawn are nearby, because the residual is what makes them last.

### Is there a pet safe driveway weed killer?

No herbicide label promises to be pet safe; most say to keep people and pets off until the spray has dried. A flame weeder or hand crack weeder avoids chemicals entirely, and contact sprays without soil residual are the next best choice.

### Can I use a crabgrass preventer on my driveway?

Lawn pre-emergents are labeled for turf, not hardscape, and do nothing to weeds already growing. For lawn edges next to the driveway, see our [best crabgrass preventer](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/best-crabgrass-preventer/) guide.

### How long before I can walk on a treated driveway?

Most labels allow people and pets back once the spray has dried, often within an hour or two in warm, dry weather. Store all products away from children and pets.
