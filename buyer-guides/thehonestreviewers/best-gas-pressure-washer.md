## How to choose the best gas pressure washers in 2026

### 1. Decide if you really need gas

A gas pressure washer for a driveway or a two-story house is a good fit. For cars, patio furniture and a small patio, a 1.2 to 2.0 GPM electric unit is quieter and easier. Our [electric vs gas pressure washer](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/electric-vs-gas-pressure-washer/) comparison walks through the line in more detail.

### 2. Look at GPM, not just PSI

PSI breaks dirt loose; GPM rinses it away. Multiply them to get cleaning units (CU). A 3,200 PSI, 2.5 GPM machine gives 8,000 CU; a 4,200 PSI, 4.0 GPM machine gives 16,800 CU. For flatwork, more GPM shortens the job more than more PSI. Rules of thumb:
- Vehicles, outdoor furniture: under 2,000 PSI (use a 40-degree tip on a gas unit)
- Decks, fences, vinyl siding: 2,500 to 3,000 PSI with a 25- or 40-degree tip
- Concrete driveways and walks: 3,000+ PSI and 2.5+ GPM

See [what PSI pressure washer you need](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/what-psi-pressure-washer-do-i-need/) and our [PSI vs GPM explainer](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/pressure-washer-psi-vs-gpm/) for more.

### 3. Pick the pump for your hours

- **Axial cam pump:** found on nearly every gas unit under about $600. Compact, light and maintenance-free, but sealed. When it wears out, you replace it. Fine for a few uses per year.
- **Triplex plunger pump:** three ceramic plungers driven by a crankshaft, usually from AAA, CAT or General. Runs at lower RPM, holds oil you can change, and can be rebuilt. Expect to pay $200 to $400 more, and expect it to last far longer if you use the machine regularly.

### 4. Know your engines

- **Honda GCV / GC (e.g. GCV200, GC190):** residential engines. Easy starting, light, widely serviced.
- **Honda GX (GX200, GX390):** commercial engines with cast-iron cylinder sleeves, built for long run times. Common on contractor machines.
- **Kohler and Briggs & Stratton:** both appear on pressure washers from several brands. Kohler's commercial lines are well regarded; Briggs residential engines are common on budget units.
- **House brands (Westinghouse, DeWalt, Generac, Champion):** often fine and often carry longer warranties, but fewer shops stock parts.

### 5. Cold-start and convenience features

Electric or battery start (like DeWalt's PressuReady) helps in cold weather and for anyone who struggles with a pull cord. Low-oil shutdown protects the engine. A 50-foot, 5/16- or 3/8-inch hose saves moving the machine. Onboard soap tanks only work with the low-pressure soap tip.

### 6. Plan for maintenance, noise and safety

- Change engine oil after the break-in period the manual specifies, then about every 50 hours or once a season.
- Use fresh gas with no more than 10% ethanol and a fuel stabilizer if it will sit more than a month.
- Gas pressure washers commonly run in the 85 to 95+ decibel range at the operator. Wear hearing protection, and check local noise rules before early-morning jobs.
- Engines produce carbon monoxide. Never run one in a garage, shed or other enclosed space, even with the door open, and keep the exhaust away from windows and doors.
- Before the first freeze, follow our [winterizing guide](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/how-to-winterize-a-pressure-washer/). Water left in the pump can freeze and crack it.

### Mistakes to avoid

- Using the 0-degree tip on wood, vinyl or car paint. It can gouge in a second.
- Running the pump with the trigger released for long periods. Even with thermal relief, the water heats up and wears seals.
- Starting the engine before water is flowing through the pump.
- Buying 4,000+ PSI for a deck and siding house. You'll spend more and risk more damage.
- Skipping a surface cleaner for big driveways. It cuts time and leaves no stripes.

## FAQ

### What is the best gas pressure washer for a driveway?

For a typical two-car driveway, a 3,200 to 3,400 PSI, 2.5 GPM unit like the Simpson MegaShot MSH3125 paired with a surface cleaner works well. For long driveways or frequent use, a 4.0 GPM machine like the Simpson PowerShot PS4240 roughly halves the time. Our [best pressure washer for concrete](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/best-pressure-washer-for-concrete/) guide covers more options.

### Are gas pressure washers better than electric?

They're more powerful and don't need an outlet, so they're better for large concrete areas, two-story siding and remote spots. Electric units are quieter, lighter and nearly maintenance-free, which suits cars, patios and small decks.

### How long does a gas pressure washer last?

It depends mostly on the pump. A homeowner axial pump used a few times a year can last many seasons, but it's sealed and gets replaced when it wears out. A triplex pump with regular oil changes and a commercial engine like the Honda GX200 can run for many years of heavy use and can be rebuilt.

### Why is my gas pressure washer not building pressure?

The most common causes are a clogged or worn nozzle tip, low inlet water supply, air in the pump, a kinked hose, or a worn unloader valve or pump seals. Start with the nozzle and the water supply before suspecting the pump. Our [pressure washer not building pressure](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/pressure-washer-not-building-pressure/) guide covers each fix.

### Can I use a gas pressure washer in California?

Yes, but you need a model certified by the California Air Resources Board (CARB). Many listings are 49-state versions that won't ship to California, so look for a 50-state or CARB-compliant variant of the model you want.
