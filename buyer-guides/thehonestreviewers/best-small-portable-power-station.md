## How to choose the best small power station in 2026

### 1. Size it with the runtime formula

Use this rule of thumb: **runtime (hours) = battery Wh x 0.85 / device watts.** The 0.85 accounts for inverter and conversion losses (DC loads lose less, sometimes closer to 0.9).

- 286 Wh unit, 20 W router plus modem: 286 x 0.85 / 20 = about 12 hours
- 288 Wh unit, 40 W CPAP (no humidifier): about 6 hours
- 1,024 Wh unit, 40 W CPAP: about 21 hours
- 1,024 Wh unit, 60 W laptop: about 14 hours

Check real draw with a plug-in watt meter. A CPAP's heated humidifier can double or triple its draw.

### 2. Continuous vs surge watts

Continuous output is what the unit can sustain. Surge (or peak) is a brief burst for startup. Motors and compressors (fridges, pumps, blenders) can pull two to three times their running watts for a second. "Boost" modes like X-Boost and Power Lifting are different: they lower voltage so heating appliances run slower. They don't help start motors.

### 3. UPS/EPS switchover

If a router, PC or CPAP must keep running through an outage, look for a published switchover time. Under 10-20 ms is enough for most electronics. Many units call this EPS rather than true online UPS, so a sensitive desktop PC may still reboot. Test it once by pulling the wall plug.

### 4. Weight and outlets

245-300 Wh units weigh about 6-10 lb; 1 kWh units about 22-28 lb. Count your plugs: one AC outlet means a power strip, and DC-only means adapters.

### 5. Solar input and recharge speed

Solar input caps how fast panels can refill the battery. A 300 Wh unit with 100-220 W input refills in about 2-4 hours of good sun. Real panel output is often 60-80% of the rating. See our [best portable solar panel](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/best-portable-solar-panel/) guide for pairing advice, and [how long it takes to charge a portable power station](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-charge-a-portable-power-station/) for wall and car charging times.

### 6. Chemistry and cycle life

Every pick here is rated for roughly 3,000-4,000 cycles to 80% capacity, years of weekly use. Older lithium-ion (NMC) units are lighter but rated for far fewer cycles. See [LiFePO4 vs lithium-ion power stations](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/lifepo4-vs-lithium-ion-power-stations/).

### 7. Air travel

Under FAA rules, spare lithium batteries up to 100 Wh can go in carry-on bags; 101-160 Wh needs airline approval (two spares max); over 160 Wh isn't allowed on passenger flights. **Every unit in this guide is over 160 Wh, so none can fly.** Airlines can be stricter.

### Mistakes to avoid

- Buying on peak watts. Size to continuous output.
- Forgetting conversion losses. Plan on about 85% of the label Wh.
- Running a CPAP's heated humidifier off a 300 Wh unit and expecting a full night.
- Storing at 100% in a hot car. LiFePO4 is tough, but heat still ages it. Store around 50-80% in moderate temps and top up every few months.

## FAQ

### What is the best small power station in 2026?

For most buyers, the EcoFlow RIVER 3 Plus: 286 Wh, 600 W, sub-10 ms UPS switchover, about 10 lb, and expandable to 858 Wh. For about 1 kWh, step up to the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2.

### What's the best 256Wh power station?

Several older models used 256 Wh packs, but most current compact units land between 245 Wh and 300 Wh. In that class, the RIVER 3 Plus (286 Wh) is the best all-rounder, the Bluetti Elite 30 V2 (288 Wh) offers the most outlets, and the RIVER 3 (245 Wh) is the budget pick. A few watt-hours either way makes little practical difference.

### What's the best portable power station under 500Wh?

The RIVER 3 Plus for general use, the Anker SOLIX C300 DC if your devices run on USB-C or 12 V, and the RIVER 3 if price and weight matter most. If you think you'll want around 500-850 Wh later, the RIVER 3 Plus is the only pick here that expands.

### Can a small power station run a CPAP all night?

A 288 Wh unit typically covers about 6 hours of a 40 W CPAP without heated humidification, and longer on a DC connection. With a heated humidifier, plan on 1,000 Wh or turn the humidifier off. Check your machine's draw first.

### Can I take a mini power station on a plane?

No. Every unit in this guide is well over the FAA's 160 Wh limit for passenger flights. Power banks under 100 Wh are allowed in carry-on bags, and 101-160 Wh needs airline approval.

### Are small portable power stations worth it?

For short outages, camping and CPAP use, usually yes. They're quiet, need no fuel and can be used indoors, unlike a gas generator. See [are portable power stations worth it](https://www.thehonestreviewers.com/are-portable-power-stations-worth-it/) for the full cost breakdown.
